## Editorial contract

Each module explains the capability the homework tests, names the public starter boundary, suggests a local self-check that does not depend on hidden Gradescope tests, and does not publish answers. Homework dates inside reused templates are not treated as authoritative when they conflict with the Spring 2026 schedule; the schedule and compiled bundle identity control the edition.
