## Version decision

Spring 2026 is the latest completed official offering and has a same-term chain of 28 content lectures, slides, YouTube recordings, MediaServices recordings, and extensive recitation/bootcamp assets. Fall 2025 remains a historical fallback, not the canonical edition.

The lecture series is publishable from the lecture evidence. The official homework chain is **not anonymously reproducible**: HW1–HW4 specifications, starter files, tests, and submission feedback live primarily in CMU Autolab and Piazza; Part 2 tasks also rely on course Kaggle competitions. Articles may explain what the public table says each assignment tests, but must not present a full assignment walkthrough or claim that the graded environment can be reconstructed.
