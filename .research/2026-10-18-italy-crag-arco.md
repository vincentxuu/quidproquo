## Sources opened
- theCrag Valle del Sarca https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/arco (Exa): 3,191 routes, "Mostly Sport climbing", aka Arco-Sarca; high season early spring–late fall, climbing year round; Massone very polished; Easter crowds; access issues: many crags on private land; Arco <40 km SW of Trento; A22/E70; some crags walkable from town, many need car; first Arco Rock Masters 1985 (theCrag history). Area list incl. Monte Colodri 112 routes, Monte Brento "Mostly Trad" 920m, Placche Zebrate 260m (warning), Monte Casale 560m, Sarche 315 routes, Monte Colt–San Paolo 301 routes.
- Sestogrado / Versante Sud "Arco Falesie ed. 2018" https://sestogrado.it/en/prodotto/arco-falesie-130-proposte-5000-tiri: 136 crags, 5,260 routes, finished March 2018, 23 new crags; busiest Massone, Nago, La Gola; up to 9a+; authors Manica, Cicogna, Negretti.
- Planetmountain Massone https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/arco-massone.html: 4a–9b, 40m, 163 routes, E, 70m rope; three sectors Massone Basso (5a–7a), La Valletta (7a–8a, 35m), Il Pueblo (7c+); polished, crowded; climbing prohibited at Pueblo from Hochzeitspfeiler rightwards; land privately owned; access SS45bis.
- Planetmountain five must-see crags https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/five-must-see-crags-at-arco-the-birthplace-of-sport-climbing-as-we-know-it.html: Belvedere (Nago, 50+ routes, to 7c, S), Calvario (E, 1987 Rock Master Lynn Hill & Glowacz), Massone = Policromuro officially, 150 routes 4a–9b, limited parking walk 10/20 min, E facing, Ora wind summer afternoons; Nago/Segrom 120 routes 4b–9a, S, year-round; Spiaggia delle Lucertole S, Ora, classics Luisa violenta / Non seguitemi / La signora degli appigli; don't leave anything in car.
- Planetmountain Mescalito https://planetmountain.com/en/routes/mescalito-monte-colodri.html: 25 Nov 1982 Bernard & Vettori, 260 m 9 pitches to 6c/6c+, sun until 13:00, spring/autumn, park at Climbing Stadium, descend Colodri via ferrata, 12 draws.
- Garda Trentino crags https://www.gardatrentino.it/en/outdoor/climbing/crags ; safety https://gardatrentino.it/en/outdoor/climbing/climbing-safety-tips (Garda Trentino Outdoor Park; yearly check of equipment by professionals).
- Garda Trentino Abissi https://gardatrentino.it/en/info/crag-abissi_8880: ~40 routes 6b+, overhang shelters in rain, limited parking, walk from Massone 10 min / Arco 20 min, no roadside parking.
- Garda Trentino Massi di Prabi https://gardatrentino.it/en/info/crag-massi-di-prabi_8680: boulders + rope, 46 routes 3c–7a (search snippet: exposure north), path opposite town swimming pool parking.
- Garda Trentino Family San Martino https://gardatrentino.it/en/info/family-crag-san-martino_8678: kids crag 3a–6b, few parking, leave car in San Martino walk 20 min.
