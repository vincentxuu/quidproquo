## Sources opened
- theCrag Cala Gonone https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/sardinia/cala-gonone — 1,001 routes mostly sport, 7,501 ascents; areas: Cala Fuili 251, Cala Luna 179, Margheddie 113, La Poltrona 76, Monte Irveri 67, Biddiriscottai 60, Buchi Arta 53, Millennium 35, Millennium multipitch 29 (140 m), Grotta dei Colombi 4 (160 m), Monte Bonacoa 36, I Tornanti 36, ...; guidebooks: Rock Climbing in Cala Gonone (Corrado Conca, 2025, ISBN 9788888776675, >700 routes in 9 areas), Arrampicare a Cala Gonone (C. Conca, 2017).
- Climbing Sardinia / climbingitaly.com https://climbingitaly.com/area/cala-gonone — over 50 sectors; Biddiriscottai titanium rebolt 2021 (46 titanium routes + 5 multi); Puschtra (S end Cala Fuili) titanium 2024; summer easy: Sardi di Cuore (shade from 2 pm, long walk), Fuili Spigolo (shade 2 pm, crowded, slippery), Fuili Ferry (shade 5 pm); Buchi Arta shade until 1 pm, 7a max; winter: La Poltrona, Budinetto slabs; Biddiriscottai 10 multi 6a–6a+; Millennium extreme multi 7b+–8c+; Grotta dei Colombi abseil in, L'alchimista 6c titanium; Fuili canyon Pederiva tufas, Thailandia, Raoni, Zedda Piras; Millennium 1 mile walk + via ferrata; S'atta Ruja (Dorgali side of tunnel) >90 routes 6a–7b; La Poltrona Alta vertical slab; sector cards: Cala Luna approach "Gommone".
- Climb Europe Cala Gonone https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-sardinia-cala-gonone — hard limestone, mostly single pitch; Millennium F6a–F8c+ compared to Grande Grotta Kalymnos; Grotta dei Colombi bolted multi up to 210 m 6b–6c; best autumn–spring, too hot summer; 60 m + 16 qd; multi 70 m + slings, some double ropes; Supramonte big walls to 400 m west of Dorgali; 24-sector table.
- calagonone.eu climbing page https://www.calagonone.eu/localita/territorio/vie-arrampicata — best spring & autumn, all months possible (sea-moderated); 2021 bolt-state update: galvanic corrosion broke anchors near sea; Biddiriscottai >50% titanium; Millennium partly rebolted by Oviglia (marine inox + titanium anchors); Cala Fuili beach Piguet titanium; Codula Fuili ASD Ortoblock Nuoro; "Arrampicare nella spiaggia di Cala Luna e nel settore Puschtra ... molto, molto pericoloso!" (2021; Puschtra since rebolted 2024 per Climbing Sardinia).
- calagonone.eu transport https://www.calagonone.eu/en/place/services-and-utilities/travels — ARST phone numbers; arriving from north at Dorgali, ignore navigator shortest path, follow signs through tunnel on Monte Bardia.
- Climbing Sardinia titanium etiquette (2025-01-18) https://climbingsardinia.com/en/how-to-climb-on-titanium-bolted-crags — add own carabiner at top for second; don't leave quickdraws (galvanic corrosion); leave a carabiner or lower from a bolt.
- theCrag Millennium https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/sardinia/cala-gonone/area/720129579 — parking 40.290570, 9.640568; 1.8 km gravel path; fixed ropes, single PAS/sling, one section worth clipping; ~45 min car→cave; Millennium Bug 7b+ 110 m 5 pitches, 12 qd, 2×50 m ropes, FA 2010 (theCrag); corroded-bolts warning on a route.
- Planetmountain Millennium Bug https://planetmountain.com/en/routes/millennium-bug-millennium-cave.html — 7b+ 110 m, oblig 7a, FA Giupponi & Oviglia 30/06/2011, FFA 19/10/2011; follow signs "Carto/Osalla", cement road → dirt track; stainless bolts.
- ARST line 520 PDF http://arstspa.info/520.pdf — Cala Gonone – Dorgali – Oliena – Nuoro; e.g. Nuoro Centro Intermodale 06:55 → Cala Gonone Via Cala Luna-Poste 08:04; Dorgali→Cala Gonone ~20 min; runs vary by day type (FER/FEST/SCO/GIO...).
- La Favorita hotel directions https://lafavoritahotel.com/en/where-we-are — Olbia: SS131DCN ~70 km exit Dorgali, SP38 → SS125 → SP26; Olbia airport 120 km ~1 h 30.
- SardegnaTurismo Cala Gonone https://sardegnaturismo.it/en/places/est/cala-gonone — short tunnel outside Dorgali; Cala Luna by boat or strenuous hike.
- SardegnaTurismo Ziu Martine https://sardegnaturismo.it/en/explore/ziu-martine — >3 km from Cala Gonone on Viale del Bue Marino, rock face for climbers; Parco Nazionale del Golfo di Orosei e Gennargentu.

## Disagreements
- Route counts: theCrag 1,001; Conca 2025 guide >700 (selective, 9 areas); Climbing Sardinia "over 50 sectors".
- Millennium Bug FA: theCrag 2010 vs Planetmountain 2011.
- Season: Climb Europe autumn–spring; calagonone.eu spring & autumn best, all year possible.
