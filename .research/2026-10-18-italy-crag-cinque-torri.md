Scoiattoli; helmet for stonefall; Scoiattoli founded 1939, sport climbing from 1970s.
- planetmountain.com/en/crags/cinque-torri.html: 3a–8b, height 160 m, 130 routes, "18 are classics and 19 are modern" multipitch mainly Torre Grande; Period "Summer"; SS48, turn left at km 112 to Cianzopè–Rif. Cinque Torri, 10–20 min walk; road closed every Sunday in August 9:30–15:30 with shuttle; Bai de Dones chairlift; bus from Cortina to Cianzopè and Bai de Dones; path 439; classics need full alpine rack; descents almost all abseil; in Dolomiti d'Ampezzo park; WWI open-air museum. - ledolomiti.it/en/trails/falesia-cinque-torri-arrampicata: Rif. Scoiattoli 2,255 m; chairlift from Bai de Dones; season "June to September"; afternoon thunderstorms; avoid wet rock.
- guidedolomiti.com/en/climbing/cinque-torri-crag (Enrico Maioni, Scoiattoli/guide): >140 single pitch + dozens multipitch; pitches 10–40 m, 80 m rope helpful; III UIAA to 8b; 2200/2360 m; bad weather only some single pitches S face Torre Grande Cima Sud; best May–November, south face Torre Grande even winter; bolted ring belays since 1940s; Torre Grande 170 m; Cianzopè junction km 112.2, chairlift junction Bai de Dones km 110; private road closed August to 2nd Sunday September; road snow-free end May–Oct; guidebook "Falesie a Cortina d'Ampezzo" (Idea Montagna, English version); favourite classics: Torre Inglese, Torre del Barancio, Miriam, Olga, Diretta Dimai, Raponzole.
