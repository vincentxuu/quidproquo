# Research notes: Finale Ligure (checked 2026-10-18)

## Sources opened
- finaleoutdoor.com/en/stories/climbing-in-the-finale-area-all-you-need-to-know (Finale Outdoor Region consortium, published 2022-01-29): "almost 4000 routes"; climb winter (grip) and summer on shady crags, best spring/autumn; guidebooks Finale 51 (Andrea Gallo, Idee Verticali) and Finale Climbing (Marco Tomassini, Versante Sud); LIVE INFO page; map Fraternali "Map 20"; year-round bans: Bric Pianarella settore meridionale, Rocce del Borgo, Rocca di Corno Parete Ovest, Falesia del Vacchè Versante Est, Scogliera di Capo Noli; seasonal 01/01–31/07: Monte Tolla, Bastionata Centrale e Destra di Boragni, Rocca degli Uccelli, Bric Reseghe; parking banned almost everywhere on narrow roads (Perti, Monte Sordo, Rocca di Corno, Boragni, Bric Reseghe); Perti–Pian Marino last 3–5 km length/weight limits (no campers/vans); parking bans from Piazza Martiri Perticesi onward and Calvisio Vecchia road (Via Bedina); no camping / overnight in vehicles outside authorised areas in Finale Ligure municipality.
- finaleoutdoor.com/en/stories/finale-outdoor-region-a-paradise-for-climbers-and-for-birds-of-prey (8 Jan 2025): bans since 1994, Province of Savona, agreement climbers/WWF/LIPU; >200 sectors, >4,000 routes; seasonal ban 1 Jan–31 Jul; list: Caprazzoppa total (TV repeater to sea cliff under Torre Colombara, E–S side); Pianarella total after "Via Lunga" (open) until San Bernardino; Casa del Vacchè total beyond "Peter Pan" to Cornei bridge; Rocca di Corno total beyond "Il Futuro è un'Ipotesi"; Bastionata di Boragni seasonal right and central sectors.
- finaleoutdoor.com/en/live/climbing: per-crag open/closed status page.
- finaleoutdoor.com/en/climbing: limestone walls; climbing year-round; some crags climbable in bad weather.
- finaleoutdoor FOR YOU CARD page: card funds trail/crag maintenance via partner businesses; valid calendar year.
- finaleoutdoor Rocca di Perti – Grotto di Perti: 6c–8c+, north, possible when raining yes, suited hottest months.
- finaleoutdoor Monte Cucco – Canyon: 5b–8a, E/W, "one of the few that can be visited in the summer", ~40 routes.
- finaleoutdoor Valle Aquila – Bric Pianarella Paretone: multipitch avg 250 m; Via Lunga 320 m, 13 pitches, hardest P12 6a; directions: Feglino exit, right toward Finale ~2 km, park near abandoned chapel, red triangles.
- thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/finale-ligure (Exa): 4,353 routes, mostly sport, limestone; "Possible to climb year-round, just choose the suitable aspect"; "Popular routes are more or less polished"; "High season aka parking spots are full: February-March to October"; reachable by highway or train; crags up to 45 min drive; approaches 5 min to 30–60 min; camping only on commercial sites; areas list (Rocca di Perti 525, Valle Aquila 667, Monte Cucco 315...); classics list (Dancing Dalle 7a, Bombolo 6b, Golden Lady 6c, Hyaena 8b...). Guidebook blurb "around 4,000 routes in 213 sectors".
- ukclimbing.com/logbook/books/finale_climbing_2022-2710 (Exa): Finale Climbing, Marco Tomassini, Versante Sud 2022, 3rd edition adds 21 crags, aerial photos, QR GPS parking, GPS tracks.
- versantesud.it/en/shop/catalogue/finale-climbing_1446: 2022, 792 pages, Italian (also English/German editions, iClimbing app).
- climbing-guide.eu/finale-51-10166 (search snippet only — not cited) ; oliunid Finale 51 snippet "200 sectors with about 4000 routes" (snippet only — not cited).
- italia.it/en/liguria/things-to-do/free-climbing-finale-ligure (ENIT): "3,500 climbing routes and about 200 specially-equipped walls"; first routes 1968 by Gianluigi and Eugenio Vaccari.
- planetmountain.com/en/crags/dancing-dalle-finale.html: sea cliff past Varigotti after Malpasso tunnel; 10 routes 5c–8a; Dancing Dalle 7a, Alessandro Grillo 1982, RP Patrick Berhault, first 7a sport route at Finale.
- visitfinaleligure.it/en/how-to-get-there (IAT): railway station Genoa–Ventimiglia, Piazza Vittorio Veneto; TPL line 40 Andora–Savona, line 40/ Finalborgo–Savona; A10 exit Finale Ligure; Genoa airport ~50 km.
