## Grotta dell'Arenauta / Spiaggia dell'Arenauta
- UKC logbook (last updated 16/Oct/2026): 30 climbs, limestone, 24 m asl, faces SW; 6b–8c mostly above 7a; ~30 m of fully horizontal; behind sand dune, protected from bad weather; beach not private, camping illegal; owner of stairs charges €2; off-season free, toilets & shower; busy in winter; ~3 km north of Gaeta; 300 steps; cave ~200 m south of shop. https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/grotta_dellarenauta-9762
- theCrag Spiaggia dell'Arenauta: 91 routes; Approdo dei proci 39 routes, Grotta 52. Access issues: approach via private property, open all year, summer €3; owner claims cave on his property, may limit winter access by closing gate off-season; alternative "300 gradini" access ~100 m toward Sperlonga (closed in winter). Approach: parking bay with "Ultima spiaggia" sign. https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/spiaggia-dellareonauta
- theCrag Approdo dei proci: warning "La falesia è chiusa — L'intera falesia approdo dei proci è interdetta con ordinanza del 2014." https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/spiaggia-dellareonauta/approdo-dei-proci
- theCrag Grotta: 52 routes; "Always shady, Dry in the rain, Overhung, Roof"; parking rest areas (3) ~km 24,400 Via Flacca, first with sign "ultima spiaggia selvaggia"; approach 10–20 min. Routes incl. L'Arenauta 9b (set Giuliano Tarquini, FFA Ghisolfi 8 Feb 2022), Lux Est Lux I 7a, Il guastafeste 6a, Cavalieri selvaggi I 6a+, Invidia I 6b, La Fuga dei Cavalieri 7c+ 32 m. https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/spiaggia-dellareonauta/grotta-dellareonauta
- 27crags Spiaggia dell'Arenauta: 99 sport; "Access is sensitive"; access may be closed sometimes because private; 2009 agreement; signs prohibiting climbing in certain areas since 2008; Approdo dei Proci ⛔; high season €3 fee at bottom of stairs; beach: right = Approdo, left = Grotta. https://27crags.com/crags/grotta-dell-arenauta/access
- CSF Grotta: 40+ routes; 8–10 parking places; approach 10'; big cave half-hidden by tall sand dune; concretions, stalagmites; lower part not very overhanging, upper part flat roof; climbable when it rains → crowded on rainy weekends/holidays; dripping after long heavy rains; humidity affects except strong sea wind; old routes on the beach wall impracticable (rusty bolts). From Ristoro da Guido continue 3.7 km toward Gaeta to white sign "Ultima spiaggia", park in two widenings. "don't leave anything into the car". https://climbingspotfactory.com/index.php/en/climbadvisor/gorotta_dell_arenauta/info_about
- Climbook Grotta: 56 routes, 3761 repetitions; most repeated Super Daytona L1 6a, Il guastafeste 6a, Cavalieri selvaggi L1 6a+, Invidia L1 6b, L'ombra dello scorpione L1 6a+, Red sky 6b+, Lux est lux L1 7a. https://climbook.com/falesie/51/grotta-dellarenauta
- Planetmountain 23 Sep 2010: on 27 July (2010) Gaeta council fenced 700 m of Arenauta beach after landslide-risk complaint; Grotta & Approdo dei Proci closed; 4 Sept mayor partially revoked for beach but not crags; Gaetaventura preparing safety project. https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/grotta-dellarenauta-high-hopes-for-the-crag-to-be-reopened.html
- Planetmountain 8 Feb 2022: Ghisolfi L'Arenauta 9b, 20 m horizontal roof, first ~8b+ to rest. https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/stefano-ghisolfi-frees-l-arenauta-9b-at-sperlonga-italy.html
- Oliunid: Grotta "within the municipality of Gaeta"; Rogora 2016 Grandi Gesti proposed 9a, Ondra 2017 suggested 8c+.
- italia.it: Arenauta beach 300 steps or winding path.
- MP forum Mar 2024: train Rome→Formia-Gaeta, bus or cab to downtown Gaeta (cab €30); no official taxi in Gaeta, hotel/Airbnb give WhatsApp driver; ~7 min drive to crag, flat €20 each way; no cell service in cave, signal just outside. https://mountainproject.com/forum/topic/125555168/gaeta-and-sperlonga-without-a-car

## Montagna Spaccata
- falesia.it (updated 29/12/2025): 4 sectors/47 routes, 4a/7c, 20 m alt; long routes, not all modern bolts; once started you must top out unless you have a boat; Beatrice sector 2003–05; routes re-equipped by Scuola Franco Alletto CAI Roma; "E' consentita la scalata solo lungo le vie ... alla sinistra della Grotta del Turco e limitatamente al periodo dal 1° agosto al 20 febbraio. Non è possibile inoltre abbandonare in alcun momento i sentieri."; 2009 agreement; access by boat (from Gaeta port or Serapo beach) or abseil Via dei Camini (12 m then two 50 m rappels); good resin bolts; limestone "lavorato e tagliente"; diedri, placche, strapiombi, camini. https://www.falesia.it/crag/12/gaeta-montagna-spaccata.html
- falesia.it forum: 2016 user says ban unclear (poster says one sector south all year); another says climbing normal for 2+ years, CAI courses resumed. https://www.falesia.it/Forums/divieto-di-arrampicata-a-gaeta-vt3027.html
- theCrag Montagna Spaccata: 46 routes. https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/central-and-southern-italy/rome-area/area/2521355394
- Planetmountain Via dello Spigolo: FA Camilleri, Gradi, Morandi 1960; 125 m; 5c S2; second route after Via dei Camini; park ~100 m before Santuario at tree-lined clearing overlooking Serapo; don't leave valuables; enter sanctuary, tunnel, mule track to 7th hairpin, path to cliff edge, concrete marker, rappel stations of Via dei Camini (2 now); descent: single 70 m rope 4 rappels or 60 m half ropes; pitches 5c/5b/5b+/5a+/5b-c. https://www.planetmountain.com/it/itinerari/via-dello-spigolo-montagna-spaccata-gaeta.html
- Planetmountain 19 May 2025: titanium rebolting of Croce del Sud, Via dello Spigolo, Via Beatrice, all Via dei Camini rappels; guides Lorenzo Trento & Mattia Beltrame; many routes still in bad condition; ~1 in 10 old bolts popped with one hammer blow; a bolt on Spigolo L1 snapped; ~€5,000 crowdfunded; CAI Roma handles relations with Park & Comune. Via dei Camini opened 1950s by Gigi Mario. https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/richiodatura-montagna-spaccata-gaeta.html
- italia.it: up to 120 m above sea level; abseil Via dei Camini or small boats, watch currents.
- Parchi Lazio: Monte Orlando urban regional park; peregrine falcon nesting on cliffs; park area home to Santissima Trinità sanctuary, Grotta del Turco, Montagna Spaccata. https://parchilazio.it/rivieradiulisse-schede-32058-the_riviera_di_ulisse_regional_park
- Versante Sud "Gaeta Moneta Sperlonga Leano Circeo" (new edition, author Fabrizio Antonioli): 18 km coast, 249 multi-pitch routes (prev ed 192); Gaeta numerically smaller but more lines & restoration. https://versantesud.it/en/shop/catalogue/iclimbing_5289
- theCrag guidebook listing: older edition Antonioli, Innocenti & Filocamo, 2008, ISBN 9788887890570.
