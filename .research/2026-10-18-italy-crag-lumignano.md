# Research notes: Lumignano (2026-10-18)

Tool chain same as Arco notes (Groundlane unavailable → Keenable + jina reader + Exa).

## Sources opened
- Planetmountain Lumignano Classica https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/lumignano.html: 5a–8b+, 80m, 104 routes, limestone, SW (orientation from page snippet), 60m rope; first route 1924 Sbrega (Casara); access SS247 (Este), turn for Costozza/Lumignano after Longare, park near large boulder or signed car park 300m lower; season spring, autumn, winter, best winter, summer too hot; technical, bouldery, undergraded; route list with RP history (Margherita 7a+ Mariacher 1980; Arco d'oro 7c Scheel 1985; Atomic café 8a Mariacher 1986; El Somaro 8a Mariacher 1986; Mare allucinante 8b+ Dal Prà 1987; Papillon 7b Mariacher 1986; Boomerang 8a+).
- Planetmountain Brojon https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/lumignano-brojon.html: 5a–8b, 61 routes, 70 m rope; four sectors; Brojon classico easy, SW; Strapiombi short powerful; spring/autumn + sunny winter days; SE/SW; park before church, path to Eremo.
- theCrag Lumignano https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/verona/area/2013113019 (Exa): 682 routes, 13 sectors; parking ten cars (water, trash), 15 min walk to Classico.
- theCrag Classica sector https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/verona/area/2013121002 (Exa): 184 routes, "Climbing in this area is closed." "In October 2024 theCrag was contacted by the landowners with the request to close the area for climbing."
- CAI Vicenza https://organizzazione.cai.it/sez-vicenza/falesia-di-lumignano-itinerari-interdetti/: ordinanza with Comune di Longare, rockfall danger; 21 routes south side + 32 routes west side of Dente di Lumignano (left of Classica); first bolt hangers removed; list per sector (Minetti, Anfiteatro, Sopra la Chiesa Riga rossa, La Spiaggia, Caucciù, Mostro Fornaio, Placca Campi).
- GognaBlog 2014 (reprint of Vicenza Report 10 Jul 2014) https://gognablog.sherpa-gate.com/nuovo-regolamento-per-larrampicata-sui-berici/: approved Province regulation; green all year Classica→Vomere + Brojon Classico + Brojon Strapiombi Pilastro; orange 1 Jul–31 Dec Lumignano Nuova + Brojon Strapiombi Piardi; light blue 1 Apr–30 Sep Il commercialista → Sotto l'Eremo; red other; no new routes without authorisation + VIncA; protected birds list.
- Planetmountain draft rules (2014) https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/lumignano-and-the-climbing-in-the-berici-hills-at-risk.html: draft differed (Brojon Classico/Strapiombi orange); fines 25–500 €; 2004 Longare regulation.
- avevolevertigini 2024 (CAI article by Angriman) https://avevolevertigini.com/2024/10/20/lumignano-classica-questioni-in-sospeso/: CAI Vicenza contract with landowner since Oct 1992; 2002 Longare regulation; 2014–16 SIC; regulation still in force; 2021 maintenance by guides.
- avevolevertigini 2023 https://avevolevertigini.com/2023/03/14/lumignano-classica-la-storia-dellarrampicata-il-futuro-dellecosostenibilita/: three climbers bought Classica after an owner planned to bar central section; beginner sectors Maruska, Scacciapuffi (V), Fessura Rossi; Pancia Casarotto 6b "add half grade"; grades severe.
- Mountain Project Lumignano https://www.mountainproject.com/area/106350549/lumignano (2009): tufas, caves; park at end of Via Borgo, trail from Via Rialto.
- falesia.it Lumignano Nuova https://www.falesia.it/crag/968/lumignano-nuova.html: mostly grade 6; parking via Borgo dirt road.
- Oliunìd / Idea Montagna guide https://oliunid.com/idea-montagna-lumignano-climbing-guidebook: Michele Guerrini, 2023 edition, 288 pages, spring/autumn/winter, gives seasonal access rules per sector.
- SVT news https://www.svt.vi.it/it/news/lumignano-2023-06-12 : line 8 terminus Piazza Mazzaretto Lumignano; https://www.svt.vi.it/it/news/lumignano-2023-07-28 : lines 8 and E04 between autostazione SVT/Viale Roma and Ponte di Lumignano; https://www.svt.vi.it/it/news/lumignano-2025-10-09 : festival detours.
