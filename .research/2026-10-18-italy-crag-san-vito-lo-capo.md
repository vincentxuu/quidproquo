## Sources opened (full page)
- theCrag San Vito lo Capo https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/trapani — 1,306 routes "Mostly Sport climbing"; 17,089 ascents; "more than 1200 routes"; nearly all rebolted; season "October to May, in the summer it gets too hot"; humid/rainy/strong wind days; limestone overhangs/slabs/caves/tufas; min 60 m rope + 15 qd, better 70 m + 18; majority single pitch; Monte Monaco for trad/long (>100 m); access: cross cattle fences, don't damage; airports Palermo & Trapani; rent car best, buses exist; walk to most areas from El Bahira; bikes rentable at El Bahira; "As of 2020 it's forbidden to bolt new routes as re-bolting is still the main focus"; bolting started ~2005 (Scott Titt, Josef Gstöttenmayr, Daniele Arena, Jim Titt); Sicily-Rock main guide, sold at El Bahira; La Roccia di Sole covers whole Sicily; sub-areas: Rocca Firriato 38 (sport + DWS), Monte Monaco 142, Scogliera di Salinella 868, Valanga 23, Castelluzzo/Macari, Custonaci/Scurati. Guidebook sidebar: Sicily Rock 2024 (Oelze & Röker, ISBN 9783938680551, 1,280 routes); Rockfax Sicily 2021 (Daimon Beail, nearly 1,500 routes incl. Palermo).
- Climb Europe San Vito https://climb-europe.com/pages/san-vito-rock-climbing — over 1,300 routes; Salinella nearly 4 km, over 900 single-pitch on limestone+marble, F2a–F9a, over 700 at ≤6c+; Monte/Pizzo Monaco multi-pitch 150–400 m mostly ≥6b, trad gear + double 60 m; Via Fratelli Titt 645 m 5c 18 pitches, first 8 bolted; Castelluzzo (Macari)/Custonaci (Scurati) harder ≥7a tufas; best Oct–mid-May, most crags face S & W; Monte Monaco east-facing for hot days; routes 20–30 m, some 35 m with intermediate lower-offs; 60 m + 14–16 qd; campsite above Salinella, first routes 1 min walk. Sicily Rock 2024 = 1,304 routes (shop text).
- Mountain Project Trapani/San Vito https://www.mountainproject.com/area/107611524/trapani-san-vito-area — "something like 5km of ocean-facing cliff"; El Bahira between cliffs and ocean; some climbs on campground land, flood lights; recommends car: no groceries at campsite, few miles walk to town.
