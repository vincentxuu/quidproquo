## 名稱與位置
- Climbing Spot Factory (CSF) Sperlonga info: "Sperlonga" climbers mean coastal strip between Sperlonga and Gaeta, in municipalities of Itri and Gaeta; southern limestone walls of Monte Vannelamare (257 m), closing to the west the Sant'Agostino beach.
  https://climbingspotfactory.com/index.php/en/climbadvisor/sperlonga/info_about
- italia.it: Parete del Chiromante on Monte Vannellamare, easy access; Castello Invisibile ~20 routes, shade, good in summer, beginners; Moneta "perfect climb for winter... sunny facade with no breezes"; Palestra del Pueblo climbable on rainy days. Ristoro da Guido historic meeting point, free parking incl. campers, showers.
  https://www.italia.it/en/lazio/things-to-do/free-climbing-lazio

## Tetti Rossi / Moneta
- Alpinisti d'Appennino (2025-02-21): Tetti Rossi on Monte Moneta behind Sant'Agostino; abandoned 20 years, being rebolted; 5a–8b+; overhang/roofs; S; approach 40 min; 10 mm bolts; alt 180 m; period Dec–Mar; climbable in rain (main sector under big roof); salt + scirocco humid days bad; 14–16 quickdraws, 80 m rope; park near Ristoro da Guido "aperto tutto l'anno". https://www.alpinistidappennino.it/2025/02/21/falesia-tetti-rossi-moneta/
- Climbook Moneta (Tetti Rossi, Paretone): 52 routes; "DIVIETO di arrampicata dal 25 febbraio al 15 aprile (Solo parte sinistra fino ad Avatar)"; info Dec 2018; popular: Nonno in carriola 6a+, Il tormento e l'estasi 6c+, Avatar L1 7a, Bacini ai pupi 6b+, La forza di mozzarella 7a. https://climbook.com/falesie/145/moneta-tetti-rossi-paretone

## 歷史與代表路線
- CSF: Kajagogoo 7a 1983, Blues per Allah 7a+ 1984, Reggae per Maometto 7b+ 1984, Polvere di Stelle 7b+ 1984, Elixir 7b+ 1985, Il Ricordo del Tempo 8a 1986 (Parete del Chiromante & Fascia Superiore). Bruno Vitale main bolter/maintainer; "green box" at Ristoro Da Guido raises funds for re-bolting. Bolts evolved to stainless/resin.
- Oliunid (2023, updated 2026): Il Ricordo del Tempo first free Lamberti 1986 8a; D'Addazio 2018 repeat suggests 8b. Spigolo di Ferrante 5c, Flippaut 6a+, Serena Alienazione 6b, Kajagoogoo 7a at Chiromante; Polvere di Stelle 7b+ Fascia Superiore. Guidebooks: ArrampicaRoma Sud; Versante Sud Gaeta Circeo Leano Sperlonga Moneta; Blue Rock 2 Sperlonga. https://oliunid.com/blog/climbing-historic-limestone-sperlonga

## 交通
- CSF driving: A1 exit Ferentino → SR156 Monti Lepini → SA255 Priverno–Terracina → SS7 variant → SS213 Flacca; from Rome south SS148 Pontina; from Naples A1 Cassino → SS630 Formia → Gaeta → Flacca, ~6 km after Gaeta Piana di S. Agostino; Ristoro da Guido km 20,900 SS Flacca.
- Sperlonga Turismo: get off at "Fondi-Sperlonga" station; Autoservizi Piazzoli bus from station square to Sperlonga; ticket on board or at station bar. https://sperlongaturismo.it/en
- daytours.rome.it (tourism site, secondary): Fondi-Sperlonga station is in Fondi inland; Piazzoli bus ~25 min; Cotral serves Sperlonga along Via Flacca and to Terracina station. https://daytours.rome.it/sperlonga-beaches
- scoprigaeta.it (Cotral open-data timetable, viewed 2026-10-18): 0 direct runs Sperlonga→Gaeta that day; direct runs are few, not every day; change at Formia. https://scoprigaeta.it/orari-autobus-gaeta/
- MP forum (2024): renting car easiest. https://mountainproject.com/forum/topic/125555168/gaeta-and-sperlonga-without-a-car
