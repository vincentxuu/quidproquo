# Research notes: Val di Mello (2026-10-18)

Tool chain same as Orco note (Groundlane MCP not connected, direct 401 -> Keenable search + r.jina.ai / Exa fetch).

## Sources opened
- Riserva Naturale Val di Mello (official) https://riservanaturalevaldimello.org/ — DCR 27 Jan 2009 n. VII/802; ~4,562 ha (516 integrale, 3,862 orientata, 184 paesaggistica); car: max 40 vehicles/day, 12 € pass from parcometro in San Martino parking in front of InfoPoint; shuttle no longer active; walk from San Martino ~25 min, ~100 m; train Milano-Lecco-Sondrio-Tirano to Morbegno, STPS bus Morbegno–Val Masino; rules: respect nature, no litter, dogs on leash, no hunting, no protected flora, no fires, no bicycles, no camping (whole municipality).
- Riserva "Vivere la riserva" https://riservanaturalevaldimello.org/vivere-la-riserva/ — "Tempio del sassismo"; info office at San Martino parking in summer.
- valmasino.info how to reach https://valmasino.info/en/come-arrivare — STPS line A20 Morbegno-Ardenno-Val Masino.
- Rifugio Mello https://rifugiomello.it/en/dove-siamo — Ardenno 7 km after Morbegno, ~16 km to San Martino.
- in-Lombardia https://www.in-lombardia.it/en/tourism-in-lombardy/tourism-sondrio/val-di-mello — compared to Yosemite; park San Martino, pass at Info Point; train to Morbegno + bus.
- UKC destination guide https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/page.php?id=9471 — three zones (Val Masino, Valle dei Bagni, Val di Mello); south side riserva integrale, rock chossy/damp; "Access by car is closed during the summer ... paying the rest of the year" (older rule); Bagni shaded, wetter; Val di Mello bouldering 30–60 min walk-in; Sasso Remenno well-bolted sport mostly up to 6b+; multi-pitch: sport routes need trad gear, trad routes in-situ belays + pegs; run-out slabs; large cams (BD4+) for Kundalini/Luna Nascente; rock season May–September, sub-zero nights into May; bouldering best autumn/spring; train to Morbegno + bus; Camping Sasso Remenno; trad must-dos: Il Lamone e sue Placche 6a+ 7p, Kundalini HVS/E1 10p, Luna Nascente E1/E2 9p, Oceano Irrazionale E3/E4 14p, Transqualidiana E4/E5 21p.
- UKC logbook Val di Mello https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/val_di_mello-18140 — 300 climbs, granite, 1041 m, S; predominant style slabs, well bolted to fully trad, long run-outs; €10 day pass for Val di Mello parking, sells out; San Martino €7/day some free; approaches 20 min–1.5 h.
- theCrag Val di Mello https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/val-di-mello — 363 routes (mostly bouldering, incomplete index); Mello Boulder guide "around 2,800 problems in 33 different areas".
- theCrag Oceano Irrazionale https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/val-di-mello/route/378945990 — UIAA 7, 400 m, 12 pitches; FA 2 Jun 1977 Guerini & Villa.
- 27crags Val di Mello https://27crags.com/areas/val-di-mello — 800–1100 m; birthplace of bouldering in Italy; compact alpine granite; slabs, edges, compression; 2416 routes on 27crags; best season spring and autumn.
- Planetmountain Rock climbing in Val di Mello https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/rock-climbing-in-val-di-mello.html — "temple of friction climbing"; ~300 routes; gear two 60 m ropes, 10 qd, full trad rack, hammer for classics; campsites Sasso Remenno and Ground Jack.
- Planetmountain Oceano Irrazionale https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/oceano-irrazionale-precipizio-degli-asteroidi.html — FA 2/07/1977; S; 400 m; VII; April–October; first VII in Central Alps; nuts + twin friends to #4.
- Planetmountain Luna Nascente https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/luna-nascente-scoglio-delle-metamorfosi-val-di-mello.html — FA Boscacci, Ghezzi, Milani 3/09/1978; S; 360 m; VII (VI obl.); April–October.
- SummitPost Il Risveglio di Kundalini https://www.summitpost.org/il-risveglio-di-kundalini/817568 — 6a+ (5c obl.), 10 pitches, FA Guerini & Villa 1976; south, hot in summer; grassy ledges stay wet after rain; often overcrowded; not bolted, old pegs.
- Planetmountain Melloblocco 2026 https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/events/melloblocco-2026-everything-you-need-to-know.html — 6–9 May 2026; A20 extra runs; campsite at San Martino sports field.
- Planetmountain "gift that keeps on giving" https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/events/melloblocco-the-gift-that-keeps-on-giving.html — >2,000 participants 2026.
- Versante Sud Val di Mello https://versantesud.it/en/shop/catalogue/val-di-mello_1506 — 2023, Niccolò Bartoli, ISBN 9788855471046, "cradle of Italian free climbing".
- Versante Sud Mello Boulder https://versantesud.it/en/shop/catalogue/mello-boulder_1544 — 2024, Andrea Pavan, 656 pp.
- Montagna.TV 2026-05-30 https://www.montagna.tv/274231/val-di-mello-luca-schiera-sono-stato-io-a-schiodare-le-vie-vi-spiego-perche/ — Schiera removed added bolts/belays on Il Gioco dello Scivolo and Uomini e Topi (Placche dell'Oasi); "migliaia di spit ... la grande maggioranza delle pareti è attrezzata" but historic routes kept original.
