## Sources opened
- theCrag Valle Orco https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/area/389911830 — gneiss; "famous for its gneiss crack climbing, which is said to be the best in the Western European Alps"; Sergent/Caporal/Torre di Aimonin long multi-pitch trad, minimal walk-ins; majority of belays bolted, easy retreat; sport around Ceresole Reale, Locana, Sparone; climbing from Sparone (~550m) up to Colle del Nivolet (~2641); Sergent/Caporal ~1600m; after Noasca, 4 switchbacks, long tunnel, turn sharp left at exit to carpark ~1 km along old Orco road to a gate. Stay: Camping Casa Bianca, Camping Piccolo Paradiso (Ceresole). Ethic: clean; bolts for anchors/abseils, discouraged where natural pro; bolts removed from some classics bolted in 80s/90s. Climbing since early 1970s. Sub-areas: Pont Canavese e Courgnè 182 (181 sport); Sparone 58 (sport); Noasca e Locana 202 (108 trad, 89 sport); Sergent Area 155 (87 trad, 50 sport, 19 boulder); Ceresole Reale 249 (125 boulder, 77 sport). Sum 846. Guidebooks: Valle dell'Orco 2024 (Dalla Gasperina); Sport Climbing in Orco 2025 (Oviglia & Pinotti, also Val Grande di Lanzo); ORCO 100 selected crack climbs (Seimandi 2020).
- theCrag Nautilus https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/route/390070722 — 6a, 270m, "Pitches: 9" (8 pitch grades listed), FA 13 May 1982 Giorda/Ogliengo/Perucca; descent abseil x2.
- Climb Europe https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-italy-orco-valley — best time late May–early October; bouldering Fb5–Fb8a, slopers and crimps; fly Milan or Turin, hire car; most climbers stay at campsite.
- UKC Sergent https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/sergent-4114/ — 61 climbs, Rocktype Granite, 1659 m, faces SW.
- SummitPost Sergent https://www.summitpost.org/sergent/1002020 — gneiss; Caporal/Sergent named as "minor brothers" of El Capitan; Sergent suitable even at beginning of season; Ceresole 1613 m, 35 km from Pont Canavese; Red Tape "No permits, no fees and no particulary restrictions"; When to climb: spring & fall, also summer when not too hot. Routes: Locatelli 6a (4c,A1) 170 m 1974; Nautilus TD- 6a 270 m 1982; Cannabis 7b or 5b/A2 180 m 1973; Incastromania 6a 25 m.
- Planetmountain Nautilus https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/nautilus-sergent-valle-orco.html — 260m SW; gear set nuts + set cams, double medium/small, slings, 8 qd; bolts added later then removed; approach 30 min from below.
- Planetmountain Il lato oscuro https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/il-lato-oscuro-sergent.html — park next to campsite; "Car thieves operate slightly further up the valley."
- Planetmountain Via Locatelli https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/via-locatelli-sergent.html — easiest crack on Sergent, popular weekends, all belays bolted; park in front of Camping la Peschera, 200 m after tunnel exit.
- Planetmountain Itaca nel Sole Free https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/itaca-nel-sole-free-caporal.html — most repeated on Caporal; FA Motti & Morello 06/1975 with A2; FFA Brenna 2003; 8b; P1 6a+ P2 6c P3 8a P4 8b.
- Planetmountain interview http://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/interviews/free-itaca-nel-sole-valle-dellorco.html — "long been regarded as the birthplace of modern free climbing in Europe".
- Planetmountain I 50 anni del Caporal https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/i-50-anni-del-caporal-in-valle-dell-orco-nuovo-mattino.html — Tempi Moderni first route on Caporal 4 Nov 1972 (Manera, Motti); Nuovo Mattino; spring pilgrimage every year; Kosterlitz showed first nuts, clean style.
- 27crags Atlantide https://27crags.com/crags/atlantide/description — by Lake Ceresole, 1585 m, S-SE, 70 problems + 30 sport mostly 6th grade; best March–June & October; not accessible when lake high (esp. summer).
- PNGP How to reach us https://pngp.it/en/visit-park/how-reach-us — SS460; SP50 Nivolet closed in winter; SFM1 to Rivarolo and Pont; bus Rivarolo-Pont-Locana-Noasca (GTT 5137 PDF link); dogs on leash only.
- inalto Noasca https://inalto.org/en/points-of-interest/noasca — SFM1 Porta Susa -> Rivarolo; GTT line 137 Pont–Ceresole stops Noasca, infrequent; alt line 3131 from Porta Nuova to Pont.
- GTT line 5137 stops https://www.gtt.to.it/cms/percorari/extraurbano?Regol=OR&bacino=E&linea=5137&percorso=513794&verso=Di&view=percorso — Pont (Stazione) … Noasca … Ceresole (ingr. Galleria) … Ceresole Reale. Timetable PDF 404, not read.
- Versante Sud Valle dell'Orco https://www.versantesud.it/prodotto/valle-dellorco/ — 2024, Stefano & Matteo Dalla Gasperina, 520 pp, ISBN 9788855471329; covers low-valley crags to Caporal/Sergent, Valle Soana, Piantonetto.
