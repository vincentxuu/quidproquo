## 資料來源（已打開）
- theCrag Italy https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy — "Mostly Sport climbing 97,000 routes in region"; Grade context: FR; 19 個子區（Liguria 9.6k、Piemonte 14.5k、Lombardia 13.6k、Trentino-Alto Adige 15.6k、Sardinia 7,773、Sicily 4,097 …）。
- 27crags Italy https://27crags.com/countries/italy — 區域清單（Arco、Finale Ligure、Val di Mello、Valle dell'Orco、San Vito Lo Capo、Cala Gonone…）；Muzzerone 283 sport；"Nago (Closed for now)"。
- 27crags Val di Mello https://27crags.com/areas/val-di-mello — 800–1100 m；"birthplace of bouldering in Italy"；season virtually year-round；Bagni di Masino ~1300 m summer；2715 routes in 31 crags。
- Planetmountain crags DB https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags — 每個岩場頁有難度範圍、岩質、方位、交通。Cinque Torri 頁 https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/cinque-torri.html：約 2200 m、3a–8b、130 routes、夏天避暑；Cianzopé–Rifugio road closed every Sunday in August 09:30–15:30。
- UKC logbook Finale Climbing https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/books/finale_climbing-2710 — Marco Tomassini, Versante Sud 2022, 3rd ed.；UKC 列出指南書涵蓋的岩場與路線數、岩質、方位。
- Versante Sud https://versantesud.it/en/ — 出版社；例：Trento and Valsugana Rock "64 crags"；也出 Up Climbing 年刊。
- falesia.it 論壇 — 站長說「sulle Falesie è praticamente il primo d'Italia」(自稱)。
- Rockfax Dolomites 2014 p.60 https://rockfax.com/wp-content/uploads/flipbooks/Dolomites_2014_intro/files/assets/basic-html/page60.html — "UIAA is the standard grading system used throughout the Italian Dolomites" for trad；UIAA 只考慮技術難度不含客觀危險；sport routes 用 sport grade。
- Rockfax Sicily intro PDF https://uploads.rockfax.com/intros/Sicily-Intro.pdf — main season Oct–mid-May, optimum mid-Nov–end Apr。
- theCrag San Vito (trapani) https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/trapani — season Oct–May, too hot in summer; >1200 routes, nearly all rebolted。
- SardegnaTurismo https://www.sardegnaturismo.it/en/climbing-sardinia-best-walls-climb-island — year-round；spring and autumn prime；winter/spring/autumn recommended。
- Finale Outdoor Region https://finaleoutdoor.com/en/stories/climbing-in-the-finale-area-all-you-need-to-know (2022-01-29) — almost 4000 routes；全年可爬、春秋最好；指南 Finale 51 (Idee Verticali)、Finale Climbing (Versante Sud)；LIVE INFO 頁公告封閉；猛禽（eagle owl, peregrine）全年禁攀：Bric Pianarella 南段、Rocce del Borgo、Rocca di Corno 西壁、Vacchè 東面、Capo Noli；1/1–7/31 暫禁：Monte Tolla、Boragni 中右段、Rocca degli Uccelli、Bric Reseghe；Perti、Monte Sordo 等窄路幾乎全面禁停；Finale 市境授權區外禁止露營與車宿。
- Up-Climbing 2023-01-24 https://www.up-climbing.com/falesia/news-falesia/finale-attenti-ai-rapaci/ — 同上封閉清單，來源 Finale Outdoor Region。

## 救援
- italia.it 緊急電話 https://www.italia.it/it/italia/informazioni-per-viaggiare/numeri-emergenza-e-assistenza — 112 歐盟統一；118 醫療緊急，「anche per soccorso in montagna o in grotta」；1530 海上救援。
- CNSAS https://www.cnsas.it/ — 志工協會，CAI 的全國分部；2025 年 13,037 次任務、救助 9,624 人；GeoResQ app。
- trekking.it 2023-07-14 https://www.trekking.it/news/soccorso-alpino-pagamento-ticket/ — 山區救援由 118 協調；DPR 1992 art.11 未住院時不由國家健保負擔；阿爾卑斯弧帶大區（Liguria 除外）收 ticket；Trento、Bolzano 住院也收少額；Veneto 對岩/冰攀等運動即使住院也收；Piemonte 2023 起實施。
