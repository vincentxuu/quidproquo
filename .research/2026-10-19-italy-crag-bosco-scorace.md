## Sources opened

- Gripped 2025-10-04 (Pardy & Kuepfer; print Jun/Jul 2025) https://gripped.com/profiles/bouldering-in-sicily-exploring-the-sandstone-blocs-of-bosco-scorace/: development began 2016 led by Davide Catalano; brambles; "over 400 lines scattered across seven sectors"; potential 1,500–2,000; biannual Scorace Block Fest; between Palermo and Trapani; eucalyptus woods; pockets, slopers, crimps, "belly button" holes; sore tips; V0–V14; fair grading; boulders 2–8 m; good landings; shade; family friendly; best Oct–May, summer possible with shade and breeze; elevation cooler; ~1h15 by car from Palermo, 45 min from San Vito; approach 30 s–15 min; need a car; B&B Polvere di Stelle in Ummari (Catalano & wife) crash pad rental, guidebook, training room; Ummari ~15 min by car; guidebook at Polvere di Stelle or Dolmen Climb Trapani; problems on 27crags updated; ethics: wait for rock to dry, no climbing 2–3 days after heavy rain; minimal chalk, brush, soft brushes; access sensitive—fields and private properties; stay on trails, dogs on leash (sheep dogs/herds); fires and camping prohibited in bouldering field and woods.
- 27crags description https://27crags.com/crags/bosco-scorace-boulder/description: province of Trapani; wooded plateau between Ummari and Buseto Palizzolo; ~600 m asl; eucalyptus, oaks, holm oaks, cork; Davide Catalano & Giorgia Borsani from Varese began cleaning 2016, opened B&B Polvere di Stelle in Ummari; 3 main sectors, two inside protected wooded area, one just outside park inside private properties; relations with owners & managers good; avoid disturbing livestock; (older text) 200+ lines 4a–8a+; conditions Sept–May.
- 27crags/thetopo access https://thetopo.com/crags/bosco-scorace-boulder/access: "No special access issues"; ~15 min drive from Ummari, asphalt + dirt road; two car parks (high and low); B&B rents gear, crash pads.
- 27crags main https://27crags.com/crags/bosco-scorace-boulder: 317 boulders; sectors Bevaio, Rocche Forbici, Astronave, Astrocava, La Methode, etc.; nearby Rocche Casale 81 boulders.

- Planetmountain 2021-06-30 https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/libri-stampa/bosco-scorace-uscita-nuova-guida-boulder-sicilia-occidentale.html: guide "Bosco Scorace, Bouldering in Sicilia Occidentale" by Marco Ramerio (The Crimp / crimpandgoodlife.com), ~70 pages comic/pop style; 180 boulders developed by Davide Catalano & Emanuele Vigani; ~600 m asl between Trapani and San Vito; B&B 20 min by car from area; three big sectors.
- Oliunid interview 2021-03-10 https://www.oliunid.it/blog/alla-scoperta-dei-boulder-del-bosco-di-scorace/: grey-to-orange sandstone, up to 7–8 m, flat landings, ridge; first guide on ClimbAdvisor; then 150+ lines 4–8a+, the 8a+ by Gabry Moroni; ~40% cleaned of upper part; old animal shelters among rocks; B&B 500 m asl, constant wind.
- B&B Polvere di Stelle climbing page https://bebpolveredistelle.it/climbing: climb Sept–June in Sicily; services: rope/quickdraw/belay rental, crash pad rental, guided trips to Bosco Scorace, Bosco Scorace guidebook by The Crimp for sale; 3 main sectors, over 200 lines 4a–8a+ (older text); Never Sleeping Wall & Parco Cerriolo 20 min by car.
- UKC Destination Sicily (Massimo Cappuccio, 2022-06-01) https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/destinations/sicily-14498: multipitch east "wild Rocche del Crasto on the Nebrodi"; Caltavuturo district centre of island "most mountainous area and the most suitable during the hot Sicilian summers"; sport list Palermo, Caltavuturo; multipitch San Vito, Palermo, Rocche del Crasto, Capo Calavà; boulder Bosco Scorace, Bronte; airports Palermo Catania Trapani; train/public transport "not recommended... long and unnerving"; essential to rent car; multipitch -> west between San Vito and Palermo; "If you are a boulderer, the best place is Bosco Scorace"; winter Dec–Mar mild coast cold inland; Jul–Aug too hot; spring/autumn best; rainfall late autumn & winter–early spring. Guides: Di roccia di sole (Versante Sud, all cliffs), Rockfax Sicily (west), "Bosco Scorace" guide by Crimpandgoodlife, Sicily Rock, Vertical life. Bosco Scorace: hinterland Trapani; eucalyptus, oaks, Mediterranean scrub; sandstone compared to Fontainebleau; 4 to 8A+; landings comfortable safe; three sectors Bevaio, Rocche Forbici, Spaceship, total 200 lines; crash pads can be hired; guide sold at BB Polvere di Stelle; BB Polvere di Stelle base camp: driving, crash pad rental, climbing wall.
- Ministero dell'Interno 2019-10-08 https://www.interno.gov.it/it/notizie/attivo-anche-palermo-numero-emergenza-unico-europeo-112: NUE 112 operational in Palermo, Sicily first southern region with full coverage; old numbers 113/112/115/118 still routed; free, multilingual; central for western Sicily at Palermo (Agrigento, Trapani provinces), Caltanissetta for east.
- Versante Sud Di Roccia di Sole https://www.versantesud.it/it/negozio/catalogue/di-roccia-di-sole_1399/: Cappuccio, Gallo; 2022; ISBN 9788885475830; 560 p; Italian (variants English, German, iClimbing); 5 editions in ~20 yrs; falesie (single pitch) + boulder + DWS; multipitch split into a separate volume "che uscirà a seguito"; €35; Disponibile.
