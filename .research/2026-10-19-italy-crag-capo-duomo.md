# Research notes: Capo d'Uomo (Monte Argentario) — 2026-10-19

Scope: Capo d'Uomo sea-facing limestone crag under the Torre di Capo d'Uomo, Monte Argentario (Grosseto): single-pitch sector + bolted multi-pitch sector (Spigolo Bonatti, Argento Argentario, Magnificat, Maria...Mare, Gechi di Maremma). Series 〈義大利岩場〉 order 903.

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain crag page https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/capo-d-uomo.html (by guide Eraldo Meraldi): 15 routes, 5c–7a, height 30 m, exposure SSE; "una delle più belle falesie affacciate sul mare d'Italia"; vertical slab of white-grey limestone "a gocce, buchi e concrezioni", technical and sustained; bolted mid-1990s by Roman and local climbers, bolts suffered from the sea; rebolted with stainless steel by Meraldi with CAI Grosseto; access: Porto S. Stefano, at first roundabout at the port turn left, up Vallone del Campone ~3.5 km, at left fork park after the col with sea view, dirt road down ~300 m, right road to big uphill bend, path left along ridge toward the tower, short steep descent to base, 15–20 min; "Andateci in tutte le stagioni"; route table 1–15 (Capo d'Uomo 6a 22 m, Il mare d'inverno 5c, L'isola persa 5c, Histeria 6a, Pilastro Bonatti 6a+ 28 m, Stalattiti 5c+, Omega 6a, Spirito saraceno 6b, Fulmicotone 6c+ 30 m, Barabba 6c, Le confessioni di Suor Germana 6b+, Capo Caccia 6b+/6b, Il sogno di Maria 6a, Gab 6a+/6c, Aiutati che Dio t'aiuta 6a/5c); related news: Magnificat 6b+ 80 m (2016), Spigolo Bonatti 6a 240 m (2012).
- Planetmountain Spigolo Bonatti https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/spigolo-bonatti-capo-d-uomo-argentario.html: FA Eraldo Meraldi 08–09/2012; SSE; length 240 m; height 350 m; 6a, obligatory 5b; 7 pitches 6a/5c/3c/5b/3a/5c/3a; 61 10 mm stainless bolts + 16 on belays; 15 quickdraws, helmet; friable rock on P5; loose scree on approach descent, 30 min; dedicated to Walter Bonatti; ends near the tower.
- Montagna.TV 26 Jan 2025 https://www.montagna.tv/249787/ampliata-la-storica-falesia-di-capo-duomo-allargentario/: "alle 18 linee originarie il chiodatore toscano Lorenzo Masala ne ha aggiunte altre 10, di cui 3 saranno completate per la fine di gennaio"; new routes left of the old ones, 19–38 m; old routes 5c–6c+, new up to 8a; vertical or slightly overhanging, generously bolted; good-quality marine limestone, still grippy; exposure East/South-East: winter sun all morning, good in cold season until early afternoon; shoulder seasons all day; summer only late afternoon; multi-pitch mixed exposure, winter only on calm days; crag born 1985–86 (Rustici, Pozzi); 2012 Meraldi rebolt with CAI Grosseto; multi-pitch sport ones resecured with 12 mm 316L fix; 2021 Profico "Gechi di Maremma" 3 pitches 5c–6a; crowded on weekends when bad weather elsewhere in central Italy (Lazio climbers); Masala rebolt at own expense + voluntary contributions.
- theCrag Capo D'Uomo https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/central-and-southern-italy/firenze-florence-area/area/957418218: 38 routes; 40 min drive from Grosseto, ~2 h from Rome; two areas: sport near the trail, multi-pitch further down toward the sea; bolted 1985–1995 by Rustici & Pozzi; 2012 Meraldi 304 stainless; 2022 spring & 2025 winter Masala rebolt with 316 stainless bolts and chains; Access: "Cars are not allowed on the dirt road"; parking coordinates 42.40102579598612, 11.11294566340444; barrier ~300 m down the dirt road (for cars, walkers bypass); approach 15–18 min; tower ~350 m asl; routes incl.
