# Research notes: Champorcher bouldering — 2026-10-19

Scope: boulders in Val de la Legne / Valle della Legna below Champorcher village (Valle di Champorcher, lower Aosta Valley). Groundlane MCP worked; 27crags and Visit Champorcher via curl; UKC, 27crags overview and Planetmountain via Exa.

## Sources opened

- MadBoulder Champorcher https://www.madboulder.org/champorcher: "grades up to 8B+"; north-east exposure, ideal spring and autumn, "even in summer nights or winter mornings depending on snow conditions"; celebrated for the Murano boulder; Niccolò Ceria and Gabriele Moroni; easily accessible boulders and others with a walk through fir trees and streams; rock "Serpentine"; 1,315 m; 6–7 videos.
- MadBoulder Champorcher sector list https://www.madboulder.org/sectors/champorcher/unknown: Gliese 581 8b+; Murano 8a+; Murano Low 8b+; Pompa 7b; The Ghost Ship 8b+; The Wheel of Champo 8b.
- UKC Logbook Champorcher https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/champorcher-24250/: 163 climbs; rock type Granite; 1,224 m; faces all; "The finest collection of granite boulders in the Aosta Valley. Routes from f5-f8b but mainly in the 6b-7b range"; carved wooden signs lead off the main road; parking in large area in front of the Ghost Ship boulder on the right after crossing the river; last updated 11/Mar/2025.
- InfoBoulder Champorcher https://www.infoboulder.com/?area=159&mod=boulders: about 100 problems; rock Serpentino; altitude 1,400 m; period spring, summer, autumn; GPS 45.6199, 7.63303 (parking); access: Donnas → roundabout to Hône → valley road; about 13 km from Hône turn left (after right hairpin) into Val de la Legne, parking 800 m after junction just after bridge; news: Ceria The Ghost Ship 8B+ (2013), Moroni repeats Murano 8B (2013).
- 27crags Champorcher Central https://27crags.com/crags/champorcher: 145 boulders; "No special access issues"; every zone has a parking; free camper area in Champorcher; topos Albicocca, Anfibio, Aquilante, Baracca, Bienvenue; nearby Champorcher Haut 4 boulders, Cubo 62 boulders.
- 27crags Champorcher route list https://27crags.com/crags/champorcher/routelist: Moschetto 5+ (Muffa, 27 ascents); Muffa centrale 6C (21); Canino 6B+ (Canini, 19); Pompa 7B (The Ghost Ship, 18); Nemo 7A (Baracca, 16); Mirò 7A (16); Senza collo 6A (15); il 13 6B+ (15); Movimento Sexy 6C+ (Camomillo, 15); Canino left 6C+ (15); Muffa 7A (15); Mediterrone 6A (Anfibio, 14); Poli 6B (14); Cloè 7A (Diamante, 9); Autogrill 5+ (Moss, 8); Paperella 7B+ (4); Un' emozione forevva 7C (4); Fidel Castro 7C (2); Boxwood hill 8A; The ghost ship 8B+; Minorenni a carico 8B; Minorenni corazzate 8B+; Murano Low 8C+ (0 ascents).
- Planetmountain 2013 https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/niccolo-ceria-drifts-up-the-ghost-ship-at-champorcher.html: May 2013 FA The ghost ship 8B+ by Ceria on "the most famous boulder", slopey crimps to powerful finish; Ceria: Champorcher "might be the best bouldering area in the entire Valle d'Aosta"; some boulders incredible friction, others glassy; getting there: from Donnas to Hône, up Valle di Champorcher, about 12 km after Hône turn left into Val de la Legne, about 800 m, over a bridge, park; road splits in two after bridge with boulders along each.
- 8a.nu Italy bouldering crags https://www.8a.nu/crags/bouldering/italy: Champorcher 1,694 ascents, 13th on the Italy list (2026-10-19 order: Algund, Varazze, Val Masino, La Plana, Foppiano, Ceresole Reale, Val di Mello, Aufhofen, Daone La Curva, Unknown Crag, Rio Tinello, Daone Nudole, Champorcher).
- Visit Champorcher climbing https://visitchamporcher.com/en/arrampicata-27575: valley climbing list incl. "Valle della Legna – 1200 – 1400mt", Placche di Oriana, Bec Raty, Dondena – Craton Boulder, Dondena falesia; views to Mont Avic Park.
- Visit Champorcher come raggiungerci https://visitchamporcher.com/come-raggiungerci-27595: train to Pont-Saint-Martin or Hône-Bard stations then local bus; bus from Pont-Saint-Martin (station or Piazza IV Novembre) if from Milan/Turin, from Verrès bus stop if from Aosta; timetable "Linea Champorcher - Verres - Pont Saint Martin"; car: A5 exit Pont-Saint-Martin, SS26 toward Aosta, after Bard ~1 km roundabout left to Hône, then Pontboset, Champorcher; from Aosta exit Verrès, past Arnad ~3 km roundabout right; winter: chains and snow tyres mandatory, alpine/arctic diesel.
- Regione VdA Elettrificazione Aosta–Ivrea https://trasporti.regione.vda.it/trasporto-ferroviario/infrastruttura/elettrificazione-aosta-ivrea: Ivrea–Aosta line closed since January 2024; substitute buses; reopening planned with December 2026 timetable; Hône-Bard station works.
- Regione VdA news 2026 https://trasporti.regione.vda.it/notizie/2026/riapertura-della-tratta-ferroviaria-ivrea-chivasso-2: reopening from 11 January 2027 Ivrea–Pont-Saint-Martin; to Aosta by spring 2027.
- Regione VdA orari autobus https://trasporti.regione.vda.it/trasporto-su-gomma/trasporto-pubblico-locale/orari-autobus: Champorcher, Hône, Pontboset, Bard, Pont-Saint-Martin served by Arriva and VITA.
- Versante Sud Aosta Valley Crags vol. 1 https://www.versantesud.it/en/shop/catalogue/english_1724/: 2025, 424 pages, English, ISBN 978 88 55472 258; covers Champorcher Valley among others (sport crags book; bouldering coverage not stated).
