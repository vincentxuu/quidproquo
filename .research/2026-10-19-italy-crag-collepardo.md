# Research notes: Cueva di Collepardo — 2026-10-19

Crag: La Cueva (and Cuevita, La Pala, Classica), Collepardo, Frosinone, Lazio. Series 義大利岩場 order 1204. All pages opened 2026-10-19.

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Cueva di Collepardo crag page https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/cueva-di-collepardo.html: difficulty 6b - 9a; routes number 31; rock limestone; orientation "NNE, the crag is in the shade all day long"; link www.verticalpark.it; sectors La cueva parete sinistra (19 lines) and parete destra (12 lines); Traverso della muerte 7a 18 mt; Traverso dei sogni 8c 30 mt; Tomorrowland 8a+ 20 mt; Bella Lu' 8b 27 mt; Clorophilla 7c+ 25 mt; Donkey kong 7a+ 18 mt; L'assassino 7a 18 mt; Sadomasoclimb 6c 14 mt; Madonnazinonvedol'ora 6b+ 14 mt; Spermatocanna 6c+ 15 mt; L'anno mio 6b+ 12 mt; Abbasso la pinna 6b+ 12 mt; La gasparata 7b 15 mt; 95 bpm 7c+; La spada Jedy 7b; Fuckaldo 7c+; La scallona 6b 18 mt.
- Planetmountain "La Cueva di Collepardo - arrampicata nel cuore dei Monti Ernici" (June 2016) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/la-cueva-di-collepardo-arrampicata-nel-cuore-dei-monti-ernici.html: oltre 30 vie dal 6b+ al 8c, climbable "anche in giornate di forte pioggia"; Vallefiume valley of the torrente Cosa, right bank limestone with karst; grotta about thirty metres high full of concretions; discovered by Massimo Gambineri; bolted from 2015 by instructors of the Vertical Park gym in Rome, ground-up; area has about a hundred routes in 4 main sectors; La Pala first lines by Sebastiano La Bozzetta in the 90s, freed by Fabrizio Peri (first probable 8c in Ciociaria); Settore Classico and Grotta dei Bambocci around 2003 (Gambineri, Logoreci, Mazzacano); La Pala 2015 by Fabrizio Peri; Intorre recommends Tomorrowland and Bella Lù (30 m on concretions); about an hour's drive from Rome.
- Planetmountain Rogora Rèveille-toi (04 June 2019) https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/laura-rogora-fires-reveille-toi-new-9a-at-cueva-of-collepardo.html: Collepardo "circa 1.5 hours southeast of Rome"; Rèveille-toi 9a is the direct finish to the 8c Il traverso dei sogni bolted by Roberto Podio; direct finish bolted by Bertrand Lemaire; route seeps in winter; Rogora list: 11/2016 Tomorrowland extension 8c+/9a FA; 12/2016 Supercrack 8b+/8c FA; 01/2017 La Gasparata 8c+/9a FA; 03/2017 Ercole 8c/+ FA; 04/2018 Sitting Bull 8c+/9a FA; 06/2019 Rèveille-toi 9a FA.
- theCrag Cueva di Collepardo https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/central-and-southern-italy/rome-area/area/1774694010: 67 routes; "Ombra tutto il giorno, Asciutta durante pioggia, Sole al mattino"; left wall longer endurance routes always in shade, right wall in sun until about 14:00 with shorter bouldery routes; access: from Collepardo road 115 toward Certosa di Trisulti, about 4km after town restaurant « Lo scoiattolo », park there, hike down left of restaurant, steep slope through forest, then right to the cave; "Accesso ripido 10-20 minuti"; aspect nord nord-est; routes: Traverso della Muerte 7a ★★★ 18m, Réveille Toi 9a FA Laura Rogora Giu 2019, Tomorrowland Extension 8c+, Bella Lù 8b ★★★ 25m, Clorophilla 7c ★★★ 20m, L'Assassino 6c+/7a, Il bullo, il manesco el'autistico 6a+, La Gasparata Extension 8c+/9a FA Laura Rogora 2016, Super crack 8c; location-unknown list: Sitting Bull 8c+/9a, Ercole 8c/c+ FFA Rogora Mar 2017, The Bomb 9a (Fabrizio Peri, Feb 2020), Bruce Lee L2 8c FFA Rogora Feb 2022; guidebooks Rome South (Innocenti 2016).
- Climbook Collepardo (Cueva, Cuevita) https://climbook.com/falesie/15121/collepardo-cueva-cuevita: "Grotta di calcare immersa nei boschi sopra Collepardo"; "una delle falesie simbolo del centro Italia"; style "prevalentemente fisico e di resistenza"; climbable in uncertain weather, "ideale per le mezze stagioni"; "ATTENZIONE: vige un divieto di arrampicata in tutto il comprensorio di Collepardo dal 1 gennaio al 31 luglio"; "Aggiornamento maggio 2026 (Fonte: il chiodatore, R. Podio "Limetta")"; 1751 repetitions; most repeated Madonnazinonvedol'ora 6b 202, Sadomasoclimb L1 6c 168, L'assassino L1 6c+/7a 140, Donkey kong 7a+ 100 (7a in earlier topo), La gasparata L1 7a+ 91 (7b in earlier topo), La spada Jedy L1 7b 88, Traverso della muerte L1 7a 84, L'anno mio 6b+ 76, Spermatocanna 6c+ 76, Riabassa la pinna 6b 66; comments about 10 years ago: Limetta wrote that a ban runs 1 Jan–31 Jul and the Forestale would enforce it, vigili urbani warned climbers at the parking.
- Alpinisti d'Appennino Falesia La Cueva (17 Marzo 2023, updated 25 Settembre 2024) https://www.alpinistidappennino.it/2023/03/17/falesia-la-cueva/: grotta with concretions and canne, 6b–8c+; left sector longer endurance, right shorter bouldery; La Cuevita sector on the path before the cave (overhanging, edges); climbs well even in heavy rain unless after long unstable periods when tufas seep; exposure Nord e Nord-Est; approach 15 min; bolts spit 10 mm e fittoni inox; altitude 830 m; "Periodo: Da settembre a novembre. Interdizione dal 1 gennaio al 31 luglio"; road: A1 exit Frosinone, SS156 toward Alatri/Fiuggi, after Alatri SP48 to Collepardo, in town SP115 toward Trisulti, park at ristorante "Lo Scoiattolo da Anna"; approach from parking left of restaurant, descend, cross a fence, right at junction to La Cuevita then the cave; gear 12-14 quickdraws, 60 m rope up to 7c+, 80 m for some harder routes, knee pads useful; route list incl. Reveille-toi 9a 30 m, Tomorrowland L2 8c+ 40 m, Bella Lù L1 8b 27 m, La gasparata L2 8c+ 25 m, Super Crack 8b+ 18 m, Il ritorno 8a+, Sadomasoclimb L2 7b+, Madonnazinonvedol'ora 6b, La scallona 6b.
- Oliunid Seven summer climbing crags (3 Jul 2023) https://oliunid.com/blog/seven-summer-climbing-crags-umbria-lazio-abruzzo: Collepardo – La Cueva, La Pala, Classica; "In 2023, climbing in La Cueva was restricted for nesting birds of prey, with a ban from January through 31 July"; routes from 8b up generally need at least 7a; many bolted by Roberto "Limetta" of Vertical Park; autumn best season, rock dries after first rains; La Pala and Classica 20 to 40 degrees steep, routes 25–30 m, Ercole 8c/+ and Sitting Bull 8c+/9a there; many Lazio crags affected by Jan–Jul closures for nesting raptors in the Monti Simbruini Park.
- parks.it ZPS Monti Simbruini ed Ernici https://www.parks.it/IT6050008/par.php: Natura 2000 code IT6050008; 52,099 ha; comuni include Collepardo; ente gestore Parco Naturale Regionale Monti Simbruini; species include aquila, grifone, gracchio corallino; normativa DGR Lazio 612/11, DGR Lazio 498/19.
- parks.it / Parco Nazionale del Circeo notice on Precipizio climbing ban https://www.parks.it/parco.nazionale.circeo/dettaglio.php?id=69177: "ai sensi del DGR 612/2011, nel periodo che va dal primo gennaio al 31 luglio sono sospese le attività di avvicinamento a tali pareti mediante ... arrampicata libera o attrezzata" (Lazio-wide ZPS conservation measure for nesting walls).
- Planetmountain Peschio delle Cornacchie ban https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/alpinismo/divieto-arrampicata-a-peschio-delle-cornacchie.html: Regione Lazio note 0057206 of 21.01.2020 banned climbing Jan–Jul at Peschio delle Cornacchie (Filettino) inside ZPS IT6050008 "in applicazione delle misure di conservazione delle ZPS" — same ZPS as Collepardo.
- Monti Ernici forum (2007) http://www.montiernici.it/forum/viewtopic.php?t=315&highlight=&sid=2501a957dd5fd0688ab4be4a98c8e61d: map of ZPS/SIC; a local bolter writes the Collepardo sectors they equipped are in the ZPS; admin says only constraint found is no climbing at bird nesting sites in nesting period.
