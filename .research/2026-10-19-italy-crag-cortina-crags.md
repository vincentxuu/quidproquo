# Research notes: Cortina single-pitch crags (Crépe de Oucèra Alte/Basse + Bèco d'Ajàl) — 2026-10-19

Series: 義大利岩場 order 505. All three crags are along the SP638 Pocol–Passo Giau road. Cinque Torri (order 502) is covered separately; Oucèra Alte lies at its foot.

## Sources opened

- Cortina tourist board — Crépe de Oucèra Basse https://cortina.dolomiti.org/it/arrampicata-sportiva/crepe-de-oucera-basse/: forest crag on Cortina–Passo Giau road, walls visible from Malga Peziè de Parù; 1,610–1,660 m; faces north, sheltered by forest, morning sun, very rarely damp; ideal summer season (May–October); one of Cortina's first crags (second only to Béco d'Ajàl) and among largest; "oltre 80 tiri" medium-easy, from 4th grade to 7c, most grade 6; suitable for beginners and children; bolted by Scoiattolo Diego Ghedina "Tomas" early 1990s; pitches up to 30 m, 70 m rope advised; pocketed water-worked solid dolomite, vertical walls; close bolting; access: Pocol, past Hotel Argentina take SP638 toward Passo Giau, ~3 km, end of first series of 5 tight hairpins park on left small lay-by; flat path opposite side ~400 m, 5 min; nearby Malga Peziè de Parù, Baita Il Cervo, Hotel Argentina bar
- Cortina tourist board — Crépe de Oucèra Alte https://cortina.dolomiti.org/it/arrampicata-sportiva/crepe-de-oucera-alte/: long band of rock along Cortina–Passo Giau road at the base of the Cinque Torri; 1,700–1,800 m; faces south: summer plus spring/autumn, sunny winter days with little snow; first modern crag of Cortina; "oltre 80 vie" medium-hard, some multi-pitch, 6a–8b+; up to 40 m, 80 m rope advised; flaky/pocketed dolomite, overhanging in places; bolting less close than Basse; historic routes "Latte Acido", "Melassa", "Patapunfete"; 4 sectors right to left: Fedora (vertical, technical; named after a 6a warm-up), Magica (overhanging slab, compact; Magica Armonia 7c), Babuska (more friable, steeper, athletic), Dita di Burro (pockets 6b–7, plus "Polvere" 8b+ freed by Luca Zardini "Canon", hardest of the crag); access: SP638 4 km, after straights park on right in long narrow lay-by; also start of path 437 to Cinque Torri and 434/437 to Croda da Lago; take path to the right of the Cinque Torri one, round a wooded hill, ~100 m to first sector (visible from parking)
- Cortina tourist board — Bèco d'Ajàl (EN) https://cortina.dolomiti.org/en/arrampicata-sportiva/beco-dajal-en: dense fir forest north of Croda da Lago; tower visible from Cortina centre; 1,750 m; walls of different aspects; cool, suitable June–September; walls stay wet a few days after prolonged rain; three large boulders 60–80 m high; solid dolomite with holes, yellow and black; largely overhanging, difficult; "around 50 routes", bolted by Scoiattoli, 6a–8c, 20–40 m, endurance style; protections distant but safe; Cortina's first 8c "Schiaffo" freed 2003 by Scoiattolo Luca Zardini Canon; approach: SP638 ~1.5 km after Hotel Argentina, just before Malga Peziè de Parù park on left, mule track to Val di Formin, after bridge turn left at sign, ~30 min to clearing, then 5 min right to crag
- Planetmountain — Crépe de Oucèra Alti https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/crepe-de-oucera-alti.html: "one of the most important crags in the Cortina area and, together with the nearby Cinque Torri, ... one of the most important crags in the entire eastern Dolomites"; 1,800 m; 70 pitches 6a–8a+; SSE; 70 m rope; bolting excellent; crag "almost 20 years old"; Bassi a few hairpins lower: 80 pitches 5a–6b; access SS638 ~4 km long straight, Alta Via 1 path, rightmost path 5 min; period spring/summer/autumn, winter if little snow; bibliography "Arrampicata sportiva a Cortina d'Ampezzo" Roberto Casanova, Cierre; sectors Primo Settore (most popular, mostly 6a–6c; recommends Pikindolor, Latte acido), Magica, Tetti (athletic >7b, stays wet after heavy rain); route table: Didò 8 m 5; Momo 10 m 5+; Gogo 10 m 6a; Snorki 14 m 6a+; White Line 14 m 6b; Uomini gatto 15 m 6b+; Klamera 16 m 6c; Melassa 16 m 6b+; P38 6b+,6b; Latte acido 20+18+12+8 m 6b+,6b,6a+,6c; Fedora 22 m 6a; Spitta e spera 20 m 7a; Pikindolor 20+15+12+8 m 6c,6b+,6a,6b+; Profumo di donna 20 m 6c+; Odore di sudore 20 m 6c; Delirio cosmico 6a,7c,7b,6c; Elettroshock 15 m 7a; Cuba libre 26 m 7b; Gin Fizz 31 m 7b+; Petorada 32 m 6c+; Magica: Tequila gold 20+15 m 7a+,6c; Lemon Wodka 7b+; Whisky and Soda 18 m 7a; Magica armonia 24 m 7c; Schizofrenica follia 23 m 7c+; Patapunfete 22 m 7a; Fisiognomica 22 m 8a+; Rebus 16 m 7c; Tetti: Oro 20 m 7b; Incenso 7b+; Babuska 22 m 7b; Caraibi bic 7c; Delta force 22 m 7c; Resinata 24 m 8a; Attibabao 26 m 8a+; Urla nel silenzio 26 m 7c+
- guidedolomiti — Crags in Cortina d'Ampezzo https://www.guidedolomiti.com/en/crags-in-cortina-dampezzo/: page says "more than two thousand single-pitch routes spread across the Cortina basin"; Basse: technical slab on vertical featured rock, most routes 6th grade, approach ~10 min, 20–35 m, "more than 80 lines", spring to autumn; Alte: four sectors right→left Fedora, Magica armonia, Babuska, Dita di burro; technical vertical walls and sculpted slabs; mid grades upward; approach 10–20 min; 15–40 m; "more than 80 lines"; 80 m rope essential for longer routes; Bèco: long sustained routes on overhangs, bolts relatively spaced, most routes around grade 7, approach ~30 min, 15–40 m, "more than 60 lines", best season summer, 80 m rope for longer routes, "for many climbers, one of the best sport climbing experiences in the Cortina area"
- guidedolomiti — Via del Dodo (2026-08-30, Enrico Maioni) https://guidedolomiti.com/en/climbing/via-del-dodo-beco-aial: Bèco d'Aial tower in Fedèra forests at foot of Croda da Lago, WWI observation post remains; 1962 north-face route Michielli & Zardini ~200 m V–VI; new route 26–27 Aug 2026 by Giordano Riello & Massimo "Mox" Da Pozzo, ~150 m, five pitches, fully bolted: 6c, 6b, 6b, 6b, 5c; grades not yet confirmed; approach: SP638 ~1.5 km after Hotel Argentina, parking before Peziè de Parù, dirt road to Val di Formin, bridge over Rio Costeana, left, do NOT follow crag signs, continue to clearing, path right to wall
