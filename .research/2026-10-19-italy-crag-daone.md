# Research notes: Valle di Daone（抱石）— 2026-10-19

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Valle di Daone Boulder https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/valle-di-daone-boulder.html: 4C–8C; granite; routes number 600; side valley of Valle del Chiese, Adamello; valley starts at village 686 m, road 21 km to Bissina dam 1700 m; famous first for ice falls, then multi-pitch, then bouldering; "some have described it as a new Mecca for bouldering"; first problems end of 1990s (Faletti, Tomasi, Savio, Montanari and others); "7 sectors with circa 600 problems, spread out over 15km and 800m altitude difference"; climbing possible almost all year; bibliography Daone Prog (Montanari, Versante Sud); listed problems Karmelo 6C, Brostravers 7A, Antrace 7B, In visita 7A, Muller thurgau 6C, Antologia 7C, L'Infame 6B, James Hutton 7B, Nimbus 7A, James Tont 7B, Bitter Emotion 8A, Lo Squalo 6B, Il Membro del Guapo 6C, L'avenir nous reserve rien de bon 8B, Spigolo dell Anguria 7C, Thor o the king 8A (all shown 5 stars); link daoneclimbing.webnode.com
- 27crags Val Daone https://27crags.com/areas/daone: "well known among the climbers as the best boulder area in the north of Italy"; 2070 routes in 14 crags; premium topo 1565 routes; best season March to October; getting there: drive or fly to Venice/Milan and rent a car
- lacrux（2023-05-06）https://www.lacrux.com/en/bouldern/daone-the-bouldering-paradise-not-far-from-arco-boulder-guide/: international hotspot since first edition of Daone Boulder; 14 km, 700 m altitude; ten sectors, over 1800 lines in granite or verrucano; sectors at 800–2000 m; Boulder Park in Plana forest: obstacles removed, landings levelled with mulch, two parcours for adults and one for children; majority of lines 6a–7b; hardest: Big Illusion 8C, L'oppio dei climbers 8B+, 8B lines Il Pusher, Fuori Tempo, L'avenir nous reserve big bamboo; public transport: train to Trento or Brescia, then bus to Valle del Chiese/Daone; third edition of guide (Montanari) over 1800 boulders in ten sectors with QR codes
- Campiglio Dolomiti APT https://www.campigliodolomiti.it/en/arrampicata-bouldering/bouldering-in-valle-di-daone-and-the-acroriver: "Over 600 clean, accessible boulders"; Boulder Park with signposting, pathways, routes of different levels, safe even for beginners; info Daone Climbing website; Acroriver closed for 2026 summer season; Pieve di Bono infopoint tel +39 0465 901217
- theCrag Valle di Daone（Exa fetch）https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/northern-italy/arco-trento-area/area/3220470405: 1,154 routes, mostly bouldering, 572 ascents; "12 sectors with more than 1800 problems, spread out over 25km and 1000m altitude difference"; sub-areas La Plana/Foresta 342, La Curva 284, Dos dei Aser/Casetta degli Alpini 245, Nudole 146, Fiume 45, 18:12 44, Lago 6; also Placche di Danerba and Parete del V tornante areas (sport/trad, no route data shown); seasonality table empty
- Oliunid（2025-04-07）https://www.oliunid.com/blog/val-daone-three-all-round-bouldering-areas-in-trentino/: Gramitico bouldering meeting since 2015 at La Plana; no mobile signal in the valley; not possible to camp in PNAB, Nudole car park with toilets for campers; spring/autumn performance conditions; road "usually closed from October to May"; Nudole 1550 m, ~50 boulders, Virgola 6b+ visible from car park; Spigolo dell'anguria 7b+ (sit start), Thor The King 8a, Insetto Stecco 7a tall slab; La Plana: Magic Line 8a (Ghisolfi/Ondra), Mad Line 7c, L'ombelico del mondo 8a, Clean for Climb 7c+/8a, Cocoo 7c; Eye of the Cyclops upper La Plana, landings need more care; Dos dei Aser & Fish-Man near Casa degli Alpini: shade in summer, almost no approach, Orkaloka 8a, Piccolo ma caratteristico 8a, Lime 7c, Fuori Tempo 8b; L'avenir nous reserve rien de bon 8b (sit, 8a stand) by Lorenzo Puri; Big Illusion 8c by Stefan Scarperi
- Daone boulder blog（Matthias Basetti）Mappa https://daoneboulder.blogspot.com/p/mappa-daone.html: valley entrance 510 m near Creto; in PNAB; ~70 km from Valle del Sarca (Arco); sectors Foresta, La Curva, Scoglio, Subacqueo, 18:12, Casetta degli Alpini, The Fishman, Caos Blocchi, Nudole; boulders from ~1000 m (Foresta) to 1540 m (Nudole); recommended March–November; road closed at Lago di Boazzo from about end of November to end of May (usually), sectors up to D Scalette reachable by car, Casetta degli Alpini 20 min walk from barrier; Foresta: drive across river to dedicated parking; Caos Blocchi little parking and river crossing, water level changes
- Daone boulder blog Casetta degli Alpini（2013-05-23）https://daoneboulder.blogspot.com/2013/05/casetta-degli-alpini.html: sector around hairpins after Lago Boazzo to Casetta degli Alpini; park along road or in front of hut; Kamikaze 7a; The Fishman 7c/7c+ only when river is low; Nimbus 2000 and James Tont "i blocchi più ripetuti della valle"; Matti con le ali 7b
- InfoBoulder Val Daone https://infoboulder.com/?area=52&mod=boulders: granite; crimps, roofs, slopers, some highballs; some bolted/trad routes too; "Divieti": at Foresta (loc.
