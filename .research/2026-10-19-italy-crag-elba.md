# Research notes: Isola d'Elba — 2026-10-19

Scope: granite (and, at Costa dei Gabbiani, volcanic) crags of Elba: Monte San Bartolomeo (Chiessi; sport + bolted multi-pitch), Fetovaia, La Freccia (Pomonte), Madonna del Monte (Marciana), Costa dei Gabbiani (Ginepro, Remaiolo, Baia dei Pirati). Series 〈義大利岩場〉 order 902.

## Sources opened

- Climb Europe Tuscany https://www.climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-italy-tuscany: Elba "only one hour on the ferry from the coast"; 8 separate crags "from sea cliff climbing to multi-pitch routes"; many mid-range routes F5–low F7; mostly single-pitch sport; bolted multi-pitch up to 170 m on Monte San Bartolomeo; Tuscany best in spring or autumn.
- Climb Europe central Italy https://www.climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-italy-central-climbing-areas: Elba "everything from bolted sea cliff climbing to multi-pitch routes, with many mid-range climbs (F5's through to low F7's) on granite rock".
- infoelba San Bartolomeo https://www.infoelba.it/sport-tempo-libero/sport-terra/free-climbing/san-bartolomeo/: from Portoferraio via Procchio, Marina di Campo, Cavoli, Seccheto, Fetovaia, Pomonte; ~30 km to Chiessi; park in church square or lower car park; CAI path 103 from granite steps in front of church; ~45 min; two tiers: lower tier (signed turn-off) multi-pitch, upper mostly single pitch; short via ferrata to summit; at least 12 quickdraws, 60 m rope, helmet advised; topo 1–27: Spigolo all'ombra 5c, Olympus L1 5a L2 6b L3 6c, Variante ascari 7c, Mediterranea L1 6b L2 7a L3 6c, Spigolo al sole 6b, Ale 7a+, Fabbricante di chiavi 7b, Partenza micidiale 7a, La fessura 6b, Aspide 6c, Prunella 6a+, 6 luglio 7a, Ventarella 6a+, Nina 6b+, Pinta 6a+, Santa Maria 6b, Papavero 6a, Sara 5c, 111 e lode L1 5b L2 5a L3 6a, Via dei gigli martagoni L1 5b L2 IV L3 IV+, Via dei veci L1 IV L2 IV L3 V.
- infoelba free climbing index https://www.infoelba.it/sport-tempo-libero/sport-terra/free-climbing/: crags San Bartolomeo, La Freccia (Pomonte), Fetovaia (Seccheto–Fetovaia), Madonna del Monte, San Francesco Severo, Ginepro, Grotta del Ginepro, Remaiolo, Baia dei Pirati; selection by Renato Bardi (FASI instructor); "non è facile raccogliere informazioni aggiornate sullo stato delle vie, degli spit e delle soste… verificare sempre la sicurezza delle vie chiodate".
- infoelba Fetovaia https://www.infoelba.it/sport-tempo-libero/sport-terra/free-climbing/fetovaia/: four sectors from parkings along the road Seccheto→Fetovaia; sector 3 "il più praticabile"; most routes have no equipped lower-off, only two fittoni → bring slings; max height 15 m; routes incl. Tempest 6b+, Blu Blu 5c, Poco Poco 6a, Diedrix 5c+, Toccata e fuga 6c+, Luna 6c+, Via della formica 7a, Fabricrack 7a+, Ombra Nera 7a+.
- infoelba La Freccia https://www.infoelba.it/sport-tempo-libero/sport-terra/free-climbing/la-freccia-pomonte/: arrow-shaped wall above Pomonte; road to parkings right of church, footbridge, path; Via della freccia diretta L1 5b L2 6a+ L3 IV; Via della freccia IV+.
- infoelba Madonna del Monte https://www.infoelba.it/sport-tempo-libero/sport-terra/free-climbing/madonna-del-monte/: from Marciana follow Museo Archeologico, cobbled path toward the church; Spigolo Caprile 5c+, L'Alveare 6b+, Smeraldo 6a, Topazio 6a+, Via delle capre 6a+, Green Feeet 7a+.
- infoelba Ginepro https://www.infoelba.it/sport-tempo-libero/sport-terra/free-climbing/falesia-ginepro/: Capoliveri → Costa dei Gabbiani; dirt road ~10 km to Tenuta delle Ripalte; chain-closed roads to Remaiolo/Baia dei Pirati and to Ginepro beach (~20 min walk); "Questa zona è proprietà privata della Tenuta delle Ripalte e l'accesso è regolamentato… è necessario annunciarsi alla reception… La parete non è attrezzata dalla Tenuta delle Ripalte"; routes Dulpherina 5a, Altamarea 5b, Dobloni e Galeoni 5c, Dolphina 6c+, Verdesca L1 5c L2 5b.
- theCrag Monte San Bartolomeo https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/monte-san-bartolomeo: 27 routes; "Fixed Gear: Rusty anchors and some spits. I would not recommend it. Not easy path to reach sector 1." (created ~2 years ago); "Multi-pitch climbs on granite"; slab; approach steep 30–45 min, path 103; grades 5a–7c.
- Climbook Monte S. Bartolomeo https://climbook.com/falesie/251/monte-s-bartolomeo-monotiri and /vie: 33 entries; updated April 2026, source "Guida Toscana e Isola d'Elba – Ed. Versante Sud – Marzo 2025 – Richiodatori"; two sectors one behind the other; "nel Settore Vie Lunghe, oltre alle 3 multipitch marcate recentemente richiodate, sono presenti altre 6 Vie lunghe non segnate perchè oramai pericolose e necessitanti di richiodatura"; 111 e lode L1–L6 6a/5c/5c/6c/5a/4c; Via dei gigli Martagoni L1–L6 5c+/6a, 5a, 5b, 5b+/5c, 4c, 4c; Via dei veci L1–L6 5c+/6a, 5b, 5b, 6a, 5b, 4c.
- Bergführer Dolomiten (guide, secondary) https://www.bergfuehrerdolomiten.it/it/arrampicata-all-isola-d-elba-toscana/: granite, lava at Costa dei Gabbiani; 2025 reports rusty bolts breaking at Baia dei Pirati and Remaiolo; lichen makes rock slippery when wet (Madonna del Monte, San Bartolomeo ferrata and lower sector); 50 m rope and 10 draws; always rethread, don't lower on rusty krabs; Sant'Andrea not climbable Nov 2021 (old rusty bolts); Madonna del Monte 3–4 of ~12 pitches OK (Nov 2021), boulders; San Bartolomeo ~50 min approach, lower sector all impractical (rusty), upper sector near ferrata 15 pitches, 12 climbable, stainless bolts but rusty chains; upper faces north, ~400 m asl; take 103 then 103a, ignore old sign to lower sector; Fetovaia stainless resin bolts except sector 1 (only Tempest 6b+ resin), top-rope possible; La Freccia unfrequented, rusty, lichen (Nov 2021).
- SiViaggia (editorial, secondary) https://siviaggia.it/idee-di-viaggio/falesie-isola-delba/235753/: San Bartolomeo ~40 min from Chiessi, "esposta a sud, per cui si può arrampicare anche in inverno, se la giornata è poco ventosa"; Madonna del Monte ok in summer, shaded zones.
- Blu Navy timetable page https://www.blunavytraghetti.com/orari/: "Oltre otto tratte al giorno da Piombino a Portoferraio sia in estate che in inverno"; average 1h00, 13 miles; check-in 15 min (foot) / 30 min (vehicle).
- Toremar https://www.toremar.it/: routes Piombino–Portoferraio, Cavo, Rio Marina; call centre 800 304035.
- Moby https://www.moby.it/: ferries to Isola d'Elba.
- Trenitalia Piombino Marittima link https://www.trenitalia.com/it/regionale/collegamenti-regionale/piombino-marittima-link.html: Piombino Marittima station on the passenger port; train to Piombino Marittima or to Campiglia Marittima + bus link; bus stop in front of Campiglia station; combined ticket; Elba Link train+ferry to Portoferraio, Rio Marina, Cavo.
- Autolinee Toscane line 116 https://www.at-bus.it/it/linee-e-orari/livorno-extraurbano-116: Portoferraio–Marciana–Pomonte–Marina di Campo–S.Ilario, stops include Marciana, Chiessi, Pomonte, Seccheto, Fetovaia; "Servizio regolare".
- Parco Nazionale Arcipelago Toscano regulations https://www.islepark.it/ente-parco/normativa/regolamenti: list of regulations (fauna damage, sanctions, filming, fishing, research…), none on climbing.
- SAST https://www.sast.it/richiedi-aiuto/: call 112 (tel links 112/118) and ask for CNSAS.
- Versante Sud Toscana e Isola d'Elba https://www.versantesud.it/it/negozio/catalogue/toscana-isola-elba_1650/: Giannetti 2025, ISBN 978 88 55471 244, 672 pp, Italian, 116 crags incl. "l'incantevole Isola d'Elba".
- Versante Sud Toscana Pareti https://www.versantesud.it/it/negozio/catalogue/toscana_1379/: 221 multi-pitch Apuane–Argentario, 2021, out of stock (no Elba mention in description).
