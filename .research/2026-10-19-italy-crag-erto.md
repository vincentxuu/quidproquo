# Research notes: Erto — 2026-10-19

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Erto (EN) https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/erto.html: Italy > Friuli Venezia Giulia > Pordenone > Erto; difficulty 4a–9a; height 100; 81 routes listed; limestone; orientation SE; rope 70 m; "long been known as one of the hardest crags in Italy", The big Mother 8c+ (Luca Zardini); routes need power and stamina, rock "extremely polished"; No big sector a few hundred metres away, slab routes 4a–7a+; access A27 Mestre–Belluno exit Longarone, then right for Erto, Val Cellina, Pordenone, crag after tunnels and Vajont dam; hotels in Longarone; "Climbing is possible all year round as the overhang protects against the rain", autumn/spring best, sunny winter days; bibliography Falesie delle Dolomiti Bellunesi; route list incl. La capra 6b, Saten 7a+, W le donne 6c+, La cassana 7b, Tramonto Rosso 7a, Pop-pon-pin 7c, R.E.M. 7b (All. 7c+), Poltergeist 8a+, Joker 8a, Il ritorno di Ringo 8b, Sperandio 6b+, Brooklin 6c, Sandriccio 7c+, Erto express 8a/b, Mano di clown 6c, Cassiopea 7c, La conchiglia 8a, Bricolage 8a+, Sogni di gloria 8b+, The last way 8c+, The big mother 8c+, Pole position 8a (first onsight Guido Kostermaier when graded 7c+), Jurassic climb 8b, Tucson 8a, Lucrezia Borgia 7b+ (All. 7c), Pip crash 6c+ (All. 7a+), Contessa 7a, Principessa 7b, Alien 6b+, Matha Hari 6b, Pensionati 6a+, Molina 6a, Pipistrelli 6b, Passera scopaiola 6c, Il telefono 6b+; related news: 2018-06-21 Ghisolfi onsighted Tucson 8a, Bricolage 8a+, Super Pole Position 8a+ and did Jurassic Climb 8b+ on first visit; 2009-04-21 Adam Ondra FA "La grande linea dei sogni" 8c+/9a; 2009-06-10 Zardini first repeat; 2009-04-02 Zardini FA "La linea dei Sogni" 8c+ (14/03/2009), extension of Sogni di Gloria
- Planetmountain Erto (IT) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/erto.html: "uno dei più famosi terreni di scontro e confronto per i 'big'"; The big Mother 8c+ by Luca "Canon" Zardini, repeats Cristian Brenna, Christian Bindhammer; athletic, endurance; "svasi, piattoni e 'l'unto' delle vie più ripetute"; No big right of the big overhangs, from III to 7a+, grey rock mostly slabby; Mauro Corona first saw potential; "Ragazzi dello zoo di Erto": Sandro Neri, Icio Dall'Omo, Gigi dal Pozzo; autumn/spring ideal, sunny winter days; "occhio all'umidità"; train to Longarone: Padova–Calalzo and Treviso–Calalzo lines; from Pordenone/Udine via Valcellina, Barcis, Cimolais; "In prevalenza strapiombante"
- Planetmountain Erto No Big (IT) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/erto-no-big.html: Big sector: with rain, huge roofs protect climbing; No Big created by Mauro Corona for medium-low grades, "quasi un centinaio di vie"; visible right of main sector; Placche numerate: technical on grey compact limestone, spit, old homemade hangers on first routes; small overhanging sector above, 70 m rope and 14 quickdraws without intermediate anchor; a sector with "Quasi tutte sul grado 5"; a sector with short well-bolted routes "così che anche i bambini possano divertirsi"; bibliography Falesie della Valle del Piave (Climband Belluno, Edizioni Rocciaviva, 2006)
- falesia.it Erto (updated 05/06/2026) https://www.falesia.it/crag/231/erto.html: "Una delle falesie più conosciute d'Italia"; 3b–8c+ in three sectors (page summary 3b/9a); 210 routes; Big Area I 81 (6a/9a), Big Area II 34 (4a/8a+), No Big 95 (3b/7b); excellent resin bolts and fix, lower-off chains; "Arrampicata molto tecnica e spesso non molto intuitiva"; "Calcare giallo a piattoni, sovente unto"; altitude 500 m; climb September to May, summer afternoons after 17:00; exposure south; Big climbable on rainy days; No Big after heavy rain dry in 2 hours on windy summer days; from Udine via Spilimbergo, Maniago, Val Cellina, crag just before dam on right with parking; from Venice A27 to Longarone, right towards Pordenone/Vajont, over the Piave bridge, left, hairpins, tunnels before dam; most repeated: Stuntman (No Big) 13, Aria acida 9, La Bepi Manarin 8, Batman 8, Per i più piccini 8, Pensionati (Big Area II) 6, Pipistrelli (Big Area II) 5
- falesia.it No Big sector (updated 2026/06/05) https://www.falesia.it/sector/650/erto/no-big.html: 95 routes; exposure south-west; sector for less experienced; most frequented routes have polished ("unta") rock; route grades: Stuntman 4c 14 m (13 repeats), Shany 4c 13 m, Born to run 5a+ 10 m, Par te 5b 10 m, luna 5c 10 m, Simon 5c, Iua! 5a, Maris Bad Rock Day 5c+ 14 m, Gianna 6c+, N°9A 6c+
- theCrag Erto（Exa fetch）https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/dolomites/erto: 188 routes, 575 ascents; "Overhung Slab Vertical All day sun"; "huge yellow cave where the hard routes are countless" and No Big for everyone; Big Area 1 63, Big Area 2 36, No Big 89; no approach info
- Climbook Erto (Piccolo Eden, Big e No Big) https://climbook.com/falesie/9081/erto-piccolo-eden-big-e-no-big: Piccolo Eden new sector by Giuseppe Troso next to Big; historic sectors discovered by Mauro Corona in the 70s, then Icio Dall'Omo and Ragazzi dello Zoo; most repeated: La passera scopaiola 6c (12), Contessa 7a (11), Mata Hari 6b (8), Pipistrelli 6b+/6c (8), La Bepi Manarin 4a (8), Pensionati 6a+ (7), Pip Crash L1 6c/6c+ (7), Mate 6a (7), Molina 5c+ (6), Alien 6b+/6c (6)
- abcDOLOMITI Erto (2020-07-09) https://www.abcdolomiti.com/falesia/falesia-di-erto/: from Longarone to Vajont dam, after a few hundred metres the "grottone" on the left; Big (left, harder) and No Big (right, easier); "circa 120" routes from 3a (Per i più piccini) to 8c+ (Big Mother); 60 m or 70 m rope sometimes needed; new sector Piccolo Eden; info in Climband guide, €27
- guidedolomiti（Enrico Maioni）https://www.guidedolomiti.com/en/crags-in-cortina-dampezzo/: Erto not in Cortina area but historic milestone; many lines 8a–8c+; edges, slopers, polished holds; No Big "many easy and intermediate routes"; large overhangs allow climbing in rain in several sectors, No Big less protected; from Cortina SS51 to Longarone (~49 km), SS251 to Vajont dam; photo caption: Contessa "a classic route"
- Parco Naturale Dolomiti Friulane, Erto e Casso https://www.parcodolomitifriulane.it/en/i-paesi-del-parco/erto-e-casso/: climbing wall at Vajont dam born 1978; under the Moliesa rock "più o meno 300 vie" of every grade; hardest in central ochre part up to 8c (XI); easiest at far right numbered 1–18, first pitch max grade V
- Up-Climbing（2023-02-22）https://www.up-climbing.com/bimestrale/storia-di-copertina/i-ragazzi-dello-zoo-di-erto/: late 1970s overhang without bolts; at its base the scree of the Vajont landslide (9 October 1963); Mauro Corona bolted ground-up the logical lines Molina, Pensionati, Pipistrelli, La Passera
