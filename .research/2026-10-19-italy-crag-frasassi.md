# Research notes: Gola della Rossa / Gola di Frasassi (Falcioni, Monte Revellone) — 2026-10-19

Scope: limestone crags and multi-pitch routes in the Parco Naturale Regionale della Gola della Rossa e di Frasassi (Ancona, Marche): Sulfuria and the multi-pitch walls of the Gola di Frasassi, the Gola della Rossa walls (Paretone Oggioni, Placche), Falcioni and Monte Revellone, La Sbarra. Series 〈義大利岩場〉 order 1101.

## Sources opened

- Climb Europe Marches https://www.climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-italy-marches: largest crags Furlo, Cingoli, Falcioni, Gola di Frasassi, Pioraco, San Giorgio; over 60 limestone crags, typically single-pitch sport; Gola di Frasassi bolted multi-pitch up to 170 m, Falcioni up to 150 m; altitudes 300–700 m, many orientations; best early autumn to late spring; guidebook Calcare di Marca covers 65 crags.
- Planetmountain Gola di Frasassi – Sulfuria https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/gola-di-frasassi-sulfuria.html: area "conosciuta a livello alpinistico fin dal 1932"; "oltre 500 itinerari" (single pitch, boulders, multi-pitch, alpine routes); Sulfuria 34 routes 4a to 7c+/8a, faces east; access from San Vittore over the Roman bridge on the Sentino, path left (drinking fountain), wooden footbridge, cairn, 20 min; summer afternoons ideal, shade from 13:00; in winter sun arrives late (9:00 on Liscioni, ~10:00 rest), shade from 12:00; first Frasassi Rock Climbing Festival 7–9 June 2013; sector lists Maestro Rampichino (10–15 m: Banana boat 5b …), Centrale (30–35 m: Amarilla 6a, Lunetta 6a+, In mia Faria 6b, Little Boy 6b+, Naxxramas 6c, Ice Crown 6c+, Arancia Meccanica 7a, Sax Appeal 7a+, Welcome into the Big Wall 7b+, Russian Roulette 7c, Excalibur 7c+), I Liscioni (15–20 m: Marcellus Augustus 6a, Il Demiurgo 6a+, L'aquila e la Fenice 7c+).
- theCrag Sulfuria https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/area/517709952: 38 routes; "A great cliff for summer afternoons"; approach 10–20 min; developed by climbers from Ancona and Jesi (Romagnoli brothers, Mosca, Piacenza).
- Climbook Sulfuria https://climbook.com/falesie/7951/gola-di-frasassi-sulfuria: 39 routes, 1,073 repetitions (data Aug 2019); most repeated Amarilla 6a (73), Lunetta 6a+ (62), In mia faria 6b (59), Naxxramas 6c (58), Icecrown 6c+ (50), Little boy 6b+ (48; 6b+/6c elsewhere), Il Demiurgo 6a+ (46), Marcellus Augustus 6a (41), Arancia meccanica 7a (38).
- Vertical-Life / 8a.nu Frasassi https://8a.nu/areas/italy/frasassi/crags: 517 routes in DB, 15 crags incl. Sulfuria, Chez Maxim, Altromondo, La Sbarra, Falcioni, Moai, Falesia del Santuario.
- Climbook Gola della Rossa area https://climbook.com/aree/651/gola-della-rossa: crag list incl. Falcioni (Triangolo, Grottone, Pilastro Centrale) 67 routes, Falcioni Anfiteatro multipitch 43, Gola di Frasassi Multipitch 53, La sbarra 23 (596 repetitions), Sulfuria 39.
- Climbook Falcioni https://climbook.com/falesie/2421/falcioni-triangolo-grottone-pilastro-centrale: 67 routes, 398 repetitions; most repeated Fantasie di primavera 6a+ (26), Dritto per dritto 5a (20), Mai a sinistra 6a (prop. 5c+, 17), Armonia 5c (16), Ombra del dubbio 6c (prop. 6b+, 16), Via di noi L1 4c (16), Il fachiro Casimiro 6b (15).
- Climbook La Sbarra https://climbook.com/falesie/22211/la-sbarra: 23 routes, 596 repetitions; Skywalker 5c (45), Spigolandia 6a (45), Kartoffeln a colazione 5c (40), Rotta su marotta 6b+ (prop. 6b, 32).
- theCrag Falcioni https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/area/517709727: 34 routes; sectors Triangolo, Grottone, Pilastro Centrale (33 routes, 29 m), Anfiteatro (alpine, 300 m).
- Climbing Spot Factory, Lorenzo Rossetti "The Cliff of Falcioni" (2019) https://climbingspotfactory.com/en/article/lorenzo.rossetti/the-cliff-of-falcioni: SW face of Monte Revellone; first classic ascents 1930s (CAI); 1980s lines up to ~7a; late-90s expansion bolts; ~20 years later rebolting by CAI mountain schools → glue-in bolts in the first area; "dozens of 6a/6b level pitches… especially for the beginners"; re-equipping not complete; Il Triangolo (leftmost) has spaced bolts; hard pitches often short bouldery; "In summer the spot is fresh in the morning and in winter it is hot in the afternoon"; polished holds on easy lines.
- CAI Perugia / Scuola La Fenice "Compendio delle vie MultiPitch nel Parco Gola della Rossa e Frasassi" https://www.caiperugia.it/attachments/693_Gola%20di%20Frasassi%20e%20Gola%20della%20Rossa.pdf: Monte Revellone routes start just after Falcioni village near a fountain, ~30 min walk; Fata morgana 115 m IV/V+/A1/VI; Al ballo mascherato delle celebrità 120 m spit S2, obbl. 6a, 6c/5c/6b+/7b or A1; Raggio di luna 140 m A2 V+/VI (7c free), original protection; Gola della Rossa: exit before the tunnel, old road now closed, park near a house before the no-access signs; Paretone Oggioni 15–20 min; La Regola II S1 max 8a+ obbl. 6c+ (6a+, 6b+, 6c+ e A1/8a+, 6b+, trees); Il mercante di appigli S2+ max 7a obbl. 6b+ (6b, 7a, 6c, 6b, 6a, 6a); Badiali Conti R1 max VI obbl. - Parco Gola della Rossa e Frasassi, Regolamento del Parco (June 2022) https://www.parcogolarossa.it/doc-normative/dmdocuments/REG_PARCO_30-06-2022.pdf: art. 24 c. 4.1 "L'accesso alle pareti è sempre vietato in determinate aree": Valle Scappuccia (whole valley); Gola di Frasassi left hydrographic side within mapped zones (Allegato 2/a); Valle del Vernino left orographic wall (2/b, "Valle del Vernino – Monte Revellone"); Monte Scoccioni, La Costa (2/c); Albacina, S–SW wall below La Rocchetta above the old quarry (2/d); Allegato 4 lists all rock routes incl. those in permanent bans and "sottoposte a limitazioni periodiche"; Park may vary ban zones after monitoring; art. 27 c. 2.3 access "consentito esclusivamente lungo le vie di roccia di cui all'elenco riportato nell'Allegato n. 4"; c. 3.1 only stainless/titanium protection on sport routes, no drilled aid, opening/reopening routes needs Park Nulla Osta; motor vehicles banned on minor roads.
- Unione Montana dell'Esino Frasassi annex index https://www.halleyweb.com/unmoesifra/zf/index.php/servizi-aggiuntivi/index/index/idtesto/4: Regolamento aggiornato giugno 2022 (Delibera n.18 del 30/06/2022); Allegati 2.a–2.d "Aree divieto accesso pareti di roccia", Allegato 4 "Elenco vie di roccia e falesie".
- Parco Gola della Rossa, Normative https://www.parcogolarossa.it/normative/: links to the Regolamento and annexes.

- parks.it services https://www.parks.it/parco.goladellarossa.frasassi/ser.php: "in alcune zone l'attività alpinistica è regolamentata per evitare disturbo ai rapaci che nidificano sulle pareti… consultare la sezione normative".
- e-borghi Frasassi Climbing Festival https://www.e-borghi.com/en/events/serra-san-quirico-it/frasassi-climbing-festival: 27–30 Aug 2026; launched 2015; contributed to restoring historic crags and creating new ones.
