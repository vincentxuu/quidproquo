# Research notes: Gallura 花崗岩抱石（La Cerra／Monte Pulchiana、Arzachena／Lu Branu、Luogosanto、Capo Testa） — 2026-10-19

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Boulder Gallura (IT) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/boulder-gallura-sardegna.html: Gallura granite boulders "infestata", especially eastern part; Olbia airport and port; all spots within one hour by car from Olbia; bases: agriturismo Lu Branu for Arzachena sectors, agriturismo La Cerra for Monte Pulchiana (Tempio Pausania); link to La Sportiva Gallura bloc guide (issuu).
- La Sportiva "Bouldering guide to Gallura" PDF (Sardinia Bloc Scouting 2013, hosted by lacrux.com) https://www.lacrux.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/Topo-Bouldern-Sardinien_Guidebook.pdf: areas Arzachena – Parco Giochi De Li Conchi, Arzachena – Lu Branu, La Cerra, Tempio Pausania – Monte Pulchiana (Prato SX, Prato DX, Grottini, Nuraghe), extra Fessura di Monte Mazolu; Lu Branu "in the private grounds of the Branu Lu farmhouse"; Lu Branu boulder 1 A 8a+ "climbed by Nalle Hukkataival", hardest of the Scouting; access Lu Branu: SS125 toward Palau, km 348 after Arzachena, signs for farmhouse; La Cerra: SS127 → SP10 Luogosanto → SS133 left, 1 km, boulders 5 min walk from farm; owner Alberto Pesenti; Monte Pulchiana areas "located on private land to which access is allowed, but courteous behaviour is required at all times"; Monte Pulchiana boulder 3 "3 grappini" A 8a highball, only Hukkataival; boulder 4 "Il Diamante" A 7c vertical crimps; Parco Giochi: SP115 toward Bassacutena 5–6 km from Arzachena, park roadside, climb over a gate, follow cairns; grades written lowercase (Font context).
- Planetmountain Sardinia Bloc news (2013-11-14) https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/sardinia-bloc-new-boulder-problems-in-sardinia.html: 70 problems cleaned and climbed, 5a to 8a+, four areas: two around Arzachena and two beneath Monte Pulchiana.
- Planetmountain Sardinia Bloc Scouting full report (Pietro Dal Prà, 2013-12-17) https://planetmountain.com/en/news/events/sardinia-bloc-scouting-the-full-report.html: over 150 problems in five days, fifth grade to 8a, four areas; landowners happy to help; strong winds and heavy rain, climbed 6 of 9 days (November).
- Agriturismo La Cerra bouldering https://agriturismolacerra.it/en/bouldering-climbing: 9 sectors, 100+ blocks, 10–30 min walk; crash pads for rent; guidebook "Bouldering La Cerra" (Iva and Felix); history from 2013 La Sportiva Sardinia Bloc Scouting; Monte Pulchiana "largest granite monolith in Europe" (owner's claim); rope routes Pochaontas (4 pitches, bolted), Arcu de Chelu (6 pitches, rebolted by Maurizio Oviglia); Monte Tuvu 20 min by car, 30+ routes.
- Agriturismo La Cerra blog history https://agriturismolacerra.it/en/blog/agritourism-la-cerra/bouldering-la-cerra: November 2013 Sardinia Bloc Scouting; ~150 lines 5th to 8a+, four areas, two in Arzachena two in Tempio Pausania.
- SardegnaTurismo Monte Pulchiana https://sardegnaturismo.it/en/explore/monte-pulchiana: "largest granite monolith in Sardinia", Tempio Pausania territory, nearest village Aggius; inselberg; "the Sardinian Regional Authority declared it a natural monument in 1994"; favourite destination for climbers; access from Tempio SS133 toward Palau, km 10.7 dirt track.
- climb-europe La Cerra https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-sardinia-olbia-la-carra: La Cerra "best place to go bouldering in northern Sardinia", rough reddish granite, "over 200 documented problems … Fb 2's to Fb 7b+", best autumn and spring; La Cerra Bouldering Guidebook; Capo Testa mixture of sport routes and bouldering; Parco Giochi near Arzachena and Luogosanto other significant areas.
- As We Flow (2024-09-02) https://asweflow.it/2024/09/02/bouldering-in-sardinia-what-you-need-to-know: best months spring (Mar–May), autumn (Sep–Nov) and especially December; climb year-round except hottest months; tafoni; Luogosanto discovered by Filippo Manca, smooth granite, good when mistral blows, V0 to V13 (first of its kind on island, Gabriele Moroni); guidebook Pietra di Luna end Oct 2022, 31 locations; local collective "Primitive Boulder".
- 27crags (thetopo) Lu Branu https://27crags.com/crags/lu-branu: 73 boulders; "Beautiful Boulder area in the wood of agriturismo lu branu private land"; approach from agriturismo parking, wooden gates; route list https://27crags.com/crags/lu-branu/routelist: Mincammino 6C+ (10 ascents) visible; most grades Premium-hidden.
- thetopo La Cerra (Keenable snippet + curl 200 of routelist) https://thetopo.com/crags/la-cerra: 82 boulders.
- theCrag Luogosanto https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/sardinia/capo-testa/area/5300513910: 22 boulders FB 4C–7B+; "Always shady, Wet in rain"; cork oak forest; free topo bloccobirra.com; access issues "Designated — no fires, no camping, don't leave waste"; park at small car park in "Parco di Mònti Castéddu" at west end of Luogosanto, <5 min; Block 12 5c ★★★, Block 16 4c ★★★; Agriturismo Concasoni crashpad rental.
- InfoBoulder Luogosanto https://www.infoboulder.com/?mod=boulders&area=250: ~100 blocks, 4 to 7c–8a, granite, 375 m, all seasons; Parco delle tre cime–Monte Juanne; Rock&Walls contest May 2015; "Divieto di accendere fuochi e di rimuovere eventuali muretti a secco sotto i boulder in quanto sito archeologico, divieto di campeggio"; contact Filippo Manca.
- climbingitaly Luogosanto https://climbingitaly.com/sardegna/luogosanto/: Bouldering, Shade all day, 5 min approach, Sheltered from all winds, Summer crag; 79 routes listed: Basfemia in Luogosanto 7a; Patagarroso 8b; Bette papassino 7c; Il pentito di Luogosanto 6b; La bella energia 5c; Lo squalo 7b; The wave under the shark 7a+; Altri concetti 8a+.
- 8a.nu Luogosanto https://www.8a.nu/crags/bouldering/italy/luogosanto/routes: 28 boulders, 65 ascents; Blasfemia a Luogosa 7A (13 ascents); La Grotta del Pentito 8A (5); Patagarroso 8B (5); Altri Concetti 8A (4); Lo Squalo 7B (4); Bette papassino 7C (3); Concasoni 6C; Smile 6A; Cavallo Pazzo 5B.
- Planetmountain Luogosanto Rock & Walls (Filippo Manca, 2015) https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/luogosanto-rock-walls-and-the-bouldering-in-sardinia.html: first Rock & Walls 9–10 May 2015 launched the area (title/lead only read).
- climbingitaly Monte Pulchiana https://climbingitaly.com/sardegna/monte-pulchiana: 7 multi-pitch routes, Sun all day, Winter crag; Grandi laghi 6a 260 m (Azzalea e Beuchod 1981; 5b,5a,5a,2,5a,4,6a); Pochaontas 6a+ 120 m (2000; 5c,6a,4,6a+, the only BOLTED); Aperitivo con le capre 5b 110 m (2014); Sete Satanica 6b 85 m (2014); Spiffero del diavolo 4 180 m (1984).
- theCrag Capo Testa https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/sardinia/capo-testa/area/3096830247: 52 routes FR 3a–7c; Cala Poltusinu crag 17 routes (Warning); La Turri / Parete della Luna 18 routes 110 m; guidebooks: "Bouldering in Sardinia" (Oviglia, Manca, Soddu, 2022, ISBN 9788889661451, 31 areas); "La Cerra Bouldering Guidebook" (Iva Mirastschijski & Felix Clotten, 2021, ISBN 9783945271704; Alberto landowner of some boulders); icons Fluttuazioni 5c ★★★, Spigolo 6c+ ★★★.
