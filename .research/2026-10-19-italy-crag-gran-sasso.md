- SummitPost Corno Piccolo https://www.summitpost.org/corno-piccolo/288292: 2655 m, third summit of the range; "some of the hardest and beautiest climbing routes of all central-southern Italy"; three faces (north smooth, east vertical "very dolomitic", south-west broken, normal route); access via Prati di Tivo (A24 exit S.
