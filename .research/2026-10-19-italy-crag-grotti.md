# Research notes: Grotti — 2026-10-19

Crag: Grotti (frazione of Cittaducale, Rieti, Lazio). Series 義大利岩場 order 1203. All pages opened 2026-10-19.

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Grotti crag page https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/grotti.html: "The great crag in central Italy" (photo caption); 230 routes; 4c – 9a/a+; height 40m; orientation S; rope 70m; rock "Magnificent pocketed limestone. The left-hand sector transforms into conglomerate limestone"; "Well-placed glued-in resin bolts"; new sector La Curva with 70 new lines, many routes at Grotti Classica and Grotti Alta rebolted; "Grotti remains enormous and you'll never have to queue for a route"; pockets painfully sharp, rest days obligatory; Ferentillo "a mere 40 minutes from Rieti"; roughly 100km NNE of Rome; from Rieti follow signs for Aquila, Valle del Salto, Grotti after 8km; park discreetly on the left near a hunting shop, walk past a chicken pen (10mins); from the south leave A24 at Valle del Salto; no campsites nearby, campers can sleep in Piazza delle Feste 100m from centre; "It gets hot in Grotti, so go in winter, late autumn or early spring"; "It is possible to climb in light rain"; grade spread La Curva total 63 (5c+:2, 6c+:17, 7c+:33, 8a+:11), Grotti Alta total 155 (2/31/81/41); bibliography "Arrampicare in Abruzzo" by Sergio Di Renzo called "most updated guidebook"; Grotti Alta Paretone route list: Caffe atomico 7a, Karacat 6c+, Karajad 7a, Aragorn 7a+, Bombadill 7b, Grigio pirla 7a+.

- Climbook Grotti (Grotti Alta) https://climbook.com/falesie/23/grotti-grotti-alta: park right after the Grotti village sign, path near a chicken pen, few minutes to Iniziazione; "calcare solido tendente al conglomerato, prevalentemente a buchi, con stile atletico e spesso violento e traumatico"; bolted mainly by Toffa, Finocchi, Ciato, Mati, Smargiassi; exposure south/south-east; Iniziazione and Nirvana typically winter sectors facing south; Paretone, Cengia di Jacopo, Mano rossa S/SE, in summer shade arrives in the afternoon; routes toward Eu and X-Files in shade by 15–15:30 in summer; mid-season shade after 14, hottest months climb after 17 on dry days, avoid hot humid days; Erotica right side (Cattivi di natura, Etica patetica) faces more east, shade from 15 in summer; most repeated: Guardia rossa 6a 225, Guardia nera 5c 202, Jolly fan 6a+ 193, Provaci 5c 190 (diedro), Gladiatori 6b 181, Àncora 6a+ 177, Gladio 6a+ 175, Mondo meschino 6c 175, Il nervoso 5b 171.
- falesia.it Grotti - Alta https://www.falesia.it/crag/3/grotti-alta.html: "una delle più belle falesie laziali"; "Pochi gradi facili, non adatta a principianti"; from Rome Salaria to Rieti, statale 578 for Avezzano about 7 km where the road runs beside a limestone wall (one of the Grotti sectors), Iniziazione 2 km further; Il Deposito from first small road after first house on the left of the village; "Chiodatura eterna a fittoni resinati"; space under the wall suits families; N. vie 161; 3c+/8c; altitude 520 m; "Calda, molto calda"; frequentabile tutto l'inverno or spring/autumn evenings in shade.
- Alpinisti d'Appennino Falesia di Grotti Alta (2023-12-01) https://www.alpinistidappennino.it/2023/12/01/falesia-grotti-alta/: calcare-conglomerato with pockets, needs finger tendon strength; most sectors south, some south-east going into shade in the afternoon; bolts "fittoni inox e fix da 10 mm"; difficulty 5a–8c; exposure S and SE; approach 8 min; altitude 490 m; period "Da novembre a marzo"; "Si scala con la pioggia: No"; road access from Rome SS4 Salaria to Rieti Est, SR578 for Avezzano ~7 km, Grotti Alta a few km further at first houses; from Abruzzo A24 exit Valle del Salto, SR578 toward Rieti, exit Grotti/Villa Grotti; route list Er cozza 6a+, Jolly fan 6a+, Provaci 5c, Gloria 5a, Fioro 5a, Snoby 5a, Il nervoso 5b, Guardia nera 5c, Guardia rossa 6a, Gladio 6a+, Gladiatori 6b, Diapositore 6b+, Storie tese 7c, Mai più senza 8a+.
- Climbing Spot Factory Grotti Alta https://www.climbingspotfactory.com/en/climbadvisor/grotti_alta/info_about: big cliff of conglomerate with pockets; "almost never very technical, but you need to be powerful"; "Most of the routes of high difficulty presents handholds chipped"; altitude 450 m; approach 10'; good for children yes; route via A1 Fiano Romano, SS4 Salaria, SR578 Cicolana, past Casette, after a bridge turn left and park for Grotti La Curva and new sector; for Grotti Alta and Deposito continue to first houses and park at the "Grotti" board.
- Visit Rieti sport climbing https://www.visitrieti.com/sport-climbing/: Grotti "between Rieti and the Salto Valley"; "very compact rock, with tiny holes and dents"; "The routes' equipment is excellent, but it is not recommended during the summer season due to its southern exposure".
- Anima Reatina https://animareatina.it/en-gb/body: "Falesia di Grotti in Cittaducale, among the most beautiful crags in Latium".
- Oliunid Climbing at Grotti (archive article 26 October 2022) https://oliunid.com/blog/climbing-grotti-historic-routes-arrampicaroma-nord: municipality of Cittaducale, overlooks Valle del Salto; "a landmark of sport climbing in Lazio and central Italy"; popular winter-weekend destination since the late 1990s alongside Ferentillo; in 2022 reports of rebolting and approach improvements; Innocenti calls Grotti the embodiment of hard climbing; El Grinta in Iniziazione; small pockets load fingers, warm up gradually; not every route ends within twenty metres, check rope; Ultimo Tango a Zagarolo bolted by Alfredo Smargiassi, January 2020 Elias Iagnemma first ascent proposed 9a/+; Alto Gradimento 8a about 35 m in some topos; Assalto Frontale 8a+ in Muro dell'Inverosimile sector; ArrampicaRoma Nord covers Grotti.
- Oliunid Italian version https://www.oliunid.it/blog/arrampicata-a-grotti-tra-vie-storiche-e-nuove/: same facts; Innocenti quote «il duro per antonomasia, uno dei templi dell'alta difficoltà nel Lazio».
- Planetmountain Iagnemma news (January 2020) https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/elias-iagnemma-completes-historic-grotti-project-ultimo-tango-a-zagarolo.html: first ascent of 20-year-old project; suggested 9a/+; "hardest route at the crag and in Central Italy"; bolted by Alfredo Smargiassi; Grotti "circa 90 minutes NE of Rome"; Lamberti tried it for a decade; Iagnemma splits it as 8b+ roof boulder plus 8a continuity.
