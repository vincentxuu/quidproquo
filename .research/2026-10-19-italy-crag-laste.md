# Research notes: Laste（Rocca Pietore, Agordino）— 2026-10-19

## Sources opened

- DMO Dolomiti Bellunesi, Falesia Laste（Exa fetch）https://www.visitdolomitibellunesi.com/it/cosa-fare/escursionismo/falesie-attrezzate/falesia-laste: "Più di 200 vie, dal grado 4 all'8c, sul calcare liscio e compatto della Marmolada"; summer crag; blocks Sass De Ròcia, Sass De La Murada, Sass de La Gusela, Sass de l'Aghinèl; except Aghinèl (overhanging routes) all require technique and footwork, "anche le vie di grado basso possono risultare severe al primo approccio"; view of Civetta NW face; summit of Sass de Ròcia via equipped path to bivacco Pian de le Stelle
- CAI Lo Scarpone, cinque falesie Dolomiti orientali https://www.loscarpone.cai.it/dettaglio/fuga-dal-caldo-cinque-falesie-per-arrampicare-nelle-dolomiti-orientali/: "probabilmente la falesia più emblematica delle Dolomiti Venete"; compact limestone, technical slabs, footwork; at 1500 m; four sectors with different aspects, some still being developed; "non rappresenta un'ottima idea per chi arrampica da poco: le vie sono nel loro complesso impegnative"; mostly vertical, some overhangs/bulges; crimps, balance
- Climbook Laste (Sass de la Gujela) https://climbook.com/falesie/1659-laste-sass-de-la-guesla: updated Aug 2020 (data May 2019); "Una delle falesie più belle delle Dolomiti, forse una delle più belle falesie italiane"; view of Civetta north face; long routes on smooth slabs or slightly overhanging crimps; 5c–8b+; from Laste di Sopra follow to Ronch, leave car, path for via ferrata; circular path round the crag ~20 min; climb on all sides, choose sun or shade; mountain crag, summer, late spring, early autumn; Gujela most repeated: Piccola vendetta lastesana 6b+ (7), No iè pi o sovegn de… 6a (6; 5c in older topo), King lizard 6a+ (6), Polvere di diamanti 6a+ (4), Dromedaria 7b (proposed 7a+/7b), Senza nome 1 L1 5a
- falesia.it Laste（Exa fetch; updated 30/09/2023）https://www.falesia.it/crag/958/laste.html: Laste di Sopra near Rocca Pietore; "vie per tutti i gusti"; access via Pordoi, Falzarego, Alleghe → Digonera; south of village take Laste di Sopra to the parking; walk the asphalt road to Ronch; path behind the fountain; 167 routes; 5a/8b+; altitude 1560 m; exposure all directions; sectors: Sass de la Gusela 9 (5c/6b+), Sass de la Murada north A 8 (7a+/7c), north B 10 (7a/7c+), Murada south 20 (6b/7b+), Sass de l'Aghinèl 23 (5a/8a+), Sass de Rócia east 17 (7a+/8b+), Rócia north 41 (5a/8a+), Rócia south 39 (6b/8b+)
- falesia.it Sass de Rócia north https://www.falesia.it/sector/2306/laste/sass-de-rcia-north.html: 41 routes; Merlin e la precision L1 6a+ 18 m; La chiave 7c 70 m; Passaggio a nord-ovest 7c 75 m; Il terzo incomodo 8a 85 m; Solstizio d'estate 7b 85 m; Trippa per gatti 7a+ 35 m; Il nano ghisato 7c 35 m; Cilum 7a 20 m; Via attraverso le pesche 5a 20 m; Ugo 5a 20 m
- falesia.it Sass de Rócia south https://www.falesia.it/sector/2308/laste/x.html: 39 routes; Austin Powers 7c 16 m; Ballando coi climbers 6c 25 m; Giada 7b 25 m; Perfetta 8a 24 m; Hortensio 8a+ 13 m; L'attimo 8b+ 35 m; Attenti al buco 8a+ 35 m; La mota che smuota 6b+ 30 m (3 repeats); Pippo alla tavola rotonda 6b 17 m
- falesia.it Sass de l'Aghinèl https://www.falesia.it/sector/2304/laste/x.html: 23 routes; Albero Ganealogico 5a 20 m; El te guzza 5b 20 m; Primero Citles 6a+ 22 m; Laste sfugenti 6b 18 m; Naneddu Meo 6c+ 18 m; Laste forever 7a+ 18 m; Bombetta al plastico 7c+ 22 m; Zapzarap 8a 30 m; Furore 8a 22 m; Gioiello 8a+ 30 m
- theCrag Laste（Exa fetch）https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/dolomites/laste: 188 routes, 185 ascents; Sass de Rócia 112 (31 m), Aghinèl 23 (23 m), Gusela 8 (20 m), Murada 45 (26 m); access issues: None
- Alpinisti d'Appennino（2026-08-12）https://www.alpinistidappennino.it/2026/08/12/falesia-laste/: near Caprile and Rocca Pietore at entrance of Val Pettorina; larch wood; Sass de Rocia ~100 m high boulder with routes on all sides (some 2nd/3rd pitches), three other smaller blocks; comfortable bases, sectors close together; vertical/slightly overhanging slab, crimps and slopers, easy routes very technical, hard ones finger endurance; bolting good with some runouts never dangerous; road: Caprile → Saviner di Laste → right to Digonera → hairpin left to Laste di Sopra → left to Ronch; several parking spots, last at the fountain; exposure all; period June–September; Gujela list incl. King lizard 6a+, Piccola vendetta lastesana 6b+
- La Rivolta ASD, Laste https://www.larivolta.it/falesie/laste: Comune Rocca Pietore; 215 routes; 6a–8c; altitude 1550; all aspects; approach 10 min; from over 120 routes in 2005 to over 200; "si raccomanda il massimo rispetto dei luoghi"; small sectors sometimes hundreds of metres apart; four main blocks; ruins of a watchtower on Sas de la Murada destroyed ~1390; bivacco Pian delle Stelle via short ferrata; summer crag, south faces possible in other seasons
- La Rivolta, Guida https://www.larivolta.it/guida: second edition May 2019, adds Caprile, Abisso de Dea (Garés), Rifugio Scarpa; boulder areas made unusable by storm Vaia (end 2018) not included; 448 pages, 950+ pitches, 22 crags incl. Laste; Italian–English; €30; online via Libreria Campedel; first edition 2015 "Falesie e aree boulder dell'Agordino - Dolomiti" sold out
- Outdooractive / Consorzio Turistico Marmolada Rocca Pietore Dolomiti https://www.outdooractive.com/it/route/arrampicata/rocca-pietore/falesia-laste/52883838/: Marmolada limestone, compact and smooth; slab to overhang; "Arrampicata di aderenza, delicata"; Sass de Rocia south face climbable on mild winter days; by bus from Belluno (58 km to Laste) with Dolomiti Bus
- Dolomiti Bus, linee extraurbane invernale 2026 https://dolomitibus.it/it/linee-extraurbane-invernale-2026: line 4 Caprile–Pescul/Sottoguda/Laste; quadro integrato Belluno–Agordo–Falcade/Caprile–Arabba valid from 12/10
