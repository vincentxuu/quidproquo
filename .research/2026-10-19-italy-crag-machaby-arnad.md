# Research notes: Corma di Machaby / Arnad — 2026-10-19

Scope: Paretone (multi-pitch), Pilastro Lomasti, Gruviera (single pitch + short multi), Corno di Machaby (sport crag above). Groundlane MCP worked; lovevda, Climbook, 27crags/thetopo, Versante Sud via curl; theCrag and UKC via Exa; Planetmountain reopening article via jina.

## Sources opened

- lovevda Paretone https://www.lovevda.it/it/banca-dati/7/arrampicata/arnad/palestra-di-arrampicata-paretone/2645: historic wall marking passage from classic alpine to modern sport routes; appreciated in mid-seasons by climbers from beyond the Alps; first routes climbed in boots with pegs, now modernised but bolt spacing kept not too close; period April–October, "In estate può risultare troppo caldo"; exposure S–SE; start Champagnola – Arnad; height 250 m; access from Verrès motorway exit, past Arnad, after an overpass park on the left, path opposite the car park; slabby friction and balance on long routes taking half a day or more; requires technical experience, guide advised; belays with chains and rings; descent on foot east side or path north side, possible second car at Machaby car park; abseil discouraged (disturbs climbers below); grades 5c–7a, average 6a; 41 routes + 6 in Case Fara sector; average 200 m, min 35 m, max 280 m.
- lovevda Gruviera https://www.lovevda.it/it/banca-dati/7/arrampicata/arnad/palestra-di-arrampicata-la-gruviera/2644: historic, "probabilmente la prima chiodata in Valle d'Aosta"; short access; compact well-worked rock keeps its friction; period April–October, in summer only in the cooler hours; exposure south; height 110 m; access Verrès exit, past Arnad, after overpass turn left to Osteria Arcaden, park, crag 50 m in front; big corners and slabs, cracks and holes; mostly technical, many routes ideal for first steps; bolting very good, all routes recently re-equipped with stainless; watch rope length on longer routes; lower-off with 60 m rope, abseil for multi-pitch; grades 5b–7b+, average 6a; 34 routes; average 30 m, min 18, max 110 m.
- UKC Logbook Corma di Machaby https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/corma_di_machaby-12447: 54 climbs; gneiss; 563 m a.s.l.; faces SW; 1970s Turin climbers looked for alternatives to Rocca Sbarua, Cristalliera, Parete dei Militi, names Traversella and Machaby became known; "big slab requiring a grip climbing… small holes and small holds"; "There a NO EASY routes on it"; "ATTENTION: Lot of differences between grads indicated on Mani Nude 1 and the very harder reality!"; access on old SS26 between Donnas and Arnad; exit Verrès (from Aosta) or Pont-Saint-Martin (from Turin/Milan); restaurant below the wall, crowded on Sundays, big parking; approach 20–25 min; last updated 16/Oct/2026.
- Courmayeur Mountain Guides Machaby https://guidecourmayeur.com/en/scheda_valle.php?id_n=&id_prg=52&id_stag=1: "250m wall of perfect gneiss rock" in lower Aosta Valley, visible from motorway near Arnad; 70 km from Courmayeur, 90 from Torino; "perfect spot for rock climbing for the spring"; Gruviera perfect for single pitches; private guide 1 person €450, 2 people €500.
- Planetmountain 2012 closure https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/chiusura-della-falesia-di-arrampicata-la-corma-di-machaby-arnad.html: Friday 27 April 2012 mayoral act closed "La Corma di Machaby", owned by Comunità Montana dell'Evançon, after a serious accident on 21 April 2012; sectors Pilastro Lomasti, Paretone, La Gruviera, Case Fara (Opera Rock, Mitico Vento), La Paretina (near car park by SS26).
- Planetmountain 2012 reopening https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/riapertura-di-la-corma-di-machaby-ad-arnad.html: 18 May 2012; ordinance n. 6 of 18/05/2012 of the Mayor of Arnad revoked ordinance n. 5/2012; all five sectors reopened.
