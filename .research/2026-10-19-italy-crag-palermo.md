## Sources opened

- visitsicily (official, published 2015-11-27, Ignazio Mannarano) https://www.visitsicily.info/en/climbing-and-dws-in-sicily/: Palermo favourites Schiavo, Valdesi, Mariella Crack'n Up, Preistoria, Anfiteatro on Monte Pellegrino and Bauso Rosso on Monte Gallo; Madonie Regional Nature Park: Monte d'Oro in Collesano, Rocca di Sciara in Caltavuturo; Nebrodi park: Rocche del Crasto, Rocca Calanna, Rocca Traura, "eagles and griffons hover undisturbed".
- theCrag Palermo area https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/central-and-southern-italy/sicily/area/4413607140: 499 routes, 1,311 ascents; "comparable to San Vito, not only sport but also trad and especially multi pitch"; sub: Bauso Rosso 105, Grotta degli Svizzeri 15, Monte Gallo area 13 (trad, 210m), Kemonia 11, Monte Pellegrino cliff 327 (1,142 ticks), Uqbar 10, Terrasini 17, Monti Calamigna; other sources Allclimb, ClimbAdvisor paid topos, climbing-sicily.com multipitch topos. Rockfax: Pellegrino & Monte Gallo.
- Mountain Project Palermo Area https://www.mountainproject.com/area/107611547/palermo-area: Pellegrino within city, >600 m, S–N several km, limestone walls every side; majority west side; 8 major crags; single & multi up to 200 m; "most important rock climbing area in southern Italy"; Monte Gallo north of Palermo rising from sea, walls up to 400m, similar to Dolomites; all routes multi-pitch, bolted up to 370 m.
