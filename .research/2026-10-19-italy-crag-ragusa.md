# Research notes: Ragusa／Modica（Cava d'Ispica、Cava Gisana 等峽谷） — 2026-10-19

範圍：拉古薩省（Ragusa、Modica、Ispica）的峽谷運攀。Cava d'Ispica（Wild、Pernamazzone）為主，Cava Gisana（Pandora、Umpa Lumpa）、Iaddinara、Timpa Rossa 帶過。

- Planetmountain Pernamazzone (IT) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/cava-d-ispica-pernamazzone.html: Esposizione Sud; access from Modica Sorda commercial area on SS114 toward Siracusa, roundabouts, dirt road into valley, park at third bend not blocking; walk past houses, down to riverbed (fence, pass on left), to Rifugio Pernamazzone, 10 min; three small sectors, names written at base.
- Planetmountain news (Flaccavento & Iurato, 2018-04-13) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/cava-ispica-arrampicata-a-ragusa-e-un-ritorno-alle-sue-origini.html: historic crag rebolted ("importante lavoro di richiodatura"); rusty chains, corroded bolts replaced; new routes added; Meccu "tra i più interessanti del settore"; final sector yellowish rock, small crusts can break; sun hot "anche in pieno inverno"; contributors incl. CUS Catania.
- Planetmountain Pandora (IT) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/pandora.html: born Nov 2009; Modica, via Sorda-Sampieri 8 km from "Moda Italia" roundabout toward Marina di Modica; park on clearing opposite old farmhouse leaving gate free; follow guardrail 300 m, enter Cava Gisana nature trail, "attraversare il terreno (tutto con il massimo rispetto)", red dots, 5 min; faces east, shade after midday, March–November; wall ~100 m wide, max 25 m, 41 routes 5a–8b, some need 13 quickdraws; routes: Pellecuccia 5a; One sound 5c+; La mia lei 6a+ ("Bellissima placca atletica da non perdere"); Polvere di luna 7c; Pandora 8a; Dragula 8b; Dragon trainer 8b.
- Planetmountain Umpa Lumpa (EN) https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/umpa-lumpa.html: 25 routes 5c–7b+, 18 m, compact limestone; from Modica toward Marina di Modica on Sorda-Sampieri road ~8 km, dirt track 400 m, park leaving room for farmers' vehicles; red-marked path into Cava Gisana, fixed rope, riverbed (care on rainy days); faces NE, shade from midday, ideal March–November.
- climb-europe Sicily https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-italy-sicily: Ragusa canyons, Cava d'Ispica and Modica main areas, "100's of single-pitch sport routes"; autumn to spring; Di Roccia di Sole only guidebook for Siracusa and Ragusa.
- UKC destination guide 2022 https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/destinations/sicily-14498: Modica listed under sport climbing (east Sicily); spring/autumn best; July–August too hot; rent a car.
- visitsicily https://www.visitsicily.info/en/climbing-and-dws-in-sicily: "The most well-known cliffs of Ragusa are Ambiguità, Laddinara, Umpa Lumpa, Pandora and Cava D'Ispica".
- lasiciliainrete Cava d'Ispica ITA080009 https://lasiciliainrete.it/en/directory-tangibili/listing/cava-dispica-ita080009: Natura 2000 SAC/ZSC, 947 ha, municipalities Modica, Ispica, Rosolini; chasmophytic cliff vegetation, refuge and nesting area for vertebrates.
- Comune di Ragusa linee extraurbane https://www2.comune.ragusa.it/viaggiare/autobus/extraurbana.html: AST (Ragusa–Modica–Ispica–Siracusa etc.); Etna Trasporti Ragusa–Catania airport; Interbus Catania–Siracusa.
- Trenitalia Sicilia servizi minimi https://www.trenitalia.com/content/dam/trenitalia/regionale/treni-garantiti/Regionale%20Sicilia_sito.pdf: line "Caltanissetta – Siracusa" includes Modica–Siracusa trains.
