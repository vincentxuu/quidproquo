# Research notes: Roccamorice (Parete dell'Orso) — 2026-10-19

Crag: Parete dell'Orso, Vallone di Santo Spirito, Roccamorice (Pescara, Abruzzo), Parco Nazionale della Majella. Series 義大利岩場 order 1302. All pages opened 2026-10-19.

## Sources opened

- Comune di Roccamorice Parete dell'Orso https://www.comune.roccamorice.pe.it/vivere_il_comune/luoghi/luogo_17.html: "una delle mete più iconiche e amate per l'arrampicata sportiva in Abruzzo"; compact limestone with holes and drops; norms extracted from "Ordinanza Sindacale nr. 2 del 12 febbraio 2020": follow Parco Nazionale della Maiella rules; no litter, no disturbing wildlife; no fires; "vietato pernottare, bivaccare, accamparsi, stazionare con tende, camper o roulotte nell'area circostante la falesia"; "vietato in maniera assoluta parcheggiare vetture nel settore della strada comunale di Santo Spirito a Maiella sottostante la Parete"; area below the wall exposed to rockfall (grazing and wild animals above), stay only for the time needed to climb; "E' obbligatorio l'utilizzo del caschetto protettivo" while climbing and at the base; dynamic ropes only; beginners and minors must be accompanied by experts; courses only by registered mountain guides (L. 6/1989, L.R. 86/1998), guides must notify the Comune; no altering bolts; no new routes without Comune permission; no signs on the wall; dry tooling forbidden; page "Ultimo aggiornamento: 02-01-2026".
- Montagna.TV (27 Aprile 2025) https://www.montagna.tv/255676/aperte-13-vie-a-roccamorice-tempio-del-climbing-nella-majella/: 13 new routes in sectors Ghaza and Linea Gotica; history from 1981 trad routes; historic bolters Rosica, Sulpizio, Bennato, Di Renzo, Persico, Di Federico, Rubini; decline from 2000, only first 50 routes maintained; from 2014 a group of young climbers rebolted all historic routes in certified stainless steel with personal funds and collections (Renzani, Costantini, Viola, Ferrini, Giordano, D'Anteo, Liberato, Adorante); Linea Gotica sector born 2018; 2025 additions 5c–7a Linea Gotica, 6a–7c+ Ghaza; key moves on hard routes not "azzerabili", falls not dangerous because walls steep; new routes up to 6b modern bolting, harder with obligatory sections; a few 1990s routes with chipped holds kept; "più di 300 vie distribuite in una dozzina di settori"; ~850 m; "dai quinti all'8c, con una via non ancora liberata che potrebbe essere di 9a"; first sector directly on the paved valley road, others higher via a path traversing the wall; mostly vertical or slightly overhanging; mainly south-facing, irregular, some recesses shaded on summer afternoons, perfect for mezze stagioni; in winter climb on sunniest days even with snow if walls dry; Sentieri Celestiniani to Eremi San Bartolomeo, Santo Spirito.
- Abruzzo Verticale Roccamorice (2020-11-27) https://www.abruzzoverticale.it/falesie/pescara/roccamorice/: grey compact pocketed limestone on slabs, yellow limestone on overhangs; "Con i suoi 2km di lunghezza, la Parete dell'Orso ... è tra le strutture più grandi d'Italia"; slight prevalence of technical slabs; 5A–8B, average 6C; bolting excellent on almost all routes, some lack the carabiner at the chain, bring a lanyard; sectors Giallon, Strapiombi, Placche Grigie, Cinghiale Bianco, Pancia d'Elefante, Placche Nere, La Poltrona, I Buconi, Gare, Ghaza, La Pecorella Smarrita; esposizione Sud; 900 m; ideal all year except sunless winter days; from Roccamorice follow Block House ~4 km, right toward Eremo di Santo Spirito, 1 km, park on the left near a fountain; Giallon first; 200 m further a small layby and path to Placche Grigie, Strapiombi left, others right (5–15 min); Pecorella Smarrita: 100 m past Giallon, climb over guardrail, 25 m abseil from a large U-bolt.
- Caramanico Terme natura (official tourism) https://www.caramanicotermenatura.it/it/pianifica/cosa-vedere/Parete-di-arrampicata-a-Roccamorice-poi146: Parete dell'Orso ~2 km single wall; "uno dei luoghi di arrampicata più famosi dell'Abruzzo"; A25 exit Caramanico, then Lettomanoppello, Roccamorice; Block House 4 km, right for Eremo di Santo Spirito, 1 km, park left by the fountain.
- theCrag Roccamorice https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/roccamorice: 215 routes; 4b–8b average 6c; one long wall with sectors along the road; "The sun is on the wall in the morning and in the shade during the evening except for sectors (Galon, Buconi, Ghaza, Plache)"; brown signs "Roccamorice Climbing School" from town; Giallon 51 routes 17m, Strapiombi 12, Placche Grigie 24, Cinghiale Bianco 17, Pancia d'Elefante 32.
- falesia.it Roccamorice https://www.falesia.it/crag/27/roccamorice.html: aggiornato 30/09/2023; N. vie 190; 4b/8a; 910 mt; esposizione sud; ~2 km "Parete dell'Orso"; bolting excellent almost everywhere, lower-off chain always present; sectors Cinghiale Bianco 11 (5c/7b+), Gare 8 (5b/7a), Ghaza 12 (6b+/7c), Giallon 44 (4b/8a), I Buchoni 11 (5a/7a), Il Terrazzo 6 (5a/6c), La Poltrona 8 (6b/7b), Pancia d'Elefante 29 (4c/8a), Placche Grigie 27 (5b/7b+), Placche Nere 20 (5a/7c+), Strapiombi 14 (4c/7c).
- UKClimbing Roccamorice (last updated October 16 2026) https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/roccamorice-25833: 204 climbs; limestone; 869 m; faces S; approach as above, layby for a couple of cars after Giallon; Pecorella Smarrita 25 m rappel over guardrail.
- Climbing Spot Factory Roccamorice https://climbingspotfactory.com/en/climbadvisor/roccamorice/info_about: heart of Majella national park, many hermitages; 1981 first trad routes; late 1980s guide Roberto Rosica and Claudio Sulpizio raised difficulty; 1990s Sergio di Renzo and Biase Persico; "biggest climbing site of the Abruzzo country, with around 250 routes"; access A25 exit Alanno/Scafa, SS5, signs San Valentino/Abbateggio/Roccamorice.
- Climbook Roccamorice (Giallon) https://climbook.com/falesie/340/roccamorice-giallon: "Aggiornamento dicembre 2024 (Fonte: Abruzzo Rock, ed. 2023)"; 1826 repetitions; most repeated Zig zag 5c 89, Silverado 5b 86, Avanzi 6a+ 77, Avantino 5b 77, Nikita 6a 74, Longa manus L1 6a 72, Tiramisù 5c (proposed 5b+) 64, Tatoon 6a 59, Diedrino 5b 54, Goku 5a 54; comment: Immorale 7a added.
- Climbook Roccamorice (Settore Alto) https://climbook.com/falesie/26645/roccamorice-settore-alto: "Aggiornamento gennaio 2025 (Fonte: Abruzzo Rock, ed. 2023)"; Costa ole 6a, Il colore della pelle 6a, Al cubo 5c+, Nonno Fernando 6b+, Minor 5a.

- AbruzzoLive.tv (1 nov 2025) https://www.abruzzolive.tv/cronaca/masso-si-stacca-dalla-montagna-e-uccide-35enne-di-montesilvano-durante-arrampicata-a-roccamorice-it38205.html: Stefano D'Anteo, 35, of Montesilvano killed when a boulder fell from the wall above and broke, in the area below via Betacam, "parete molto frequentata"; two other climbers lightly injured by fragments; CNSAS Abruzzo and 118 helicopter, 60 m winch; "L'area è stata messa in sicurezza e posta sotto sequestro"; carabinieri and Soccorso Alpino investigating cause.
