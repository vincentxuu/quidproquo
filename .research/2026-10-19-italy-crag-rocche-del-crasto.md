## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Rocca Calanna – Artino's wall https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/rocca-calanna-artino-s-wall.html: Alcara Li Fusi; 4a–8b (header); 16 routes; limestone; faces South; 70 m rope; slab routes "roccia eccezionale" in Rocche del Crasto setting; access: A20 Messina–Palermo exit Sant'Agata di Militello, 15 km to Alcara Li Fusi; road toward Portella Gazzana (bad in places), toward Contrada Cammara, park near trattoria Artino (info on crag); 10 min approach; many grades to confirm; developed top-down by Max Flaccavento & G. Barbagallo 2012; helmet recommended. Routes: Sailor Moon 5a 18 m; King Artin 6c 24 m; Storia di una meteora 6c+ 24 m; Defibrillation 6c 24 m; Alacras in the heart 7c/7c+ 22 m; Hasta siempre Presidente 6c 25 m; Nato per me 7b+ 25 m; Konan il Barbagian 7a 25 m; Ciliegie bianche 6a 20 m; Numeri e lettere 4c 10 m; White tiger 4c 10 m; La pazienza di Erika 4a 15 m; U Tenente 3a 12 m (for novices); Primavera ribelle 7b/7b+ 30 m; Cumparanza project; Figli delle stelle 6c+ 35 m ("via da non perdere").
- Parco dei Nebrodi zonizzazione https://www.parcodeinebrodi.it/pagina.php?id=3: 4 zones; Zona A (riserva integrale) 24,546.513 ha incl. "alcuni affioramenti rocciosi (Rocche del Crasto)"; allowed in A: hiking on foot/horse, motor traffic on existing roads, grazing, agriculture... (climbing not mentioned).
- Parco dei Nebrodi griffons https://www.parcodeinebrodi.it/pagina.php?id=43: reintroduction at Rocche del Crasto, Alcara Li Fusi; colony ~35; 9 nestings that year, fledging July–August (page undated). Park HQ Caronia tel 0921 333015, info@parcodeinebrodi.it.
- Parco dei Nebrodi nest cams https://www.parcodeinebrodi.it/pagina.php?id=64: telemetry/webcam study of golden eagle and griffon nesting in Rocche del Crasto area.
- L.R. 98/1981 art. (replaced by art.16 LR 14/88) http://www.ambientediritto.it/Legislazione/aree%20protette/Decreto%20istitutivo1981n.98.htm: in regional parks & reserves forbidden: capture, killing, damage or disturbance of animal species; open fires; etc.
- Parco dei Nebrodi itinerari Rocche del Crasto https://www.parcodeinebrodi.it/itinerari-rocche-crasto.php: Mesozoic grey crystalline rocks & white/pink dolomitic limestone; near Longi, San Marco d'Alunzio, Alcara Li Fusi; three hiking itineraries on Zona "A" trails; raptors incl. golden eagle & griffon; livestock grazing (cows, horses, goats, sheep).
- Planetmountain 2017 (Massimo Flaccavento) Ritorno al passato https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/ritorno-al-passato-la-nuova-via-arrampicata-alla-rocca-traura-in-sicilia.html: Rocca Traura SW pillar, >350 m wall above Alcara Li Fusi, dolomitic look; rock not always good; FA Flaccavento, Iurato, Latina; first attempt 2–3 Jan 2015 with snow on summits; one of best pitches in Nebrodi; delicate vegetated band mid-pillar with few protections; family Artino house at base of Rocca Calanna. (no grade given in article)
- Planetmountain 2012-04-19 https://www.planetmountain.com/it/notizie/arrampicata/nuove-vie-di-arrampicata-a-rocca-calanna-in-sicilia.html: 15 Apr 2012 Flaccavento & Barbagallo FFA Il Paradiso all'improvviso (7c, 180 m, 6 pitches, opened 2009–2012 with Iurato, L. Emmi) on Rocca Calanna; Sognatori allo sbaraglio (6b, 110 m) 2011; Rocca Calanna south face.
- Planetmountain Sognatori allo sbaraglio https://planetmountain.com/Rock/vie/itinerari/scheda.php?id_itinerario=1231&id_tipologia=38&lang=ita: FA 02-05-2011 Flaccavento, Barbagallo; SW; 110 m; quota 1054 m; 6b, obl 6a; L1 6a 20 m, L2 6b 35 m (trad crack), L3 6a 25 m, L4 5c 15 m; A20 exit S. - Comune di Militello Rosmarino "Come arrivare" https://comune.militellorosmarino.me.it/vivere-il-comune/il-territorio/come-arrivare/ (search snippet + page, published 2024-02-14): Autolinee Sberna Mon–Sat Sant'Agata Militello (Stazione FS) – Alcara li Fusi; run 1 dep 8:00, Militello Rosmarino 8:20, Alcara 8:35; run 2 10:45...
- UKC Destination Sicily (Massimo Cappuccio, 2022-06-01) https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/destinations/sicily-14498: multipitch east "wild Rocche del Crasto on the Nebrodi"; Caltavuturo district centre of island "most mountainous area and the most suitable during the hot Sicilian summers"; sport list Palermo, Caltavuturo; multipitch San Vito, Palermo, Rocche del Crasto, Capo Calavà; boulder Bosco Scorace, Bronte; airports Palermo Catania Trapani; train/public transport "not recommended... long and unnerving"; essential to rent car; multipitch -> west between San Vito and Palermo; "If you are a boulderer, the best place is Bosco Scorace"; winter Dec–Mar mild coast cold inland; Jul–Aug too hot; spring/autumn best; rainfall late autumn & winter–early spring. Guides: Di roccia di sole (Versante Sud, all cliffs), Rockfax Sicily (west), "Bosco Scorace" guide by Crimpandgoodlife, Sicily Rock, Vertical life. Bosco Scorace: hinterland Trapani; eucalyptus, oaks, Mediterranean scrub; sandstone compared to Fontainebleau; 4 to 8A+; landings comfortable safe; three sectors Bevaio, Rocche Forbici, Spaceship, total 200 lines; crash pads can be hired; guide sold at BB Polvere di Stelle; BB Polvere di Stelle base camp: driving, crash pad rental, climbing wall.
- visitsicily (official, published 2015-11-27, Ignazio Mannarano) https://www.visitsicily.info/en/climbing-and-dws-in-sicily/: Palermo favourites Schiavo, Valdesi, Mariella Crack'n Up, Preistoria, Anfiteatro on Monte Pellegrino and Bauso Rosso on Monte Gallo; Madonie Regional Nature Park: Monte d'Oro in Collesano, Rocca di Sciara in Caltavuturo; Nebrodi park: Rocche del Crasto, Rocca Calanna, Rocca Traura, "eagles and griffons hover undisturbed".
- Ministero dell'Interno 2019-10-08 https://www.interno.gov.it/it/notizie/attivo-anche-palermo-numero-emergenza-unico-europeo-112: NUE 112 operational in Palermo, Sicily first southern region with full coverage; old numbers 113/112/115/118 still routed; free, multilingual; central for western Sicily at Palermo (Agrigento, Trapani provinces), Caltanissetta for east.
- Versante Sud Di Roccia di Sole https://www.versantesud.it/it/negozio/catalogue/di-roccia-di-sole_1399/: Cappuccio, Gallo; 2022; ISBN 9788885475830; 560 p; Italian (variants English, German, iClimbing); 5 editions in ~20 yrs; falesie (single pitch) + boulder + DWS; multipitch split into a separate volume "che uscirà a seguito"; €35; Disponibile.
