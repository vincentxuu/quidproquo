- outdooractive Pichl (AVS Sektion Schlern, Helmut Kostner) https://www.outdooractive.com/en/route/alpine-climbing/dolomiten-groeden/the-pichl-1000-m-north-face-on-langkofel/63033073/: Difficulty IV; 7:00 h; highest 3,171 m, lowest 2,183 m; approach 0:45 h; wall 1,000 m; climbing length 1,300 m; descent 700 m 3:00 h; rope 1 x 60 m; 3 quickdraws; descent via normal route must be prepared; metal bivouac below summit; start: Sellajoch toward Comicihütte, after Gran Paradiso lift; "Wait for good stable weather and warm temperatures."; rest stops Hotel Passo Sella, coffin lift Forcella Sassolungo.
- Alberto De Giuli Pichl (2018-10-03) https://www.albertodegiuli.com/en/2018/10/climbing-pichl-route-north-face-sassolungo/: grade IV; FA 1918; 27 pitches 1200 m; often climbed solo by locals; topo in Mauro Bernardi guidebook very good; "more East facing"; slabs & chimneys 50/50; key pitches doable in approach shoes; just over 6 h to top; back at Demetz hut 4pm, "one hour before the closing of the coffin lift"; plan timing not to miss last run.
