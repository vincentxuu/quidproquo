# Research notes: Sella group — 2026-10-19

Areas: Sella Towers, Piz Ciavazes, Sass Pordoi, Cansla, Città dei Sassi. Series 義大利岩場 order 405.

## Sources opened
- UKC Sella Towers https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/sella_towers-2631: Climbs 150; altitude 2499 m; faces all; "The Sella Towers are iconic in terms of Dolomite climbing"; four towers (4th debated); 1st tower smallest & most popular, sunny south face, short accessible routes, "increasing amount of polish"; 2nd tower steep north face compact rock, Messner (North Face Direct) in "The Seventh Grade", committing; Kasnapoff route N face to NW ridge classic; 3rd tower largest, Vinatzer route classic; Available on Rockfax Digital; updated 16/Oct/2026.
- UKC Piz Ciavazes https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/piz_ciavazes-3948: Climbs 100; 2291 m; faces SW; south side "one of the most impressive and accessible faces in the Dolomites"; most routes end at Gamsband (Chamois Terrace) mid-height ledge due to poor rock above; Big Micheluzzi, Little Micheluzzi, Schubert/Route of Friendship classics; lower pitches vegetated; some single-pitch sport, lower quality; dries quickly except Buhl Direct; avoid hottest midsummer days; easy access from Sella Pass.
- UKC Città dei Sassi https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/citta_dei_sassi_city_of_rocks-10601: Climbs 339; 2250 m; faces all; "undoubtedly one of the best sport and bouldering venues in the Dolomites"; sector layout from Rockfax 2014; boulder problems red (older) / blue (newer) paint; roadside parking along top of Sella Pass + 2 rifugi; comments: "There's is no free road side parking" (JCCivico 02/Jul/25); grades "very many are extremely underestimated" (2017); good in rain, sector 2 dry (Steve Perry 2015); takes work to find sectors (2012).
- Planetmountain Cansla https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/cansla.html: 6a–8a; height ~200 m; 48 routes; limestone; SW; rope 70 m; "synonymous with highly technical, multi-pitch dream routes"; discovered 1984 Stefan Stuflesser & Carlo Grossrubatscher, Traumpfeiler; access: A22 Bolzano Nord, Val Gardena to Gardena/Sella pass fork, continue to Sella pass 200 m, park left large car park, 10 min walk; ~2000 m summer only; early-afternoon sun; dries quickly after storms; technical delicate slab, "A winter's strength training is of little use here"; bibliography "Arrampicare intorno al Passo Sella" (Stefan Stufflesser), "Val di Fassa Climbing" (Renato Bernard, Gabriele Bonanno); sectors Traumpfeiler (Traumpfeiler 6a+,6c+,6b,7a; Traumschiff 6c,6c+,6c,6c+,7a,6c; Amoklauf 6a+,7b,6c,6c+,7a; Odeon 7a,7a,7a; Kante 6c,8a+,7c,6c; Dai me to 8a; Sech o nia 7c+,7c; L Julon 7c,7a,7b; Benny Hill 6c,6b,7b; Tamburdl 6a+,6c,6c+,7b), Wasserfall (La Bela 6a, La Saurida 5c, Pisciadoi 6a, Tobai 6b, Solandra 6b+), Woodstockplatte (L Cianton 6a+ 28 m, Woodstock 6c, Kranebit 7a 40 m, L Var 7a+, HMN-Pudding 7b).
