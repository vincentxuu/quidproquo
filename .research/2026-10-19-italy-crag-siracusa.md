# Research notes: Siracusa（Canicattini Bagni ＋ Plemmirio／城市海岸 DWS） — 2026-10-19

範圍：錫拉庫薩省的峽谷運攀（Canicattini Bagni 五大區＋Antro dell'Eco）與錫拉庫薩海岸 DWS。Cava Grande、Monti Climiti、Gole della Stretta（Buccheri）只在文中帶過。

## Sources opened

- theCrag Siracusa https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach: 913 routes in area, 2,761 ascents; summary "Hidden gem in the south-east of Sicily, offering hard sport climbing in the many canyons around Siracusa. Canicattini Bagni is the most popular and quickly developing area"; topo best found in 5th version of La Roccia di Sole (2022); Canicattini Bagni 626 routes; sector list marks "Closed Sortino cliff", "Warning Grotta Dei Pipistrelli", "Warning La Pagoda"（警告內文未讀到）; seasonality table empty.
- theCrag Canicattini Bagni https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/2662762029: 626 routes, 2,187 ascents, limestone, canyon systems, "Routes generally follow natural lines, emphasising movement and technical climbing"; Contrada Alfano 155, Cava Bagni Alta 194 (1,305 ascents), Cava Bagni Centrale 93, Cava Bagni Bassa 40, Contrada Cavadonna 115, Cugno Lupo; nearest airport Catania; "Public transport is possible, but a car is strongly recommended"; by car E45/SS114 then SP14 to Canicattini; ethics: all routes fully bolted, stay on paths, close all gates, rebolt with stainless glue-in (Inox 316L), not equipped for camper tourism, use campsites; no climbing shop in town (Vertical Climbing Center & Decathlon in Siracusa); Nearby Icons: Doug 5c ★★ King of Queens (Cava Bagni Alta) 29 ascents; Canale 5c+ ★★★ Belvedere 8 ascents; 3 con Crauti 6a ★★★ Powerbank 28 ascents; Ton, Steine, Scherben 6b+ ★★★ Lost Places (Bassa) 2 ascents; Chaos 6c ★★★ Lost Places 1 ascent; Vampir 6c+ ★★★ Lost Places 6 ascents; Route 66 7a ★★★ Terminal II (Cavadonna) 2 ascents; Carpe Diem 7a+ ★★ Contralfano (Alfano) 3 ascents; Hysteria 7b ★★★ Cafe Med (Centrale) 1 ascent; STAHR FIGHTER 7b+ ★★★ Terminal I (Cavadonna) 1 ascent.
- theCrag Cava Bagni Alta https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/12980966541: canyon closest to town, reachable on foot from village via Via Bagni; parking 1 Solar Field Plant (SP74 toward Floridia ~1 km, after bridge); parking 2 wastewater treatment plant via Via Bagni; do not block gates; history: 2017 Stefan Löw, Christian Leube, Christoph Stahr, later Andreas Schebesta, Toni Löw; since 2017 more than 350 routes in Cava Bagni; guidebook Cappuccio & Gallo 2022 ISBN 9788885475854 "122 different crags".
- theCrag Belvedere https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/11195422287: 20 routes 5a–7b+, mostly vertical to slightly overhanging, suited to beginners/families; right wall shaded until ~midday, left wall longer; 12 min from hairpin parking; vegetation/thorns, pruning shears useful; warning (created ~13 days before 2026-10-19, by Friends of Canicattini): ~20 bags of rubbish dumped near route "Laura", removal being arranged; icons Wunderbar 6a ★★★, Behind the green Curtain 6b+ ★★★.
- theCrag Jungle Book https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/5813728410: 8 routes 6b–7b, overhanging, shade until ~1 p.m.
- theCrag Fuchsberger https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/3878818380: 31 routes 6a–8a, faces E–SE, morning sun, better in afternoon on warm days.
- theCrag B4 https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/11193850836: 8 long mid-grade routes, shady until noon, 70 m rope required.
- theCrag Glashaus https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/2662766568: 8 routes 6b–7b+, tufas, morning on hot days, evening sun.
- theCrag Contrada Alfano https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/12980492862: where Canicattini climbing began; seven sectors, over 150 routes, 5b–8b, steep athletic pockets/tufas.
- theCrag Contrada Cavadonna https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/12980350374: 115 routes, six sectors, 5b–8b, long open canyon; SP74 toward Floridia ~4 km then SR15.
- theCrag Cava Bagni Bassa https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/brucoli-beach/area/12980970870: 40 routes; SP14 toward Siracusa ~2 km, small parallel road 400 m; Lost Places 26 routes 33 m.
- Planetmountain crag list Siracusa https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags?provincia=Siracusa: Antro dell'eco 5a–8a (Canicattini Bagni); Gole della Stretta 5b–8a (Buccheri); Nome e cognome 4a–8a.
- Planetmountain Antro dell'Eco https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/antro-dell-eco.html: 21 routes 5a–8a; Flint 5a 14 m; La roccia Immacolata 5b 16 m; Fratelli Karamazov 5c+ 22 m; The love is capper juice 6a 20 m; L'egregio bastaso 6a+ 25 m; Gli Egrimlins 6b+ 14 m; La vita non è un film 6b+/6c 26 m; La rossa ruggente 6c+ 24 m; Qui non è Holliwood 7a 16 m; Baloo forever 7b+ 20 m; La forza tranquilla 7c 18 m; Munnu persu 7c 20 m; Fantasia al potere 8a 22 m; bolters M. Flaccavento & A. Tidona.
- Planetmountain Gole della Stretta https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/gole-della-stretta.html: 29 routes 5b–8a, slabs face south, overhangs south-west, 70 m rope; access from Buccheri via SP5, 10 min.
- climb-europe Sicily https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-italy-sicily: Siracusa 3rd largest climbing area in Sicily, Monti Iblei plateau; Canicattini canyons up to 200 m deep, "over 400 routes"; Monti Climiti bolted routes up to 100 m, 9 crags; ideal season autumn to spring; Di Roccia di Sole covers Siracusa and Ragusa.
- UKC destination guide (Massimo Cappuccio, 2022-06-01) https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/destinations/sicily-14498: Canicattini Bagni listed under sport; DWS Syracuse; "Neil Gresham climbed the first hard DWS lines in Syracuse"; climb all year; summer not recommended, July–August too hot; spring and autumn best; rain late autumn and winter–early spring; train/public transport not recommended, rent a car; Agriturismo La Frescura near Syracuse cliffs.
- visitsicily (Ignazio Mannarano, 2015) https://www.visitsicily.info/en/climbing-and-dws-in-sicily: Siracusa canyons ("cave"): Cavadonna, Arena, Gole della Stretta, Cava Grande, Nome e Cognome, Antro dell'Eco, Timpa Rossa (Rosolini); "Siracusa is the leading location for lovers of Deep-Water Soloing"; DWS above caves in the Plemmirio Marine Protected Area.
- Up-Climbing (Roberto Zampino, 2014-06-16) https://www.up-climbing.com/en/boulder/news-boulder/deep-water-solo-siracusa-neil-gresham-daniel-jung-epic-tv-video/: Gruppo Roccia Siracusa started DWS ~10 years before (Simon Alberto Piera, Riccardo Lentini); city coast 6 km along bike path, reached without boat; 3 main spots: Bar Tunisi traverse cave 15–17 m deep water, 7c+/8a FA; an Arch at end of bike path (some routes with rocks emerging); Cannone, north of Arch, afternoon sun, 6a–8a; all sectors 20–25 min by car; "don't go alone".
- Plemmirio decreto istitutivo (DM 15/09/2004, GU n.32 2005) https://www.mase.gov.it/portale/documents/d/guest/plemmirio_dm_15-09-2004-pdf: zone A riserva integrale off Capo Murro di Porco; zone A permits only rescue, surveillance, research, authorised guided dives; balneazione allowed from zone B; art. 6 prohibits activities disturbing species, removal/damage of geological formations, open fires.
- EU MSP platform Plemmirio https://maritime-spatial-planning.ec.europa.eu/practices/zoning-marine-protected-areas-case-plemmirio-mpa: 2,400 ha; zone A 80 ha, B 1,470 ha, C 950 ha; bathing and swimming only permitted in zones B and C.
- Consorzio Plemmirio Disciplinare integrativo 2025 https://www.plemmirio.eu/wp-content/uploads/2025/Disciplinare_Integrativo_Plemmirio_2025_MASE.pdf: annual rules approved by MASE, valid for 2025; dive sites with zone; no bathing in mooring fields; no climbing/DWS rule found.
- Consorzio Plemmirio zonazione https://www.plemmirio.eu/area-marina/zonazione-e-regolamento/: decreto istitutivo 15/09/2004 and regolamento 26/01/2009 downloadable.
- CNSAS Sicilia "Come chiamare soccorso" https://www.cnsas.sicilia.it/come-chiamare-soccorso/: in Sicily call 112 and ask specifically for Soccorso Alpino e Speleologico; NUE transfers to 118.
- infopoint provincia Siracusa trasporti https://www.infopointprovinciasiracusa.it/trasporti-interprovinciali/: Canicattini Bagni bus operator A.S.T.; Interbus Catania–Siracusa timetable PDF (2023).
- AST homepage https://www.aziendasicilianatrasporti.it/: operator notices 2026 (Ragusa–Siracusa runs etc.).
- Trenitalia Sicilia servizi minimi https://www.trenitalia.com/content/dam/trenitalia/regionale/treni-garantiti/Regionale%20Sicilia_sito.pdf: lines "Messina – Catania – Siracusa" and "Caltanissetta – Siracusa" (Modica–Siracusa trains) exist.
- Planetmountain video (2016-10-17) https://www.planetmountain.com/en/videos/climbing-at-siracusa-sicily-with-the-ragni-di-lecco.html: Michele Caminati introduces Canicattini Bagni, developed with Ragni di Lecco.
