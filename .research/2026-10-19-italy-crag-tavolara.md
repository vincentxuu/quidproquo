# Research notes: Tavolara（Olbia 外海私人島、Area Marina Protetta Tavolara–Punta Coda Cavallo） — 2026-10-19

## Sources opened

- Comune di Olbia news (14 maggio 2025, ultimo aggiornamento 13 maggio 2025) https://www.comune.olbia.ot.it/it/news/divieto-di-attivita-di-arrampicate-a-tavolara-per-tutelare-l-incolumita-delle-persone-e-il-patrimonio-naturalistico: mayor Settimo Nizzi; ordinance "che vieta di svolgere questo tipo di attività a Tavolara, se non ai soggetti in possesso della qualifica di guida alpina e della specializzazione di arrampicata sportiva, esclusivamente a scopo di studio e scientifico"; landslide hazard Hg3 and Hg4 on all slopes; PAI maps (BURAS n.1, 2 Jan 2025) show widespread active landslides; accident 3 May 2025 on "Ferrata degli Angeli": 14 CNSAS technicians (Olbia, Sassari, Nuoro), Capitaneria di Porto patrol boat, Areus helicopter from Alghero.
- SassariToday (Francesca Arcai, 2025-06-02) https://www.sassaritoday.it/cronaca/escursioni-isola-tavolara-divieto-regolamento-1-giugno-30-settembre.html: ordinanza n. 28 del 13 maggio 2025 bans all climbing and ferrata on the island except alpine guides with sport-climbing specialisation "solo a scopo scientifico o di studio"; rettifica 19 May limits ban "solo nel periodo estivo" to 30 settembre 2025, with a municipal regulation for the trails to be drafted by autumn for council approval; ~90% of island private property of Marzano family (since 1930s, after Bertoleoni); small community at Spalmatore di Terra (west); NATO radio base since 1962 on eastern half, "zona militare strettamente off-limits"; AMP since 1997; access to summit previously by tolerance; Comitato "Tavolara Libera", lawyer Edvige Baldino, autotutela request 29 May; Punta Cannone 565 m.
- Climbing Sardinia (2025-05-26) https://climbingsardinia.com/escursioni-a-tavolara-sospese-per-la-stagione-2025/: trekking and climbing to Punta Cannone not available until 30 Sept 2025; ordinance 13 May 2025 rectified 19 May 2025; island partly private, military base, AMP.
- L'Unione Sarda (2025-07-03) https://www.unionesarda.it/news-sardegna/gallura/tavolara-off-limits-due-escursionisti-spagnoli-violano-il-divieto-di-arrampicata-k250kljn: two Spanish hikers violated ordinance on Passu Malu path, lost, Soccorso alpino intervened; council motion asked revocation and guided access.
- La Nuova Sardegna (2025-06-25) https://www.lanuovasardegna.it/olbia/cronaca/2025/06/25/news/olbia-nizzi-in-consiglio-comunale-a-tavolara-non-si-puo-salire-1.100726973: in council the mayor kept the ordinance; ban to 30 September after the rectification.
- L'Unione Sarda (2026-09-18) https://unionesarda.it/en/sardinia/tavolara-two-tourists-lost-in-the-woods-two-rescued-during-the-night-dh4uleni: two English hikers lost in scrub at night on Tavolara, rescued by firefighters and coast guard (no mention of ordinance status).

- Planetmountain (Maurizio Oviglia, 2016-10-18) https://planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/tavolara-climbing-on-the-sardinian-island-shrouded-in-myth.html: first routes early 1970s by Predazzo finance police; written up in Gogna's "Mezzogiorno di Pietra"; "the island is private and access to its summit remains prohibited, unless you possess valid authorisation" (as Wikipedia states); Buscaini removed it from guidebook, Oviglia excluded it from Pietra di Luna; Habel late 70s trad; Stenghel many routes; first modern route 1990s by Lecis and Sarti (Affora sa nato 6b 215 m); "can one climb on Tavolara?
