# Research notes: Tofana di Rozes (south face & Pilastro) — 2026-10-19

Series: 義大利岩場 order 504. Scope: south face of Tofana di Rozes, its three pillars/edges, Costantini-Apollonio. Cinque Torri post covers nearby Cinque Torri only.

## Sources opened

- SummitPost — Tofana di Rozes https://www.summitpost.org/tofana-di-rozes/151405: Tofana di Rozes (Tofana I) 3,225 m; Tofana di Mezzo 3,244 m, Tofana di Dentro 3,238 m; Dolomia Principale; south wall "almost 1000 meters high ... consisting of eight distinct pillars"; NE face least steep, normal route on NE scree, snow and ice can remain into full summer; Ferrata Lipella on west face; road: SS48 to Passo Falzarego, just after Pocol (6 km after Cortina) narrow road up to Rifugio Dibona 2,083 m = start for south wall; route overview updated 2014 incl. two new descents from the south-wall pillars "after the landslide occurred in 2011"; routes W→E: Via della Tridentina TD VI 850 m (Bonatti, Contini 1952), Via Mirka D- max IV 850 m (1964), Ey de Net TD+ VI+ 800 m, Dimai-Eötvös D+ max V 1150 m (1901), Direttissima Stösser TD+ VI 850 m (1929), SSW edge D IV 850 m (Dibona et al. 1930), Tissi-Andrich TD+ VI 800 m, Via della Julia TD V/VI 850 m; Great Pillars (E end, Third, Second=Pilastro, First from W to E): Third Pillar Alverà-Pompanin D sup IV/V 600 m (1946); Pilastro: Compagni di merenda ED+ 7b obl. 350 m (1998), Leviti ED VII- 500 m, Costantini-Apollonio TD+ V+ A2 (VII free) 500 m (1944), Never give up ABO VIII+/IX- 500 m (2007), Costantini-Ghedina TD inf VI- 630 m (1946; mostly IV+ with V+ and VI- pitches), Paolo VI ED- VI+ A1 A2 600 m (1963); First Pillar: Pompanin-Alverà D+ max V+ 380 m (1946), Lele Vedani TD sup VI A1 (VIII+ free) 460 m; history: Scoiattoli founded July 1939; Buhl quote
- Planetmountain — Costantini-Apollonio https://www.planetmountain.com/it/itinerari/costantini-apollonio-tofana-di-rozes.html: FA Ettore Costantini, Renato Apollonio 13/07/1944; SW; 500 m; VII; obligatory V+ A2; most famous and repeated route of the face; first part crack in grey base to first ledge (bail possible by rappels in gully between Pilastro and Primo Spigolo); two hard roofs and "schiena di mulo" often wet are the cruxes; exits with Costantini-Ghedina; pitoned, normal rack; access: from Cortina toward Falzarego past Pocol, after hairpins junction right for narrow road to Rifugio Dibona; 30 min approach to base; descent: ledge toward Punta Marietta spur, scree, exposed terrain, down to Rifugio Giussani
- Planetmountain — vie Tofane list https://www.planetmountain.com/it/itinerari/vie-di-arrampicata?gruppo=Tofane: Aspettando la vetta 6c (first of three edges, exit shared with Primo Spigolo classic); Compagni di merenda 7b (left of Pilastro, compact water-worn rock, crux pitches 4–5); Costantini-Apollonio VII; Goodbye 1999 7b (left of Pilastro, same wall as Compagni di merenda, sustained, equipped); GuGu Castelletto max 6+ (or A0); Il mio criceto 7b (Primo Spigolo, Koren & Vidmar summer 2011, ~10 m from Aspettando la vetta); Il vecchio leone e la giovane fifona 6c (Primo spigolo, Da Pozzo & N. Alexander); La danza del tapiro 7a+ (wall above Grotta della Tofana, "forse la via più abbordabile tra quelle moderne chiodate da Massimo Da Pozzo"); La storia è finita 7a (Tofana di Mezzo — out of scope); Leviti 7- (comparable to Costantini-Apollonio, join it high up); Mai molar 7a+ (ground-up bolted with drill, 10 mm spits, bring some medium/small cams); Never give up VIII+/IX- (Grmovsek & Krajnc, freed Aug 2007)
- winterclimb — Constantini-Apollonio https://winterclimb.com/climbing-base/hydepark/39-constantini-apollonio-tofana-di-rozes: VII+, "the most famous route in Cortina"; "Almost 600 meters"; must be comfortable on VI+ trad; 3 cruxes: roof VII+ (can aid), roof VII, chimney VI+ (often wet); difficulty VI A0 (VII+); height 450 m + 80 m to col; length 580 m + 100 m; 23 pitches; 8–10 h; gear standard + 8 quickdraws + nuts + middle cams; descent: many false trails, go up over a pass following painted dots toward Rifugio Giussani then steep scree gully
- REDclimber — Primo Spigolo Alverà-Pompanin https://www.redclimber.it/relazione/arrampicata/arrampicata-dolomiti/arrampicata-dolomiti-ampezzane/tofana-di-rozes-primo-spigolo-via-alvera-pompanin/: 400 m, D+, II/RS2, V+; FA 4 Aug 1946; road to Rifugio Dibona, unpaved last part; parking 2,083 m; approach toward Rifugio Giussani, left at cableway start on path to Ferrata Lipella, path 404, ~45 min; 11 pitches described, most belays on two spits; descent by obvious trail/ledge (many fixed pins) to path, ~1 h to car park; "probabilmente il più facile e il meno lungo dei tre"
