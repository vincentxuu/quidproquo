# Research notes: Val Pennavaire (Oltrefinale) — 2026-10-19

Scope: Val Pennavaire, Albenga hinterland, Comune di Castelbianco / Cisano sul Neva / Nasino / Alto (SV, Liguria). Planning question: Finale 內陸的冬季運攀怎麼挑區？

## Sources opened

- UKC destination guide (Rob Greenwood, 27 Jan 2020; logistics by Alan James) https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/destinations/val_pennavaire_-_finale_ligures_lesser-known_neighbour-12544: inland from Albenga, ~30 km west along coast from Finale; "clear distinction of 35km in distance and climbing styles"; Finale "now more famous for polish and very spaced bolts", Pennavaire "mostly developed in the last 20 years and is very much more 'modern'"; car advisable; Colletta di Castelbianco = social centre (village abandoned 1950s, converted 1990s); Telematica 5a–6b, 5 min from Colletta parking, sun-trap; Terminal mid 6s–high 7s, gets sun most of day; Erboristeria opposite Terminal, morning sun, mostly harder grades; Cineplex ~40 min walk, hot-weather crag 7a+; Emisfero all-day shade near Martinetto; best time October to April, December and January can be too cold; May–September too hot for most; seepage after prolonged rain; 70 m rope + 16 quickdraws; no trad, all routes have lower-offs; dusty bases; CPR gear shop Cisano sul Neva closed Monday; Roc Pennavaire guide sold at Colletta bar; routes: CD ROM 6a+, Kazaa 6a+, Newark 6b+, Ministro Pistarini 6c+, Tomachevo 7a+, Lester Pearson 7c, Nino Aquino 7c, Saltasassi 6a+, Tacche Ticche 6c+ (Bausu), Donostia 6b+, Tapas 7a, Los Equipadores 7b, Kepa 7c (Euskal), Bob 7b, Sostegno Volante 7b+ (Red Up), Merci Laurent 7a (Reunion), Picasso 7b, Kandinsky 7c (Guggenheim); comment 27 Jan 2020: fixed lines between left and right Erboristeria.
