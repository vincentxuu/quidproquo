# Research notes: Val Rosandra（+ Napoleonica）— 2026-10-19

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain Val Rosandra (EN) https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/val-rosandra.html: limestone, mainly crimps, some pockets and tufas, mostly vertical with some steep overhangs; 60 m rope; numerous sectors, "doesn't offer a single, magnificent crag" but a puzzle; Sidarta guidebook "Arrampicare senza frontiere" by Erik Svab; karst canyon protected by a park, crossed by the Italian–Slovenian border, memory of interwar alpinism; "Sunny winter days"; sunnier right side sectors Canarini, Giardini d'Inverno, Oci de Moccò, 12 Vie, Piccola Ferrovia, La Bianca, Falchi, Falchetti, Bosco Incantato, Vergini, Parete del Casello, Stena, Montasio, Concave, Amicizie, Altari, Bedrock, Cartoonia, Pergolo; other side shady Crinale, Jugove, Castighi di Dio, Piccola Comici, slab Formai near Santa Maria in Siaris; Montasio: 25 m, technical, "the most admired limestone architecture in the valley"; route tables: Canarini (Piccola lady 5c+, Che fa la vacca? 6b+, Sendero luminoso 6a+, El cugno 6a, Yuppidù 5c, Diedro bianco 5b, Pungiclimber 6b, Nuvola 7c, Patto col Diavolo 7c+, La rosa dei venti 7b, Strapiombo dei canarini 7a+, Variante canarina 6c+, Uomini Veri 6c, Diedro dei cucadores 5a, Festival 5b); Montasio (Strapiombi de Comici 5c/A0, Naso de Comici 5a/A0, Diedro Barisi 5c, Via de Berto 6a, Via de Comici 6b, Baronova 6b+ FA Jose Baron); Piccola Ferrovia (Indian summer 7b, Tetto di Dumbo 7c, Tetto di Mauro 7b+, Via Baldi 5b, Kriptonite 6c+, Mercante dei sogni 7a+, Dottor destino 8a, Specchio di Alessio 7c, L'Orecchino 7a, Boulder man 7c+, Satyricon 6b, L'alberetto 5a, Diedro impestado 7a+)
- discover-trieste (Comune di Trieste) Rosandra Valley Reserve https://discover-trieste.it/live/natural-wonders/rosandra-valley-reserve: visitor centre Bagnoli della Rosandra – Boljunec 507; "one of the most valued rock climbing areas in Europe"; Emilio Comici "first learnt his craft" here; 15 km Rosandra stream from Slovenia; karstification, Flysch; "Bora Saddle"; Premuda hut (lowest mountain hut in Italy), trail No. 1 along Roman aqueduct; fork to Comici memorial stone and Santa Maria in Siaris or Sentiero dell'Amicizia to Bottazzo; old railway now Giordano Cottur cycle path to Hrpelje
- discover-trieste Seaside mountaineers https://discover-trieste.it/live/outdoor-activities/climbing/seaside-mountaineers: Kugy, Comici, Tiziana Weiss; classic multi-pitch in Rosandra Valley; routes "almost always fully or partially bolted", combined with nuts/friends; La Grande five pitches to summit of Mount Stena; Spigolo Verde; alpine-style cliffs used by CAI for teaching (Concave, Amicizie, Montasio, Bianca, Falchi, Altari) not for free-climbing purists from safety perspective; maps in "Arrampicare senza frontiere" (Edizioni Sidarta, IT/SL/EN); "always wear a helmet on the multi-pitch routes and cliffs - the rock in this area is quite crumbly"; Premuda hut lowest CAI hut in continental Italy
- falesia.it Val Rosandra（updated 25/08/2024）https://www.falesia.it/crag/192/val-rosandra.html: "I gradi spaziano dal 5c all'8b e l'esposizione a sud la rende impraticabile d'estate"; access via Basovizza, S. Antonio in Bosco, Hervati, follow railway, path uphill; resin bolts; 67 routes; 4a/8b; 400 m; exposure south
- theCrag Val Rosandra（Exa fetch）https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/val-rosandra-glinscia: 541 routes, 2,835 ascents; "Climbable all year as you will find cliffs in all expositions"; almost all routes resin bolts and anchors; some anchors without lower-off carabiners, need to thread; best equipped: Parete di Bagnoli, Giardini d'inverno, Caprette, Bedrock–Cartoonia–Il pergolo, Piccola ferrovia, Vergini, Altari, most of Crinale; approach 10–20 min, from Osp (Slovenia) or Trieste; sectors incl. Parete di Bagnoli 25 (12 m), Giardini d'inverno 16, Crinale 82 (29 m), Dodici vie 37, Piccola Ferrovia 49, Falchi 44 (marked "Warning"), Vergini 57, Cippo Comici 1 (140 m); guidebooks listed: Climbing without Frontiers (2021), Kraški Rob / Karst Edge (2019), Friuli e Terre di Confine (2020)
- inItaly（2026-07-19）https://initaly.it/en/articolo/val-rosandra-un-ecosistema-da-proteggere-tra-tutela-e-fruizione-28379: goal is not to ban access; Engreen II and PALLIPES projects, white-clawed crayfish; issues: bathing in stream, littering, noise, leaving marked trails, spray-paint vandalism on karst rocks; reserve "suitable for hiking, climbing and nature observation"; stick to marked paths, avoid bathing, carry out waste, minimise noise
- Turismo FVG Val Rosandra Reserve https://turismofvg.it/en/carso/itinerari/val-rosandra-reserve?LangSetCMS=en: established 1996, about 750.54 ha, municipality of San Dorligo della Valle – Dolina

- Planetmountain Napoleonica (IT) https://www.planetmountain.com/it/falesie/napoleonica.html: 4c–8b; all sectors face S/W, sun from 11/12 to sunset; 60 m rope; sea view above Trieste; historic routes climbed from Emilio Comici to today; sectors re-equipped, new ones by Walter Perdan and Luciano Frezzolini; access A4 to end, superstrada exit Prosecco, signs for Monte Grisa sanctuary, roundabout left, closed road "strada Vicentina" with parking near a fountain; season: mid-seasons and winter; Sopra la Napoleonica 8 routes 6b–7c, not sheltered from bora; La Napoleonica main sector 4th grade to 8b, traverses up to 8a; easy: Cico, Texi 4c, Banana Rama 5c/6a; Lo Scudo 6c+, Hells Bells 7b+, Atmosfera Zero and Poker d'Assi 7c, Orcobaleno and Mud Club 8a; climbable in light rain under the Scudo wall; three towers below, 20 routes 6a–7a+ with abseils; grotto 13 overhanging routes 6b–8a+: la Fontana 6c, Scorpions 7b, Oklaoma 7c, Martedì Grasso 8a, Skaywalker 8a+
- Gravità Zero Trieste, Napoleonica https://www.gravitazerotrieste.it/news/napoleonica/: "una delle falesie storiche triestine"; since the 1930s a training ground for alpinists; more than 200 routes from IV to 8b+; Comici's first tries on still-existing passages; Berto Pacifico traverse; Andrea "Arci" Varnerin: Mud Club 8a+, Orcobaleno 8b, Pugacioff 8b+; Sbisigando con l'Orango 8b+ (Gabriele Gorobey); Frezzolini's 1998 collection to re-equip, Mano di Fatima; Progetto Verticale Trieste (2022) replaced all anchors of sector Vedetta Italia in January 2024; some old routes still need maintenance
