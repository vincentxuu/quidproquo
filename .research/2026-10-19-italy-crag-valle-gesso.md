# Research notes: Valle Gesso bouldering — 2026-10-19

Scope: alpine boulders of Valle Gesso (Cuneo): Vallone di San Giacomo above Entracque (Isengard, Vanilla Sky, Madonnina, Settore basso/Bellerofonte) and the Valdieri side (Sant'Anna di Valdieri, Pian della Casa). Groundlane MCP worked; 27crags, MadBoulder sectors, Benese PDFs via curl; theCrag via Exa.

## Sources opened

- Oliunid blog (EN, 2021-02-07) https://www.oliunid.com/blog/bouldering-valle-gesso-2021-en/: Valle Gesso among Cuneo valleys most appreciated for bouldering in spring and autumn; Blu Edizioni guide "Bouldering in Valle Gesso" covers Vallone di San Giacomo; start Entracque ~900 m, San Giacomo 1,250 m; developed from 1990s by Angelo Siri, then guide authors Marco Torielli, Paolo Bertolotto, Giova Massari, Antonio Stazio; Christian Core and Stella Marchisio FA of very hard lines; areas not obvious, vegetation hides boulders, contact locals for parking and paths; Isengard among most popular, grassy plateau, every difficulty, good landings; La Madonnina, Bellerofonte overhanging walls; "The rock is highly featured gneiss"; may take several days to dry after rain depending on aspect; areas far apart, drive between; Feb 2021 Sangia Gnus sector temporarily prohibited (footbridge missing); consult Cuneoclimbing for current info.
- Oliunid blog (IT, 2022-04-08) https://www.oliunid.it/blog/fare-boulder-sullo-gneiss-lavorato-della-valle-gesso/ (search snippet + title only): attributes the guide to Versante Sud — conflicts with EN version and other sources (Blu Edizioni); treated as error.
- MadBoulder Valle Gesso https://www.madboulder.org/valle-gesso: "a premier alpine bouldering destination in Italy"; "high-quality granite boulders"; two main sections, lower area "over 100 problems", upper sector at higher elevation; best season autumn; summer possible but quick weather changes; located in Maritime Alps Nature Park, climbers must respect conservation rules; 1,359 m; 10 videos; sectors Pian della Casa, Sant'Anna di Valdieri, Vanilla Sky, Unknown.
- MadBoulder sectors https://www.madboulder.org/sectors/valle-gesso/pian-della-casa : Crino 6b, La fessura della Valle 5+, Micky's Traverse 6c; https://www.madboulder.org/sectors/valle-gesso/santanna-di-valdieri : Bis 6b+, Il mostro di Bermess 6c; https://www.madboulder.org/sectors/valle-gesso/vanilla-sky : Inout 5+, La Lupa 6c, Wolf 6a; https://www.madboulder.org/sectors/valle-gesso/unknown : Bomba a mano 8a, Wallace traverse 6b+.
- 27crags Valle Gesso Boulder (Isengard) https://27crags.com/crags/valle-gesso-isengard: 216 boulders; "No special access issues"; approach 20 min from parking to Isengard, 15 min to Vanilla Sky lowest; nearby San Giacomo Madonnina 10, San Giacomo settore basso 26, Pian della Casa Boulder 142, Sant'Anna 147, Remondino 110 boulders.
- 27crags Valle Gesso route list https://27crags.com/crags/valle-gesso-isengard/routelist: Gandalf Sit 7A (4 ascents), No way 5+ (3), Milligan 6B, Otto Von Boulder 6C+, GTA 7C, Blues 6B+, Wolf 6A, La lupa 6C, Gandalf 6A, Miniere di Moriar 6A+, Bomba a mano 8A (Barbablu, 2), Isengard 7A+, Isengard Sit 7B+, Danny the dog 7C, Barbablu 7B+, Povero io 7C+, Castelli di rabbia 7C.
- 27crags Pian della Casa Boulder https://27crags.com/crags/pian-della-casa-boulder and route list https://27crags.com/crags/pian-della-casa-boulder/routelist: 142 boulders; no special access issues; access from Cuneo via Borgo San Dalmazzo, Andonno, Valdieri, then SP239 toward Sant'Anna di Valdieri and Terme di Valdieri; La fessura della valle 5+ (5 ascents), Origin 6A (3), Razor Edge 6B, Luna nuova 6A, Spritz 6A+, You 7C, King Kong 7B, Gollum 7A.
- 27crags San Giacomo settore basso https://27crags.com/crags/san-giacomo-settore-basso and route list https://27crags.com/crags/san-giacomo-settore-basso/routelist: 26 boulders; parking 500 m after two narrow hairpins, descent on left of road; Bellerofonte 8A, Green anarchy 7C+, Tekuni 7B, Big fish 8A+, Jakarta 6C+, La normale 5; ("Kuk 9A+" entry judged unreliable, not used).
- theCrag Vallone di San Giacomo https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/northern-italy/cuneo-area/area/4862760717: only 8 bouldering routes listed (La Madonnina 4, Isengard 1, Settore basso 3); FB 6A–8A.
- 8a.nu Cuneo crags https://www.8a.nu/areas/italy/cuneo/crags: San Giacomo di Entracque – Basso & Giardino Zen 720 ascents; San Giacomo – Sangiacomo 141; San Giacomo – Vanilla Sky 71.
- 8a.nu San Giacomo Basso & Giardino Zen https://www.8a.nu/crags/bouldering/italy/valle-gesso/routes: 401 boulders in DB, 720 ascents, coordinates 44.27598, 7.39712; route list requires login.
- climbing-guide.eu Bouldering in Valle Gesso https://www.climbing-guide.eu/bouldering-in-valle-gesso-11180/: 108 pages; Italian, legend IT/EN/DE; edition 2015 + Vertical-Life app; P. Bertolotto, G. Massari, M. Torielli; Blu Edizioni; €18.
- Baita Monte Gelas bouldering https://bmgelas.com/bouldering/: guide covers boulders of Vallone di San Giacomo, "oltre 500 linee", 5a/8a, maps and short history.
- Aree Protette Alpi Marittime, Arrampicata sostenibile https://www.areeprotettealpimarittime.it/visit/norme-di-comportamento/arrampicata-sostenibile: inside protected areas follow regulations; check from home that sector is accessible (temporary/permanent bans for nests/dens/species); new routes and securing existing ones must be authorised, fine €1,152 under LR 19/2009 in protected areas and Natura 2000; don't park on meadows; avoid shortcuts; clean only holds with soft brush, leave moss/lichen; limit chalk; brush tick marks; report occupied nests; don't scare animals; carry rubbish down; Natura 2000 sites not marked on ground, check EU portal.
- Parco Alpi Marittime navetta https://www.parcoalpimarittime.it/visit/info-pratiche/la-navetta-del-parco: (2023 text) on-call shuttle by Audisio of Entracque, booking ≥36 h, min two users, max 30 km, not beyond Terme di Valdieri, communes Entracque, Roaschia, Valdieri, Vernante; links Line 77 Cuneo–Valdieri–Entracque, Line 82 Cuneo–Valdieri–Terme (benese.it PDFs).
- La Guida 2026-07-04 https://laguida.it/2026/07/04/piu-bus-per-le-terme-di-valdieri-e-la-navetta-del-parco: 2026 Line 82 Cuneo–Terme di Valdieri doubled from July (8:10 and 14:30 from Cuneo Corso Nizza 4; returns 12:05, 18:05) Mon–Sat to 31 Aug, 1–5 Sep morning only; Navetta del Parco 14 June–14 September 2026, call 351 8969729 / 334 8590124 or online, book 36/24 h ahead, car 4 seats, min two paying.
- Benese Linea 77 PDF http://www.benese.it/linee/linea77.pdf: Cuneo – Borgo S. Dalmazzo – Andonno – Valdieri – Entracque P. Mercato; some runs all year (e.g. 6:15, 9:55 from Cuneo C. Nizza), others summer only or school days; Cuneo FS → Entracque about 30–40 min.
- Benese Linea 82 PDF http://www.benese.it/linee/linea82.pdf: service 15/06/2026–05/09/2026 Mon–Sat; 8:10 Cuneo C. Nizza → Sant'Anna di Valdieri 8:55 → Terme 9:10; July–August also 14:30; returns 12:05 and 18:05 from Terme; Cuneo–Terme single €5.00.
- TargatoCN 2026-10-01 https://www.targatocn.it/2026/10/01/leggi-notizia/argomenti/attualita/articolo/valdieri-chiusa-la-falesia-di-andonno-lavori-di-messa-in-sicurezza-da-50mila-euro.html: Comune di Valdieri Ordinanza Sindacale n. 55 of 01/10/2026: total ban on access and climbing at Andonno crag from 1 Oct 2026 until end of works (€50,000 FOSMIT funds); sectors Sinistro, Anfiteatro, Centrale, Destro, Parete del Forte, Omaggio. (Andonno = sport crag in same valley, not a bouldering area; mention only as nearby.)
- Cuneoclimbing https://www.cuneoclimbing.it/new/: "Il portale dell'arrampicata cuneese dal 2006"; news (e.g. 29 Sep 2026 Baracco crag accessible again); relazioni and map.

- CNSAS richiesta di soccorso https://www.cnsas.it/richiesta-di-soccorso/: for mountain or cave accidents call 118 or the European single emergency number 112 where active and ask for CNSAS; rescue call free throughout Italy; give identity, phone number, location, time, number and condition of injured.
