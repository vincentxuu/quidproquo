# Research notes: Varazze — 2026-10-19

Scope: Varazze inland boulder areas (Potala, La Cava, Lilliput, Eden, Futurama, Area 51, La Collina, Harem) plus the small sport crag Falesia di Alpicella (Monte Castellaro). Series 義大利岩場 order 704.

## Sources opened

- Planetmountain crag page Varazze https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/varazze.html: text by Marco Bagnasco; first sector La Cava discovered 1999 with his dog Carlotta; Christian Core and Stella Marchisio helped clean and free problems; "Last year a new guidebook was published to the are with over 1100 problems"; access A10 Genova–Ventimiglia, from Ventimiglia exit Celle Ligure, from Genova exit Varazze; along the promenade to the fork for Alpicella, past town hall, follow Alpicella, narrow one-way road by river, right for "Eremo del deserto - Faie", past a small petrol station; Eden, Hollywood, Lilliput, Up-there: 200 m after petrol station right for "Faie"; Potala, Harem, La Cava, Futurama: continue to village "Il Pero", fork "Alpicella" / "Scavi archeologici"; 2013 Bagnasco opened a B&B near Potala; bibliography BluBloc Bouldering in Varazze and Trioria (Core, Marchisio, Bagnasco).
- topoguru Varazze https://topoguru.com/en/content/varazze: "one of the most popular Italian boulder climbing crag"; good option in winter; town between Finale Ligure and Genoa; 15–20 min drive; 400–600 m above sea level; hills face south; Potala main area with 6 most popular sectors; Gioia "the first 8c+ boulder in the world, Christian Core did the first ascent in 2008"; metamorphic rock, hard-grained surface, careful with skin; locals: season end of September to May; Potala parking 2 min walk from first blocks; many 7b and harder; flat landings, 1–2 crashpads enough; La Cava after village Alpicella, park next to blocks, small sunny sector good in winter; Lilliput 10–15 min walk on forest road, boulders 5a–6c, need guide to find boulders.
- theTopo area Varazze https://thetopo.com/areas/varazze: best season September to May; summer typically too hot; area part of Parco del Beigua; rock serpentinite, metabasite, metagabbro; slopers, notches, rare pockets; problems marked with black arrows at start, can fade; in summer some sectors overgrown and infested with ticks (roe deer); A10 exit Celle Ligure for areas near Alpicella or Varazze; "1067 routes in 9 crags"; Premium topo 1035 routes.
- theTopo Potala description https://thetopo.com/crags/potala-druidi-alto-18374/description: "The whole area of Potala is situated on a private property … The owners of the area allow us access as long as there's no damage"; Potala biggest sector, along the riverside, nine sectors; each sector has parking; path 10' from Antro dei Druidi parking to Terra di mezzo; Antro dei Druidi largest sector, first blocks 2 min from parking; Druidi alto highest, 3 min from parking, morning sun; Graal in shade in winter; Terra di Mezzo, Dimora degli Elfi, Druidi basso, Antro dei Druidi: morning sun, afternoon shade; summer: wait until after 5 p.m.; Baby Druidi many problems for children; Dimora degli Elfi small lakes; Tana del Tasso: large overhang climbable "even when it rains heavily", discovered 2015, private land, bring lamp for B side; Antro dei Druidi discovered 2001 by Bagnasco and dog Carlotta.

- montagnapertutti Alpicella https://www.montagnapertutti.it/roccia/arrampicate/liguria/alpicella/alpicella.php: Monte Castellaro near Alpicella, "limitrofa ai confini del Parco Naturale del Beigua"; metamorphic schistose rock; access: A10 exit Varazze, follow Sassello–Alpicella along Torrente Teiro, past Il Pero, right for Alpicella; just before village (bus stop on left) turn left at cemetery ("scavi archeologici"), small bridge, parking for 4–5 cars; 10 min walk; "Si tratta di un bosco privato, il cui proprietario ha dato consenso"; bolted by "Vecchie Beline" team, works started end 2005, last pitches end 2006; from 3+/4 to overhangs above 6b; some 2–3 pitch routes max 90 m; 60 m rope enough; helmet advised; almost 40 routes; guide sold at trattorie in Alpicella.
