## Article facts

**Rock and style.** Limestone; a mix of vertical and overhung ground, with a cave section. The whole Batu Caves cluster is karst roughly 400 million years old under cliffs of about 150 m; Damai's own wall is described by MP as a "300+ foot crag" with most routes shorter.

**Routes and grades.** 80 routes, 76 sport, 3,487 ascents (theCrag, 2026-10-15). Range 5b–7c, concentrated at 5c–6c. Subareas: Left 19 routes to 25 m, Main 30 to 39 m, Cave 3 at 22 m, Right 28 at 18 m. Gua Damai's own marketing claims "more than 100 bolted routes" from 5c to 7a+ — a commercial count that does not match the database; do not repeat it as fact.

**Season and shade.** North-facing. theCrag states all-day sun from about mid-March to August and all-day shade from August to about mid-March. A 2023 blog advises early morning or afternoon and calls October–December monsoon a poor window. No official closure.

**Access.** About 20 minutes' drive from central Kuala Lumpur; Grab or taxi to "Gua Damai Extreme Park". Approach is flat and under five minutes. Buses 14 (from Central Market) and 41 (from Lot10) reach Pinggiran Batu Caves bus station. Driving into Kampung Wira Melayu is confusing and some taxis drop at the temple instead.

**Fees and rules.** Permit/entry RM5 for Malaysians, RM10 for non-Malaysians (official site). Helmet mandatory for both climber and belayer (theCrag, 2023 blog, 2023 guide) — this rule is not stated on the official page, so it is community-attested only. Rental helmets available at the entrance and described as sweaty. Toilet 50 sen per use or RM1 per day; shower RM1. The official 2026 price list covers courses and guiding only and does not restate the entry fee.

**Land and management.** Managed as a commercial extreme-sports park by Gua Damai Extreme Park, with lighting for night climbing and cave climbing permitted. No public land status or lease details found.

**Bolts and anchors.** Developed in the late 1990s with expansion bolts. Bolts are now 20–30 years old and some anchors are rope slings worn by sun and rain (December 2023 note). theCrag warns: "Caution some routes at Damai have old bolts!" No rebolting programme or organisation is named.

**History and naming.** Mustapha and Wira Adventure Consultants bolted 3 Brothers and Monsoon in the mid-to-late 1990s; Akmal later added Nyamuk Ades and Jah Love Climbing. Many lines are unnamed on the rock, which causes confusion between the topo and the wall.

**Gear.** At least 70 m rope and 16 quickdraws per theCrag; a 2023 blog used 70 m and 14 draws for the wider Batu Caves area.

**Contacts and guidebook.** Park contact +6012 481 2005. theCrag links a topo at http://ramonfadli.com/topo-batu-caves/ — not opened here, currency unknown. No current printed guidebook verified.

**Emergency.** 999. Hospital Selayang, Lebuhraya Selayang–Kepong, 68100 Batu Caves, +603-6126 3333 (Selangor health department).

## Gaps

- Whether the RM5/RM10 charge is a permit, an entrance ticket or both is unclear; the official site uses "permit", blogs use "entrance".
- Helmet enforcement is not documented by the operator, only by visitors.
- Route lengths and pitch counts are mostly unpublished; theCrag route pages returned 403.
- No dated bolt inspection or rebolting record; sling anchors dated only to a December 2023 note.
- Official route total (100+) conflicts with the database (80); no reconciliation.
- Land tenure, lease and insurance arrangements not published.
- No verified rescue service or response time.
