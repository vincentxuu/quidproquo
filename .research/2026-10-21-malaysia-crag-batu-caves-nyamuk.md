Checked 2026-10-21. One wall only; Batu Caves is a cluster of separate cliffs. theCrag and Mountain Project are community databases, not land-manager or club publications.

## Article facts

**Rock and style.** Limestone karst, about 400 million years old. Nyamuk is steep and technical rather than slabby; the 2023 blog singles out Fumakilla for tufas. Sectors run 4–32 m under cliffs of roughly 150 m.

**Routes and grades.** 106 routes: 73 sport, 29 boulder, 4 trad (theCrag, 2026-10-16). UKC lists 100. Histogram 4a–8b+; theCrag says Nyamuk is "generally harder" than Damai but has easier lines. 2,628 ascents. Sector counts: Anopheles 4, Shieldtox 26, Dengue 7, Larva 3 (trad), Fumakilla 23, Ridsect 15, Boulder 14, Malaria 14.

**Season and sun.** No published season. North-east aspect, morning sun; how early it leaves depends on the time of year, and MP advises climbing after the wall goes into shade. November–February is the north-east monsoon across peninsular Malaysia — wet, but no crag-specific closure found.

**Access.** 13 km north of KL. KTM Komuter to Batu Caves then Grab or walk, or about 20 minutes by car from the city centre. Park on Jalan TIB 3 or nearby streets; do not park on Lorong Melati. 2026 approach: start beside Surau Al Muhajirin at the Jalan TIB 3 / Lorong Melati junction, follow Lorong Melati to the last house, turn left through residents' gravel parking, path begins at a cleared alcove. Under 10 minutes, moderate uphill, proper footwear.

**Fees and land.** Free; no permit found. Red roadside signs read, translated: "Warning, High risk limestone zones are prohibited from invading. Any accident is under its sole responsibility." theCrag calls this a discouragement, not a closure; UKC records no access issues. Land status is unpublished — do not assert public or private.

**Bolts and anchors.** Mixed and community-maintained. MP describes stainless hardware, one large glue-in staple per anchor and a ram's-horn lower-off. theCrag warns humidity accelerates corrosion. No dated bolt-condition report for Nyamuk was found; no bolting organisation is named beyond UKC's "Supported by the Malaysian Bolting Fund" tag.

**Hazards.** Mosquitoes define the place — "nyamuk" is Malay for mosquito, and belayers are most exposed. Heat and humidity. Assume loose or corroded hardware until inspected.

**Contacts and guidebook.** No club, guide or landowner contact verified. MP says Climb Malaysia is mostly current but gives no edition or date.

**Emergency.** 999. Hospital Selayang, Lebuhraya Selayang–Kepong, 68100 Batu Caves, +603-6126 3333 (Selangor health department).
