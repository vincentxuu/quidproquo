## Article facts

**White Wall.** 30 routes, 5c–7c, wall about 25 m, 865 ascents. East aspect, morning sun, shade from 12.30 pm, wet in rain. Full bolted route list published with lengths and bolt counts; two multipitch lines stand out — Kick a Cloud 6b, 33 m, 2 pitches, and White Awake 6a, 55 m, 4 pitches. Known issues logged on the wall page: loose holds on Lockdown Madness, a loose fifth bolt on Kick a Cloud, loose rock and runout on The Swinging Gumby, brittle rock on pitch 4 of White Awake. No usage fee per the 2023 blog; described as newly bolted then.

**Comic Wall.** 12 routes, 5c–7a, 16 m, 203 ascents. Always shaded, overhung, south aspect, next to a small Indian temple. Private land and only "tolerated": the page asks climbers to respect it or "the owners… might start cutting off the bolts". Bolting warning created roughly mid-August 2026: anchors have been replaced but "almost all the lines have quite rusty bolts on the way up… should be earmarked for rebolting". A separate comment reports a loose top-anchor nut on Kampai. Many anchors were changed to stainless chain in 2020.

**Red Rocks.** 15 routes, 5c–7b, 16 m, 56 ascents, 13 sport and 2 trad. North aspect, shade most of the day; very soft, polished limestone likened to Pra-nang; 60 m rope needed for some lines; most bolts aged, some rebolted, newer routes far left. Two dated community warnings: glass shards at the trail start under a large tree, with a reported foot impalement near the fixed ropes (created about June 2026); and a bee colony in a hole at the start of Yen's Route / Nacho Cheese, just below the fixed rope (created about late August 2026). A loose sixth bolt was reported on Return of the Spotted Owl – Left; both Owl routes were rebolted on 14 May 2023. Approach is from Gua Damai Extreme Park, parking by the BMX park, about 5 minutes.

**Nanyang Wall.** 47 routes, 5c–8b, 18 m, 979 ascents, all sport. East aspect, shade around 10 am. Left side is government land looked after by a caretaker (chickens, street dogs); right side has tufas and moderates. The "area is OPEN" notice dates to January 2013 and is stale as a current access statement. Approach: park at the end of Jalan SM 3A, 50 m over a small bridge, left between gardens.

**Volleyball Site / Gua Lepak.** 45 routes, 5c–8b, 19 m, 961 ascents, all sport. East aspect; morning sun, better in the afternoon. Sits on the rooftop of a food court built around 2023 against the wall, which cut most original routes to roughly half length. Entry RM10 for tourists, RM5 for locals. The gate to the roof is sometimes unattended.

**Bolting and land.** No single bolting organisation is named across these pages; individual developers are credited per route (Radzi, Danial Idraki, Laurent Lacabanne, Nasarizal, Patrick Andrey, Man Dyno, Akmal Noor, Aril). Land status differs wall by wall and is only explicitly stated for Comic (private, tolerated) and Nanyang left (government).

**Emergency.** 999. Hospital Selayang, Lebuhraya Selayang–Kepong, 68100 Batu Caves, +603-6126 3333.
