## Article facts

- Rock type: limestone, with caves and paddy-field views; 55 m walls in the Si Mata Tiga sector.
- Routes: 28 sport routes, 5c to 7b+, split between a 9-route Siputeh sector and a 19-route Si Mata Tiga sector.
- Season: a morning crag that heats up around 15:00; most routes are described as staying dry in rain; the Kedah wet season follows the northeast monsoon (November–March), so the driest overlap with cool mornings is late December to February.
- Access: own transport recommended; from the junction either a sturdy bridge and a 20-minute jungle hike to Si Mata Tiga, or a temporary log bridge and a 5-minute walk to Siputeh; the walk itself is under 5 minutes at the crag.
- Rules: parking on the empty plot beside the paddy field without blocking the road; the farm tracks are narrow and farmers need access; rockfall is a known hazard and the belayer must wear a helmet (trip notes).
- Land status: open; no permit entry for Bukit Keplu appears in the Kedah/Perlis lists reviewed.
- Stay: Bayu Damai Homestay about 500 m, Sena Village Homestay about 2 km.
