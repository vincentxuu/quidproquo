# Fairy Cave / Gunung Sireh, Bau — publication evidence

Checked 2026-10-21. Covers the Fairy Cave area above Bau, Sarawak: Batman Wall, Fairy Cave Wall, Nepenthes Wall, Tiger Wall, Zoo Wall, Staircase Wall, The Groove, Orchid Wall and Stage Wall. Fairy Cave is the cave and nature reserve; Gunung Sireh is the hill. theCrag is a community database; Sarawak Forestry Corporation is the land-manager source.

## Article facts

**Rock and style.** Limestone. Batman Wall is steep and overhung with a large roof; the surrounding walls mix vertical and slabby ground. "Crown jewel among Sarawak's climbing places" is theCrag's phrase, not an official one.

**Routes and grades.** 88 routes, FR 5a–8c, 1,904 ascents. Batman Wall alone: 49 routes, 6a–8c, 22 m, 1,601 ascents. Subareas: Tiger 13, Batman 49, Zoo 4, Staircase 3, Fairy Cave Wall 3 (closed), Nepenthes 4 (closed), The Groove 5, Orchid 3, Stage 4.

**Aspect and season.** Except west-facing Zoo Wall, all sectors face south. theCrag says all-day shade May–September; October–April the walls catch sun but trees give some shade; December–April the rock gets wet in the heavy-rain season. Batman and Stage walls stay climbable through heavy rain because the roof shades them — until about 1 pm in December.

**Closures.** Fairy Cave Wall and Nepenthes Wall are closed under "new legislation by Sarawak Forestry"; no date is published and the Sarawak Forestry page carries no notice. Zoo Wall is at Red Turn Mini Zoo and was closed for renovation as of September 2014 — an old note, not a current statement.

**Access.** About 30–50 km from Kuching depending on the source (Sarawak Tourism says about 50 km; other pages say 30 km), 45–60 minutes by road. Bus No. 2 from Kuching from 6 am to Bau, then about 9 km by bus, minivan (RM2.50), taxi or hitching. Taxi from Kuching about 30 minutes to Bau plus 15 minutes to the caves. No food or water at the caves.

**Fees.** Cave entry is "around RM1" in 2026 sources; a 2023 blog paid RM10. Walls inside the nature reserve are quoted at RM1 for citizens and RM5 for foreigners. Batman Wall is community-managed and the two theCrag pages disagree: the Batman page says RM15 per climber per day since 2015, contact Daniel +60 137804548; the area page says RM50 for two people, RM100 for up to 10, RM200 for larger groups, contact Joseph +60138085516, and reads as stale. Do not pick one silently — verify on arrival.

**Ethics and rules.** Batman Wall has only one ground-up start. All other routes require a "Batman start": clip-stick the first bolt, then have a partner hoist the leader. Route-finding is hard without a local; only some lines are labelled.

**Bolts and anchors.** All routes use stainless glue-in bolts with double-bolt chain anchors and two permanent keylock carabiners — clip and lower off. First bolting was 1999 by an Australian team organised by Malcolm and Eunice Jitam with state funding; Ch'ien Lee and Glenn MacNair added lines; Alexander Hölke developed most of the wall between 2007 and 2020. Anchor warning logged on Rama-Rama (6c+, 40 m). Boleh Bah Kalau Kau 6a has had its anchor removed and is marked do not climb.

**Contacts.** Daniel +60 137804548 (Batman Wall, per theCrag). Kuching Climbers is the community to contact for weekend access.

**Emergency.** 999. Nearest hospital not verified.
