## Article facts

- Rock type: limestone karst (Perlis shares the Thai karst belt); theCrag tags the crag under Perlis with limestone cliffs. Section C carries the only trad route in the area listing.
- Sectors: five named groups (A+B, C, D, E, F); heights 15–43 m; only Section A+B and E have meaningful ascent counts (36 and 26).
- Grades: 4a, 4a+, 4b, 5b, 5c, 6a, 6a+, 6b, 6b+, 6c and above; the distribution is densest from 5c to 6b.
- Season: Perlis best January–April; public park means weekend/holiday crowds.
- Access: 33 km north of Kangar past Kaki Bukit; small car park at the cave entrance, roadside parking allowed if it does not block traffic; sector approaches are described on the individual section pages.
- Rules and fees: public park entrance RM5 foreigners / RM2 Malaysians (theCrag, 2023 figure); camping at the park (theCrag Perlis page: about a dollar a night), free camping at the other Perlis crags; Perlis forest-permit lists do not name Gua Kelam.
- Land status: public park; the same parent region notes the possibility of long cave approaches elsewhere in Perlis.
- Local contacts: Perlis Climbers, perlisclimb@gmail.com, +60129466569 (WhatsApp, Amirul) — theCrag Perlis page states a lot of Perlis information is not online and to contact them.
