## Article facts

- Rock type: limestone, east side of a big rock in a public recreational park; walls from vertical to slightly overhanging, 20 m.
- Routes: 28 sport routes with a wide grade spread (theCrag lists 15 grade steps; the community travelogue calls it "about 30 routes from 5b to 8a"). Both figures are given with their sources.
- Season: shade after noon, so afternoon and early morning work; Kedah/Perak interior rains follow the northeast monsoon.
- Access: Grab about RM10 from the Ipoh old town, about 3 km; enter through the short rail gate; street or park car park parking; the approach from the car park is flat.
- Rules: private property but freely accessible; park responsibly; no fee or permit published.
- Land status: private land inside a public recreational park.
- Stay: Ipoh has hostels through hotels; the city is on the KTM ETS line from KL.
