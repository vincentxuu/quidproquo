## Article facts

- Pulau Tuba: 20 sport routes on limestone, 6a to 7a+, 17 m, in a single sector (Gua Batu Beach); private land with a named permission contact; RM5 boat from Kuah Ferry Station, service 09:00–19:00 at odd hours only; walk west from the jetty to Kampung Tepi Laut and Gua Batu.
- Pulau Jong: deep-water bouldering only, boat charter, no access process verified this session.
- Season: Langkawi sits on the west coast of the peninsula, outside the northeast-monsoon rain belt that hits the east coast and Sarawak west (MetMalaysia). Perlis (the closest climbing region) is driest January–April.
- Land status: private land on both islands; permission is by phone, not by permit.
- Local contacts: Rozaiman, BlankFace Adventures, 014 817 5946.
