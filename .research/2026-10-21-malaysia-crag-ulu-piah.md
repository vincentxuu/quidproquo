## Article facts

- Rock type: limestone, on the Mount Hugh Low massif near Tambun, Ipoh, Perak; discovered by More Manjat and bolted by the Climbing Addict Society crew.
- Routes: 27 sport routes, 5c to 8b, in four lettered sectors; the C(icak) sector also carries bouldering problems.
- Season: the crag page gives a "Morning sun" aspect note; Perak is inland, so the northeast-monsoon rain is not the coastal type but the wet season still shortens usable mornings.
- Access: own transport; search Bai Yun Si Thai Buddhist Temple, drive about 50 m past it, the crag is on the left beside a river; higher ground clearance advised because the approach is rough and pothole-ridden.
- Rules: notify the landowner by call or WhatsApp before going, giving headcount and time (Haji Hamid, +60 19-573 6787); no published fee or permit; community reports describe a fatal rockfall risk and require the belayer to wear a helmet.
- Land status: private land, access by landowner permission rather than a permit.
- Stay: any homestay near Tambun; no shops at the crag itself.
