## Sector descriptions and ethics (verbatim points)

- Fisher Valley: "This crag was started in early 2019 by asia outdoors. The area is now under the ownership of CatBa Climbing. Further development since 2022 has seen access trail improvements, toilet and shower facilities were built, platforms for relaxing and viewing put up. There is WiFi at the crag now to improve safety and the number of climbs has doubled. Typhoon Yagi devastated the area in 2024 but repairs and improvements continue into 2026 with a new bouldering area and rebuilt viewing platforms." Tags: Dry in the rain, Morning sun, Sheltered from wind. "The land owner intends on allowing camping here too, inquire with Mr. Zoom for details." "The access pass for the crag is included with a stay at Kingfisher Nest. Kingfisher Nest is operated by Cat Ba Climbing, where you can also rent climbing gear."
- Hidden Valley: "Climbing area owned and maintained by Cat Ba Climbing / Mr. Zoom. Wide range of styles to climb, from juggy lines and slab routes to overhanging roof climbs... Great place to learn lead and / or multi-pitch climbing. Small bouldering cave. Toilet. Camping and campfires allowed. Wasps nests are more likely to appear during the summer months. There are many mosquitos, bring repellent." Tags: Dry in light rain, Always shady, Sheltered from wind. "It's better than it looks in the topos; the jungle rules out getting photos that do it justice."
- The Cave: "Tucked in the residential area of Cat Ba Town... Stays dry all the time except when the humidity is 100% the walls will sweat, with the exception of Sheltered Nook, Slingswing and after heavy rain possibly Breakout." "Houses a shrine as well as protected wildlife (owls)." "There is a Buddhist meditation area in the cave, please show your respect and only climb in designated areas." "This is government land but access is through private property... it's a great crag for a rainy day and thus worth it." History: "Bolted by team from Asia Outdoors in 2014/2015 and Cat Ba Climbing 2016. new routes 2024 by Catba climbing."
- Butterfly Valley: "The biggest crag of Cat Ba Island." Tags: Dry in the rain, Morning sun, Wet in rain. "Due to the potential of rock fall anytime outdoors please understand that helmets are considered REQUIRED to climb anywhere here on the island. The Vietnamese government is looking for an excuse to shut down climbing in the area, don't be the reason we lose access to this beautiful area." "Many of the routes here have either rams horn anchors, permanent carabiner anchor or quick links. please only use this equipment for lowering and cleaning purposes. If you are top roping on any line please set up your own anchor system." History: "Bolted mainly by Erik Ferjentsik and Onslo Carrington between 2007 and 2010."

## Handling notes

- The article's recommended plan (Fisher + Hidden rather than Butterfly) is based on the sector descriptions: Butterfly's own page carries "Most of the bolts as well as the top look pretty rusty" and "Rock still falling", while Fisher records 2024 typhoon damage with repairs continuing into 2026 and Hidden records broken-bolt and rust warnings. The article states which warning belongs to which area rather than implying a general safety verdict.
- Ben Beo: the source says the government closed it and also still lists 8 routes and a 25,000 VND access fee. The article prints the closure, labels it as a database-tier statement, and does not publish a route table for it.
- Access passes are per-area and differ (30,000 Fisher, 50,000 Hidden, Butterfly pass at Langur's plus 30,000 to the farm, 20,000 The Cave on exit). All dated 2026 in the source except Butterfly's farm fee, which is undated.
- "Helmets are considered REQUIRED to climb anywhere here on the island" is repeated on every Cat Ba sector page, so the article states it once as an island-wide rule.
- Top-rope etiquette: several sector pages say the fixed rams-horn anchors, permanent carabiners and quick links are only for lowering and cleaning, and that anyone top-roping must set up their own anchor. The article carries that instruction.
