# Hữu Lũng east side and new areas evidence

2026-10-21. Scope: the twelve sub-areas east of the village and north/south of it — WoofStuck (13), Passe-Muraille (11), Thánh Gióng (23), Cobra Wall (14), Primitive Wall (7), Infinity Cave (10), Yuji Fire Cave (4), Weeping Wall (9), The Crying Moon (8), Floatin' Shore (9), Papaya Wall (7), The Arch (5) = 120 routes, with the 147 routes of the main-valley article making up the crag's 267.

The split follows the coordinates on the theCrag sector pages: the main-valley article covers the walls from Head Wall (21.604,106.349) west along the road to Dead End and K Wall (21.609,106.310); this article covers the cluster around Duc Bungalow at WoofStuck (21.615,106.376) plus Papaya Wall (21.632,106.373) and The Arch (21.566,106.411). Coordinates are straight-line; they are not walking distances.

## Grade conversion notes

Irregular source grades and the conversion the script produces: "6a+/b" (Mango Crisis is in the other article) — in this scope "6b+/c" (Eugene) → 5.10d; "7b/b+" (Heart of the Beast) → 5.12b; "7a+/b" (Buckshot) → 5.12a; "8a/a+" (Lotus and Áo Dài) → 5.13b; "8c - 9a" (Chipu hâm) → 5.14b, the script takes the first grade of the two. "7c+" → 5.13a; "8b+" → 5.14a. All YDS cells were generated by `climbing-grade-convert.mjs --write` and re-checked with `--check`.

## Handling notes

- Primitive Wall is explicitly flagged as under development with a rockfall hazard on the source page. The article repeats that warning rather than presenting the seven routes as normal.
- Floatin' Shore's own page says the grades are "only indicative" and that the names come from a VietClimb Instagram post of 8 October 2025. The article keeps that caveat with the table.
- The Arch's approach is a 20-minute uphill clay hike that the source calls extremely slippery when wet, and the source itself says to budget a full day. The article puts it on a full-day plan, not a two-hour one.
- The Crying Moon has no published approach text; the article says the approach is described on the way to Weeping Wall rather than inventing a walk time.
- The crag page's own advice is 20+ quickdraws, 25 for the longest lines; Cobra Wall's page asks for at least 16 draws and a 70 m rope. Both numbers are printed with their source.
