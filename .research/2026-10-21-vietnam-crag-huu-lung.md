## Grade conversion notes

## Rain and sun tags — what the sector pages actually say

Re-checked on 2026-10-21 after the first draft of the article inherited a per-sector sun/rain table from the trip note. The Hữu Lũng sector pages carry their weather tags inline in the Description block, not in a separate tag list. What actually exists:

- "Dry in the rain": Dragon Wall, Infinity Cave
- "Morning sun": L'Ile Noire, Passe-Muraille, Thánh Gióng, Drone Wall
- "Afternoon sun": Dragon Wall, Weeping Wall
- "Always shady": WoofStuck
- "Afternoon shade" (in prose, not a tag): Drone Wall
- Floatin' Shore: "One of the few crags enjoying all-day shade" in the description prose
- Head Wall, Squirrel Wall, La Conche, Papaya Wall, Cobra Wall, Primitive Wall, Yuji Fire Cave, K Wall, The Arch, The Crying Moon, Chúc Sức Khỏe: no sun or rain tag at all

The trip note `docs/travel-notes/2026-12至2027-02-東南亞攀岩行程.md` §5.8 lists per-sector sun/rain values (for example "La Conche 擋小雨", "Passe-Muraille 擋小雨", "Squirrel Wall 擋小雨", "Papaya Wall 全曬") that could not be reproduced from the sector pages. Those values are not used in the articles; a missing tag is written as "來源沒標" rather than inferred.

## Handling notes

- The 5 Dead End boulders are recorded by theCrag as V grades, not Font, so no Font→V conversion applies; the article keeps the V column as given and says so.
- Route length is the listed length, not a descent prescription. Good Morning Vietnam is 55 m over 3 pitches and Les Sorbonnards (Passe-Muraille, other article) is 60 m over 3 pitches; the crag page advises 20+ draws, 25 for the longest lines.
- Access fees differ per sector (15,000 / 20,000 / 25,000 VND) and are collected on site by land owners; the article lists them per sector rather than giving one "typical" number.
- K Wall and Squirrel Wall have no published approach text; the article says so instead of guessing a walk time.
