## Conflicts and how they are handled

- Homestay prices: travel note (older) vs live VietClimb pages. Live pages win; articles state 470,000 half board / 550,000 full board per person per night, checked October 2026, for both Mao and Duc.
- Cat Ba guide book edition: the shop product page says "2018 edition", the theCrag Lan Hạ description describes a guide published February 2016 (140 pages). Both statements are printed; the article tells the reader to confirm on site.
- Asia Outdoors: the Lan Hạ page still tells independent climbers to get a pass "from Asia Outdoors" and mentions Slo Pony Adventures; the Cat Ba Island page says Cat Ba Climbing and Langur Adventures, and the operator's own site says "Since the closure of Asia Outdoors, the situation with gear rental is a little more difficult". The articles use the current operators and flag the stale reference.
- Hữu Lũng route count: theCrag 267, 8a.nu / Vertical-Life 231 (20 crags), guidebook 122. Three numbers, three sources, all printed with their source.
- Ben Beo: DB says government closed it and 8 routes are still listed. Printed as a closure with a DB-tier label, not as a gazette.
- Quốc Oai: DB says sport climbing forbidden since 2019, bouldering tolerated. Printed as a DB-tier statement with no government notice located.
