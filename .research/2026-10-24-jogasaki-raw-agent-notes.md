# 城ヶ崎 (Jogasaki) — climber trip reports and videos

Compiled 2026-10-24. Every item below was opened (page text fetched, or the YouTube metadata and description pulled with yt-dlp). Quotes are copied verbatim from the source. Grades are written the way each author wrote them; where sources disagree, the disagreement is noted.

## (a) Trip reports

### Seaside area (シーサイド; sport, reached by rappel)

1. **初めての城ヶ崎シーサイド(2023.03.28)** — note / H.Tohsaki — https://note.com/htohsaki/n/nafb43bfa496a
   - Climbed 2023-03-28, posted 2023-04-05. Parked at 八幡野 一本松駐車場, about 10–15 min walk to the rappel point. To get in, you climb over a fence, then rappel from an anchor.
   - On the climb back out: 「岩にはボルトやつかむための半円の金属器具も十分についており…スリング・カラビナとかヌンチャクとかをハーネスと支点接続して行けば、安全確保しながら登れるのでアプローチシューズでも問題なかった」. The author's mentor said approach shoes are better than climbing shoes, because climbing shoes slip on the metal staples.
   - Routes: 名探偵登場 5.8 (大磯ロック) RP; ハジノライン 5.10a, 1 tension; 海賊フック 5.10b, several tensions on the 「核心部のスラブトラバース」. On the rock: 「海辺だからかどれ触ってもなんかざらついてて滑る気がして怖さを少し感じる」. The crag was empty (「貸切状態」) on a rainy-forecast day.

2. **ナイスマウンテンズ：城ヶ崎 シーサイド** — nicecats.sakura.ne.jp — https://nicecats.sakura.ne.jp/mountains/2023/230221.html
   - Climbed 2023-02-21 (Tue), staying at the climbers' minshuku 大重丸 (八幡野). 「シーサイドの磯は歩いては降りられないので、懸垂下降でアプローチします」.
   - 「シーサイドはすごく人気のあるエリアなので、いつも結構人が居るらしいですが、この日はなんと僕らの貸し切り」. It was a cold day with snow on the hills that morning.
   - On 風に吹かれて: 100岩 gives 5.11a and the ガメラ本 gives 5.10c–5.11b; 「登るラインによって5.10c、5.11a、5.11bとグレードが変わる」. The author topped out the 5.10c line but needed many tensions on the 5.11b line. Also mentions トムボーイ 5.10b/c, チェシャネコ 5.10d, ピスタチオ 5.12b (this author's grade), タイトボーイ 5.10d and 気分は最高 5.10c (OS by a partner).

3. **城ヶ崎体感グレードメモ** — FC2 blog "Don't Think, Feel" (tkd0831) — http://tkd0831.blog.fc2.com/blog-entry-597.html?sp
   - Posted 2025-01-27 after Seaside days on 1/5 and 1/16. 「城ヶ崎のグレードはデシマルど真ん中！って思っていたけど、5.10は辛めだった」.
   - Personal grades (PG):
     - ピーターパン 5.10b → PG 5.10c (「全然10bではなさそう。中間部の距離だし、最後のスローパー地帯」)
     - タイトボーイ 5.10d → PG 5.11a (「シーサイドの意見としては10じゃない、11aだという意見が多かった」)
     - 風に吹かれて 5.11a: the lower line is about 11b; the easiest line is about 5.10+
     - 海賊フック 5.10b → PG 5.10c. The official line is the slab traverse: 「ノーハンドに近いトラバースも脚力が必要でバランシー」
     - チェシャネコ 5.10d → PG 5.11a
     - ホワイトシャーク 5.11c, "hard 7a"
     - シンデレラボーイ 5.13a/b: "hard 8a / soft 13b"
   - Also notes that the crux crimps on スプラッシュ were 「ぬめりすぎて」 (too slick) in January.
   - The same author (竹田遼) also left a 5.10c felt-grade for ピーターパン on ROCKTOPO: https://www.rocktopo.net/routes/763/climbing_reports. Most other ROCKTOPO logs there say 5.10b (dated examples: 2025-02-20 and 2026-01-17).

4. **城ケ崎 シーサイドエリア クライミング** — kubo's.blog (s-kubota) — https://kuboclimbingta.com/2025/01/19/climbing_at_jyougasaki_seaside/
   - Posted 2025-01-19. Paid parking at 八幡野. Toilet at the はしだての浪洗い lot, which is free but meant for tourists, so long-staying climbers should use the paid lot. No convenience store nearby.
   - Rappel point: the self-belay tree 「既に枯れており、かなり腐っていたので積極的に体重をあずけるのはやめた方が良い」. Exit: 「ハーネスを付けたままで、セルフビレイをハンガーにかけ替えながら登ります。最後の疲れたタイミングで登るので、事故には十分注意」.
   - Routes:
     - ルーズガール 5.10b: the starting crack has no bolts, so cams are needed. Loose jug on the left at the exit (「浮石には"×"が書かれていることも」).
     - エアーダンス 5.12a
     - ピスタチオ 5.12a (mid-route dead-point on small holds)
     - 腰痛 5.12b: one of the few bolted routes in the Sunrise area, which is mostly trad.

     - シンデレラボーイ 13a: 「確かにすごく難しいムーブはないけど、繋げるのは超キツそう。特に前半のカチ」

7. **【城ヶ崎】虎の穴RP！** — きい山歩 — https://kiisampo.com/jogasaki-toranoana-rp/
   - Climbed 2025-01-18, two days before 大寒 (the coldest time of year): 「朝から快晴でポカポカ。むしろ暖か過ぎてフリクションの低下が心配」. 「南向きのシーサイドはあまり北風も入らない」.

8. **城ケ崎でクライミング生活を営もう①** — note / Sugiyann — https://note.com/sugiyann/n/n2d5cfd456171
   - Posted 2026-02-12. Recommends the paid lot; then a 10–15 min walk. 「懸垂下降があるため、スキルのない人は岩場にたどり着くことすら出来ない。登り口をランヤードで無理やり降りられなくはないが、万一事故ると間違いなく致死」.
   - On the rock: 「塩っ気のあるチャリチャリした皿ホールドは抜け感があって信用ならないが、意外に滑らない」. On chalk: 「チョーク跡がつきすぎて、一体何が何かわかるまでに時間はかかる」.
   - Routes: ホワイトシャーク 11c as a warm-up; シンデレラボーイ, about 30 moves (「おすすめ13の中に間違いなく入る１本」); スプラッシュ (「ビレイ点は波が来ていて」, with a scary runout above).

9. **城ヶ崎シーサイド コロッサス記録** — 秀峰登高会 (河合) — https://syuho.but.jp/?p=6556
   - Posted 2025-03-01; attempts from 2023-01 through 2025, completed after 18 days and 49 tries. Colossus was published as 5.12c and is now considered 5.13a/b; the author felt hard 13b to easy 13c.

10. **岩登り・城ヶ崎シーサイド** — 三峰山岳会 会報 363 (西村宏一) — https://www.mitsumine.gr.jp/kaiho/363/jogasaki.html

### Area access notes (from each `BG/area/<slug>/` page)
- フナムシロック: 伊豆急富戸駅 徒歩約15分. No dedicated parking; use paid diving-shop lots (伊豆海の森スマイルキャンプ場, 4 min walk; マリンステージ富戸, bookable, 18 min). Close the entrance door behind you. The descent has bad footing.
- 漁火: steep descent with bad footing, slippery after rain. 12 min walk from フナムシロック.
- ジャクソン: 10 min walk from 漁火; the homepage says 道路から徒歩20秒.
- 日蓮崎: 城ヶ崎海岸駅 徒歩約30分. Paid lot 伊豆海洋公園 (bring exact change; pay at the back of the sign board with an envelope into the post box). **蓮着寺駐車場は利用不可**.
- 田中磯: いがいが根駐車場, about 20 min walk on a gentle uphill. 伊豆高原駅 徒歩約20分.
- こさいつな / 橋立: 八幡野有料駐車場, about 15 min walk. **橋立観光駐車場は不可**. こさいつな: some problems collapsed in a typhoon.

## 3. RULES / SAFETY updates

### JFA (freeclimb.jp). Dates come from the WP REST `date` field.
- **Access issue list** (https://freeclimb.jp/activities/deal-with/issue-list/), entry 伊豆・城ヶ崎海岸 under「利用注意・利用規約注意」: after accidents on consecutive days in **2009-02**, the 県, 市, 警察 and 消防 held an on-site investigation in **2009-04**. JFA negotiated with the authorities and use continues. 「登攀禁止と隣り合わせの岩場であることをしっかり認識し、各エリアの利用ルールを遵守してほしい」. The same page lists nearby 西伊豆・雲見烏帽子山 as 登攀禁止 after a 2016-03 fatality, which is useful context.
- **2025-03-29 アクセス情報：静岡県・城ヶ崎 – テント泊** (https://freeclimb.jp/access/10179/): someone is tent-camping in a 城ヶ崎 parking lot. 「伊豆では、テント泊は指定地区以外は禁止」. Copycats could lead to parking-lot or crag closures.
- **2024-12-09 アクセス情報：静岡県・城ヶ崎ボルダー – ホールド欠損** (https://freeclimb.jp/access/10085/): a key hold on 鬼火SD (三段) broke. A slightly worse hold appeared. The reporter thinks the grade is probably unaffected but has not confirmed it.
- **2022-04-15 注意喚起：静岡県・城ヶ崎** (https://freeclimb.jp/access/6859/): after the 2022-04-08 climbing fatality at 南伊豆町妻良・吉田海岸, 伊東市観光課 (which manages 城ヶ崎海岸) contacted JFA: ① safety inquiries are increasing; ② **the city does not officially sanction climbing at 城ヶ崎**, so it cannot post signs or make announcements itself; ③ the city knows it is a climbing spot and is not considering a ban, **but would have to consider one if accidents become frequent**. Older than the 2023–26 window but the most relevant official-stance item.
- 2022-03-10 注意喚起(エリア関連) (https://freeclimb.jp/access/6823/): a loose jug near the end of マザークラック (ファミリークラック area). This is a route, not a boulder.
- JFA 静岡県・城ヶ崎 area page (https://freeclimb.jp/tokai-hokuriku-area/%e9%9d%99%e5%b2%a1%e7%9c%8c%e3%83%bb%e5%9f%8e%e3%83%b6%e5%b4%8e/, posted 2025-01-14) links only the 2024-12-09 notice.
- **Rebolting**: the JFA rebolt list (https://freeclimb.jp/activities/environment/rebolt-achievement/) has only 2 城ヶ崎 entries, both **2007-12-01** at ファミリークラック (イヴ, インディアンドール; FIXEリング付きハンガー). **No 2023–2026 bolt replacement or closure notice found** for 城ヶ崎 on freeclimb.jp. A WP site search for 城ヶ崎 returned only the items above.
- Site-level hold-loss item: 城ヶ崎ボルダーガイド news 2026-02-15 and the 潮風初段 page: foothold broke on 2026-02-14 (BG/kosaitsuna/shiokaze/).

### Ito city / Geopark / 環境省
- Ito city, 観光施設及び公園内行為許可申請 (https://www.city.ito.shizuoka.jp/gyosei/soshikikarasagasu/kankoka/shinseishodownload/830.html, 更新日 2026-08-19): 城ヶ崎海岸 is listed as a city 観光施設. A permit is needed for commercial photo or film shoots, events/competitions and **any drone flight (commercial or personal)**. Apply 6 months to 5 business days ahead. Flights over the sea are outside the city's jurisdiction (ask 県 or 海上保安庁). **Nothing about climbing** on this page. No Ito city climbing page found.
- 伊豆半島ジオパーク, 門脇埼 geosite (https://izugeopark.org/geosites/kadowakizaki/): the 危険情報 section says 崖は崩れたりすることもあります, don't go near the edge, watch for 落石 below cliffs. The 城ヶ崎ピクニカルコース (ふたまた～ぼら納屋) was closed for works 2024-08-21 to 2025-03-31. 門脇駐車場 costs ¥500/day (first 15 min free).
- Geopark いがいが根 geosite (https://izugeopark.org/geosites/igaigane/): 「いがいが根は城ヶ崎の中でも崖の高さが低い場所です。**高潮や高波の際には注意が必要**」. いがいが根駐車場 has 20 spaces and a toilet about 10 min away. (This is the 田中磯 parking.)
- Geopark signboard PDFs say 設置・管理者：伊東市 観光課 (e.g. https://izugeopark.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/ito_s_jogasaki_kadowaki_90_80.pdf).
- 環境省: 城ケ崎 is listed as a spot of 富士箱根伊豆国立公園: 「静岡県伊東市…長さ9㎞におよぶ溶岩岩石海岸」, contact 伊東市観光案内所 0557-37-6105 (https://www.env.go.jp/nature/nationalparks/list/fuji-hakone-izu/spot/ ; also https://www.env.go.jp/park/fujihakone/guide/view.html). The national park designation is confirmed; no MOE climbing rule for 城ヶ崎 was found. The park's special-area zoning boundaries were not checked; MOE says to ask the 自然保護官事務所 (沼津) for exact boundaries.

### Tide and emergency
- **JMA 潮位表 伊東 (ITO)**: https://www.data.jma.go.jp/kaiyou/db/tide/suisan/suisan.php?stn=Z3. The station is at **静岡県 伊東市 富戸**, 34°54′N 139°8′E, the same village as フナムシロック/漁火/ジャクソン. The page says no datum-elevation data exists, so from 2026 onward heights are not shown relative to elevation. The tide times still show. The values are predicted astronomical tide, not observed.
- **海上保安庁 118番** (https://www.kaiho.mlit.go.jp/doc/tel118.html): the emergency number for incidents and accidents at sea. Report いつ・どこで・何があった. NET118 is available for people with hearing or speech disabilities. (Background: https://www.gov-online.go.jp/article/202506/entry-7901.html says 118 was set up in 2000.)
