## Source coverage and boundaries

RockTopo base records: 86. Article route rows after supplements: 87. Four principal grade bands: 87 rows. Ungraded projects and explicitly destroyed/restricted base records are not presented as confirmed available climbs; publication keeps route status limits explicit.

Complete public returned RockTopo rows were retained when graded and within the destination/style scope. Additional readable local-guide entries and cross-source grade differences are integrated, rather than silently replacing grades. TheCrag regional pages sometimes returned sector headings without complete route lists; guessed /routes endpoints redirected to the homepage and were not treated as route evidence. Publisher image topos (Kamuiiwa and Kogashi) exposed headings but not legible grade images in Exa; platform image open failed; no browser was available. Paper/paid guide interiors were not read. These are real access limitations, not evidence that no additional routes exist.

Asahikawa means the regional boulder group (Kamuikotan, Amekon, Shunshunai etc.), whereas Kamuiiwa is the roped cliff destination. No duplicate boulder guide is created for Kamuiiwa. MP original V1-2 / V4- values remain in notes; the displayed Font-to-V column follows the project comparison chart (5→V1, 6B→V4), never a Japanese-grade conversion.
