## Reading limits and local editorial decisions

All source checks belong to 2026-10-26; that local check date is not a claim about when any source page was updated. Sources with no dated update are not assigned invented update dates.

The route-list fetch requested a maximum of 85,000 characters and the shuttle fetch requested 30,000. Relevant returned route rows and the entire returned shuttle extraction were read. These request limits do not establish original-HTML completeness and are not remote facts to monitor. Prior CLIMBING-net text in central-sources.jsonl and the Aichi/Horai section of jfa-access.txt were re-read alongside current public fetches.

Route names, grades and sectors are single-source ROCKTOPO facts. Original grades are YDS, so no French reverse conversion was done. Blank route lengths stay unlisted; bolt counts are not used to infer length, rope requirements or safety. No guidebook interior was read. Publisher details establish bibliographic facts, not current access.

ROCKTOPO's Kotakibashi instruction conflicts with current city parking directions. The articles follow the city's park-and-ride instruction. JFA's 2022 closure alone does not prove permanent closure, and its 2025 cleanup meeting alone does not authorize ordinary parking. The event remains counterevidence in this note, not narrative history in the guide. CLIMBING-net's historical parking prose is not used to authorize parking.

The tourism page footer gives an older 25-minute walk estimate, while its main current directions say approximately 30 minutes; the latter is used. Neither its sightseeing-loop duration nor its summit duration is a climbing approach estimate.

The Mountain Project grade-comparison URL appears in the mandated project-script conversion note; no new numerical conversion relies on that page because all route grades are already YDS.
