## Source coverage and boundaries

RockTopo base records: 101. Article route rows after supplements: 100. Four principal grade bands: 100 rows. Ungraded projects and explicitly destroyed/restricted base records are not presented as confirmed available climbs; publication keeps route status limits explicit.

Complete public returned RockTopo rows were retained when graded and within the destination/style scope. Additional readable local-guide entries and cross-source grade differences are integrated, rather than silently replacing grades. TheCrag regional pages sometimes returned sector headings without complete route lists; guessed /routes endpoints redirected to the homepage and were not treated as route evidence. Publisher image-topo extraction exposed headings only. After Exa and platform image failures and unavailable CUA browser, unauthenticated curl downloaded six already-discovered public publisher JPEG grade tables; all 87 graded records were visually read with view_image and compared with RockTopo. Grade differences and alternate spellings were added per row. Cliff-line drawings were not read. Paper/paid guide interiors were not read. These are real access limitations, not evidence that no additional routes exist.
