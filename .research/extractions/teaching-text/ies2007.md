## 研究設計

這不是一篇研究，是 IES 委託的實務指引（practice guide）。

- **作者**（第 15–28 行）：Harold Pashler（主席，UC San Diego）、Patrice M. Bain（Columbia Middle School 的中學教師）、Brian A. Bottge、Arthur Graesser、Kenneth Koedinger、Mark McDaniel、Janet Metcalfe。2007 年 9 月。
- **不是系統性回顧**（Preamble，第 116–123 行）：「Authors of practice guides seldom conduct the types of systematic literature searches that are the backbone of a meta-analysis, though they take advantage of such work when it is already published. Instead, they use their expertise to identify the most important research with respect to their recommendations, augmented by a search of recent publications to assure that the research citations are up-to-date.」

- **適用科目**（第 693–697、632–634 行）：「generally applicable to subjects that demand a great deal of content learning. Social studies and science instruction are obvious examples, but the recommendations are by no means limited to those areas.」

### 證據等級的定義原文（Table 1，第 206–261 行）

**Strong**（第 207–226 行）：「In general, characterization of the evidence for a recommendation as strong requires both studies with high internal validity (i.e., studies whose designs can support causal conclusions), as well as studies with high external validity (i.e., studies that in total include enough of the range of participants and settings on which the recommendation is focused to support the conclusion that the results can be generalized to those participants and settings). Strong evidence for this practice guide is operationalized as:」
- 「A systematic review of research that generally meets the standards of the What Works Clearinghouse ... and supports the effectiveness of a program, practice, or approach, with no contradictory evidence of similar quality; OR」
- 「Several well-designed, randomized, controlled trials or well-designed quasi-experiments that generally meet the standards of the What Works Clearinghouse and support the effectiveness of a program, practice, or approach, with no contradictory evidence of similar quality; OR」
- 「One large, well-designed, randomized, controlled, multisite trial that meets the standards of the What Works Clearinghouse and supports the effectiveness of a program, practice, or approach, with no contradictory evidence of similar quality; OR」
- 「For assessments, evidence of reliability and validity that meets The Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing.」

**Moderate**（第 227–250 行）：「In general, characterization of the evidence for a recommendation as moderate requires studies with high internal validity but moderate external validity, or studies with high external validity but moderate internal validity. In other words, moderate evidence is derived from studies that support strong causal conclusions but where generalization is uncertain, or studies that support the generality of a relationship but where the causality is uncertain. Moderate evidence for this practice guide is operationalized as:」
- 「Experiments or quasi-experiments generally meeting the standards of the What Works Clearinghouse and supporting the effectiveness of a program, practice, or approach with small sample sizes and/or other conditions of implementation or analysis that limit generalizability, and no contrary evidence; OR」
- 「Comparison group studies that do not demonstrate equivalence of groups at pretest and therefore do not meet the standards of the What Works Clearinghouse but that (a) consistently show enhanced outcomes for participants experiencing a particular program, practice, or approach and (b) have no major flaws related to internal validity other than lack of demonstrated equivalence at pretest (e.g., only one teacher or one class per condition, unequal amounts of instructional time, highly biased outcome measures); OR」
- 「Correlational research with strong statistical controls for selection bias and for discerning influence of endogenous factors and no contrary evidence; OR」
- 「For assessments, evidence of reliability that meets The Standards for Educational and Psychological Testing but with evidence of validity from samples not adequately representative of the population on which the recommendation is focused.」

**Low**（第 257–261 行）：「In general, characterization of the evidence for a recommendation as low means that the recommendation is based on expert opinion derived from strong findings or theories in related areas and/or expert opinion buttressed by direct evidence that does not rise to the moderate or strong levels. Low evidence is operationalized as evidence not meeting the standards for the moderate or high levels.」

**小組自己對三個等級的白話說明**（Scope，第 649–660 行）：「When we stated that a recommendation is backed up by "strong" evidence, this generally meant that it received considerable support from randomized experimental studies, both in wellcontrolled laboratory contexts and within the context of schools. Strength levels of "moderate" and "low" imply a correspondingly weaker and narrower evidence base. When the evidence level fell short of "strong," this was usually because although the evidence was experimental in character, it was limited to laboratory studies, thus making the applicability of the results to other situations (e.g., classroom instruction) less certain.」

### 建議 1：Space learning over time（第 827–1020 行）

- **建議原文**（第 828–831 行）：「we recommend that teachers arrange for students to be exposed to key course concepts on at least two occasions—separated by a period of several weeks to several months.」
- **不適用的情況**（第 835–841 行）：課程本身就會自動複習的內容（例子：二年級數學天天用到個位數加法），「this recommendation may be unnecessary in courses where this is the case」。
- **等級與理由**（第 843、868–878 行）：Moderate。「based on three experimental classroom studies examining the effects of this practice for improving school-aged students' performance on academic content (e.g., mathematics, spelling), two experimental classroom studies that examined the effect of this strategy for improving college students' academic performance, and the hundreds of laboratory experiments which have been completed examining the effects of massed versus distributed practice on memory.」
- **證據摘要**：
  - 間隔相對於保留期太短時，期末表現較差（第 885–888、845–846 行）。
  - 間隔的下限（第 846–851 行）：「when the interval between these two occasions is not less than about 5 percent of the interval during which the information has to be retained.」
  - 總學習時間相同（第 851–857 行）：「researchers have kept constant the amount of time that students have to learn the information; thus, the observed improvement in learning is not a result of learners having more time to study the material.」
  - 間隔太長的代價小於太短（第 857–866 行）：「it makes sense to be sure to have enough spacing, but it rarely makes sense to worry about having too much.」
  - 課堂研究的數字（第 912–919 行）：八年級美國史，初次呈現後 1 週複習對 16 週複習；複習後 9 個月的期末測驗，「the 16-week delay group showed significantly greater performance (almost 100 percent increase) as compared to the 1-week delayed group.」
- **作者列的限制**（第 924–936 行）：「One limitation of the literature is that few studies have examined acquisition of complex bodies of structured information. For measurement reasons, researchers have mostly focused on acquisition of isolated bits of information (e.g., facts or definitions of vocabulary words).」接著說：「There does not appear to be any evidence to suggest that spacing benefits are confined to isolated elements of course content.」註 15（第 994–995 行）：Ausubel 與 Youssef (1965) 在一篇連貫的內分泌學文章上看到延後複習的好處，「but the comparison was with a procedure that lacked the delayed review (rather than one that included a review at a short lag).」
- **How to carry out**（第 967–978、921–954 行）：重要內容在初次接觸後至少數週、最好數月再複習。三個做法：(1) 用課堂時間複習（例：每隔一週用半堂課複習幾週前教的事實）；(2) 作業裡放 1–2 個月前教過的題目；(3) 期中與期末考採累積式。
- **Roadblocks**：
  - 1.1（第 956–965 行）：教科書的複習與習題只涵蓋最近教的內容。解法：教師自己在習題裡「sprinkling」較早的題目。
  - 1.2（第 980–986、1007–1020 行）：複習時發現學生忘了很多會令人氣餒。解法：提醒教師即使想不起來，重新學會比初學容易（savings），延後複習之後遺忘速度明顯下降。

### 建議 2：Interleave worked example solutions and problem-solving exercises（第 1032–1235 行）

- **建議原文**（第 1034–1039 行）：「When teaching mathematical or science problem solving, we recommend that teachers interleave worked example solutions and problem-solving exercises—literally alternating between worked examples demonstrating one possible solution path and problems that the student is asked to solve for himself or herself—because research has shown that this interleaving markedly enhances student learning.」適用範圍限定在數學或科學的解題教學。
- **等級與理由**（第 1041、1067–1077 行）：Moderate。「Numerous laboratory experiments provide support for the benefits of interleaving worked example solutions and problem-solving exercises. Some classroom experiments provide further evidence that the recommendation can be practically and effectively implemented in real courses at the K-12 and college levels. These experiments have explored these techniques in a variety of content domains, particularly in mathematics, science, and technology.」
- **證據摘要**：
  - 對照條件是「只做題」，要解的題目是「相似題」（第 1080–1086 行）：「students learn more by alternating between studying examples of worked-out problem solutions and solving similar problems on their own than they do when just given problems to solve on their own.」
  - 代數的實驗室實驗（Sweller 與 Cooper 1985；第 1086–1087、1043–1051 行）：八、九年級；實驗組在四組「例題＋題目」之間交替，對照組解八題。「Students in the interleaved example/problem treatment condition not only took less time to complete the eight problems, but also performed better on the post-test.」
  - **交替對「先全部例題再全部題目」**（Trafton 與 Reiser 1993，程式設計；第 1051–1056 行）：「if students are given all six examples before all six problems, they learn significantly less than if the examples and problems are interleaved, with the same six examples and six problems alternating in order.」這是指引裡直接支持「例題後面緊接題目」（而不是把例題集中放）的那一個研究。
  - 早期課堂研究（Zhu 與 Simon 1987；第 1057–1063 行）：把部分課堂活動（尤其講課）換成讀例題，「students finished a 3-year course sequence in 2 years with as good or better final test performance.」
  - **例題要不要帶解釋**（第 1111–1119 行）：「The amount of guidance and annotation that should be included in worked examples presented to students probably varies depending on the situation and the student. But at least in some studies, worked examples that did not include instructional explanations of the steps were found to be most effective. Other studies have found that labeling groups of steps within a problem solution according to what goal they seek to achieve can be helpful.」（註 26：Hausmann 與 VanLehn (in press)；Schworm 與 Renkl (2002)。註 27：Catrambone 1996、1998。）
  - **隨程度調整**（第 1130–1137 行）：「As students develop greater expertise, decreased example use and correspondingly increased problem solving appears to improve learning. Gradually "fading" examples into problems, by giving early steps in a problem and requiring students to provide more and more of the later steps as they acquire more expertise with the problem type, also seems to benefit student learning.」
  - **題目要相似還是有變化**（第 1143–1149 行）：「Finally, using worked examples and problems that involve greater variability from one example or problem to the next (e.g., changing both the values included in the problem and the problem formats), after students receive instruction on a mathematical concept, puts greater demands on students during study but pays off in better learning and post-test performance.」（註 30：Paas 與 van Merriënboer 1994；Renkl 等 1998。）注意原文附了條件「after students receive instruction on a mathematical concept」，以及代價「puts greater demands on students during study」。
- **How to carry out**（第 1150–1178、1201–1207 行）：
  - 「Instead of giving students a list of problems to solve as a homework assignment, teachers should provide a worked out solution for every other problem on the list.」（第 1151–1154 行）
  - 示範：八題作業，奇數題附解答、偶數題自己解。例題是「Solve 12 + 2x = 15 for x」，下一題是「Solve 5 + 3x = 20 for x」（第 1108–1128、1139–1141、1161–1162 行）——同一種題型、數字不同。例題上的指示語：「Study each step in this solution, so that you can better solve the next problem on your own」（第 1126–1128 行）。示範的例題只有算式步驟，沒有文字說明。
  - 直覺上解八題練習較多，但（第 1169–1173 行）「much research has shown that students typically learn more deeply and more easily from the second approach, when examples are interleaved between problems.」
  - 全班教學的做法（第 1174–1178、1201–1207 行）：先討論一個例題解法，再讓學生自己解一題（「just one!」），再回到例題（學生報告解法、其他人解釋步驟，連到建議 7），再解第二題。
  - 檢核表另有一條（第 770–772 行）：「As students develop greater expertise, reduce the number of worked examples provided and increase the number of problems that students solve independently.」
- **Roadblocks**：
  - 2.1（第 1209–1217 行）：教材不常提供大量例題解答。解法：教師合作編作業；把課本講解段的例題穿插進習題。
  - 2.2（第 1218–1235 行）：擔心學生背解法步驟而不理解概念。解法：「By having problems to solve in between the worked examples, students are motivated to pay more attention to the worked example because it helps them prepare for the next problem and/or resolve a question from the past problem.」；「students are less inclined to skim the example because they believe that the answer is obvious」。

### 建議 3：Combine graphics with verbal descriptions（第 1243–1400 行）

- **建議原文**（第 1244–1247 行）：「We recommend that teachers combine graphical presentations (e.g., graphs, figures) that illustrate key processes and concepts with verbal descriptions of those processes and concepts in order to facilitate student learning.」
- **等級與理由**（第 1249–1261 行）：Moderate。「Many laboratory experiments provide support ... Some classroom experiments and quasi-experiments provide further evidence that the recommendation can be practically and effectively implemented in real courses at the K-12 and college levels.」領域以數學、科學、科技為主。
- **證據摘要**（第 1264–1292 行）：
  - 圖加文字優於只有文字；多數研究的主題是科學過程（閃電、碟煞、打氣筒、火山）。
  - 文字要靠近圖中對應的部分（第 1269–1273 行）：「it is important that text descriptions appear near the relevant elements in visual representations to best enhance learning.」
  - 語音優於書面文字（第 1273–1277 行）：「students learn more when the verbal description is presented in audio form rather than in written text, probably because a learner cannot read text and scrutinize an accompanying graphic at the same time.」
  - 靜態圖不輸動畫（第 1277–1280 行）：「current evidence suggests that a well-chosen sequence of still pictures with accompanying prose can be just as effective in enhancing learning as narrated animations.」
  - 數學：數線（第 1281–1292 行）。
- **How to carry out**（第 1318–1387 行）：
  - 書面教材裡的圖要有簡短文字標示不熟悉的物件、描述步驟，而且（第 1336–1339 行）「These descriptions should be positioned as close as possible to the parts of the visualization being described and help students identify what specifically they should be looking at.」
  - 圖要與教的內容相關（第 1344–1351 行）：「a picture of a high school football player whose football helmet has been scarred by lightning is interesting, but it may well detract from learning about how lightning works.」
  - 不必寫實（第 1352–1363 行）：「Sometimes a more abstract or schematic picture will best illustrate a key idea, whereas a more photorealistic graphic may actually distract the learner with details that are irrelevant to the main point.」；「a well-chosen sequence of still pictures is often as, or more, effective in enhancing learning.」
  - 數學裡要定期把符號步驟連到圖（第 1372–1374 行）。
  - 多重表徵時要指出與抽象概念相關的部分（第 1383–1387 行）。
- **Roadblocks**：只有 3.1（第 1389–1400 行）：教材把圖的文字說明放在另一頁，或沒有對應的說明。解法：教師預先看教材、把學生的注意力引到對應說明；自己編教材時注意圖與說明的位置對齊。

### 建議 4：Connect and integrate abstract and concrete representations of concepts（第 1406–1643 行）

- **建議原文**（第 1408–1412 行）：「We recommend that teachers connect and integrate abstract representations of a concept with concrete representations of the same concept. Connecting different forms of representations helps students master the concept being taught and improves the likelihood that students will use it appropriately across a range of different contexts.」
- **等級與理由**（第 1414–1427 行）：Moderate。「A substantial number of laboratory experiments provide support for the benefits of connecting and interleaving both abstract and concrete representations of problems. A growing number of classroom experiments and quasi-experiments provide further evidence」。註 41 列的課堂研究六筆全部是 Bottge 掛名的（第 1469–1470 行）；Bottge 是小組成員，利益揭露段有載明（第 460–465 行）。
- **證據摘要**：
  - 只用抽象或只用具體都較差（第 1430–1437 行）：「teaching students about key principles or concepts using only abstract or only concrete representations of those concepts leads to less flexible knowledge acquisition and use」。
  - **具體表徵的代價**（第 1437–1452 行）：「other research finds that learning with concrete objects supports initial understanding of the instructed concept, but does not support the transfer of that knowledge to novel but relevant contexts.」；「although students have an easier time acquiring an initial understanding of a concept presented in a concrete form, those same students are unable to use that knowledge in a different context」（註 43：Kaminski、Sloutsky、Heckler 2006；Sloutsky 等 2005）。
  - **抽象表徵**（第 1452–1462 行）：「those students struggle slightly more to master the concept initially, but are then able to use their new understanding successfully in a different context.」；「teachers need to be aware of both the limits and benefits of providing initial instruction using concrete representations.」
  - **具體漸退**（第 1463、1482–1511 行）：指引用了「concreteness fading」這個詞並定義它（第 1488–1492 行）：「Concreteness fading is a process by which initial learning with a concrete representation occurs, and then over time, key components of the concrete representation are replaced by more idealized and abstract representations.」例子是 Goldstone 與 Sakamoto (2003) 的螞蟻覓食模擬（註 45），螞蟻換成黑點、食物換成綠色區塊；「Students in the concreteness fading condition outperformed students in the concreteonly or abstract-only condition, both on measures of initial learning and on their ability to use the principle ... to understand a different problem.」作者的用詞是「An emerging set of research」「one line of research suggests」「holds promise」（第 1463、1487、1509 行）。
  - **明確標出對應關係**（第 1512–1527 行）：「explicit marking of the relationships between different types of representations supports learning.」；「When students are not provided guidance, it is difficult for learners to identify which components of the problem can be transferred to new problems. On the other hand, when teachers explicitly identify the critical components of a representation and draw students' attention to those critical components, their performance improves.」
  - 低成就學生解實作或真實問題時學習進步（第 1527–1531 行；註 49 是 Bottge 等 2007）。
  - 小結（第 1535–1546 行）：「teachers use both abstract and concrete representations of key concepts and highlight the critical aspects of the concept to be learned (e.g., pointing out to the student which variables in the mathematical function being taught are related to which aspects of the word problem).」
- **How to carry out**（第 1547–1565、1584–1602、1617–1643 行）：
  - 以 y = 2x 為例，同一個想法的五種形式：符號、折線圖、表格、健走募款每英里 2 美元的情境、以每小時 2 英里走路。「although the "surface" form may vary, it is the "deep" structure—what does not change—that is the essence of the idea.」（第 1563–1565 行）
  - 變動一個條件（每英里 3 美元），讓學生看到各表徵怎麼跟著變（第 1584–1588 行）。
  - 學生可能抓到例子的錯誤特徵（第 1589–1602 行）：例子多半是運動，就以為平均數跟運動有關；「A variety of representations and explicit discussion of the connections between them can help students avoid such misconceptions.」
  - 太快引入新術語或符號（第 1617–1632 行）：學生可以背定義或操作符號卻沒有建立連結，知識變成「inert」。
  - 把新想法錨定在學生熟悉的故事或情境，再用「a variety of successively more abstract representations」（第 1633–1643 行）。
- **Roadblocks**：只有 4.1（第 1605–1615 行）：教科書與教師用書常沒有明寫抽象概念與具體表徵的對應。解法：「textbook publishers and teachers should clearly identify which aspects of an abstract representation and its concrete instantiation are connected.」

### 建議 5：Use quizzing to promote learning（總說，第 1651–1657 行）

「we recommend two ways of using quizzing to help students learn: (a) using "pre-questions" to activate prior knowledge and focus students' attention on the material that will be presented in class; and (b) using quizzes to re-expose students to key course content. Recommendation 6 includes a third way to use quizzing」。總項沒有自己的證據等級。

### 建議 5a：Use pre-questions to introduce a new topic（第 1659–1800 行）

- **建議原文**（第 1661–1666 行）：「We recommend that teachers use pre-questions as a way to introduce a new topic. Pre-questions (or pre-tests) help students identify what material they do not yet know, and hence need to study. In addition, responding to pre-questions automatically activates any relevant prior knowledge in the student's mind.」
- **等級與理由**（第 1668–1675 行）：Low。「based on a series of laboratory experiments primarily carried out on learning from reading written text. Most of this research has been completed with college students. It has not yet been tested as a component of regular classroom instruction.」
- **為什麼是低而不是中**（第 1678–1693 行）：證據來自**讀書面文字**的實驗，而且方向一致；評低的原因是沒在課堂口頭教學上測過。「A body of experimental studies on learning from written text has established that when students are given pre-questions to answer prior to reading both expository and narrative text, they learn more from the text than when they do not respond to such prequestions. Some of these studies have used actual classroom material (e.g., textbook chapters). However, there is little or no published experimental evidence regarding whether pre-questions will promote the learning of orally presented classroom content as well. Accordingly, even though the evidence is reasonably consistent, it has not been demonstrated using methods of delivery common in classroom practice, and thus we cannot characterize it as "strong" or "moderate."」
- **作者標明的重要但書：沒被問到的內容**（第 1694–1708 行）：「There is one important caveat to this recommendation. In some experiments in which students were not explicitly discouraged from reading the text selectively based on the pre-questions, pre-questions tended to reduce learning for non-questioned material. However, researchers have demonstrated that pre-questions do not hinder learning of non-questioned material when learners are explicitly required to read all of the material. Moreover, in such cases the advantages shown for learning of pre-questioned material remains.」結論：指定閱讀「that students are encouraged (or required) to read in full」時，被問到的內容有進步、沒被問到的內容沒有損失。
- **How to carry out**（第 1726–1732、1742–1746、1760–1781 行）：
  - 題目要針對新內容的少數重要概念；「teachers should avoid creating prequestions that highlight extraneous information.」
  - 用在口頭教學是外推（第 1742–1746 行）：「Although existing research has focused on learning from reading, we believe that a reasonable extrapolation from this research is the use of pre-questions when teachers begin oral instruction on topics.」
  - 題目的形式（第 1771–1774 行）：「These questions should be relatively quick to answer, but should require students to describe or explain their responses to the questions.」
  - 例題：「Why were people imprisoned in concentration camps?」；「What is the main cause of ocean currents?」
  - 檢核表的寫法（第 726–727 行）：「Prepare pre-questions, and require students to answer the questions, before introducing a new topic.」——要學生作答。
- **Roadblocks**：
  - 5a.1（第 1794–1800、1721–1724 行）：怎麼讓學生也注意沒被問到的內容。解法：強調整堂課與全部閱讀都重要，說明讀前題目涵蓋不了所有重要概念。
  - 5a.2（第 1734–1740、1748–1758、1783–1791 行）：這等於先給答案、助長只學考試會考的。解法：課堂討論各選項為什麼對或錯；**考題換措辭**，「to encourage learning of a complex fact, rather than learning of a particular answer when given a particular question, teachers could change the wording of test items from those used for prequestions.」
- **註 50 與內文對不上**：等級理由那一句的註 50（第 1710–1712 行）列的是 Beck 等 1997、Craig 等 2006、Driscoll 等 2003、Gholson 與 Craig 2006、King 1994/1996/2006、Rosenshine 等 1996、Wisher 與 Graesser 2007。這份清單與建議 7 的引用相同或相近，是否排版錯置見下面附錄段的對照。

### 建議 5b：Use quizzes to re-expose students to information（第 1805–1942 行）

- **建議原文**（第 1807–1816 行）：「We recommend that teachers use "closed-book" quizzes or tests as one method for re-exposing students to key course content. ... quizzes or tests that require students to actively recall specific information (e.g., questions that use fill-in-the-blank or short-answer formats, as opposed to multiple-choice items) directly promote learning and help students remember information longer. To use quizzes or tests to promote learning and retention of information, correct-answer feedback should be provided.」三個條件寫在建議本文裡：閉卷、要主動回想的題型、要給正確答案的回饋。
- **等級與理由**（第 1818–1828 行）：Strong。「based on nine experimental studies examining the effects of this practice for improving K-12 students' performance on academic content or classroom performance, over 30 experimental studies that examined the effect of this strategy for improving college students' academic performance, and the large number of carefully controlled laboratory experiments that have examined the testing effect.」
- **證據摘要**：
  - 材料與年齡的範圍（第 1836–1840 行）：字表、圖像、散文；小學到大學。
  - 小考題型與期末題型不同時仍有效（第 1841–1845 行）：「Testing effects surface when the intervening tests are different from the final tests; for example, intervening tests with fill-in-the-blank items improve subsequent performance on tests that use multiplechoice and true/false items and vice versa.」
  - 對照是「多花時間再讀」（第 1846–1852 行）：「having students take a test is almost always a more potent learning device than having students spend additional time studying the target material. This is especially true when the test requires students to actively recall information」。
  - 課堂例子（McDaniel 等 2007，大學線上課程；第 1857–1865、1883–1889 行）：三個條件是小考（選擇或簡答）、重讀重點事實、不再接觸；小考後有回饋（重述題目與正確答案）。「Facts that students had been re-exposed to through the quizzes were more likely to be remembered correctly on the unit tests as compared to facts that students had simply reread (or restudied). In addition, the benefit of completing short-answer questions on the weekly quizzes extended to performance on the final test.」
  - 減緩遺忘（第 1910–1917 行）：高中歷史，小考式複習在之後 16 週遺忘較少，對照是呈現相同內容但不要求提取的複習。
- **How to carry out**（第 1918–1942 行）：
  - 在初次接觸與期末評量之間給閉卷小考，正式或非正式（如搶答遊戲）都可。
  - **答錯會不會有害**（第 1930–1942 行）：「in the absence of corrective feedback, any errors produced on one test will remain present, and will reappear on subsequent tests. However, guessing when unsure has not so far been shown to have detrimental effects, at least with memory for facts and vocabulary. In sum, then, our recommendation is to take every opportunity to prompt students to retrieve information, and whenever a substantial number of errors are expected, to be sure to make corrective feedback available.」
- **Roadblocks**：只有 5b.1（第 1891–1908 行）：教師沒有時間出題與批改。解法：用出題網站（舉 www.quia.com）與教科書附的自動批改小考。

### 建議 6：Help students allocate study time efficiently（總說，第 1957–1961 行）

「we recommend that teachers help students more accurately assess what they know and do not know, and to use this information to more efficiently allocate their study time. Teachers can help students break the "illusion of knowing" that often impedes accurate assessment of knowledge in two ways.」總項沒有自己的證據等級。

### 建議 6a：Teach students how to use delayed judgment of learning techniques（第 1963–2219 行）

- **建議原文**（第 1966–1969 行）：「First, teachers can teach students how to create accurate "judgments of learning" during study. Second, students can use their performance on closed book quizzes to identify what material they need to restudy in order to master all critical course content (see recommendation 6b).」
- **等級與理由**（第 1971–1984 行）：Low。「because the body of evidence supporting this recommendation is primarily experimental research completed in the laboratory using academic content rather than in classroom experiments. The research provides direct evidence supporting causal links between delayed judgments of learning and accurate assessments of knowledge, delayed keyword generation and accurate assessments of knowledge, and links between accurate assessments of knowledge, study behavior, and improved performance on tests. Research has been completed both with college students and school-aged children.」評低的原因同樣是「只在實驗室」，不是沒有實驗。
- **證據摘要**：
  - 未經訓練的學習者高估自己學會的程度（第 1990–1994 行）：「without training, most learners cannot accurately judge what they do and don't know, and typically overestimate how well they have mastered material when they are finished studying.」
  - cue-only delayed judgment of learning 的三個要件（第 2012–2021 行）：隔一段時間再測（「an hour, a day, or a week」）；只看線索、不看答案；判斷自己答對的機率，而不只是試著答。
  - 讀文章的版本（第 2044–2072 行）：隔一段時間後回想、自己寫出關鍵詞或摘要句。「when people were asked to type into the computer the definitions for four central terms from a text after a delay, and were then asked to evaluate how well they understood what they just read, they were more able to accurately identify their level of understanding.」；類似研究裡「their study of the text and eventual test performance also improved」。
  - 兒童（第 2073–2103 行）：研究很少（「There have been only a few studies」）；年紀小的孩子知道自己會不會，但選擇複習內容時沒有用上。
- **How to carry out**（第 2104–2219 行）：
  1. 課堂上辦學習時段：教完新內容幾天後，準備約 10 題關鍵問題，一次一題、不附答案，學生先試著答，再用 0–100 評估隔天考試答對的機率；沒有評 100 的題目都要回去找答案。整學期在同一批材料上重複使用。
  2. 之後教學生自己在家用（西班牙文單字卡的例子：leche 評 100%、pan 評 60%、huevo 評 0%）。
  3. 延遲關鍵詞：讀完指定閱讀後先做別科作業，再拿白紙不看課文寫出四個關鍵詞與定義；寫不出來就重讀再做一次。
  - 檢核表另有一條（第 767–768 行）：「Remind students to complete judgments of learning without the answers in front of them.」
- **Roadblocks**：原文這一項沒有 Possible roadblocks 段落。

### 建議 6b：Use tests and quizzes to identify content that needs to be learned（第 2229–2342 行）

- **建議原文**（第 2231–2237 行）：「We recommend that teachers use tests or quizzes after the presentation of new material to help students identify content that requires further study. As a companion technique to Recommendation 5b, we recommend that teachers and students use tests and quizzes with feedback identifying incorrect responses, providing correct answers to those incorrect responses as tools for helping students identify content that they have not yet mastered during study.」
- **等級與理由**（第 2239–2249 行）：Low。「based on three experimental studies that examined the effect of this strategy for improving college students' performance on academic content (text material, vocabulary), and a handful of laboratory experiments ... To date, no experimental studies have been completed examining this question with K-12 learners or in the context of classroom instruction.」
- **證據摘要**（第 2252–2278 行）：學習者無法準確判斷自己記得多少；「Quizzes may help students identify which items are not well learned」；小考比額外學習時間更能提升外語單字的學習；兩次閱讀之間插入測驗，重讀的收穫較多。作者的保留：「Wellcontrolled research on this issue is at present limited to laboratory experiments.」接著是小組的信念而不是證據：「We believe there is little reason to doubt that in the classroom setting, quizzes improve learners' awareness of what they know and do not know」。
- **How to carry out**（第 2279–2318 行）：(1) 教完後給閉卷小考，口頭或書面、正式或非正式都可；(2) 課堂開頭做「spot checks」，考完立刻對答案，並明說答錯的人該讀課本哪幾頁。
- **Roadblocks**：
  - 6b.1（第 2320–2330 行）：課堂時間不夠。解法：部分小考當作業；用教科書網站的線上自我檢查小考，這些網站「often identify pages in the text where the concept, principle, or skill was taught, or where the students can locate a worked example.」
  - 6b.2（第 2331–2342 行）：學校已經考太多。解法：這裡的測驗是幫學生找出還沒學會的內容，「tools to help them learn, rather than as tools used to evaluate their performance」。

### 建議 7：Help students build explanations by asking and answering deep questions（第 2348–2574 行）

- **建議原文**（第 2350–2363 行）：「When students have acquired a basic set of knowledge about a particular topic of study and are ready to build a more complex understanding of a topic, we recommend that teachers find opportunities to ask questions and model answers to these questions, in order to help students build deep explanations of key concepts. By deep explanations, we mean explanations that appeal to causal mechanisms, planning, well-reasoned arguments, and logic.」題型：「why, why-not, how, what-if, how does X compare to Y, and what is the evidence for X?」
- **前提條件寫在建議本文第一句**：學生已經有這個主題的基本知識。Overview（第 525–527 行）與 How to carry out（第 2429–2431 行）各重複一次：「Posing these questions during instruction is usually most useful after students have mastered the more basic factual content relating to a topic.」
- **等級與理由**（第 2365–2375 行）：Strong。「based on over a dozen experimental studies examining the effects of this practice for improving K-12 students' academic performance, over a dozen experimental studies that examined the effect of this strategy for improving college students' academic performance, and the large number of laboratory experiments」。
- **證據摘要**（第 2378–2409 行）：
  - 對照條件（第 2378–2382 行）：「students are randomly assigned either to conditions that encourage deep explanations or to comparison conditions that expose the students to similar content, but without the process of building explanations.」
  - 對象四年級到大學；材料含科學、歷史、說明文、故事、論說文、數學、統計。
  - 一份統合分析（Rosenshine、Meister、Chapman 1996；第 2397–2409 行）支持的是**訓練學生自己問深層問題**：「interventions that explicitly train students how to ask deep-level questions while reading text, listening to lectures, or studying material.」做法包含課堂討論、練習簿、電腦上的教學代理人；結果是「increase the rate of student questions, the depth of their questions, and/or their comprehension of the material.」
- **How to carry out**（第 2425–2514、2526–2527 行）：
  - 深層問題需要的時間多得多（第 2431–2434 行）；教師常要示範怎麼回答（第 2435–2437 行）。
  1. 鼓勵學生放聲思考（口說或書寫）；之後看到好的解釋當回饋（第 2439–2472 行）。「The quality of self-explanations improves when students are exposed to high-quality explanations provided by teachers and peers.」；「It is important to give a student enough time to think and prepare responses rather than quickly providing the correct answer.」
  2. 用能引出解釋的題幹（第 2473–2498 行）：why、what caused X、how did X occur、what if、what-if-not、how does X compare to Y、what is the evidence for X、why is X important。對比的淺層題例子：「What organism pollinates flowers?」
  3. 問挑戰既有信念的問題（第 2499–2514、2526–2527 行）：例「Why is it good for a forest to periodically have forest fires?」
- **Roadblocks**：
  - 7.1（第 2533–2549 行）：學生的學科知識不夠，答不出來，結果學習效果差或失去動機。解法：教師判斷學生是否已有足夠知識（可用建議 5、6 的小考），並在要求學生獨立使用之前提供充分的示範。
  - 7.2（第 2550–2557 行）：學生的解釋淺或離題。解法：給回饋或示範範例。
  - 7.3（第 2558–2574、2529–2530 行）：學生不願花力氣。解法：挑戰信念的問題、錨定在真實世界的問題。

**建議 4**（第 2918–2922、2933–3080 行；附錄的標題寫成「Interleave abstract and concrete material」）

Thus, classroom instruction ... should include instruction in the abstract or generic representations of the concept being taught, and teachers should not expect students to be able to infer the underlying symbolic or abstract representation of a problem from learning how to solve a problem using a single concrete instantiation of the problem.」

In addition, research completed on the use of advanced organizers during reading lends additional support to this recommendation.」註 113：Pressley、Tannebaum、McDaniel 等 (1990)；Rickards (1976)。註 114：Rickards (1975-1976)。
- 舉的研究（Pressley 等 1990；第 3118–3130、3082–3083 行）有兩個發現：
  - **要作答才有效**：「First, only learners who were required to answer pre-questions prior to reading a text showed gains in acquisition of content; learners who read but did not answer the pre-questions did not show significant gains relative to learners not given prequestions.」
  - **答錯也有效**：「Second, the attempt to answer pre-questions was beneficial regardless of whether the learners provided a correct answer. That is, answering prequestions incorrectly did not eliminate the pre-question advantage—the advantage simply required an attempt to answer the pre-questions.」
- 正文註 50 的錯置：正文建議 5a 等級段的註 50（第 1710–1712 行）列的文獻和建議 7 的註 73（第 2411–2412 行）、附錄註 125（第 3359–3360 行）幾乎相同，都是深層提問的研究；附錄給 5a 的依據（註 113–115）是另一批。註 50 裡的 King (1996, 2006) 在書目裡找不到（書目只有 King 1992、1994，第 3649–3652 行）。原文沒有說明，哪一份才是 5a 的依據以附錄較明確。

**建議 5b**（第 3087–3100、3114–3115、3131–3148 行）
- Butler 與 Roediger (2007)（第 3131–3140 行）：實驗室、大學程度的藝術史講課；看完立刻做選擇題或簡答小考，30 天後的成績比沒有小考好很多。「In addition, short-answer but not multiple-choice quizzes improved performance on the test 30 days later relative to a condition in which the target facts were given for additional study immediately after viewing the lecture.」——對照換成「把重點事實再給一次」時，只有簡答題有優勢。
