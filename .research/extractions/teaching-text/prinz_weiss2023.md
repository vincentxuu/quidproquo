## 研究設計

- 研究類型：單一實驗，單因子受試者間設計，自變項是文本類型（有標題／純文字）（第 384–406 行）。
- 樣本數的決定：依 Schneider 等人 2018 年的統合分析（文本內的組織性訊號對保留成績 Hedges's g = 0.71）預期中到大的效果，檢定力分析得到需要 N = 52（α = .05，β = .20，d = 0.70）（第 518–537 行）。
- 受試者：52 位就讀教育科學的大學生參加；標題組 1 人因閱讀時間太短被排除（每字 0.03 秒），剩 51 人（第 537–541、355–367 行）。平均 22.82 歲（SD 4.26），82% 女性，平均在學第 3.32 學期（SD 2.08）；可選學分或酬金（第 363–383 行）。
- 分組：隨機分派，標題組 n = 25，純文字組 n = 26（第 385–394 行）。
- 教材：五篇自然科學短文，主題是矽藻、聖嬰現象、葉酸、r/K 選擇理論、沼澤；每篇 482 到 505 字（不含標題），分五到六段（第 429–445 行）。
- **標題的形式是問句**（第 445–499 行）：「In the headings group, a heading preceded each paragraph.」作者說標題可以是敘述或問句，並引附加問題（adjunct questions）的研究說明問句可能對深層理解較有利，接著：「Therefore, we included headings in the form of questions in this study. The question headings were printed in boldface on a separate line. The function of the headings was to foreshadow the upcoming topic. They did not convey any additional content information. Thus, the text versions with and without headings retained the same information.」圖 1 是矽藻一文在兩個條件下的範例段落，文字檔裡看不到圖的內容；五篇有標題的文本在補充資料，不在原文檔裡。
- 先備知識：對五個主題各自評 1（沒有）到 5（非常完整）（第 531–543 行）。
- 記憶與理解的測量：每篇三題細節題（量記憶）與三題推論題（量理解），共 30 題；答對 1 分，答錯或沒答 0 分；兩位評分者獨立評 10% 的答案，細節題 κ = .97（95% CI [.92, 1.00]），推論題 κ = .92（[.83, 1.00]）（第 561–611、677–678 行）。細節題的例子：「What is the thick layer of dead diatoms on the seabed called?」推論題的例子：「What happens with diatoms after they die?」
- 判斷：每讀完一篇、還沒看到題目之前，預估自己細節題與推論題各能答對幾題（0–3）（第 621–637 行）。判斷前拿到兩類題目的定義，但不知道具體題目（第 847–862 行）。
- 判斷準確度的兩種算法（第 652–661、559–599 行）：偏誤（bias）＝判斷的答對數減實際答對數，正值是過度自信，負值是信心不足，零是完全準確；相對準確度（relative accuracy）＝每個人跨五篇的判斷與實際成績的 gamma 相關。
- 認知負荷：Klepsch 等人 2017 年的問卷，八題，分內在、外在、增益（germane）三種負荷，5 點量表；讀完全部並答完題目後才填（第 612–640、743 行）。信度：「Internal consistency was rather low for the subscales on intrinsic (α = .50) and germane (α = .38) load but acceptable for the subscale on extraneous load (α = .73).」
- 對標題的感受：標題組另填八個敘述，5 點量表（第 655–663、770–779 行）。
- 流程：因為疫情，受試者在 2020–2021 冬季學期於家中線上參加，約 75 分鐘；每篇閱讀時間 10 分鐘（第 794–866 行）。
- 假設（第 431–495 行）：記憶假設（標題有正向效果）、理解假設（同）、後設記憶假設與後設理解假設（標題讓判斷更準，「in terms of both bias and relative accuracy」）、認知負荷假設（標題降低外在負荷、提高增益負荷，內在負荷不變）。
- 統計：α = .05；效果量 Cohen's d，作者採 0.30、0.50、0.80 為小、中、大的門檻；每個依變項取五篇的平均；各篇分開的結果在補充資料表 A1，「the pattern of results was quite similar for most individual texts」（第 760–768 行）。

## 作者自己的解釋與保留

- 記憶效果不顯著，作者說和先前的研究不一致，並用「標題是問句」來解釋（第 1048–1105 行）：「This result stands in contrast to previous studies that found effects of headings on learners' memory (e.g., Hartley & Trueman, 1985; Sanchez et al., 2001). A potential explanation for the discrepancy is that the headings enhanced transfer-appropriate processing … In our study, the headings were formulated in the form of questions that tapped important relations and processes reported in the texts. Therefore, the headings might have increased learners' focus on these aspects, helping them gain a deeper understanding, but might not have similarly increased their attention with regard to specific details」。接著引 Reynolds 等人的附加問題研究當支持（第 1113–1173 行）。
- 作者強調理解變好不是用記憶換來的（第 1177–1201 行）：「although the headings only yielded a significant positive effect on comprehension, this enhancement did not occur at the expense of learners' recall. Descriptively, memory performance was superior to comprehension performance, and compared with plain text the headings also tended to support memory albeit to a lesser extent.」
- 後設理解的相對準確度沒有組間差異，作者仍說標題「有一些潛力」（第 1281–1107 行）：「Nonetheless, relative metacomprehension accuracy was .33 and did not significantly differ from zero for learners who read texts without headings, but was .60 and significantly differed from zero for learners who read texts with headings. Compared to the former value, the latter value more clearly exceeds the average level of relative metacomprehension accuracy of .24 found in a recent meta-analysis (Prinz et al., 2020a). Therefore, although the difference between groups was not significant, it seems that headings have at least some potential」。
- 後設記憶：沒有標題的組反而準，有標題的組信心不足（第 1111–1291 行）：「Learners who read texts without headings tended to provide accurate judgments, whereas learners who read texts with headings were on average underconfident.」作者的解釋是標題把監控導向關係與過程，「the learners did not monitor or to a lesser extent monitored their memory of details. As a result, they were rather unsure about their achieved memory performance」。
- 外在負荷的解釋，作者自己加了保留（第 1342–1394 行）：「although the finding failed to reach the level of significance, it should be noted that … learners who read texts with headings also tended to experience lower extraneous load.」「Nonetheless, due to the lack of significance, this interpretation must be treated with caution.」
- 中介分析不顯著的可能原因（第 1406–1450 行）：「the sample might have been too small to uncover mediation effects, or the way in which cognitive load was assessed might have contributed to the outcomes.」
- 3.6 節第一句把標題稱為「the questions」（第 974 行）：「Overall, the participants perceived the questions as supportive for their learning」。
- 結論的措辭比結果強（第 1529–1535 行）：「Concisely, headings within texts represent a powerful way to support self-regulated learning. Therefore, authors of instructional texts should consider including headings.」

## 作者列的限制

第 5 節 Limitations and future directions（第 1456–1521 行），逐條：

1. 只測了問句形式、針對關係與過程的標題，能不能推到其他形式的標題要再驗證（第 1462–1320 行）：「We incorporated headings in the form of questions that highlighted the text topics to follow … However, headings can be implemented in a variety of ways … For example, headings can be formulated as statements instead of questions. Headings can also differ concerning their wordiness or degree of elaboration … Moreover, headings can differ regarding whether they target relations and processes or details and facts reported in a text. … These variations should be taken into account in further investigations to determine the extent to which the conclusions drawn from this study are generalizable to other types of headings.」
2. 「遷移適切處理」的解釋沒有被直接檢驗（第 1320–1384 行）：要比較針對關係與過程的標題和針對細節與事實的標題，「Such an outcome would indicate that headings need to be adapted to the specific learning goal associated with reading」。
3. 沒有量閱讀當下的監控與線索使用（第 1388–1428 行）：「we did not assess learners' online monitoring or cue use. For example, it is unclear to what extent learners who read texts with headings were indeed aware of their improved comprehension when making their metacomprehension judgments.」
4. 只看監控，沒有看之後的調節與成績（第 1428–1453 行）：「The present study focused on the monitoring aspect only. Further studies should examine whether more accurate judgments due to headings in fact lead to more favorable regulatory decisions, for example, about which texts to restudy, and thus to enhanced performance.」
5. 認知負荷是事後用問卷回溯量的，而且在判斷與答題之後才填，限制因果推論（第 1454–1521 行）：「That we measured cognitive load retrospectively also limits causal conclusions that can be drawn with regard to the impact of cognitive load on memory and comprehension and the accuracy with which they were judged」。

散在別處的限制：

- 內在負荷與增益負荷兩個分量表信度偏低（α = .50、.38）（第 639–640 行）。唯一顯著的負荷結果（增益負荷）用的是 α = .38 的量表；作者在限制一節沒有再提這一點。
- 相對準確度有 6 人與 10 人無法計算（第 1008–1012、1042–1050 行）。
- 樣本可能太小，看不出中介效果（第 1438–1446 行）。
- 線上在家施測（第 794–800 行）；作者列了三項確保資料品質的做法，沒有把它當成限制。

原文沒有說的事：測驗在讀完五篇之後多久進行（從流程看是同一場次，沒有延宕測驗）；受試者的母語與文本的語言；有沒有預先註冊。

## 與摘要不一致的地方

- 摘要寫「headings supported learners in comprehending the texts as well as in accurately judging their comprehension」（第 38–39 行）。內文裡判斷準確度有兩種量法，只有偏誤顯著（d = 0.68）；相對準確度的組間差異不顯著（p = .248），作者在結果一節寫的是「In contrast to the metacomprehension hypothesis」。摘要沒有區分。
- 摘要寫「Moreover, headings affected learners' cognitive load.」（第 40 行）內文三種負荷只有增益負荷顯著（該量表 α = .38）；外在負荷「Contrary to the cognitive-load hypothesis」不顯著；中介分析不顯著。
- 摘要與標題都只說「headings」，沒有說這個實驗的標題是問句。問句形式寫在材料一節，而且是作者解釋「理解有效、記憶不顯著」的核心理由，也是限制的第一條。
- 摘要寫「The effects of headings on memory and judgment accuracy concerning memory were not significant.」與內文相符；內文另有記憶 d = 0.52、p = .069 與「tended to support memory albeit to a lesser extent」的描述。
