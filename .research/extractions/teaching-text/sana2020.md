## 研究設計

- **研究類型**：三個實驗室實驗，全部是受試者間設計、隨機分派（第 70、149、212 行）。作者自稱「three laboratory experiments」（第 13 行）。
- **受試者**：McMaster University 修 Introductory Psychology 的大學生，以課程學分為報酬（第 67、148、210 行）。實驗結束時問受試者是否學過目標內容，確認全部不熟悉（實驗一，第 68–69 行）。
  - 實驗一：164 人（平均 18.54 歲）。控制組 56、LOs massed 53、LOs interpolated 55（第 67–72 行）。
  - 實驗二：159 人（平均 18.58 歲），1 人因技術錯誤排除；LO statements 53、MC pretest 53、fact statements 52（第 148–151 行）。
  - 實驗三：193 人（平均 18.55 歲）；MC 無回饋 49、MC 有回饋 50、SA 無回饋 47、SA 有回饋 47（第 210–213 行）。
  - 檢定力分析：假設中等效果 f = 0.25，實驗一、二至少 159 人，實驗三至少 128 人（第 72–73、211 行）。
  - 作者沒有受試者的認知能力或學業成績資料，無法確認各組同質；以依序隨機分派降低時段差異（第 73–76 行）。
- **教材**：五篇改寫自神經科學教科書的短文，主題是鏡像神經元。第 78 行：「Each passage, which consisted of a single paragraph ranging from 171 to 267 words, focused on a central theme」。每篇就是一個段落。
- **學習目標（LO）**：每篇 3 條，共 15 條。第 81–83 行：「These statements were based on key facts covered in each passage and did not include the correct response. … The answers to all LOs were explicitly stated in the passages.」例：「In the first passage, you will learn about where the mirror neurons are located.」
- **目標與考題各考什麼層次**（第 83–85 行）：「The final test consisted of 15 applied MC questions. Each question corresponded to an LO fact, and therefore targeted the same content. According to the Blooming Biology Tool (), the LOs in the current study targeted basic, but necessary knowledge (e.g., memorization and recall of facts), whereas the final test questions assessed the application of that knowledge (i.e., conceptual understanding).」
  - 也就是：目標（以及實驗二、三的讀前題目）針對基本事實；期末測驗 15 題四選一、考應用，而且**每一題都對應一條 LO 的事實**。沒有任何考題考 LO 沒指到的內容。
  - Table 1 的樣本（第 87–103 行）：LO 敘述「In the first passage you will learn where the mouth movements are located in the frontal lobe.」；期末題「If Lindsay damages the posterior region of her inferior frontal gyrus, which of the following actions can she not perform?」
- **給受試者的指示語**（實驗一，第 105–111 行；實驗二「the same instructions」第 159 行）：受試者被告知會讀短文再考試，而且「Participants were encouraged to study these statements carefully and look for that information in the passages, because it would help them on the final test.」指示語原文：
  > 「You may engage in study activities before a passage. In one activity, you may be presented with clear learning objectives—these are statements that articulate the important information you are expected to learn by the end of the session. Read the statements carefully, use them to guide your study, and look for the information in the passages. Doing so will help you on the final test.」
- **程序**：LimeSurvey；全程 1 小時。學習階段每篇短文呈現 5 分鐘（各組相同）；LO 敘述、事實敘述、讀前題目都是自訂步調、一次一條、額外加在短文之前（第 112–115、159–160 行）。之後 5 分鐘干擾作業（列出各字母開頭的動物與國家），再做期末測驗；作答時看不到短文（第 116–118 行）。**沒有延宕測驗**，期末測驗就在同一節進行。
- **各實驗的條件**：
  - 實驗一：沒有 LO（控制）／15 條 LO 全部在第一篇之前一次一條呈現（massed）／每篇之前呈現對應的 3 條（interpolated）。
  - 實驗二：LO statements（與實驗一的 interpolated 條件相同，第 151 行）／MC pretest（把 15 條 LO 改成 15 題事實型四選一，每篇之前 3 題，不給回饋，第 153–160 行）／fact statements（LO 敘述裡直接寫出答案，例：「you will learn that mirror neurons are located in the ventral premotor cortex」，第 155–156 行）。
  - 實驗三：2（讀前題型：MC 對 SA）× 2（回饋：有對無）。回饋就是正確答案本身（例：「ventral premotor cortex」），答完每題下一個畫面呈現，不論答對答錯（第 217–224 行）。回饋沒有附解釋。
- **量了什麼**：期末測驗答對比例；實驗二、三另報告讀前題目的答對比例。
- **效果量**：Cohen's d 與 ηp²；成對比較用 Bonferroni 校正（第 123、167 行）。

## 作者自己的解釋與保留

1. **分到各篇前的好處，條件是有明確指示**。General Discussion 第 256–257 行：「In experiment 1, we found that interpolating LO statements throughout the lesson improves learning (compared with not presenting LOs), particularly when explicit instructions on the importance of LOs are provided in order to direct students' attention to LO content.」註 1 第 294 行：「We found that explicitly stating the importance of these pre-study activities and their benefits on learning encouraged participants to pay attention to them.」
2. **分散擺放沒有勝過集中擺放**。第 127–128 行（見結果第 5 列）；實驗二開頭再說一次，第 130–131 行：「However, interpolating LOs across study did not significantly benefit learning beyond massing LOs before study.」
3. **讀前題目的好處不只是引導注意力**，作者引別人的實驗支持，並用推測語氣（「could encourage」）談找回饋的行為（第 172–178 行）。
4. **回饋為什麼降低成績：作者列的是可能原因，並說需要更多研究**。第 242–245 行：「There may be several reasons for this observed negative feedback effect on learning. For example, feedback may have reduced the processing depth of and the time spent on passages—participants may have become overreliant on feedback, and memorized the feedback, which could have resulted in an increased feeling of content fluency and decreased attention during the subsequent study session. While more research is needed to examine the possible explanations for when and why feedback on pretest responses is ineffective, the current results provide theoretical insight by demonstrating that that at least part of the pretesting benefit could come from feedback-seeking behaviors.」
5. **含答案的敘述（實驗二）與回饋條件（實驗三）可能有同一種代價**。第 276–278 行：「Indeed, there may be a cost associated with the feedback conditions (experiment 3) and the fact statements condition (experiment 2). Being provided with immediate feedback or studying the key facts may have encouraged shallow processing of subsequent passages. This could be due to a lack of motivation to read the passages carefully, as students may have incorrectly assessed that they have sufficiently acquired the key information needed to do well on the final test.」
6. **題型沒差的兩個可能原因**（第 250–254 行）：本研究的選擇題選項不算有競爭力；而且「we did not measure the learning on non-pretested, but related information. Specifically, only the concepts pretested were assessed for retention on the final test.」
7. **不建議直接照做**。第 267–268 行：「Based on the current findings, it may be tempting to prescribe that LOs should be converted into pretest questions or that teaching or study sessions should begin with pretests. However, such recommendations are premature.」
8. 作者自認的貢獻（Concluding Remarks，第 283–291 行）：第一個有系統地示範 LO 改成讀前題目有好處；讀前題與期末題不同，所以好處不只是練過同一題；用了不同的 Bloom 層次，「demonstrating that knowledge-level pretest questions can facilitate the comprehension and application of pretested information」。
9. 研究背景的說法（第 26–27 行）：「To date, very few studies have systematically evaluated the effect of LOs on student learning.」摘要第 13 行：「limited research has investigated the effect of LOs on learning」。實驗二第 172 行：「To our knowledge, this experiment is the first in the literature to directly compare LOs as statements with LOs as pretests.」

## 作者列的限制

LIMITATIONS AND FUTURE DIRECTIONS（第 266–281 行）逐條：

1. 建議把 LO 改成讀前題目、或每次上課以讀前測驗開始，都還太早（第 267–268 行）。
2. 單一主題、單一課程、實驗室情境。第 268–269 行：「the findings are restricted to one topic area from a single course in laboratory contexts.」
3. **LO 與讀前題目只針對基本事實**。第 269–271 行：「while instructors in biology and other STEM courses routinely include a wide range of LOs that vary across different levels of the Bloom's taxonomy (), the LOs and pretests used in the current study exclusively targeted knowledge of basic facts.」需要在不同主題、不同學習層次、課堂情境重做。
4. **可能把注意力從沒被指到的相關內容拉走，而本研究沒量**。第 272–274 行：「while LOs and pretests can focus attention on information related to the pre-studied content, they may take attention away from related information that is not pre-studied (; ).  argued that this is particularly true for self-paced expository text; students tend to fixate on or selectively attend to parts of the text that are on the LOs or pretests. Given that the current study did not separately assess the learning of pre-studied versus related information, future research should investigate this critical gap.」
5. **受試者被指示要特別注意並主動找答案**。第 275–276 行：「In the current study, participants were instructed to pay close attention to pretest questions and were encouraged to actively seek out the correct answers to those questions. Future research may further investigate how attention and motivation may interact with pretesting of LOs.」
6. 回饋條件與含答案敘述可能鼓勵淺層處理（見上節第 5 點；作者列在限制節）。
7. 沒有檢驗回饋類型（只告訴對錯 vs 給正確答案）會不會改變結果（第 278–281 行）。

散在別處的限制：

- 沒有受試者能力或成績的資料可確認各組同質（第 73–76 行）。
- 期末測驗只考被 LO／讀前題目指到的概念（第 83–84、252–253 行）。

## 與摘要不一致的地方

1. **摘要只報告實驗一顯著的那一組比較**。摘要第 15 行：「Presenting LOs before each corresponding passage increased performance on the final test compared with not presenting LOs (experiment 1).」內文還有三件事摘要沒寫：集中擺放對控制組不顯著（p = 0.21，第 124–125 行）；兩種擺法互比沒有差別（p = 0.999，第 125–126 行）；拿掉指示語後分散擺放對控制組也沒有差別（註 1，第 294–296 行）。
2. **摘要把測驗說成量 retention，內文說考的是應用**。摘要第 13–15 行：「we examined the extent to which LOs improve retention of information … took a final test that measured how well they retained the information」；內文第 83–85 行：「15 applied MC questions … assessed the application of that knowledge (i.e., conceptual understanding)」。內文其他地方也稱它 comprehension test（第 70、119 行）。
3. 摘要的實驗二（讀前選擇題最高）、實驗三（給回饋成績較低，兩種題型皆然）和內文相符（第 167–170、236–241 行）。
4. 摘要結語「pretesting (especially without feedback) is a more active method that optimizes learning」；內文限制節說據此提建議「premature」（第 267–268 行）。語氣強度不同，摘要沒有帶這個保留。
