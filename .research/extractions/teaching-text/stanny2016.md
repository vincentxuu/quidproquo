## 研究設計

- **研究類型**：對網路上公開的動詞清單做內容分析（計數）。單一作者；資料檔由一位助理彙整（致謝，第 1323–1324 行）。沒有學生、沒有教學介入、沒有量任何學習成果。
- **研究動機**（第 118–134 行）：作者 2006 年自己彙整過一份 Bloom 動詞表，當時發現不同來源會把同一個動詞放在不同層次；想知道其他大型清單是否也如此。
- **三個研究問題**（第 140–145 行）：
  - 「How consistently do authors use specific verbs to describe learning at different levels of Bloom's taxonomy?」
  - 「Will an analysis of large collections of verbs for Bloom's taxonomy produce consensus about the level of thinking skill described by an SLO that uses a specific verb?」
  - 「Can we make unambiguous recommendations about which verbs correspond to specific levels of cognitive skill implied by the structure of Bloom's taxonomy?」
- **清單怎麼蒐集**（第 148–153 行，逐句）：「Materials were obtained by conducting a Google search based on the string, "action words for Bloom's taxonomy", in March 2016 and downloading the lists posted in the top 30 web sites produced by this search. The 30 highest-ranking web sites each contained a unique collection of words. However, sites with lower rankings in the Google search frequently mirrored a list posted by one of the top 30 sites.」
  - 也就是：一個搜尋字串、一個時間點、Google 排名前 30 的網站。
  - **30 個網站列在 Appendix A（第 1328–1364 行），不全是大學網站**。從網址看，除了大學與社區學院（cornell、valdosta、apu、uncc、pamlicocc、umn、marshall、cloud.edu、uni.edu、worwic、psu、usf、iastate、uci、schoolcraft、kent、colorado、cmu、tulane、ua.edu、odu、moreheadstate、dkit.ie）之外，還有 teach-nology.com、美國空軍大學（au.af.mil）、highperformancetrainingworks.com、兩個中小學學區（farmington.k12.mi.us、trenton.k12.nj.us）、美國建築師學會（aia.org）、qualitymatters.org。原文沒有對這 30 個網站的性質做分類。
- **資料處理**（第 154–165、171–172 行）：每份清單把某個字列在某個層次，就在該層次記一次；非動詞刪除（how、what、when 等）；名詞化的字還原成動詞（organization → organize）。
  - **citation frequency**：把某動詞列在某層次的清單數，1 到 30。
  - 之後刪掉同一層次內的重複，保留跨層次的重複。
- **兩種「一致性」的定義**（第 202–207 行）：
  - inter-list agreement：把某動詞歸到某層次的清單所佔的百分比。
  - within-list（原文也寫 intra-list）agreement：整個彙整後的集合裡，只被歸到一個層次的動詞所佔的百分比。
  - 參照標準：「Researchers establish 75% agreement as the threshold criterion for acceptable inter-rater agreement for assessment instruments」。
- **分析的四種切法**（第 173–186 行）：全部動詞；至少 4 份清單提名的動詞；至少 10 份清單提名的動詞；以及把六個層次併成三類（know + understand＝事實型／低階；apply＝應用；analyze + evaluate + create＝概念型／高階）後重做。
- 沒有推論統計。

## 作者自己的解釋與保留

- **Bloom 分類本身的階層假設已經有人質疑（導言，轉述）**（第 93–97 行）：「Anderson and Krathwohl raised questions about the developmental sequence of categories in Bloom's hierarchy [9]. Paul also criticized the hierarchical structure of Bloom's taxonomy, arguing that critical thinking skills permeate every category of Bloom's taxonomy [10].」
- **主要結論（討論段）**（第 1178–1183 行）：「If verbs were consistently aligned with specific levels of Bloom's taxonomy, this taxonomy would reduce ambiguity about levels of expertise… However, the analysis reported here suggests that existing lists of words organized by level of Bloom's taxonomy cannot unambiguously support decisions about the differences in learning expected and distinguish between academic achievement expected for associate's, bachelor's, and master's degree programs.」
  - 作者關心的用途是**區分副學士、學士、碩士學位的學習成果**，這是全文的主軸（摘要第 19–21 行、第 90–117、1200–1295 行）。
- **為什麼會不一致：脈絡決定意思**（第 1184–1196 行）：「The challenge faced by verb-based frameworks is that language is notoriously flexible. Context modifies meaning, which explains why a verb associated with a low level in Bloom's taxonomy in one context (recognize the definition of technical terms) may be associated with a high level of Bloom's taxonomy in another context (recognize professional situations that produce a conflict of interest).」另舉 rewrite（抄寫對修訂）與 Paul 對 recall 的論點。「Paul's interpretation… casts doubt on the power of verbs alone to specify the level of expertise described by an SLO.」
- **作者不主張丟掉多義動詞**（第 1197–1199 行）：「One solution might be to abandon verbs with multiple interpretations and select verbs with more precise meanings instead. However, this solution eliminates many wonderful, measurable verbs that serve well to articulate learning in language suitable for a broad audience.」
- **作者對 Bloom 分類的正面評價**（第 1253–1264 行）：「Bloom's taxonomy is a useful heuristic for describing the cognitive demands imposed by learning tasks and assessments [4].」「In the context of a specific question or task, the action verbs we associate with levels of Bloom's taxonomy have specific and unambiguous meanings. Taken out of context, these verbs lose their specificity.」
- **給審閱者的建議**（第 1267–1269 行）：「The context created by the object of an SLO also creates specificity of meaning. Thus, reviewers must consider both the verb and the remaining content of an SLO when they make judgments about the level of expertise an SLO describes.」
- **結論段**（第 1297–1322 行）：
  - 「The enduring value of large collections of verbs is their ability to suggest measurable language when faculty write student learning outcomes. However, the lack of consistency in how lists align verbs with levels of thinking reduces the value of these collections as frameworks that will help programs articulate SLOS to describe lower- and higher-order thinking skills and differentiate between the learning achieved upon completion of associate's, bachelor's, and master's degree programs.」
  - 「Thus, Bloom's taxonomy is a useful, but incomplete, framework for articulating measurable SLOs for advanced degrees. Instead, these verb collections function like a thesaurus or a rhyming dictionary.」
  - 「authors who use a list of Bloom's taxonomy verbs to write measurable SLOs have no guarantee that the verbs they select for their learning outcomes will provide all the detail needed to clearly describe increasing levels of expertise. Authors must also consider the contextual content of the SLO.」
- **可量測的學習成果這個想法，作者是支持的**（第 45–48、57–67 行）：用描述具體、可觀察行為的動詞寫學習成果可以減少歧義；把學習成果比作研究裡的操作型定義。
- **逆向設計（backward design）在這篇只是被描述**（第 78–89 行）：作者說明 Fink 與 Wiggins、McTighe 的做法是先定學習成果、再選教材與活動、最後選評量；沒有引用任何成效資料，也沒有評價。
- **放寬到三類的代價**（第 1143–1144 行）：「Increased consistency may come at the cost of making fewer discriminations.」

## 作者列的限制

這篇沒有 Limitations 節，全文也沒有一句以限制為名的話。以下是從方法與致謝直接讀到的事實，原文沒有把它們稱為限制：

- 樣本是 2016 年 3 月、單一搜尋字串、Google 排名前 30 的網站（第 148–150 行）；原文沒有說這 30 份清單能否代表所有動詞表。
- 排名較後的網站常常直接複製前 30 名的清單（第 150–152 行）；前 30 份清單彼此之間是否獨立編成、有沒有互相抄，原文沒有檢查。導言第 125–126 行另說「Although some sites mirror lists created by others, many sites provide their own collection.」
- 「inter-list agreement」把「某份清單沒有收這個動詞」和「某份清單把它放到別的層次」都算成不一致（第 163–165 行的定義）。原文沒有分開報告這兩種情形，也沒有報告每份清單的大小。
- 資料檔由一位助理彙整（第 1323–1324 行）；非動詞的刪除與名詞化的還原沒有報告第二位編碼者或一致性檢查。
- 沒有檢驗「使用動詞表寫出來的學習成果」品質是否較好或較差，也沒有任何學生資料。
