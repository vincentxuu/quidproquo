## 作者自己的解釋與保留

1. **整體定性帶但書**：「Our results suggest that the expertise reversal effect can be considered a well-confirmed ATI that can be used to guide instruction, albeit with some caveats.」（第 684–685 行）結論段重複：「…albeit with some caveats.」（第 1026–1028 行）
2. **不對稱的說法**（任務書指定要查）：
   - 依據是平均效果量的比較：「Judging from the average effect sizes, the effect for high prior knowledge learners is smaller than the one for low prior knowledge learners, however.」（第 900–902 行）。**原文沒有報告 0.505 與 0.428 兩者大小差異的統計檢定**；兩個信賴區間取絕對值後是 [0.260, 0.750] 與 [0.209, 0.647]，重疊（重疊是我對數字的觀察，作者沒有討論）。
   - 順序型交互作用：「Even in studies where the low assistance condition was better for both prior knowledge groups the difference between conditions was generally larger for learners with low prior knowledge, showcasing an ordinal interaction between prior knowledge and instructional assistance.」（第 902–906 行）
   - 註腳 1（第 928–935 行）：「Note that whether an interaction is ordinal or disordinal only applies to the observed range of values. If the relationship between treatment effectiveness and prior knowledge is monotonic, an ordinal interaction will at some point become disordinal as the range of observed values of prior knowledge increases. If the relationship is linear, this crossover point can even be estimated (Widaman et al., 2012).」
   - 結論句：「the Expertise Reversal Effect should not be conceptualized as a symmetrical effect: providing novices with instructional assistance has a more pronounced beneficial impact on learning outcomes than preventing redundant assistance for experts.」（第 907–911 行）
   - 兩種解釋：(a) 協助本身平均是正效果，只是在領域知識太高時被另一個效果蓋過（第 913–918 行，引 Hattie 2023）；(b) 低領域知識的學習者本來就對教學設計的變化較敏感（第 918–702 行，引 Stipek 與 Chiatovich 2017）。
   - 實務含意：「Independent of the mechanism that can be used to explain this asymmetry, the instructional implication is to rather provide assistance than to withhold it when in doubt.」（第 702–705 行）
   - 結論段的措辭比較軟：「Providing additional assistance to learners with low domain knowledge appears to have stronger effects on learning outcomes than withholding assistance from experts.」（第 1029–1031 行）
3. **調節變項彼此混淆**：「Note that those moderators are partially confounded, as some content domains as well as some of the types of prior knowledge assessment (e.g., distinction based on grade level) are related to specific educational statuses.」（第 469–472 行）；「the moderator educational status is partially confounded with age」（第 875–878 行）。
4. **小學樣本太少**：「although the number of studies in this context is too low to derive meaningful conclusions」（第 545–547 行；Table 1 是 2 個研究、4 個效果量）。
5. **為什麼小學弱、高等教育強**：兩個解釋，元素互動性較低（第 795–800 行）、專長累積時間較長所以組間差距較大（第 800–875 行）。作者用「can be explained in several different ways」，沒有檢驗。
6. **為什麼評量方式會調節**：組間的先備知識差距大小不同。量測後二分造成的差距較小，短介入能誘發的差距有限（第 879–892 行）。
7. **與 Kalyuga 2007 的 1.72 相比小很多**（第 894–899 行，見結果 #9）。
8. **相反方向的研究存在**：「(but see Amadieu, van Gog, et al., 2009 or Richter & Scheiter, 2019 for opposite effects)」（第 235–236 行）。
9. **實務意涵**（8.4，第 965–991 行）：
   - 「The major implication for instructional design is that our findings underscore the need to adjust instructional methods and procedures based on the domain knowledge of learners.」（第 966–968 行）
   - 「it is also important for other design factors such as the presentation format or the amount of additional explanatory information. The lower the domain knowledge of the learner, the more assistance should be provided.」（第 971–974 行）
   - 適用於程度大致相同的群體（如班級），在針對個別學習者調整時更重要（第 974–977 行）。
   - 要隨學習過程中新獲得的知識調整，需要定期評量（第 977–981 行）；「Such adaptations and the required repeated assessment of domain knowledge are no trivial task」（第 981–983 行）。
   - 作者提的做法是有學生模型的智慧型家教系統，可搭配讓學生自己選協助的做法：「allow students to self-individualize their learning, such as by providing adaptable learning activities or letting them choose assistance (Brod, 2024)」（第 983–991 行）。
   - 原文的實務意涵沒有談「一份固定的教材同時給不同程度的讀者」該怎麼寫。
10. **未來研究**（8.3，第 993–1023 行）：其他學習者特徵的高階交互作用；協助要隨專長成長逐步撤除（fading）；到非實驗的真實情境檢驗。

## 作者列的限制

8.2 Limitations（第 920–963 行）：

1. 總括句：「Whereas the results imply high generalizability of the existence of the expertise reversal effect, the size of the effect shows substantial heterogeneity across age groups and domains.」（第 921–923 行）
2. 第一：這份統合分析無法分辨各種解釋理論（第 924–926 行）。
3. 第二：研究數少加上異質性大，數個調節層級只有很少的自由度，推論較不可靠：「several of the moderator levels had only a few degrees of freedom, making inferences less reliable.」（第 926–943 行）。作者建議未來徵集未發表的論文以增加樣本（第 943–945 行）。原文沒有說這次是否納入未發表研究（書目裡 Adcook 2004 是博士論文，第 1140–1142 行）。
4. 第三：異質性大表示有別的研究特徵沒被編碼：「other study characteristics—which have not been assessed in this meta-analysis—might play an important role in determining the size of the Expertise Reversal Effect.」（第 945–948 行）
5. 異質性最可能的原因是二分：「The most likely reason for the large heterogeneity of effect sizes is the categorical grouping of both domain knowledge and assistance. Domain knowledge and assistance are both continuous variables, not categories. Dichotomizing them not only ignores this and reduces statistical power, but also means that the results of group comparisons crucially depend on the distance between the groups, which will vary between studies.」（第 949–954 行）例子：職訓末期的美髮師對心理系學生，差距大於同一樣本用短測驗中位數切成的兩半（第 954–960 行）。

散在別處的限制：

6. 調節變項部分混淆（第 469–472 行）、教育階段與年齡混淆（第 875–878 行）。
7. 小學樣本數太少（第 545–547 行）。
8. 差的差（0.971）沒有做調節分析（第 477–478 行）。
9. 16 個研究的初次編碼漏了結果量數，由第一作者一人補上（第 445–446 行）。
10. 資料可得性：開頭註記寫「All data and analysis code will be made publicly available upon publication」（第 120–121 行），文末寫「Data will be made available on request.」（第 1047–1048 行）。
