## 研究設計

- **研究類型**：統合分析（meta-analysis），把 Visible Learning 研究（Hattie 與 Timperley 2007；Hattie 2009；Hattie 與 Zierer 2019）用過的既有統合分析拆回原始研究重新合併。目的是「replicating and expanding the Visible Learning research … from meta-synthesis」（48–51 行）。
- **檢索方式**：沒有做資料庫檢索。原文：「we searched 32 existing meta-analyses that were used in the context of the Visible Learning research for information on primary studies」（258–261 行）。也就是原始研究的來源只限這 32 份既有統合分析。
- **納入標準**（270–280 行）四條：（1）有回饋介入的實驗組對控制組比較，**或前後測比較**；（2）能算效果量；（3）至少一個與學生學習有關的依變項（成就、動機、行為改變）；（4）可辨識的教育情境（幼兒園、小學、中學、大專的學生**或教師**樣本）。
- **32 份既有統合分析的處置**（Table 1，351–615 行；我數的結果）：Included 11 份、Partially included 13 份、Excluded 8 份（Brown 2014、Getsie 等 1985、Lysakowski 與 Walberg 1980、Schimmel 1983、Skiba 等 1985、Walberg 1982、Wilkinson 1981、Witt 等 2006）。部分納入的原因多半是原始研究不在教育情境或資料無法重建，例如 Standley 1996「82 of 98 studies excluded because they do not deal with a school context」（535 行）、Yeany 與 Miller 1983「45 of 49 studies excluded because data was not reconstructable」（615 行）、Kulik 與 Kulik 1988「33 sample size values missing」（430 行）。
- **資料取得**：既有統合分析有報告就直接用；沒有就寫信給作者（「Four authors responded and three of them provided the effect sizes and sample sizes」，307–309 行）；再不行就從原始研究重建（309–314 行）。
- **最終資料**：檢索得到 732 個原始研究；最終 435 個研究、994 個效果量、「about 61,000 subjects」（910–913 行）。出版年份中位數是 1985；「Fifteen percent of the integrated effect sizes are taken from studies published in the last 15 years」（915–917 行）。
- **受試者**：原文沒有給年級、國別、學科的分布表。只在討論裡說範圍從幼兒園兒童到大學教授（1468–1473 行）。
- **編碼的六個調節變項**：研究設計、出版類型、結果指標、回饋類型、回饋管道、回饋方向（627–633、859–908、919–928 行）。原本想編的「perspective of feedback」「way of measuring the outcome」因資料不足或「the feature could not be defined based on the article abstracts」而放棄（634–637 行）。
- **編碼依據是摘要**：原文「we used the article abstracts of the primary studies to define meaningful moderator variables and set the moderator values for each primary study according to our coding scheme」（853–856 行）。
- **編碼信度**：隨機抽 10% 由兩位編碼者分開編，六個變項的 Krippendorff's alpha 0.81–0.99；之後「The lead author then used these operational rules to code the rest of the studies」（617–624、640–642 行）。
- **三類回饋的編碼定義（原文，859–878 行）**：
  - 獎懲：「Forms of reinforcement and punishment apply pleasant or aversive consequences to increase or decrease the frequency of a desired response or behavior. These forms of feedback provide a minimum amount of information on task level and no information on the levels of process or self-regulation.」
  - 訂正：「Corrective forms of feedback typically contain information about the task level in the form of “right or wrong” and the provision of the correct answer to the task. Feedback not only refers to how successfully a skill was performed (knowledge on result), but also to how a skill is performed (knowledge of performance). For some forms of feedback, i.e., modeling, additional information is provided on how the skill could be performed more successfully.」
  - 高資訊量：「Feedback was classified as high-information feedback when it was constituted by information as described for corrective feedback and additionally contained information on self-regulation from monitoring attention, emotions, or motivation during the learning process.」
- **其他變項的定義**：結果指標分 cognitive（成就、保留、認知測驗表現）、motivational（內在動機、控制信念、自我效能、堅持）、physical（動作技能）、behavioral（課堂行為、紀律）（923–928 行）。管道分 oral、written、video-/audio-/computer-assisted（881–883 行）。方向分教師→學生、學生→教師、學生→學生（886–889 行）。研究設計分有控制組與前後測；作者自己寫「Effect sizes from pre-post designs are generally less reliable and less informative about the effects of the intervention because they are likely to be influenced by confounding variables」（893–896 行）。
- **效果量**：Cohen's d，用合併標準差（650–671 行）；以變異數倒數加權（692–711 行）。
- **模型**：隨機效果模型（777–793 行）；同一研究多個效果量的相依性用 robust variance estimation 處理（681–688 行）；調節分析是混合效果模型（850–853 行）。軟體是 R 的 Meta 與 Metafor（647–649 行）。
- **偏誤與異質性檢查**：funnel plot、normal-quantile plot、Egger 迴歸檢定、Q 檢定、I²（734–770 行）。I² 的判讀門檻照 Deeks 等 2008：30–60% moderate、50% 以上 substantial、75% 以上 considerable（766–770 行）。
- **離群值**：用 adjusted standardized residuals，大於 2 列為極端值（809–817 行）。

## 作者自己的解釋與保留

1. **平均值不能當成單一處理的效果**。摘要：「the significant heterogeneity in the data shows that feedback cannot be understood as a single consistent form of treatment」（52–54 行）。討論：「Care is needed, however, with focusing too much on the average effect size as the integrated studies show high heterogeneity, suggesting that, conform to expectations, not all feedback is the same and one (average) effect size does not fit all.」（1454–1458 行）
2. **資料對「回饋」這個構念的捕捉有偏誤**：「The funnel- and normal-quantile-plots illustrate that the observed data does not capture the construct of feedback in an unbiased way」（1458–1463 行）。
3. **前後測與期刊論文的效果較高，符合預期**：「the average effect of feedback as reported in pre-post design studies is higher than in controlled studies and higher reported in published journal articles than in dissertations. Feedback effects seem to be less likely to be published when they are low or even negative.」（1476–1482 行）
4. **結果指標的結論要保留**：「These claims must be partly interpreted with some caution because there are few studies available related to physical and behavioral outcomes, substantially reducing the precision of the average effect size.」（1484–1488 行）認知指標內部「significant heterogeneity remains within the sub-group」（1493–1495 行）。
5. **動機指標效果低的解釋**：作者說結果不代表回饋對動機的效果本來就低，而是沒資訊量的回饋對動機效果低甚至為負：「The results do not indicate that feedback effects on motivation per se are low but that effects of uninformative forms of feedback on motivation are low or even negative.」（1510、1403–1405 行）
6. **回饋類型的結論句**：「Feedback is more effective the more information it contains. Simple forms of reinforcement and punishment have low effects, while high-information feedback is most effective.」（1406–1408 行）
7. **訂正回饋的數字受到未納入的調節變項影響**：「the effectiveness of corrective feedback is influenced by additional moderating variables, such as learners’ proficiency, the setting, and the genre of the task (Kang and Han, 2015). These variables were not taken into account in our meta-analysis.」（1419–1425 行）
8. **高資訊量回饋的詮釋（討論段，不是編碼定義）**：「High-information feedback contains information on task, process and (sometimes) self-regulation level. Its effect is very large, which suggests that students highly benefit from feedback when it helps them not only to understand what mistakes they made, but also why they made these mistakes and what they can do to avoid them the next time.」（1425–1430 行）注意這裡寫「(sometimes) self-regulation」，和方法段的編碼定義「additionally contained information on self-regulation」（874–878 行）說法不同；原文沒有解釋這個差別。
9. **時機的重要性是轉述 Hattie 與 Timperley，不是本研究的結果**：「Hattie and Timperley (2007) have noted the importance of not just the information in the feedback, but the appropriateness of the timing of the feedback relating to where the students are in the instructional cycle」（1408–1414 行）。
10. **學生→教師的回饋**：效果量多半來自高等教育，「the data does not allow conclusions on the effectiveness of student > teacher feedback in the K-12 context」（1438–1445 行）。
11. **學生→學生的回饋**：「student-student-feedback is the most effective form, although, again care is needed as these estimates are based on a very small sample of only eight studies」（1449–1452 行）。
12. **和 0.79 的差距從哪裡來**：作者列了排除重複研究、依精確度加權兩個可能原因（1379–1387 行），但接著說隨機效果模型不是原因（1356–1362 行），最後的判斷是「We assume that the different results mainly stem from the fact that a number of studies used in the synthesis were excluded from our meta-analysis」（1363–1368 行）。
13. **結論段**：「Feedback must be recognized as a complex and differentiated construct that includes many different forms with, at times, quite different effects on student learning.」（1569–1571 行）「Feedback, on average, is powerful, but some feedback is more powerful.」（1537–1538 行）
14. **對資訊量的估計還需要研究**：「feedback is more effective, the more information it contains and research on estimating this information would be a valuable addition to the area」（1571–1572、1528–1530 行）。

## 作者列的限制

General Limitations 一節（1514–1556 行）：

1. 把異質的研究合併會被批評是拿蘋果比橘子；作者認為合併仍然正當，但這類概括「are often the first step to later understanding the critical moderators」（1517–1519、1540–1543 行）。
2. 只看平均效果量、忽略變異有危險：「a danger lies in over-simplifications, simply using average effect sizes, and ignoring the variability across many studies, influences, contexts, and moderators」（1547–1550 行）。
3. 統計模型：用了隨機／混合效果模型加 RVE；階層線性模型可能更適合處理研究巢套在統合分析內的結構（1551–1556 行）。

散在別處的限制：

4. 樣本比 Visible Learning 的綜合小，因為排除了資料不足或不屬教育情境的研究（1369–1373 行）。Table 1 顯示 8 份既有統合分析整份排除，多份只納入一小部分（351–615 行）。
5. 原本想編的兩個變項（回饋的 perspective、結果的測量方式）因資料不足或無法從摘要判定而放棄（634–637 行）。
6. 調節變項的值是依原始研究的摘要編的（853–856 行）。原文沒有把這一點列為限制，只在方法段陳述。
7. 訂正回饋的調節變項（學習者程度、情境、任務類型）沒有納入（1419–1425 行）。
8. physical 與 behavioral 兩類研究少，估計不精確（1484–1488 行）。
9. 學生→教師的效果量多來自高等教育；學生→學生只有八個研究（1438–1452 行）。
10. 前後測設計的效果量「generally less reliable and less informative」（893–896 行）；994 個效果量裡前後測占 244 個（排除極端值後的數字，Table 3）。
11. funnel plot 不對稱、Egger 檢定顯著，不對稱來自期刊論文（998–1005 行）。
12. 出版年份中位數 1985，近 15 年的效果量只占 15%（915–917 行）。原文只陳述，沒有列為限制。
13. Tenenbaum 與 Goldring 1989 的資料取自該統合分析，「but no references of integrated studies provided」（551 行）。

## 與摘要不一致的地方

1. **摘要的 k 與效果量對不上同一批資料**。摘要：「A meta-analysis (435 studies, k = 994, N > 61,000) … Overall results based on a random-effects model indicate a medium effect (d = 0.48)」（48–52 行）。內文：994 個效果量全部合併是 d = 0.55（933–934 行）；0.48 是排除 35 個極端值之後、959 個效果量的結果（1012–1016 行）。摘要沒有提 0.55，也沒有提排除極端值。
2. **「medium」對「medium-high」**。摘要說「a medium effect」（52 行），討論說「the overall effect … is medium-high」（1064–1065、1375 行）。
3. **N**。摘要「N > 61,000」（48 行），內文「including about 61,000 subjects」（913 行）。
4. **行為指標**。摘要：「feedback has higher impact on cognitive and motor skills outcomes than on motivational and behavioral outcomes」（55–57 行）。Table 3 的點估計：cognitive 0.51、physical 0.63、motivational 0.33、behavioral 0.48，行為指標的點估計和認知指標接近，但信賴區間 [−0.09, 1.06] 跨過零（1259–1265 行）。內文討論有加「must be partly interpreted with some caution」（1484–1488 行），摘要沒有。
5. **摘要沒有提**：17% 的效果為負、Egger 檢定顯著、前後測設計的效果較高（0.63 對 0.42）、高資訊量只有 42 個效果量。

內文自己前後不一致的地方（不涉及摘要）：

6. 學生→教師：Table 3 k = 27（1329 行），討論寫「26 effect sizes」（1440 行）。
7. 學生→學生：Table 3 k = 16（1341 行），討論寫「only eight studies」（1452 行）。一個是效果量數、一個是研究數，原文沒有對起來說明。
8. 高資訊量回饋：編碼定義要求有自我調節資訊（874–878 行），討論寫「(sometimes) self-regulation level」（1425–1426 行）。

10. 結果指標四組 k 相加是 755，Table 2 的 QW df 是 750（四組應為 751）；其餘四個有子組數字的變項 df 都與 k 相符。原文沒有說明。
