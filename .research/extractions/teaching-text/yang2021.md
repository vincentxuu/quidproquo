## 研究設計

- **研究類型**：系統性回顧加統合分析，只做課堂研究。
- **檢索**（1010–1036 行）：檢索式 `[testing effect* OR test-enhanced learning OR test* OR retrieval practice* OR quiz* OR exam* OR assessment*] AND [classroom* OR class* OR lecture* OR course*]`；資料庫是 Web of Science、PubMed、PsycARTICLES、PsycINFO、Google Scholar、ProQuest Dissertation & Theses Global。Google Scholar 回傳超過 421,000 筆，「Because Google Scholar only allowed access to the first 1,000 results, we preplanned to only screen this number of articles」（1024–1025 行）。另外篩了七份既有統合分析的參考文獻與引用、Roediger 與 Karpicke 2006a 的 1,965 筆引用，並寫信給 134 位通訊作者徵求未發表研究。
- **納入與排除標準**（1038–1075、1112–1121 行；原文編號 1–6，文字檔的順序被雙欄打亂）：
  1. 只收課堂研究，「at least the initial learning phase (e.g., lecturing) occurred in the classroom. In other words, curriculum content had to be delivered in the classroom.」小考在課堂外（例如線上）做的研究有收。「Laboratory and online education (distance education) studies were not included.」
  2. 只收「testing/quizzing with no quizzing (or quizzing with fewer test questions)」的比較。比較小考頻率（同樣題數、同樣教材，只是分次不同）的研究排除。
  3. 排除重複；同一結果的學位論文與期刊論文只留期刊。
  4. 只收實證研究；只有主觀指標、沒有客觀學習成果的研究不收。
  5. 要有足夠資訊計算效果量。
  6. 「Only articles written in English were considered.」
- **篩選流程**（Figure 3，1142–1189 行）：資料庫 8,812 筆、相關文章與引用 2,954 筆；標題與摘要篩選後 1,671 筆；全文篩選排除 1,450 筆（拿不到 63、重複 387、非英文 29、不相關 623、非實證 275、資料不足 74）；220 篇合格，聯絡研究者再加 2 篇未發表，共 222。
- **最終資料**：「From those 222 projects …, 573 effects and 48,478 students’ data were extracted.」（1123–1126 行）出版年份 1929 到 2019（1265–1266 行）。
- **介入與對照**：介入組定期做小考（「by recalling or applying studied information to solve new problems」，101–104 行；註 1，162–167 行說主觀態度量表、智力與標準化測驗不算）。對照組編成四類：No/Filler activity、Restudying、Fewer questions、Elaborative strategies（1358–1360、2025–2029 行）。註 5：「Elaborative strategies included many varieties, such as concept mapping, note-taking, summarizing, and so on. These strategies had few effect sizes and hence were combined into a single category.」（2038–2040 行）
- **量了什麼**：課程考試或學期成績這類客觀的學業成就。考試內容編成 Fact、Concept、Problem-solving、Mixed、Unknown（838–847、2307–2310 行）。
- **編碼**：第一作者與一位受過訓練的研究助理各自抽取與編碼，「All divergences were settled through discussion」（1081–1085 行）。原文沒有報告編碼者間信度的數值。
- **效果量**：原研究有 Cohen's d 就直接用，否則依 Borenstein 等 2009 計算，再轉成 Hedges' g（1086–1102 行）。受試者內設計需要兩個條件的相關係數，多數研究沒報；作者用 59 個有資料的效果量估出 r = .457，「the current meta-analysis imputed a correlation of 0.457 for all within-subjects design effects」（1092–1099 行）；註 2 說改用 0.500 結果型態相同（1128–1133 行）。
- **模型**：多層次隨機效果模型，研究層級加隨機截距；調節分析是多層次 metaregression；R 的 metafor（1102–1108 行）。離群值診斷、trim-and-fill、3PSM 因為軟體不支援多層次，改用一般隨機效果模型；PET-PEESE 改用 robust variance estimation（1203–1206、1309–1312、1323–1324、1342–1343 行）。

## 作者自己的解釋與保留

1. **出版偏誤的工具都不完美**：「there is currently no perfect technique for assessing and correcting publication bias … and existing methods suffer from various limitations and weaknesses」；PET-PEESE 常過度校正、trim-and-fill 校正不足（1289–1298 行）。
2. **Q2，和其他精緻化策略比只是邊緣優勢**：「testing was only marginally better than other elaborative strategies (g = 0.095, p = .062). Of course, no proponent of testing in the classroom would claim that it is the only method for enhancing learning and retention. Elaborative strategies included many methods, such as concept mapping, note-taking, summarizing, and so on, and there were relatively few effect sizes for any of these individual methods. Future research to assess in more detail what strategies match testing in effectiveness would be valuable」（2872–2880 行）。討論另一處的措辭是「Although the effects were modest when comparing testing with other elaborative strategies (which were relatively few in number), the enhancement effect demonstrates that testing is numerically superior」（2561–2564 行）。
3. **Q5，沒被考到的內容為什麼在課堂上有效果**。這個效果是拿來和 Pan 與 Rickard 2018 的不顯著結果對照的，作者給了三個解釋（2578–2606 行）：
   - 檢定力：「the current review included a greater number of effects (k = 43) than in Pan and Rickard (2018) (k = 17)」。
   - 語意關聯：「The second explanation is semantic coherence, which has been shown to be an essential requirement to observe an enhancing effect on untested material (Little et al., 2011). Different sections of lecturing contents (and textbooks) are deeply related, but the stimuli in many of Pan and Rickard’s (2018) studies were unrelated.」導言的 Q5 段有同一個說法，出處同樣是 Little 等 2011：「Little et al. (2011) found that semantic coherence between tested and untested material is a key modulator of the enhancing effect on untested materials (i.e., testing promotes retention of untested/related materials but results in little enhancement for untested/unrelated materials)」（748–757 行）。
   - 前向效果：課堂研究是多輪「學—考」循環，「testing prospectively facilitated learning of new information in subsequent classes, and hence new materials studied in subsequent classes could benefit from prior quizzes regardless of whether they were tested or not in the subsequent quizzes」（2594–2606 行）。作者在動機理論一段把前向效果的機制寫成「Testing stimulates learners to commit more effort in the subsequent learning process, which in turn boosts new learning」（2808–2809 行）。
   - 這三個解釋都沒有在這份統合分析裡被檢驗：Untested 沒有再依關聯程度或研究期間拆開。
4. **Q6，回饋**：建議是「corrective feedback should routinely be provided following quizzing」（2607–2608 行）。作者把這個差距解釋成再次接觸教材（reexposure），不是解釋內容的品質（2229–2231、2767–2771 行）。
5. **Q3，回想與再認沒有差別要小心解讀**：「This finding must be interpreted with caution as additional exposure might contribute to the null difference」（2788–2790 行）。
6. **Q8，繼續教育不顯著**：只有 6 個效果量，而且全部是單堂（工作坊）處理（2620–2628 行）。
7. **Q12，課堂內較好的解釋**：課堂外的小考「are usually undertaken without supervision and unproctored, and students may not fully engage in those quizzes or may even cheat in them」（861–865 行）；結論是「classroom quizzes supervised by instructors are more beneficial than those administered outside the classroom without direct supervision」（2657–2662 行）。這個差距只到邊緣顯著（p = .051）。
8. **Q13，課前小考的預期機制**：「Preclass quizzing is expected to enhance learning by stimulating students to keep up with course readings before class and by informing them what to remember during class」（880–883 行）。原文沒有進一步說明課前小考時學生是否已經讀過指定教材；也沒有解釋為什麼課前的效果較小。
9. **Q16，計分與否沒差不等於否定動機理論**：高風險小考可能引發考試焦慮而抵銷動機的好處（2819–2828 行）。
10. **Q17，合作小考**：「At the current stage, no firm conclusions can be drawn」（2955–2957 行）；合作只有 21 個效果量。
11. **Q18，受試者內設計的效果較大**：作者歸因於誤差變異較小使效果量估計變大（2475–2479 行）。
12. **實驗室結果不一定能推到課堂**，以及課堂與實驗室的差別（430–473 行）：課堂學生動機較高、通常有回饋、教材量大、學生有背景知識、是多輪學—考循環。
13. **效果大小的定位**：「Although g = 0.499 is only a medium-sized effect according to conventional descriptors, it is a notably large effect by the standards of educational interventions.」（2542–2544 行）
