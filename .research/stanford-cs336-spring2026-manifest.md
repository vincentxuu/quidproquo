## Editorial contract

- The existing course overview remains `series.order: 1`; Lectures 1–17 use orders 2–18.
- One post maps to exactly one numbered regular lecture. Dates, titles, speakers, and primary artifacts come from the Spring 2026 schedule.
- `.py` means an executable lecture; PDF means a conventional slide deck. Both are L2 or better because the official lecture artifact is public and date-aligned.
- Recordings supplement the primary artifact. A missing or inaccessible recording does not change the lecture identity when the official artifact is complete.
- Lecture 18 (Daniel Selsam) remains blocked because the schedule exposes no material. Lecture 19 (Dan Fu) is a guest lecture and is outside the approved 17-lecture main series.
- Each article states its offering, lecture number/date, speaker, source type, and any material gap. No Spring 2025 material is merged into Spring 2026.
