## Five gates
1. Source tier: Stanford, Meta, HackerOne and PortSwigger are primary sources for their own framework/platform. No borrowed authority from a secondary summary.
2. Reading extent: full extracted bodies above; Cybench/Meta reused raw snapshots, current HackerOne/PortSwigger fetched. Interactive instances and actual cloud states not inspected.
3. Claim mapping: environment choice/manifest/case migration are authored teaching design. External platform facts are inline-linked exactly where introduced. No finding about security of a real service.
4. Counterevidence: CTF success does not cover product tenant rules; a safe harbor does not expand assets; AI welcome does not remove each program's automation restrictions; local/cloud results differ if identity setup changes; cannot always reset real systems. Article explicitly carries these limitations into choices.
5. Reproduction: owned offline manifest and checker, 16 positive/negative cases run; no target connection or network scanning. Record validity explicitly cannot prove authorization, operational isolation, sensible quotas, working stop enforcement, or meaningful prose.
