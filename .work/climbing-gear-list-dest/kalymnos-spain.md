# 目的地查證：希臘 Kalymnos／西班牙 Chulilla＋Siurana

查證日：2026-10-19。格式：事實｜原文引述｜URL｜發布或更新日期。所有 URL 都抓過正文（Keenable fetch）。日期抓不到的寫「未標示」，並註明為何仍採用（現行組織頁、指南頁或工具頁）。
西班牙選 Chulilla（長路線、80 m 代表）和 Siurana（經典、繩長需求較低），因為這兩地 2020 年後的資料最多。Margalef 只有 2020 年以前的 UKC 文章和彙整站，沒有列入。

## 1. Kalymnos（希臘）

### 岩質與攀登型態
- 頂級石灰岩，局部偏利；有板岩、垂直壁、口袋、鐘乳石／tufa 大仰角｜"The rock of Kalymnos is limestone of the very best quality. It can be a bit sharp in places, but it is free of choss … slabs, delicate walls, pumpy routes with pockets, and stalactites or tufas on overhanging rock and roofs."｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜未標示；內文寫到「By May 1, 2026」，所以至少是 2026-05 之後更新（指南作者 Aris Theodoropoulos & Katie Roussos 的官方網站）
- 純運動攀，沒有抱石和傳攀；有已打好掛片的多繩距｜"Kalymnos is strictly a sport climbing venue—there is no bouldering or trad climbing. There are, however, several very good bolted multi-pitches on Kalymnos and Telendos"｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜同上
- 平均路線長約 30 m｜"Most routes are about 30m long on average, but there are several longer routes around 3-5 pitches"｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜同上

### 繩長
- 官方：最低 70 m，帶 80 m 以上「總是好主意」，因為近年開了很多 40–45 m 長線｜"70m is the minimum standard single rope length for Kalymnos (9–10.5mm diameter). However, many remarkable long routes (40–45m) have been opened on Kalymnos in the past few years. Bringing an 80m rope (or longer) to Kalymnos is always a good idea."｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05
- 官方最低配備：15 支快扣＋70 m 繩｜"At a minimum, plan on 15 quickdraws + a 70m rope."｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05
- Climb Europe：至少 70 m，有 35–40 m 路線，理想是 80 m；需要 12–20 支快扣｜"At least a 70m rope is essential for rock climbing in Kalymnos, though there are routes 35 to 40m long, so ideally an 80m rope is required. All of the routes on Kalymnos are well bolted, which means around 12 to 20 quickdraws will be required for each route."｜https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-greece-kalymnos-logistics ｜未標示（頁內引用 2023 版指南，指南經銷商的現行頁面）
- 依 theCrag 4,440 條單繩距統計：70 m 可下放 80%，80 m 可下放 90%；中位長度 25 m；估計約 17 支快扣｜"Bring an 80 m rope. It lowers off 90% of the 4,440 sport pitches listed here. A 70 m does 80%." / "About 17 quickdraws."｜https://gearloop.app/crag/kalymnos/ ｜資料抓取時間 2026-09（第三方工具，以 theCrag 資料計算）
- 需要 80 m 的區域：Telendos 的新路線；極少數要更長的繩｜"While a 70 will work fine for a large majority of the routes, especially the older classics, a number of the newer routes, especially on Telendos, do require an 80. There are even a handful that need a longer rope"（Alan Rubin）｜https://www.mountainproject.com/forum/topic/124849185/best-rope-length-kalymnos ｜2023-08-31
- 反例：有人在 Grande Grotta 用 70 m 也夠，10 天只避開少數幾條路線｜"In the grande grotta my belayer put his shoes on thinking he would have to climb up some … but even there the 70 was just fine."（Kyle McPheeters）｜同上 MP 討論串｜2023-09-02
- 支持 80 m：Kalymnos 的 40 m 路線多半沒有中途確保站｜"Kalymnos was the first place I climbed where 40 m routes didn't have an intermediate anchor"（Lena chita）｜同上 MP 討論串｜2023-09-01
- Planetmountain 把 Grande Grotta 標為 70 m 繩、岩壁高 40 m｜"Height 40m … Rope 70m rope"｜https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/kalymnos-grande-grotta.html ｜未標示（頁內引用 2025 版指南）
- Grande Grotta 的大路線另外要 5–6 條長扁帶，用來減少繩子摩擦｜"For the monster routes at Grande Grotta and Sikati Cave you will also need 5–6 long slings to reduce rope drag"｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05
- 從 3 Stripes 走 Grande Grotta 中段垂降下來，要兩條 80 m 繩｜"You must have two 80m ropes and be experienced in abseiling"｜https://climbkalymnos.com/3-stripes-a-nice-easy-new-multipitch-on-kalymnos ｜未標示（climbkalymnos 路線公告）
- 部落格（2025）：建議 80 m，很多路線 40 m；買新繩就買 80 m｜"80 meter rope. Yes, 80 meters is really necessary, a lot of routes are 40 meters … If you have a 70 meter rope you can do most routes, but if you're buying new, go for 80."｜https://www.jadescapades.com/pack-for-kalymnos/ ｜2025-09-24

### 快扣數
- 官方依路線長度分級：20–25 m 用 10–12 支，25–35 m 用 15 支，35–40 m 用 20 支；超過 15 支時指南會註明｜"Routes up to 20–25m: 10–12 quickdraws / Routes from 25–35m: 15 quickdraws / Routes from 35–40m: 20 quickdraws … if you need more than 15 quickdraws it will be mentioned in the route description"｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05
- 官方建議總共帶 30–40 支，因為超長繩距要 24–28 支｜"we'd strongly urge you to bring 30–40 quickdraws, both for climbing extra long pitches (24–28 quickdraws) and for the flexibility to leave some of your QDs on a project"｜同上｜≥2026-05
- 部落格：15–20 支｜"15-20 quickdraws."｜https://www.jadescapades.com/pack-for-kalymnos/ ｜2025-09-24

### 掛片狀況與重打
- 2026-03 有人因老舊硬體失效而死亡，之後官方標準改為：認證鈦合金 glue-in 掛片＋環氧樹脂；不鼓勵開新路線｜"Following a fatal accident in March 2026 caused by the failure of ageing hardware … rebolting existing routes using the highest international standard for marine environments: certified titanium glue-in bolts and epoxy resin."｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05
- Rebolt Kalymnos（2025 年成立的非營利組織，登記為 Kalymnos Climbing Association）使用符合 EN 959 和 UIAA 123 SCC 的鈦合金 glue-in 掛片｜"Rebolt Kalymnos uses titanium glue-in bolts and anchors certified to both EN 959 and UIAA 123 SCC, installed with high-quality epoxy glue."｜https://reboltkalymnos.org/faq ｜未標示；內文寫「The average cost in August 2026 is €250」，所以是 2026-08 之後
- 重打優先順序：304/A2 不鏽鋼、材質不明或混用的路線先處理｜"routes with 304/A2 stainless steel, unknown hardware, mixed hardware, or known problem products are prioritized"｜https://reboltkalymnos.org/faq ｜≥2026-08
- 出發前查 Bolt Beta：鈦合金或 316 不鏽鋼標綠色，共 383 條鈦合金＋約 2,393 條 316；老 304 在海風中會發生應力腐蝕，外觀看不出來｜"Sectors with titanium or 316 stainless steel are marked green, which covers 383 titanium routes plus about 2,393 routes with 316 steel" / "In a marine climate, stress corrosion cracking can weaken a bolt without any visible sign. Several 304 bolts have broken in the past year."｜https://climbing.place/news/kalymnos-383-routes-rebolted-with-titanium-check-bolt-beta-before-you-climb-180 ｜2026-09（引用 8a.nu 2026-09-08 和 Lacrux 2026-09-23）
- 全島近 5,000 條路線，383 條已全面換成鈦合金；每條路線材料費約 €250｜"383 routes have been completely re-equipped – with titanium instead of stainless steel." / "Renovating a single route costs approximately 250 euros."｜https://lacrux.com/en/klettern/Following-a-fatal-climbing-accident-on-Kalymnos--almost-400-routes-were-refurbished-with-titanium-bolts. ｜2026-09-23（日期依 climbing.place 的引註）
- 市政府在 2025 年另外啟動重打計畫，但規格公開資訊很少；Rebolt Kalymnos 提醒，只換頂端確保站不等於整條路線都換新｜"a separate municipal rebolting program started at some point in 2025 … limited information has been made publicly available regarding its scope, specifications"｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05；另見 climbing.place："The municipality plans to replace lower-offs with titanium. Rebolt Kalymnos points out that most falls load the intermediate bolts, not the anchor."

### 氣候與季節
- 秋季最理想，10 月是旺季，10 月中到 11 月底通常完美；春季幾乎一樣好，但雨多、tufa 可能滲水；冬季常有溫和晴天；夏季要永遠爬陰面｜"Autumn: Ideal … mid-October to late November is usually perfect. There's a reason October is peak season." / "Spring: Almost as good as autumn" / "Summer: … The obvious rule is: Always climb in the shade."｜https://climbkalymnos.com/climbing ｜≥2026-05
- Climb Europe：最佳是 4–5 月和 9–10 月｜"the best two times to visit Kalymnos are in the spring (April and May) and the autumn (September and October)"｜https://climb-europe.com/pages/rock-climbing-greece-kalymnos-logistics ｜未標示
- 攀岩季每年約 8 個月｜"The climbing season on Kalymnos is relatively long, lasting eight months a year"｜Lacrux 同上｜2026-09-23

## 2. 西班牙：Chulilla（Valencia）

### 岩質與型態
- 圖里亞河（Turia）切出的橘色石灰岩峽谷，路線難度 5 到 8c+｜"known for its breathtaking orange limestone canyon shaped by the Turia river … grades ranging between 5 and 8c+"｜https://valenciaclimb.com/climbing-areas/chulilla ｜未標示
- 垂直到微仰角的技術型路線，平均長度超過 30 m，需要耐力；指南收錄超過 1,400 條｜"technical climbing on vertical or slightly overhanging limestone rock … long, with an average route length of over 30m. The guidebook covers over 1,400 routes"｜https://climb-europe.com/products/chulilla-sport-climbing-guidebook ｜未標示（指南經銷頁）
- 峽谷長 4 km、岩壁最高 160 m；本地攀岩者持續重打舊路線並開新區｜"un gran cañón de 4 km de largo con paredes que alcanzan los 160 metros … debido principalmente al continuo trabajo de reequipamiento y equipamiento de nuevos sectores por parte principalmente de los escaladores loc[ales]"｜https://www.femecv.com/escalada/chulilla ｜未標示（瓦倫西亞登山協會 FEMECV 的區域頁）

### 繩長與需要 80 m 的區域
- UKC Chulilla North（Pantano）：路線最長 55 m，強烈建議 80 m，可以全部下放｜"Long vertical walls with climbs up to 55m … An 80m rope is highly recommended, and it will be enough for all climbs"｜https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/chulilla_north__pantano-30666 ｜未標示（UKC 現行區域頁，頁內提到 2022 版指南）
- 「(L1+L2)」是一口氣從地面爬到第二個確保站的延伸線，不是多繩距｜"When a line says "(L1+L2)" the grade is obtained by climbing from the ground in one push. These are not multipitch routes."｜同上｜未標示
- 依 theCrag 996 條統計：70 m 只能下放 58%，80 m 可下放 75%；最長 60 m；估計約 21 支快扣｜"Bring an 80 m rope. It lowers off 75% of the 996 sport pitches listed here." / "Your 70 m only lowers off 58% of pitches here" / "About 21 quickdraws."｜https://gearloop.app/crag/chulilla/ ｜資料抓取時間 2026-09
- 部落格：80 m「100% 必要」，經典路線多為 35–40 m；快扣至少 18 支｜"An 80m rope is 100% recommended here, as most of the classics are 35-40m." / "you'll want to have a set of at least 18, maybe even more."｜https://thewanderingclimber.com/rock-climbing-chulilla-guide ｜2020-03-24 發布（標題標示 2023 更新）
- 部落格：70 m 是很多新路線的基本要求，有些人偏好 80 m｜"A 70m rope is essential for many of the newer routes and for some climbers an 80m rope is preferred."｜https://climbonline.co.uk/2020/05/10/climbing-in-chulilla ｜2020-05-10
- 長線舉例：Oasis/Chorreras 有 40–50 m 路線，整天陰涼｜"The highlight of Chulilla is Oasis/Chorreras with 40-50m routes, all in the shade all day."｜https://ukclimbing.com/forums/destinations/climbing_chulilla_spain_first_week_of_may-756494 ｜日期未取得（討論串）
- 長線舉例：Los Caminantes L1+L2（7c）55 m｜theCrag 路線標題 "7c ★★★Los caminantes L1+L2, 55m Sport climb in Chulilla"｜https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/spain/chulilla/route/629582409 ｜只看到搜尋結果標題，沒打開，算「未直接讀」
- 指南：Pedro Pons／Nuria Martí《Chulilla Climbing Guide》2025 版，定價 €25｜"Fecha de la edición:2025" / "En tienda: 25,00 €"｜https://www.libreriadesnivel.com/libros/chulilla-climbing-guide/363000003/ ｜2026-10-19 查價

### 氣候
- 冬季最熱門，溫暖；聖誕和復活節很擠；其他地方天氣差時可當備案｜"visited by climbers from around the globe, especially in winter, its warm weather making the climbing more pleasant on cold days. Note that the area is particularly crowded during Christmas and Easter holidays"｜https://valenciaclimb.com/climbing-areas/chulilla ｜未標示

## 3. 西班牙：Siurana（Tarragona, Priorat）

### 岩質與型態
- 石灰岩（Muschelkalk），以 old school 的垂直指力路線聞名；朝南，冬天也能爬｜"És coneguda pel seu estil de vies "old school", verticals i de dits. L'orientació sud afavoreix la pràctica de l'escalada durant bona part de l'any, fins i tot a l'hivern." / "El tipus de roca que trobem a Siurana és la calcària (Muschelkalk)"｜https://www.turismepriorat.org/ca/escalada-siurana ｜未標示（Priorat 官方觀光頁）
- 超過 1,700 條保護良好的運動攀路線；保護「好但不過度」｜"more than 1700 well protected sportclimbing routes … Generally the routes are good protected but never overprotected."｜https://climbingsiurana.com/en/information/siurana-general-information ｜未標示（當地攀岩學校頁）
- 指南收錄 75 區、2,014 條｜"In total 75 separate rock climbing areas are documented detailing 2,014 routes"｜https://climb-europe.com/products/siurana-rock-climbing-guidebook ｜未標示

### 繩長與快扣
- 依 theCrag 1,499 條統計：60 m 可下放 73%，70 m 90%，80 m 97%；15 支快扣涵蓋 90% 的路線，最長一條要 22 支；El Pati、Salt de la Reina Mora、L'Aparador、Can Codolar 有 35–40 m 繩距｜"It lowers off 97% of the 1,499 sport pitches listed here. A 60 m does 73%." / "15 quickdraws cover 90% of pitches. The longest needs 22." / "El Pati, Salt de la Reina Mora, L'Aparador and Can Codolar have 35–40 m pitches."｜https://gearloop.app/crag/siurana/ ｜資料抓取時間 2026-09
- Planetmountain 標示 60 m 繩｜"Rope 60m rope"｜https://planetmountain.com/en/crags/siurana.html ｜未標示（只涵蓋 195 條的精選頁）
- 結論：Siurana 用 70 m 可涵蓋九成；想爬上面四個區的長線再帶 80 m（根據上面 gearloop 的統計推論）
