## A. 韓國

### 仁壽峰 Insubong（北漢山國立公園）
- 岩質與型態：花崗岩圓頂，多繩距，以 slab 與裂隙為主，有傳統攀登也有 bolt 很疏（run-out）的運動路線｜"Fantastic multipitch granite domes within an ancient fortress... Trad as well as super run-out sport. Mostly slab and crack climbing."｜https://www.mountainproject.com/area/106225638/insu-bong-bukhansan｜區域描述未標日期；Getting There 段落標 6/25/2022
- 地形圖可在公園入口附近的裝備店買到｜"Route maps can be found in gear shops near park entrance."｜同上｜同上
- 岩壁高度超過 200 m；路線從簡單的山脊到很難的 slab 都有；重新打 bolt 的工作已加裝數十組現代化的鏈條確保站｜"The peak rises over 200 meters... Rebolting efforts have added dozens of modern chained anchors"｜https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/south-korea/insubong/route/272137737（theCrag 頁面引用的指南 Alex Buisse/Mont Blanc Lines 簡介）｜指南 2025
- 代表路線 Chouinard A（취나드 A）長 182 m、5.10a，傳統攀登，要帶雙套 cam｜"5.10a ★★★취나드 A, 182m Trad climb... Double rack."｜同上｜theCrag 頁面，警示欄有 2024-03-25
- 下降：可沿路線垂降，或登頂後從南側 Pigeon Road 垂降，兩段 30 m 垂降可以到底｜"Rappel Pigeon Road. (2 30m rappels will make it. If you do not use the correct anchor you may end up short.)"｜同上｜同上
- 多繩距都有 bolt 確保站；60 m 單繩垂降在每個地方都夠用，但道峰山常被建議帶兩條 60 m｜"All multipitches have bolted anchors... For rappelling, a 60 m rope was enough everywhere, although recommendations for Dobongsan often mention 2×60."｜https://alp.org.ua/en/archives/98218｜未標日期；圖片上傳路徑是 2026/03，推測 2026 年 3 月發布；文中說他們 11 月中去
- slab 上 bolt 很疏，有的路線會接到 A0 段｜"many trad lines that subtly transition into bolted slabs or A0 sections... The bolting on slabs still triggered nervous laughter."｜同上｜同上
- 某條 slab 路線後半段大約 40 m 只有 2 支 bolt｜"The last half of the slab is very runout, about 2 bolts for ~40m of climbing."｜https://www.mountainproject.com/route/119725057/a-ujeong-a｜Keenable 搜尋摘要；路線頁未另外標日期（※只讀到搜尋摘要，引用前要再開頁確認）
- 季節：春、秋；夏天能爬但像東南亞一樣濕熱；11 月中白天 10–15°C｜"The climbing season here is spring and autumn. Summer is possible too, but it's humid like Southeast Asia. We were there in mid-November. Daytime temperatures were around 10–15°C."｜https://alp.org.ua/en/archives/98218｜推測 2026-03
- 春季防火封閉：大型國立公園（含仁壽峰、雪嶽山）春天會因防火暫時封閉攀登｜"A lot of the big parks (including Insubong and Seoraksan) are closed to climbing... periodically in the spring for fire prevention... they were both closed at the start of April when I arrived. Insubong had opened by the end of April"｜https://www.mountainproject.com/forum/topic/125344433/advice-for-1st-time-climbing-in-south-korea｜回覆日期 2023-11-23
- 2026 年融雪期岩場禁入公告：北漢山 10 處岩場（含仁壽峰）列為對象；公告只寫出北漢山道峰 14 處的期間（2026-03-23～04-05），北漢山（仁壽峰所屬）的期間沒有寫出｜"북한산국립공원 봄철 해빙기 암벽장 이용제한(출입금지) 공고... ▣ 북한산도봉(14개소) ○ 기 간 : 2026.03.23.(월 )~ 4.05.(일)... ▣ 북한산(10개소)... 우이지구 / 인수봉"｜https://saf.or.kr/forum_01/11188｜2026-03-16（首爾特別市山岳聯盟）
- 國立公園公團的春季防火期：2026 年 1/20–5/15，部分步道從 2/15 起分批封閉；北漢山在受管制的公園名單內｜"1월 20일부터 5월 15일까지 '봄철 산불조심 기간'... 2월 15일부터 순차적으로 출입을 통제... 지리산·월출산·팔공산·계룡산·설악산·북한산 등"｜https://www.s-journal.co.kr/news/articleView.html?idxno=37947｜2026-01-20
- 國立公園公團正在推動仁壽峰等岩壁的攀登者申報制度（「암벽이용시스템」）；大韓山岳聯盟反對｜"국립공원공단이 추진 중인 '암벽이용시스템'... 인수봉 등 주요 암벽의 등반자 신고제도"｜https://kaf.or.kr/committee_04/52｜未標日期（大韓山岳聯盟官網）
- 交通：首爾地鐵牛耳新設線北漢山牛耳站 2 號出口，共乘計程車到登山口每人 2000 韓元｜"Take the UI-Sinseol line to Bukhansan Ui Station. Come out of Exit #2... These taxis take people to the trailhead for 2000won per person"｜https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/south-korea/insubong｜未標日期
- 週末人很多（登山學校和山岳會）；從地鐵站走到仁壽峰約 1 小時｜"The climbing can be very busy on the weekends because of the many mountaineering schools and clubs... about an hour hike up to Insubong from the Bukhansan Ui metro station."｜https://moredaysoff.wordpress.com/2020/11/25/climbing-above-seoul-korea｜2020-11-25（頁內照片到 2023-10）
- 仁壽峰南壁有裂隙、岩脈、煙囪、slab 等多繩距路線，最長到 12 段，接近 60 分鐘｜"INSUBONG – SOUTH FACE: Lots of crack (finger, hand, fist), dike features, flakes, offwidth, corners and slab multipitch routes up to 12 pitches. Approach 60 minutes."｜https://moredaysoff.wordpress.com/2023/10/25/where-to-rock-climb-in-bukhansan-national-park-seoul-korea/｜2023-10-25
- 嚮導公司的仁壽峰行程：7–8 段，要從山頂垂降，最好有垂降經驗｜"Climb 7 or 8 pitches of face climbing, friction slab climbing and crack climbing... We will have to rappel/abseil off the peak, so having prior experience is beneficial."｜https://rockclimbingseoul.com/multipitch-climbing｜未標日期（嚮導公司頁）

### 運動攀登區：仙雲山 Seonunsan（全羅北道高敞）
- 岩質與規模：石灰岩，數百條路線，5.8～5.14+；1990 年代開發，被稱為韓國首要的運動攀登區｜"Excellent limestone with hundreds of routes from a few beginner (5.8) to 5.14+... Seonunsan is the premier sport climbing area in the country"｜https://www.mountainproject.com/area/106675927/seonunsan｜交通段落 Updated 6/2/24
- 裝備：60 m 繩、10–15 支快扣；很多路線已掛著快扣，可以用但要先問；可以直接從確保站下放｜"60 meter rope, 10-15 draws, and your best limestone shoes. I have found that many routes will have quickdraws up already... You can lower off anchors directly."｜同上｜同上
- 5.10 級路線不多｜"WARNING: Not too many routes here for 5.10 climbers"｜同上｜同上
- 季節：春秋好天氣會排隊｜"On a nice day in the Spring/Fall, there will be waits for climbs."｜同上｜同上
- 仙雲山約 150 條路線，高 10–25 m，多數在 6a–9a｜"These limestone crags offer about 150 routes from ten to twenty-five meters high... The majority of routes are graded from 6a to 9a"｜https://moredaysoff.wordpress.com/2020/07/01/the-south-korean-sport-climbing-of-seonusan-best-area-korea-and-halmae-bawi/｜2020-07-01
- 附近할매바위 Halmae-bawi：石灰岩礫岩，大量換成黏膠式不鏽鋼 bolt，路線 12–30 m；建議帶 60 m 繩、16 支快扣、GriGri、頭盔、刷子｜"There has been a lot of rebolting with glue-in steel bolts... routes lengths range from twelve to thirty meters. Some recommended equipment would be a 60 meter rope, 16 quick draws, a GriGri, a helmet and a brush"｜同上｜2020-07-01
- 5.12a 以上很多路線掛有固定快扣，大多狀況好但有些磨損；到處都有竹竿可以掛第一支快扣；bolt 整體不錯但路線很密，容易爬錯｜"On many harder routes (5.12a [7a+] and up), fixed quickdraws are in place. Most are good, though some are worn. Bamboo sticks are everywhere for clipping the first draws... Bolting is generally good. Routes are packed close together"｜https://alp.org.ua/en/archives/98218｜推測 2026-03
- 交通：不建議大眾運輸；可搭長途巴士到高敞仙雲山巴士站；從停車場走到主岩區｜"I would recommend against Public Transit, but buy a intercity bus pass for Gochang Seonunsan Bus Terminal"｜https://www.mountainproject.com/area/106675927/seonunsan｜Updated 6/2/24
- 岩高最高 25 m，5 個岩區 100 多條｜"More than 100 routes, from 5a to 8c, spread over 5 crags... Max Height : 25 m. Rock : limestone."｜https://climbingaway.fr/en/climbing-areas/seonunsan｜未標日期（社群資料庫）

### 繩長與快扣數（新查）
- 日本一般繩長是 50 m 或 60 m，長路線會用 60 m 以上；自由攀登用單繩｜「フリークライミングでは1本で使うシングルロープが適しています。…ロープの長さは50ｍか60ｍが一般的。長いルートを登る時は60ｍ以上のロープを使うこともあります。」｜https://www.montbell.jp/generalpage/disp.php?id=711｜未標日期（mont-bell 官方裝備指南）
- 小川山：有些地方不是 60 m 繩下不來（ガマスラブ）；ノイズイノ 25 m、6 支 bolt，50 m 繩剛好；生ハム 50 m 繩剛好；エクセレントパワー 80 m 繩勉強下得來｜「60mロープでないと降りられないところもあるので注意」「25mでボルト6本…ロープは50mロープでギリギリ」「80mロープでギリギリ降りられるが、すっぽ抜け注意」｜https://www.akatsuki-climbing.com/%E3%83%AB%E3%83%BC%E3%83%88%E7%B4%B9%E4%BB%8B%E5%B0%8F%E5%B7%9D%E5%B1%B1/｜頁內日期 2019～2022-11-04（攀岩學校路線介紹，持續更新）
- 小川山ザ・ラストリゾート：一條裂隙長 70 m，通常分 2 段爬，有 70 m 以上的繩可以 1 段爬完｜「ザ・ラストリゾートは1本のクラックが70mも続いている」「70m以上のロープがあればもちろん1ピッチで登っても構わない」｜https://kyokarakimiwa.com/the-last-resort-ogawa/｜圖片 2025-03（推測 2025 年 3 月發布）
- 快扣數：小川山和城ヶ崎都找不到明確的「帶 N 支」建議；只有個別路線的 bolt 數（例：ノイズイノ 25 m 6 支）｜—｜見上｜—

## C. 繩索保養（原廠）

### Petzl
- 洗繩：只能用家用洗臉、沐浴的肥皂；不要用高壓水槍；手洗用 30°C 以下的中性溫肥皂水，再用自來水徹底沖淨；機洗選 30°C 化纖輕柔、不脫水；晾在通風室內，避開 UV 和熱源；洗後繩可能變短，要重新量長度｜"Use only household face and body soap... Do not use a high-pressure water sprayer... Wash ropes in lukewarm soapy water (ph neutral, 30° C maximum), then rinse thoroughly with fresh tap water... Choose the 30° C delicate synthetic setting, without spin cycle... Air-dry your equipment in a ventilated room away from exposure to UV or heat sources... washing a rope may reduce its length."｜https://petzl.com/US/en/Sport/News/2026-6-3/How-do-I-wash-a-rope-｜2026-06-03
- 晾乾：避開 UV 和熱源，可以攤成大圈或打成鏈結｜"Dry it away from UV and heat sources. The rope can be spread out in large loops or chained."｜https://petzl.com/us/en/sport/tech-tips-by-product/product-information-and-specifications/how-should-my-rope-be-cleaned-/CORDES-entretien_SPO_a77w0000000KyvvAAC.html｜未標日期（Tech Tip）
