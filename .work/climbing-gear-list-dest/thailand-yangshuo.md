## 一、泰國喀比 Railay / Tonsai / Ao Nang

### 岩質與攀登型態
- 岩質是石灰岩；Railay 半島上幾乎全是有掛片的運動攀路線（法式難度）｜"The routes are all bolted sport-climbing routes. The French grading system is used."｜https://www.mountainproject.com/area/105894664/laem-phra-nang-railay-tonsai ｜MP 共編頁，未標日期【社群】
- 泰國大部分是運動攀，不用帶傳攀裝備｜"Most of the routes are bolted sport routes with anchors, so leave your trad gear at home."｜https://mountainproject.com/area/105894648/thailand ｜MP 共編頁，未標日期【社群】
- 喀斯特石灰岩，岩面光滑、容易被磨亮，加上濕熱會讓體感難度降一兩級｜"The karst limestone here is very special… much of the rock is very smooth, even when virgin. It doesn't take much for it to feel polished and glassy, which when mixed with the heat and humidity can take your grade down a peg or 2."｜https://thecragjournal.com/2023/02/01/climbing-in-thailand-a-journey-through-south-east-asia/ ｜2023-02-01（2026-03-26 更新）【部落格】
- Tonsai 的多繩距也全是運動攀，不用傳攀裝備｜"All multi-pitches in Tonsai are sport routes so no traditional gear such as cams, nuts, etc., is needed."｜https://bigwall.eu/?cat=70 ｜文章日期 2024-03-08／2024-03-13【部落格】
- 深水獨攀（DWS）：Koh Poda 最有名，安達曼海群島上有大量合適的岩壁｜"Another great experience is deep water soloing… Koh Poda is the best known spot, but there are hundreds of suitable walls on the islands of the Andaman sea."｜https://thecragjournal.com/2023/02/01/climbing-in-thailand-a-journey-through-south-east-asia/ ｜2023-02-01【部落格】
- DWS 船團行程約每人 1500 泰銖，要湊滿 4–8 人才開｜"If you want to do DWS the price was 1500 baht each if I remember correctly. And they only take you if there are between 4 and 8 people."｜https://ukclimbing.com/forums/rock_talk/where_to_stay_in_krabitonsai_for_climbing_2023-756750 ｜2023（討論串標題寫 2023）【社群】
- Basecamp 不出租 DWS 用的鞋｜"SORRY WE DO NOT RENT SHOES FOR DEEP WATER SOLOING."｜https://basecamptonsai.com/climb-on-your-own ｜頁面寫 2025 update，封面圖上傳路徑是 2026/06【在地】
- Ao Nang 的 North Wall 是比較新的岩場，岩質石灰岩，路線長，大部分下雨天也能爬｜"Climbing is similar to Railay-Tonsai - steep and 3D with some very long routes… Majority of the routes can be climbed even on rainy days… Type of rocks : Limestone"｜https://mountainproject.com/area/123536911/the-north-wall-ao-nang-krabi ｜MP 共編頁，未標日期【社群】

### 路線長度與繩長
- 在地店家 Basecamp 建議：自己帶裝備的話，12 支快扣加一條 70 m 繩就夠｜"If you want to bring all gear needed; 12 quickdraws and a 70m rope will suffice."｜https://basecamptonsai.substack.com/p/climb-on-your-own-or-get-guided-or ｜2023-11-03【在地】
- 另一個說法：60 m 夠爬大部分 Tonsai 路線，但有些長線，所以帶 70 m 或先查指南｜"60 metre rope – This will be enough for most routes in Tonsai, although there are some longer routes, so either take the extra weight of a 70m rope or double-check the guidebook if in doubt"｜https://weareexplorers.co/climbing-routes-tonsai-thailand/ ｜2025-07-14【部落格】
- Ao Nang 運動攀：至少 60 m 繩、16 支快扣｜"For sport climbing in the Ao Nang area, take at least a 200-foot (60m) rope, 16 quickdraws, a belay device, a helmet, a harness, and shoes."｜https://www.climbing.com/travel/ao-nang-thailand-climbing-wellness/ ｜2025-01-16【部落格／媒體】
- 需要 70 m 以上的例子（Tonsai 多繩距）：一般建議帶兩條繩，一條至少 80 m、一條 60 m；HMS 鉤環要選標準大小，因為確保站的環很粗｜"As a rule, it's best to have two ropes with you – usually one at least 80m and the other 60m… It's good to have fairly standard-sized HMS carabiners as the rings at the belay stations are quite thick"｜https://bigwall.eu/?cat=70 ｜2024-03【部落格】
- 經典多繩距 Humanality（6b+，110 m，7 段）：單條 60 m 繩要多做一次短垂降；70 m 可以從第 6 段頂直接垂降到下一組錨點｜"With a single 60m rope, it's one short rappel from the top of pitch 6 to the top of pitch 5, then three raps to separate abseil anchors. If you have a 70m rope, you can rap straight from the top of pitch 6 to the next set of anchors."｜https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/15687961 ｜theCrag 共編頁，未標日期【社群】
- 同一面牆的 Eviction（8a）是 52 m 兩段，全部鈦合金掛片｜"8a ★★★ Eviction Sport 52m, 2, 19 … All titanium bolts (P1: 12 bolts, P2: 7 bolts)."｜https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/15687961 ｜theCrag 共編頁【社群】
- Thaiwand 經典 Crystal Flame（6b+，70 m）第 1 段 35 m、12 支掛片，全部鈦合金｜"16a 35m 26b+ 25m 35a 10m … All titanium bolts… 12 bolts… 11 bolts."｜https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/route/17722507 ｜theCrag 共編頁【社群】
- Ao Nang Dream Wall 的 Fun Dee（6b+，138 m，2024-03 開線）標示需要 16 支快扣、單條 60 m｜"Required: 16 draws Single 60m rope Long anchor sling (optional)"｜https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/route/9489822705 ｜開線 2024-03【社群】

- 在地出租套組的單位是 12 支快扣｜"Set of 12 quickdraws"｜https://basecamptonsai.com/climb-on-your-own ｜約 2025–2026【在地】

### 掛片狀況與重打
- 不是鈦合金就不要爬；鋼加膠在 Tonsai 2–3 年內就會失效｜"If it is not Titanium, do not climb it! For a variety of reasons, any combination of steel and glue fails after 2-3 years in Tonsai. The combination of humidity, salt and the constant acid bath (damp calcium carbonate flow stone) eats even the highest marine grade steel."｜http://railay.com/railay/climbing/climbing_rebolting.shtml ｜未標日期的在地網站頁（內容年代偏舊）【在地】
- 同一頁：Thaitanium 只在旺季活動幾週，其餘時間由攀岩店重打；點名 Railay Climbing Rock Shop 的 Toto、Tonsai The Rock Shop 的 Gop，以及 King、Basecamp｜"The Thaitanium project tends only to be active for a few weeks in high season. For the rest of the year the various Climbing shops do the heavy lifting- rebolting and changing anchors."｜http://railay.com/railay/climbing/climbing_rebolting.shtml ｜未標日期【在地】（註：這頁連到的 thaitaniumproject.com 已被賭博網站佔用，不要引用那個網域）
- Thaitanium Project（創辦人 Josh Lyons）自稱過去 15 年重打了半島約 2/3 的路線，與 UIAA、ASCA、Titan Climbing、Access Fund 合作；官網已關閉，改用 YouTube 與 GoFundMe｜"For the last 15 years the Ti has been an integral part of the re-bolting process on the Peninsula… successfully re-bolted around 2/3 of the routes throughout the Peninsula… I have decided to take the website down"｜https://gofundme.com/f/thaitanium-project ｜頁面沒標日期；推測約 2024–2025（「過去 15 年」，組織者頭像時間戳是 2025-02）
- 鋼掛片在 Railay 幾年內就失效，因此啟動大規模鈦合金重打；Thaitanium 是志工計畫，部分由 American Safe Climbing Association 支援｜"In places like Railay Beach, Thailand, stainless steel bolts began failing after only a few years in use. The failures eventually led to large-scale titanium rebolting efforts through projects like the Thaitanium Project, a volunteer initiative supported in part by the American Safe Climbing Association."｜https://climbing.com/community/why-do-seaside-crags-around-the-world-still-have-so-many-bad-bolts ｜2026-05-26 更新【媒體】
- 大部分路線已經重打成鈦合金，但還有少數舊不鏽鋼掛片｜"A huge effort by the ThaiTanium Project has ensured that climbing can continue safely in the region, though some old stainless steel bolts remain. Check TheCrag or recent guidebooks for updated information."｜https://weareexplorers.co/climbing-routes-tonsai-thailand/ ｜2025-07-14【部落格】
- 實例：theCrag 上 Bird's Nest Drop 仍是舊不鏽鋼膠黏掛片，標示「不要爬」｜"Old stainless steel glue-in bolts. Do not climb!"｜https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/15687961 ｜theCrag【社群】
- 重打不是單一組織在做，而是個人和在地公司自掏腰包；建議捐給在地 bolt fund｜"It seems there is not one singular group doing the work but a combination of dedicated individuals and local companies. These people are not supported by sponsors or grants and pay for the new bolts out of their own back pockets"｜https://thecragjournal.com/2023/02/01/climbing-in-thailand-a-journey-through-south-east-asia/ ｜2023-02-01【部落格】
- 現行指南 The Pocket Guide 第 10 版（2026 年 1 月更新）作者是有在參與重打的泰國在地攀岩者，書中標示特定掛片的警告｜"fully updated in January 2026… important safety notes, including warnings about specific bolts… the only guide written by a local Thai climber who is actively involved in rebolting routes"｜https://thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/13949881 ｜2026-01【社群／指南出版方文案】
- Basecamp 指南 2025 更新版依掛片狀態重新標示所有路線｜"All routes newly evaluated and labelled according to their bolt status."｜https://basecamptonsai.com/climb-on-your-own ｜2025 update【在地】
- Ao Nang North Wall 全部是鈦合金掛片，用 Hilti 500 膠固定；入場費 50 泰銖｜"all bolts are titanium set with Hilti 500 glue. There is an entry fee of 50 baht"｜https://mountainproject.com/area/123536911/the-north-wall-ao-nang-krabi ｜MP 共編頁，未標日期【社群】

### 氣候
- 乾季是 11 月到 3 月初，4 月最熱，5–10 月是雨季｜"the dry season, which runs from November until early March. April is usually the hottest month and May to October is the rainy season. No matter what time of the year you are climbing in Southern Thailand it will be hot."｜https://thecragjournal.com/2023/02/01/climbing-in-thailand-a-journey-through-south-east-asia/ ｜2023-02-01【部落格】
- 另一個說法：12–3 月最佳，溫度和濕度較低，但人多｜"Tonsai, Thailand is best climbed during the country's dry season, which is between December and March. Temps and humidity are lower"｜https://weareexplorers.co/climbing-routes-tonsai-thailand/ ｜2025-07-14【部落格】
- 雨季 6–10 月平均濕度 87%，南部全年平均約 28°C，全年都能爬｜"rainy with plenty of sunshine from June to October (average day temperature 29 degrees Celsius and 87% humidity)… Climbing in Thailand is possible all year round."｜https://mountainproject.com/area/105894648/thailand ｜MP 共編頁【社群】
- 雨天備案：Tonsai Wall & Roof 是外傾岩頂，全天有陰影，下雨也乾｜"Overhung Roof Always shady Dry in the rain"｜https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/15687961 ｜theCrag【社群】

## 二、中國廣西陽朔

### 岩質與攀登型態
- 喀斯特石灰岩，以運動攀為主，有少數傳攀線｜"The climbing is typical Asian limestone. Smooth and white where sheltered from the environment, and spiky and black where the rain hits it. Most of the climbing is sport though there are a few traditional lines."｜https://www.mountainproject.com/area/106763709/yangshuo ｜MP 共編頁，未標日期【社群】
- 主要是運動攀；抱石和河上 DWS 只是附帶｜"Sport climbing, overwhelmingly. The bouldering is incidental, and the deep water soloing is a novelty day rather than a reason to come."｜https://climbgrades.com/blog/yangshuo-rock-climbing ｜2026-08-05 更新（作者 2025-11 實地攀爬一個月）【部落格】
- 熱門經典線已經被磨得很亮；岩質不穩定，要戴頭盔｜"Polish. Thirty years of traffic on a soft-ish limestone has left the popular lines genuinely slick… Hollow flakes and crumbly sections are common… A helmet is not optional here"｜同上
- 有多繩距：Low Mountain 120 m 5.8、興坪 Nine Horse Mural Hill 180 m 12c 等｜"Low Mountain – L'Echo des Montagnes, 120m, 5.8… Xingping – Nine Horse Mural Hill, 180m, 12c. This must be the Mecca of multipitching in Yangshuo"｜https://www.thewanderingclimber.com/climbing-yangshuo/ ｜2020-04-07【部落格】
- 規模：全縣 70 多個岩場、1200 多條線路（官媒數字）｜"全县2000多座山峰已开发成70余个独立岩场，1200多条线路"｜http://society.people.com.cn/n1/2025/0929/c428181-40574035.html ｜2025-09-29【官媒】

### 路線長度與繩長
- 大多數路線 15–32 m，最長的例外是 Twin Gates 的 Xin Jiang Black，45 m；70 m 是實際下限，80 m 比較從容｜"Route length runs from 15 m to 32 m for most things, with the odd long outlier: Xin Jiang Black at Twin Gates is 45 m. A 70 m rope is the practical minimum and an 80 m is more comfortable."｜https://climbgrades.com/blog/yangshuo-rock-climbing ｜2026-08-05【部落格】
- 同文的裝備清單：繩至少 70 m、最好 80 m，繩尾要打結；快扣 16 支｜"Rope: 70 m minimum, 80 m preferred. Routes are commonly 25 to 32 m and Xin Jiang Black is 45 m. Knot the ends. Quickdraws: 16. Several routes have more bolts than you would expect for their length."｜同上
- 不同意見（2020）：60 m 綽綽有餘，但很多熱門線是 35 m｜"You will be more than prepared with a 60m rope, but a lot of Yangshuo's favourite routes are 35m."｜https://www.thewanderingclimber.com/climbing-yangshuo/ ｜2020-04-07【部落格】（註：35 m 的線用 60 m 繩放下來會很緊，原文本身就有矛盾；文章寫的建議範圍是 "Rope – 60-80m"）
- PlanetMountain 岩場頁：White Mountain 標示用 70 m 繩，大部分路線 25–30 m；不是每條線都有下降鉤環，要多帶一個螺旋鎖鉤環和幾支快扣｜"Rope 70m rope… Most routes are between 25m - 30m long… not all are equipped with a lower-off carabiner. Always take a screwgate carabiner and some additional quickdraws for the lower-off manoeuvre."｜https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/yangshuo-white-mountain.html ｜未標日期（內容引用 Paul Collis 的舊指南，年代偏舊）【指南類頁面】

### 快扣數
- climbgrades：16 支（見上，2026-08-05）。
- 指南作者 Andrew Hedesh 的建議（70 m＋20 支；只待一週的話 60 m＋14 支）出自 2018 年 MP 論壇，早於 2020，依規則不採用｜https://mountainproject.com/forum/topic/114945354/gear-for-yangshuo ｜2018-08-13（排除）

### 掛片狀況與維護
- 偶爾會遇到生鏽或會轉動的掛片｜"Rock quality is inconsistent: hollow flakes, crumbly huecos and the occasional rusty or spinning bolt"｜https://climbgrades.com/blog/yangshuo-rock-climbing ｜2026-08-05【部落格】
- 最老的掛片約 2000 年打在月亮山，2020 年已經換了很多；洞穴內的掛片特別容易壞（Bamboo Cave、Twin Gates），曾有人因掛片脫落摔斷腿；建議帶扳手｜"The oldest bolts in Yangshuo are from around the year 2000 on Moon Hill. Many of these were actually replaced this year… I've also spied more than one ruined cave bolt.
