## Notes / unverified / discontinued

**Possibly discontinued (popular models, worth flagging):**
- **Black Diamond Stonehauler Pro 45L**: product URL now 301-redirects to the `haul-bags-duffels` collection and is gone from the BD store search. **Stonehauler Pro 30L** and **Stonehauler 45L** are on clearance (US$139.97 / US$113.97 vs MSRP US$199.95 / US$189.95). The current BD line is Stonehauler 45/65/95/125 plus the new Mission 100/130.
- **Arc'teryx Carrier Duffle 55 / 80** (old names): arcteryx.com GB/CA/US pages show "no longer exists or currently unavailable". Successor: **Carrier 40 / 75 / 100 Duffle**.
- **Mammut Cargon (90/110)**: on clearance at REI. Successor: **Mammut Cargo 35/50/70/100/140** plus Wheeled Cargo 45/90.
- **Grivel Expedition Duffel / Duffel Bag Light 45**: both URLs 301 to `/products/expedition-duffel-45`, which then redirects to the Grivel homepage. The Grivel store search returns no duffel. Likely discontinued.
- **Exped Tempest 70/100/140 gear bags**: exped.com/en/products/gear-bags/tempest-100 returns 404, and the Exped USA Shopify search returns no duffel. Likely discontinued.
- **Osprey Transporter (F21 generation)**: the old US URLs (…tranptr65f21…, …rolltrn90f21…) are 404. Replaced by the S25 Transporter / Rolling Transporter, which I verified on the EU/UK sites. The US S25 URL also returned 404 or could not be crawled, so I could not confirm the official US price.
- **Lowe Alpine AT Kit Bag 40/60/90/120**: lowealpine.com/eu and /uk return 404. Lowe Alpine appears to have moved under rab.equipment (search shows `rab.equipment/us/lowe-alpine-at-kit-bag-90`), but that page could not be opened (Jina returned a tracker stub; Exa returned CRAWL_NOT_FOUND). Status: **Unverified**.

**Unverified / gaps:**
- **Fish Products**: the catalog page is live but looks unchanged for years, with no cart or stock signal. The Grade V and Budget prices did not appear in the extract. I opened the OGL Fish Deluxe review but could not extract a date.
- **Mystery Ranch Mission Duffel**: mysteryranch.com could not be read via Jina (the mission-duffel-55/90 pages are 404 and the site is NetSuite, not Shopify). Exa returned a snapshot of `/mission-duffel-bag` showing US$199 and out of stock, with a BV timestamp from 2024. The specs are reliable; the current price is unverified. The brand blog still lists 40/55/90.
- **Mammut Cargo weight**: mammut.com does not show weight in the readable text. The 2.98 lb (Cargo 100) and 3.06 lb (Cargon 110) figures are REI listing data, not the official spec.
- **BD Stonehauler 65 / Mission 130 weight**: no official spec extracted. Mission weights come from BD's own description copy (130L 2.5 lb, 100L 2.2 lb). The Shopify `grams` values are shipping weights and are excluded.
- **Osprey Transporter 65 weight**: the spec table was truncated in the Exa fetch.
- **Brands with no relevant duffel/haul product found**: Ortovox (no duffel in search), Edelrid (only rope/dirt bags; Kurt haulbag seen only at a reseller, not verified), Climbing Technology (carrying/work bags only), Simond/Decathlon (Decathlon's Extend duffels are Forclaz, not Simond), and Millet (Divino Duffle 40/60 at retailers, not checked on the official site). CAMP/Cassin Torre 40/70 haul bags appear at retailers (Bent Gate) but were not verified on the official site.
- UKC's Lowe Alpine AT Kit Bag 90 review appears to date from 2015, so it is excluded under the 2020+ rule.
- Retailer prices marked "search card" come from Keenable price cards and were not opened. Treat them as indicative only.

**Review coverage summary:** OGL Best Duffel Bags (2026) and Switchback Best Duffel Bags / Best Wheeled Duffel Bags (2026) cover Patagonia, TNF, Osprey, Gregory and BD (as "Stonehauler 60L"). OGL Best Haul Bags (2024) covers Metolius and BD Wall Hauler. Switchback's 2026 pick for "Best Duffel for Mountaineering Expeditions" is the Samaya Mountainproof60 (US$310). Samaya is outside the brand pool and is noted here only for context.
