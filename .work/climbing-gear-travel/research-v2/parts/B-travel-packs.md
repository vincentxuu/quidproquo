## Notes / unverified / discontinued

- **Deuter AViANT line looks discontinued on deuter.com.** The AViANT Access 38 and AViANT Carry On Pro 36 official URLs both return 404, and the AViANT Access Pro 70 page says "No longer…". The current travel-backpacks category lists only Carry On 30 (carry-on), Access 55, Access Pro 60 SL and Access Pro 65 (all too large for carry-on), plus duffels. Older reviews: Pack Hacker Access 38 (2023-04-24), https://packhacker.com/travel-gear/deuter/aviant-access-38 . Retailers still list the AViANT bags at clearance prices.
- **Osprey Farpoint/Fairview 40 are mid-refresh.** The F22 version is selling out (UK F22 page OOS; US Fairview F22 page 404) and the S26 version is live on the EU, UK and DK sites. The S26 Farpoint UK page was behind Cloudflare and could not be read, so the Farpoint weight above is the F22 figure.
- **Osprey Daylite Travel 24** (24L, below scope): Paddy Pallin (AU) lists it as "Clearance" in search results. Possibly discontinued; I did not open that page, so this is unverified.
- **Lowe Alpine Halo 40:** the official EU URL renders no product content and retailer listings are old. Possibly discontinued; unverified.
- **Arc'teryx** has no current 35–45L travel pack. REI's Arc'teryx carry-on filter shows only the Granville 25 ($230, on sale at $160.93), Mantis 26 and Heliad 15, all daypacks.
- **No dedicated carry-on travel pack found for:** Rab (Muon 40 is a hiking pack), Exped (Lightning 40, Verglas 40 and Mountain Pro 40 are trekking/alpine packs that onebag.travel rates as fitting about 2% of airlines), Black Diamond, Hyperlite, Mammut (Seon Transporter 26 is a 26L commuter pack, below scope).
- **Thule Subterra 2** is in OGL's roundup but is an urban luggage line, so I left it out. Thule Landmark has an outdoor/trek angle; Aion is borderline.
- **Patagonia** US and CA pages returned a queue/"Sit tight" page, so prices come from Patagonia AU (AUD). The Black Hole Pack 32L is listed as 30L in Patagonia's own size table.
- **Mission Rover 45** volume is listed as 45L by the brand, 42L by Pack Hacker, and 2565 cu in (~42L) on the official spec block.
