## Notes / unverified / discontinued

- **Patagonia Ascensionist 25L — Possibly discontinued.** Only Worn Wear (used) listing found; patagonia.com climbing-pack collection now lists Ascensionist 35L/55L, Cragsmith 32L/45L, Free Wall 44L — no 25L. The popular 25L leader pack seems gone; nearest current options are the Ascensionist 35L or Cragsmith 32L (Cragsmith not researched).
- **Arc'teryx Alpha SK 32 and Alpha AR 20:** old arcteryx.com URLs return "no longer exists / currently unavailable". SK 32 is a ski pack (out of scope). AR 20 shows only at retailers on markdown (REI −26% snippet). Status Unverified / likely discontinued; current Arc'teryx climbing line = Alpha FL 20/30 + Alpha SL 30.
- **Mammut Trion 18** is on 30% markdown at mammut.com and retailers. The newer Trion 15 (vest-style, USD 169.95) looks like its replacement. Not confirmed by Mammut.
- **Blue Ice Wadi 15:** US store shows it unavailable (Wadi 22 too); the EU store (blueice.com) shows it in stock at 90.00 (currency not confirmed). Firecrest 28 (USD 169.95, US store) exists but wasn't fully captured (alpine-oriented).
- **Petzl:** petzl.com product URLs (US/INT, www and m.) all 404 on 2026-10-19, which looks like a site migration. Specs come from Petzl's official 2026 Sport workbook. BUG is Petzl's only climbing pack (Bernard is a caving/canyon bag, not checked).
- **BD Shopify `grams`** (420/440/620) are shipping weights. Official spec weights are taken from the product pages (403/374/408/587 g). BD Speed 30 (USD 219.95) and Cirque 25 (USD 219.95, ski/alpine) exist but are out of scope. No BD "Ethos" pack found (Ethos is a clothing line).
- **Grivel Zen** (20/30/35/40) is gone from us.grivel.com. Current packs are Spartan X 20/30/45, Rapido Evo 18 and Alpine Pro 40+10. Spartan 30 (non-X, USD 208) shows out of stock → likely superseded by Spartan X.
- **Climbing Technology** sells no structured multipitch pack; besides Magic Pack it only has haul bags (Azimut 25–35 L, Craggy, Zenith).
- **CAMP Rox** (ref 3073) is a 40 L crag pack, so it's out of range. M30 (1180 g, 30 L) also exists, retailer-only info.
- Haul column: "—" means the official page doesn't mention a haul loop. Many of these packs do have a top grab loop, but it isn't stated.
- Retailer prices marked "snippet" come from search-result text only and were not opened.
- Reviews not used: Alpinist Mutant 22 (2018, before 2020); Packstack (not an approved outlet); Backpacker Alpha FL 30 (opened only as a snippet).
