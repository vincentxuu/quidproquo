- **Not found / out of scope**: Mystery Ranch — no 15–30 L climbing-specific pack found; Scepter 35/50 are its alpine packs (35 L, above range; official page not fetchable). Hyperlite Prism is 40 L (out of range; reviewed by Climbing.com and Switchback 2026). Edelrid "Vertigo" does not exist in current line. Exped Couloir 30 is ski-touring, not included. Sterling: rope-access rope bag; official page 404 — omitted. TNF Phantom 38 (US$200) is out of range.
- **"Under harness" column**: no brand states this explicitly; packs with removable/webbing-only hip belts (Rab Latok, Gregory Alpinisto LT, HMG Summit, Millet Prolighter, Deuter Guide Lite) are the practical candidates — inference, not a spec.
- **Haul loop**: only Edelrid Rubi explicitly lists a haul loop among fetched pages; others not stated.
- Reviews: approved-outlet (2020+) reviews found only for Deuter Guide Lite (Climbing.com 2020, UKC 2020) and rope bags (OGL 2024). Switchback Travel 2026 and Climbing.com 2026 climbing-pack roundups do not cover the D2 models. Other "reviews" found were retailer user reviews or low-quality SEO sites (not cited).
- Weight caveats: HMG Summit 30 and Simond Alpinism 22 weights are Shopify `grams` fields (likely product weight, not certified spec). Ortovox Trad 28, Exped Black Ice 30 weights not exposed on fetched pages.
