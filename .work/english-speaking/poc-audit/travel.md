## Read sources and scope

- [Have got and have](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/have-got-and-have): possession, supports map/charger and pen/power-bank substitutions. Full phrases adapted.
- [Here and there](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/here-and-there): speaker/listener location; front-position here + noun subject; found-object Here it is; handing-object Here you are/go. Supports ticket presentation as an adaptation and found-object expression directly. Does not make all alternatives equivalent.
- [There is / there are](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/there-is-there-s-and-there-are): introducing existence or presence. Supports cafe/bus/toilet and swaps; questions and locations composed with here-and-there, not quoted dialogue.
- [Get](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/get): REACH means reaching/arriving at a place; get to + destination and get there examples. Supports station/museum and destination swaps, as composed practice sentences. Travel intentions are not service requests; requests politeness advice cannot make I want to categorically wrong.
- [Requests](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/requests): can/could/would request structures; limited supplemental support, not a native dialogue/frequency study.

- [Walk](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/walk): Groundlane selector extraction read destination and can-walk examples. Supports motion/possibility structure; complete question still adapted.
