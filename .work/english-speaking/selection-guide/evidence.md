# Speaking practice selection guide — evidence

Checked2026-10-19. Reader question: which practice arrangement fits a Taiwan adult's task, starting ability, actual schedule, financial commitment and feedback needs? Scope is synthesis of this session's already read official evidence, not a new exhaustive market ranking or efficacy comparison. All examples and two-week criteria are author-designed.

- NTNU English listing and individual class evidence: distinct current adult noncredit courses. No university-wide quality claim, no guarantee any particular course remains open. Budget example requires actual term total/material/travel, not quoted price fitting automatically.
- EV PowerSpeaking page43 and ESL42: combinations of private and group teaching differ from WSE GeneralEnglish. Raw original course structure adopted without converting class count into private speech time or prescribing workload.
- WSE GeneralEnglish official and its worker evidence: general English group route; housing/trip costs separate. Current public terms and dated course-info cancellation rules differ, so synthesis preserves order-specific written confirmation before payment. No claim a particular rule wins.
- Speak purchase and ELSA plan help: AI course/roleplay/pronunciation-feature categories, plan-specific access. No claim these outperform tutors, same privileges across tiers, validated proficiency scores or account-wide unlimited use.
- Tandem FAQ/call help: reciprocal exchange, partner not paid teacher; calls Pro with some earlier-member exceptions. Full raw independently rechecked in this task (see tandem/independent-review.md). Paid discovery does not guarantee partner commitment.
- Review-routine official BritishCouncil teaching/recording guidance: selecting a task, baseline, feedback and repeated attempt are supported teaching options. A two-week assessment period is our practical fit check, not an intervention trial, CEFR assessment or vendor free-trial term.

Official references deliberately support category and payment differences. Community sources are only in the linked individual guides; this article does not add three anecdotal documents or infer comparative effectiveness from them. Same source repeated across provider guides is not independent corroboration.

## Design examples and factual guardrails

Persona1:2×15minutes/week, trial spending capNT$500, no annual prepayment. Those are invented constraints, not market quotes. Independent review identified real duration mismatch; both languages now say solo practice first, book only if an additional full actual trial slot can be made, otherwise do not buy. Preply minimum4 additionally requires feasible full-cycle attendance.
Persona2:Saturday3hours, Taipei commute, total-termcapNT$8000. Compare named class level/opening/cost; reject if over cap, no automatic NTNU/BritishCouncil purchase recommendation.
Persona3:fourweeks leave, everydayconversation, strict totaltravelbudget. Compare same dates/housing and all components; no unverified GBP/TWD conversion, cheapest-country claim or overseas outcome advantage. If cost/terms/schedule fails, stay with local practice.

Two-week table: user-defined observation window, explicitly not a shared free trial; financial decision may be due before first week ends. Continue/adjust/stop criteria concern actual scheduling, intelligible feedback, retry and changed-condition performance. Stopping additional purchases isn't a guarantee of refund; paid existing lessons follow their actual rules. Boundary violations may end interaction immediately, aligned with community guidelines, no unsolicited service-wide safety ranking.
