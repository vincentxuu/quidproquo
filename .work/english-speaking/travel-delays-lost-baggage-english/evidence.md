## Language review
- Distinguish a delayed flight from a cancelled one; duration uses by, expected departure asks when.
- The bag dialogue is specifically a carry-on left on the plane. Do not teach “I’ve lost my bag” as a precise report of missing checked baggage.
- Question about return does not promise recovery. Fictional staff responses below are authored practice, not copied policy.
- Translation checked per paragraph; recall prompts align with scenario/card meanings.
