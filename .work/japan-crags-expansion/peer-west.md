## Findings

This review does not determine the biological claim false. A specific habitat source would improve traceability, or the sentence can be omitted; no invented seasonal ban should be added.

## Primary and relevant sources independently reopened

- Mikuradake manager https://mikura.org/trekking/ : requires advance entry form at rest hut; when closed place form in the supplied postbox; hikers may park in the square in front. Article preserves these actual requirements.
- Miyazaki tourism https://www.kanko-miyazaki.jp/spot/1053 : national scenic designation; 25 parking spaces; approximately 100 m visitor walk from Senjojiki trailhead. Article distinguishes this visitor walk from cliff approaches and identifies the Miyazaki destination.
- Yaese tourism association https://yaese-kankou.com/about/ : Asahibashi/Naha terminal connection and Ryukyu Bus 50 to Gushichan stop. Article applies it to the settlement rather than all coastal boulders.
- Tamano City bouldering page https://www.city.tamano.lg.jp/soshiki/16/2213.html : official bouldering map, brushing and carry-out requests; no safety guarantee. Article’s environmental duties are consistent with the page.
- Oita official facility page https://www.visit-oita.jp/spots/detail/5911 : Onagara Forest Park Campground and facility parking. Article explicitly does not generalize facility parking to every Honjo cliff.
- Nakatsu City https://www.city-nakatsu.jp/doc/2026020600059/ : Hachimensan hiking routes, maps, lodge parking and visitor trails. Article does not call this climbing-bolt or sector-opening certification.
- Rokko Cable https://www.rokkosan.com/cable/ : current operator information with dated older notices also present. Article tells readers to check applicable dates rather than presenting an older interruption as current.
- Nodake 2023 local account https://note.com/sac24chelp/n/n8fd18447c29f : reports renewed use after rain damage, with parking still an issue. Article keeps its date and does not promote it into 2026 managerial permission.
- Shimane Free Climbing Association https://sfa.jpn.org/eria1.html : local destination descriptions and requests concerning parking, toilets and rubbish. Article keeps independent coastal access boundaries.
- JFA Washizuyama warning https://freeclimb.jp/access/10123/ : published January 2025, approximately 30 kg collapse; Dome Crack and ギブマインドトウ no-climbing warning; Cosmic Dancer/Jet and routes toward Jagaimo/Amphitheatre carry overhead-rockfall warnings. The article’s scope is accurate. Dome Crack’s table row explicitly says restricted and identification-only; the four gallery clips are other named Katchin Rock lines, not the restricted routes.
- Minami Town https://www.town.minami.lg.jp/docs/296.html : June 2026 notice concerns Ohama Beach, 20 May–20 August, with mapped night restrictions. Hiwasa article does not extend that notice into a blanket Arase-no-hana closure.
- CLIMBING-net Hiwasa https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E6%97%A5%E5%92%8C%E4%BD%90/ : older bird-nesting restraint after Golden Week through July; marine-corrosion and tidal considerations. Article labels this secondary information separately from the current municipal beach notice.
- CLIMBING-net Gushichan https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E5%85%B7%E5%BF%97%E9%A0%AD%E3%83%9C%E3%83%AB%E3%83%80%E3%83%BC/ : explicitly based on pre-2002 information; sacred rocks, private areas, no vehicles on beach and no beach camping. Article treats it as old secondary rules, not blanket current permission.
- CLIMBING-net Honjo https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E6%9C%AC%E5%8C%A0/ : fragile area above Koshinto at Inoue Tower is restricted; tourist paths and parking require care. Article retains the named local limit rather than closing the whole destination.
