The Chinese part of the series opens with costume romance and xianxia: feuding clans joined by marriage, a butcher who saves a fallen marquis, a woman buried alive who returns to the capital under another’s name, and an orchid spirit bound to a moon lord by a curse. All 18 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. Intrigue, mystery, wuxia and fantasy are in the [intrigue and mystery post](/posts/film/2026-11-12-cdrama-intrigue-mystery-wuxia-en); compared with the [Korean romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-romance-fantasy-en), Chinese costume romances run much longer.

Two rules for Chinese dramas differ from the Korean and Japanese posts:

- **State-run awards don’t count.** The Feitian Awards, Golden Eagle Awards and the annual drama recommendation list are run by state bodies, so they are not counted as award juries and appear separately on each card as “State-run lists”.
- **iQIYI’s international site and WeTV don’t count as legal Taiwan channels.** Since 2020 Taiwan’s economics ministry has barred local agents and distributors for Chinese streaming services, and the Mainland Affairs Council deems iQIYI Chinese-funded ([CNA](https://www.cna.com.tw/news/firstnews/202008185009.aspx), in Chinese). Shows available only there are on the watch-later list.

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Long, and mostly on Hami Video.** 16 of the 18 run 30 to 40 episodes, and The Story of Ming Lan has 78; 15 are on Hami Video.
- **International fans score higher than Douban.** The chart below covers the 5 shows whose Douban score has a readable source; all five score higher on MyDramaList.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, state-run lists (where any), scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: Chinese dramas have no Metacritic scores, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_chinese_dramas), IMDb and Douban. The score line gives Douban only where the source is readable; Douban figures from Taiwan media roundups appear in the review entry and are snapshots in time.
- **Content**: China has no rating system; Taiwan platform ratings are given where shown. Content notes come from the IMDb Parents Guide; most shows have no votes, so the notes follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages. Netflix listings backed only by media reports or chart entries appear as notes.

### Palace, family feuds and revenge

A merchant’s daughter, a woman buried alive, a concubine’s daughter, a family stripped of its rank, and noblewomen who swap places or are reborn. Beyond romance, most centre on family power struggles or revenge.

#### Flourished Peony

In the Tang dynasty, He Weifang, a merchant’s daughter who grows rare peonies, leaves a marriage of convenience for Chang’an, partners with imperial envoy Jiang Changyang and opens a flower shop with a group of downtrodden women ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flourished_Peony)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #57 (8.5)` `C Douban top-rated Chinese series of 2025 #8 (7.8)` `D Dengta 2025 domestic series views #9 (1.28B)` `D Mango TV 2025 views #2` `E Maoyan 2025 TV heat #2` `E Weibo 2025 quality work of the year` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 year-in-review (mixed on part one, positive on the sequel)` `G MyVideo 2025 drama chart #2 (sequel #8)`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: Feitian Awards 2026 outstanding TV drama, Golden Eagle Awards 2026 nomination
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (Flourished Peony) (9,049 ratings) | IMDb 5.7 (1.5K votes) | Douban 7.8 (Douban 2025 annual list page, as of November 2026)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. Writing mid-run about the first half, [潘光中 at 500times](https://500times.udn.com/wtimes/story/120840/8513255) (in Chinese) argues that character depth decides everything; not flawless, he says, but a sign the genre is coming down to earth. MyDramaList 8.5 and Douban 7.8 sit unusually close, with awards and viewing numbers on the same side; only IMDb (5.7) and Taiwan’s PTT chatter lag.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, business rivalry and romance with nothing specific flagged.
- **Length**: 32 episodes, January 2025, finished. The sequel, In the Name of Blossom, has 24 episodes (June 2025), also finished; MyDramaList rates it separately at 8.4 (4,628 ratings). IMDb merges both into one entry.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Yi Qianzhong’s web novel; no need to read it. Finish this before the sequel, In the Name of Blossom.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/4702) (free tier + 影劇 plan); [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/286412.do?cs=2) (subscription, leaves 6 January 2030); [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/17037/eps/1) (available). Taiwan streaming for the sequel, In the Name of Blossom, is unconfirmed

#### The Double

Betrayed and buried alive by her husband, a magistrate’s daughter survives and returns to the capital as Jiang Li, the dying chancellor’s daughter who saved her, to take revenge ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Double_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asia Contents Awards 2024 drama nomination` `C IMDb 8.2 (2.4K votes)` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #20 (8.8)` `D Yunhe 2024 new-series views #3 (2.67B)` `D Dengta 2024 web series #1 (3.37B)` `F PTT China-Drama 2024 favourites thread (8 users, several unhappy with the ending)` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #1 (23 June 2024, 9 weeks on chart)` `G LINE TODAY 2024 Chinese dramas #2`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: the 2024 state recommended-drama list
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (29,937 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (2.4K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Bhavna Agarwal at India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/ott/story/the-double-review-wu-jin-yan-wang-xiang-yuen-chinese-drama-2578035-2024-08-06) gave the full series 3.5/5, praising its balance of politics, revenge and romance but finding it too long. Near the end of its China run, [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/283619/) (in Chinese) read it as a revenge thriller and a coming-of-age story whose heroine is not driven by romance. Douban sits around 6.6 per Taiwanese media against 8.8 on MyDramaList, one of the widest gaps here.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; it opens with a woman buried alive and a killing, and revenge drives the rest.
- **Length**: 40 episodes plus one bonus episode, 2024, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Qianshan Chake’s web novel; no need to read it. The core is an identity-swap revenge plot, with romance on the side.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/262525.do?cs=2) (subscription, leaves 2 June 2029); [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-9cc94fc6-afa3-40e0-81d1-8a14c4723936) (subscription). [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81900131) also topped Taiwan’s weekly chart in 2024

#### Blossoms in Adversity

When disaster sends the Hua family’s men into exile, eldest daughter Hua Zhi stops hiding her talents, leads the women and children through hardship, and grows close to the feared commander Gu Yanxi ([Wikipedia](https://mydramalist.com/749431-the-story-of-hua-zhi)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asia Contents Awards 2024 best actress nomination (Zhang Jingyi)` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #41 (8.6)` `D Yunhe 2024 new-series views #9 (1.69B)` `D Dengta 2024 web series #3 (2.00B)` `F PTT China-Drama 2024 favourites thread (14 users)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.6 (14,320 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (640 votes)
- **Reviews**: In a short issue piece, [Liana Garcellano at The Diarist.ph](https://www.thediarist.ph/just-the-point-of-view-we-need-tiger-women-hold-up-half-the-sky-in-c-dramas/), a Philippine lifestyle site, reads Hua Zhi as a Mulan figure and praises a story of women’s self-reliance that never turns on men. No Taiwanese review could be found. Douban rose from 7.1 to 7.4 after the finale per Taiwanese media, with MyDramaList at 8.6: a slow-burn reputation.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, a family is ruined and exiled and struggles with hunger and cold.
- **Length**: 40 episodes, 2024, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Kong Liu’s web novel; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/266774.do?cs=2) (some episodes free, leaves 18 July 2029)

#### The Story of Ming Lan

In the Northern Song, Minglan, a concubine’s daughter who loses her mother young, hides her intelligence to survive the family’s infighting, then marries Gu Tingye and takes his household in hand ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E7%9F%A5%E5%90%A6%EF%BC%9F%E7%9F%A5%E5%90%A6%EF%BC%9F%E5%BA%94%E6%98%AF%E7%BB%BF%E8%82%A5%E7%BA%A2%E7%98%A6)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Magnolia Awards 2019 best Chinese TV series nomination` `C IMDb 8.3 (1.5K votes)` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #26 (8.7)` `D Yunhe 2024 older-series views #3` `F PTT China-Drama 2019 favourites thread (~16 users)` `F PTT China-Drama 2020 top-three thread (~18 users)`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: Golden Eagle Awards 2020 outstanding TV drama
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (11,301 ratings) | IMDb 8.3 (1.5K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. About 34 episodes in, [柯志遠 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/3601350) (in Chinese) strongly recommended it, finding its household intrigue more relatable than palace dramas, though he held back on the two leads. Douban stood at 7.5 at the finale per Jiemian and has since climbed to 7.8–7.9 per Taiwanese media, against 8.7 on MyDramaList; China’s Guangming Online (February 2019) faulted the adaptation for chasing easy thrills.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, a mother is murdered and the household infighting is relentless.
- **Length**: 78 episodes (73 in the international DVD cut), aired from late 2018 into 2019, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Guan Xin Ze Luan’s web novel; no need to read it. The same author wrote the source of Love Like the Galaxy, but the stories are unrelated.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/76386.do?cs=2) (subscription, leaves 24 December 2026); [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/9008/eps/1) (available). The Hami Video licence ends in December 2026

#### Where the Mask Ends

Afraid of being widowed by a gravely wounded fiancé, noblewoman Wei Rao sends a look-alike night-soil collector, A Chou, to marry him in her place; then he wakes up, and A Chou must survive the household ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Where_the_Mask_Ends)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #50 (8.6)` `D Netflix global top 10 non-English TV #7 (week of 12–18 October 2026, 1.6M views)` `G DailyView October 2026 Chinese drama buzz #1 (30% negative)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.6 (8,989 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (748 votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found, nor a Taiwanese one. Douban opened at 7.0 per Taiwanese media against 8.6 on MyDramaList; it leads Taiwan’s online buzz, but 30% of that is negative. It has been out less than a month, so the scores will move.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. The IMDb Parents Guide notes an attempted rape, a woman beating someone to death with her face covered in blood, and self-cutting; violence and frightening scenes are Moderate.
- **Length**: 30 episodes, premiered 16 October 2026. MyDramaList records the streaming run as complete on 3 November, so it counts as finished; the CCTV-8 broadcast schedule is unconfirmed.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Xiao Jiaren’s web novel, serialised on Jinjiang Literature City; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/83150089) (subscription). Netflix is the only Taiwan option; availability is based on Netflix Taiwan’s official trailer

#### Blossom

Wronged and killed in her past life, Dou Zhao wakes up years earlier with her memories and a prophetic book, and allies with Song Mo, a general who has also been reborn, against court plots ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Blossom_(Chinese_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `C Douban top-rated Chinese series of 2025 #10 (7.8)` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #24 (8.8)` `E Weibo 2025 breakthrough work of the year` `F PTT China-Drama 2024 favourites thread (3 users, mid-run)` `F Dcard Chinese drama board review, 4 stars`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: the 2024 state recommended-drama list
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (24,517 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (1.2K votes) | Douban 7.8 (Douban 2025 annual list page, as of November 2026)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. After the China run, [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8533990) (in Chinese) found the rebirth formula familiar and the heroine a little stony-faced, but welcomed an ending where the villains are punished. Douban 7.8, MyDramaList 8.8 and positive Taiwanese fans: a dark horse everyone agrees on.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, it opens with the heroine betrayed and killed in her past life.
- **Length**: 34 episodes, 2024, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Zhizhi’s web novel; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/280740.do?cs=2) (subscription, leaves 5 June 2030); [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/16880/eps/1) (available); [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/4714) (free tier + 影劇 plan). Netflix also carries it, per Harper’s Bazaar Taiwan

#### The Glory

Abandoned as a child, Zhuang Hanyan returns to the Zhuang family, catches the eye of judicial official Fu Yunxi, and together they expose the hidden side of her father, Zhuang Shiyang ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E9%9B%81%E5%9B%9E%E6%97%B6)).

- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, family revenge and mystery.
- **Length**: 30 episodes, 2025, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Qianshan Chake’s web novel; no need to read it. The core is family revenge and mystery, with romance on the side.

### Period romance

Feuding clans joined by marriage, a butcher and a marquis, a woman disguised as a general, and Cheng Shaoshang, left behind by her parents.

#### The Prisoner of Beauty

Amid wars between clans, Xiao Qiao is married off to Wei Shao, head of the Wei family whose men died when her family broke an alliance; the two move from mutual suspicion to partnership ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Prisoner_of_Beauty)).

- **Recommended by**: `C IMDb 8.2 (2.6K votes)` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #9 (8.9)` `D Tencent Video 2025 heat index #3` `E Weibo 2025 outstanding work of the year` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 recommendations thread (~19 users)` `F MyDramaList user list, best Chinese dramas of 2025 #2` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #1 (25 May 2025, 8 weeks on chart)` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Chinese dramas #2`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (31,696 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (2.6K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. Around the end of its China run, [廖芳潔 at Opinion@CommonWealth](https://opinion.cw.com.tw/blog/profile/517/article/16212) (in Chinese) defends the much-criticised heroine as a woman rationally fighting to survive, while admitting the plot circles and pads. It topped Netflix Taiwan and scores 8.9 on MyDramaList, yet Douban sits at 6.4–6.6 per Taiwanese media: one of the sharpest home-versus-abroad splits.

## Limits of this list

- **Almost no English reviews.** Only 4 of the 18 have a signed English piece, and the only major outlets are Collider and the South China Morning Post, both on Love Between Fairy and Devil; the rest is blogs and MyDramaList user reviews, which aren’t used.
- **Taiwanese reviews come from a few writers.** 7 shows have no signed Taiwanese review; of the 12 pieces found, 9 are by 潘光中, 雀雀 and 柯志遠, and several cover only the first episodes.
- **Douban scores mostly come from Taiwan media roundups.** The score line shows Douban only for the 5 shows with a readable source; the rest appear in the review entries and change over time.
- **Taiwan policy limits Chinese streaming.** Many popular costume romances are only on iQIYI’s international site and sit on the watch-later list. Taiwan evidence for Love Between Fairy and Devil stops in 2022, so it may have been removed; The Story of Ming Lan’s Hami Video licence ends on 2026-12-24. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-12.
