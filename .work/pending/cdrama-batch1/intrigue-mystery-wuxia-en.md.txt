> 🌏 [中文版](/posts/film/2026-11-12-cdrama-intrigue-mystery-wuxia)

This post covers Chinese intrigue, mystery, wuxia and fantasy: two demoted Tang-dynasty officials solving strange cases on the road west, a time-traveller surviving palace politics, young swordsmen on the road, and an 18-year-old dismemberment case in a northeastern industrial town. All 15 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, not counting state-run awards, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. The rules for Chinese dramas (state awards excluded; iQIYI’s international site and WeTV not counted as legal Taiwan channels) are explained at the start of the [costume romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-12-cdrama-costume-romance-xianxia-en); the Japanese equivalent is the [Japanese mystery and crime post](/posts/film/2026-11-10-jdrama-mystery-crime-action-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Long.** Period and wuxia shows mostly run 28 to 40 episodes, and Joy of Life has 82 over two seasons; the modern mysteries are far shorter, 10 to 21.
- **Douban and international scores often disagree.** Guardians of the Dafeng scores 5.6 on Douban but 8.6 on MyDramaList; The Long Season runs the other way, 9.4 on Douban and 8.0 on MyDramaList. Each entry gives both.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, state-run lists (where any), scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: Chinese dramas have no Metacritic scores, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_chinese_dramas), IMDb and Douban; for multi-season shows, MyDramaList is season 1. Douban scores appear only where a readable year-end list or press report gives them, with the date; scores seen only in headlines are left out.
- **Content**: China has no rating system; Taiwan platform ratings are given where shown. Content notes come from the IMDb Parents Guide, which has a handful of votes or none for most shows, so many notes follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages. Shows backed only by old evidence say they may have been removed.

### Period intrigue and mystery

Tang-dynasty mysteries, a time-traveller at court, a massacre survivor’s revenge and Mei Changsu’s fight to clear old names. The longest group, and the one most often measured against Douban.

#### Strange Tales of Tang Dynasty II: To the West

Su Wuming, a disciple of Di Renjie, and former imperial guard Lu Lingfeng are demoted and sent west, solving uncanny cases along the way ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/唐朝诡事录之西行)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards 2025 Grand Best Drama Series` `A Magnolia Awards 2026 nomination (part three, Chang’an)` `C Douban top-rated Chinese series of 2024 #5 (8.6)` `D Yunhe 2024 new-series views #6 (2.02B)` `D Dengta 2024 web series #2, top mystery series` `E Weibo 2026 outstanding work of the year (Chang’an)` `F PTT China-Drama 2024 favourites thread (~24 upvotes)` `G LINE TODAY 2024 Chinese dramas #5`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: Golden Eagle Awards 2026 nomination (Chang’an), the 2024 state recommended-drama list, Feitian Awards 2026 shortlist
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (To the West) (1,331 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (167 votes) | Douban 8.6 (To the West, end of 2024, Yunhe Data annual report)
- **Reviews**: The only English review found is from the blog [Chasing Dramas, by Karen and Cathy](https://www.chasingdramas.com/reviews/currently-watching-strange-tales-of-tang-dynasty-ii-to-the-west-2024-%E5%94%90%E6%9C%9D%E8%AF%A1%E4%BA%8B%E5%BD%95%E8%A5%BF%E8%A1%8C/), who finished it and liked it more than part one: "Though some cases were stronger than others, this drama is highly re-watchable." Writing during the run, [潘光中 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8196683) (in Chinese) credited its puzzling logic, uncanny cases and visual spectacle, with no hit IP or idol stars behind it. Douban, viewing charts, awards and Taiwanese fans agree; mainstream English media never reviewed it.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; the cases are murders and uncanny crimes with horror touches.
- **Length**: To the West has 40 episodes (2024), finished. Part one has 36 (2022) and part three, Chang’an, has 40 (2025). Chinese Wikipedia lists a follow-up, and MDL has entries for further parts, none with an air date.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Wei Fenghua’s novel series; no need to read it. The leads’ relationship carries over from part one, so start there, though part one has left Hami Video.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/285371.do?cs=2) (some episodes free, leaves 31 January 2028). Part one has lapsed on Hami Video; LINE TV’s 唐詭奇案 is a different show

#### Joy of Life

Fan Xian, born into the fictional ancient state of Qing with memories of the 21st century, heads to the capital and is drawn into succession intrigue while digging into his origins ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Joy_of_Life_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Magnolia Awards 2020 nomination` `A Magnolia Awards 2025 nomination (season 2)` `B Beijing News drama of the year 2024 (season 2)` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #7 (8.9)` `C IMDb 8.2 (2.7K votes, both seasons combined)` `D Yunhe 2024 new-series views #1 (season 2, 4.51B)` `D Dengta 2024 TV series #1` `F PTT China-Drama 2019 favourites thread (~35 upvotes)` `G LINE TODAY 2024 Chinese dramas #1 (season 2)`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: Golden Eagle Awards 2026 nomination, the 2024 state recommended-drama list and a Feitian Awards 2026 shortlist place (all season 2)
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (season 1) (14,583 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (2.7K votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing all of season 1, [Deborah Valentine at easternkicks](https://www.easternkicks.com/reviews/joy-of-life/), a UK site on Asian film, recommended it: "Does it sometimes verge on ridiculous? Yes! Brilliantly so! … But for all the humour and complications it never falls into the abyss of spoof-dom." After season 1, critic [麥若愚 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/4631646) (in Chinese) set it against Nirvana in Fire, calling one solemn and tragic and the other witty. Season 2 was China’s most-streamed series of 2024, yet Hong Kong’s Ta Kung Pao and English bloggers judged it weaker than season 1.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a court-intrigue drama with assassination attempts and fights.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 82 episodes: season 1 has 46 (2019–2020), season 2 has 36 (2024); both finished. Season 3 has no confirmed date.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Mao Ni’s web novel; no need to read it. Season 2 picks up straight from the season 1 finale, so watch season 1 first.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-ddb257a8-7b22-4b72-b87c-a446ed15586f) (subscription, seasons 1–2); [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/210932.do?cs=2) (season 1, some episodes free, leaves 20 February 2027)

#### Legend of Zang Hai

The sole survivor of a massacred family of court astronomers returns to court ten years later as the strategist Zang Hai, using geomancy and mechanisms to plot his revenge ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Legend_of_Zang_Hai)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Magnolia Awards 2026 nomination` `C IMDb 8.2 (24K votes)` `D Yunhe 2025 new-series views #1 (2.23B)` `D Dengta 2025 views #1 (2.875B)` `E Weibo 2025 influential work of the year` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 picks thread (~19 upvotes)` `G DailyView 2025 Chinese drama buzz #5` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Chinese dramas top 10`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: Golden Eagle Awards 2026 nomination, Feitian Awards 2026 shortlist
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (8,780 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (24K votes) | Douban 7.1 (end of 2025, Dengta year-end chart via news)
- **Reviews**: The only English review found is from [Alessandra at Red Taotie](https://www.redtaotie.com/blog/legend-of-zang-hai-2025/), the English edition of an Italian C-drama blog; after all 40 episodes she gave it 8/10, praising characters and visuals but noting the twists lose tension because the schemes are usually shown in advance. No signed Taiwanese review could be found. It was 2025’s most-watched series with IMDb 8.2, but Douban sits at 7.1, and a PTT fan found the motives unconvincing despite the polish.
- **Content**: Disney+ Taiwan: 12+. About 13 IMDb Parents Guide votes: violence and frightening scenes are mild, sex and nudity none; it opens with a family massacre.
- **Length**: 40 episodes (2025), finished. MDL lists a season 2 entry with no air date.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Nanpai Sanshu’s novel; no need to read it or his Daomu Biji series first.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/321612.do?cs=2) (subscription, leaves 2 April 2029); [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-93d59aa6-12c4-4d6e-8f5e-7fadbe662862) (subscription)

#### Nirvana in Fire

Lin Shu, survivor of a framed army, returns to the capital twelve years later as Mei Changsu to help the overlooked Prince Jing toward the throne and clear the old case ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nirvana_in_Fire)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Magnolia Awards 2016 nomination` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #3 (9.0)` `C IMDb 8.8 (3.7K votes)` `D Yunhe 2024 older-series views #10`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (14,182 ratings) | IMDb 8.8 (3.7K votes)
- **Reviews**: Having watched all 54 episodes, [George Koo at Asia Times](https://asiatimes.com/2016/06/review-nirvana-in-fire-a-historical-drama-from-china/) wrote a nostalgic, essay-style piece: "The script paid careful attention to details. Over the 54 episodes, inconsistencies were rare." In an op-ed at [The Storm Media](https://www.storm.mg/article/11147824) (in Chinese), freelance writer 聽風的先生 (a pen name) said its decade-long following in Taiwan comes from treating restraint as an aesthetic. MDL 9.0 and IMDb 8.8 agree; the only English dissent, at The Cultural Gutter, notes it still pins its hopes on a good ruler rather than reform.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. IMDb Parents Guide rates violence mild (3 votes): battles, swords, arrows and some blood spatter; other categories none.
- **Length**: 54 episodes (2015), finished. Nirvana in Fire II (2017) is a separate story in the same world.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Hai Yan’s novel; no need to read it. It stands alone; the 2017 follow-up can wait.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/303491.do?cs=2) (some episodes free, leaves 4 September 2028). It has lapsed on LINE TV and friDay

#### Guardians of the Dafeng

A young man playing a murder-mystery game wakes up in a Dafeng dynasty jail as Xu Qi’an, reasons his way out, and joins the night-watch investigators ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardians_of_the_Dafeng)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #42 (8.6)` `C IMDb 7.8 (1.2K votes)` `D Yunhe 2025 new-series views #2 (2.21B)` `D Maoyan 2025 top comedy by views` `E Weibo 2025 influential work of the year`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.6 (11,694 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (1.2K votes) | Douban 5.6 (end of 2025, Dengta year-end chart via news)
- **Reviews**: [Mary at Read Between the Subtitles](https://www.readbetweenthesubtitles.com/post/guardians-of-the-dafeng-dropped-drama-diaries), a blog, dropped it after episode 10: "Guardians of Dafeng is the glossy, less sophisticated answer to Joy of Life that no one asked for, but it was still fun." No signed Taiwanese review could be found. Douban 5.6 against MDL 8.6 is a three-point gap, the widest of these 15 shows.
- **Content**: Disney+ Taiwan: 12+. Only one or two IMDb Parents Guide votes: violence is marked severe; brothel scenes show only singing and dancing, and there are two or three kisses.
- **Length**: 40 episodes (2024–2025), finished. A spin-off film followed. MDL lists a season 2 entry with no date.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Mai Bao Xiao Lang Jun’s web novel; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/282506.do?cs=2) (subscription, leaves 29 December 2030); [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-4719e5ff-e420-4173-aaa9-e5da0db283ca) (subscription)

#### The Vendetta of An

After ten years in hiding, Xie Huai’an, whose family was massacred, gathers a painter, a general and a swordsman to outwit General Yan Fengshan and foil a plot to split the empire ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/長安二十四計)).

- **Recommended by**: `E Maoyan 2025 TV buzz chart #5` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 year-end review reply: “strongest of 2025”` `G #5 on Netflix Taiwan (3 weeks)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (2,994 ratings) | IMDb 5.8 (10K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Iya Moyya at Cnovelholic](https://cnovelholic.com/drama-review/mystery/the-vendetta-of-an-drama-review/), a blog, gave it 8.8/10 after the full run and named it one of her favourite C-dramas of 2025. In Taiwan, [Hayley Yu at Harper’s BAZAAR](https://www.harpersbazaar.com/tw/culture/filmandmusic/a69803343/c-drmam-the-vendetta-of-an/) (in Chinese) wrote in its first week, in a Netflix-linked introduction-cum-review, that online buzz was huge but split. MDL 8.5 against IMDb 5.8 (10K votes) is a 2.7-point gap.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a revenge story built on a family massacre.
- **Length**: 28 episodes (2025), finished. No word of a season 2.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; neither Chinese Wikipedia nor MDL lists a source work.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82648017) (subscription)

### Wuxia and fantasy

Cultivation, superpowers, young swordsmen and an assassins’ guild. Mostly adapted from web novels or comics, with plenty of fights.

#### The Immortal Ascension

Han Li, a poor village boy with mediocre talent, finds a mysterious little green bottle and survives the cultivation world through caution and calculation ([Wikipedia](https://mydramalist.com/35477-a-record-of-a-mortal-s-journey-to-immortality)).

- **Recommended by**: `D Maoyan 2025 web series views #3` `D Youku 2025 buzz #4` `E Weibo 2025 audience favourite` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 favourites thread (~12 upvotes)` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 picks thread (12 upvotes)` `G #3 on Netflix Taiwan (5 weeks)` `G Hami Video 2025 drama chart #10`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (4,526 ratings) | IMDb 7.5 (658 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Iya Moyya at Cnovelholic](https://cnovelholic.com/drama-review/wuxia/the-immortal-ascension-drama-review/), a blog, gave it 8.5/10 after the full run, saying even a non-wuxia fan could enjoy the intricate plot. No signed Taiwanese review could be found. It did well on TV ratings, Netflix Taiwan and PTT, while critics barely noticed it. The anime of the same name is a separate work; do not mix up their scores.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a xianxia story with magical combat and battles against demonic sects.
- **Length**: 30 episodes (2025), finished. MDL lists a season 2 entry with no date.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Wang Yu’s web novel; no need to read it or watch the anime first.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/300397.do?cs=2) (some episodes free, leaves 27 July 2030). Netflix Taiwan also carries it; the versions on friDay and myVideo are the anime

#### I Am Nobody

After his grandfather’s body vanishes, student Zhang Chulan is pulled into the hidden world of “Outsiders”, meets the mysterious Feng Baobao, and uncovers his grandfather’s past ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/異人之下)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asia Contents Awards 2025 best visual effects (season 2)` `B Southern Metropolis Daily top 10 dramas of 2023 #8` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #46 (8.6, season 1)` `C IMDb 7.6 (764 votes, both seasons combined)` `F PTT China-Drama 2023 favourites thread (~11 upvotes)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.6 (season 1) (8,110 ratings) | IMDb 7.6 (764 votes) | Douban 8.4 (season 2, August 2026, The Storm Media ranking)
- **Reviews**: The only English review found is from the blog K & C Drama Musings, signed only with the site name; it recommended season 1 and said it ended on a high, mixing folklore with superpowers. No signed Taiwanese review could be found. Season 2 fared better on Douban, yet English reviews cover only season 1.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. IMDb Parents Guide has 1–5 votes per category, all mild; in one scene a woman undresses in front of the hero, with no nudity shown.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 40 episodes: season 1 has 27 (2023), season 2, The Showdown between Yin & Yang, has 13 (2025). Season 1 was pulled the day after its premiere and resumed a month later. No word of a season 3.

## Limits of this list

- **Few English reviews.** None of the US or UK outlets this series usually draws on reviewed these 15; only The Long Season, Nirvana in Fire and Mobius have signed pieces in Foreign Policy, Asia Times and Gulf News. The rest rely mostly on blogs, and Whispers of Fate has none.
- **Few Taiwanese reviews too.** 8 shows have no signed Taiwanese review; The Vendetta of An, Blood River and Mobius have only Harper’s BAZAAR pieces that double as Netflix promotion.
- **Douban scores come from mixed sources.** Some come from Douban’s year-end lists or data reports, some from Taiwanese media rankings, and most are snapshots in time; The Vendetta of An’s Douban score is left out.
- **Taiwan policy limits Chinese streaming.** Many popular Chinese dramas are only on iQIYI’s international site or WeTV and sit on the watch-later list. Taiwan evidence for The Long Season and I Am Nobody is old, so they may have been removed. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-12.
