The last Chinese post covers modern and era dramas: a reunited crush, a female pilot in training, a hotel manager who quits for Dali, a grocery shop on the Altay grasslands and a business war on 1990s Shanghai’s Huanghe Road. All 21 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, not counting state-run awards, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. The rules for Chinese dramas (state awards excluded; iQIYI’s international site and WeTV not counted as legal Taiwan channels) are explained at the start of the [costume romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-12-cdrama-costume-romance-xianxia-en); the Korean equivalent is the [Korean life and family post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-life-family-period-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Urban romance rides on viewers; era dramas on juries.** All 7 era dramas have a non-state award behind them; of the 9 urban romances, only The Tale of Rose does.
- **Douban and international fans pull opposite ways.** Urban romances usually score 1 to 3 points higher on MyDramaList than on Douban; Blossoms Shanghai and Romance in the Alley run the other way.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, state-run lists (where any), scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_chinese_dramas), IMDb and Douban; only Blossoms Shanghai has a Metacritic score. The score line gives Douban only where the source is readable, with the date; Douban figures from lifestyle sites appear in the review entry and are labelled.
- **Content**: China has no rating system, and none of the 21 Taiwan platform pages shows a rating. Content notes come from the IMDb Parents Guide; most shows have no votes, so the notes follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages; Netflix listings backed only by Taiwan news reports appear as notes.

### Urban romance

A crush reunited, a secret love for a brother’s friend, a meeting at the snooker table, a female pilot and a hidden office romance. Mostly on Netflix, and the most popular with international fans.

#### The First Frost

Years after turning him down in high school, TV reporter Wen Yifan becomes Sang Yan’s housemate by pure chance, and old feelings resurface ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_First_Frost)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #14 (8.8)` `C IMDb 8.4 (5.1K votes)` `D Yunhe 2025 new long-form series views top 20` `E Weibo 2025 audience favourite of the year` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 year-in-review (“very polished”)` `F Dcard first-half 2025 Chinese drama poll #2 (143 votes)` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Chinese dramas #1` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #1 (2 March 2025, 7 weeks on chart)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (47,582 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (5.1K votes) | Douban 5.4 (reported by Sohu, February 2025; not stated whether this is the opening score)
- **Reviews**: [Lakshana N Palat at Gulf News](https://gulfnews.com/entertainment/the-first-frost-c-drama-rewind-a-breathtaking-tale-of-love-and-trauma-that-rewrites-the-rules-of-romance-1.500164071) reviewed all 32 episodes as a story of trauma and healing and recommends it strongly. After the finale, [雀雀 at 500times](https://500times.udn.com/wtimes/story/120840/8608497) (in Chinese) said it moves romance dramas into an age of detail, though stretching it to 32 episodes left the story struggling. MyDramaList 8.8 against a Douban 5.4 reported by Sohu: the widest gap in this group.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. IMDb Parents Guide: frightening scenes Moderate (all 5 votes), sex None; the heroine’s past trauma and harassment are part of the plot.
- **Length**: 32 episodes, 2025, finished. Chinese Wikipedia lists Zhe Yue Liang (2026) as a follow-up work.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Zhu Yi’s web novel; no need to read it. Same author and series as Hidden Love, but with different leads, so it stands alone.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.cosmopolitan.com/tw/entertainment/movies/g61941830/the-first-frost/) (subscription). Availability is based on a 2025 Cosmopolitan Taiwan report

#### Love’s Ambition

Star presenter Xu Yan marries Shen Haoming on a carefully dressed-up family background; after the marriage collapses she rediscovers herself, while he realises he wants her back ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%AE%B8%E6%88%91%E8%80%80%E7%9C%BC)).

- **Recommended by**: `B New Weekly 2025 hit drama of the year` `D Yunhe 2025 average views per episode during run #3` `D Dengta 2025 domestic series views #5 (1.96B)` `F PTT China-Drama 2025 recommendations thread (3 upvotes)` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #2 (26 October 2025, 7 weeks on chart)` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Chinese dramas #3`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (14,612 ratings) | IMDb 7.4 (931 votes) | Douban 6.5 (cited in coverage of Dengta’s 2025 chart, December 2025)
- **Reviews**: [Bhavna Agarwal at India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/ott/story/loves-ambition-chinese-drama-zhao-lusi-romance-ambition-2800188-2025-10-09) recommends it with reservations, praising Zhao Lusi’s layered performance and finding William Chan’s male lead weak. No signed Taiwanese review could be found; on udn’s creator platform 女子漾, influencer [柒默lisa](https://woman.udn.com/woman/story/123164/9330973) (in Chinese) recommends it while noting a rushed pace and a thin business plot later on. MyDramaList 8.3 against Douban 6.5: it did far better on Taiwan streaming than with Chinese viewers’ ratings.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. Only 1–2 IMDb Parents Guide votes per category, with only alcohol rated Mild; going by the plot, mild, centred on deception within a marriage.
- **Length**: 32 episodes, 2025, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Zhang Yueran’s novel; no need to read it. The same novel was also made into a 2024 film.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/17522/eps/1) (available); [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82050339) (subscription). Netflix availability is based on Netflix Taiwan’s official YouTube channel

#### Amidst a Snowstorm of Love

Lin Yiyang, a former snooker prodigy who quit after a ban, meets nine-ball player Yin Guo on a blizzard night, and she draws him back into competition ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Amidst_a_Snowstorm_of_Love)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Beijing News drama of the year 2024` `C IMDb 7.9 (2K votes)` `D Yunhe 2024 new-series views #19 (1.19B)` `F PTT China-Drama 2024 favourites thread (5 upvotes)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (24,008 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (2K votes)
- **Reviews**: Blogger [kfangurl at The Fangirl Verdict](https://thefangirlverdict.com/2024/06/30/review-amidst-a-snowstorm-of-love-china/) reviewed all 30 episodes and recommends it to viewers who enjoy a slow, everyday romance. No signed Taiwanese review could be found. MyDramaList 8.5 against a Douban 6.8 compiled by a lifestyle site; opinion split sharply during its run.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, sport and romance, mild.
- **Length**: 30 episodes, 2024, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Mo Bao Fei Bao’s novel; the author also co-wrote the script. No need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.beauty321.com/post/60022) (reported in 2024). The Taiwan evidence is old, so it may no longer be available; GTV Drama aired it on TV in 2024

#### The Tale of Rose

Raised in a scholarly family, Huang Yimei goes from office newcomer to running her own exhibitions, living through a lost first love, divorce and a soulmate’s death, and still chooses to live fully ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tale_of_Rose)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Magnolia Awards 2025 nomination` `D Yunhe 2024 new-series views #5 (2.29B)` `D Dengta 2024 TV series #3 (2.08B), top urban drama` `F PTT China-Drama 2024 favourites thread (1 user: “can’t believe nobody picked it”)` `G Named in LINE TODAY’s 2024 Chinese dramas`
- **State-run lists (not counted)**: Golden Eagle Awards 2026 nomination, the 2024 state recommended-drama list, Feitian Awards 2026 shortlist
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.2 (2,215 ratings) | IMDb 6.8 (568 votes) | Douban 7.1 (opening score from 87,000+ ratings, reported by Hualong, June 2024)
- **Reviews**: [Elle Morie at The Odd Apple](https://theoddapple.com/the-tale-of-rose/), a small site, recommends it strongly, calling it one of Liu Yifei’s best performances in years and noting it suits mature viewers. [廖芳潔 at Opinion@CommonWealth](https://opinion.cw.com.tw/blog/profile/517/article/15044) (in Chinese) argues it was never about romance but about the choices a life brings. Douban opened at 7.1 against MyDramaList 8.2; a lifestyle site later put Douban at 7.4.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, several relationships, divorce and terminal illness.
- **Length**: 38 episodes, 2024, finished. The director has said the ending is open but there will be no sequel.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Yi Shu’s novel; no need to read it. The 1986 film of the same name is not a prequel.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/271369.do?cs=2) (some episodes free, leaves 7 June 2029)

#### The Early Spring

Newcomer Shang Zhitao joins a Beijing ad agency and grows up under acid-tongued creative director Luan Nian; their six-year secret affair ends, and they meet again years later ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Early_Spring)).

- **Recommended by**: `C IMDb 8.2 (2.2K votes)` `D Netflix global top 10 non-English TV #2 (two weeks running, up to 3.2M views)` `E Weibo 2026 influential work of the year` `G DailyView October 2026 Chinese drama buzz #7`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (18,399 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (2.2K votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing all 24 episodes, [Bhavna Agarwal at India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/ott/story/the-early-spring-review-jing-boran-sun-qian-mature-workplace-romance-netflix-2991074-2026-09-09) recommends it: “The romance is built on chemistry, but The Early Spring does not confuse chemistry with compatibility.” She found the post-breakup stretch too compressed. No signed Taiwanese review could be found. MyDramaList 8.5 against a Douban opening score of 6.7 reported by World Journal, which called it a gap between hype and reputation.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. IMDb Parents Guide: sex Mild (3 of 5 votes), with implied sex scenes.
- **Length**: 24 episodes, 2026, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Gu Niang Bie Ku’s web novel; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/83067197) (global exclusive)

#### Hidden Love

Sang Zhi has had a crush on her brother’s friend Duan Jiaxu since middle school; years later she goes to university in his city, and the two slowly fall in love ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hidden_Love_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `B Southern Metropolis Entertainment Yearbook 2023 top 10 dramas #10` `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #4 (9.0)` `C IMDb 8.6 (9.9K votes)` `F PTT China-Drama 2023 favourites thread (~32 upvotes)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (97,413 ratings) | IMDb 8.6 (9.9K votes) | Douban 6.7 (opening score, Weibo post reposted by Sina, July 2023)
- **Reviews**: A week after it ended, [Bhavna Agarwal at India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/ott/story/why-chinese-drama-hidden-love-should-be-on-your-binge-watch-list-this-season-2405778-2023-07-13) recommended it for handling the five-year age gap gently. No signed Taiwanese review could be found; [an unsigned Vocus piece reposted by Storm Media](https://www.storm.mg/lifestyle/4829065) (in Chinese), covering only the first half, says the show greatly improves on the novel’s greasy dialogue. MyDramaList 9.0 against a Douban opening score of 6.7, with most of the dispute over the age gap.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. IMDb Parents Guide: sex and violence Mild (12 of 26 and 13 of 16 votes), with only simple kisses; the heroine’s crush on a university student begins in middle school, so the age gap is built into the story.
- **Length**: 25 episodes, 2023, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Zhu Yi’s web novel; no need to read it. Same author as The First Frost and When I Fly Towards You, with different leads.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81705641) (subscription). Availability is based on a July 2026 Netflix list by Storm Media

#### When I Fly Towards You

One rainy day, outspoken 16-year-old Su Zaizai falls for her schoolmate Zhang Lurang at the tuck shop, and her open affection slowly wins him over from high school to university ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_I_Fly_Towards_You)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Chinese drama #2 (9.0)` `C IMDb 8.5 (4.4K votes)` `F PTT China-Drama 2023 favourites thread (~34 upvotes)` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #8 (27 August 2023, 1 week on chart)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (62,728 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (4.4K votes) | Douban 7.9 (compiled by Cosmopolitan Taiwan, February 2025)
- **Reviews**: [Claire Hartwig](https://www.thelittlehawk.com/62229/culture/when-i-fly-towards-you-review/), a student writer at The Little Hawk, an Iowa City high school paper, gave it 10/10: “a must watch for all hopeless romantics.” No signed Taiwanese review could be found. MyDramaList 9.0 and a Douban 7.9 compiled by a lifestyle site; critics are largely absent and its reputation rests on fans.
- **Content**: No rating system in China; no Taiwan platform rating found. IMDb Parents Guide: sex Mild (8 of 11 votes), with kisses and implied intimacy after university; a later storyline involves a boss’s inappropriate attention at work.
- **Length**: 24 episodes, 2023, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Zhu Yi’s web novel; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81712983) (subscription). Availability is based on 2026 reports by Storm Media and Beauty321

#### Flight to You

Aspiring captain Cheng Xiao trains under the exacting pilot Gu Nanting at a fictional airline, proving herself in a male-dominated field as the two fall for each other ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%90%91%E9%A3%8E%E8%80%8C%E8%A1%8C)).

- **Recommended by**: `D Yunhe 2023 top 10 (no rank given)` `F PTT China-Drama 2023 favourites thread (7 upvotes)` `G myVideo 2023 drama chart #2` `G iQIYI Taiwan site 2023 #9 (popularity signal only)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.2 (4,257 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (196 votes) | Douban 6.9 (opening score from 21,000 ratings, Weibo post reposted by Sina, January 2023)

## Limits of this list

- **English reviews cluster on Blossoms Shanghai.** Only Blossoms Shanghai has reviews from major outlets such as RogerEbert, IndieWire and Collider; the English reviews of 4 Netflix urban romances are all by one India Today writer, and the rest are mostly blogs. Me and My Family, Northward and Uncle have no signed English review.
- **9 shows have no Taiwanese review.** Most urban romances have only platform promotion pieces in Taiwan, which aren’t counted as reviews.
- **Douban scores come from mixed sources.** Some come from Dengta or Yunhe year-end reports, others from news or lifestyle sites, mostly as snapshots in time.
- **Rating counts vary widely.** A Lifelong Journey and Uncle have under 60 MyDramaList ratings each; This Thriving Land scores only 3.3 on IMDb, far below its Douban 7.2 and MyDramaList 8.2.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** To the Wonder’s Hami Video licence ends on 2026-12-01; Taiwan evidence for Amidst a Snowstorm of Love and A Lifelong Journey is old; Blossoms Shanghai is only on myVideo, whose page can’t be checked from outside Taiwan. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-12.
