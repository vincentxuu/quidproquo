This post covers Japanese mystery, police, thriller and action: an espionage mission in Central Asia, the autopsy tables of a forensic lab, a blind FBI agent and death games played for your life. All 18 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. Episode counts and the share of original scripts in Japanese drama are covered at the start of the [romance and comedy post](/posts/film/2026-11-10-jdrama-romance-comedy-bl-en); the Korean equivalent is the [Korean crime and mystery post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-crime-mystery-revenge-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Two levels of content.** The 13 broadcast shows centre on investigation and deduction; the 5 Netflix and Disney+ originals include cannibalism, beheadings and death games, noted for each show.
- **Lengths vary widely.** From Yu Yu Hakusho’s 5 episodes to Emergency Interrogation Room’s 46 over five seasons; the bar chart below helps you pick by length.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: most Japanese dramas have no Metacritic score, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_japanese_dramas), IMDb, Japan’s Filmarks and Douban; for multi-season shows, MyDramaList is season 1 and IMDb the whole show. Streaming originals with a Metacritic or Rotten Tomatoes score have it in the review entry.
- **Content**: Japanese TV has no single rating system; Taiwan platform ratings are given where shown. Content notes come from the IMDb Parents Guide, which has votes for only 6 shows; the rest follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages; where only some seasons are available, the entry says so.

### Police, forensics and lawyers

A mobile investigation unit, a forensic lab, defence lawyers, an interrogation room and police in an immigrant neighbourhood. Many run a case per episode, so you can dip in.

#### MIU404

Two detectives with opposite temperaments team up in the Tokyo police’s 4th Mobile Investigative Unit, facing culprits and victims shaped by social problems ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/MIU404)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama (summer 2020)` `A Tokyo Drama Awards 2021 award of excellence` `C MyDramaList top Japanese drama #22 (8.5)` `D Finale Kanto rating 24.8% (#21 of the Reiwa era)` `F PTT Japandrama 2023 year-end review (third rewatch)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (3,589 ratings) | IMDb 7.3 (1.2K votes) | Filmarks ★4.3
- **Reviews**: Writing at episode 9, [Orangevine at MyDramaList News](https://news.mydramalist.com/article/currently-watching-buddy-cop-j-drama-miu404-011015463), a fan-oriented news site, recommended it: "MIU404 is constructed as masterfully as Unnatural but hides that behind tons of fun stuff." After the finale, [重點就在括號裡 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/132620/) (in Chinese) praised its honest look at Japan’s social problems, but said the pandemic cut from 14 to 11 episodes left the ending rushed. Reviews broadly agree; the split is a flat start versus a strong finish.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; each case turns on a social problem.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, finished. It shares a universe with Unnatural; the 2024 film Last Mile extends both.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original by writer Nogi Akiko. Unnatural is not required, though fans of both will spot the crossovers.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/23124/eps/1) (available)

#### Last Man

A blind FBI investigator famed for cracking the toughest cases comes to Japan on an exchange and pairs up with a detective obsessed with catching criminals ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/ラストマン-全盲の捜査官-)).

- **Recommended by**: `C Filmarks 2023 Japanese drama #14` `D Finale Kanto rating 24.2% (#2 commercial-TV episode of 2023)` `F PTT Japandrama 2023 year-end review #3` `G KKTV 2023 most watched #3`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.8 (220 ratings) | IMDb 7.5 (128 votes) | Filmarks ★3.9
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/7266903) (in Chinese) loved the double act between Fukuyama Masaharu and Oizumi Yo, and said the last two episodes tied up a 41-year-old case well. High ratings, but no awards and no English reviews.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes.
- **Length**: 10 episodes (2023), plus the 2025 special FAKE/TRUTH. The film First Love opened in Japan in December 2025 and in Taiwan in August 2026.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. The special and the film follow the series, so watch in order.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/314916.do?cs=2) (subscription, some episodes free, leaves 9 January 2028). The series has lapsed on LINE TV; the 2025 special FAKE/TRUTH is on [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21250/eps/1)

#### Tokyo Salad Bowl

A green-haired police officer and a former police interpreter team up on cases involving foreign residents in an increasingly diverse Tokyo ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/東京サラダボウル)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Tokyo Drama Awards 2025 award of excellence` `B Real Sound best of 2025 (one critic’s #2)` `C Filmarks Awards 2025 TV drama nominee` `F PTT Japandrama 2025 picks thread` `F PTT Japandrama 2025 year-end hidden gem`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.9 (584 ratings) | IMDb 7.3 (234 votes) | Filmarks ★4.0
- **Reviews**: [Olivier Fabre at Tokyo Prism 3CK](https://tokyoprism3ck.com/2025/08/tokyo-salad-bowl-food-for-thought-queerbait-crumbs-or-just-patronizing-2/), a Tokyo-based blog-style site, says "it plays like something served with plastic gloves – sanitized, cautious, and slightly over-rehearsed". Reviewing episode 1 only, [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8478556) (in Chinese) enjoyed the leads but felt uneasy about stereotypes from a Japanese point of view. Japanese juries rewarded it; both reviewers question its viewpoint.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes.
- **Length**: 9 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Kuromaru’s manga; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/3926) (available)

#### Unnatural

Forensic doctor Misumi Mikoto works at a new Unnatural Death Investigation Laboratory, solving cases of deaths from unnatural causes with her colleagues ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Unnatural_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama (winter 2018)` `A Galaxy Award of excellence (2017)` `C MyDramaList top Japanese drama #8 (8.5)` `C IMDb 8.1` `F PTT Japandrama gateway-drama thread` `F PTT Japandrama 2024 picks: endless rewatches`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (5,969 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (3K votes) | Filmarks ★4.4 | Douban 9.3 (2018)
- **Reviews**: [Adriana Rosati at Asian Movie Pulse](https://asianmoviepulse.com/2025/07/series-review-unnatural-2018-by-ayuko-tsukahara/) calls it Japan’s CSI and writes that "although it doesn't shine for originality, a few clever choices earn it some extra credit and elevate it above many of its peers". [卡爬 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/4432994) (in Chinese) writes only about the episode 1 epidemic case, reading it against online witch-hunts during Covid. Critics, Douban and overseas fans agree; it has the highest Filmarks score in this group.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. Only one or two IMDb votes per category; one case involves an attempted rape that is only talked about, and there is a school bullying beating.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original by writer Nogi Akiko. MIU404, set in the same universe, makes a good follow-up.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21215/eps/1) (VIP subscription); [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81714729) (subscription)

#### 99.9 Criminal Lawyer

With a 99.9% conviction rate in Japanese criminal trials, a defence lawyer hunts for the truth in the remaining 0.1%, one case per episode ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/99.9-刑事専門弁護士-)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama (spring 2016)` `C MyDramaList top Japanese drama #46 (8.3, season 2)` `D #1 average rating of 2018 (season 2, 17.4%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (season 1) (2,818 ratings) | IMDb 7.4 (670 votes) | Filmarks ★4.1 | Douban 8.4 (season 2, 2018)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found, and no Taiwanese review either. Season 2 was Japan’s top-rated drama of 2018, yet critics barely wrote about it.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a courtroom drama from TBS’s Sunday prime-time slot.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 19 episodes (2016 and 2018), plus a 2021 special and a film.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Episodes stand alone, so season 2 works by itself; the special and film follow season 2.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82072564) (subscription). The listing does not say which seasons are up

#### Don’t Call It Mystery

A university student taken in as a murder suspect talks nonstop in the interrogation room, picking apart the detectives’ blind spots ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Don%27t_Call_It_Mystery)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards best drama series, Japan 2022` `A Tokyo Drama Awards 2022 award of excellence` `C MyDramaList top Japanese drama #19 (8.5)` `D #12 Kanto rating of the Reiwa era (25.8%)` `D TVer Awards 2022 special prize`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (3,628 ratings) | IMDb 7.6 (679 votes) | Filmarks ★4.1
- **Reviews**: The only English review found is from [Karen Mansoldo at The Blossom Review](https://theblossomreview.com/dont-call-it-mystery-viki-j-drama-review/), a small review site; it is positive, though only its first third could be read. [雪奈 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/216506/) (in Chinese) calls it a healing drama first and a mystery second, with no big twists. Ratings, streaming and awards are all strong; viewers after hard puzzles may be disappointed.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; it touches on child abuse and bullying.
- **Length**: 12 episodes (2022), plus a 2023 special and a film. No word of a season 2.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Tamura Yumi’s manga; no need to read it. The film follows the series, so watch the series first.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/2275) (free tier or drama plan)

#### Go Home

Two officers in a Tokyo police unit for unidentified remains use the faintest clues to name the dead and send them home to their families ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/GO_HOME〜警視庁身元不明人相談室〜)).

- **Recommended by**: `D #17 Kanto rating of 2024 (14.4%)` `F PTT Japandrama 2024 year-end hidden gem` `G KKTV 2024 most watched #10`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.8 (108 ratings) | IMDb 6.5 (23 votes) | Filmarks ★3.4
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8260788) (in Chinese) found nearly every episode moving but light on mystery, with the leads’ own storyline wrapped up in a hurry. A small show: 23 IMDb votes, and the lowest Filmarks score in this group.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; every episode centres on an unidentified body.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/268346.do?cs=2) (subscription, some episodes free); [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/22252/eps/1) (VIP subscription)

#### Emergency Interrogation Room

A detective demoted after taking the blame for a failure joins a squad of veteran detectives who break suspects through psychological duels in the interrogation room ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/緊急取調室)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Tokyo Drama Awards 2022 award of excellence (season 4)` `D #7 Kanto rating of 2025 (16.5%, season 5)` `E ORICON 2025 drama satisfaction #8 (season 5)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.6 (season 1) (439 ratings) | IMDb 7.4 (135 votes) | Filmarks ★4.0
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. In Taiwan only an introductory piece could be found: a series overview by [雪奈 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/269869/) (in Chinese). A long-running ratings hit; critics honoured only season 4, and no proper single-season review exists in either language.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; it is mostly talk in the interrogation room.
- **Length**: 5 seasons, 46 episodes (2014–2025), plus 2 specials; the December 2025 film The Final closes the series. Only season 4 (9 episodes) is confirmed in Taiwan.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Season 1 is the best start, but season 4’s standalone cases work on their own.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/321851.do?cs=2) (season 4, subscription, some episodes free); [myVideo](https://www.myvideo.net.tw/details/3/19638) (season 4). Other seasons and the film were not found on Taiwan streaming

#### School Lawyer

A timid lawyer, bullied as a student, is sent three days a week to a private high school formed by merging a boys’ and a girls’ school ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/僕達はまだその星の校則を知らない)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama (summer 2025)` `B Real Sound best of 2025 (two critics: #1 and #3)` `B Eiga Channel 2025 top five (two writers)` `F PTT Japandrama 2025 year-end review #4` `F MyDramaList user’s 2025 list #2`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.0 (101 ratings) | IMDb 6.9 (17 votes) | Filmarks ★4.1
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. In Taiwan only an introductory piece could be found: [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8876226) (in Chinese) previewed episode 1. Japanese critics love it and it won a Television Drama Academy Award, yet it has only 17 IMDb votes.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; school bullying is part of the story.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. It is a school-lawyer drama, not a crime mystery.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/18212/eps/1) (VIP subscription)

### Mysteries and conspiracies

Espionage, serial killings in an apartment block, property fraud, a missing child and a wife’s past. Best watched in order, ideally in one go.

#### VIVANT

A Japanese businessman with a secret personality is sent to Central Asia to recover money his company wired out by mistake, and is drawn into an international conspiracy ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vivant_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Tokyo Drama Awards 2024 Grand Prix` `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama (summer 2023)` `B Real Sound best of 2023 (2 of 5 critics)` `C Filmarks 2023 Japanese drama #2` `D #1 Kanto average rating of 2023 (finale 29.8%)` `E Yahoo! Search Awards 2023 drama category` `F PTT Japandrama 2023 year-end review` `G #1 Japanese drama on friDay 2023` `G KKTV 2023 most watched #2`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.1 (season 1) (935 ratings) | IMDb 7.0 (1K votes) | Filmarks ★4.3
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be read in full; the Japan Times season-1 recap is paywalled. Reviewing season 1, [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/7452917) (in Chinese) liked the middle stretch back in Japan, but called the late twist that whitewashes the terror group a reversal for its own sake. It swept Japanese ratings and awards, drew almost no English criticism, and 56% of its October 2026 buzz on Taiwan’s DailyView was negative.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; it aired in TBS’s 9 pm Sunday prime-time slot.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 24 episodes: season 1 has 10 (2023), season 2 has 14 and ended on 8 November 2026. A film opens in Japan on 4 December 2026.

## Limits of this list

- **Few English reviews.** 8 of the 18 have no signed English review that could be read in full, and the Japan Times review of VIVANT is paywalled. All three Decider reviews cover only the first episode, and 5 others come from outlets outside the usual list, such as Asian Movie Pulse, MyDramaList News, The Blossom Review and Tokyo Prism 3CK.
- **Taiwanese reviews come mostly from one writer.** 雪奈 wrote the Taiwanese review for 11 of the 18; 99.9 Criminal Lawyer has none, and Tokyo Swindlers, Emergency Interrogation Room and School Lawyer have only introductions.
- **Douban for only 5 shows.** Unnatural, 99.9 Criminal Lawyer, Tokyo Swindlers, Last Samurai Standing and Alice in Borderland have quoted scores.
- **Newer shows have few ratings.** Go Home and School Lawyer each have under 30 IMDb votes.
