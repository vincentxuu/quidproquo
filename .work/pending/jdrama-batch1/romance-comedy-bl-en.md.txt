The Japanese part of the series opens with romance, comedy and BL: a chauvinist who learns to cook after his girlfriend leaves, an alien co-worker at a small-town hotel, a Showa-era gym teacher dropped into the present, and two middle-aged men and their dinners. All 17 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. Mystery, crime and action are in the [mystery and crime post](/posts/film/2026-11-10-jdrama-mystery-crime-action-en).

Compared with the [Korean romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-romance-fantasy-en), three things stand out:

- **Short.** 16 of the 17 run 9 to 12 episodes, and most finish in one season; only Ossan’s Love’s first season is shorter, at 7.
- **More original scripts.** 10 are original, 6 adapt manga and 1 adapts a novel; none needs the source first.
- **Generally mild.** Japanese TV has no single rating system and IMDb’s Parents Guide has almost no votes, so content notes are based on the plots.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: most Japanese dramas have no Metacritic score, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_japanese_dramas), IMDb, Japan’s Filmarks and Douban; for multi-season shows, MyDramaList is season 1. Douban scores appear only where a news report or review quoted them, and quotes from self-published posts are flagged.
- **Content**: Japanese TV has no single rating system, so ratings shown come from Taiwan platforms or IMDb’s Taiwan entry. Content notes come from the IMDb Parents Guide; most shows here have no votes, so the notes follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages. Shows backed only by Taiwan news reports, with no platform page to check, say so.

### Romance and romantic comedy

Contract marriage, a hospital crush, a love story told in sign language and a middle-aged romance. Mostly single broadcast seasons of 10 to 11 episodes.

#### Then You Try Making It!

Katsuo’s proposal is turned down after six years together because he believes cooking is women’s work; after the breakup he starts cooking, and Ayumi finds herself again ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Then_You_Try_Making_It!)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama, autumn 2025` `B Eiga Channel 2025 drama of the year (all 3 writers)` `B Real Sound 2025 top 10 (3 of 5 critics)` `C Filmarks Awards 2025 TV drama nominee` `D TVer Award 2025 drama grand prize (61M+ plays)` `E ORICON 2025 drama satisfaction #4` `F PTT Japandrama 2025: "if I recommend one show this year, it’s this"` `G friDay 2025 Japanese drama chart #2`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.0 (734 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (89 votes) | Filmarks ★4.2
- **Reviews**: [Karen Mansoldo at The Blossom Review](https://theblossomreview.com/tbs-then-you-try-making-it-review/) calls it a "romantic comedy" that "defies the expectations of its genre" to talk about equality and self-reliance. [Yusmoke at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/695fbc9ffd89780001f91770/) (in Chinese) likes that it skips the believe-in-love ending, but says it goes easy on its man. Awards, ratings and Taiwanese fans agree; the debate is whether Katsuo is forgiven too easily.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; the core is the hero’s controlling, old-fashioned attitudes, with no violence or sex reported.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, 2025, finished. A special has been announced; a weekly magazine reports January 2027, but the broadcaster has not confirmed a date.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Natsuko Taniguchi’s manga; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/4325) (影劇 plan); [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21240/eps/1) (available)

#### A Calm Sea and Beautiful Days with You

In 1936, Natsumi is married off to a navy lieutenant who misses their own wedding; from awkwardness and misunderstandings, the two slowly grow closer ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Calm_Sea_and_Beautiful_Days_with_You)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama, spring 2025` `A Tokyo Drama Awards excellence prize 2025` `B Real Sound 2025 top 10 (one critic’s #4)` `B Eiga Channel 2025 writers’ picks (2)` `C Filmarks Awards 2025 TV drama nominee` `D 2025 commercial-TV time-shift ratings #18 (6.1%)` `E ORICON 2025 drama satisfaction #1` `F PTT Japandrama 2025: "the year’s best romance, no contest"` `G friDay 2025 Japanese drama chart #1`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (season 1) (3,786 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (201 votes) | Filmarks ★4.2
- **Reviews**: Over the full run, [Karen Mansoldo at The Blossom Review](https://theblossomreview.com/a-calm-sea-and-beautiful-days-with-you-review/) found no twists or villains: it "can be a tad saccharine, but it’s still a wholesome, gentle, and inspiring love story". After three episodes, [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8729696) (in Chinese) called it a fresh take on marry-first romance. It topped ORICON’s satisfaction ranking while household ratings hovered near 6%.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, the wedding night is played for shy comedy, and season 2 may touch on the coming war.
- **Length**: Season 1: 10 episodes in 2025, plus a one-episode FOD-exclusive special. Season 2 began on 15 October 2026 and is still airing; episode count not yet known.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Hachi Nishika’s manga; no need to read it. Watch season 1 before season 2.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/4095) (影劇 plan). Season 1 is also on friDay’s free tier; season 2 is airing on [friDay](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/5398)

#### The Full-Time Wife Escapist

A jobless graduate becomes a housekeeper for a single office worker; they sign a contract marriage, and the fake couple slowly fall for each other ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Full-Time_Wife_Escapist)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Tokyo Drama Awards grand prix 2017` `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama, autumn 2016` `C IMDb 7.8` `D TVer Award 2021 special prize (New Year special)` `F PTT Japandrama year-end review: rewatched` `G DailyView October 2026 buzz chart #6`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.1 (9,464 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (2.9K votes) | Filmarks ★4.2 | Douban 8.4 (cited by a self-published article)
- **Reviews**: [David Yamaguchi at the North American Post](https://napost.com/2017/nigeru-wa-haji/) found the premise flaky, but "truly outstanding is the way that the drama repeatedly turns on a dime from light romantic comedy to real-life issues". Writing mid-run, [陳韻竹 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/55901) (in Chinese) read it as a look at women’s place in Japanese marriage and work. Critics and awards largely agree.
- **Content**: IMDb Taiwan listing 0+ (user-editable). No IMDb Parents Guide votes; IMDb’s Singapore NC16 and Taiwan 0+ are far apart, and no specific content notes were found.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, 2016, finished. A 145-minute New Year special in 2021 shows their third year of marriage.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Tsunami Umino’s manga; no need to read it. Finish the series before the 2021 special.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21218/eps/1) (available). Taiwan availability of the 2021 special was not checked

#### No Domestic Work for Ms. Saionji!

A capable woman who refuses to do housework buys her own home and takes in a homeless single father and his son as a makeshift family ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E8%A5%BF%E5%9C%92%E5%AF%BA%E3%81%95%E3%82%93%E3%81%AF%E5%AE%B6%E4%BA%8B%E3%82%92%E3%81%97%E3%81%AA%E3%81%84)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama, summer 2024` `B Real Sound 2024 top 10 (one critic’s #9)` `C Filmarks Awards 2024 TV drama nominee` `D 2024 commercial-TV time-shift ratings #13 (7.3%)` `G KKTV 2024 most-watched #8`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.6 (477 ratings) | IMDb 6.7 (29 votes) | Filmarks ★4.0
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found, and no Taiwanese one either. A Japanese critic at [Minpo Online](https://minpo.online/article/2024-44.html) read the cheerful family comedy as a thought experiment on new kinds of family. Discussion in English and in Taiwan lives mostly in MyDramaList and PTT user posts.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; nothing specific flagged.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, 2024, finished; no special or sequel found.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Satoru Hiura’s manga; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21222/eps/1) (available)

#### Silent

Tsumugi’s high-school boyfriend Sou vanished after graduation; meeting him again eight years later, she learns he has been losing his hearing ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/Silent_%28%E3%83%86%E3%83%AC%E3%83%93%E3%83%89%E3%83%A9%E3%83%9E%29)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Tokyo Drama Awards excellence prize 2023` `A Japan Commercial Broadcasters Association excellence award 2023` `C MyDramaList top Japanese drama #14 (8.5)` `D TVer Award 2022 drama grand prize (73M+ plays, a TVer record at the time)` `F PTT Japandrama year-end review: rewatched`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (9,424 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (935 votes) | Filmarks ★4.1 | Douban 9.0→8.5 (during broadcast, per media reports)
- **Reviews**: [Kristen Lazur at The Review Geek](https://www.thereviewgeek.com/silent-s1review/) gave it 7.5/10: "While the drama isn’t entirely unpredictable, it’s still a lovely, heartfelt watch". [吳思恩 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/63a6a923fd8978000132a06a/) (in Chinese) praises its gentle use of silence and its second lead. It set a TVer streaming record, but a deaf advocacy group objected to a baby being named "Yusei" (eugenics) in episode 8, and Chinese critics said it fell off late.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a tearjerker about deafness and sign language, with nothing specific flagged.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, 2022, finished; no special or sequel.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. A tearjerker romance, not a comedy.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/5300) (影劇 plan). Relaunched in September 2026

#### Second to Last Love 3

Eleven years on, TV producer Chiaki is 59 and her Kamakura neighbour Wahei 63; they stay as close and as undefined as ever ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Second_to_Last_Love)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Real Sound 2025 top 10 (one critic’s #2)` `B Eiga Channel 2025 writers’ picks (2)` `D 2025 commercial-TV drama episode ratings #6 (16.5%)` `E ORICON 2025 drama satisfaction #3` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Japanese drama top 10` `G VERSE 2025 ten must-watch shows`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (season 3) (26 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (153 votes) | Filmarks ★4.2
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [雪奈日劇部屋 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/359781/) (in Chinese), writing as a long-time fan, likes that the pair stay unmarried and admits some find the dialogue too long. Strong ratings and satisfaction scores, almost no English coverage.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; nothing specific flagged.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, 2025, finished; no word on a fourth. The third instalment after 2012 and 2014, plus a 2012 special. The IMDb score covers all three; only 26 MyDramaList users rated it.
- **Watch first**: It continues the first two instalments and 13 years of relationships. Only this third one is confirmed in Taiwan; the earlier ones were not checked.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/4081) (影劇 plan). Also on friDay’s ad-supported free tier

#### An Incurable Case of Love

Nanase becomes a nurse to meet a doctor again, only to find he is a sharp-tongued "demon lord"; she keeps confessing anyway ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/An_Incurable_Case_of_Love)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama, winter 2020` `A Tokyo Drama Awards excellence prize 2020` `C MyDramaList top Japanese drama #54 (8.3)` `C IMDb 7.4` `D Reiwa-era drama episode ratings #21 (25.3%)` `D TVer Award 2020 drama grand prize`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (29,444 ratings) | IMDb 7.4 (2.6K votes) | Filmarks ★3.8
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [Shel Lin at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/133658) (in Chinese) panned it as formulaic, with a doctor-nurse power gap that recycles old gender roles. Huge ratings against critical scorn: the clearest split in this post.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. Only 1–4 IMDb Parents Guide votes per category: kissing, drinking, and bleeding and surgery in hospital scenes.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, 2020, finished. A short web spin-off ran alongside it.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Maki Enjoji’s manga; no need to read it. The hospital is just the setting; romance is the point.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81423569) (subscription). Taiwan availability is based on an August 2026 media list

#### Eye Love You

A woman who hears people’s thoughts through eye contact has given up on love, until she meets a Korean student who thinks in a language she can’t understand ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/Eye_Love_You)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Seoul International Drama Awards jury special prize 2024` `B Real Sound 2024 top 10 (one critic’s #8)` `C Filmarks Awards 2024 TV drama nominee`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.7 (8,449 ratings) | IMDb 6.8 (800 votes) | Filmarks ★3.8
- **Reviews**: Across two reviews, [Thomas Storai at Future of the Force](https://thefutureoftheforce.com/2024/03/11/tv-review-eye-love-you-episodes-1-5/) declared that "Eye Love You is undoubtedly the best rom-com of 2024", while [Karen Mansoldo at The Blossom Review](https://theblossomreview.com/eye-love-you-netflix-viki-review/) found the heroine weak. No Taiwanese review could be found. An international prize and keen fans, but the writing draws faint praise.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; nothing specific flagged.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, 2024, finished; no special or sequel.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. The Japan–Korea romance is the draw, with Korean actor Chae Jong-hyeop as the lead.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21226/eps/1) (VIP); [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81747048) (subscription)

#### Ichiban Suki na Hana

Four people who have never been good at pairing up, each hurt by friendship, love or work, meet by chance and open up to one another ([Wikipedia](https://ja.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E3%81%84%E3%81%A1%E3%81%B0%E3%82%93%E3%81%99%E3%81%8D%E3%81%AA%E8%8A%B1)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Real Sound 2023: one critic’s #1 of the year` `C Filmarks Awards 2023 Japanese drama #20` `D 2023 commercial-TV time-shift ratings #17 (7.9%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.0 (393 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (49 votes) | Filmarks ★4.0
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [雪奈日劇部屋 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/270712/) (in Chinese) found the four leads’ meeting contrived until episode 7 pulled the threads together. It is an ensemble piece about whether men and women can be just friends, with romance as one thread. A critic’s No. 1 of the year, on low ratings.
- **Content**: No Japanese rating; no Taiwan platform rating found. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; nothing specific flagged.
- **Length**: 11 episodes, 2023, finished. A tie-in special with an international football match also exists; its length and format are unknown.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Same team as Silent, but unrelated.
- **Where to watch**: [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/21032/eps/1) (VIP). LINE TV is the only confirmed Taiwan platform

### BL and gay couples

A workplace love triangle, a middle-aged couple’s home cooking, and two men trying to buy a home together.

#### What Did You Eat Yesterday?

A middle-aged gay couple in Tokyo, a lawyer and a hairdresser, share a home-cooked dinner each episode, touching on family, work and gay life in Japan ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/What_Did_You_Eat_Yesterday%3F)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Television Drama Academy Awards best drama, spring 2019` `A Tokyo Drama Awards excellence prize 2019` `B Real Sound 2023 top 10 (2 of 5 critics, season 2)` `F PTT Japandrama 2023 year-end review #2 (season 2)` `G KKTV 2023 awards best Japanese drama #2 (season 2)`

## Limits of this list

- **Most English reviews come from small outlets outside the usual list.** The Blossom Review alone covers 6 shows; An Incurable Case of Love, Second to Last Love 3, No Domestic Work for Ms. Saionji! and Ichiban Suki na Hana have no signed English review. Both Japan Times reviews sit behind a paywall, and only their openings could be read.
- **Taiwanese reviews come from a few writers.** 雪奈 wrote the main review for 5 shows; Eye Love You and No Domestic Work for Ms. Saionji! have no Taiwanese review.
- **Douban for only 3 shows.** Only Silent’s score comes from the press; The Full-Time Wife Escapist and What Did You Eat Yesterday? rely on self-published posts carried by portals.
- **Ossan’s Love’s audience ratings and fan picks actually come from the 2024 sequel, Returns.**
- **Some Taiwan streaming evidence is thin.** The Hot Spot, An Incurable Case of Love, Just a Bit Espers and Chihayafuru: Full Circle rest on Taiwan news reports; Chosen Home only on a search snippet. Only season 2 of What Did You Eat Yesterday? is available in Taiwan. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-09.
