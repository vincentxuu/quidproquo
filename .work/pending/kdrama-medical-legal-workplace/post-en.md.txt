This post covers Korean medical, legal and workplace dramas: a trauma surgeon back from war zones, a rookie lawyer on the autism spectrum, a housewife returning to hospital as a first-year resident, and a middle-aged manager pushed to the margins at work. All 14 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. How K-dramas differ from American shows is covered at the start of the [romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-romance-fantasy-en); shows centred on family and life are in the [life and family post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-life-family-period-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Mostly medical.** Six of the 14 are set in hospitals; The Trauma Code, Daily Dose of Sunshine and Hyper Knife are streaming originals with no TV ratings.
- **Generally mild.** Only Hyper Knife is rated 19+ in Korea; the other 13 are 15+. Surgery scenes, self-harm and workplace bullying are noted for each show.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: most K-dramas have no Metacritic score, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_korean_dramas), IMDb and Douban; for multi-season shows, MyDramaList is season 1 and IMDb the whole show. Douban scores appear only where a news report or review quoted them.
- **Content**: Korean ratings come from Korean Wikipedia or IMDb's certification list, with Taiwan platform ratings where shown; content notes come from IMDb Parents Guide contributors, and most shows here have only a handful of votes or none.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" marks licensed shows whose latest Taiwan evidence is from 2020–2023; if the link doesn't play, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Medicine

Trauma centres, obstetrics, psychiatry and a small country hospital. More than half the list, from crowd-pleasers to comfort viewing.

#### The Trauma Code: Heroes on Call

A brilliant trauma surgeon who honed his skills in war zones takes over an understaffed university-hospital trauma team and turns it around with unorthodox methods ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Trauma_Code:_Heroes_on_Call)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Blue Dragon Series Awards best drama 2025` `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2025` `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #6 (9.1)` `D Netflix H1 2025: 34M views` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 #7`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.1 (64,565 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (12K votes) | Douban 8.3
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/01/28/the-trauma-code-heroes-on-call-netflix-review/) said skip it, calling it "The Pitt but a whole lot less realistic". [潘光中 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8548803) (in Chinese) argues the swagger sits on a real crisis in Korean hospitals. Real trauma surgeons called it exaggerated, yet with viewers it scores highest here.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Plenty of surgery, organs and blood, though not gory; the English dub swears a lot.
- **Length**: 8 episodes. Seasons 2 and 3 went into production in October 2026, with no date yet.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a web novel and Naver webtoon; no need to read them.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81677629) (subscription)

#### Dr. Romantic

At a small regional hospital, a genius doctor known as Teacher Kim shows a series of young doctors what being a real doctor means ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Romantic)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2017` `C MyDramaList 8.9 (season 3)` `D #2 in 2020 ratings (27.1%, season 2)` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #3`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (season 1) (48,435 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (7.3K votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing the start of season 3, [Hidzir Junaini at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/dr-romantic-season-3-review-medical-melodrama-finest-3441100) wrote that "season three is shaping up to be Dr. Romantic's best yet". During season 1, [廖佩玲 at Mirror Media](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20161207inf002) (in Chinese) called it an old-fashioned medical drama lifted to greatness by Han Suk-kyu. Both admit it is traditional.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (Disney+ Taiwan 15+). Only a handful of IMDb votes; lots of surgery, and season 3 opens with a shootout.
- **Length**: 3 seasons, 52 episodes plus 2 specials, 2016–2023. Seasons 2 and 3 share the same young doctors.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Watch in order; Han Suk-kyu plays Teacher Kim throughout.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-37b31212-ee3e-4440-9d10-b9247c830d5c) (subscription)

#### Resident Playbook

In an era of falling birth rates, a group of young residents choose unpopular obstetrics and gynaecology, making mistakes, growing up and becoming friends ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Resident_Playbook)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 #6` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `E FUNdex Awards 2025 lead and supporting acting awards` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G #3 on Netflix Taiwan (8 weeks)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (37,394 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (5.5K votes) | Douban 8.3
- **Reviews**: No formal major-outlet review could be found; at launch, [Kayti Burt at Time](https://time.com/7278386/resident-playbook-netflix-medical-k-drama/) explained it was delayed a year by the 2024 resident walkout and that "its story is broadly informed by the systemic conditions that have led to the stalemate". [Winnie at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/355327/) (in Chinese) found the first two episodes warm but loose. Ratings and buzz picked up later.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Surgery and childbirth on screen; only a few IMDb votes.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: A spin-off of Hospital Playlist, same hospital with new leads; no need to watch the original first.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81739043) (subscription)

#### Daily Dose of Sunshine

A nurse transferred from internal medicine to psychiatry looks after a varied ward of patients at a university hospital ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Daily_Dose_of_Sunshine)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Blue Dragon Series Awards best drama 2024` `A International Emmy comedy nomination 2024` `B Cine21 series of 2023 #7` `C MyDramaList 8.8` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #5`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (39,485 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (6.6K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Carmen Chin at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/daily-dose-of-sunshine-review-3533868) notes Korean screen culture usually stereotypes mental illness: "It is incredibly refreshing to finally have a Korean drama that doesn't relegate them to violent and antagonistic stereotypes." [九號球 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/656f4bb8fd89780001e3842e/) (in Chinese) praises how animation conveys what symptoms feel like.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. One episode shows self-harm and there are two suicide scenes; the main thing to know before watching.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, a Netflix original, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a former nurse's Kakao webtoon based on her own experience; no need to read it. Healing in tone, not surgical tension.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81572595) (subscription)

#### Doctor Cha

After twenty years as a housewife, a medical-school graduate goes back to medicine as a first-year resident following a health scare ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Doctor_Cha)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time K-drama picks 2023 (Netflix)` `D #8 all-time Korean cable ratings (18.5%)` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #7`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.1 (20,672 ratings) | IMDb 7.5 (2.9K votes)
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/doctor-cha-k-drama-review-uhm-jung-hwa-aces-this-journey-towards-empowerment-and-independence/article66942259.ece) finds the second half soapy: "Choppy writing aside, Jeong-suk's journey towards putting herself first is refreshing." [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/7224576) (in Chinese) enjoys the absurd hospital-and-affair comedy. Huge ratings; most reviews say it weakens later.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; the husband's affair is a main thread.
- **Length**: 16 episodes. The cast's agencies confirmed a season 2, but the producer says nothing concrete is set.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Half medical drama, half marriage comedy.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81682438) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Hyper Knife

Stripped of her licence, a brilliant neurosurgeon performs illegal surgery in the shadows until she meets her old mentor again ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyper_Knife)).

- **Recommended by**: `A International Emmy TV movie/miniseries nomination 2026` `B Cine21 series of 2025 #10` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.9 (10,753 ratings) | IMDb 7.3 (2.7K votes)
- **Reviews**: After two episodes, [Lee Yoon-seo at The Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10446226) called Park Eun-bin's psychopathic surgeon career-defining, but "where ‘Hyper Knife’ falters is in its credibility". [推坑人妻 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/353676/) (in Chinese) found it relentless. The acting is praised; the plot divides. The International Emmy result comes on 2026-11-23.
- **Content**: Korea 19+ (IMDb only; Disney+ Taiwan 15+). The heaviest here: a heart is cut out and the victim forced to bite it, nudity in episode 4; severe violence, swearing, drugs and intensity (few votes).
- **Length**: 8 episodes, a Disney+ original, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. A dark medical crime thriller, nothing like the warmer hospital shows.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-2ec24dc1-0c67-44c9-b043-b5d0537a66cb) (subscription)

### Law and the workplace

Lawyers, a talent agency, an ad agency, M&A negotiators, a brokerage and a baseball front office. Most leads are swimming against the system.

#### Extraordinary Attorney Woo

An autistic rookie attorney with a phenomenal memory joins a major Seoul law firm and proves herself case by case ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Extraordinary_Attorney_Woo)).

- **Recommended by**: `A International Emmy drama nomination 2023` `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2023` `B Cine21 series of 2022 #1` `C MyDramaList 8.8 (most ratings here)` `D #10 all-time Korean cable ratings (17.5%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (117,052 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (41K votes) | Douban 9.3 (mid-run)
- **Reviews**: Autistic writer [Geoffrey Bunting at Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/23308322/extraordinary-attorney-woo-netflix-autistic-representation-episode/) concludes "yes… and no": strong on workplace prejudice, but its subtext "reinforces the idea that disabled people's value is derived from what they can contribute". Psychiatrist [楊添圍, republished by The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/171148) (in Chinese), argues the idealisation distorts reality. The clearest representation debate in this post.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Light; one case concerns a disabled woman and an alleged sexual assault (nothing shown), and a character attempts suicide.
- **Length**: 16 episodes. A season 2 was announced in 2022, with no air date since.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81518991) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Agency

A woman climbs the ranks of a major advertising agency to become its first female executive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Agency_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `D #18 all-time Korean cable ratings (16.0%)` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.2 (4,783 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (850 votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed major-outlet review could be found; two Seoul ad professionals writing in UK trade magazine [MediaCat](https://mediacat.uk/agency-to-have-and-have-not/) say the sexism rings true, but "Agency is an over-the-top, glamorised and soapy version of an Ad agency". [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/289921/) (in Chinese) rates it highly. Ratings climbed from 4.8% to 16%, yet a Korean women's newspaper graded it C.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; mostly office bullying and power games, with lots of drinking.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original, with no romance.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81672245) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan). Its LINE TV licence has expired

#### The Dream Life of Mr. Kim

A sales manager with 25 years at a conglomerate and a Seoul apartment thinks he has won at life, until he loses everything at work and at home ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Dream_Life_of_Mr._Kim)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2026` `B Cine21 series of 2025 #6` `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 honourable mention` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites (about 20 posters)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.0 (1,314 ratings) | IMDb 7.7 (533 votes) | Douban 8.3
- **Reviews**: [Baek Byung-yeul at The Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/shows-dramas/20251202/the-dream-life-of-mr-kim-unpacks-midlife-crisis) calls it a consolation for middle-aged Koreans: "the emotional strength of the drama clearly came from Ryu's performance". [Irene Chen at Tatler Asia](https://www.tatlerasia.com/lifestyle/entertainment/the-dream-life-of-mr-kim-review-zh-hant) (in Chinese) likens it to an office documentary. It premiered to 2.9%; PTT fans say push through the first four episodes.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Light: mild swearing and some drunkenness.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Song Hee-gu's novel; no need to read it. The first four episodes are slow.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82068378) (subscription)

#### The Art of Negotiation

A conglomerate's legendary M&A negotiator and his team work through one corporate takeover after another ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Art_of_Negotiation)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Cine21 series of 2025 #5` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G Hami Video 2025 dramas #4`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.0 (4,772 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (891 votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed major-outlet review could be found; [Karen Mansoldo at The Blossom Review](https://theblossomreview.com/the-art-of-negotiation-viki-review/) calls it "a business drama with a healthy dose of realism and an extraordinary use of subtext", one takeover every two episodes. [推坑人妻 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/329775/) (in Chinese) found it picked up from episode 2.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a corporate drama with very light content.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. In Taiwan it is exclusive to Hami Video, not on Netflix.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/286760.do) (subscription, some episodes free)

#### Undercover Miss Hong

In 1997 Seoul, a 35-year-old financial inspector with a baby face poses as a 20-year-old new hire to go undercover at a suspicious brokerage ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Undercover_Miss_Hong)).

- **Recommended by**: `D #27 all-time Korean cable ratings (13.1%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2026 starter threads` `G 9 weeks on Netflix Taiwan's chart`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (11,359 ratings) | IMDb 7.5 (1.9K votes)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2026/01/20/undercover-miss-hong-netflix-review/) said skip it: "There's too much going on in the first episode", though Park Shin-hye is strong. No Taiwanese review could be found. It then climbed from about 3.5% to around 13%, the widest gap here between first-episode reviews and final ratings.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; mostly comedy.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. A 1997-set undercover office comedy, not a realistic workplace drama.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82632108) (subscription)

#### Hot Stove League

A baseball team that has finished last four years running hires a general manager with no baseball experience but a record of championship teams, and he rebuilds it from the front office ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hot_Stove_League)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2020` `D #5 in 2020 ratings (19.1%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (16,148 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (2.6K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed major-outlet review could be found; [Greg Wheeler at The Review Geek](https://www.thereviewgeek.com/stoveleague-s1review/) says it "seamlessly weaves character drama with political and societal issues", with little on-field action. [薇琪吳不負責任的報告書 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/159691/) (in Chinese) calls it a true workplace drama. It rose from about 3% to 19.1%.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Light; IMDb contributors call the language clean.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. No baseball knowledge needed; it is set almost entirely in the front office. In Taiwan only friDay's buy-to-own version is confirmed; JustWatch also lists Netflix.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/1112) (buy to own)

### A prison and a pub

One is set in prison, the other in an Itaewon bar. Not typical workplaces, but both are about a group finding its feet somewhere unfamiliar.

#### Prison Playbook

A star pitcher about to join the Boston Red Sox goes to prison for injuring a man who attacked his sister, and has to adapt, train again and get to know his fellow inmates ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prison_Playbook)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #19 (8.9)` `D #39 all-time Korean cable ratings (11.2%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (39,407 ratings) | IMDb 8.3 (6.5K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed major-outlet review could be found; [Vasia Orion at HanCinema](https://www.hancinema.net/hancinema-s-drama-review-prison-playbook-118114.html) calls it "the most unlikely feel-good series I can confidently vouch for". [廖佩玲 at Mirror Media](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20180208insight007) (in Chinese) says stick with it past the odd start.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (HBO Max Taiwan 16+). Ordinary fights, nothing gory; drug addiction is one theme.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished. Episodes run about 90 minutes.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original from the Hospital Playlist team. A prison ensemble comedy, filed here for that team's workplace-ensemble style.

#### Itaewon Class

After his father dies in a crash caused by a chaebol heir, a young man goes to prison for revenge; released, he opens a bar in Itaewon and builds it up to take on the conglomerate ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Itaewon_Class)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Seoul International Drama Awards miniseries runner-up 2020` `C IMDb 8.1` `D #16 all-time Korean cable ratings (16.5%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (104,421 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (26K votes)
- **Reviews**: [E. Alex Jung at Vulture](https://www.vulture.com/2020/04/itaewon-class-a-korean-drama-that-just-hits-different.html) calls it a remixed classic with an unusual supporting cast: "The show delivers that great salve of middlebrow television: It surprises and delights." [田育志 at 500輯](https://500times.udn.com/wtimes/story/12672/5276612) (in Chinese) says the four-year jump in episode 13 made the climb feel unearned.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Lots of drinking and partying, plenty of swearing in the subtitles; light violence.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Daum webtoon; no need to read it. Revenge and start-up at heart, filed here because most of it is about running the bar.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81193309) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

## Limits of this list

- **Many English reviews cover only the first few episodes, and several come from outlets outside the usual list.** Agency, The Art of Negotiation, Hot Stove League and Prison Playbook rely on MediaCat, The Blossom Review, The Review Geek and HanCinema; for Resident Playbook only a Time background piece was found, not a review.
- **No Taiwanese review of Undercover Miss Hong was found,** and its English review covers only episode 1.
- **Douban for only 4 shows.** The Trauma Code, Resident Playbook, Extraordinary Attorney Woo and The Dream Life of Mr. Kim have quoted scores; the rest are left blank.
- **New shows have few ratings.** The Dream Life of Mr. Kim has about 1,300 ratings on MyDramaList and 533 votes on IMDb.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** Taiwan evidence for Itaewon Class, Extraordinary Attorney Woo, Doctor Cha and Agency stops between 2020 and 2023, and Agency's licence on LINE TV has expired. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-08.
