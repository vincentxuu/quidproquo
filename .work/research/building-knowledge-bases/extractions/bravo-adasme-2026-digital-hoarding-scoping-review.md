## 研究設計、受試者、比較對象、量了什麼

- 設計：scoping review，依 Arksey & O'Malley (2005) 五階段。選 scoping 而非 SLR 的理由是文獻稀少、零散；不評估個別研究品質（引 Peters et al. 2015）。
- 檢索：Scopus 與 Web of Science，不限年份；字串 `"Digital possession" OR ("digital" AND ("hoarding" OR "disposophobia"))`。因為專講數位囤積的研究太少，把範圍擴大到 digital possessions（數位所有物）。
- 篩選：134 筆 → 去重 43 → 91 篇摘要 → 75 篇再審 → 36 篇全文納入（Method Stage 2–3，Fig. 1）。納入條件：英文、完整、同儕審查期刊。
- 分析：第一作者抽主題、其餘作者審；概念模型經三位／組外部專家驗證（工作與組織心理學研究者、認知科學研究團隊、數位科技使用心理學專家）。
- 受試者：無（文獻回顧，不涉人類受試者）。
- 納入研究的組成（Results 開頭）：16 篇一般成人、5 篇特定工作者族群、8 篇年輕人（其中 5 篇學生）、7 篇未說明；方法上 18 篇量化（50%）、11 篇質性（31%）、6 篇概念（17%）、1 篇混合（2%）。發表量 2024 年最多（8 篇）。

## 可引用結果

1. 定義沒有共識（Results "Definitions of digital hoarding"，Table 1）
   - Table 1 列六個定義：Van Bennekom 2015、Schüll 2018、Sedera & Lokuge 2018、Uğur & Çalışkan 2022、Tugtekin 2022、Sedera, Lokuge & Grover 2022。
   - 原文："Table 1 shows there was no single definition of what constitutes digital hoarding."
   - 共通三要素：acquisition、accumulation、difficulty discarding；部分作者加 disorganization。原文："Another consensus in the definitions following Van Bennekom et al. (2015) is that this behavior has three key characteristics: acquisition, ... accumulation, ... and difficulty discarding digital content, which is related to the feeling of loss or distress when deleting digital files"。
   - 定義多傾向病理化。原文："there was broad agreement that digital hoarding is pathological or disorderly in nature, meaning it negatively impacts individuals' mental health."

2. 類型是連續光譜：minimalist、collector、problematic digital hoarder（Results "Types of digital hoarders"；Discussion "Types of digital hoarders"）
   - 原文："The three identified groups (digital minimalist, digital collector, and problematic digital hoarder) can be understood and classified as part of a behavioral continuum ranging from digital minimalism to problematic digital hoarding."
   - collector 的描述："Collectors tend to organize and preserve their possessions more systematically (Neave et al. 2020), highlighting that these individuals exert a high degree of control over their data and often use organizational tools such as folders, tags, and classification systems."
   - problematic 的描述："These individuals lack control over their data, struggle to delete content, and their digital possessions are disorganized (Sedera et al. 2022; Sedera & Lokuge, 2018)."
   - 證據強度：collector 只有概念提出、未經實證；minimalist 只有一篇研究（Vitale 2018）。原文："the digital collector has only been proposed conceptually and has not yet been empirically confirmed (Neave et al. 2020; Schüll, 2018). In contrast, in the case of digital minimalists, although their existence has been empirically validated, this type of hoarder has only been described in a single study (Vitale et al. 2018)."
   - 作者另提第四型「traditional hoarder」，代表多數人的一般囤積，位置在 minimalist 與 collector 之間，需實證。原文："This type lies between the minimalist and the collector and would represent the typical hoarding behavior of most people. However, this proposal requires empirical validation."
   - 用途註記：這組區分正好可對應「一般知識工作者什麼都存」（traditional）與「有系統地收」（collector）與「問題型囤積」（problematic）。但要寫明 traditional 是作者提出、尚未驗證的假設；而且文中把 traditional 放在 minimalist 與 collector「之間」，不是在 collector 與 problematic 之間。

3. 負面後果：壓力、焦慮、生產力下降（Results "Negative consequences of digital hoarding"）
   - 原文："Among the individual consequences are stress, anxiety, and reduced productivity. Sweeten et al. (2018) found that participants reported stress when confronting the volume of their digital hoarding, which intensified when they could not locate critical files or had to spend significant time organizing them (Luxon et al. 2019; Sedera & Lokuge, 2018)."
   - 原文："Additionally, hoarding large volumes of digital files reduces productivity, primarily by making it difficult to locate saved items and resulting in wasted time (McKellar et al. 2023; Neave et al. 2020)."
   - 其他後果：資安威脅與機密資料外洩（McKellar 2020、Neave 2020）、能源與環境成本（Sillence 2023、Uğur & Çalışkan 2022）。

4. 但這些後果的證據都是相關性，因果方向不明（Discussion "Consequences of digital hoarding"）
   - 原文："The first is that most studies on negative consequences are correlational and therefore do not allow definitive causal inferences. For example, although individuals with higher levels of digital hoarding also report more stress and anxiety (Sedera et al. 2022; Sedera & Lokuge, 2018), it is not clear whether hoarding causes these negative outcomes or whether pre-existing stress and anxiety contribute to hoarding behaviors."
   - 組織層級證據稀少。原文："While some studies suggest that digital hoarding can affect productivity or increase cybersecurity threats within organizations (Neave et al. 2020), research in this area is still scarce."
   - 正面後果（照片的享樂、治療效益，Agarwal 2024）也沒有實證。原文："However, there are no empirical studies that confirm these claims."
   - 列為 RO12：用實驗建立因果（Table 2）。

5. 環境因素（便宜儲存、快速搜尋）同樣沒有實證（Results "Contextual factors"；Discussion）
   - Results 的說法：儲存便宜、容量近乎無限，「retention, rather than deletion, has now become the norm (Chen, 2014)」；人們囤積是因為搜尋引擎很有效率（Sweeten 2018）。
   - Discussion 的保留：原文："We also did not find empirical studies validating that contextual factors such as search engine efficiency or storage capacity influence digital hoarding (Chen, 2014; Schüll, 2018; Sweeten et al. 2018)."

6. 動機分兩大核心：instrumental（「可能有一天用得到」）與 sentimental（Results 概念模型段；"Motivations"）
   - 原文："From this flow emerge two motivational cores: one instrumental (I keep it because 'I might need it someday') and one sentimental (I keep it because the file symbolizes part of my identity)."
   - 工作情境以 instrumental 為主；email 被當成「證據」保留。原文："Among the most commonly retained content for this reason are emails, which are primarily saved as "proof" of work completed, tasks sent, or received (Sweeten et al. 2018)."
   - 未解問題：人們如何判斷「未來有用」沒被研究過；很多東西存下後再也沒用到。原文："This is relevant because many digital objects are stored under the instrumental assumption that they will be useful at some point in the future, even though they are never used again (Cushing, 2013; Van Bennekom et al. 2015)."（RO6：囤積者是否高估未來用處）

7. 量表的限制，含對 Neave 2019 的批評（Discussion "Types of digital hoarders"）
   - 原文："At the theoretical level, these scales adapt instruments originally developed by psychologists to measure physical hoarding. As a result, researchers implicitly assume that digital hoarding is behaviorally equivalent to physical hoarding, a premise that is challenged by the results of this study."
   - 原文："Methodologically, the limitations of these instruments concern their statistical validation: one scale omits a dimension without justification, and another applies a literal term replacement that may distort the original meaning of the items (Neave et al. 2019; Sedera et al. 2022)."
   - 量表只測程度、不能分類型。

8. 組織政策的效果不一致（Results "Contextual factors"）
   - 原文："When organizations provide little guidance on deletion policies, employees tend to retain more digital content (Mckellar et al. 2020). However, more recent studies have found that regulation and policies do not always drive digital hoarding (McKellar et al. 2023). The effect of corporate policies is a nascent topic with inconclusive results."

9. 14 個研究機會（Table 2，Fig. 4）：因素 RO1–4、動機 RO5–7、類型 RO8–11、後果 RO12–14。

## 作者自列的限制

- 數位囤積專門文獻稀少；許多研究用便利樣本（如大學生）、集中在特定文化（如亞洲）或特定職場。
- 不少關於心理疾患與技術特性的研究缺乏穩健實證。
- 主要檢索詞限於 'digital hoarding' 與 'digital possessions'，沒納入 'digital clutter'、'information overload'，未來可擴大。
- 上述限制影響推論到其他族群與情境。
- （方法本身）scoping review 不評估研究品質；資料抽取主要由第一作者完成。

## 與摘要或常見轉述不一致的地方

- 「普遍」沒有數字：摘要第一句 "Digital hoarding has become a common practice"，全文沒有任何盛行率數據，也沒有引用任何盛行率研究。要寫「數位囤積多普遍」，這篇不能當數字來源。
- 摘要說「practical guide for designing individual and organizational interventions」，但 Discussion 明說後果多為相關性、組織層級研究稀少、正面後果無實證；模型本身是文獻整合加三次專家意見，沒有經過實證檢驗。
- 「囤積造成壓力／找不到」：Results 段落的寫法讀起來像因果（"reduces productivity, primarily by making it difficult to locate saved items"），但同一篇的 Discussion 承認因果方向不明。引用時要用 Discussion 的保留說法，不要只截 Results 那句。
- 「定義普遍認為是病理性」（Results）跟作者自己提出的「traditional hoarder 代表多數人的一般行為、非問題型」（Discussion、Conclusion："a more common and non-problematic behavior"）之間有張力：作者實際上把多數人的「什麼都存」放在非病理端。
- 對 Neave 2019 的轉述：Results 寫 "individuals responsible for data protection exhibit higher levels of digital hoarding (Neave et al. 2019)"。對照 Neave 2019 全文，DPR 組多的是部分類別的檔案數量，刪除頻率沒差、DHQ 分數組間差異沒報告（見 neave-2019-digital-hoarding.md）。這篇的轉述沿用了 Neave 摘要的說法。
- Introduction 寫 "Digital possessions, defined as digital items that individuals recognize as their own, have been studied more extensively than digital hoarding (Neave et al. 2019)"：Neave 2019 全文沒有給 digital possessions 下定義，也沒比較研究量；這個引用掛得不準。
- Sweeten 2018 那句的引用標記掛的是 Luxon 2019 與 Sedera & Lokuge 2018，不是 Sweeten 本身；要引「找不到關鍵檔案時壓力加劇」須回原文確認出處。
- 定義的引文字句跟 Neave 2019 引用的 van Bennekom 版本措辭不同（"losing perspective ... stress and disorganization" vs "loss of perspective ... stress and disorganisation"），原始出處是 van Bennekom et al. 2015 BMJ Case Reports；要逐字引用請回原文。
