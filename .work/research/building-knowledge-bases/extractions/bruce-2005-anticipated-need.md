# Bruce 2005 — Personal, anticipated information need

- 書目：Harry Bruce, "Personal, anticipated information need", Information Research 10(3), paper 232, 2005. https://informationr.net/ir/10-3/paper232.html 。讀的版本：期刊 HTML，exa web_fetch（maxCharacters 120000，未截斷，57,129 字元），存為 scratchpad/fulltext/bruce2005.txt，折行後 bruce2005w.txt 455 行。
- 性質：**理論／概念文章**，沒有新實證資料。以文獻（資訊需求、Kuhlthau 不確定原則、訊號偵測理論）推出五個命題，作者自己說是「framework for further research and for clarification, empirical validation or correction」（Conclusion，l.346–347）。引用實證時引 Whittaker & Hirschberg 2001、Jones et al. 2002 的觀察。
- 可引用結果：
  1. 三種選擇（Personal information collections，l.55–57）：「1) to keep the information; 2) to leave the information where it is (it is useful but I can find it when I need it); or 3) to ignore the information」——引 Jones 2004。
  2. 訊號偵測（l.77–83）：「people sometimes keep information that turns out to be useless – a false positive; and they sometimes take no steps to keep information that turns out to be useful – a miss. Individuals, therefore, adjust their threshold for keeping information to reflect the relative costs of misses and false positives」。儲存變便宜→多收的代價變低→門檻下降（l.86–87）。律師、醫師這類漏收代價高的職業門檻較低（l.85–86）。
  3. 系統性偏差（l.93–97）：「When an individual has information 'in hand', there may be a strong tendency to overvalue its importance … The costs of a false positive–the costs associated with increasing clutter or the greater likelihood that good information is obscured by the arrival of new information– are less apparent. The threshold for keeping the information … is then set too low.」接著用 Whittaker & Hirschberg 2001 的 premature filing 當例子。
  4. 「check-this-out-later」堆（l.211–221、235–236）：資訊先暫收、沒想好放哪；Jones et al. 2002 觀察到一位受試者把我的最愛分三區，底部「之後再看」區長到要捲動時就清（刪除或移到其他區）。作者：「A person may ultimately process … only a percentage of items in such a stack. The percentage of items that is eventually determined to be useful may be smaller still.」——這句是推論，不是測量。
  5. Proposition 4（l.307–323）：需求清楚的兩端（明顯沒用、明顯有用）決策成本低；最花力氣的是中間「模糊、說不出用途」的那一段。
- 作者列的限制：命題待實證驗證（l.346–347）。
- 與摘要或常見轉述不一致：Bruce, Jones & Dumais 2004 只用一句話引用「signal detection (Jones, 2004)」；Bruce 2005 才把漏收／多收、門檻與偏差寫完整。order 1 寫訊號偵測時引 Bruce 2005（理論）＋Jones 2004（原始提出者，見 jones-2004 紀錄），不要引 Bruce 2004。「多收的代價被低估」是理論推論，實證要另找（Bergman & Whittaker 2016、GrayArea 2009 待讀）。
