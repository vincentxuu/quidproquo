## 作者主張

### 四類定義

- **2017 原版的精確定義**（作者說「These are very precisely formulated」）：
  - Project：「a series of tasks linked to a goal, with a deadline.」
  - Area of responsibility：「a sphere of activity with a standard to be maintained over time.」
  - Resource：「a topic or theme of ongoing interest.」
  - Archives：「inactive items from the other three categories.」
- **現行版的寫法比較鬆**：Projects 是「short-term efforts (in your work or personal life) that you take on with a certain goal in mind」；Areas 是「important parts of your work and life that require ongoing attention」；Resources 是「on a range of topics you're interested in and learning about」；Archives 是「anything from the previous three categories that is no longer active, but you might want to save for future reference」。現行版沒有「deadline」與「standard」兩個關鍵詞。
- **Project vs Area 的判準**（2017 原版）：project 有 goal 和「specific moment in time」，可以整個劃掉；area 有「standard」要維持，「continues indefinitely」。作者說兩者混淆是「a deeply rooted cause of many personal productivity problems」。
- **Area vs Resource 的判準**（Part 2）：關鍵是「responsible」對比「merely interested」。另一條準則是「put personally relevant information in Areas, and generally useful information in Resources」。

### 組織原則：依可行動性，不依主題

- 現行版的核心批評對象是學校式的主題分類：「We were taught to categorize our class notes, handouts, and study material by academic subject」，成年後仍照「incredibly broad subjects like "Marketing," "Psychology," "Business," or "Ideas."」分類，「This makes zero sense in your post-academic career.」
- 主張：「organize it according to the projects and goals you are committed to right now. This is what it means to "organize by actionability,"」
- 2017 原版的三個核心原則：(1) 用 4 當準則，「four categories wide」而且「no more than four levels deep」；(2)「perfectly mirrors your task management and project management systems」；(3) 區分 actionable 與 non-actionable，而且可行動性是漸層，對應 progressive disclosure。
- 同一份專案清單要在每個工具裡複製，「down to the exact same spelling, punctuation, and capitalization」。

### 為什麼 Areas 不放在最上層（Part 2）

- 三個理由：(1) 把少量可行動資訊和大量不可行動資訊分開；(2) 視覺雜亂，用 areas 當頂層會面對 22 個起點；(3)「projects be stored all in one place」，才能快速周轉。
- 「it's not that important to associate projects very directly with areas」，比較重要的是把專案連到目標。

### 搬移時機（Part 2：Flows and upkeep）

- PARA 是「a dynamic system, not a static one」，強度來自「constantly evolving flows of information between the four categories」。「The rapid turnover of projects becomes not a risk to be managed, but the actual mechanism by which we maintain the system.」
- 四類之間 12 條流向都有用例。重點幾條：
  - Projects → Archives：「one of the most common flows」。
  - Projects → Resources：專案結束、封存前，先快速掃一遍中間產物（brainstorms、背景研究、訪談筆記等），作者說通常只有一小部分，但「the value it creates in the future is immense」。
  - Areas → Archives：「Although it is rare」。例子是幫父親維護網站多年後停手。
  - Resources → Projects：「This is one of the primary use cases for resource notebooks actually.」
  - Archives → Projects：「one of the primary reasons to keep archived projects in the first place.」
- **什麼時候做**：不排固定時程。「I would suggest performing organizational work opportunistically, as opportunities arise, instead of pedantically」，稱為 Just-In-Time Organization：已經在翻某個專案的筆記時，順手把放錯的移走。
- 2017 原版另有審視節奏：每天看 Projects；weekly review 時擴到 Areas；monthly review 時擴到 Resources。這是**審視**的節奏，不是**重新整理**的節奏。

## 例子

- Project 清單其實都是 Area（現行版與 2017 版都有）：生技公司 Senior Director 交出的「project list」沒有一項是專案；作者問「Does "strategic planning" ever end for good?」
- 現行版的例子：Projects：Complete webpage design、Buy a new computer、Renovate the bathroom；Areas：Marketing、Human Resources、Health、Finances、Kids、Car；Resources：Graphic design、Coffee、Japanese language、Habit formation、Marketing assets。
- 2017 原版的 project／area 成對例子：「Running a marathon is a project, whereas Health is an area」、「A vacation to Thailand is a project, whereas Travel is an area」。
- Area vs Resource：Health（area）放血檢、看診筆記、帳單、疫苗紀錄；Workouts（resource）放運動研究、文章、訓練計畫。作者用大寫標 area、小寫標 resource。
- 作者自己有 22 個 Areas；約 160 個 Evernote notebook、約 3,000 則筆記（Part 2）。
- Resources → Projects：BASB 學員追蹤區塊鏈多年，後來做成線上課。

## 原句

- 「the ultimate system for organizing your life is one that is actionable.」（現行版）
- 「if your organizational system is as complex as your life, then the demands of maintaining it will end up robbing you of the time and energy you need to live that life.」（現行版）
- 「Instead of organizing information according to broad subjects like in school, I advise you to organize it according to the projects and goals you are committed to right now.」（現行版）
- 「So much of what we call "organizing" is essentially procrastination in disguise.」（現行版）
- 「There is a very clear line between things that you are responsible for, and those that you're merely interested in.」（Part 2）
- 「There is no "done," thus you don't have to worry about what "done" looks like! You always have search as a backup tool to find whatever you've missed or misplaced.」（Part 2）
- 「define your projects, or they will define you」（2017 原版）
- 「a project without a corresponding goal is known as a "hobby." ... if you have a goal without a corresponding project, that's called a "dream."」（2017 原版）

## 作者自述的限制或適用範圍

- 「4」的依據作者自己保留：「Whether or not it's a real limit, it is a useful constraint to prevent the two primary sins of organizational overengineering: too many categories, and too many levels of hierarchy.」（2017 原版）
- 跨平台的資料夾會慢慢「drifting apart」，作者說「This is actually fine」，等到找不到東西或影響工作時才對齊。（Part 2）
- 不必先為每個專案在每個平台建空資料夾，要用時才建。例外是手機捕捉不便時，只在 Evernote 和任務管理器預先建專案資料夾。（Part 2）
- 目標不是完美：「our goal with making things more discoverable is not mythical "perfection." It is "better than nothing," which is what most people have.」（Part 2，談 Evernote 250 notebook 上限）
- Areas 切多細「is really a personal decision」。（Part 2）
- 現行版只有一處限定：「There are other, more complex, sophisticated, and specialized ways of organizing information out there」，但作者接著說 PARA「is the only one that stands the test of time」。這是作者的主張，頁面沒有給證據。
- 全部三份都沒有實證資料，證據形式是作者的教學與教練經驗（「After more than a decade of personal experimentation, teaching thousands of students, and coaching world-class professionals」）。

## 與常見轉述不一致處

- **常見定義其實出自 2017 版**：網路上流傳的「a series of tasks linked to a goal, with a deadline」與「a sphere of activity with a standard to be maintained over time」，在指定的現行頁（2026-04-15 更新）裡已經找不到。引用時要標 2017 原版，不能標 fortelabs.com/blog/para/。
- **搬移規則與 Area vs Resource 不在現行頁**：現行頁只有四類定義、依可行動性的論證與 project list 故事。「什麼時候搬」「area 和 resource 怎麼分」要引 Part 2（2017）。
- **「PARA 是四個資料夾」不完整**：作者在 2017 強調它是 dynamic system，靠流動與專案周轉維持，而且「no more than four levels deep」。
- **「專案放在對應 Area 底下」與作者本人相反**：作者在 Part 2 明確反對把 Areas 當頂層再往下放專案，三個理由見上。概念上「Projects always fall into Areas」（2017 原版），但儲存上分開。Exa 摘要顯示 PARA for Teams（2018）有人採用 nested Areas，那是團隊情境的變體，不是原始規則（全文未讀）。
- **「依主題分類是錯的」要加限定**：作者批評的是把**主題當頂層組織原則**。Resources 本身就是依主題放（coffee、web design），只是降成可行動性最低的一層。
- **「定期整理」不是作者的建議**：作者明說不建議排固定的 re-org，主張 Just-In-Time。weekly／monthly 是審視範圍的擴大，不是搬檔時間表。
- **「Areas → Archives 很常見」不對**：作者說「Although it is rare」。最常見的是 Projects → Archives。
