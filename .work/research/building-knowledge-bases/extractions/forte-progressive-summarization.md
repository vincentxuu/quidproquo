## 各層定義（原文照抄，Part I）

- Layer 0：「Layer 0 is the original, full-length source text.」
- Layer 1：「Layer 1 is the content that I initially bring into my note-taking program. I don't have an explicit set of criteria on what to keep. I just capture anything that feels insightful, interesting, or useful.」
- Layer 2：「Layer 2 is the first round of true summarization, in which I bold only the best parts of the passages I've imported. Again, I have no explicit criteria. I look for keywords, key phrases, and key sentences that I feel represent the core or essence of the idea being discussed.」
- Layer 3：「For Layer 3, I switch to highlighting, so I can make out the smaller number of highlighted passages among all the bolded ones. This time, I'm looking for the "best of the best," only highlighting something if it is truly unique or valuable.」
- Layer 4：「For a small number of notes that are the most insightful, I summarize layers 2 and 3 in an informal executive summary at the top of the note, restating the key points in my own words.」
- Layer 5（Part I 沒標「Layer 5」編號，Part II 行 127 已寫「a Layer 5 note」，Part III 行 120 稱「Layer 5 remix」）：「for a tiny minority of sources, the ones that are so powerful and exciting I want them to become part of how I think and work immediately, I remix them. After pulling them apart and dissecting them from every angle in layers 1–4, I add my own personality and creativity and turn them into something else.」
- 前層保留：「Note that all the previous layers are preserved in context, giving you the freedom to leave things out without worrying that you'll lose them. Summarization is risky — you may be making the wrong decision about what's important.」

## 何時加一層

- 不在收進來當下加，只在「本來就為了別的事要看這則筆記」時加（Part I Layer 2）：「I do this bolding layer at a later time, when I'm already reviewing this note anyway. I'm essentially using the attention I'm already spending for a dual purpose」。Layer 3 同樣：「I'm only adding this third layer when I'm already reviewing the note anyway.」
- 定義為「opportunistic compression」（Part I）：「summarizing and condensing a piece of information in small spurts, spread across time, in the course of other work, and only doing as much or as little as the information deserves.」
- **明確不是排程式回顧**（Part II）：「I don't summarize notes on any sort of schedule, in any particular order, or as a part of a workflow. I summarize them completely opportunistically, when I'm already reviewing the note for some other purpose anyway.」
- 規則（Part II）：「If you follow the simple rule of summarizing a note every time you touch it, you'll organically create a collection where the layers of summarization correspond to how integral that note is to your work.」
- 很多筆記永遠不會被摘要（Part II）：「A given note may not be summarized until months or years after it's been captured. Many notes may never be summarized. This is not just acceptable, it is absolutely fundamental」。
- PARA 是「條件式排程」（Part I）：project 資料夾＝短期會被回顧；area＝評估該領域時回顧；resource＝決定行動時；archive＝「cold storage」。「scheduled contingently — if X happens, when Y arrives」。
- 作者自報的各層比例（Part III，個人估計、非量測）：只對約 50% 的來源存筆記；其中一半（全部的 25%）到 Layer 2；Layer 2 的 4/5（20%）到 Layer 3；Layer 3 的 1/4（5%）到 Layer 4；Layer 4 的不到 1/5（<1%）到 Layer 5。

## 作者說的限制與警告

1. 不是每則都要做完所有層（Part III guideline 1，作者稱「perhaps the biggest mistake」）：「It is absolutely NOT the goal to stuff every single note through all the layers of summarization. This isn't a funnel」；「There is no "preferred" level of summarization. More summarization is not better.」
2. 沒有明確篩選準則，靠「resonance」直覺（Part III guideline 2）；作者自己承認：「It seems terribly vague and error-prone. And it is.」
3. 不要加功能（Part III guideline 3）：標籤階層、命名規則、每則目錄或配圖，「This is the biggest pitfall, and the thing most likely to make you fall off the wagon.」——Part III 裡「biggest mistake」（guideline 1）與「biggest pitfall」（guideline 3）兩處都用最高級，作者沒統一。
4. 劃重點越多越糟（Part III guideline 4）：「more highlights are not better. Generally speaking, more highlights just dilute the discoverability」。
5. 壓縮會丟脈絡（Part I）：「you cannot compress something without losing some of its context」；壓太短一年後看不懂，太長又找不到重點。
6. 別人的重點對你沒用、所以不能外包（Part II，**與 LLM 直接相關**）：讀者看他 Layer 3 的三段，「They are enough for me only because of my personal context, prior reading, and experience summarizing this note. This is why this summarization process has to be done manually and individually. There are some layers and some sources where external tools can help, but until we develop not only artificial intelligence, but extended artificial cognition, summarization won't be something we can fully outsource.」（2017 寫、2022 更新時未改。）
7. 筆記系統不是用來找答案（Part III）：「your note-taking system is not meant for finding answers. You have Google for that.」目的是「help you ask better questions」。

## 其他可引用

- 數字例（Part II，單一範例）：Layer 1 筆記 373 字（約 2 分鐘），Layer 2 剩 181 字，Layer 3 剩 60 字、10–20 秒可掃完；作者說「6–12 times faster」。
- 刪除的態度（Part II）：「You have to be comfortable not only letting things fall through the cracks, but placing the cracks strategically so notes that don't end up being useful automatically recede from your attention.」「We need to add to our "digital cognition" mechanisms for forgetting.」——是「讓它沉下去」而不是主動刪除。
- 反標籤（Part II）：「The text itself is its own best tagging system.」
- 認知原理（Part III）：「recognition over recall」，目的是「create an environment of rich triggers」。

## 與常見轉述不一致處

1. 常見轉述把漸進摘要說成「定期回顧筆記、逐層加工」的維護流程；作者明說**不排程、不依序、不屬於工作流程**，只在為別的事碰到時順手做（Part II 原句見上）。寫 order 5 的「回顧」時不能把它歸為週期性回顧技術。
2. 常見轉述是「四層」或「五層」不一：Part I 列 Layer 0–4 再加未編號的 remix；Part III 才把 remix 稱 Layer 5。所以「五層（含原文）」「四層」「五層（含 remix）」三種說法都能在原文找到依據，引用要說清楚數法。
3. Part III 的漏斗比例常被當成「建議比例」，原文是作者自己的估計（「Here's a breakdown of approximately how many layers I apply」），不是規範也不是量測。
4. Layer 4 常被轉述成「用自己的話摘要整篇」；原文是「For a small number of notes」，且是摘要 Layer 2、3 的內容，不是重讀原文。
5. 作者對 AI 的立場常被拿來支持「AI 可以幫你做摘要」；2017 原文其實是反方向：摘要依賴個人脈絡，「won't be something we can fully outsource」。（作者後來的書或課程立場是否改變，本紀錄未查，不可推論。）
