## 可引用結果

### Part I（描述性與前因／後果）

1. 樣本組成（Part I §3）
   - 方法：quantitative 24、qualitative 48、mixed 19。原文："50% of quantitative studies (n = 12/24) but only 27% of qualitative studies (n = 13/48) have been published since 2017."
   - 質性研究多為個案："Qualitative studies in the sample typically use a case-study design (n = 34/48), one of which is longitudinal (Ciuk & Kostera, 2010)."
   - 53 本期刊；Journal of Knowledge Management 22 篇（24.2%）。
   - 40 國；理論上 52 篇只用單一視角（KM 41、OL 11）。
   - 志願組織只有 1／91。大型組織研究較多。

2. 定義鬆散（Part I §4.1）
   - 原文："Most studies use "knowledge loss" or "organizational knowledge loss" without providing a specific definition"
   - 常見說法："knowledge walking out the door"（Griggs & Hyland 2003）、"organizational Alzheimer's"、"enterprise dementia"、"corporate/institutional amnesia"。

3. 前因：自願離職難管理（Part I §4.2）
   - 原文："This type of KLT is found to be generally difficult to manage due to failure to predict which employees are about to leave and when (Leon et al., 2017) and unwillingness of departing employees to share the knowledge they possess with the organizations they leave (Yang & Wan, 2004)."
   - 非自願：退休（嬰兒潮）與裁員為兩大脈絡；專案制組織因重新指派造成高流動。
   - 退休文獻的主張："shifting focus from employee replacement to knowledge retention"

4. 後果：多數負面，但有反例（Part I §4.3）
   - 組織層：績效／生產力下降，經由 "erosion of key organizational routines"、"damage of organizational memory"、營運中斷、關係流失、客戶流失、吸收能力下降。
   - 原文（反例）："several studies report KLT's positive impact on organizational productivity, employees' learning behaviors (Starke et al., 2003) and learning organization capacity (Massingham, 2018)."
   - 調節因素：小組織受害較大（Durst & Wilhelm）；標準化程度高的產業較不受影響。
   - 原文："The reviewed studies provide inconsistent evidence on the relationship between characteristics of departing organizational members and effects of KLT." Starke et al. 2003 找不到關鍵員工離職造成更大損害的證據。

### Part II（應對與預防）——跟「寫文件」最相關

5. 應對研究還很早期（Part II §2.1）
   - 原文："The analysis shows that the research on coping with KLT is in its emerging stage."

6. 多數組織沒有策略、臨時應付（Part II §2.2.1）
   - 原文："However, studied organizations are often reported to lack holistic and circumspect KLT prevention strategies (Durst & Wilhelm, 2012; Sumbal, Tsui, See-to, & Barendrecht, 2017) and instead act on an ad-hoc basis (Doloriert & Whitworth, 2011)."
   - 策略三階段（Cross & Baird 2000）：targeting（找出會流失的重要知識）→ structuring（建立轉移機制）→ embedding（整合進流程、系統、產品以便再用）。

7. 文件化（codification）只適合明確知識，而且要附使用指引（Part II §2.2.2.3）
   - 原文："Codification-based approaches, relying on both IT-based tools (e.g. KM systems, organizational databases) and non-IT-based tools (e.g. standard operating procedures) are consistently reported to be suitable for the integration of explicit knowledge if complemented with proper guidelines for knowledge use/re-use"
   - 複雜知識要靠人："evidence is available for effective use of personalization-based approaches (e.g. workshops, informal learning opportunities) for the integration of knowledge with high level of complexity"
   - 原文："Regardless of the approach used, knowledge integration is reported to be challenging (Yang & Wan, 2004)."

8. IT 與非 IT 工具效果證據互相矛盾；離職面談與錄影的適當性受質疑（Part II §2.2.2.2）
   - 原文："several studies in this cluster address effectiveness of various KT tools, providing contradictory findings for IT-based and non-IT-based approaches (Daghfous, Belkhodja, & Angell, 2013; Lin, Chang, & Tsai, 2016)."
   - 原文："appropriateness of their use is overlooked by KLT studies with the exception of Massingham (2014a), raising concerns about using video recordings and exit interviews due to unwillingness of departing employees to share knowledge."
   - 多數研究關注隱性知識流失，把轉移工具分成 codification 與 personalization 兩種機制。

9. 文件化配招募、人對人配留才（Part II §2.2.3，Haesli & Boxall 2005）
   - 原文："The former links high emphasis on knowledge documentation and continual recruitment, which is argued to be mostly relevant in the short run to firms experiencing high turnover rates and operating in stable/mature markets. On the contrary, the latter puts emphasis on person-to-person knowledge sharing with strong efforts aimed at employee retention."

10. 時間面幾乎沒研究（Part II §3.1.3）
    - 原文："temporal aspects are barely addressed in the KLT empirical research."

## 作者限制（Part II §4）

- 原文："limiting the reviewed sample to peer-reviewed articles published in the journals ranked by Academic Journal Guide 2018 may have resulted in omitting some relevant studies."
- 原文："although the "Antecedents–Phenomenon–Outcomes" logic has served well for the purpose of the synthesis in the review, it should be admitted that there are other valuable approaches for synthesizing findings of SLR"
- 另在 Part II §3.1：研究多把發現「情境化」，"naturally sets limits for generalising"；"who" 與 "when" 兩個情境維度研究很少。

## 與摘要或常見轉述不一致處

- 這是「兩篇」而不是一篇。ERIC EJ1376413 只是 Part I（前因、後果）；「怎麼防止」「文件化有沒有用」全在 Part II。只讀 Part I 的轉述無法支持任何「寫文件能減少離職知識流失」的主張。
- 不是 meta-analysis，沒有效果量，也沒有研究品質評分。「91 篇研究證明離職造成知識流失」這類轉述誇大了證據強度：48 篇是質性，34 篇是個案研究；只有 1 篇縱貫質性研究。
- 「離職的知識流失一定有害」：作者自己列出正面效果的研究，也說離職者特徵與損害的關係證據不一致。
- 「寫文件可以留住知識」：本回顧說 codification 適合明確知識、而且要配「使用／再使用指引」；複雜或隱性知識要靠人際機制；IT 與非 IT 工具的效果證據是矛盾的。不能轉述成「文件化有效」。
- 納入門檻只收 AJG 2018 有列的商管期刊，資訊系統、軟體工程、CSCW 領域（例如 wiki、程式文件研究）基本不在樣本內。所以這篇談不到 wiki 或軟體文件。
- 樣本到 2022-11；不含任何 LLM／生成式 AI 時代的研究。
