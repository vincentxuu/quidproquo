## 作者主張

### 定義

- 「An M—O—C is a cluster of information that maps "things" in context with other "things".」
- 用途：「MOCs can help you gather, develop, and navigate ideas.」作者要讀者把自己當製圖者：「You are the map-maker. You are the cartographer.」
- 不只是索引：「In link-based thinking tools (like Obsidian), an MOC often gets mistaken as a boring index. Sure, the end-product sometimes resembles an index. But an MOC is so much more. It is a new kind of thinking tool that supports and extends cognition.」
- 認知支援：MOC 能「reliably holding any ideas you put on it」，同時讓人快速重排、編輯相關想法，達到「thinking at a higher level, learning more deeply, and generating unexpected insights.」
- Maps 頁：「Maps of Content are a special type of note that supercharges how fast you can build relationships between ideas. They came alive in 2020 at the start of the age of linked notes.」

### 什麼時候建 MOC

- 觸發點是 Mental Squeeze Point：「when your unsorted knowledge becomes so messy it overwhelms and discourages you.」
- 這是一種情緒：「The Mental Squeeze Point is an emotion.」
- 解法：「create a new safe space to assemble and organize the knowledge you're currently working on—before it completely overwhelms you. That dedicated space for thinking is called an MOC」。
- 3 Phases 頁的情境：讀到 habits 的東西寫一則筆記，之後越寫越多，「"I'm starting to worry I might lose some of these ideas."」於是建 MOC。

### 三階段：Gather → Collide → Navigate

- **Gather**：「assemble, collect, gather, curate, incubate」。把相關筆記的連結丟進一則叫「Subject XYZ MOC」的新筆記，像一張「digital workbench」。這一步「I have only "gathered" the links. They are not organized yet.」
- **Collide**：「This is where you generate all the insights. This is where ideas collide.」把 MOC 當坩堝，讓想法爭位置，「forcing you to grind them to their fundamental essence」。作者說這是「the most joyous and valuable stage」。
- **Navigate**：把 MOC 接到其他知識網路，「navigate your notes near the speed of thought」。MOC 留下來成為「a renewable summation of thought for Future You」。
- 三階段不是線性：「The 3 phases of MOCs are non-linear. They are not distinct like these examples. They overlap. They are messy. Don't make this rigid. Keep it fluid.」
- MOC 會持續演變：「An MOC is rarely a finalized concrete statue.」

## 例子

- MOCs (defn) 列四種「map」：一則把連結分組的數位筆記；桌上分組的索引卡；任何地圖（世界地圖、概念圖、Google Maps）；偵探牆上用線連起來的嫌犯與物件。
- Habits 示範：
  - Gather：收了五則舊筆記（2013-03-10 Habit Planning、2015-02-20 兩則、Atomic Habits (book)、2019-01-25 Resiliency Routines）。
  - Collide Day 1：把 Habit Concepts and Theory 改寫成 evergreen note，命名時發現太大，拆成兩則（The neural formation of habits is additive、The truest habit metaphors are additive）。
  - Collide Day 2：問「What are they trying to say? What is redundant? What note needs to be split into two? What outside concepts relate to this?」產出約 10 則新筆記，並記下引入的獨立概念（Feedback Loop、Natural Selection 等）。
  - Navigate：Habits Map 分成 Understanding Habits、Designing Habits、Important Habits、Related Concepts、Other Miscellaneous。
- MOC 的三種用途（Habits Map）：「as a reference point in the future」「for final products (content creation)」「for the inherent joy the ideas provide」。
- Maps 頁列出的 map 種類：Concepts、Thinking、People、Sources、Life、Efforts。

## 作者自述的限制或適用範圍

- 示範是刪減過的：「Because this is a vertical slice of my actual PKM system, I can't include everything in this vault and left out some notes and material」，原因是方便理解與隱私。
- 未連結的筆記是可以的：「it's okay to have some unlinked notes in your own vault. They won't all be built out at once.」
- 三階段不要僵化（見上）。
- 作者自評部分筆記價值有限：「Words I've used to describe important habits - this is more like a journal; it's of limited value to others」。
- 除此之外，這幾頁幾乎沒有談限制或失敗情況；也沒有證據或研究，全部是作者自己的經驗與示範。「When they "click" for you, wow, you'll feel your mind supercharged」屬於宣傳語氣。
- 什麼時候建 MOC 沒有數量門檻，只有「感覺被淹沒」這個情緒訊號。

## 與常見轉述不一致處

- **「MOC 就是索引或目錄筆記」**：作者明確反對，說最終產物有時像索引，但 MOC 是思考工具，重點在 Collide 階段的重排與改寫。
- **用語不一致**：MOCs (defn) 說「gather, develop, and navigate」，導覽說 Gather／Collide／Navigate，「develop」對應「collide」。另外示範從「Habits MOC」改叫「Habits Map」，Maps 頁也用「Maps」當總稱，顯示作者後來改用「Map」這個詞。
- **「MOC 是 2020 年發明的新東西」**：Maps 頁說「came alive in 2020」，但 zettelkasten.de 入門文（2020-10）把 structure notes 追溯到 Luhmann 的 hub notes／Sammelverweise，Luhmann 檔案館也記錄了一張最多 25 個連結的匯總卡。MOC 的新意比較在數位連結工具裡的用法，不是「把相關筆記列在一張卡上」這個概念本身。
- **「MOC 取代資料夾」**：這幾頁沒有這個主張，沒有比較資料夾與 MOC。
- **「什麼時候建 MOC 有數量規則」**：作者給的是情緒訊號（Mental Squeeze Point），沒有數字。
