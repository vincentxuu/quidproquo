## 作者主張

### 定義

- 「Evergreen notes are written and organized to evolve, contribute, and accumulate over time, across projects.」
- 重點不是筆記本身：「these practices aren't about writing notes; they're about effectively developing insight」。
- 「It's hard to write notes that are worth developing over time. These principles help」，接著列五條。
- 來源：「This concept is of course enormously indebted to the notion of a Zettelkasten」。

### 1. Atomic

- 「It's best to create notes which are only about one thing—but which, as much as possible, capture the entirety of that thing.」
- 太大：遇到新想法不會發現，連結也會「muddied」；太碎：連結網路也跟著碎。
- 類比軟體的 separation of concerns，「Evergreen note titles are like APIs」。

### 2. Concept-oriented

- 「It's best to factor Evergreen notes by concept (rather than by author, book, event, project, topic, etc).」
- 依書或專案記筆記，同一概念散在各處，「there's no accumulation」，也沒有「pressure to synthesize your new ideas on the concept with your prior thoughts about it」。
- 新的連結常出現在不預期的地方，依概念整理才看得到。
- 代價是寫起來比較難，「but in a useful way: when writing new notes, we have to find where they fit into the whole.」

### 3. Densely linked

- 多加連結迫使人想「how ideas relate to each other」，也透過 elaborative encoding 加深理解。
- 找連結要讀舊筆記，所以也是一種間歇複習。
- 「Prefer fine-grained associations」；「Tags are an ineffective association structure」。
- 可以連到還沒寫的筆記：「it's very freeing to be able to link to a stub.」

### 4. Associative ontologies over hierarchical taxonomies

- 「Let structure emerge organically. When it's imposed from the start, you prematurely constrain what may emerge and artificially compress the nuanced relationships between ideas.」
- 預先分類會「fuzz their edges」，新類別即使出現也看不到形狀，「further sorting requires undoing existing structure.」
- 引 Vannevar Bush〈As We May Think〉：「The human mind does not work that way. It operates by association.」

### 5. Write for yourself by default

- 想「save time」直接寫成可發表的形式，會大幅增加負擔，「often to the point of producing blockage.」
- 作者自己的失敗模式：寫原子筆記時把它當成文章的段落來寫，結果「I often find that I can't write anything at all!」
- 「I'll write notes for myself "by default," and only "opt into" writing notes for an audience explicitly.」

### 與 Zettelkasten 的異同

- 相同：概念導向、原子性、重連結、把意外當優點、重視自己的想法與自己的話。
- 不同：多了 writing inbox、用 spaced repetition 管 inbox 與發展雛形想法、每日晨間寫作；更廣的筆記類型階層（「Zettels are mostly meant to represent a few "middle" levels of this hierarchy」）；標題是重要索引；contextual backlinks；筆記內嵌 SRS；筆記公開。
- 帶刺的一點：「Most people in the contemporary Zettelkasten culture seem to use their systems primarily to write notes about others' ideas.」

## 例子

- 依書記筆記：讀第二本談同一概念的書，就寫一則新的書筆記，概念散成「a scattered set of notes on the concept, perhaps referring to it by different names」。
- 寫給自己的頁面附兩張 SRS 卡（Q／A），是作者把記憶卡嵌進筆記的實例。
- 引 Luhmann 的博物館例（am-concept 的 References）與 Ahrens：「In the old system, the question is: Under which topic do I store this note? In the new system, the question is: In which context will I want to stumble upon it again?」

## 原句

- 「Evergreen notes are written and organized to evolve, contribute, and accumulate over time, across projects.」
- 「It's best to create notes which are only about one thing—but which, as much as possible, capture the entirety of that thing.」
- 「There's no clear litmus test or correct answer here—just a bunch of tradeoffs.」
- 「It's best to factor Evergreen notes by concept (rather than by author, book, event, project, topic, etc).」
- 「Let structure emerge organically.」
- 「I'll write notes for myself "by default," and only "opt into" writing notes for an audience explicitly.」

## 作者自述的限制或適用範圍

- 原子性沒有標準答案：「There's no clear litmus test or correct answer here—just a bunch of tradeoffs.」太碎會有「cohesion problem」。
- 聯想式結構的代價：「It's hard to navigate to unlinked "neighbors" in associative note systems」（只列為連結，沒展開）。
- 標籤無效（同上，只列為連結）。
- 寫給自己：熟悉的主題可以同時寫給自己和讀者，「But that sometimes produces the false impression that I can pull this off all the time!」
- 公開筆記的影響：「I don't understand the impact of this practice yet」。
- SRS 嵌入筆記：「This has substantially changed the medium, but in ways I don't yet understand very well.」
- 使用情境：作者說系統的目的是發展他的核心創作專案，「my context of use substantially shapes the note-writing practice」，暗示做法不一定能直接搬到別的情境。

## 與常見轉述不一致處

- **「atomic = 短」**：作者要的是「only about one thing」而且「capture the entirety of that thing」，不是越短越好；太碎同樣是問題。
- **「Evergreen notes 就是 Zettelkasten 的 permanent notes」**：作者刻意換名詞，說有差異，也想和 Zettelkasten 文化保持距離。
- **「不要任何結構」**：作者反對的是**一開始就強加**的階層分類，主張結構「emerge organically」，看到形狀後再思考。這和 zettelkasten.de 入門文推薦 structure notes 並不衝突，但和「完全不要階層」的轉述不同。
- **「寫給自己＝不公開」**：作者的筆記是公開的。他的意思是預設不考慮讀者，需要時才明確「opt into」。
- **「dense links 要連到已經寫好的筆記」**：作者說可以連到 stub，靠回連隱性定義節點。
- **概念導向反對的範圍比 PARA 寬**：Matuschak 反對依「author, book, event, project, topic」分筆記，包含 PARA 的 project 與 resource（topic）兩種單位。兩者處理的對象不同：PARA 管行動資料，evergreen notes 管想法；寫系列文時要分清楚。
