## 作者自己列的限制（可直接寫「作者說」）

- "Overviews of reviews do not permit inferences about the comparative effectiveness of different interventions"
- "cohesive QoE evaluations, such as pooling Grading of Recommendations Assessment, Development and Evaluation assessments (14), were not possible"
- "randomized trials in sports and exercise medicine-related fields are often poor quality with small sample sizes"
- "Overlapping data are an issue in overviews of reviews"（只對力量做了重疊分析）
- 範圍：只含健康成人；沒有比較不同身體部位或肌群；"insufficient evidence to form recommendations on emerging RTx strategies"。

## 要小心的

1. **建議的文字多半是作者的立場，不是研究結果。** 例如「每週至少兩次」是 "primary recommendation"，表 6 的 "≥80% 1RM"、"2–3 sets/session" 是作者從回顧整理的條件；表 6 把 "≥80% 1RM" 與 "≥10 sets/wk" 都標為 (dose-response)（2026-10-24 更正：先前誤寫「並非嚴格劑量反應」）。討論另引用 [187] 的迴歸：約每週 18–20 組後報酬遞減（未讀 [187]）。作者也寫："in the view of the authors" 才給出四到六個身體區域的建議。
2. **表 4「力量：負荷」有明確的 ✔，「肌肥大：負荷」是 ✖（無差別）**；但作者在討論再補："RT frequency was found to impact strength; however... the impact is diminished when volume is equated"。引用時要把「在總量相同時」帶上。
3. **「超過約 2–3 組／每週 18–20 組報酬遞減」** 來自引用的另一篇統合迴歸 [187]，不是本聲明自己的結果；聲明接著說「建議至少兩組」，是作者建議。
4. **利益揭露：** "B. J. S. formerly served on the scientific advisory board for Tonal Corporation, a manufacturer of fitness equipment." 其餘作者聲明無相關衝突。
5. 作者承認 "a considerable carryover of training effects on general muscular performance in various domains in nonadvanced trainees"（對進階者不適用）；且結論 "only a few RTx variables can be manipulated to enhance adaptations in experienced trainees"。
6. SPPB 的「無效」被作者自己說成是證據稀少而非真的沒效，示範了「沒證據 ≠ 沒效果」，可作為寫作用的例子。
7. 這篇的結果對象是力量、肌肥大、功率等，**不含手指力量或攀岩表現**；作者也說 "The available evidence did not compare distinct body regions or muscle groups."

## B. Lopez 等人 2021，負荷與肌肥大、肌力（網絡統合分析）

- 書目：Lopez P et al. "Resistance Training Load Effects on Muscle Hypertrophy and Strength Gain: Systematic Review and Network Meta-analysis." *Med Sci Sports Exerc* 53(6):1206–1216, 2021（PMID 33433148，PMC8126497，CC BY-NC-ND）。
- 取得：Europe PMC XML，60,180 字元，`truncated: false`；讀了摘要、結果（肌力）、討論、限制。
- 設計：28 項研究、747 名健康成人，**只納入練到力竭的組別**（低負荷 >15RM、中 9–15RM、高 ≤8RM）；研究期間 6–12 週；受試者平均 23.4 ± 2.9 歲、多為年輕人。

| 內容 | 原文 |
|---|---|
| 規模 | "Twenty-eight studies involving 747 healthy adults were included." |
| 結論：肌肥大與負荷無關，肌力高負荷較佳 | "muscle hypertrophy improvements seem to be load independent, increases in muscle strength are superior in high-load RT programs" |
| 高對中負荷的肌力差異未達顯著 | "nonsignificant but superior effect for high compared with moderate load (SMD, 0.26–0.28, P = 0.068)" |
| 研究品質低 | "studies included in the present review were mostly of low quality because of concerns regarding the randomization process and measurement of the outcome" |
| 不適用於高度訓練者 | "the present results cannot be extrapolated for highly strength-trained individuals" |
| 年輕人為主 | "the present investigation included mostly studies undertaken in young adults (23.4 ± 2.9 yr)" |
| 期間短 | "ranging from 6 to 12 wk" |

注意：Lopez 的結論限定「**練到力竭**」的組別；ACSM 則另外說力竭不必要。兩篇不衝突，但回答的是不同問題。引文 `CC BY-NC-ND`：只引短句，不改寫成衍生翻譯大段。

## 對 order 6 的影響
