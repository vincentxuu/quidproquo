# Mainland Chinese TV (陸劇) candidates — awards juries (A) + critics / media year-end lists (B)

Collected 2026-11-12 for the zh-TW 「各國影劇導讀」 series (mainland China). Raw data only; no curation applied. Scope: scripted live-action series produced in mainland China (CCTV, satellite channels, and streaming originals on iQIYI / Tencent Video / Youku / Mango TV / Bilibili), mainly 2016–2026; a few 2015-aired titles appear because the 2016 award cycles cover them. Hong Kong (TVB / ViuTV) and Taiwanese productions, US/foreign productions about China, variety / reality, animation (donghua), documentaries and films found in the sources are in 2c so nothing is silently dropped.

**Read this first — state vs. independent.** Every domestic Chinese TV award is tied to the state in some way, so the table separates three kinds of evidence:
- `A` = jury award not run directly by the national regulator (Magnolia = Shanghai TV Festival, run by the Shanghai municipal government / SMG but judged by an industry jury; and all foreign awards: AACA, SDA, Intl Emmy, ACA).
- `A*` = **state / Party-affiliated award** — `FT` 飛天獎 is organised by 國家廣播電視總局 (NRTA) itself and its categories are explicitly ideological (e.g. 信仰使命篇 / 時代奮鬥篇, 2026); `GE` 金鷹獎 is run by 中國文聯 / 中國視協 (CFLAC, a Party-led "people's organisation") and mixes expert, member and audience voting.
- `S` = state / industry **recommendation list** (主旋律-style 推優), not an award with a published nominee field: `S-TJ2024` 「年度劇集推優」 (20 titles, 首屆中國電視劇製作產業大會, Feb 2025). Who selected it was not verified — treat as industry/official, not critics.
- `B` = media / critics year-end lists (all Chinese-language; see each key for how independent it is).

The table therefore has two counts: **all sources** and **excluding state keys (FT, GE, S-TJ2024)**. Sort is by the first, then the second.

**Year conventions** (read before using tags):
- `MAG` (上海電視節白玉蘭獎 最佳中國電視劇): tag = **ceremony year** (June). Eligibility is roughly the previous Apr–Mar (e.g. 30th / 2025 = shows aired 1 Apr 2024 – 31 Mar 2025, per zh.wikipedia). No ceremony in 2022; the 28th (2023) merged two cycles and has 22 nominees and two co-winners (人世間, 縣委大院). 22nd (2016) – 31st (2026) extracted, nominees + winner.
- `FT` (中國電視劇飛天獎 優秀電視劇): tag = ceremony year; biennial, covers the **previous two calendar years** (34th / 2024 = shows aired 2022–2023; 35th / 2026 = 2024–2025). Only the 16 優秀電視劇 **wins** per ceremony are counted; the ~32 other 入圍 titles are shown as `FT-nom` and **not counted** (titles whose only placement is an FT nomination are listed in 2b instead of the table). 31st (2018) – 35th (2026).
- `GE` (中國電視金鷹獎): tag = ceremony year; biennial, covers roughly the previous two years. Counted: 最佳電視劇, 優秀電視劇, and (32nd 2024 / 33rd 2026 only) the published 最佳電視劇 nominee list. 28th (2016, no 最佳 awarded) – 33rd (2026).
- `AACA`, `SDA`, `IntlEmmy`, `ACA`: ceremony year. ACA = Asia Contents Awards & Global OTT Awards (Busan; 2025 ceremony was branded "International Streaming Festival", 2026 "Global OTT Awards").
- B tags use the year **the list covers** (the 南方周末 2025 list was published Jan 2026, etc.).

**Distinct sources** = count of distinct source keys a show appears in. Keys (12): A — `MAG`, `AACA` (Best Drama Series grand + China national winners 2018–2025, plus 2024–2025 China national acting/craft wins), `SDA` (Seoul International Drama Awards, competitive categories only), `IntlEmmy` (any category), `ACA` (Asia Contents Awards, competitive categories only); A* — `FT`, `GE`; S — `S-TJ2024`; B — `NFZM` (南方周末 文化原創榜·劇集, named critic/academic panel), `XZK` (新周刊 中國視頻榜, 推委會), `NDY` (南都娛樂年鑑 2023 十大劇集, editorial, ranked), `BJN` (新京報 年度劇集 2024 + 2025, editorial, explicitly weighs 播出熱度 too). Several placements inside one key count once.
- **Seasons merged**: 大江大河 / 大江大河2 / 大江大河之歲月如歌; 慶餘年 / 慶餘年第二季; 唐朝詭事錄 / 之西行 / 之長安 (the original season-specific title is shown in brackets in the tag). 琅琊榜 and 琅琊榜之風起長林 are kept separate (different story).
- **Not counted**: SDA popularity prizes (Most Popular Foreign Drama, Asian Star), ACA invitation / People's Choice / Rising Star prizes, award citations of a person for several works, reader / netizen polls (南都 網友評選), anything marked snippet.

**Metadata**: Simplified titles are as printed in the sources (zh.wikipedia wikitext, news pages). **Traditional titles are a machine conversion (OpenCC s2tw), not checked against Taiwanese release titles** — e.g. 漫長的季節 is right, but Taiwanese platforms sometimes rename shows; verify before publishing. English titles without `†` are as printed in an English-language source read here (AACA, ACA, SDA, Intl Emmy pages); `†` = general knowledge, unsourced. "Years in sources" = span of ceremony/list years in the tags, **not** first-air year (first-air years were not collected). Network is shown only where a read source printed it.

## 3. Could not access / caveats

- **toolDegradation**: (1) Groundlane `web_extract` / `web_fetch` / `web_search` was primary and handled zh/en Wikipedia wikitext (`action=raw`), AACA official pages, yearendlists.com, Guardian. Groundlane search reported `tavily unavailable` / `exa unavailable` / `brave unavailable` on some calls and fell back to brave/you. (2) Groundlane failed on bjnews.com.cn (DEADLINE_EXCEEDED), m.mp.oeeee.com (HTTP 493 "JFE Forbidden"), resetera.com (browser challenge) → **Exa** `web_fetch_exa` / `web_search_exa` used for those and for Chinese-media discovery (南方周末, 新周刊, 南都, 新京報, 光明網). jina, Tavily, Firecrawl not used; terminal curl not used.
- **No critics' list outside the Chinese press was found that includes a mainland series** (NYT Hale 2019 decade / 2024 / 2025, Guardian 2023 checked). Guardian 2016–2022, 2024–2025, Variety, Time, Vulture, THR, IndieWire were **not** checked for Chinese titles (the JP/KR passes searched those pages only for Japan/Korea). A targeted search for *Blossoms Shanghai* / *The Long Season* / *Three-Body* in English year-end lists returned nothing countable — Blossoms Shanghai only reached US streaming (Criterion Channel) in Nov 2025, so 2025–2026 lists may still pick it up.
- **B lists are thin and not fully independent**: all four B keys are Chinese mainland media working under the same content rules. NFZM uses a named critic/academic panel (closest to a critics poll); XZK uses a 推委會 (named in reports: 郝蕾 chair, 嚴飛, 韓松落 etc.); NDY and BJN are editorial lists that explicitly weigh popularity / 熱度. 澎湃 / 界面 / 三聯生活周刊 / 中國新聞週刊 year-end drama lists, 豆瓣 (audience, out of B anyway) and 中國電視劇品質盛典 were not found or not read.
- **NFZM gaps**: 2019 and 2020 not found; nominations only for 2023 (possibly truncated) and 2025; 2024 4-title shortlist not read; 2016 not checked.
- **XZK** only 2023–2025 read; earlier 中國視頻榜 / 中國電視榜 years exist (since 2000, per zh.wikipedia) but were not read.
- **BJN 2024/2025** are image-based on the original pages; counted entries come from a full-text repost (2024) and the official Weibo text (2025), so a few list entries may be missing. 新京報 also publishes quarterly 劇集榜 (Q2/Q3 2024 seen in snippets) — not counted.
