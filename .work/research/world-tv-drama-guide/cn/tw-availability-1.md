# Mainland Chinese dramas, batch 1: where to watch legally in Taiwan (checked 2026-11-12)

Scope: the 40 shows in `cn/tw-batch-1.tsv`. For each one, this file records the official Taiwan title, the Taiwan platform(s) with a title-page link, and the evidence. We have no Taiwan IP, and no proxy or bot-check workaround was used.

Evidence rules (same as `jp/tw-availability-1.md`, plus the user rule for this round):
- **iQIYI international (iq.com, 愛奇藝台灣站) and Tencent WeTV do NOT count** as legal Taiwan channels. In 2020 the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA) moved to ban Taiwanese agents and distributors of Chinese OTT services. The Mainland Affairs Council (MAC) deems iQIYI Chinese-funded (CNA, 2020-08-18, https://www.cna.com.tw/news/firstnews/202008185009.aspx). If iq.com or WeTV is the only Taiwan option, the verdict is `iq/WeTV only`, and the iq.com/WeTV URL is recorded for information. iQIYI's own YouTube channel is treated the same way.
- **YouTube**: the channel, title and date come from Exa's fetch of the watch URL (Exa reports the channel as author and gives the publish timestamp). "Netflix Taiwan" is the Taiwan channel. Some Shorts did not expose a date.
- **Netflix `netflix.com/tw/title/<id>`** links are leads only. Netflix serves those pages by requester IP. An ID is quoted only when it came from a Netflix Taiwan YouTube description, or from a search hit whose page title names the show (「觀賞《…》 | Netflix」). Netflix Tudum Taiwan Top-10 placements are taken from `cn/candidates-fans-taiwan.md` (S21, read via Exa in the candidates round). They are official Netflix data, but dated.
- **Disney+ `/zh-tw/browse/entity-…`** pages count when the fetch returns 「觀看<title> | Disney+」 for the show.
- **Hami Video** pages count when `<title>` carries the show's name. The 「下架時間」 (licence end) is quoted. A Hami product URL that now returns the generic title 「HamiVideo」 (about 99 KB, no 下架時間) counts as **lapsed**.
- **LINE TV**: a page counts when it loads with the title and has no 「授權到期」. The check was calibrated on this batch's own lapsed page, 獵罪圖鑑 S1 (`linetv.tw/drama/13044`), which shows 「授權到期」 and 「即將上線」.
- **friDay影音** pages count when they load with the title. The 「適用方案」 line is quoted: 免費 = free tier, 影劇 = paid drama pack.
- **myVideo** pages are **inconclusive**, because to our non-TW IP they show 「本影片因授權因素 暫時無法提供觀看」.
- **KKTV** ended service on 2026-01-01 and is not counted.
- **Linear TV** (緯來戲劇台, 八大, Hami's live channels) is noted where seen but does not count.
- **Verdicts**:
  - `confirmed`: an official channel, a service page, a company press release or Taiwan media, with a date.
  - `licensed-old`: the only evidence is from 2023 or earlier, or is an indexed Netflix page or JustWatch-type data.
  - `lapsed`, `iq/WeTV only` and `not found`: as named.
