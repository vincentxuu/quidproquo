# Japanese dramas, batch 1: where to watch legally in Taiwan (checked 2026-11-09)

Scope: the 33 shows in `jp/tw-batch-1.tsv`. For each one, this file records the official Taiwan title, the Taiwan platform(s) with a title-page link, and the evidence. We have no Taiwan IP, and no proxy or bot-check workaround was used.

Evidence rules (same as `kr/tw-availability-1.md`):
- **YouTube**: Groundlane `web_extract` on watch pages returned an empty YouTube shell this session (no `meta[name=title]`, `link[itemprop=name]` or `meta[itemprop=datePublished]`). Channel, title and date were therefore read from Exa's page fetch/search of the same watch URL (Exa reports the channel as author and the publish timestamp). "Netflix Taiwan" is the Taiwan channel.
- **Netflix `netflix.com/tw/title/<id>`** links are given so readers can click through. They are **not** proof, because Netflix serves those pages by requester IP. IDs come from the `netflix.com/title/<id>` link in a Netflix Taiwan YouTube description or from search results. Netflix title IDs are global.
- **Disney+ `/zh-tw/browse/entity-…`** pages count as the service's own Taiwan catalog pages (fetched, showing the TW title and a rating).
- **friDay影音 / Hami Video / LINE TV** title pages count when they load with the show (HTTP 200 and the show's title in `<title>`). friDay「適用方案：免費」= free ad-supported tier; 「影劇」= paid drama pack. Hami pages show a 「下架時間」 (licence end date), quoted where present. A friDay page that returns HTTP 400 「資料錯誤」, a LINE TV page that says 「此戲劇因授權到期，已下架」, a Hami page that no longer shows the title, or a 公視+ page that returns 404 counts as **lapsed**. The LINE TV check was calibrated on a known-lapsed page (`linetv.tw/drama/14287`, 代理公司), which does contain 「授權到期」.
- **myVideo**: every myVideo page fetched this session (including titles that are probably current) contains 「本影片因授權因素 暫時無法提供觀看」. That is most likely served to our non-Taiwan IP, so myVideo pages are **inconclusive** and are not used as proof either way.
- **KKTV** ended service on 2026-01-01 and moved its catalogue to LINE TV (KKTV announcement https://events.kktv.me/tw/announcement-165; LINE TV list of moved titles, updated 2026-01-22: https://help.linetv.tw/hc/zh-tw/articles/53686460728217). `kktv.me` links are no longer counted. Being on the move list does not prove a title is still up: *Last Man* and *昨日的美食* S1 are on LINE TV but now say 「授權到期」.
- **Taiwan media** means a Taiwan outlet that names the Taiwan platform, with its date.
- **Linear TV** (緯來日本台, 國興衛視, WakuWaku Japan, 愛爾達) is noted where seen but does not count as streaming.
- **JustWatch TW** was tried as a lead (search for the TW pages) but no JustWatch TW page surfaced for these titles, so no row relies on it.
