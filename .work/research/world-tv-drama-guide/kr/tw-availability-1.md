# Korean dramas, batch 1: where to watch legally in Taiwan (checked 2026-11-08)

Scope: the 40 shows in `kr/tw-batch-1.tsv`. For each one, this file records the official Taiwan title, the Taiwan platform(s) with a title-page link, and the evidence. We have no Taiwan IP, and no proxy or bot-check workaround was used.

Evidence rules (same as `us/tw-availability-official.md`):
- **YouTube**: watch pages were read with Groundlane `web_extract`. The title came from `meta[name=title]`, the channel from `link[itemprop=name]` and the date from `meta[itemprop=datePublished]`. "Netflix Taiwan" is the Taiwan channel. "Netflix Malaysia" (`@netflixasia`) is Netflix's Traditional-Chinese Asia channel, which links to Netflix Taiwan. It is official but not Taiwan-only, and the table marks it "Asia zh channel".
- **Netflix `netflix.com/tw/title/<id>`** links are given so readers can click through. They are **not** proof, because Netflix serves those pages by requester IP. Most IDs came from the `netflix.com/title/<id>` link in the Netflix Taiwan YouTube description, from JustWatch TW, or from search results. Netflix title IDs are global.
- **Disney+ `/zh-tw/browse/entity-…`** pages count as the service's own Taiwan catalog pages. Each one was fetched and showed the TW title and a Taiwan rating (15+).
- **friDay影音 / Hami Video** title pages count when they load with the show (HTTP 200). friDay's 「適用方案：免費」 means the free, ad-supported tier; 「影劇」 is the paid drama pack. A page that returns 400/404 or says 「授權到期／授權因素」 counts as **lapsed**.
- **Prime Video `zh_TW`** pages are language pages, not Taiwan pages. They are given as links only.
- **Taiwan media** means a Taiwan outlet that names the Taiwan platform. Listicles that say 「Netflix…韓劇」 count, with their date.
