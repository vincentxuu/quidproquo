# Korean dramas, batch 2: where to watch legally in Taiwan (as of 2026-11-08)

Source list: `kr/tw-batch-2.tsv` (39 shows). Evidence rules follow `us/tw-availability-official.md`:

- No Taiwan IP, no proxy, no bot-check workaround.
- **Netflix `netflix.com/tw/title/<id>` links are given for readers only. They are not proof**, because Netflix serves those pages by requester IP. Netflix availability is proved with an official Netflix YouTube upload (title, channel and date read with Groundlane `web_extract` from `meta[name=title]`, `link[itemprop=name]`, `meta[itemprop=datePublished]`) or with Taiwanese media. "Netflix Taiwan" is the TW-only channel. "Netflix Malaysia" (`@netflixasia`) is Netflix's zh-Hant Asia channel, which links to Netflix Taiwan; it is official but not TW-only.
- Netflix title IDs come from JustWatch TW offer links. Where Netflix's own trailer description links a different ID (+1), the JustWatch ID is kept. Both are noted.
- **Disney+ `/zh-tw/browse/entity-…`** and **HBO Max `hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/…`** pages count as Taiwan catalog pages. Every one cited here was fetched on 2026-11-08 and returned the zh-TW title plus a TW-style age rating. Two HBO Max TW URLs (德魯納酒店, 天空之城) returned the HBO Max error page 「哎唷！」. Those shows are treated as no longer on HBO Max TW.
- **Taiwan-only platform pages** (friDay, Hami Video, LINE TV, myVideo) count when the title page loads with the show and has no takedown notice.
- **JustWatch TW** (`justwatch.com/tw/節目/…`, checked 2026-11-08) is a lead only. Its page titles are in Simplified Chinese, so they were never used for the official TW title.
