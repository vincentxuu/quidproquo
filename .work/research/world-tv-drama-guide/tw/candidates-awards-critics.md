# Taiwanese TV (台劇) candidates — awards juries (A) + critics polls / year-end lists (B)

Collected 2026-11-13 for the zh-TW 「各國影劇導讀」 series (Taiwan). Raw data only; no curation applied. Scope: Taiwanese-produced scripted series (terrestrial/cable incl. 公視・公視台語台・客家電視台・大愛, and Taiwanese streaming originals on Netflix / CATCHPLAY+ / friDay / LINE TV / myVideo / Hami / GagaOOLala), mainly 2016–2026. Taiwan–foreign co-productions are kept but flagged `[co-pro]`. TV films (電視電影／人生劇展), single dramas, short anthology films, animation, kids, variety/non-scripted, documentaries and non-Taiwanese productions found in the sources are in 2b so nothing is silently dropped.

**金鐘獎 counts as A.** The Golden Bell Awards are organised by the Ministry of Culture's 影視及流行音樂產業局, but nominees and winners are decided by an independent jury of industry practitioners (the 59th-ceremony page lists the 戲劇類 jury: 主任委員 薛聖棻; 戲劇節目 panel 高慧君、李志薔、林君陽…; 迷你劇集/電視電影 panel 林孝謙、呂蒔媛…). The government does not pick the winners, so the award is treated as a jury award. This is unlike the Chinese state awards (飛天獎 etc.) excluded in the CN round.

**Year conventions** (read before using tags):
- `GB` (金鐘獎): tag = **ceremony year** (第51屆 = 2016 … 第61屆 = 2026, held 2026-10). Only the two program awards were extracted: 戲劇節目獎 (`drama`) and 迷你劇集獎 (`mini`; in 2016 the combined 迷你劇集／電視電影獎). Nominee rows that are not wins are `nom`. Individual awards (acting, directing, writing, craft) were **not collected** as separate tags; where a read source mentions them they appear in notes only.
- `AACA` (Asian Academy Creative Awards): ceremony year. National = Taiwan national winner, Grand = Asia-Pacific grand winner. Best Drama Series national winners were extracted for 2018–2024 (Wikipedia) plus 2025–2026. **All** Taiwan fiction categories were read only for 2024 and 2025 (official pages). Grand winners in the major acting and single-drama categories were read for 2018–2025. 2026 = national round only (grand final is Dec 2026); only Best Drama Series and the acting nods named in the press release were extracted.
- `SDA` (Seoul International Drama Awards): ceremony year.
- `GOTT` (Busan Asia Contents Awards / Global OTT Awards; called International Streaming Festival in 2025): ceremony year. Wins and nominations from Wikipedia year pages (2023, 2024, 2026) + winners table (2019–2026).
- `SM` (Series Mania, Lille): festival year. **These are competition selections, not wins.** Counted as a jury signal because programmers chose them, but flagged.
- B tags use the year of the list's publication/coverage. `KM2025 #3` = rank in the 台劇 category of the 2025 「台灣劇評人年度最佳電視／串流劇集排行榜」.

**Distinct sources** = number of distinct source keys a show appears in. Keys (12): A — `GB`, `AACA`, `SDA`, `GOTT`, `SM`; B — `VAR-Intl2021` (Variety Best International TV Shows 2021), `ECON2023` (The Economist, best TV of 2023), `LINE2024` / `LINE2025` (LINE TODAY 聚光賞 十大追劇榜, voted by a panel of media/critics/KOLs), `KM2025` (今敏木木-run 台灣劇評人年度榜, 72 critic/reviewer accounts voting, 台劇 top 10), `TNL2023` (徐佑德, 關鍵評論網「2023年度五大最佳台劇」), `500輯2022` (影評人鄭秉泓, 「三部關鍵台劇」 2019–2021). Several placements inside one key count once. Seasons are merged (我們與惡的距離 + II; 誰是被害者 + 2; 俗女養成記 + 2; 我的婆婆怎麼那麼可愛 + 2; 麻醉風暴 + 2).

**Metadata**: Chinese titles and producers come from the 金鐘 pages; English titles come from AACA, Global OTT, SDA, Focus Taiwan, Variety and KM2025 where shown. `†` = the English↔Chinese mapping or the English title is **general knowledge or comes from a search snippet; verify**. **All first-air years are unsourced** (approx. the year before the 金鐘 ceremony). `—` = no English title known/checked.

## 3. Could not access / caveats

- **toolDegradation**: (1) Groundlane `web_fetch` / `web_extract` / `web_search` was the primary tool. It handled zh/en Wikipedia (`action=raw`), the AACA official pages, Focus Taiwan, Variety, IndieWire, TheWrap, yearendlists and konsatoshi631012.com. Its search reported `tavily unavailable` / `exa unavailable` / `brave unavailable` on some calls and fell back to brave/you. Several fetches failed mid-run with "upstream closed the stream" (focustaiwan 2025-08-25, gol-studios, the first MOC attempt). The MOC and The News Lens pages hit a browser challenge, and Taipei Times returned 403. (2) Exa `web_search_exa` was used for Series Mania / Berlinale / Canneseries, the NYT/Variety lists, the Taiwanese critics' lists and the MOC page (via highlights). Exa `web_fetch_exa` worked once (The News Lens, paywall-truncated). jina, Tavily and firecrawl were not needed; terminal curl was not used.
- **金鐘**: the program awards are complete for 2016–2026 from the two list pages. The ceremony pages for 第51–58, 60 and 61屆 were not opened individually, so individual-award data exists only for 2024.
- **International Emmy**: not properly checked for Taiwan (only search results, which showed no Taiwanese nominee). The 44th–54th ceremony pages need a pattern search for "Taiwan"/"TWN".
- **AACA**: Taiwan-national non-drama-series categories for 2018–2023 and the full 2026 Taiwan list are missing. The Grand Final of December 2026 has not happened yet.
- **GOTT**: categories for many 2023/2024/2026 nominee rows are unresolved, and so is Buffy Chen's 2023 win category. For the 2019–2022 Busan Asia Contents Awards only the winners table was read; no nominee pages.
- **Asian Television Awards, Busan ACA nominee lists 2019–2022, Canneseries archives, ATF**: not read.
- **Taiwanese critics' lists are thin.** No year-end series list was found from 釀電影, 鏡週刊, 聯合報〈娛樂有評〉, 報導者, 天下, VERSE or 台灣影評人協會 (which covers films).
