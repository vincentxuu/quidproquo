### 🟢 Confirmed

1. 〈3 Techniques Crucial For Crack Success〉：L 形、手變薄放鬆、"many people start making a hand jam shape before it actually enters the crack"；插到約手腕深；拇指收向掌心中央、推向小指；薄手（paddle）"crimp with the index and middle finger to enable that thumb to get deeper … Push against the crack wall with the outside of your thumb"。
2. 〈The one way to truly rest on a hand jam〉：70/30 規則，"The pressure from your fingers should be minimal, it is there for the extra 20-30% … you should be able to hang easily off the jam without the opposing pressure of the fingers"；手指痛是手指壓太多、拇指不夠。
3. 김종식「L・인・썸・팁」：엄지를 세워서 L자 → 크랙에 손날을 집어넣는다（인）→ 엄지를 구부리면 이 부분 근육이 딱딱 → 손가락 끝을 바위면에 대고 잠근다（팁）。
4. Geek Climber：2:10「not have them too close to your chest … arms kind of go out to the side … end up leaning back」；4:42–5:19「The feet felt at least two times more painful than the hands」、drop heel。
5. Ellison〈How to Hand Jam〉（2023-03-13）："Thumbs-down is great for placing gear or trying to shake out and rest. For the most efficient upward progress, thumbs-up is the way to go."；拇指朝下 "can't reach as high"。
6. UKC Wild Country Crack School Part 2："if you're looking to make an extra long reach from a hand jam, then you will almost certainly want to use a thumbs-up jam"；"how overlapping the edges of the crack are or whether the crack lies in a corner … almost impossible to cross hand-over-hand … one hand stays on the bottom in a 'thumbs-up' … the other leads in a 'thumbs-down'"。
7. Achey〈Jam Session〉（2012）：thin hands 三要訣 "twisting techniques such as thumbs-down jams; meticulous footwork …; letting go of the idea that the jam should feel secure"；cups 指尖＋掌根一側、只有指節背面頂另一側，"one sharp point of contact … Tape over the knuckles is highly recommended"。
8. Jewell〈How to Climb Wide-hand Cracks〉（2023-08-09）："Cup your hand with your fingers straight, tips pointing into the crack. Then twist towards your pinky (clockwise for your right hand, counterclockwise for your left, regardless of whether your hand is thumb up or thumb down …)"。
9. MP thumb-up 串："I avoid thumbs down whenever possible. It is harder anatomically, and limits movement per reach/move … with practice thumbs up is more efficient. Except in corners."
10. MP rattly 串："avoid cupping hands … bruising pressure on the bones on the back of the hand. Rather prefer thumbs up and roll the thumb laterally"。
11. MP sustained 串：多人 Supercrack 兩手拇指朝上；也有人放保護／休息才用上手朝下；一人說 wide hands、尤其 Supercrack 這類深縫，幾乎都用兩拇指相對平移。
12. Kristiansund 電子報：#0.75/#1 "Smaller hands get perfect hand jams … larger hands are forced into thin or paddle jams"；#2 "Larger hands get a basic, solid hand jam. Smaller hands often have to use a cupped hand jam"。
13. Indian Creek 十誡：大手男性開線、手掌 5.10、0.75／#1 多 5.11。
14. Chouinard：MP 仁壽峰頁 "Y. Chouinard has a route here from the 1960s"；中央日報〈Special Knowledge 73 인수봉〉：1963 年 9 月駐韓美軍 Yvon Chouinard 與선우중옥、이강오開〈취나드 A〉。
