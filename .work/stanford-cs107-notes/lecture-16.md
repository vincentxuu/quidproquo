## Agenda and evidence

- Data-size suffixes and subregisters: byte, word, long and quad operations.
- Sign- and zero-extension via `movs` and `movz`; writing a 32-bit register zeroes the upper half.
- `lea` computes an effective address without reading memory and is also used as arithmetic.
- Special-purpose registers and reverse-engineering exercises.
- Unary/binary arithmetic and logic, then shift instructions and their operand constraints.
