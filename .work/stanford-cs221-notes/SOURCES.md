- Course schedule and assignments: https://stanford-cs221.github.io/autumn2025/

## Per-lecture access

Lectures 1–16 and 18 have executable Python artifacts in the repository. Lecture 17 has `language_models.pdf`. Lecture 19 is linked by the repository as a Google Slides deck titled “Economics of AI,” while the course schedule calls the session “AI Supply Chains”; both names must be reported. Lecture 20 has no repository artifact, but its Stanford Online recording is public: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5u5I5jvWR5k.
