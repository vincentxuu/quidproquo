## Agenda evidence

- The course decomposes intelligence into perception, reasoning, action, and learning, all constrained by computation and information. This replaces a human-imitation definition with one that can be analyzed through problems, resources, and decision quality.
- The history artifact follows symbolic, neural, and statistical traditions. Its purpose is not to crown one winner, but to show how abstractions trade interpretability, data requirements, and computational cost.
- The tensor artifact moves from scalars, vectors, and matrices to batches, sequences, and image shapes. It establishes the shared data language used throughout the course.
