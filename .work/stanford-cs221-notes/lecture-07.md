## Agenda evidence

- An MDP adds transition probabilities and rewards to a search problem; a policy chooses an action for every state rather than returning one fixed path.
- The Bellman equation expresses state value as immediate reward plus discounted future value. Value iteration repeatedly applies this backup.
- Discounting controls both the weight of distant rewards and computational convergence. It is part of the problem definition, not merely a numerical trick.
