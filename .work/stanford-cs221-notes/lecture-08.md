## Agenda evidence

- Reinforcement learning replaces a known MDP model with experience from interaction; the agent observes only reward and next state after acting.
- Q-learning updates state-action values with a bootstrapped target and does not require known transition probabilities.
- Epsilon-greedy alternates exploration and exploitation; too little exploration misses good actions, while too much keeps paying opportunity cost.
