## Agenda evidence

- TD learning updates values from experienced transitions, using the next-state estimate as part of its target.
- In simultaneous games, neither player observes the other's action first, so strategies are distributions; the minimax theorem connects optimal guarantees in zero-sum games.
- In non-zero-sum games, Nash equilibrium describes profiles where no player benefits from a unilateral deviation, but equilibria need not be unique.
