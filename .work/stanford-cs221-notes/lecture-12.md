## Agenda evidence

- Probabilistic inference can be viewed as conditioning, marginalizing, and normalizing a joint distribution.
- A Bayesian network represents conditional dependencies with a directed graph and factors a huge joint into smaller local conditional tables.
- Factorization saves representation cost, but answering a query can remain expensive; compact representation does not guarantee easy inference.
