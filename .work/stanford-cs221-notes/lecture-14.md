## Agenda evidence

- With fully observed variables, conditional probabilities follow from counting matching events and normalizing.
- Laplace smoothing prevents unseen events from receiving zero probability and introduces a prior-like perspective beyond raw maximum likelihood.
- With latent variables, EM alternates posterior estimation and parameter updates; it does not decrease likelihood but need not find a global optimum.
