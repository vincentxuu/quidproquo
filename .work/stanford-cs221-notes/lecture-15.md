## Agenda evidence

- Propositional logic separates syntax from semantics: a formula is symbolic structure, while a model assigns truth values to atoms.
- A knowledge base entails a proposition when every model satisfying the KB also satisfies it, a stronger claim than truth in one example.
- Ask and Tell operations reduce to satisfiability, turning a general SAT solver into a logical inference engine.
