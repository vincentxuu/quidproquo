## Agenda evidence

- First-order logic adds objects, predicates, functions, and quantifiers, allowing rules to range over entities without repetition.
- Universal and existential quantification bind variables differently; terms and formulas are distinct types and cannot be interchanged.
- Greater expressiveness makes inference harder. In language-to-logic translation, quantifier scope and negation placement often fail before the solver does.
