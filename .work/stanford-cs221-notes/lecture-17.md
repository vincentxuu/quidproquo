## Agenda evidence

- A language model uses the chain rule to factor a sequence's joint probability into next-token conditional probabilities.
- Training uses next-token cross-entropy, while generation must select tokens from the model distribution; temperature and sampling policies change diversity.
- Fluent prediction does not guarantee sourced knowledge. The probabilistic objective, prompt context, and external tools address different limitations.
