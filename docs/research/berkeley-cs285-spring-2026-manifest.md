## Compute and missing-asset ledger

| Work | Official compute signal | Self-study label |
| --- | --- | --- |
| HW1 imitation learning | README says local laptop CPU was faster in testing; Modal likely unnecessary | CPU-first; GPU optional |
| HW2 policy gradients | PDF says seconds to at most about ten minutes per run on a modern laptop CPU | CPU-first |
| HW3 DQN/SAC | Most tasks are locally feasible; MsPacman and HalfCheetah may take about three hours even on GPU | GPU recommended for two expensive tasks |
| HW4 LLM RL | Official wrapper defaults to a Modal H100 and requires four training runs | H100-class cloud path; do not call free |
| HW5 offline RL | A run may take up to about six hours; tuning across tasks/algorithms accumulates quickly | GPU/cloud strongly recommended |
| Final project | Two public default options and outline are available; course-only credits, staff help, Ed, Gradescope, and bCourses remain unavailable | Scope down or bring a separate budget |
