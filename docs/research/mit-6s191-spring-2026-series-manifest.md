# MIT 6.S191 Spring 2026 series manifest

Drafted: 2026-08-22

## Scope and source policy

- Main edition: Spring 2026 only; coverage is nine lectures and three software labs.
- The existing bilingual overview is order 1; the new bilingual units are orders 2–13.
- Schedule, titles, slides, and videos come from the [official 2026 course site](https://introtodeeplearning.com/).
- Labs use the [`2026` branch of the official repository](https://github.com/MITDeepLearning/introtodeeplearning/tree/2026), not the moving `master` branch.
- The lecture entries are study guides, not claims that every recording was watched end to end. Image-based slide decks are described at course-outline level; no 2025 content is used to fill gaps.

- No assessment of teaching quality or spoken claims absent from official artifacts.
