- 2026-08-06: post(learning) hybrid paper+digital study methods (zh + en). Four
  research rounds; I retracted two of my own claims mid-way, both recorded in
  the article. (a) "Voyer minus Flanigan = distraction" was invalid — Flanigan's
  inclusion criteria are unrelated to distraction and admit lab video studies;
  the defensible version is that all four meta-analyses ORDER by how much the
  design separates medium from distraction (Allen d≈0.25 → Flanigan g=0.248 →

  strong — Flanigan puts conceptual smallest, Urry puts it largest, neither
  significant. Strongest evidence is the READING literature (Delgado 171,055
  participants g=-0.21; scrolling moderator 0.35-0.48 vs 0.03-0.12), not
  note-taking. Read 7 of 8 core papers in full; only Voyer's body text stays
  paywalled. Device prices from official stores only (reMarkable Connect is
  $3.99/mo, not the $2.99 SEO sites print). Added 7 site-wide glossary terms:
