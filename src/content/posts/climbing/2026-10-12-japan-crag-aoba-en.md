Aoba (青葉, あおば; the local tourism site calls it 青葉の岩場, "Aoba crag") sits in Hiwatari, Joban-Yumoto-machi, Iwaki City, Fukushima Prefecture. The [Iwaki tourism site](https://kankou-iwaki.or.jp/spot/52122) places it "in a corner of the Tokiwadai forest reserve between JR Yumoto and Uchigo stations" (JR湯本駅と内郷駅の間にあるときわ台生活環境保全林の一角). The rock is sandstone, the climbing is mostly bolted sport, and there is one small boulder sector.

Size is not the selling point. [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E9%9D%92%E8%91%89/) says outright that most routes are 5–6 m and that the crag hardly looks high quality (ほとんどのルートが5、6メートル。とても質の高い岩場とは思えない). Then it names the real draw: winter warmth. On a clear winter day you can climb in a T-shirt and wonder whether you are really in Tohoku. Flat bases, short walks between sectors and short routes mean you can tick ten or twenty routes in a day.

## What kind of crag is it

| Item | Detail | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Location | Hiwatari, Joban-Yumoto-machi, Iwaki City, Fukushima | [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E9%9D%92%E8%91%89/), [Iwaki tourism site](https://kankou-iwaki.or.jp/spot/52122) |
| Rock | Sandstone | CLIMBING-net, Iwaki tourism site, [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/200601664/aoba) |
| Wall height | "Up to a little over 10 m; most routes 5–6 m" | CLIMBING-net |
| Wall height (other source) | "About 5–10 m" | Iwaki tourism site |
| Angle | 90–120° | CLIMBING-net |
| Elevation | Hiroba sector about 115 m; parking about 31 m | [ROCKTOPO Hiroba sector](https://www.rocktopo.net/rocks/74) |
| Development | From around 1988, by members of the local Iwaki Alpine Club (石城山岳会) and the late Mitsunori Shiga | CLIMBING-net |

On rock quality, CLIMBING-net says it is "hard to call it hard" (お世辞にも硬いとはいえず). [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/200601664/aoba) warns that "sometime the route grades change with broken pieces" and that grading is "in general softer than other crags".

### Route counts and grade spread (checked 2026-10-12)

The sites disagree, so each is listed separately:

- **[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/73)**: 100 lines, 85 sport (face) and 15 boulders. Sport by grade: 5.2+: 14, 5.10a+: 30, 5.11a+: 29, 5.12a+: 10, 5.13a+: 2. Boulders range from 10-kyu to 3-dan.
- **[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E9%9D%92%E8%91%89/)**: "60+ routes", broken down as up to 5.9: 8, 5.10s: 28, 5.11s: 29, 5.12s: 10, 5.13s: 1. That breakdown adds up to 76, more than the headline figure and closer to ROCKTOPO.
- **[Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/200601664/aoba)**: only 5 routes entered.

ROCKTOPO splits the crag into seven sectors ([route list](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/73/routes)):

| Sector | Type and count | Difficulty (ROCKTOPO: E easy / M moderate / H hard) |
|---|---|---|
| 北のエリア (North) | Sport 13 | E 8, M 5 |
| 日だまりエリア (Hidamari) | Sport 7, boulders 2 | E 3, M 5, H 1 |
| ボルダーエリア (Boulder) | Boulders 13 | E 6, M 3, H 4 |
| 広場エリア (Hiroba) | Sport 14 | M 8, H 6 |
| 展望台エリア (Tenbodai) | Sport 15 | E 8, M 6, H 1 |
| 南のエリア2 (South 2) | Sport 19 | E 8, M 10, H 1 |
| 南のエリア (South) | Sport 17 | E 1, M 12, H 4 |

The [Iwaki tourism site](https://kankou-iwaki.or.jp/spot/52122) lists six sectors (no boulder sector) and says most routes are beginner to intermediate (初級～中級者向けのルートが多いのが特徴です).

Who it suits: the 5.10s and 5.11s make up about 70% of the sport routes (59 of 85 on ROCKTOPO), packed into short lines, which makes Aoba a good place to rack up outdoor leads in winter. The hardest is 絆 (Kizuna) in the South sector, graded 5.13c/d on ROCKTOPO, so there is little here for climbers hunting hard long-term projects.

## When to go

| Source | Season |
|---|---|
| [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/73) | Season field lists January–May and September–December |
| [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E9%9D%92%E8%91%89/) | "September–May" (9月~5月) |
| [Iwaki tourism site](https://kankou-iwaki.or.jp/spot/52122) | "Sunny, so it is warm even in winter and you can climb in short sleeves" (日当たりが良いため冬場でも暖かく、半袖でクライミングが楽しめる) |
| [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/200601664/aoba) | "a good choice for winter climbing. It is sunny although sometime it is windy and chilly" |

In short:

- **September to May**: both crag databases agree, with winter as the core season. The reason is sun; the tourism site and CLIMBING-net both mention short sleeves in winter.
- **Windy winter days are cold**: Mountain Project notes it is sometimes windy and chilly.
- **North sector**: the developers' [North sector topo](http://japan-alps.jp/gensdarmes/iwaki/Topo/Aoba_NorthArea_20200510.pdf) says it gets good sun even in winter and has no seepage (冬でも日当たりはよく、しみ出しもありません).
- **June to August**: left out by both databases, but none of the sources read for this post says why.
- **Autumn on the trails**: the tourism site says the かえでの路 (maple path) turns red from late November to early December, and the approach path has ginkgo color in autumn.

## Getting there

### By car (the main option)

- **Interchange**: **Iwaki-Yumoto IC** on the Joban Expressway. The [Iwaki tourism site](https://kankou-iwaki.or.jp/spot/52122) says about 8 minutes from the IC; [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E9%9D%92%E8%91%89/) says about 10 minutes "but a little hard to find".
- **CLIMBING-net's directions**: head toward Yumoto Station and get onto Route 6, turn left, pass the Coal and Fossil Museum (石炭化石館) and a 7-Eleven, turn left at the next unmarked railway crossing, then right at the gas tank.
- **Parking (tourism site)**: use the lot next to the former toilet (10 cars) and the lot beside 青葉弁天池 pond (3 cars). When both are full, **on weekends and public holidays only**, a landowner lends the plot next to the かえでの路 entrance, and only "the left half from the entrance" (入口から左半分のみ). Parking near the roadside entrances is prohibited (道路脇の出入り口付近は駐車禁止です).
- **Parking (other sources)**: the [ROCKTOPO parking page](https://www.rocktopo.net/parking_spots/210) says about 10 cars; CLIMBING-net says you can park beside the pond and on both sides of the materials yard, taking care not to get in the way.
- None of the sources read mention a parking fee.

### By public transport

- The nearest station is **JR Yumoto**. The tourism site gives "about 7 minutes by car" from the station, with no walking time or bus.
- [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/200601664/aoba): "Perhaps it is not convenient if there is no car".
- No source found for a bus to the crag. Without a car, check taxi or bus options from Yumoto Station before you go.

### Approach

- CLIMBING-net: start on the walking path, then branch onto a climbers' trail; about 15 minutes to the Hiroba sector.
- The Iwaki Alpine Club's [North sector page](https://iwakialpineclub.wixstudio.com/iwakisangakukai/post/%E9%9D%92%E8%91%89-%E5%8C%97%E3%81%AE%E3%82%A8%E3%83%AA%E3%82%A2-%E3%82%AF%E3%83%A9%E3%82%A4%E3%83%9F%E3%83%B3%E3%82%B0%E3%82%B9%E3%83%9D%E3%83%83%E3%83%88): for the North sector, start at the gazebo by the park pond and follow the しらうめの路 path and red tape up the east slope opposite the pond, about 10 minutes.
- ROCKTOPO's Hiroba sector page: 295 m straight-line from parking, 84 m of elevation gain.

| | By car | By public transport |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Iwaki-Yumoto IC, about 8–10 min | JR Yumoto Station, about 7 min by car |
| Parking | Former-toilet lot 10, pond lot 3; landowner's plot (left half) only on weekends/holidays when both are full | No bus information in sources |
| To the walls | About 15 min to Hiroba; about 10 min to North | — |
| No parking | Near the roadside entrances | — |

## Rules and local practice

- **Park only in the designated spots**: follow the tourism site's three-tier rule above; no parking near the entrances. The landowner's plot is weekends and holidays only, only when both lots are full, and only the left half.
- **Most routes finish by mantling onto the top**: CLIMBING-net says that on most routes you stand up on top of the rock after clipping the anchor, and on some that move is the crux (ルートによってはその部分が核心となっているものもある). Only routes marked * after the grade finish at the clip. Mountain Project agrees: "Most routes require to mantle at the end, after the anchor clipping, which is often the crux."
- **Build your own anchor in the North sector**: the [North sector topo](http://japan-alps.jp/gensdarmes/iwaki/Topo/Aoba_NorthArea_20200510.pdf) says every route tops out and the anchors are bare chemical bolts. Set them up yourself with slings, carabiners or quickdraws before climbing and remove them afterward.
- **Bring a small wrench**: the same topo notes that the sandstone wears the bolt nuts loose, so a small wrench is essential (小型のレンチは必携品です).
- **No camping**: CLIMBING-net says camping at Tokiwadai sports park, used in the past, is prohibited (以前利用されていた、ときわ台運動公園でのキャンプは禁止).
- **Rockfall**: CLIMBING-net records that entry was banned right after the Great East Japan Earthquake and that the crag was back in use as of May 2012. Part of the South sector between 青葉ビバーク and 猿の撹乱 had collapsed, and the approach had several collapsed spots needing detours. That is the 2012 picture; judge current conditions on site.
- **At your own risk**: both the tourism site and the Iwaki Alpine Club state that you climb at your own risk; the club advises beginners to always go with experienced climbers (かならず経験者と同行する).
- **JFA**: the Fukushima section of the [JFA access issues list](https://freeclimb.jp/activities/deal-with/issue-list/) does not list Aoba (checked 2026-10-12).

Registration and fees: none of the CLIMBING-net, ROCKTOPO, Iwaki tourism site or Iwaki Alpine Club pages read for this post mention registration or a fee.

## Notable sectors and routes

ROCKTOPO's popular routes ([Aoba page](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/73), 2026-10-12):

- **両手にヘビ (5.9)**: South 2.
- **シーラカンス (5.10b)**: South 2.
- **バラ色のエアメール (5.11b)**: South.

The ROCKTOPO [popular routes page](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/73/popular_routes/all) also lists カエルの喉チンコ (5.11c) in Tenbodai, and 野獣死す (5.12a) and 猿の撹乱 (5.12d) in South.

**North sector**: in December 2019 and February–April 2020, Ryogo Konno, Kenta Takakura and Mitsuyuki Kobayashi of "Team Gendarme" cleaned up and added routes ([topo](http://japan-alps.jp/gensdarmes/iwaki/Topo/Aoba_NorthArea_20200510.pdf)). Walls are about 8 m and 75–95°, with 13 routes from 5.7 to the 5.11s; the topo calls it a beginner-oriented sector. The Iwaki Alpine Club also runs rope-work courses here. Grades differ from ROCKTOPO in places, for example ひねくれ者 is 5.10c on the topo and 5.11b on ROCKTOPO; the topo itself says grades will be reviewed (グレードは今後、見直す予定).
