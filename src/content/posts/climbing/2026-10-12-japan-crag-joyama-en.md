> 🌏 [中文版](/posts/climbing/2026-10-12-japan-crag-joyama)

Joyama (城山, じょうやま; listed on theCrag as Jo-yama) is a rocky hill on the Kano River in Izunokuni, Shizuoka Prefecture, 342 m high according to [Izunokuni City](https://www.city.izunokuni.shizuoka.jp/kankou/documents/shizen_keikan.pdf). People have been climbing its river-facing walls since 1960. Today it is a large, mostly bolted sport crag with a good number of multi-pitch lines: [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/10) lists 362 routes across 18 sectors.

The crag is close to Ohito Station on the Izuhakone Railway, so you can walk to the trailhead. Driving is harder because of parking: the trailhead has room for only a few cars, and the riverside park car park below the crag has opening hours and only takes park visitors (see "Getting there").

This guide helps you decide whether Joyama suits your grade, which months to go, how to get there without a car, where to park, and which sector to start in at each of the four grade bands.

| Rock | Tuff (凝灰岩) | CLIMBING-net, ROCKTOPO; theCrag and Mountain Project also say tuff |
| Angle | 70–100 degrees | CLIMBING-net |

The climbing sites all call the rock tuff. On how the hill formed, the Izu Peninsula Geopark sign describes Joyama as a "volcanic neck" (火山の根, 火山岩頸): magma that solidified in the conduit of a submarine volcano, exposed after the outer layers eroded away. The sign adds that "the precipitous cliff fronting on the Kanogawa River is a consecrated place for rock-climbing cherished by many climbers."

### History

From the [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E5%9F%8E%E5%B1%B1/) overview:

- 1960: first ascent of 三日月ハング (Mikazuki Hang), followed by many aid routes.
- 1979–81: many of those routes were freed, led by Naoki Toda (戸田直樹).
- The longest-running and most active developer has been Makoto Kokubun (國分誠) of Team RP. His large-scale development of the Wild Boar Gorge (ワイルドボアゴージ) "wiped out the image" that Joyama simply meant the South Face slabs.
- The North Face is still being developed.
- Under the JFA crag maintenance program, most routes at the base of the South Face, plus Mikazuki Hang and the Direct Route, were rebolted with stainless chemical anchors.

ROCKTOPO's list of main developers names 國分誠, 寺田正造, 岩井純一, 坂本宏和 and 宮崎秀夫.

### Route counts and grades (checked 2026-10-12)

The four sites give very different numbers:

- **[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/10)**: 362 routes, 334 single-pitch face routes and 28 multi-pitch.
  - Single-pitch: 5.2+ 38, 5.10a+ 124, 5.11a+ 112, 5.12a+ 54, 5.13a+ 3, 5.14a+ 3.
  - Multi-pitch: 5.2+ 3, 5.10a+ 17, 5.11a+ 6, 5.12a+ 2.
- **[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E5%9F%8E%E5%B1%B1/)**: "約300本" (about 300), tagged "初心者・中級者OK" (fine for beginners and intermediates).
- **[theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/japan/joyama)**: 183 routes entered; the description says "over 300 bolted sportsclimbing routes graded range of 5.7 to 5.14," citing "Japan 100 Climbing area" by KITAYAMA Makoto (Yama-kei Publishers).
- **[Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/107001457/joyama)**: the description says "well over 100 sport routes" from 5.7 to 5.13, but only 19 are entered.

Who it suits: in ROCKTOPO's breakdown, 5.10 and 5.11 routes add up to 236, about 70% of the single-pitch routes. There are still 54 routes in the 5.12 range, but only 3 each at 5.13 and 5.14. Multi-pitch routes cluster at 5.10, so intermediate climbers who want multi-pitch practice have the most to choose from.

The rest are クッキングワールド, ライオンボトム・ライオンサミット, ウェーブロック・エンドレスウォール, ミッドウェーウォール (玉子岩), インナーウォール 1–4, 二間バンド大ハング, ひよこ岩・ねずみ岩 and 西壁.

The hard routes are concentrated in the Wild Boar Gorge (19 rated H, more than its E and M routes combined). Beginners are better off starting on the South Face.

## When to go

| Source | What it says |
|---|---|
| ROCKTOPO | Season field lists January, February, March, November, December |
| CLIMBING-net | "9月下旬~6月上旬" (late September to early June) |
| theCrag | "Many routes face south and thus allow climbing in winter." |
| Izunokuni City | The vertical south-side walls are "known as a rock climbing spot you can enjoy all year round" (一年中楽しめるロッククライミングのポイント) |

In short:

- **Winter is the main season.** ROCKTOPO lists only November to March, and theCrag says many routes face south and can be climbed in winter.
- **Spring and autumn:** CLIMBING-net's window is wider, from late September to early June.
- **Summer (mid-June to mid-September):** neither ROCKTOPO nor CLIMBING-net includes it, but neither says why, while Izunokuni City says the south walls can be climbed all year. The sources disagree; if you go in summer, judge the sun and heat on the south-facing walls for yourself.
- The same city document includes a comment that the area is a hill "you can enjoy in winter because it does not get snow" (積雪しないため、冬期に楽しめる山).

## Getting there

| | Train | Car |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Ohito Station, Izuhakone Railway | About 40 min from Numazu IC (Japanese Geoparks Network) |
| Parking | — | Joyama trailhead shoulder, about 3 cars; Katsuragiyama trailhead, about 10 cars (shared with hikers) |
| To the trailhead | 20–25 min walk depending on source, or 5 min by taxi | — |
| To the South Face | About 10 min from the trailhead | Same |
| Watch out | — | The Kawa-no-Eki car park locks at 17:00 and is for park visitors only |

### By train

1. Take the Izuhakone Railway Sunzu Line to **Ohito Station**. The [Izunokuni City](https://www.city.izunokuni.shizuoka.jp/kankou/documents/shizen_keikan.pdf) document gives about 30 minutes from Mishima Station.
2. Ohito Station to the trailhead: [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E5%9F%8E%E5%B1%B1/) says a 25-minute walk (5 minutes by taxi), and [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/japan/joyama) also says 25 minutes. The [Japanese Geoparks Network](https://geopark.jp/geopark/izu_hantou/) entry for the Joyama/Katsuragiyama geosite says about 20 minutes on foot.
3. Trailhead to the walls:
   - **South Face:** CLIMBING-net says another 10 minutes. [ROCKTOPO's South Face approach](https://www.rocktopo.net/approach_informations/5): follow the trail uphill from the parking for about ten minutes, then turn right at the climbing route sign.
   - **Wild Boar Gorge (猪谷):** CLIMBING-net says 40 minutes on the hiking trail to the top of the South Face, then 5 minutes down. [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/japan/joyama/area/299848596) says 20–30 minutes from the summit car park along the marked hiking trail; look for the wooden sign on your right.

### By car

- Drive time: theCrag says "40 min drive from Mishima Inter-change"; the Japanese Geoparks Network says about 40 minutes from Numazu IC on the Tomei Expressway.
- **Driving to the Wild Boar Gorge:** CLIMBING-net says to cross the 上島橋 bridge, go straight at the T-junction after the bend in front of the houses (turn left for the South Face), and follow the forest road 2–3 km to a pass, where you can park at the pass or a little beyond. Walk 7–8 minutes to a second pass, turn left, reach the top of the South Face in 10 minutes, then descend 5 minutes.

### Parking

- **Roadside spaces at the Joyama trailhead, about 3 cars** ([ROCKTOPO parking page](https://www.rocktopo.net/parking_spots/332)): "There is room to park on the shoulder at the trailhead. Only a few cars fit, so be careful." This is the access for the South Face.
- **Katsuragiyama trailhead car park, about 10 cars** ([ROCKTOPO parking page](https://www.rocktopo.net/parking_spots/270)): "Park at the Katsuragiyama trailhead for the upper sectors of Joyama. Space is limited and hikers use it too." The [upper-sector approach](https://www.rocktopo.net/approach_informations/6) heads from here toward the summit of Joyama, and the Wild Boar Gorge is reached from the same trail.
- Together that is a dozen or so spaces, and on weekend mornings they fill quickly. Do not improvise a spot on the shoulder when they are full; this is a residential lane that also serves the temple approach.

#### The Kawa-no-Eki Izu Joyama park car park is not a climbers' car park

Below the crag on the riverbank is the Kanogawa Kamishima Park, also called "川の駅 伊豆城山" (Kawa-no-Eki Izu Joyama). [walkerplus](https://summer.walkerplus.com/odekake/detail_e/ar0622e602157/map.html) lists "123台 駐車無料" (123 free spaces), about 3 minutes from Ohito-Chuo IC on the Izu Chuo Road. It is a park car park, though, and the [official site](https://kanogawakamishima.com/enjoy/) sets these rules:

- Parking hours are 9:00–17:00, and "the gate is locked at 17:00, so take your car out before then."
- "Please do not park here unless you are visiting the park," and unauthorized parking is reported to the police.
- The car park may close, or you may be asked to move your car, when the river is expected to rise.
- A [notice dated 2025-08-22](https://kanogawakamishima.com/news/detail.php?p=1953) also asks visitors not to park on the road shoulder.

None of the climbing sources checked for this guide (ROCKTOPO, theCrag, the guidebook) lists this car park for climbers. A day on the South Face usually runs past 17:00, so parking here means packing up early.

## Rules to know before you go

- **Joyama is not on the JFA access-issue list.** As of 2026-10-12, the Shizuoka entries on the [JFA list of areas with access issues](https://freeclimb.jp/activities/deal-with/issue-list/) do not include Joyama, and the [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/10) page has no notice at the top.
- **Registration and fees:** none of the CLIMBING-net, ROCKTOPO, JFA or theCrag pages checked mentions registration or a fee.
- **The hiking trail and the climbing routes are separate.** The Izu Peninsula Geopark [hiking map](https://izugeopark.org/wp/wp-content/uploads/2017/12/n_nakaizu_jokatsuragi_joyama_200_120.pdf) marks the climbing routes "Rock Climbing Routes (Not Hiking course)." Joyama is also a hiking destination and a geosite, so expect hikers below the walls.
- **No public toilet:** CLIMBING-net's 公衆トイレ field says "無" (none).
- **Overnight stays and toilets:** CLIMBING-net says that because there is a cemetery near the trailhead, those with a car are better off beside the ground on the opposite bank of the Kano River; staying at the pass is possible for the Wild Boar Gorge, but "be very careful about relieving yourself." That entry is undated, so check the current rules before staying overnight.
- **Spend locally:** CLIMBING-net asks climbers to keep in mind that this crag sits in a tourist area and to use local restaurants.
- **Emergency numbers:** ambulance and fire **119**, police **110**, coast guard **118** ([City of Yokohama: Calling emergency services](https://www.city.yokohama.lg.jp/lang/residents/en/emergencies/20200221091958448.html)). Joyama sits on the hill behind a residential area, so say which sector you are in (South Face, Wild Boar Gorge, upper area) rather than just "Joyama".
- **Insurance:** check with your insurer that sport climbing, multi-pitch, search and rescue, medical treatment and repatriation are covered. A generic travel policy name is not enough; in Japan mountain rescue costs are in principle borne by the party rescued, subject to your policy and local rules.
- **Local etiquette:** theCrag's general note for Japan: "please ensure you follow the recommendations of local climbers and behave responsibly."

### Beginner: up to 6a (YDS 5.10a and below)

Of the 29 routes on the South Face (Minami kabe), two are 5.8, seven are 5.9 and five are 5.10a, making it the densest sector at this level ([theCrag South Face](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/japan/joyama/area/284553159)). Tube Rock has two short overhanging warm-ups as well, and ROCKTOPO's two most popular routes, バトルランナー (5.10a) and ストーンフリー (5.10c), are in these two sectors. The South Face is also the wall the hiking trail passes, so expect walkers below the routes.

The 5.8–5.9 grades on the South Face are not consistent: とんとん拍子 5.8 is described as stiff while アナザステップ 5.9 is softer. Do not warm up in grade order on a first visit; start with the ones the reports call soft.

For a first visit, Battlerunner is a shorter, easier-to-bail multi-pitch than Excursion, but it does not top out and pitch 2 needs care with the old fixed gear. The 5.8–5.9 single-pitch lines low on the South Face are the simpler way to check your feel for the rock first.

This band is the bulk of Joyama: 103 of the 183 routes theCrag lists fall between 5.10b and 5.11c. The South Face is friction and small pockets and holds all the multi-pitch; the Wild Boar Gorge and Tube Rock are overhanging single-pitch, so the same number means a different climb in each.

Excursion is the only long multi-pitch in this band: 6 pitches and 110 m, fully bolted, hardest pitch 5.10c, but from pitch 3 up you are managing ropes and belays yourself. To practise multi-pitch rhythm it is a better first full-day objective than Battlerunner.

Tube Rock has 11 routes in this band but only 28 ascents across the whole sector; comparable grades on the South Face and in the gorge have more than twice that. The sector is not hard to reach, just farther in and sunny in summer, and in practice much quieter than the numbers suggest.

How to choose: for a first multi-pitch at Joyama, Seinan Kante and スタートレック have the lowest grades, but the first needs a lead-in pitch to reach and the second is new, with sand still on the rock. Battlerunner and Excursion are fully bolted and the easiest lines to follow. 三日月ハング is the historic line; its grade depends on which variation you take, so bring a topo.

### What people who have climbed here say

Written notes on individual Joyama routes are scarce. The usable first-hand reports are MuRa's two posts ([South Face, 2025-05-09](https://blog.muraq.com/post/mt/free_climbing/1641); [new East Face line, 2025-12-08](https://blog.muraq.com/post/mt/free_climbing/2454)) plus short comments on theCrag and Mountain Project. There is almost nothing in Chinese.

**South Face conditions (MuRa, 2025-05-09)**

- Bolts are well placed and mostly replaced with chemical anchors, and the anchors are solid; some lower-offs still lack a fixed carabiner so you have to thread your own rope through the rappel ring, and some multi-pitch anchors have no rappel ring at all. The author suggests carrying a bail sling just in case.
- The base stays shaded by trees, but in summer the wall becomes "like a heated frying pan"; he puts the season at autumn until the start of the rainy season.
- When the car park is full, locals park in the zebra-striped area beyond the cemetery. That is a local habit from a personal blog, not an official space, and not something to copy.
- He gives 15 minutes from the trailhead to the South Face (CLIMBING-net says 10), following the signposted fork toward the climbing routes.

**The new East Face multi-pitch (MuRa, 2025-12-08)**

In December 2025 a party climbed **スタートレック** on the East Face: 6 pitches, hardest 5.8. The author stresses that the grades and lengths are his own estimate and that the topo would be published in ロクスノ.

- Pitches 1–3 are 5.6–5.7 slabs; pitch 4 (5.8, an inverted slab trending left into a vertical face) and pitch 6 (5.8, small roof into slab) are the cruxes.
- Pitch 5 has the lowest number (5.7) but is covered in sand; the author writes it felt like climbing in a desert and was harder than the grade.
- Gear: 10 quickdraws plus 2 alpine draws, one 50 m single rope. Descend the Southeast Ridge in 3 rappels with two 50 m ropes, or walk up past Cooking World to the summit trail.
- The belay is narrow, so rack up at the South Face or Raspberry Face first; the approach includes a short traverse with a fixed rope.

No public topo exists yet, so the route is not in the tables above.

**More first-hand reports**

- [gorotash: こだわりの城山クライミング (2025-11-24)](https://gorotamaru.hatenablog.com/entry/2025/11/24/130417): November on the South Face, a variation of 西南カンテ climbing the 黎明ルート then trending right into a slab line. The author notes the original line's pitches 2+ are "full of loose rock", while this variation is easier and cleaner, and more climbers have been doing it recently. Also mentions climbers trying かさぶた on 二間バンド大ハング.
- [きい山歩: 【城山】2026年初登り！ポコチン大魔王RP (2026-01-06)](https://kiisampo.com/joyama-pokochin-rp/): January 4, 2026 — OVERDRIVE 5.11d flashed (the last of Joyama's "Eleven", author estimates Soft 5.11d), then ポコチン大魔王 5.12d redpointed in 4 attempts. Author's feel: Soft 5.12d, clearly easier than ケレンジ 5.13a but with more endurance. Notes the Wild Boar Gorge was crowded (6-party rotation) on a New Year holiday, while 1/3 had only about 10 people at the whole crag.

**Database comments**

- theCrag's Overdrive page calls it a "Classic Jo-Yama climb"; it is the benchmark line of the Wild Boar Gorge.
- Mountain Project comments on Battlerunner focus on the old bolts and hangers on pitch 2.
- Comments on Excursion focus on "6 pitches, fully bolted", and treat it as the standard first long multi-pitch on the South Face.

**Wild Boar Gorge and right wall (field notes)**

- [nozakimakoto, Jan 2015 (First climbing at Joyama)](https://nozakimakoto.hatenablog.com/entry/2015/01/05/180123): the South Face in January is a slab, about 60° steep, almost all 貼牆 (カンチ, layback-style) holds — "5.8 felt impossible"; he onsighted とんとん拍子 5.8 and アナザステップ 5.9, and tried ステアウェイ 5.10b on the Southeast Face (△). Even with sun, a windy winter day on the South Face gets cold.
- [Kojitusanso staff, April 2014 (Joyama · Wild Boar Gorge)](https://www.kojitusanso.jp/tozan-report/detail/?fm=10787): on a weekday they had the place to themselves, but weekends in this season can mean waiting in line. 『ポコチン大魔王・5.12d』is described as "probably the most popular route in this area" — good holds, varied angles and grades from 5.10 to 5.14. Also: the hanger on the 4th bolt of Liquid Finger is missing.
- theCrag's Jambalaya page: 6 ratings averaging 78/100, with comment keywords "perfect", "nice", "crux".
