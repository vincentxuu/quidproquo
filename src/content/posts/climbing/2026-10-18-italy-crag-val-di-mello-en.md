Val di Mello is in the province of Sondrio in Lombardy, a side valley branching east from the head of Val Masino. [27crags](https://27crags.com/areas/val-di-mello) puts Val di Mello and Val Masino at 800–1,100 m and calls them "the birthplace of bouldering in Italy"; Lombardy's tourism site [in-Lombardia](https://www.in-lombardia.it/en/tourism-in-lombardy/tourism-sondrio/val-di-mello) says it is often compared to Yosemite. The reserve's own site calls it the "Tempio" (temple) of "sassismo", visited by climbers and boulderers from around the world for its historic routes and erratic boulders ([Riserva Naturale Val di Mello](https://riservanaturalevaldimello.org/vivere-la-riserva/)).

This post covers the decisions to make before going: whether it suits you, which months, how to get there, where to park, and the reserve's rules. For seasons across Italy, see the [series overview](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crags-overview-en); for another valley known for cracks and big walls, see [Valle dell'Orco](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crag-valle-dellorco-en) in Piedmont.

## What kind of crag is it

**Three areas.** [UKC's destination guide](https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/page.php?id=9471) splits the area into three:

- **Val Masino**: the main valley, with most of the accommodation and shops, plenty of single-pitch sport and bouldering, and a little multi-pitch and trad.
- **Valle dei Bagni**: a small side valley of Val Masino with a huge bouldering area in the forest; more shaded, and it stays wet longer after rain.
- **Val di Mello**: the larger side valley, a nature reserve, with every kind of climbing.

**Rock and style.** 27crags describes "exceptionally compact Alpine granite" and a style that favours slabs, edges and compression overhangs. [Planetmountain](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/rock-climbing-in-val-di-mello.html) calls the valley "the temple of friction climbing", where smooth slabs lead "ever upwards and distant from the last piece of pro". [UKC Logbook](https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/val_di_mello-18140) agrees that slabs dominate, ranging "from well bolted to fully trad, and sometimes feature very long run-outs"; the harder parts of the big classics follow cracks and corners.

**Bouldering.** This is what the valley is best known for. theCrag's listing for Versante Sud's *Mello Boulder* says the guide covers around 2,800 problems in 33 areas across Mello, Masino, Bagni and Piana del Remenno ([theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/val-di-mello)). UKC's guide says the Val Masino areas (Tarzan, Zocca, Remenno and others) are the most accessible, while Val di Mello itself needs a 30–60 minute walk and has the most scenic bouldering; Il Francese, Proprietà Privata and Casciana Piana fill up on sunny days.

**Sport climbing.** UKC's guide is blunt: "Val di Mello can't really be considered a sport climbing destination". Most modern-style sport routes are on Sasso Remenno in the main Val Masino, well bolted and long, mostly up to 6b+.

**Multi-pitch.** UKC's guide says most of the "sport" multi-pitches still need trad gear, and most trad routes have in-situ belays plus pegs at hard-to-protect cruxes. The technical crux is rarely the hardest part: off-widths, run-out friction slabs and exposed 40 m pitches are. Bring a good rack of cams including some big ones; a couple of pitches on Kundalini and Luna Nascente are tricky without a BD #4 or larger. Planetmountain's suggested kit is two 60 m ropes, 10 quickdraws, a full trad rack, and a hammer for the classic routes.

## When to go

| Source | Season |
|---|---|
| [UKC destination guide](https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/page.php?id=9471) | Rock climbing "generally considered to be from May to September"; bouldering "Autumn and spring are your best bets" |
| [27crags](https://27crags.com/areas/val-di-mello) | "Best season" field: "Spring and autumn"; the description adds "virtually year-round", with sunny sectors for winter and Bagni di Masino (about 1,300 m) for summer |
| [Planetmountain: Oceano Irrazionale](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/oceano-irrazionale-precipizio-degli-asteroidi.html), [Luna Nascente](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/luna-nascente-scoglio-delle-metamorfosi-val-di-mello.html) | "April - October" |

**Different limits at each end.** UKC's guide says nights still drop below freezing well into May and winter brings snow, while summer days get hot enough that "granite slab climbing can become torture". Bouldering is possible almost all year, but the guide still recommends spring and autumn. Many of the main multi-pitches face south: SummitPost says Il Risveglio di Kundalini "can get unpleasantly hot during summer" ([SummitPost](https://www.summitpost.org/il-risveglio-di-kundalini/817568)).

**After rain.** UKC's guide says the granite usually dries fast, but shaded Bagni di Masino stays wet longer. SummitPost adds that the grassy, tree-covered ledges on Kundalini stay wet for a while.

**Melloblocco.** Every early May the valley hosts the big bouldering gathering [Melloblocco](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/events/melloblocco-2026-everything-you-need-to-know.html); in 2026 it ran 6–9 May, and Planetmountain reported [more than 2,000 participants](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/events/melloblocco-the-gift-that-keeps-on-giving.html). Pick those days for the party, avoid them for quiet.

## Getting there

| | Public transport | Driving |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Milan | Milan |
| Middle | Train on the Milano–Lecco–Sondrio–Tirano line to Morbegno | SS36 towards Lecco and Colico, then towards Sondrio |
| Last leg | STPS bus A20 (Morbegno–Ardenno–Val Masino) to San Martino | Turn left at Ardenno into Val Masino; about 16 km to San Martino |
| Into the valley | About 25 minutes on foot from San Martino | Park in San Martino, or buy one of the limited daily passes to drive in |

### Public transport

The [reserve's website](https://riservanaturalevaldimello.org/) says: from Milan take the Milano–Lecco–Sondrio–Tirano line to Morbegno, then the bus to Val Masino; times and tickets are with STPS (Società Trasporti Pubblici Sondrio). The [Val Masino tourism site](https://valmasino.info/en/come-arrivare) names it line A20 (Morbegno–Ardenno–Val Masino). During Melloblocco, the A20 adds extra morning and afternoon runs ([Planetmountain](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/events/melloblocco-2026-everything-you-need-to-know.html)).

The reserve notes "Il servizio navetta non è più attivo" — the shuttle into the valley no longer runs — and recommends walking in from San Martino, about 25 minutes and roughly 100 m of ascent. UKC's guide reckons the train and bus take the better part of a day but save hiring a car you will probably not need.

### Driving

[Rifugio Mello](https://rifugiomello.it/en/dove-siamo) gives the route: from Lecco follow the main road along Lake Como to Colico, head for Sondrio, and at Ardenno (about 7 km past Morbegno) turn left into Val Masino; after about 16 km you reach San Martino, the highest village in Val Masino and the entrance to Val di Mello. Planetmountain says the mountain road from Ardenno takes about 15 minutes.

### Parking and the valley pass

This is the thing to sort out before you go. What the sources say:

| Source | Text |
|---|---|
| [Reserve website](https://riservanaturalevaldimello.org/) (current) | The access road "Via Val di Mello" is open to a maximum of 40 vehicles a day, with a €12 pass bought from the parking meter in the San Martino car park in front of the InfoPoint |
| [UKC Logbook](https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/val_di_mello-18140) | €10 day pass to park in Val di Mello, sells out on busy days; or park in San Martino (€7 a day, some free spaces) and walk an extra 25 minutes |
| [UKC destination guide](https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/page.php?id=9471) (older) | Car access closed in summer, paid the rest of the year |

Go by the reserve's website. UKC Logbook gives approaches of 20 minutes to 1.5 hours from the Val di Mello parking.

## Rules to know before you go

**Reserve rules.** The Val di Mello Nature Reserve was created by Lombardy Regional Council resolution VII/802 of 27 January 2009. It covers about 4,562 hectares in three zones: integral, oriented and landscape reserve. UKC's guide says the south side of the valley is integral reserve with restricted access — luckily its rock is poor, chossy and often damp. The rules listed on the reserve's site:

- Leave no litter.
- Dogs must be on a leash.
- No picking protected plants, no hunting.
- No fires.
- No bicycles.
- "Divieto di campeggio (su tutto il territorio comunale)": no wild camping anywhere in the municipality of Val Masino; stay at a campsite.

**Where to stay.** Planetmountain lists two campsites, Sasso Remenno and Ground Jack at the entrance to Val di Mello. UKC's guide says Camping Sasso Remenno is the most popular and triples in size during Melloblocco, when there is also a temporary campsite on the San Martino sports field.

**Do not add bolts to historic routes.** In a [Montagna.TV](https://www.montagna.tv/274231/val-di-mello-luca-schiera-sono-stato-io-a-schiodare-le-vie-vi-spiego-perche/) interview published 30 May 2026, climber Luca Schiera publicly admitted removing bolts and belays that had been added to two historic routes on the Placche dell'Oasi, Il Gioco dello Scivolo and Uomini e Topi. He said the valley has thousands of bolts and most walls are equipped, which nobody disputes, but the historic routes have always kept their original protection. If a topo says a route is in its original state, prepare for that and do not expect bolts.

## Routes by level

YDS and V-grade conversions follow the [Mountain Project international grade comparison chart](https://www.mountainproject.com/international-climbing-grades) and are approximate; "approx." marks grades the sources give without a/b/c (such as 4 or 5+). The original grade is the one that counts.

[Rockbusters](https://rockbusters.net/destinations/val-di-mello) counts boulders and sport routes together: 63+ beginner (F3–6A), 119+ intermediate (F6A–7A), 107+ advanced (F7A–8A) and 21+ expert (F8A–9A), with the bouldering concentrated at 6A–7B. Single-pitch sport routes are concentrated on Sasso Remenno (all four faces of the giant boulder) at the mouth of the valley in Val Masino; the multi-pitch routes in the valley mostly use UIAA or British grades and are in a separate table below.

### Beginner: up to 6a (YDS 5.10a and below)

The [UKC guide](https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/page.php?id=9471) says the bolted routes on Sasso Remenno are mostly up to 6b+; climber Max Piazza, in [a Wild Country article](https://www.wildcountry.com/val-di-mello-max-piazza), suggests any route on the Remenno south face to "warm up your shoes". The routes below are taken from [8a.nu's Val di Mello sport list](https://www.8a.nu/areas/italy/val-di-mello/sportclimbing) (sorted by number of logged ascents).

### Trad and multi-pitch (UIAA grades)

The valley's classic multi-pitch routes mostly use UIAA or British grades. UKC says bolted multi-pitch routes are often run out and still need a trad rack, while trad routes mostly have in-situ belays and pegs on hard-to-protect cruxes; long unprotected friction slabs are normal.

| Route | Original grade | YDS | Length / pitches | Wall | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Uomini e Topi (Via dell'Oasi) | IV+ (Wild Country) / 4+–5 (theCrag) | 5.5 | 350 m, 9 pitches | Placche dell'Oasi | Max Piazza's pick for a beginner's friction route, but "long stretches are unprotectable"; [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/val-di-mello/area/13927595325): first climbed by Miotti in 1977, belays on pegs and trees; bolts added later were removed in 2026 (see the rules above) |
| L'alba del Nirvana | V+ | 5.7 | about 150 m (after first climbing Stomaco Peloso) | Tempio dell'Eden | Wild Country warns that the Stomaco Peloso start has 20 m of smooth slab with no bolts and no protection |
| Tunnel Diagonale | VI- ([Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/area/108298267/val-di-mello) gives 5.8) | 5.8 | 4 pitches | Sperone degli Gnomi | Listed as a Val di Mello classic on MP |
| [Luna Nascente](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/luna-nascente-scoglio-delle-metamorfosi-val-di-mello.html) | VII (VI obligatory); UKC gives E1/E2, 8a.nu and MP give 6b | 5.10c | 360 m, 8–9 pitches | Scoglio delle Metamorfosi | First climbed in 1978 by Boscacci, Ghezzi and Milani; Planetmountain calls it "a true pearl of the Alps"; the first two pitches can be aided (UKC: HVS/E1 that way) |
| [Oceano Irrazionale](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/routes/oceano-irrazionale-precipizio-degli-asteroidi.html) | VII; UKC gives E3/E4, MP gives 6b+ | 5.10c | 400 m, 12–14 pitches | Precipizio degli Asteroidi | First climbed in 1977 by Guerini and Villa; Planetmountain says it was the first grade VII route in the Central Alps; nuts and a double set of cams to #4 |
| Il paradiso può attendere | VII | 5.10c | multi-pitch | Monte Qualido | Max Piazza's favourite multi-pitch sector, with a 1 h 40 min approach |
| Transqualidiana | E4/E5 | approx. 5.11–5.12 | 21 pitches | Monte Qualido | A UKC trad must-do |

For the first ascent of Oceano Irrazionale, Planetmountain gives 2 July 1977 and [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/val-di-mello/route/378945990) gives 2 June.

### Bouldering

Val Masino and Val di Mello have three historic boulder circuits of 10 boulders each, described by [NIVA](https://nivaclimb.com/2025/03/31/the-historical-paths-in-val-di-mello/) and [Planetmountain](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/historical-boulder-circuits-val-di-mello-val-masino.html): yellow Sassisti (5–6A, suitable for beginners), orange Melat (6B–6C) and red Gigiat (7A–7B).

| Problem | Grade (Font) | V grade | Sector | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Il Tendine dello Yeti | 5C | V2 | First boulder on the yellow circuit | A UKC bouldering must-do (spelled Tendine dello Yetti); NIVA says it is now used as a warm-up but was a mid-level grade VI in its day |
| Il Francese | 6C+ | V5+ | Il Francese sector | A UKC bouldering must-do |
| Marilù | 7A | V6 | Melloblocco sector | Listed on [MadBoulder](https://www.madboulder.org/sectors/val-di-mello/melloblocco) |
| Il Sogno di Tarzan | 7B | V8 | Tarzan sector | A UKC bouldering must-do |
| Il Banchettone | 8A+ | V12 | Melloblocco sector | Listed on MadBoulder |
