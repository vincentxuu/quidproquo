The Pollino National Park (Parco Nazionale del Pollino) straddles Calabria and Basilicata and is the largest newly established protected area in Italy (192,565 hectares, according to the [park's website](https://www.parconazionalepollino.it/en)). This post covers three sport crags on the park's Calabrian side, in the province of Cosenza: Orsomarso (the Mercuri valley) on the Tyrrhenian side, and Frascineto-Eianina and Acqua Rossa above Cerchiara di Calabria on the Ionian side.

The three sit between 200 m and 1,100 m and face different ways, so there is one for every season. This post helps you decide which suits your level and travel month, how to get there, and where in the park you may not climb. The big tufa cave further south is covered in the [Stilo post](/posts/climbing/2026-10-19-italy-crag-stilo-en); for a season-and-region comparison of Italian crags, see the [series overview](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crags-overview-en).

## What kind of crag is it

All three are single-pitch limestone sport crags, each with its own character:

| Crag | Location | Altitude and aspect | Scale (source) | Style |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Orsomarso (Mercuri, Cuba, Argentino) | Tyrrhenian side, Lao and Argentino valleys | About 200 m; mostly north-facing, Cuba faces south | Mercuri 62 routes (Climbook); 91 across the three sectors, 4c–8a ([I Sentieri del Pollino](https://www.isentieridelpollino.it/arrampicata-parco-pollino/falesia-sito-orsomarso-parco-del-pollino-calabria.html)); 90 on theCrag | Pocketed slabs to steep tufas, lots of mid-grade routes |
| Frascineto-Eianina | Ionian side, next to Frascineto town | About 500 m; mainly south-facing, sectors east to west | 55 routes (Climbook); 91 (theCrag) | Slabs, overhangs with cracks, big jugs and tufas |
| Acqua Rossa | Ionian side, above Cerchiara di Calabria | 1,100 m; faces west (CAI Lo Scarpone says north-west) | "più di 90 linee" (CAI Castrovillari); 86 (Climbook); 84 (theCrag) | Slabs, cracks and overhangs, with multi-pitch teaching lines |

**Orsomarso.** The [Calabria region's official tourism site](https://calabriastraordinaria.it/en/travel-tips/climbing-in-calabria-where-to-do-it) calls it "one of the reference points for those wishing to climb in Calabria" and describes Mercuri as "a spectacular limestone wall with sharp holes and notches, caves, reeds and stalactites", split into four sectors: Placche, Grotta, Chiesa and Ultimo. I Sentieri del Pollino says Mercuri is the most complete sector, from grade 4 to 8a with many mid-grade routes, from pocketed slabs to tufa overhangs, and "È possibile arrampicare anche in caso di maltempo" (you can climb even in bad weather). The routes were funded by the Orsomarso town council and bolted by the local club Vertical Climbing Laos and others. The town also has an indoor wall, Centro Valle Argentino, in an old sawmill (regional tourism site).

**Frascineto-Eianina.** The [Frascineto town council](https://www.comune.frascineto.cs.it/Guidaalpaese?IDDettaglio=43680) says the crag faces mainly south and the limestone is jagged, with slabs, overhangs with cracks, obvious big holds and tufas, at grades from low-medium to hard, making it a good place to start sport climbing. The regional tourism site calls it the "Little Pink Dolomites". [CAI Castrovillari](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/vie.php) (the Castrovillari section of the Italian Alpine Club) says the walls were first opened at the ViviPollino event in 2005, and the Calabria Rock Pollino meeting in April 2009 added many new routes.

**Acqua Rossa.** CAI Castrovillari says this new crag was officially inaugurated only on 7 December 2025, bolted with CAI funding under the supervision of mountain guide Cristian Candiotto: "più di 90 linee", 5a to 7b, 10–25 m, plus the option of linking 3 pitches up the tower. A [2025 report in CAI's Lo Scarpone](https://www.loscarpone.cai.it/dettaglio/cerchiara-di-calabria-91-tiri-in-falesia-a-due-passi-dal-mare/) says the bolts are 316L stainless steel, the belays have chains with screwgate or lever karabiners, the bolting follows "falesia plaisir" (easy-going sport crag) standards, and the rock is grey, grippy limestone. [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/central-and-southern-italy/calabria/area/13034481531) adds that there are dedicated sectors for beginners and children.

## When to go

| Crag | Source | Season |
|---|---|---|
| Orsomarso | [I Sentieri del Pollino](https://www.isentieridelpollino.it/arrampicata-parco-pollino/falesia-sito-orsomarso-parco-del-pollino-calabria.html) | Low altitude, so climbable all year; mostly north-facing, which helps in summer; pick the shady sectors in summer; only Cuba faces south |
| Frascineto-Eianina | [CAI Castrovillari](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/vie.php) | Sectors face east to west, but every approach path faces south: "attenzione in estate" (careful in summer) |
| Frascineto-Eianina | [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/central-and-southern-italy/calabria/area/517726152) | "All day sun", dries quickly |
| Acqua Rossa | [CAI Castrovillari](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/vie.php) | West-facing at 1,100 m, "fruibile tutto l'anno esclusi i mesi più caldi" (usable all year except the hottest months) |
| Acqua Rossa | [CAI Lo Scarpone](https://www.loscarpone.cai.it/dettaglio/cerchiara-di-calabria-91-tiri-in-falesia-a-due-passi-dal-mare/) | Climbs well on summer mornings and afternoons; can get snow in winter |
| Acqua Rossa | [I Sentieri del Pollino](https://www.isentieridelpollino.it/arrampicata-parco-pollino/falesia-acqua-rossa-cerchiara-di-calabria.html) | Summer (morning and late afternoon), spring, autumn (on milder days) |

**How to choose.** In summer, climb Acqua Rossa in the mornings and evenings, or find the shady north-facing sectors at Orsomarso; in winter, go to sunny Frascineto; in spring and autumn, all three work. The drive between the two coasts is long, so pick one side per trip. Also note that zone A of the park is open for climbing only from September to December (see the rules below).

## Getting there

| | Orsomarso | Frascineto-Eianina | Acqua Rossa |
|---|---|---|---|
| Motorway | A3 (Salerno–Reggio Calabria), exit Lagonegro Nord | Exit Frascineto | Via the SS106 to Cerchiara di Calabria |
| Then | SS585 Fondo Valle del Noce → SS18 past Scalea, via Marcellina to Orsomarso | Into town to the town hall, left at the bocce court, up the concrete road | SP162 (formerly SS92) towards the Santuario Madonna delle Armi |
| Parking to wall | Park by a restaurant, about 5 minutes | Clearing, 10–30 minutes | Roadside, about 10 minutes |

### Public transport

We found no public transport information connecting a railway station to any of the three crags. The Mercuri valley at Orsomarso, the path start outside Frascineto and the mountain road to Acqua Rossa are not on regular bus routes, so in practice you need a car.

### Driving

- **Orsomarso** ([I Sentieri del Pollino](https://www.isentieridelpollino.it/arrampicata-parco-pollino/falesia-sito-orsomarso-parco-del-pollino-calabria.html)): leave the A3 at Lagonegro Nord, take the SS585 Fondo Valle del Noce and then the coastal SS18 towards Scalea (49 km from the exit). Past Scalea and before Marina di Santa Maria del Cedro, turn inland and follow the Orsomarso signs via Marcellina.
- **Frascineto-Eianina** ([CAI Castrovillari](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/vie.php)): leave the A3 at Frascineto, or take the Castrovillari junction from the SS106 Ionian road. theCrag says it is 5–10 minutes by car from the Frascineto junction.
- **Acqua Rossa** (CAI Castrovillari): from Cerchiara di Calabria take the SP162 (formerly SS92) towards the Santuario Madonna delle Armi. Lo Scarpone says it is 5 minutes' drive from town and less than 20 minutes down to the sea.

### Parking

- **Orsomarso**: 3 km before the town, cross the bridge over the Lao, then turn right towards Contrada Mercuri; after about 1 km park on the right by a restaurant and follow the path to the Mercuri sector. If you don't turn off for Contrada Mercuri and stay on the main road for another km, the crossroads with rockfall netting is the Cuba sector. A few hundred metres further, turn right onto a dirt track and park; a steel cable takes you across the Argentino river, and the Argentino sector is a few minutes beyond (I Sentieri del Pollino; see the rules below about the Argentino sector).
- **Frascineto-Eianina**: in town, head to the town hall, go a few hundred metres further, turn left at the bocce court and follow the concrete road up to a clearing (CAI Castrovillari). theCrag says a path climbs the whole valley, branching off to each sector, 10–30 minutes from the car.
- **Acqua Rossa**: past the junction for the sanctuary and over the Bifurto pass, follow the road until you see the path on your left (a former park mountain-bike trail), then walk about 10 minutes (CAI Castrovillari). [Climbook](https://climbook.com/index.php/falesie/26633/acqua-rossa-mezzaluna-la-terrazza-virginia-linda-gio-edera-i-cinghiali-genesi-cuore-flintstones-finale) gives the parking coordinates as 39.8615, 16.3548.

## Rules to know before you go

**The park's provisional climbing rules (primary).** On 5 October 2015 the park suspended climbing and mountaineering across its whole territory to protect cliff-nesting raptors ([ALTURA 2015](http://www.altura-rapaci.org/arrampicata-sportiva-nel-parco-nazionale-del-pollino/)), then negotiated with climbing groups. The rules in force are the "[Disciplina provvisoria delle attività di arrampicata ed alpinismo](https://www.parconazionalepollino.it/images/pdf/2016/DISCIPLINA_PROVVISORIA_AGG.pdf)" (provisional rules for climbing and mountaineering), approved by management decision no. 701 of 12 October 2016 and published on the [park's regulations page](https://www.parconazionalepollino.it/l-ente-parco/documenti/regolamenti-new). The key points:

- **No-climbing areas**: climbing and mountaineering are banned outright in the areas mapped in Annex I, including La Rasa at Civita, Timpa del Demanio in the Raganello gorge, Timpe S. Lorenzo / Cassano / Gole di Barile, Colloreto, **Lao**, **Argentino** and Valle del Rosa.
- **Zone A window**: in zone A of the park plan (the core protection zone), climbing is allowed only from 1 September to 31 December (Annex II map).
- **No new routes in two reserves**: in the Gole del Raganello and Valle del Fiume Argentino nature reserves (Riserva Naturale Orientata), permanent equipment on the walls and new routes are prohibited.
- **Everywhere else**: outside those areas you may climb existing routes; new routes need park authorisation.
- **General bans**: no chipping or adding artificial holds, no leaving the paths in rocky areas, no noise that could disturb wildlife; bouldering (unbolted blocks up to 10 m) is unrestricted. Violations are penalised under article 30 of the framework law on protected areas (Legge 394/1991).

**Take care with Orsomarso's Argentino sector.** The "I.6 Argentino" no-climbing area in Annex I covers a large stretch of the upper Argentino valley and runs west to the edge of the map near Orsomarso. From this 1:22,000 map we cannot tell whether the Argentino sector, reached by crossing the river on a steel cable, lies inside the banned area; on Climbook none of its 18 routes has a recorded repeat. Ask the park before climbing there (phone +39 0973 669311, [park website](https://www.parconazionalepollino.it/en)). Annex I's "I.5 Lao" area is the valley upstream near Mormanno, not the Mercuri crag near Orsomarso. We could not tell from the park-wide Annex II map whether Mercuri and Cuba fall in zone A, so outside September–December, check with the park before you go.

**Part of Frascineto is closed.** [CAI Castrovillari](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/vie.php) opens its Frascineto section with: "UNA DELIBERA COMUNALE IMPONE CHE NON TUTTI I SETTORI INDICATI SONO ACCESSIBILI. VERIFICARE PREVENTIVAMENTE PRESSO IL COMUNE DI FRASCINETO." (a council resolution means not every listed sector is accessible; check with the Frascineto council first). [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/central-and-southern-italy/calabria/area/517726152) also says "A mayor's decree prohibits climbing in certain areas" and marks the Leoni di Montagna sector "Closed" (checked October 2026); that is the sector nearest the car park (route list compiled by Marco Ercoli), and it is left out of the route tables below. We did not find the text of the order or its scope on the council's website (the information stops at secondary sources), so call the council before you go (0981 32013).

**Belays at Acqua Rossa.** [CAI Castrovillari's route list dated 31 December 2025](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/images/acquarossa26.pdf) notes sector by sector that at Mezza Luna, Virginia, Linda and Gio "Non tutte le vie hanno il moschettone di sosta" (not every route has a lower-off karabiner), so bring your own quickdraw or karabiner to lower off; at I Cinghiali the karabiners are non-locking. Patata viola at Gio is not recommended when there are damp streaks after rain. The list also says not every grade has been confirmed by repeats, so some may change.

**Ethics at Frascineto.** theCrag lists: no modifying the rock by any means, no dry tooling, keep chalk to a minimum, and no fires inside the national park.

**Rescue.** In an emergency call the European emergency number 112; [CNSAS](https://www.cnsas.it/en/distress-call/) (Italy's mountain and cave rescue) is dispatched through 112, and CAI Castrovillari mentions CNSAS Calabria's Pollino station (Stazione Pollino).

## Routes by level

YDS and V-grade conversions follow the [Mountain Project international grade comparison chart](https://www.mountainproject.com/international-climbing-grades) and are approximate; "approx." marks grades the sources give without a/b/c (such as 4 or 5+). The original grade is the one that counts.

Orsomarso routes and grades come from [Climbook's Mercuri list](https://climbook.com/index.php/falesie/781/orsomarso-mercuri-placche-grotta-chiesa-ultimo) (updated July 2020, the most repeated of the three with 149 repeats); Frascineto from [Climbook](https://climbook.com/index.php/falesie/774/frascineto-eianina-leoni-di-montagna-calabria-rock-cattivi-poppanti-mamma-li-turchi-vashare-grotte-rosse); and Acqua Rossa from the CAI Castrovillari route list and Climbook (December 2025 data).

Very few at any of the three. I Sentieri del Pollino says Orsomarso goes up to 8a, falesia.it's Orsomarso entry says 5b/8b, and Climbook lists a single 8a.

## Where to look things up

- **[Pollino National Park: regulations](https://www.parconazionalepollino.it/l-ente-parco/documenti/regolamenti-new)**: the provisional climbing rules with Annex I (no-climbing map) and Annex II (zoning map), plus later closure decisions.
- **[CAI Castrovillari: Vie di arrampicata](https://www.caicastrovillari.it/vie.php)**: the latest Acqua Rossa route list (PDF), plus access notes and the closure warning for Frascineto.
- **[I Sentieri del Pollino](https://www.isentieridelpollino.it/arrampicata-parco-pollino/falesia-sito-orsomarso-parco-del-pollino-calabria.html)**: approach notes for Orsomarso's three sectors and for Acqua Rossa.
- **[Climbook: Calabria](https://climbook.com/index.php/regioni/19/calabria)**: routes, stars and repeat counts for each sector of the three crags.
- **[theCrag: Calabria](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/italy/central-and-southern-italy/calabria)**: route counts for Mercuri, Frascineto and Acqua Rossa, and Frascineto's access restrictions.
- **[Comune di Frascineto: Arrampicata Sportiva](https://www.comune.frascineto.cs.it/Guidaalpaese?IDDettaglio=43680)**: the council's crag description and contact details.
- **Guidebooks:** Grades and route counts here come mainly from online databases; the guidebook is the authority.
  - [*A Sud*, 2nd edition](https://www.mountainblog.it/sud-falesie-molise-puglia-basilicata-calabria/) (Graziano Montel and Pietro Radassao, Versante Sud, 2014, Italian and English, about 240 pages, ISBN 978-88-98609-02-4): 31 crags across Molise, Puglia, Basilicata and Calabria ([Libreria La Montagna](https://librerialamontagna.it/italia-altre-zone-arrampicata-e-alpinismo/3118-a-sud-falesie-e-vie-in-molise-calabria-puglia-basilicata.html)). [CAI Lo Scarpone](https://www.loscarpone.cai.it/dettaglio/arrampicata-e-alpinismo-le-guide-pi%C3%B9-recenti-per-le-salite-estive/) says it is out of print and out of date.
  - Acqua Rossa was developed after that book, so CAI Castrovillari's online route list is the most up to date; we found no newer print guide covering these three crags since 2014.
