Pian Schiavaneis sits at the top of Val di Fassa in Trentino, beside the SS242 from Canazei to Passo Sella, right under the west face of Sass Pordoi. [Planetmountain](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/pian-schiavaneis.html) says it looks like an insignificant boulder at first, yet it has become "the Dolomites' crucible of modern sports climbing, a playground for all and testing ground for the best". The databases also call it Cogoi ([falesia.it](https://www.falesia.it/crag/845/pian-schiavaneis-cogoi.html)) or Cogui ([UKC](https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/cogui-10972)).

This post helps you decide whether the grades suit you, whether a rainy day is worth the drive, and in which months a bus can get you there. For seasons and regions across Italy, see the [series overview](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crags-overview-en); for the multi-pitch towers and boulders up the same pass, see the [Sella group](/posts/climbing/2026-10-19-italy-crag-sella-en).

## What kind of crag is it

**Rock and style.** Single-pitch sport climbing. Planetmountain describes "friable overhanging limestone" that "has been chipped, glued and drilled to sculpt routes of all grades, where power and stamina are essential and never enough". The wall is 35 m high and Planetmountain recommends a 70 m rope.

**Size.** Each source counts differently (checked October 2026):

| Source | Routes | Grades | Other |
|---|---|---|---|
| [Planetmountain](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/pian-schiavaneis.html) | 62 (Placca, Centrale, Gabriel, No Big Area) | 5b–8b/c | Faces SSE, about 2,000 m |
| [falesia.it](https://www.falesia.it/crag/845/pian-schiavaneis-cogoi.html) | 100 in 7 sectors | 4a–8c+ | 1,910 m; page updated 10 May 2026 |
| [UKC](https://www.ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/cogui-10972) | 77 | — | Faces S, 1,898 m |
| [CAI *Lo Scarpone*](https://www.loscarpone.cai.it/dettaglio/fuga-dal-caldo-cinque-falesie-per-arrampicare-nelle-dolomiti-orientali/) | "quasi cento vie" (almost a hundred) | — | 1,900 m, south-facing |

falesia.it's sectors: A Placche 15 routes (5b/7c+), B Centrale 37 (6b/8c+), C Gabriel 14 (6a+/7b+), D Gabriel basso 6 (5c/6c+), E L'Angolo del Castigo 12 (5b/7b+), F Il blocco del bambino 8 (4a/6a) and F Niagara 8 (4a/6c+).

**Who it suits.** UKC calls it "A steep and imposing crag for those wishing to push their grades", with most of the better routes around 7a+. CAI's *Lo Scarpone* says there are a few routes in the fifth and sixth grade, but it "non è propriamente considerata una falesia per principianti" (is not really considered a beginners' crag) because the overhanging sections make the climbing strenuous. It recommends the Principale (central) sector for mid to high grades and Placca for "vie non estreme ma decisamente tecniche" (not extreme but very technical routes). If you climb 6a and below, your choice is limited to the left side of Placca and a few small blocks.

## When to go

Planetmountain: the crag faces SSE at about 2,000 m; "Climbing is possible all year round, but summer afternoons are best", when it goes into the shade. In practice, winter depends on the Sella road and snow; summer is the real season.

**Rainy days.** This is the crag's big selling point. Planetmountain says you can climb when it rains, though the crag seeps after long periods of rain. CAI says the overhanging central routes stay sheltered along their full length, so "è possibile continuare ad arrampicare qui anche mentre il classico temporale pomeridiano arriva" (you can keep climbing here while the classic afternoon thunderstorm rolls in). A user comment on [Climbook](https://climbook.com/falesie/1496/pian-schiavaneis-placca) from ten years ago says even the more open Placca sector is climbable in light rain. On days when the Dolomites' afternoon storms rule out long routes, this is the usual fallback.

## Getting there

| | Public transport | Driving |
|---|---|---|
| To Canazei | Trentino Trasporti line 101 (Trento–Cavalese–Moena–Canazei) | SS48 up Val di Fassa |
| Canazei to the crag | Summer line B129 (Canazei–Passo Sella), Pian Schiavaneis stop | SS48 towards Passo Pordoi, then SS242 towards Passo Sella |
| Last stretch | 10–15 minutes on foot | Same |

### Public transport

[Trentino Trasporti notice no. 556/2026](https://www.trentinotrasporti.it/images/warningsmanager/warnings/public/5337/559%20Nuovo%20collegamento%20Canazei%20-%20Passo%20Sella%20estate%202026.pdf): line B129 Canazei–Passo Sella is a new summer route for 2026, running daily from 27 June to 9 September and operated by a private coach company. Buses leave the Canazei roundabout car park (parch. rotatoria) every 40 minutes from 08:30 to 16:30, 13 runs in all, reaching the Pian Schiavaneis stop 15 minutes later; return buses leave Pian Schiavaneis from 09:19 to 17:19. From 10 September 2026 until next summer the line does not run; check the notice for the year you go.

To reach Canazei itself, the [Val di Fassa tourist board](https://www.fassa.com/it/come-arrivare/autobus-trentino-trasporti) lists line 101 Canazei–Moena–Cavalese–Trento as the year-round route; B129, B130 (Canazei–Passo Pordoi) and B131 are all marked "SERVIZIO ATTIVO SOLO IN ESTATE" (summer only).

### Driving

Planetmountain: from Canazei head towards Passo Pordoi, turn left for Passo Sella higher up, and you soon reach the Rifugio Pian Schiavaneis and Rifugio Monti Pallidi. From Val Gardena, cross Passo Sella and drive down.

### Parking and approach

Planetmountain says to park at the two refuges; UKC says to continue up to a parking place on the right just before a bridge. Planetmountain's approach: walk up the tarmac towards Passo Sella to the barrier by a large boulder, take the path, cross the stream, and reach the grassy slope below the crag in 10 minutes. [Climbook](https://climbook.com/falesie/1494-pian-schiavaneis-centrale) describes the path towards the waterfalls alongside the riverbed; UKC says to follow the riverbed for another 10–15 minutes. CAI notes that parking at these summer Dolomite crags is "quasi tutti … a pagamento e a rischio di esaurimento posti" (almost all paid, and liable to fill up).

## Rules to know before you go

**Closures and bans.** None of the sources checked (Planetmountain, falesia.it, UKC, CAI, October 2026) mention a closure or climbing ban.

**Rock and bolts.** Planetmountain says the rock is friable and the routes were shaped by chipping and gluing; the sources checked have no news of recent rebolting. Bring a helmet and a 70 m rope.

**Marmots.** Planetmountain suggests a picnic on the meadow with the "hungry marmots"; don't leave your pack open.

**Rescue.** [Soccorso Alpino Trentino](https://soccorsoalpinotrentino.it/en/prevention): in an accident call 112 and give the exact location, the activity, the number of people and their condition, the weather and the number you are calling from.

## Routes by level

YDS and V-grade conversions follow the [Mountain Project international grade comparison chart](https://www.mountainproject.com/international-climbing-grades) and are approximate; "approx." marks grades the sources give without a/b/c (such as 4 or 5+). The original grade is the one that counts.

Route names and grades often differ by half a grade or more between sources (Lambada is 6b on Planetmountain and Climbook, 7a on UKC); the tables show both. "Plus" and "lunga" are extensions continuing up the same line.

### Beginner: up to 6a (YDS 5.10a and below)

Concentrated on the left of Placca, in the No Big Area (UKC files it under Gabriel) and Punishment Corner. falesia.it also lists two small blocks in sector F, from 4a.

|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Via delle guide | 5c | 5.9 | Single pitch | Placca | 5c on Planetmountain and UKC; the most repeated route in this sector on Climbook (17 ascents) |
| Power ranger | 5b (UKC 5c) | 5.8 | Single pitch | Placca | 10 ascents on Climbook; one user suggests linking it straight into Fessura |
| Fessura | 5c | 5.9 | Single pitch | Placca | UKC; 7 ascents on Climbook |
| Usignolo | 5c | 5.9 | Single pitch | No Big Area | Planetmountain, UKC |
| Baby birba | 5c | 5.9 | Single pitch | No Big Area | Planetmountain, UKC |
| Bel cammino | 5c | 5.9 | Single pitch | Punishment Corner | UKC |
| Franiglia | 6a | 5.10a | Single pitch | Niagara | UKC |
