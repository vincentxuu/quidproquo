Roccamorice is a mountain village in the province of Pescara in Abruzzo, inside the Majella National Park. In the Santo Spirito valley outside the village runs a limestone wall about two kilometres long, the Parete dell'Orso (Bear Wall); the road to the crag also leads to the Santo Spirito hermitage in the valley, and Montagna.TV mentions the paths that link the hermitages nearby. The [municipality](https://www.comune.roccamorice.pe.it/vivere_il_comune/luoghi/luogo_17.html) calls the wall "una delle mete più iconiche e amate per l'arrampicata sportiva in Abruzzo" (one of the most iconic and best-loved sport climbing destinations in Abruzzo), and [Montagna.TV](https://www.montagna.tv/255676/aperte-13-vie-a-roccamorice-tempio-del-climbing-nella-majella/) calls it the "temple of climbing in the Majella".

This post covers what to decide before you go: which sectors suit which level, which months, where to park, what the municipal rules say, and where things stand after the 2025 rockfall accident. For high multi-pitch and bouldering elsewhere in Abruzzo, which are summer destinations, see [Gran Sasso](/posts/climbing/2026-10-19-italy-crag-gran-sasso-en); for a season-and-region comparison of Italian crags, see the [series overview](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crags-overview-en).

## What kind of crag is it

**One long wall along the road.** [Abruzzo Verticale](https://www.abruzzoverticale.it/falesie/pescara/roccamorice/) says the Parete dell'Orso is about two kilometres long and one of the largest rock structures in Italy. [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/roccamorice) describes the sectors following one after another down the valley road; Montagna.TV says the first sector is right on the paved road and the others are higher up, reached by a path that traverses the whole wall.

**Rock and style.** Abruzzo Verticale: the slab routes are on grey, very compact, pocketed limestone, the overhangs on yellow limestone that is just as compact but less featured; the style leans slightly towards technical slabs, with sustained steep lines too. Montagna.TV says most of the wall is vertical or slightly overhanging, with bulges, roofs, corners and arêtes, and the friction is good, even on the popular easy routes.

**Sectors.** [falesia.it](https://www.falesia.it/crag/27/roccamorice.html) (updated 30 September 2023) lists 11 sectors; the biggest are Giallon (44 routes) by the road and Pancia d'Elefante (29), and the others are Placche Grigie, Placche Nere, Strapiombi, Ghaza, Cinghiale Bianco, I Buchoni, Gare, La Poltrona and Il Terrazzo. There is also La Pecorella Smarrita, reached by abseil, and Linea Gotica, opened only in 2018.

**Scale.** Numbers by source (checked October 2026):

| Source | Count |
|---|---|
| [falesia.it](https://www.falesia.it/crag/27/roccamorice.html) | 190 routes, 4b–8a |
| [UKClimbing](https://ukclimbing.com/logbook/crags/roccamorice-25833) | 204 routes |
| [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/roccamorice) | 215 routes, 4b–8b, average 6c |
| [Climbing Spot Factory](https://climbingspotfactory.com/en/climbadvisor/roccamorice/info_about) | About 250 routes, "the biggest climbing site of the Abruzzo country" |
| [Montagna.TV](https://www.montagna.tv/255676/aperte-13-vie-a-roccamorice-tempio-del-climbing-nella-majella/) (2025) | Over 300 routes in about a dozen sectors, grade 5 to 8c, plus a project that may be 9a |

**History and bolts.** Montagna.TV's 2025 report: the first trad routes went up in 1981, and from the late 1980s mountain guide Roberto Rosica and others pushed the grades; after 2000 the crag was not maintained, and only the first 50 or so routes stayed climbable. From 2014 a small group of young climbers, using their own money and collections, rebolted every historic route with certified stainless steel, then opened the Linea Gotica sector, and in 2025 added 13 more routes in Ghaza and Linea Gotica. The rebolting kept the original spirit: on hard routes the crux cannot be aided past by pulling on bolts; new routes up to 6b have modern, closer bolting, while harder ones keep obligatory runouts. The few 1990s routes with chipped holds were left as they are.

**Who it suits.** Every level; Abruzzo Verticale gives the average as 6c. The grade 5 routes around Giallon have the most repeats and suit beginners; the steep grade 7 and 8 routes in Pancia d'Elefante and Strapiombi are for strong climbers.

## When to go

| Source | Season |
|---|---|
| [Abruzzo Verticale](https://www.abruzzoverticale.it/falesie/pescara/roccamorice/) | South-facing at about 900 m; climbable all year except sunless winter days |
| [Montagna.TV](https://www.montagna.tv/255676/aperte-13-vie-a-roccamorice-tempio-del-climbing-nella-majella/) | The irregular wall means some recesses are shaded by arêtes on summer afternoons, making it ideal in spring and autumn; in winter pick the sunniest days, even with snow around, as long as the rock is dry |
| [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/it/arrampicata/italy/roccamorice) | Sun in the morning, shade in the evening, except the Giallon, Buconi, Ghaza and Placche sectors |

**Summer.** South-facing and fairly low, it gets hot on midsummer days. If you climb here in summer, pick routes in the recesses that get afternoon shade, or head for the higher [Gran Sasso](/posts/climbing/2026-10-19-italy-crag-gran-sasso-en) instead.

## Getting there

|---|---|---|
| Start | Pescara | A25 L'Aquila–Pescara |
| Middle | No bus to the village found | Exit at Caramanico, via Lettomanoppello to Roccamorice; or exit at Alanno/Scafa and take the SS5 |
| Last leg | — | From the village follow signs for Block House about 4 km, turn right for the Eremo di Santo Spirito, 1 km more, park on the left by the fountain |

### Public transport

No bus route was found from Pescara or a railway station to Roccamorice village, and the parking is about 5 km beyond the village in the valley, so in practice you need a car. The regional bus operator is [TUA](https://www.tuabruzzo.it/linee-e-orari/83.html); check its site for any seasonal lines.

### Driving

The official tourism site of the spa town of [Caramanico Terme](https://www.caramanicotermenatura.it/it/pianifica/cosa-vedere/Parete-di-arrampicata-a-Roccamorice-poi146) says: leave the A25 L'Aquila–Pescara at the Caramanico exit, head for Lettomanoppello and then Roccamorice. [Climbing Spot Factory](https://climbingspotfactory.com/en/climbadvisor/roccamorice/info_about) gives an alternative: leave the A25 at Alanno/Scafa, take the SS5 and follow signs for San Valentino, Abbateggio and Roccamorice. Once in the village, theCrag says to follow the brown roadside signs with a climber on them for "Roccamorice Climbing School".

### Parking

- **The fountain car park**: from the village, follow signs for Block House for about 4 km, turn right for the Eremo di Santo Spirito, go 1 km more and park on the left by the fountain (Caramanico Terme and Abruzzo Verticale agree). The first sector, Giallon, is a few minutes' walk away.
- **Higher sectors**: Abruzzo Verticale says to walk about 200 m further along the road from Giallon to a small layby on the left, where the path to Placche Grigie starts; from there go left for Strapiombi and right for the other sectors, 5 to 15 minutes. UKClimbing says the layby only holds a couple of cars.
- **La Pecorella Smarrita**: walk 100 m past Giallon, climb over the guardrail on the right (route names are written on it) and abseil 25 m from a large U-shaped glue-in bolt.
- **No parking on the road below the wall**: see the municipal rules below.

## Rules to know before you go

**Municipal order (Ordinanza Sindacale nr. 2 del 12 febbraio 2020).** The rules for the Parete dell'Orso published on the [municipality's website](https://www.comune.roccamorice.pe.it/vivere_il_comune/luoghi/luogo_17.html), page updated 2 January 2026:

- **Helmets are compulsory** while climbing and while waiting at the base; the foot of the wall is exposed to rockfall (there are grazing and wild animals above), so you may only stay there for as long as you need to climb.
- **Parking is strictly forbidden on the stretch of the Santo Spirito municipal road below the wall**; around the crag, overnight stays, bivouacs, camping, tents, campervans and caravans are banned, as are fires, litter and disturbing wildlife.
- Only dynamic ropes; minors and beginners must be accompanied by experienced people; courses on the wall may only be run by registered mountain guides, who must notify the municipality.
- No removing, moving or adding bolts; no new routes without the municipality's permission; no writing or signs on the rock; no dry tooling.

**Fatal rockfall in November 2025.** [AbruzzoLive](https://www.abruzzolive.tv/cronaca/masso-si-stacca-dalla-montagna-e-uccide-35enne-di-montesilvano-durante-arrampicata-a-roccamorice-it38205.html) and [L'Aquila Blog](https://www.laquilablog.it/tragedia-a-roccamorice-muore-scalatore-colpito-da-un-masso-durante-arrampicata/) reported that on the afternoon of 1 November 2025, below the Betacam route (Pancia d'Elefante sector; see [Climbook](https://climbook.com/falesie/26638/roccamorice-pancia-delefante/vie) for where the route is), a large block fell from the wall above, broke apart and killed 35-year-old climber Stefano D'Anteo; two people nearby were lightly injured by fragments. He was also one of the volunteers named in Montagna.TV's report on the rebolting. AbruzzoLive wrote that the area was "messa in sicurezza e posta sotto sequestro" (made safe and placed under seizure), with the carabinieri and mountain rescue investigating the cause (secondary: local news reports; no original notice found on the extent of the seizure or its lifting; checked October 2026). Before you go, check with the municipality or the local climbing community whether the Pancia d'Elefante area is open, and wear a helmet as required.

**National park.** The crag is in the Majella National Park, and the first clause of the municipal order requires you to follow park rules. The [park website](https://www.parcomajella.it/Piano-e-Regolamento-del-Parco.htm) says its regulation was approved by the park board in 1999 and is still "in corso di adozione" (awaiting adoption by the environment ministry). Checked October 2026, no nesting-season climbing ban was found for the Parete dell'Orso.

**Bolts and anchors.** Montagna.TV says every historic route was rebolted with certified stainless steel after 2014; falesia.it says almost all routes have a lower-off chain. Abruzzo Verticale (2020) warns that some anchors have no carabiner on the chain, so bring a lanyard and thread the rope yourself.

**Rescue.** [CNSAS](https://www.cnsas.it/richiesta-di-soccorso/) says that for an accident in the mountains or at a crag you call 118 or the European emergency number 112 and ask the operator to activate mountain rescue; the call is free. In the 2025 accident, CNSAS Abruzzo and the 118 helicopter winched about 60 m down to the foot of the wall.

## Routes by level

YDS and V-grade conversions follow the [Mountain Project international grade comparison chart](https://www.mountainproject.com/international-climbing-grades) and are approximate; "approx." marks grades the sources give without a/b/c (such as 4 or 5+). The original grade is the one that counts.

Routes and grades come mainly from [Climbook](https://climbook.com/falesie/340/roccamorice-giallon), which updated the Giallon, Pancia d'Elefante and other sector pages between December 2024 and January 2025 from the 2023 edition of the Abruzzo Rock guidebook; repeat counts are also Climbook's. Where Climbook and older topos differ, both are listed.
