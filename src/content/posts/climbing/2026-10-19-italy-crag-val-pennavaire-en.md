> 🌏 [中文版](/posts/climbing/2026-10-19-italy-crag-val-pennavaire)

Val Pennavaire is in western Liguria, a dozen or so minutes' drive inland from the coastal town of Albenga. It is a narrow, wooded valley, and the crags are tucked into the trees in patches. It first appeared in a guidebook called *Oltrefinale* ("beyond Finale"), so many climbers still call it Oltrefinale, or simply "Albenga".

The [UKClimbing destination guide](https://www.ukclimbing.com/articles/destinations/val_pennavaire_-_finale_ligures_lesser-known_neighbour-12544) warns that the name makes it sound joined to [Finale Ligure](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crag-finale-ligure-en), when it is about 30 km further west along the coast and very different in style. This post helps you decide which crags to pick for a winter trip, which ones are currently caught up in a ban dispute, and how to get into the valley without a car. For a season-and-region comparison of Italian crags, see the [series overview](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crags-overview-en).

## What kind of crag is it

**Rock and style.** Mostly single-pitch limestone sport. UKClimbing says there is "no trad climbing anywhere" and every route has a lower-off. Most crags are steep walls of tufas, crimps and pockets that ask for stamina and good footwork. [theTopo](https://thetopo.com/crags/terminal/description) says the limestone is older than Finale's, which gives a wider mix of holds. The few long routes are on Rocca Rossa; [climbing-guide.eu](https://climbing-guide.eu/climbing-guidebook-roc-pennavaire-11917), quoting the local guide, puts the longest at about 130 m.

**Scale.** Numbers by source (checked October 2026):

**Popularity.** On [8a.nu's ranking of Italian sport crags](https://www.8a.nu/crags/sportclimbing/italy), sorted by logged ascents, Terminal is 6th (10,243) and Erboristeria 11th (7,991).

**Who maintains it.** The local association [RocPennavaire](https://www.rocpennavaire.it/), founded in 2012, develops new crags, rebolts and keeps the paths clear, funded mainly by guidebook sales and donations. [Planetmountain's Terminal page](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/crags/terminal-albenga.html), for example, says the association checked 800 bolts at Terminal in 2013 and replaced every unsafe one; its home page lists the 2025 rebolt of Placca dei Malavoglia and the restyling of La Galera. A [September 2026 CSEN article](https://www.csenarrampicata.it/falesia-antro-di-castelbianco/) says the association is fundraising to fit about 30 older routes in the Castelbianco cave with resin anchors and stainless-steel lower-offs.

**Who it suits.** Best for mid-grade to strong climbers looking for sunny steep walls in winter. Routes at 6a and below are concentrated in a few crags (see Routes by level), so there is not much for beginners, but the association flags several "NO BIG" crags for them.

## When to go

**North bank in winter, south bank in summer.** The valley runs roughly east–west, rising from the Martinetto junction at its mouth westwards to Alto. Walls on the north bank mostly face south and get the sun; walls on the south bank mostly face north and stay in shade (see RocPennavaire for each crag's aspect). [Planetmountain's 2013 introduction](https://www.planetmountain.com/en/news/climbing/climbing-in-val-pennavaire-albenga.html) puts it as climbing in the sun on one bank or in the shade on the other. The shady south-bank crags are the ones caught up in the ban dispute; see the rules below.

**Rainy days.** The association's rain list is Granaio, Reunion and Guggenheim. Sources differ on Telematica's sun: Mountain Project says it gets only a couple of hours of afternoon sun in winter but is very sheltered, while RocPennavaire says it is in the sun almost all day.

## Getting there

| | Public transport | Driving |
|---|---|---|
| Start | Albenga station on the Genoa–Ventimiglia line | A10 motorway, Albenga exit |
| Middle | [TPL Linea](https://www.tpllinea.it/) bus 76 from Albenga station to Castelbianco, about 45 min | SS582 towards Garessio to the Martinetto junction, then left on the SP14 into the valley |
| Last leg | Walk from the bus stop to the crag | Park at roadside clearings, church squares or village car parks depending on the crag |

### Public transport

Albenga is on the coastal railway between Genoa and Ventimiglia ([FS Italiane's line-doubling project page](https://fsitaliane.it/en/strategic-projects/completion-of-the-track-doubling-for-the-genoa-ventimiglia-line-.html)), with regional trains from both the Genoa and Nice directions.

The only bus into the valley is TPL Linea line 76 (Albenga–Castelbianco–Nasino–Alto–Caprauna). The table uses the [winter weekday timetable from 14 September 2026](https://www.tpllinea.it/wp-content/uploads/2026/09/76-%E2%80%93-CAPRAUNA-%E2%80%93-ALBENGA-Feriale.pdf) as an example:

| Direction | Departs | Arrives |
|---|---|---|
| Albenga station → Castelbianco | 9:10, 13:10, 18:30 | 9:53, 13:53, 19:13 |
| Castelbianco → Albenga station | 7:05, 11:23, 15:23 | 7:35, 11:58, 15:58 |

The timetable says "NON EFFETTUA SERVIZIO NEI GIORNI FESTIVI": no buses on Sundays or public holidays.

So the bus gives you a short day: arrive 9:53, leave 15:23. Terminal, Granaio and the Castelbianco cave are all beside the SP14, a few minutes to a quarter of an hour on foot, and suit it best. The Euskal group is 3.6 km further up the valley from Castelbianco and Red Up further still, so neither is practical by bus. UKClimbing's conclusion is that a hire car is the realistic option, and Climb Europe says "A hire car is essential".

### Driving

Planetmountain's directions: leave the A10 (Genoa–Ventimiglia) at Albenga, take the SS582 towards Garessio, turn left at Martinetto onto the SP14, and Terminal is 4.5 km on. Mountain Project says it is about 1.5 hours' drive from either Nice or Genoa.

The handiest base is Colletta di Castelbianco: UKClimbing describes an old village abandoned in the 1950s and converted in the 1990s into apartments, a bar and a restaurant, right in the middle of the most popular crags. Albenga is cheaper, but you drive further every day.

### Parking

| Crag | Where to park | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Terminal, Granaio, Erboristeria | By the houses of agriturismo "da Ferruccio", or the clearing about 100 m further on | RocPennavaire, Planetmountain |
| Castelbianco cave | The clearing below the wall, or at the roadside a little further on | CSEN |
| Telematica and the Colletta crags | Past Castelbianco, turn right at Osteria Scola towards Colletta; large car park at the village entrance | RocPennavaire, [Climbook](https://climbook.com/falesie/1434/telematica) |
| Euskal, Colosseo, Basura | 3.6 km past Castelbianco, turn right for "Chiesa- Perati- Casale" and park in the church square | RocPennavaire |
| Bauso di Veravo, La Galera | The large car park behind Veravo church | RocPennavaire |

RocPennavaire's Terminal page says "Don't park in the private parking spaces, especially on the left side!", and asks climbers on the path past the vegetable garden to "pay attention to the crops". Planetmountain wrote in 2013 that the valley has no campsite and the Bauso tourist office car park fills with vans and campers at weekends.

## Rules to know before you go

**The shady south-bank crags are under a ban dispute.** This is the single most important thing for planning.

- **The 2000 provincial ban.** [TAR Liguria (the regional administrative court) ruling no. 52 of 2021](https://www.osservatorioagromafie.it/wp-content/uploads/sites/40/2021/01/tar-liguria-52-2021.pdf) cites the Province of Savona's regulation no. 64975 of 14 September 2000. It draws a zone bounded by ridges, a stream and the valley-floor road, within which there is a "divieto permanente di arrampicata" (permanent climbing ban), spanning the municipalities of Arnasco, Castelbianco and Vendone. The area is part of the Castell'Ermo–Peso Grande Natura 2000 site, which protects breeding eagle owls and peregrine falcons.
- **The 2021 trial reopening was annulled.** In 2020 the Liguria regional government passed resolution no. 335 allowing climbing on some walls on a trial basis until the end of 2021. [WWF Italia](https://www.wwf.it/pandanews/animali/crimini-di-natura/liguria-tar-boccia-la-delibera-sulle-arrampicate-sperimentali-in-area-protetta/) and LIPU challenged it, and the TAR annulled it on the precautionary principle.
- **Which crags.** The ruling itself does not name crags. The local blog [Liguria Verticale](https://liguriaverticale.blogspot.com/2021/01/valpennavaire-divieto-parziale-di.html) says the ban covers the walls on the valley's right bank (the shady side) from Emisfero to Magic Mushroom; [Up-Climbing's July 2021 interview with WWF's Liguria delegate](https://www.up-climbing.com/multipitch/news-multipitch/divieti-val-pennavaire-regione-wwf-2/) names Emisfero, Erboristeria and Cineplex (secondary: a climbing magazine's and a blog's reading; the ruling lists no crags). The same interview says temporary-ban areas may be used from 1 August to 31 December each year while permanent-ban areas are fully closed, and that the region and WWF had set up a working group to run an environmental impact assessment and sort the walls into open, seasonally open and closed.
- **Current status.** [Up-Climbing's January 2021 comment piece](https://www.up-climbing.com/falesia/news-falesia/arrampicare-in-val-pennavaire-si-puo-si-deve-2/) says climbing at Cineplex and Erboristeria "è vietata da sempre" (has always been banned) and had simply been tolerated, while the remaining 90% or so of the valley's crags are open as normal. As of October 2026, no later decision from that working group to reopen anything could be found. RocPennavaire's crag pages still list these crags with no ban notice, while its Magic Mushroom page mentions problems with "the prohibition of bird nesting" during development. Before you go, ask RocPennavaire or the CPR climbing shop in Cisano sul Neva about the current situation; the route tables in this post include only crags on the north bank and up the valley, outside the disputed zone.

**Phone signal and rescue.** theTopo says the Castelbianco-area crags have "PHONE SIGNAL: NO". Liguria runs the EU emergency number 112 ([AGENAS](https://trovastrutture.agenas.it/eng/macroaree/emergency-network-urgency)); [CNSAS mountain rescue](https://www.cnsas.it/richiesta-di-soccorso/) says to call 112 or 118 and ask for mountain rescue, giving WGS84 coordinates where possible. Work out where the signal starts on the walk in before you need it.

**Gear.** UKClimbing says a 70 m single rope and 16 quickdraws are enough for every crag; the bases are dusty, so bring a rope bag. Euskal's bolts are further apart than elsewhere (RocPennavaire).

**Old bolts.** Many Castelbianco cave routes were bolted between 1992 and 2005; the association has about 30 scheduled for rebolting (CSEN, September 2026). Until that is done, judge the old anchors yourself.

## How it differs from Finale Ligure

| | Val Pennavaire (this post) | [Finale Ligure](/posts/climbing/2026-10-18-italy-crag-finale-ligure-en) |
|---|---|---|
| Setting | One narrow valley inland from Albenga | Limestone plateaus from the coast inland |
| Development | From the 1990s, mostly after 2000 (Planetmountain) | First routes in 1968 (italia.it) |
| Style | Steep walls, tufas, stamina; UKClimbing calls it "very much more 'modern'" | Vertical to slightly overhanging and technical; theCrag says popular routes are "more or less polished" |
| Bolts | The association keeps rebolting (Euskal's spacing is longer) | UKClimbing says Finale is now better known for "polish and very spaced bolts" |
| Winter | South-facing north-bank crags | Pick by aspect; something is climbable all year |
| Bans | The shady south-bank crags sit inside the 2000 permanent ban zone; dispute unresolved | Seasonal raptor bans 1 Jan–31 Jul plus a permanent-ban list; follow Finale Outdoor Region's LIVE page |
| Transport | Three weekday buses; you basically need a car | Train to Finale Ligure; the bus towards Feglino reaches the Monte Cucco area |

UKClimbing puts the two about 30 km apart along the coast. They pair well on one trip, with Pennavaire's sunny steep walls for the cold days.
