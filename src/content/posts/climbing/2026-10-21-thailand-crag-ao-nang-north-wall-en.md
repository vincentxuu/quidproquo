If you want one Thai crag where you do not have to think much, North Wall is the most direct answer: it is in Ao Nang town, five minutes' walk from the beach, then a one-minute walk from the car park to the wall. All titanium bolts, toilets, gear for rent, transparent fees.

**But theCrag hangs two warnings on this page, and both come from real incidents: parking in the wrong place, and people having climbing gear stolen.**

## What kind of crag this is

Limestone. theCrag's summary: "Sport climbing crag in Ao Nang town itself. Good mix of moderate to difficult climbs (5a to 8a with most being excellent 6s and 7s). **All titanium bolts and well-maintained crag overall.**"

The development history is short: "The North Wall is the newest crag in Krabi and it's in Ao Nang — so on the mainland and not Railay-Tonsai peninsula. Developed in the last few years, **all bolts are titanium set with Hilti 500 glue**."

**The first routes are on the page too**: theCrag's History says the first routes at North Wall were opened by Rock & Sun (Trevor Massiah and Desiree Verbeek-Massiah) **on Christmas Day 2016**, and **the first two were Santa Sends and Dr Desire**. The Jurassic X-Mas sector history says the same thing. (Rock & Sun are also the developers of Tonsai's Mythical Beasts and King Kong.)

### Six sectors

| Sector | Routes | Height | Character |
|---|---|---|---|
| Rock'n Roll | 28 | 24 m | The most routes in the area; **carries a bird-nest warning, and the 8a Air Jordan is here** |
| Mythical Beasts | 24 | 28 m | The main wall with 1,303 ascents; **some very long routes** |
| Jurassic X-Mas | 24 | 24 m | 3,634 ascents, the most popular wall; **carries a hornet warning (updated 2026-03-09)** |
| Life of Leif | 19 | 22 m | 2,659 ascents, 8 routes in the 7s |
| Hello Welcome | 5 | 22 m | Only 5 routes but 188 ascents |
| NW Bouldering | 3 | — | Bouldering, theCrag marks it 6A+ |

**Mythical Beasts is the main wall.** theCrag: "Climbing is similar to Railay-Tonsai — steep and 3D with some very long routes, especially in the Mythical Beasts sector."

**Jurassic X-Mas is the most popular wall** (3,634 ascents), and it carries the most important biological warning in the series.

### Why the routes are long

North Wall's routes are generally longer than the island crags: Mythical Beasts lists a maximum height of 56 metres, with Banshee at 35 m, Hercules 30 m, King Kong 32 m and Godzilla 35 m. **That means many routes want 60 metres of rope or more, and your lowering needs working out.**

Godzilla (7b+) says it directly: **"Use a 70m Rope and tie a knot or use the midway anchor to lower down."** Banshee (7a) likewise notes that **"70m rope is a minimum. Tie a knot on the end!"** And Dr Desire (6b) at Jurassic X-Mas says **"60m rope is minimum. Tie a knot at the end of the rope."**

## When to go

North Wall is in Ao Nang town and **can be climbed all year** — theCrag lists no seasonal closure.

| Period | Conditions | Planning note |
|---|---|---|
| Sep–Feb | Dry season, Krabi's peak season | The best window, and also the busiest. **The Jurassic X-Mas hornet warning was updated 2026-03-09** |
| Mar–May | Hot | Climb early and late. The wall faces north-west and is **always shady** |
| Jun–Aug | Monsoon | **Most routes can still be climbed in the rain** (see below) |

**This is North Wall's biggest advantage.** theCrag: "Majority of the routes can be climbed even on rainy days." That is rare at Thai island crags — Tonsai and Railay have walls that go completely wet.

**But mosquitoes are the catch.** theCrag's description says it plainly: **"watch out for the mosquitos - bring spray and mosquito coils."**

One more practical detail: theCrag tags the whole crag "Always shady", facing north-west. **That makes it considerably cooler than the walls around it** — a real advantage in the hot months.

## Getting there

### Public transport

**You can get here entirely on foot and by boat, which is the biggest difference from the other crags in this series.**

- From Ao Nang beach, follow the main road (4203) towards Krabi town for about 1.5 km.
- **Turn right into Soi 11, opposite the 'De Loft Hotel',** and follow the road for about 900 m.
- Where the road turns left — 200 m after the 'Emerald Muay Thai Gym' sign — **turn right for 30 m.**
- Then take the small trail slightly to the left into the forest, **follow the trail to the end (about 100 m) and park.**
- Walk about one minute; the crag is right in front of you.

Searching "Ao Nang North Wall" on Google Maps also works.

### By car or motorbike

The same route. **But parking is the biggest trap here**, and the next section covers it.

### Parking

**theCrag's first warning is about parking:**

> **Access: MUST Park Outside** — Do not park by pond at main entrance! **Best to use the entrance by the Emerald Muay Thai boxing gym.** Drive slightly past muay thai gym and Park inside. (Do not park outside Muay Thai gym compound and area).

theCrag attaches a map of the crag with the parking area marked.

There is a smaller but equally specific note too: **do not park your motorbike in front of the small lake at the end of the driveway.**

## Rules to know before you go

### The second warning: thieves

> **Access: Beware of thieves!** — Keep your valuables close-by when belaying! **Thieves are operating in the area targeting climbing equipment, phones and cash. Do not leave your stuff unattended!**

That warning is eight months old (around February 2026) and **it is the most recent warning of any kind in this series.** theCrag repeats it in the description: "Beware of theft at this crag! Especially during busy times. Keep your valuables and climbing gear close-by."

**What this means in practice**: do not put the gear bag on the ground and walk off to get water or take a photo. Rucksacks, rope bags, phones and wallets all stay on you or in sight. **This is a crag in town, not in the wild.**

### Two sectors carry wildlife warnings

**This is theCrag's most detailed warning set for North Wall, and it has a date on it.**

**Jurassic X-Mas (24 routes)**:

> **Flora and Fauna: Update: Hornet situation as of 9.3.2026** (updated 9 March 2026)
> **Big nest with a lot of traffic near the route Dr. Desire, also close to Santa Sends, watch out.**

**Rock'n Roll (28 routes)**: the whole sector carries a **"Flora and Fauna: Bird nest"** warning, applied to every route in it.

Several routes carry their own warnings:

- **Gravity (6c, Jurassic X-Mas)**: "**Do not top rope from the ring at the anchor.**"
- **The Stoneman (6a, Jurassic X-Mas)**: "**Top anchor rope super shredded.**"
- **Lady Rose (6c, Rock'n Roll)**: "**Two slings need replacing.**"
- **Tangled up in Blue (8a, Rock'n Roll)** carries a **"Hornets"** warning on top of the bird nest.

**"All titanium" does not apply to everything**: Slippery When Wet (6a+) is "1 bolt, 9 slings", Find a Heart (6a) is "4 bolts, 9 slings", Crazy Amounts of Pleasure (6b) is "4 bolts, 6 slings", and Pinball Wizard (6b, Life of Leif) is "all slings". **The bolts are titanium, but you still have to judge the slings yourself.**

### All titanium bolts

This is North Wall's main selling point: **every bolt is titanium, glued with Hilti 500.** That is completely different from Tonsai, where one route is all titanium and the one three metres away has rusty expansion bolts — **North Wall was developed after Christmas 2016 and carries none of that history.**

### Private land

theCrag's access note:

> **Access is through private property (plantation and a residence) so please be mindful as the property owners are fine with climbing there so long as climbers respect their property and privacy (house).**

### No drilling, no chipping

theCrag's Ethic field is a single sentence, but it is absolute:

> **Drilling, chipping and "enhancement" of rock is forbidden!**

It applies to every sector, since it sits at the crag level and is inherited by each one.

### Fee and facilities

- **Entry fee 50 THB, whole day.**
- **Equipment rental available**: rope, helmet, quickdraws and so on.
- **Two cubicles, one male and one female** — theCrag says "so you no longer have to answer the call of nature in a natural setting."
- theCrag also notes **good mobile signal** (a sharp contrast with Tonsai's off-grid conditions).

### Expert: 8a and up (YDS 5.13b and up)

**North Wall has three routes above 8a, and one of them is the hardest project line in Thailand.**

| Route | Grade | YDS | Length / pitches | Sector | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Can I Play with Madness | 8a | 5.13b | 20 m | Rock'n Roll | ★★★; the straight line to the right anchor, up the white face; all titanium bolts; **bird nest** |
| Air Jordan | 8a | 5.13b | 18 m | Rock'n Roll | ★★; same start as Red Lining / Dazed and Confused, then left via a crazy Superman dyno to the big hole; **bird nest** |
| Tangled up in Blue | 8a | 5.13b | 30 m | Rock'n Roll | ★★; **hornet warning on top of the bird nest** |
| Mad Max | 7c+ | 5.13a | — | Rock'n Roll | ★★★; shares the first jug of Can I Play with Madness then immediately right and up; **bird nest** |
| Furiosa | 9a | 5.14d | — | Rock'n Roll | ★★★; a four-bolt extension to Mad Max; **an open project — "please try!"** |

**Furiosa (9a) is the hardest route in Thailand as theCrag records it.** It is an open project with no length data and no ascent record.

**Mythical Beasts' per-route list tops out at 7c (Jaws).** The two 8a routes appear only in theCrag's icon list, with no length, no bolt notes and no description. **That is the "route exists but data is thin" case** — to climb them you take the guidebook and look on site.

## Where to look things up

- **Online databases:** theCrag's [North Wall page](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/1720715394) has all 103 routes, the six sectors, both warnings, the parking map, the approach, the fee and the facilities. The six sector pages each carry per-route grades, stars, lengths, bolts and wildlife warnings. **This crag has the most complete online data of anything in this series.**
- **Guidebooks:** grades and route counts here come mainly from online databases; the guidebook is the authority. **[The Pocket Guide – Thailand](https://justclimbthailand.com/krabi-climbing-guidebook)** (10th edition, 2026-01, 238 pages, 1,000 THB) covers it — **theCrag specifically notes that the 2026 edition updated the topos and photos for North Wall and Chong Phli** — as does **[King Climbers](https://railay.com/railay/climbing/climbing_book.shtml)** (10th edition, 2025, which includes Ao-Nang Mainland's Chong Phli and North Wall).
- **Live on-the-ground information:** [Krabi Bolting Fund](https://ko-fi.com/krabiboltingfund) maintains the bolts on the Krabi mainland, and both theCrag and the crag pages point at it. **It is the right channel for checking current North Wall status, or for supporting the work.**
