Koh Tao is an island off Suratthani in southern Thailand, where the ferries and the diving are the main trade. **For climbers, though, it is Thailand's only genuine granite bouldering destination** — theCrag logged 496 routes on 21 October 2026, graded with the Font bouldering system from 1A+ up to 8A+.

**The rules here are unlike every other crag in this series: this is a community crag, not an open public resource.**

## What kind of crag this is

Granite. theCrag's summary: "**Koh Tao is an amazing granite paradise!** With granite boulders all over the island offering everything from bouldering to sports climbing and even some awesome trad lines."

496 routes spread across 15 sectors, **more than two thirds of them bouldering**:

**The logic here is the reverse of everywhere else in Thailand: rather than looking for sport routes first, you look for the bouldering.** Secret garden has 21 starred problems, Backyard & Frontyard has seven, and Golden View has 22 starred sport routes — **these are the best-documented corners of the island.**

### The only Thai crag with a proper guidebook

theCrag's Koh Tao description has one unusual feature:

> For the most up-to-date info on the island contact The Koh Tao Climbing Club on Facebook or Instagram.
> Check out the link below if you want the best guidebook for the island. **It's an app** and can be downloaded easily. All money raised from the app go directly into route development and maintenance.

**This is the only crag in the Thai series where the primary guidebook is an app**, and the only one with a "buying the guide funds the development" structure. It costs US$8.

## When to go

**Koh Tao is in southern Thailand and works all year, but the monsoon matters a lot.**

| Period | Conditions | Planning note |
|---|---|---|
| Nov–Feb | Dry season | The best window. 28–33°C by day, around 23°C at night |
| Mar–May | Getting hot | Granite gets very hot in the sun, **boulder early or late** |
| Jun–Oct | Monsoon (south-west) | **Swells and rain stop the ferries** — this is the real risk |
| Oct | End of the wet season | |

**The seasonal risk here is not that the rock is un-climbable — it is that the boats do not run.** Southern Thailand's southwest monsoon from May to October regularly cancels or delays ferries. **Build slack into the plan rather than making a specific sailing the one thing you cannot replace.**

**The southern monsoon is the wettest part of Thailand.** Put the other way round, November to February is the most stable climbing window of the year.

## Getting there

### Public transport

**There are no direct flights.** All access to Koh Tao is by boat:

- Boats from **Ko Samui's airport**. **Ko Samui has international and domestic flights** and is the main way in.
- Boats from **Chumphon**.
- There is no direct boat from Krabi or Phuket.
- **Several ferry companies run daily services**; backpacker hostels and dive shops run shuttles.

theCrag puts it simply: "There are multiple ferry lines that arrive at Koh Tao daily. **The closest Airport is on Koh Samui.**"

### By car

**Pointless.** Nothing comes across by road; everything moves by boat.

### Parking

Not applicable.

## Rules to know before you go

### Bolting needs the club's permission

theCrag's Ethic field is unambiguous:

> Don't litter.
> **Please don't bolt without permission of the Koh Tao Climbing Club.**

That is the most unusual rule in the whole Thai series. **Everywhere else it is "no drilling, no chipping, no enhancing the rock"; here there is an extra layer: without the club's agreement, you should not be adding routes at all.** Because this is a place members and the community pay to maintain.

### Some sectors have sensitive access

theCrag's Access field:

> **Some of the crags on Koh Tao have sensitive access issues, please contact the Koh Tao Climbing Club for more details.**

What that means in practice: **some areas — rock on private land or inside resorts — cannot simply be walked into.** Decide which sector you want before you go, and ask in the club's Facebook group.

theCrag also flags Golden View's access as **Designated**, noting "Road is mostly paved. On the steep side. **Be mindful of residents living along the approach.**"

### Where to rent gear

theCrag says: "If you want to rent gear that can be done from either **Goodtime Adventures or The Bunker**."

**The Bunker is the island's only indoor gym and bouldering hall**, and also a hostel (The Bunker Climbing Gym & Hostel) and the meeting point for the crag community. **A free PDF guidebook is available via a QR code at The Bunker.** That is the first place to go when you land.

### Finding partners

theCrag points at three things:

- **The Koh Tao Climbing Club's Facebook page** ([Climbingkohtao](https://www.facebook.com/Climbingkohtao)) — **the club goes out climbing every Friday**.
- A public climbers' Facebook group.
- **The Rakkup app** itself.

## Routes by level

**Koh Tao uses Font bouldering grades, not French sport grades.** Following the `scripts/climbing-grade-convert.mjs` spec, bouldering gets its own table: `| 路線 | 難度（Font） | V 級 | 子區 | 備註 |`.

YDS and V-grade conversions follow the [Mountain Project international grade comparison chart](https://www.mountainproject.com/international-climbing-grades) and are approximate; "approx." marks grades the sources give without a/b/c (such as 4 or 5+). The original grade is the one that counts.

**The tables are the 28 routes of Golden View, the sport sector, read from theCrag's sector page on 21 October 2026.** It is the best-documented sport area on the island. **The island's 300-plus bouldering routes are not listed one by one here** — the guidebook *is* an app, and theCrag's per-boulder data is far less complete than its sport data.

**theCrag's icon list separately names ten island-wide representative problems** (with no lengths): Trust your Feet 5B, River 5C, Balance 6A/A+, The Edge 6A+, Grunt Force 6B, Do It! 6B+, Titan 6C+, Trauma Extension 7A+, Flying To The Moon 7B, Mucalinda Naga 7B+.

**Three boulder areas carry conditions you have to check:**

- **Boulder 5 is tagged "Abrasive rock"** — the rock in this sector is rough on hands.
- **Boulder 7 is tagged "watch the tide".**
- **Boulder 9 is tagged "low tide only".**

**This is the biggest difference between island bouldering and inland bouldering: the tide decides whether you can get up there.** Check the tide table before you go, not the weather forecast.

### Expert: 8a and up (YDS 5.13b and up)

**Golden View's route list has nothing above 8a in sport grades.** The island's theCrag page shows a grade filter going up to **8A+**, but that is the Font bouldering scale (V11 and above), not YDS 8a.

The only clue is Golden View's **Kun Touat Project** (20 m, 10 bolts) — theCrag writes it as the mixed notation "7c+/8a" and marks it as a project. **It is not a route with checkable length and bolt condition.**

**That is the state of this band: the data is incomplete.** Koh Tao's 8A+ bouldering routes exist in theCrag's filter but have no findable per-route details. **To climb this band, ask the club on the island or read the Rakkup app.**

## Where to look things up

- **Online databases:** theCrag's [Koh Tao page](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/koh-tao) has all 496 routes, the 15 sectors, the access rules, rental information and the club contact details. [Golden View](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/koh-tao/area/1226641539) has 28 sport routes with per-route details, the approach description and bolt notes.
- **The main guidebook:** the [Rakkup App](https://rakkup.com/guidebooks/thailand-koh-tao-rock-climbing/) (US$8). **This is the most complete route data on the island, and all the app's revenue goes into route development and maintenance.** theCrag also mentions a free PDF guide at The Bunker via QR code.
- **The club:** [Koh Tao Climbing Club on Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Climbingkohtao) — they go climbing every Friday, and they are the place to ask which sectors are open. **Join them before you fly.**
- **Guidebooks:** grades and route counts here come mainly from online databases; the guidebook is the authority. **Koh Tao is in none of the printed Thai guides** — The Pocket Guide and King Climbers both cover the southern islands and Krabi, not Koh Tao. **Koh Tao's guidebook is the Rakkup app and the free PDF.**
- **Rental and gym:** Goodtime Adventures and The Bunker. **The Bunker is the island's only indoor gym and the crag meeting point.**
