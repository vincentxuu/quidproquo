Railay is on the west side of the Phra-Nang peninsula, Tonsai on the east, with a narrow channel between. theCrag logged **314 routes** on 21 October 2026 and 19,094 ascents.

**This guide covers the east side**: walking right from the beach you pass Muay Thai, then the 123 Wall, then higher up The Keep, Hidden World and Diamond Cave. **This is climbing school ground, and the busiest part of the island.**

## What kind of crag this is

Limestone. theCrag's description of the whole of Railay is one line: **"Thailand's climbing mecca."** The east side's sectors:

| Sector | Routes | Height | Character |

| One-Two-Three (123 Wall) | 31 | 21 m | The most routes, many easy ones, **the most crowded** |

**One line from theCrag's Railay page has to be read before anything else:**

> **This area is actively being rebolted at time of writing (2023) and much of the info on this website is outdated. Pop into one of the many local climbing shops and grab a guidebook as well as chat with the lovely local guides for the most up to date info. Please consider donating to the local rebolting fund if you have the means.**

**That sentence is the ceiling on all the data in this series.** Every route figure here comes from theCrag, and theCrag says its own data may be behind. **So bolt condition in this guide is always written as "theCrag records this as…" — never as "this route is titanium."**

| Mar | Getting hot | |

**Muay Thai and Diamond Cave are almost permanently shaded** (theCrag tags both "Always shady"). **That is the east side's advantage over the west, where the walls are shaded in the afternoon instead.**

**Low Tide Wall's name is literal.** theCrag's One-Two-Three page carries a "Low tide" tag — some routes are only reachable at low water.

## Getting there

### Public transport

**There is no road access.** Take a longtail boat from Ao Nang; **the boats that go to Railay stop at Tonsai first**. Boats only run in daylight.

### By car or motorbike

Vehicles stay on the Ao Nang side. Motorbikes can go across by boat but it is a hassle. **Most people stay in a hotel in Ao Nang or on the Railay side and take the boat over each day.**

### Approaches

Muay Thai's approach is specific: **"Walk all the way to the right of Railay East bay. Routes start right of the beach and in the corner a little up behind the small hut."** 30 to 45 minutes.

One-Two-Three: **"Go all the way to the right side of Railay East bay. Pass the 'Muay Thai' sector right on the small beach. Behind some of the big boulders are the first routes of 'One-Two-Three'."**

Diamond Cave has two routes: up the walking street at the end of Railay beach, or **the steep jungle trail from Tonsai** — up past "Mama's Chicken" from Basecamp, through the forest for 20 minutes to a small meadow, through the high grass to the road, then right, with the Phutawan resort on your right and the crag in front of you.

## Rules to know before you go

### Crowding: the structural problem of the east side

**Muay Thai's description**: "Vertical climbing on highly structured though polished orange rock and some short routes on sharp rock up in the corner on the right side. **Always busy with climbing schools and spectators. Do not come here if you prefer quiet spots.**"

**One-Two-Three's description**: "Top quality rock with many easier graded routes. **Warning, this crag gets extremely busy with climbing schools. Do not come here between 9 am and 4 pm if you don't like crowds.**"

**Diamond Cave's description**: "**Extremely busy during climbing school hours. Not recommended to climb here between 9 am and 4 pm.**"

**Three walls, the same sentence.** In practice: **go before 8am or after 4pm**, or skip these three walls and go to Thaiwand on the west side.

### Bolt condition: different in every sector

**Muay Thai (24 routes)**:

- **The left side is mostly titanium bolts plus slings.**
- **Reaching Like a Monkey (6c+)**: "**Not rebolted with titanium. All stainless steel glue-in bolts.**"
- **Piece of Cake (6a)**: "**Old rusty expansion bolts. Do not climb!**"
- **No Name (6a, right side)**: same warning, "**Old rusty expansion bolts. Do not climb!**"
- **Deborah (6b)**: same warning.
- **Several simple routes on the right side are "all slings"** (Tamada, Hello Christine, Take It to the Left, Take It Easy, The Beacons Memorial Climb, The Devine Ascention of Sully, Unknown Route).
- **Vegan Snake (6a)** can only be climbed top-roped from Take It Easy's anchors.

**One-Two-Three (31 routes)**:

- **The three old routes on the left (No Name 6a, Mei Mi Arom 6c+, Hello Tu 6b)** are all rusty expansion bolts, marked "Do not climb unless rebolted!"
- **All four Kratoy extensions are marked un-climbable**: One Thousand Knives (6b+), Lucky (6b+), La Riba (7c+) and Triana (7b+).
- **Gandalf (8a+)**: "Second pitch of 'We Sad'… **Old rusty expansion bolts. Do not climb unless rebolted!**"
- **Massage Secrets (6b)**: of its three pitches **only P1 and P2 can be climbed**; P3 is old expansion bolts and loose rock, "Do not climb!". theCrag adds: P1 and P2's anchors have been rebolted with 4 titanium bolts, and skipping the midway anchor (which was set for 'Beginner') links P1 into a 30-metre single pitch with 7 titanium bolts.
- **The Pinnacle has been fully rebolted** — that is the good news on this wall.

**Diamond Cave (28 routes)**:

- **The three old routes around the left corner of the main face (Attic Antics 6a+, Thread of Life 6b+, Jugs-o-Plenty 6b)** have old expansion bolts and slings. **Thread of Life is marked "loose rock, old slings. Do not climb!" and Jugs-o-Plenty "old expansion bolts. Do not climb!"**
- **Keep the Jam, Man (7a)**: three pitches, **only P1 and P2 have been rebolted**; the P3 slings have been removed, "Do not climb!".
- **Mot Daeng (5)** carries a fixed-gear warning.
- **Seven Seven Seven (6c)** carries two warnings — **"Insect(wasp?) nest near 3rd sling"** and **"One of the anchor slings for the first pitch needs replacing (sheath worn through, core exposed)"**.
- **Dreamteam (6b+)**: "Old slings and expansion bolts. Do not climb!"
- **No Name 3 (6b)**: "Not rebolted with titanium. Stainless steel glue-ins."
- **The North Face Right Side area** (Nullaktion 6a, Dreamteam, Les Petites Oreilles 6b+, Diamonds are Forever 6b+, Seven Seven Seven, Attacktit 6b+, Almost Heaven 7a+): theCrag says "**often free, even during climbing school hours**" — **this is the best pick on the east side if you want to avoid the crowds.**
- **The old routes inside the actual cave** (Unknown 6b, Mr No Name 6b+, Mrs No Name 6b+, Lost Sock in the Laundromat of Oblivion 6b+, Bar You Tee Nai? 7a, Sixty Degrees North 7a+, Mao Say 7a+): "**Bolting status unclear, probably old expansion bolts. Anyway most certainly not anymore climbable due to the existence of the Diamond Cave National Park.**"

### Expert: 8a and up (YDS 5.13b and up)

**Railay East has exactly one route above 8a, and you cannot climb it.**

| Route | Grade | YDS | Length / pitches | Sector | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gandalf | 8a+ | 5.13c | 28 m | One-Two-Three | ★; the second pitch of We Sad; **old rusty expansion bolts, do not climb unless rebolted** |
| Strider (Direct) | 8a+ | 5.13c | 28 m | Low Tide Wall | ★; **no bolts, top rope from Strider** |

**The east side's 8a+ band is therefore empty.** The real high-grade climbing sits on the west side, in [Thaiwand and Candlestick](/posts/climbing/2026-10-21-thailand-crag-railay-west-phra-nang), with lines like Lord of the Thais 7b and Continental Drifters 7c+.

## Where to look things up

- **Online databases:** theCrag's [Railay page](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/13949881) has all 314 routes and the sector structure, and carries the "2023, still being rebolted, much of the info is outdated" warning. [Muay Thai](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/15687313), [One-Two-Three](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/15687337) and [Diamond Cave](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/thailand/krabi/area/13949929) have per-route details and bolt notes. **Bolt notes differ in every sector, so you have to read sector by sector.**
- **Guidebooks:** grades and route counts here come mainly from online databases; the guidebook is the authority. Two cover Railay: **[The Pocket Guide – Thailand](https://justclimbthailand.com/krabi-climbing-guidebook)** (10th edition, 2026-01, 238 pages, 1,000 THB, 800-plus routes) and **[King Climbers](https://railay.com/railay/climbing/climbing_book.shtml)** (10th edition, 2025, 85 areas and more than 1,000 routes). **theCrag itself advises "pop into one of the many local climbing shops and grab a guidebook as well as chat with the lovely local guides" — that is the most useful thing you can do here.**
- **The actual rebolting list:** **not findable online.** It is in the guidebooks and on printed lists in the King Climbers shop in Railay and Watts bar in Tonsai.
- **Rental and guiding:** [justclimbthailand's rental page](https://justclimbthailand.com/climbing-equipment-sale-and-rental/) and [course page](https://justclimbthailand.com/climbing-course-thailand/) list rope, quickdraw and private guiding prices.
