The [Insubong crag guide](/posts/climbing/2026-10-21-korea-crag-insubong-en) helps you pick a route for your team. At the foot of the wall in Bukhansan, Seoul, you need more: where does this route start, how long is each pitch, which pitch is the crux, and how do you get down afterwards? This page fills that gap.

**This is not a topo.** It is compiled from Korean climbing-community write-ups dated 2006 to 2024, with no wall photos and no route numbers. On the day, route-finding for the east-face routes should rely on the [Mont Blanc Lines East Face digital topo](https://www.montblanclines.com/products/%ec%9d%b8%ec%88%98%eb%b4%89-insubong-east-face) (an east-face photo overlaid with routes and a list of 43 routes beneath it; the product page offers a medium-resolution download for personal use and also sells printed posters; each route's section gives its number there). **The south-face routes Dongyang-gil, Haneul-gil, Georyong-gil and Villa are not among those 43**, so for those four this page and the concept maps cited below are all there is. Use this page as preparation before the trip and as a cross-check at the base.

## How to use this page

1. Read the four landmarks in the next section first. Most starts are described as "walk left or right from the Great Slab for a few dozen metres."
2. Look at the [length and grade overview](#length-and-grade-overview) and pick the routes you want.
3. Go to that route's section for the start, the pitch table, the crux and the descent.
4. Where the length or grade differs between sources, the notes give both versions. **Use the lengths to recognise a route, never to calculate rappel rope length.**
5. In this page "station" means belay station, and "band" means a horizontal ledge running across the face.

## Four landmarks on the wall

The sources keep coming back to these four landmarks when they describe a start:

- **Great Slab (대슬랩)**: the wide slab at the bottom of the east face. Most east- and south-face routes start by walking left or right from it.
- **Oasis (오아시스)**: the platform above the Great Slab. Insu A, Medical School and Sanchonji start here. A [2024 Sanchonji report](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=r9600495&logNo=223445895731) reaches it in two pitches from the Great Slab, with the first double-bolt station about 50 m up. Despite the name there is no spring.
- **Ear Rock (귀바위)**: the ear-shaped block at the top right, where Chouinard A/B and Medical School end.
- **Spring (샘터)**: the water source on the left side of the Great Slab, used as the reference point for Crony's start.

## Starting from Oasis

All three routes start from Oasis. Taking Insu A as the centre, [Power's 2013 summary](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=jsm626&logNo=70175592819) puts Gunghyeong-gil (궁형길) to the right with Medical School above it, and, going left, Yeong-gil, Yeomyeong-gil, Ujeong B, Sanchonji, PASI and Wand in that order.

### Insu A (인수 A)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #26.
- **Start**: Oasis. Reach Oasis via the Great Slab, then start from there.
- **Pitches and length**: 4 pitches, 115 m, cracks and chimneys throughout.
- **Gear**: one set of cams, 8 quickdraws.
- **Feel**: the [2013 summary](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=jsm626&logNo=70175592819) says it shares its top grade (5.8) with Lonely Road but is harder in practice, a tiring "labour route."

| Pitch | Length | Grade | Rock | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 40 m | 5.6 | Crack | |
| 2 | 20 m | 5.7 | Crack | |
| 3 | 25 m | 5.8 | Crack | Crux. A traverse with almost no hands and few feet; a slip without protection means a vertical drop. |
| 4 | 30 m | 5.7 | Crack | Ends just before Yeongja Slab (영자슬랩), where Lonely Road (고독길) pitch 6 also ends. |

When it is crowded, people use the variation line or an empty line next to it.

- **Back down after finishing**: rappel Insu A from Yeongja Crack (영자크랙) to Oasis, about 90 m; the 2013 report did 30 m then 60 m.
- **West-side descent**: see [Descent](#descent) at the end.

Sources: [Girly's 2018 concept-map page](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232), [Power's 2013 summary](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=jsm626&logNo=70175592819) and [Meruraeng's 2013 report](https://hsun-100hsun-100.tistory.com/7984297).

### Medical School (의대길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #27.
- **Start**: the middle of Oasis ([Meruraeng](https://hsun-100hsun-100.tistory.com/7984297): "Uidae-gil starts from the central Oasis").
- **Pitches and length**: 5 to 6 pitches, about 100 to 136 m; sources split it differently.

| Pitch | Length | Grade | Rock | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 27 m | 5.8 | Slab | Another summary says 25 m. |
| 2 | 22 m | 5.9 | Crack | Another summary says about 20 m and 5.10a, and calls it the hardest free pitch on the route, needing jamming technique. |
| 3 | 12 m | 5.10a A0 | Slab | Aid: five bolts in a row. The gap between the 4th and 5th is long, so stand on the 4th before clipping the 5th. |
| 4 | 28 m | 5.8 | Slab | Another summary records this as a 20 m, 5.6 crack. |
| 5 | 20 m | 5.6 | Slab | A traverse. It ends below Ear Rock where it meets Gunghyeong-gil, the busiest spot. |
| 6 | 27 m | 5.10b | Slab | Ear Rock. The hard part is the first moves past the first bolt. |

Most parties stop at pitch 5 and go down to Oasis, which is easier than climbing to Ear Rock and using the west descent. If you go to Ear Rock, rappel Medical School; [Girly's text](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232) gives two 50 m ropes. Look at who is below before you rappel: in Meruraeng's report a partner dropped a carabiner while retrieving gear after rappelling Insu A to Oasis, and it hit someone below preparing for Medical School, luckily causing only a bruise. Sources: Girly, Power, Meruraeng and a [recollection of a 2009 ascent](https://stories4rose.tistory.com/23).

### Sanchonji (산천지길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #21.
- **Start**: Oasis.
- **Pitches and length**: 5 pitches, 119 m. The [2024 report](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=r9600495&logNo=223445895731) matches Girly's table.

| Pitch | Length | Grade | Rock | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 20 m | 5.8 | Slab / crack | |
| 2 | 20 m | 5.10d | Slab | Crux. From the "mute crack" (벙어리 크랙) into a slab with three bolts. |
| 3 | 40 m | 5.8 | Slab / crack | |
| 4 | 13 m | 5.11a | Slab | Goes over an overhang; the second crux. |
| 5 | 26 m | 5.9 | Slab | |

The 2024 party of six reported:

- **Route**: 2 Great Slab pitches, then 5 Sanchonji pitches, then the summit.
- **Time**: 08:30 to 16:00, about 7.5 hours.
- **Ropes**: one 70 m rope, three 60 m ropes and one 45 m rope.
- **Great Slab pitch 1**: the existing station was too small, so they built a temporary one with cams.

## Right of the Great Slab

### Chouinard B (취나드 B)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #29 (listed there as Chouinard B – Kwibawi C).
- **Start**: walk right across the slope from the Great Slab to a small rise with a stone monument. With your back to the monument, look up: the crack with a stepped ledge is the start. [Girly's page](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232) gives about 50 m in one passage and about 70 m in another for the walk from the Great Slab to the monument, so go by seeing the monument.
- **Pitches and length**: 5 pitches, 177 m, about 2 hours for a pair, one 50 m rope and one set of cams.
- **Cruxes**: the thin undercrack on pitch 2 and the double crack on pitch 4.

| Pitch | Length | Grade | Rock | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 35 m | 5.7 | Crack | The text version says 20 m: start beside a small tree on the gentle wooded slope, climb the fine crack, then about 7 m of easy slab to a double-bolt station. |
| 2 | 37 m | 5.8 | Crack | A thin undercrack traversing right, with a few bolts; at the old pine, build the station on the double bolts to the left, not on the tree. |
| 3 | 25 m | 5.6 / 5.7 | Crack | Step across right from the pine and go straight up the crack to a double-bolt station. The two sources differ on the grade. |
| 4 | 40 m | 5.8 | Crack | From the station, climb a short face into the big crack on the left to reach a cave; when clipping the bolt beside the cave, use a long sling so the rope runs well. Past the cave is the famous double crack: steep, but in good condition. |
| 5 | 40 m | 5.6 / 5.7 | Crack | Straight up a big crack, body-sized at first and narrowing and improving higher; finish at the double bolts at Ear Rock. |

Reaching Ear Rock counts as the end. To top out, climb the roughly 20 m Ear Rock slab (5.10b) to the piton on top, make a short rappel of about 10 m into the gap on the summit side to the west, then climb Yeongja Crack. **Descent:** rappel Medical School from Ear Rock with two 50 m ropes.

### Chouinard A (취나드 A)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #36 (listed there as Chouinard A – Kwibawi D).
- **Start**: from the Chouinard B monument, walk about 50 m further right to a vertical crack splitting the wall below Ear Rock. On the pitch-1 terrace, the crack on the right is Simu-gil (심우길), the one straight up the middle is Chouinard A, and the slab on the left is Beot-gil (벗길).
- **Pitches and length**: 6 pitches, 181 m; 2 to 3 hours for a pair, one set of cams, 7 quickdraws, one 50 m rope.
- **Crux**: the overhanging crack on pitch 4.

| Pitch | Length | Grade | Rock | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 21 m | 5.7 | Low-angle | No protection; there are trees for an anchor and the crack on the right takes cams. It ends at a roomy terrace with double bolts. |
| 2 | 33 m | 5.7 / 5.8 | Crack | A wide crack close to a half-chimney, needing cams and with no bolts; a cam stuck deep in the middle takes a quickdraw, and past the pool-like wide spot you reach the double bolts. The left pair belongs to Beot-gil, the pair beside the big crack to Chouinard A. |
| 3 | 22 m | 5.8 | Crack | A good right-trending crack at about 70 degrees with one bolt in the middle; Camalot 3 and 4 work well. It ends at double bolts and a roomy ledge. |
| 4 | 40 m | 5.10a (text version 5.10b) | Crack | A good crack to an overhang with one old piton, then a bolt above the lip. After about 4 m there is another bolt on the right, then no protection: place the full set of cams, and take care at the layback-to-jam transition. |
| 5 | 40 m | 5.6 | Crack | The text version treats the climb as finished after pitch 4. To top out, follow the Simu-gil crack to below Ear Rock, traverse, and climb Yeongja Crack. |
| 6 | 25 m | 5.5 | Slab | |

**Descent:** after pitch 4, go right to the gnarled tree under the last Simu-gil pitch, where there are double bolts, and rappel from there; a sling threaded under the rock also works. **Do not rappel Chouinard A itself**: ropes often jam in the crack. The text version's setup is to rappel Simu-gil to Chouinard A pitch 3 first, then use two 50 m ropes in one rappel to the pitch-1 terrace.

## Left of the Great Slab

Routes on this side are packed onto the same wall, so positions are given relative to each other. Dongyang-gil, Haneul-gil, Georyong-gil and Villa are not on the Mont Blanc East Face topo, so there is no ready-made numbered diagram to check against. Putting several passages of [Girly's page](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232) together, the order from bottom to top is: Cheongmaek-gil (청맥길), then Dongyang-gil (about 120 m from the Great Slab), Haneul-gil, Georyong-gil (about 180 m) and Villa (about 200 m); below Georyong there is also the single-pitch Miryeon (미련). All distances are "left and up from the Great Slab," estimated rather than measured.

### Insu B (인수 B)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: no entry of that name. Several reports say the usual way in is two pitches up Yongam Slab (#6, 5.9) to Insu B's Oasis, so the topo may draw only the lower part.
- **Start**: left of the Great Slab. [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/route/108367078/insu-b) says it is on the left (south) side of the big slab near the crack system where Crony is, reached by walking up the river bed along the base, about 5 minutes from the sign with the map.
  - **Current way**: start up the 용암슬랩 (Yongam Slab) left of the Great Slab and reach Insu B's Oasis in 2 pitches (reports from [2024](https://gaussmt.tistory.com/1836) and [2015](https://ami41.tistory.com/746)).
  - **Old way**: a straight crack about 30 m up on the left, or the Great Slab itself, about 100 m cut into roughly three pitches ([route description on a Daum cafe](https://m.cafe.daum.net/hyedoji71/GqbR/109)).
- **Pitch count**: 5 to 8 depending on the source. MP gives 5 pitches and 136 m; the Daum description gives 6; the 2024 report climbed 8 (including 2 on Yongam Slab, each 40 to 60 m). The 3 pitches and 97 m in the 2012 guide and Girly's table are only the lower part, not the whole route.
- **Crux**: the pitch-3 crack. The Daum description grades it 5.8 and says the crack narrows as you go up, so foot jams hurt.
- **History**: [Hankyoreh 2012](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/specialsection/esc_section/553495.html) calls it the "eldest brother" of Insubong's routes, first climbed by Koreans in 1935; the Daum description says the first ascensionists did not use the Great Slab for the first pitch but the straight crack on the left.

The table below is MP's five-pitch version; the grades are MP users' estimates.

| Pitch | Grade | Description |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 5.6? | Up between two obvious grooves, one move out of the crack, then right to a slabby finish; the last move onto the slab is the harder one. |
| 2 | 5.8 | A few metres of slab, then follow the line between the large block on the right and the tree on the left into a crack, to the station below the off-width. |
| 3 | 5.8 | The obvious off-width with bolts on the right; a physical jam. Finishes near a tree. |
| 4 | 5.7 | The obvious crack straight up above the tree, exiting left past an interesting rock formation. |
| 5 | 5.6 | A low-angle crack, then a careful scramble to the top. |

**MP's warning**: after the pitch-4 station, followers should walk up and left to a ledge, then scramble back right to near a fallen tree (10 to 15 m) to reach the start of pitch 5; starting pitch 5 from the pitch-4 station runs you short of rope.

**More detail from Korean reports:**

- **Pitch 2**: [Hankyoreh](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/specialsection/esc_section/553495.html) calls it the "jar crack" (항아리 크랙), worn smooth, so place cams. A [2021 report](https://m.blog.naver.com/wooltraveler/222329258441) says the last move is up a jar-shaped block and is not easy.
- **Pitch 4**: Hankyoreh describes a crack about 15 m long that takes half your body, then a band to the left called the "dragon footprint"; a fall there is risky, so clip a quickdraw before crossing.
- **Variations**: a [2026 report](https://gaussmt.tistory.com/2003) climbed pitch 3 on the "splitter crack" between the Amidong crack and Insu B's jar crack; at the lying-pine station the standard line follows the band left, but they took the straight crack, whose upper part overlaps Sanggongsa. The 2021 party said the crack on the right is the original Insu B crack, climbed the thin crack in the middle of three, and merged into Ujŏng A near the end.
- **Near the top**: finish up the fixed rope on Chamgireum Rock (참기름바위, "sesame-oil rock," polished smooth); there is now a last station on top.

**Descent:** MP says to rappel the south side from the right-most stations, one rappel with two 60 m ropes tied together; the 2024 report used the west-side rappel point in two rappels of 30 m.

### Crony (크로니길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #3.
- **Start**: there is a spring on the left of the Great Slab. About 20 m straight up from the spring and onto a flat slab is Geonyang-gil (건양길); the route that starts in the crack on the right is Crony.
- **Pitches and length**: 9 pitches, 237 m. [Power's summary](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=jsm626&logNo=70175592819) says it was the longest line on Insubong before Rescuer was opened. About 3 hours for a pair, one set of cams and one 50 m rope.

| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

| 2 | 26 m | 5.7 | Crack / slab | Along the crack, then right along the band and straight up to the double bolts in the middle of the slab. |
| 3 | n/a | n/a | n/a | The older version split pitches 2 and 3; today most parties link them into one pitch to the station in the middle of the slab. |

### Villa (빌라길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: none; this route is not among its 43.
- **Start**: about 200 m up from the Great Slab. Georyong starts directly below it; from underneath, the whole of pitch 1's crack is visible.
- **Pitches and length**: 6 pitches, 165 m; one set of cams, 12 quickdraws, one 50 m rope; about 2 hours for a pair.
- **Version differences**: [Girly's text](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232) describes only 5 pitches while its table lists 6, the last being A2. Two bolts at the crux have been removed over the years, so the grade may have been revised. ## Other locations

### Lonely Road (고독의 길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #38 (5.6).
- **Start**: on the far right of the East Face.
  - [Power's summary](https://m.blog.naver.com/PostView.naver?blogId=jsm626&logNo=70175592819): from Harujae go past the Insu campground and the Pigeon Spring (비둘기샘), walk under the Great Slab all the way to its right end.
  - [theCrag](https://www.thecrag.com/en/climbing/south-korea/insubong/route/1016936403): the far right, on a ledge with some trees, just past Chouinard A.
  - [Mountain Project](https://www.mountainproject.com/route/112120315/go-deok-wei-gil): trace the base to the right until you are below the obvious crack of Chouinard A, scramble a few metres further right and go up some class 3/4 terrain to the start; it is hard to find.
  - [2017 report](https://blog.naver.com/PostView.nhn?blogId=sigint&logNo=221114366546): the official pitch-1 start is a big pine; climb the chimney gap on the left, or go slightly right and climb a short section first. Their party of seven moved right and started from "pitch 0".
- **Pitches**: MP gives 8 pitches and about 152 m, saying every pitch is around 5.6 with some walking in between; theCrag gives 6 pitches and 350 m. [Hankyoreh 2012](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/specialsection/esc_section/553495.html) gives 8 pitches, hardest 5.8, widely considered the easiest route on Insubong and the one most beginners climb. It is very crowded at weekends, so go on a weekday, and do not skimp on protection just because it is easy.

| Pitch | Length | Grade | Rock | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | about 15 m | 5.6 | Crack | Three ways; take the left crack, with good holds. |
| 2 | about 30 m | 5.6 | Crack | Place one cam; you reach a cave afterwards. |
| 3 | about 30 m | 5.8 | Layback crack | There is also a 5.6 gully crack on the left. |
| 4 | about 10 m | 5.6 | Crack | The view opens and Seolgyobyeok appears. |
| 5 | about 40 m | 5.6 | Gully crack | Climb right and pass through the "dog hole" (개구멍) to the right of Ear Rock. |
| 6 | about 5 m | n/a | Short steps | Step up like stairs, then cross the left foot to the rock on the left; this is where it joins Insu A, at the end of Insu A pitch 4. |

After that come Yeongja Crack (pitch 7) and Chamgireum Rock (pitch 8). **MP says the hardest part of the route is the 10 to 15 ft slab just below the summit**: no anchors, no bolts, nowhere for gear, so you solo it; the rock is polished and greasy from the traffic, and a fall means scraping down to uneven rocks below, so have a partner spot you. There is now a fixed rope there. Above pitch 2 there is a cave with awkward boulders that are hard to pass with a pack, and it also serves as an informal toilet, so it smells strongly (see the [2017 report](https://blog.naver.com/PostView.nhn?blogId=sigint&logNo=221114366546)). After Rescuer pitch 6 you can traverse over to pitch 3 of this route to bail.

**Descent:** MP says to follow the fixed rope down the back of the summit to a single rappel station, use the right-most station, and one rappel with two 60 m ropes reaches the ground; from the left station two 60 m ropes may be too short. theCrag's version is to walk southeast to find a rope, then west to a horizontal chain of roughly 10 m of bolts, and use the western-most anchors for a 60 m rappel to the ground.

### Pigeon (비둘기길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: none; this route is not among its 43.
- **Start**: [Hankyoreh](https://www.hani.co.kr/arti/specialsection/esc_section/553495.html) says the start is the saddle where Baegundae meets Insubong, "the descent route climbed in reverse"; from just before Rescuer, take the path to the right towards the campground and walk about 100 m towards Insubong to reach Pigeon Spring.

### Rescuer (구조대길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #39.
- **Start**: no text states it. The [2013 full-length report](https://killerwasan.tistory.com/439) only says "pitch 1 start."
- **Pitches and length**: 12 pitches, about 300 to 360 m, 25 to 30 m each. It is said to take about 5 hours for a pair; that party had a group of five ahead who waited at least 20 minutes per pitch, and took about 10 hours.
- **Gear**: two 60 m ropes, and pitch 6 needs 12 quickdraws; they carried cams but did not use them, as the bolts are well placed.
- **Escape**: after Rescuer pitch 6 you can traverse to Lonely Road (고독길) pitch 3 to bail; conversely, on Lonely Road, after pitch 2 you can switch to Rescuer pitch 7. **Crux:** pitch 6 (5.11b), an overhanging undercrack that you work along like playing piano. If a second pulls on or steps on a bolt or sling it counts as aid.
- **Descent**: rappel Medical School four times to the Chouinard B start.

### Amidong (아미동길)

- **Mont Blanc East Face topo number**: #1.
- **Start**: from a [2018 report](https://tksdkrwjs.tistory.com/1272): take a taxi to Doseonsa, walk to Harujae (하루재), then about 30 minutes uphill behind Insu Hermitage (인수암).
- **Pitch count**: Girly's table records 3 pitches and 90 m (40 m 5.6 slab, 40 m 5.10a crack / slab, 10 m); the [main guide](/posts/climbing/2026-10-21-korea-crag-insubong-en) uses the 7 pitches of the original opening. Both versions exist side by side.

## Descent

The descent setups on this page are each source's own wording. Rappel points and rope lengths should follow the topo and the situation on the day; see the [main guide's descent section](/posts/climbing/2026-10-21-korea-crag-insubong-en) for details. A few shared facts:

- **West-side rappel point**: [Girly's page](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232) says four rappels with a single 50 m rope or two with double ropes to reach the ground.
- **Around Ear Rock** (Chouinard A/B, Medical School): rappel Medical School with two 50 m ropes; parties who stop after pitch 4 rappel from the double bolts on the last Simu-gil pitch.
- **Do not rappel straight down Chouinard A**: the ropes jam in the crack.
- **Look below when you rappel**: parties are often getting ready to start around Oasis, so check that nobody is below before retrieving gear from above.

## How far to trust these notes

- **Dates**: the sources run from 2006 to 2024, mostly the 2010s. Fixed gear, stations and bolts may have changed; Crony pitch 6 used to have pitons and now has a bolt, and two bolts on Villa have been removed.
- **Shared origin**: the pitch-by-pitch text on Girly's page matches the Kim Yong-gi Mountaineering School description reposted on Sanjini's page (checked for the Crony section), and several blogs repost the same compilation, so they are not independent confirmation.
- **Versions**: pitch counts, lengths and grades often have two versions, and the notes list both. When you meet two numbers, go with the topo on the day.
- **Positions**: starts are described as "a few dozen metres left or right" and are estimates, not measurements.
- **Gaps**: no clear start description was found for Haneul-gil, Rescuer or PASI, and each says so above.

This page is no substitute for a good topo. For east-face routes, download (or buy the poster of) the [Mont Blanc Lines East Face topo](https://www.montblanclines.com/products/%ec%9d%b8%ec%88%98%eb%b4%89-insubong-east-face) before you go, then use this page to cross-check each pitch's length and crux; for the four south-face routes there is no matching topo, so look at the front-face and southwest-face concept maps linked from [Girly's page](https://han5256.tistory.com/7667232) (they are images, so their content could not be read here and it is unclear whether they cover these four), and find a partner who knows that side.
