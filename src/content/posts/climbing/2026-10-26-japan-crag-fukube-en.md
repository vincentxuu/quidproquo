> 🌏 [中文版](/posts/climbing/2026-10-26-japan-crag-fukube)

Fukube is more than one famous boulder. Problems are spread along Kataji Valley and the surrounding slopes, from rocks near parking to others requiring a separate approach. This guide helps you select a day’s cluster by level and plan transport and pads without chasing a list across the hillside.

## What kind of crag

**フクベ（Fukube）** is in Kataji, Mino City, Gifu. The [Mino tourism association](https://minokanko.com/seeing/category/nature-scenery/p5987/) describes bouldering around the valley and Fukubegatake; [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E3%83%95%E3%82%AF%E3%83%99/) identifies hard tuff.

[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/127) separates upper-mountain, upper-river, middle, lower and Kaya-no-taki sectors. They remain parts of this destination. Choose a cluster suitable for the team: easier grades do not imply flat landings, so pads, spotting and downclimbing need problem-specific plans.

## When to go

[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E3%83%95%E3%82%AF%E3%83%99/) and [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/127) both list **March–June and September–December**. Spring and autumn are the starting point; shaded forest does not guarantee comfortable midsummer rock or air temperatures.

Local rules note winter closure of the upper toilet. Use facilities before entering and carry a portable toilet. In winter check snow, ice and usable parking; after rain inspect whether pads can sit securely on the landing.

## Getting there

### Public transport

As checked in October 2026, the [official Mino tourism page](https://minokanko.com/seeing/category/nature-scenery/p5987/) describes driving from MinoIC, not a direct crag shuttle. Without a car, arrange a town pickup or shared ride and the return in advance, specifying the Kataji sector. Reaching Mino town is not reaching the boulders.

### Driving

The [Mino tourism authority](https://minokanko.com/seeing/category/nature-scenery/p5987/) directs drivers from **MinoIC** on the Tokai-Hokuriku Expressway toward Kataji. Register at the entrance before continuing to your sector. A summit or hiking-trail destination is not a substitute for a bouldering approach.

### Parking

The [local rules](http://topo.jp/modules/pico/index.php?content_id=1) require access for works vehicles and prohibit blocking entrances from paved roads to forestry roads. Follow the currently designated spaces; an old reference to a broad shoulder does not authorize parking on every clear patch.

## Rules before you go

The [local rules on Topo.jp](http://topo.jp/modules/pico/index.php?content_id=1), checked in October 2026, require a contribution and entry record. They say contributions are held at Mino’s Shimomaki office and allocated by the local property-district council. [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E3%83%95%E3%82%AF%E3%83%99/) also reproduces Fukube Climbing Club rules.

Camping spaces may be unavailable during community events. Follow that day’s notices rather than moving barriers or occupying work areas. Remove rubbish and chalk marks, and manage toilet waste rather than leaving maintenance to the next group.

For a serious injury requiring medical help, call **119**. The [FDMA guide](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf) asks callers to give the location and landmarks. Save the local crag name, target sector and road access offline; ask your insurer whether the climbing style, search and rescue, treatment and repatriation are covered.

## Routes by level

YDS and V-grade conversions follow the [Mountain Project international grade comparison chart](https://www.mountainproject.com/international-climbing-grades) and are approximate; "approx." marks grades the sources give without a/b/c (such as 4 or 5+). The original grade is the one that counts.

The full graded [ROCKTOPO table](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/127/routes) contributes 431 entries, supplemented by the linked public topo/MP rows. Original YDS or Japanese grades are retained; unlisted lengths/pitches remain unspecified. Obtain the current topo for descent and protection. Names shared by different sectors/grades remain separate. A listed route is not a current-access recommendation.

Japanese grades define the boulder groups; verified thetopo Font grades appear separately where available. Rope routes use their corresponding YDS bands within each group, with their own table.
