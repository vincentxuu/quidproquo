A visit to Hiwasa requires choosing both a climbing sector and a workable local approach. This guide helps you assess the style, season and access checks needed for a first visit.

## What kind of crag

The sandstone sea cliffs at Asebinohana include the steep Dome and easier lines at Hidamari. Salt, waves and a complex approach matter. Reaching Hiwasa Station does not mean arriving at a flat walk-in climbing area.

## When to go

[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E6%97%A5%E5%92%8C%E4%BD%90/) lists Mid-September to early May. This is established crag information, not a day-specific forecast or current access permission. Check recent rain, coastal wind and waves where relevant, and current local notices.

## Getting there

### Public transport

Arrange onward transport and the climbing entrance after reaching Hiwasa by rail. No current station-to-crag bus was verified. For a first visit, go with someone familiar with the approach.

### Driving

Identify Asebinohana and the intended sector rather than navigating to the Ohamabeach sightseeing entrance. Reassess the approach and retreat when coastal wind or swell rises.

### Parking

Older climbing descriptions use a port-side approach, but no current port-parking authorization was obtained. Confirm locally and keep fishing operations clear.

## Rules before you go

CLIMBING-net records voluntary avoidance from after Golden Week through July for bird breeding. This is secondary information; confirm current dates and scope locally. Minami also publishes night restrictions for turtle protection at Ohamabeach. Those beach rules do not establish an Asebinohana-wide climbing ban, and each conservation boundary still matters.

For a medical emergency, call **119** and give your position and landmarks, following [FDMA guidance](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf). Save the Japanese cliff name and an offline map before leaving. Ask your insurer specifically about lead climbing, mountain rescue, treatment and repatriation.
