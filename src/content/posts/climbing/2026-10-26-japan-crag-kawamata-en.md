Kawamata is a limestone sport crag near Naguri in Hanno, with many5.10–5.12 routes and specific current collapse/tree hazards to avoid. This guide helps you decide whether it suits your team, when to visit and what to confirm about transport and the approach.

## What kind of crag

The rock is **limestone** ([crag description](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E6%B2%B3%E5%8F%88/)); the readable [ROCKTOPO inventory](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/94/routes) has 111 graded entries. Its scope differs from a complete guidebook.

Kawamata is a limestone sport crag near Naguri in Hanno, with many5.10–5.12 routes and specific current collapse/tree hazards to avoid.

[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E6%B2%B3%E5%8F%88/) and [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/94) have different inventory scopes. A database count does not establish that every route is currently accessible. Sectors remain within this destination; choose the day’s objective and descent before planning equipment.

## When to go

[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/94) lists March–June and September–December. Limestone friction and shaded seepage vary with humidity. Easier routes can be polished or steep at the start; do not assume a reliable wet-weather alternative.

## Getting there

### Public transport

From Seibu Hanno Station check [Kokusai Kogyo Bus](https://5931bus.com/) services toward Naguri/Nago to Kawamata-Naguriko-Iriguchi. Plan the return and allow time for the uphill approach, preparation and descent.

### Driving

Drive toward Shimo-Naguri in Hanno and navigate to the community-agreed paid riverside parking, not a forestry-road shoulder near the cliff. Check access and collapse notices after rain.

### Parking

[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/94) records the local agreement to use paid riverside parking. Follow current parking-manager instructions rather than older sweet-shop, shoulder or free-parking reports.

## Rules before you go

[JFA’s October 2025 notice](https://freeclimb.jp/access/10282/) says **Hebi-iwa collapsed: stay away**. Its [January 2026 updated notice](https://freeclimb.jp/access/10328/) still warns of a **fallen tree above Miyazaki Midori at Komori-iwa, especially threatening belayers**. Neither is a recommended current objective here: check removal/lifting notices and otherwise avoid the affected ground.

[JFA’s history](https://freeclimb.jp/activities/deal-with/issue-list/) records negotiated reopening in 2010; noise, fires, parking and accidents could again jeopardize access. Respect residents and keep access for emergency services.

For a serious injury requiring medical help, call **119**. The [FDMA guide](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf) asks callers to give the location and landmarks. Save the local crag name, target sector and road access offline; ask your insurer whether the climbing style, search and rescue, treatment and repatriation are covered.
