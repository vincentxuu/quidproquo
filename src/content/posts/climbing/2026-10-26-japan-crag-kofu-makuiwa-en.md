Kofu Makuiwa is a woodland tuff sport crag with a broad grade range, where forestry-road conditions often matter more than distance. This guide helps you decide whether it suits your team, when to visit and what to confirm about transport and the approach.

## What kind of crag

The rock is **tuff** ([crag description](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E7%94%B2%E5%BA%9C%E5%B9%95%E5%B2%A9/)); the readable [ROCKTOPO inventory](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/6/routes) has 221 graded entries. Its scope differs from a complete guidebook.

A database count does not establish that every route is currently accessible. Sectors remain within this destination; choose the day’s objective and descent before planning equipment.

## When to go

[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/6) lists April–June and October–November; the publisher notes midsummer heat. Woodland shade does not guarantee dry rock. After rain check the cliff, approach and forestry road; in winter start with road restrictions.

## Getting there

### Public transport

The verified approach uses forestry roads; no official direct cliff shuttle was found. Arrange a rental or pickup from Kofu and specify Kofu Makuiwa and the usable road, distinct from Yugawara Makuiwa.

### Driving

[Yamanashi’s Komorigawa notice](https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/rindoujyouhou/kisei.php?id=16) closes km 6–8 for an unsafe ageing bridge, with no reopening date, and explicitly directs climbing access via **Kashiyama or Kannon-toge/Ohnoyama roads**. Check the [forestry-road index](https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/rindoujyouhou/list.php) that day and respect barriers.

### Parking

Use designated climbing parking and leave access for forestry and emergency vehicles. A navigation shortcut is not parking authorization; check the alternative road before departure.

## Rules before you go

As checked in October 2026, the prefectural notice closes **a section of Komorigawa Road**, not Kofu Makuiwa as a whole. Alternative roads have their own weather/work restrictions and need separate checks.

Hojo-no-Mori, Forest Walkway, Maerchenland and Sansho Kingdom are sectors of this destination. Respect forestry operations and established approaches; database bolt or length fields do not replace inspection.

For a serious injury requiring medical help, call **119**. The [FDMA guide](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf) asks callers to give the location and landmarks. Save the local crag name, target sector and road access offline; ask your insurer whether the climbing style, search and rescue, treatment and repatriation are covered.

## Where to look things up

- [CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E7%94%B2%E5%BA%9C%E5%B9%95%E5%B2%A9/): crag background, seasons and guidebook entry point.

- [Yamanashi road index](https://www.pref.yamanashi.jp/rindoujyouhou/list.php): Official forestry-road restriction index.
