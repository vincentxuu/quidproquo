Japan’s granite crack destinations extend beyond Ogawayama and Mizugaki. At Mikuradake, cracks, friction slabs and multipitch routes share a mountain; choosing the wall matters as much as choosing the grade.

This guide helps you judge the fit for your team, plan a spring or autumn visit, and understand the last approach and park procedures after reaching Kuritani.

## What kind of crag

**三倉岳（Mikuradake）** stands in Kuritani, Otake, Hiroshima. The [city’s official poster](https://www.city.otake.hiroshima.jp/material/files/group/4/mikura.pdf) identifies granite cliffs and climbing. Asahidake, Nakadake and Yuhidake are the three recognizable summits; climbing sectors still require a topo.

[ROCKTOPO’s list](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/155/routes) contains about 200 entries across face, crack and multipitch climbing. Hip Crack and Suisho Crack at Gensuke-kuzure and Heitai Crack near Aojiro Hang are separate objectives. **Choose one wall for a first visit and allow time to identify the line and descent.** Assess protection skills together with the route.

## When to go

Start with spring or autumn: [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/155) marks March–May and September–November. Sun, shade and exposure vary by wall, so select the face for the actual weather as well as the month.

Long belays and waits justify warm layers, and wet rock needs time to dry. The [park manager](https://mikura.org/trekking/) notes chained sections on the hiking routes; a sightseeing walking estimate is not an equipment-laden climbing approach.

## Getting there

### Public transport

Checked in October 2026, the [city](https://www.city.otake.hiroshima.jp/soshiki/somu/sangyoshinko/gyomu/kanko/mikuradake000/1456031936627.html) describes a bus from JR Kuba Station to **栗谷支所前（Kuritani Shisho-mae）**. The [official Otake–Kuritani line page](https://www.city.otake.hiroshima.jp/soshiki/shiminseikatsu/shimin/gyomu/1/1/7358.html) identifies the city service, operated by Otake Kotsu, and links its timetable.

That reaches the settlement, not each cliff. Settle the connection to the rest house and the return before departure. A car or arranged pickup is easier to fit around a full multipitch day.

### Driving

Follow the [city directions](https://www.city.otake.hiroshima.jp/soshiki/somu/sangyoshinko/gyomu/kanko/mikuradake000/1456031936627.html) from Otake IC on the Sanyo Expressway to **三倉岳休憩所（Mikuradake rest house）**. Select the A/B trail and cliff approach from the topo once at the park entrance.

### Parking

The [park manager](https://mikura.org/trekking/) allows hikers to park in the square in front of the rest house. Use designated space and keep roads and shared visitor paths clear.

## Rules before you go

**Submit the entry form first.** Checked in October 2026, the [manager](https://mikura.org/trekking/) requires it at the rest house; use the provided post box when the building is closed. Identify the intended cliff, team and expected return, and read current notices.

The [city](https://www.city.otake.hiroshima.jp/soshiki/somu/sangyoshinko/gyomu/kanko/mikuradake000/1456031936627.html) prohibits ground fires in the campsite. Line identification, protection and descent remain the team’s responsibility; open hiking facilities do not certify climbing hardware. Pack out waste and leave rock and vegetation intact.

For a medical emergency, call **119** and give your position and landmarks, following [FDMA guidance](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf). Save the Japanese cliff name and an offline map before leaving. Ask your insurer specifically about trad and multipitch climbing, mountain rescue, treatment and repatriation.

- **Guidebooks:** Grades and route counts here come mainly from online databases; the guidebook is the authority.
