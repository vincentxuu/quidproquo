Mizunami Byobuiwa combines granite cracks and friction faces, for climbers practising jams, wide cracks and precise footwork. This guide helps you decide whether it suits your team, when to visit and what to confirm about transport and the approach.

## What kind of crag

The rock is **granite** ([crag description](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E7%91%9E%E6%B5%AA%E3%83%BB%E5%B1%8F%E9%A2%A8%E5%B2%A9/)); the readable [ROCKTOPO inventory](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/129/routes) has 77 graded entries. Its scope differs from a complete guidebook.

## When to go

[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/129) lists October–April; the publisher’s directory favours autumn through spring. Sunny faces and shaded cracks can feel very different in winter: account for frost and wind. Summer is a poor starting point for a friction-focused trip.

## Getting there

### Public transport

Take the JR Chuo Line to Mizunami and arrange a rental or pickup, including the return. Check [JR Central](https://railway.jr-central.co.jp/station-guide/tokai/mizunami/); a municipal bus network is not a crag shuttle.

### Driving

Approach the Byobuyama area from Mizunami and use the climbing entrance in the current topo rather than a similarly named attraction. Drive slowly on local roads, reduce dust and yield to residents and work vehicles.

### Parking

The local notice prohibits using or crossing community-hall parking/property. Share cars and use only currently permitted spaces; change plans if full.

## Rules before you go

The [2017 local notice](https://www.climbing-net.com/news/mizunami_171112/), reproduced by the publisher, identifies private land and prohibits tree cutting, collecting, fires, sleeping and improper parking. Check current JFA and on-site notices before departure.

Past one-day road-maintenance closures do not establish this year’s calendar. Trad routes require the ability to place and assess protection; gym face grades alone are not a selection method.

For a serious injury requiring medical help, call **119**. The [FDMA guide](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf) asks callers to give the location and landmarks. Save the local crag name, target sector and road access offline; ask your insurer whether the climbing style, search and rescue, treatment and repatriation are covered.
