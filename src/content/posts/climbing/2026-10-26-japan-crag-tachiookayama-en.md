Tachiokayama combines powerful sport climbing at Koyama Rock with a multipitch left ridge, requiring different equipment and skills. This guide helps you decide whether it suits your team, when to visit and what to confirm about transport and the approach.

## What kind of crag

The rock is **andesite** ([crag description](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E5%A4%AA%E5%88%80%E5%B2%A1%E5%B1%B1/)); the readable [ROCKTOPO inventory](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/7/routes) has 70 graded entries. Its scope differs from a complete guidebook.

Tachiokayama combines powerful sport climbing at Koyama Rock with a multipitch left ridge, requiring different equipment and skills.

[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E5%A4%AA%E5%88%80%E5%B2%A1%E5%B1%B1/) and [ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/7) have different inventory scopes. A database count does not establish that every route is currently accessible. Sectors remain within this destination; choose the day’s objective and descent before planning equipment.

## When to go

[ROCKTOPO](https://www.rocktopo.net/areas/7) lists April–June and September–November; the publisher notes midsummer heat. Start with spring/autumn, and check ice and approach conditions in winter. An exposed ridge differs from the wooded sport sector.

## Getting there

### Public transport

[Kai municipal buses](https://www.city.kai.yamanashi.jp/page/4542.html), Shikishima North Line toward Shimoashizawa, have limited Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday service in the 2026 timetable. Arrival from Ryuo is late for a full climbing day: prefer a booked pickup or rental and arrange the return.

### Driving

[Kai City](https://www.city.kai.yamanashi.jp/page/3823.html) provides trailhead and parking maps. Navigate to Kamifukuzawa, then follow the topo for Koyama Rock or the left ridge rather than navigating to summit coordinates.

### Parking

[Kai City’s 2026 page](https://www.city.kai.yamanashi.jp/page/3823.html) lists two parking areas; the second can be located at535-1Kamifukuzawa. Roadside and unauthorized private parking are prohibited. The hiking toilet is at the fire/community centre at543Kamifukuzawa.

## Rules before you go

The city’s “no abnormality” statement concerns the hiking trail, not climbing anchors or the left ridge. Prepare protection, route finding and descent independently; a hardest grade of 5.9 does not make the ridge beginner sport.

Plan Koyama Rock’s powerful moves separately from a ridge day, and consider rockfall and belayer positions when sharing the cliff.

For a serious injury requiring medical help, call **119**. The [FDMA guide](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf) asks callers to give the location and landmarks. Save the local crag name, target sector and road access offline; ask your insurer whether the climbing style, search and rescue, treatment and repatriation are covered.
