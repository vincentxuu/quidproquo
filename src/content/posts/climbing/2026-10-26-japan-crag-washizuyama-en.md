A visit to Washizuyama requires choosing both a climbing sector and a workable local approach. This guide helps you assess the style, season and access checks needed for a first visit.

## What kind of crag

This tuff destination near Numazu includes the main wall, Katchin Rock, Sankyu Rock and Amphitheatre. Their safety cannot be inferred as one unit. Historic route reputation does not override current rockfall warnings. Tables are an inventory, and a restricted line’s appearance is not a recommendation to climb.

## When to go

[CLIMBING-net](https://www.climbing-net.com/iwaba_detail/%E9%B7%B2%E9%A0%AD%E5%B1%B1/) lists September–June. This is established crag information, not a day-specific forecast or current access permission. Check recent rain, coastal wind and waves where relevant, and current local notices.

## Getting there

### Public transport

Older CLIMBING-net information uses a bus from Numazu toward Shimokokaidomae. A current stop-specific timetable was not verified; check Izuhakone Bus for the date and confirm the trail entrance separately.

### Driving

Identify public-road access and legitimate parking around Shige, Numazu, then locate the trail on foot. Coastal landmarks in crag databases do not establish vehicle access or parking permission.

### Parking

No current dedicated climbing-parking notice was obtained. Keep trailheads, shoulders and residents’ entrances clear; confirm a usable location and path locally.

## Rules before you go

The [JFA’s 2025 warning](https://freeclimb.jp/access/10123/) reports roughly 30 kg of rock falling near Dome Crack on January 4, with discontinuities above. It lists ギブマインドトウ and ドームクラック as not climbable, and warns people attempting Cosmic Dancer or Jet, or passing toward Jagaimo Rock and Amphitheatre, to watch above. The notice is not a mountain-wide closure, but restricted lines cannot be treated as ordinary options without a verified clearance.

For a medical emergency, call **119** and give your position and landmarks, following [FDMA guidance](https://www.fdma.go.jp/publication/portal/items/portal001_pamphiet_english.pdf). Save the Japanese cliff name and an offline map before leaving. Ask your insurer specifically about lead climbing, mountain rescue, treatment and repatriation.
