## What This Company Does

Conveo builds "AI consumer research infrastructure" — replacing human researchers with an AI interviewer that holds in-depth video conversations with consumers, compressing what used to take months of qualitative research into days.

Its core product is an AI research interviewer that asks follow-up questions like a human researcher would, rather than serving up a static survey — running multi-turn video conversations, including in-home studies that observe how people actually use products, and probing consumer needs, behavior, pricing sensitivity, and brand perception. Its newest product, StoryLines, upgrades Conveo from a one-off research tool into infrastructure: it keeps research running continuously on a brand's strategic topics, feeding a searchable data repository accessed through dashboards, AI agents, and integrations with existing business software — turning consumer insight into a queryable data pipeline instead of a one-time report.

Conveo now serves more than 400 enterprise customers, including over 50 Fortune 500 brands. This $50M round ranks in the 99th percentile of Series A deals in its industry and country, across a sample of 877 all-time rounds — one of the largest early-stage rounds yet in the consumer-research-infrastructure niche.

## What This Funding Signals

### Implications for the Agent Ecosystem

Conveo is another data point for a pattern where AI agents replace an entire service function within an industry — not wrapped as a chatbot sold to consumers, but deployed as the actual executor of an internal enterprise process, talking directly to real people to gather first-party data. This round funds US expansion and pushes Conveo further from "research tool" toward "consumer-intelligence infrastructure enterprises run continuously" — meaning the next step is shifting data generation from one-off projects to a persistent data stream.

### What Investors Are Betting On

Balderton Capital led this round betting on a global market research industry that ESOMAR estimates at $153B, long dominated by legacy players like Nielsen and Kantar running one-off studies with human interviewers. Conveo's thesis is to push marginal cost toward zero with AI, converting a "commission a study" business model into a "subscribe to infrastructure" model — the same bet backing Simile, whose valuation jumped to $2B five months after its own Series A. Large checks flowing into AI research startups in the same window suggest investors believe this shift is happening fast.

### Numbers Worth Watching

- The $50M round lands in the 99th percentile of Series A deals in its category (across a sample of 877 historical rounds in the same industry and country) — investors are willing to pay a premium for the consumer-research niche this early.
- Only $55.8M raised total, yet serving 400+ enterprises including 50+ Fortune 500 brands — a customer-acquisition efficiency well above typical enterprise software startups at this stage.
- In the same window, Simile's valuation rose 10x to $2B in five months and GetWhy added $8.4M in debt financing — a cluster of deals in AI research suggests a capital consensus forming around the category, not an isolated event.

## Today's Takeaway

Market research looks like an industry far removed from the "agent ecosystem," but Conveo's approach illustrates a more general pattern: wherever an industry's core work is structured conversation with humans to extract information, it's a candidate for AI agents to replace the entire service function outright — not just accelerate one step of it.
