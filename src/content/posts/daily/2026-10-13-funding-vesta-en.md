## What the Company Does

Vesta automates mortgage origination. It rebuilt the loan origination system (LOS) from the ground up as AI-native, so human staff and AI agents work the same loan file inside the same platform — instead of bolting automation onto a legacy system.

Lenders configure which tasks they want Vesta's agents to handle: document review, data validation, cross-checks, and even underwriting decisions. Every agent decision is logged for audit, and anything that needs human judgment gets escalated automatically. The company says roughly 40% of tasks completed through Vesta are now handled by AI agents, a share that keeps rising.

Lenders on Vesta's platform, or migrating to it, originate more than $100 billion in mortgages annually. Customers include PennyMac, New American Funding, and NBKC Bank. PennyMac went a step further in September 2025, buying a minority equity stake in Vesta alongside becoming a customer.

## What This Round Signals

### What It Means for the Agent Ecosystem

Major lenders taking equity in their own LOS vendor is a clear sign that agents have moved from peripheral tooling into core production systems. Vesta's pitch — people and agents working the same file inside the same system — means the next thing the mortgage industry needs to solve isn't whether to use agents, but how to log, audit, and assign accountability for their decisions.

### What Investors Are Betting On

Conversion Capital has doubled down from the seed round all the way to this Series B, showing insiders' conviction hasn't faded over time. More telling is who else is in the round: beyond Citi Ventures, customers like PennyMac and New American Funding became shareholders directly. That "customer as investor" pattern is stronger proof the product is truly embedded in production than any purely financial backer's check.

### Numbers Worth Watching

- The company claims 12x year-over-year revenue growth, yet still holds under 5% share of the overall mortgage market
- About 40% of tasks on the platform are already handled by AI agents, and rising
- Only $85M raised to date is supporting roughly $100 billion in annualized loan volume — unusually capital-efficient for an AI infrastructure company at this stage

## Today's Takeaway

I assumed a conservative industry like mortgage lending would keep AI agents at "assisting humans" for a long time. Vesta's case shows that once lenders start taking equity in their vendor, it's no longer just a trial — it's a long-term bet that agents are core infrastructure, which says more about trust than any revenue number could.
