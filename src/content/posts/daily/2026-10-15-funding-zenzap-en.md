## What the Company Does

Zenzap is an enterprise team chat platform where employees and AI agents assign tasks, share documents and track progress in the same conversation.

What it wants to replace is the habit of running work through WhatsApp groups: consumer messaging leaves companies with no control over how employees use it, and business information ends up scattered across personal accounts. Zenzap built its own security infrastructure, mobile interface and chat experience, connects to thousands of enterprise apps through connectors, and ships prebuilt agents and automations. Employees can direct agents by text or voice, and the company says an agent takes under 90 seconds to set up. It claims SOC 2, HIPAA, ISO 27001 and GDPR compliance, with security leads centrally controlling which tools and data employees and agents can reach.

The company says it serves thousands of organizations, and customers have built more than 40,000 automated workflows — routing requests to the right person, chasing missing invoices, updating CRM records, and confirming medication procedures are complete before shift changes in care facilities. CEO Guy Weiss is blunt about the target market: non-tech organizations Slack never reached, such as healthcare and real estate.

## What This Round Signals

### What it means for the agent ecosystem

The first wave of AI tools was mostly one person talking to one chatbot. Zenzap is betting on the team level: agents share context with a group and pick up tasks in conversations people already have. For frontline work in warehouses, logistics and clinics, the entry point is a chat on a phone, not desktop SaaS, and whoever owns that entry point has a much easier path to distributing agents.

### What the investors are betting on

Bessemer and Lightspeed co-led, betting on the gap where work communication meets agents in non-tech industries. These organizations never adopted Slack or Teams and lack the engineering capacity to integrate agents themselves, so a ready-made chat interface with connectors lowers the bar. Note that the round closed in August 2025 and was disclosed now alongside the stealth exit.

### Numbers to watch

- 40,000+ customer-built automated workflows
- A 45-person team serving "thousands of organizations"; customer count and revenue undisclosed
- $11M seed → $33M Series A, 3x the seed

## Watchlist Status

Zenzap is not yet on the watchlist. Suggest adding to section B3 (agent platforms): an agent platform shaped as team chat, aimed at frontline work in non-tech industries, on a different path from the agents built into Slack and Teams.

## Today's Takeaway

I assumed enterprise agents would enter through existing platforms like Slack and Teams. Zenzap's positioning is a reminder that many industries never adopted those tools; their "existing workflow" is a WhatsApp group, and agents have to replace that group to get in.
