## What the Company Does

firmly builds no-code infrastructure that lets merchants plug into AI shopping agents without writing a line of code. It started in 2019 turning ad impressions into shoppable checkout surfaces, then repositioned the same zero-integration technology for the agentic-commerce era as AI agents became a new storefront.

Its core product, firmly Connect, is a single no-code integration: once a merchant connects, they show up across AI agents, marketplaces, social networks, and shoppable ads simultaneously, while keeping their own customer relationships, transaction data, and merchant-of-record status — unlike rivals that require merchants to integrate into their system instead. firmly says its existing network already lets AI assistants, publishers, and social platforms reach more than 11 million merchants representing over $900B in GMV.

Alongside the funding, firmly launched Ask Agent, a conversational shopping widget that can be added to a merchant site with a single line of JavaScript. Shoppers describe what they want in natural language, and the system pulls real-time catalog, pricing, and inventory data to recommend products, with in-chat checkout and support built in. firmly says early deployments show conversion-rate gains of up to 18% and average-order-value increases of up to 23% — self-reported figures, not independently audited.

## What This Round Signals

### For the Agent Ecosystem

firmly's announcement cites Adobe's prediction that AI-driven traffic to US retail sites will grow 130% year over year this holiday season. But capturing that traffic means merchants have to wire up payments, inventory, and checkout across a fast-multiplying, incompatible set of agent protocols. firmly is positioning itself as the shared layer in that chain — bridging browser-automation agents (the announcement names Muse and Instinct) and deterministic protocols like UCP with a single integration point.

### What Investors Are Betting On

A quant trading firm leading an early-stage commerce-infrastructure round is unusual. What stands out more is the participant list: J.P. Morgan Payments, Mastercard, and Samsung NEXT are incumbent payments and hardware players. Their logic looks closer to infrastructure positioning than pure financial return — if agentic commerce becomes a real retail channel, they want a seat at the protocol and payment layer now, rather than getting routed around by a new checkout standard later.

### Numbers Worth Watching

- firmly has raised only about $20M total since 2019, across seven years, before pulling in this $15M round — a relatively capital-efficient path, not a typical burn-to-scale playbook.
- The claimed reach of 11M+ merchants and $900B GMV, if accurate, is a legacy asset carried over from its original shoppable-ads business rather than a network built from scratch — which helps explain why strategic investors like Mastercard and JPM came in.
- The conversion-rate and AOV improvements for Ask Agent come only from "early deployments" and are self-reported, with no independent verification yet — worth flagging as claimed figures, not confirmed results.

## Watchlist Status

firmly is not currently on the watchlist. Recommend adding it to section D8 (Retail / E-commerce), tracking its no-code agentic-commerce integration layer — firmly Connect plus Ask Agent — and this $15M Series A led by Squarepoint Capital.

## Today's Takeaway

This round shows the bottleneck in agentic commerce isn't whether AI can shop — it's merchant-side integration fatigue. Every new AI shopping entry point means another engineering integration for merchants. firmly is positioning itself as that shared connective layer, one integration that works everywhere — nearly the same playbook mobile-payment aggregators ran a decade ago, just with protocol-fragmented AI agents as the new frontier instead of card networks.
