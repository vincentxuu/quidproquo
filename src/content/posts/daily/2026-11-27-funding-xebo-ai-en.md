## What the company does

XEBO.ai builds experience management software — it turns customer and employee feedback into decisions enterprises can act on, spanning customer experience (CX), employee experience (EX), and market research.

The core product bundles surveys, social listening, and customer journey mapping. This round is earmarked specifically to add agentic AI to the existing platform, alongside expanding voice and digital experience analytics — in other words, moving from "collecting and displaying feedback" toward "letting AI decide what to do with it." The company has a technology integration with Microsoft that it uses as one channel for reaching enterprise customers.

Originally founded in 2017 as Survey2Connect, the company rebranded to XEBO.ai in late 2023. It now serves more than 350 enterprise customers across 10+ industries — including banking, aviation, telecom, insurance, retail, public sector, and healthcare — with clients spanning the Middle East, Asia-Pacific, and North America, and has been recognized by Gartner, IDC, and Forrester.

## What this round signals

### What it means for the agent ecosystem

This is the first institutional capital XEBO.ai has ever raised — nine years of running on its own revenue and founder capital before reaching a Series A, which is unusual for a company in the relatively mature experience-management category. The capital is earmarked in three buckets: roughly 30-40% into product (voice analytics, digital experience analytics, agentic AI), about 30% into consolidating existing markets across the Middle East, Africa, Asia-Pacific, and India, and the remainder into entering North America. That split suggests the company is betting that deepening its AI capabilities first, then using sovereign-fund backing to go global, beats chasing scale for its own sake.

### What the investor is betting on

The lead, Emirates Growth Fund, is a flagship growth-equity fund the UAE government launched in 2025 under Dr. Sultan Al Jaber, backed by Emirates Development Bank — the UAE's only sovereign fund dedicated exclusively to growth-stage companies. EGF Investment Director Anshul Garg put it plainly: XEBO.ai "has done what many technology businesses aspire to do: build an innovative product that addresses a real market need, win the trust of demanding enterprise customers, and demonstrate its relevance alongside established global players." This isn't just equity — EGF is also committing governance support, senior-hiring help, and access to its government and private-sector network. In effect, it's packaging a homegrown AI company with a proven track record as a tech flagbearer for the UAE's push abroad.

### Numbers worth watching

- Nine years without institutional funding, yet already at 350+ enterprise customers, 100+ employees, and offices in four countries — this is not the typical "raise first, find product-market fit later" AI startup.
- $10.5M is a small Series A by 2026 standards (typical AI-agent Series A rounds this year clustered in the $20–35M range), but given the company has been self-sustaining for years, the logic here is "accelerate with outside capital" rather than "survive on outside capital."
- EGF is the UAE's only sovereign fund focused exclusively on growth-stage companies, and this is one of its few public Advanced Technology investments — worth watching whether Gulf sovereign capital repeats this playbook with other homegrown AI SaaS companies going global.

## Today's takeaway

Most CX-agent startups are born "AI-native" and raise on an agent narrative from day one. XEBO.ai did it backwards: nine years building a traditional experience-management business to 350+ enterprise customers, and only now treating agentic AI as the next feature layer. The Gulf sovereign capital here isn't backing the company with the best AI pitch — it's backing the one with real revenue and an existing customer base that's just now adding AI on top. That might be a steadier way to go global than AI-native from the start.
