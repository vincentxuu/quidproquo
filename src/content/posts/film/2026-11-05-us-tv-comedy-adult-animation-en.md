This post covers American sitcoms, comedy-dramas and adult animation: a mad scientist across infinite universes, a paper company's office, a Hollywood studio, an English football club and vampires on Staten Island. All 22 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan.

Content is much lighter than in the last three posts: only Rick and Morty counts as heavy. Watch for swearing, though: 10 of the 22 rate severe for it, including the gentle-looking Ted Lasso. Comedies also tend to have their weakest season first; The Office, The Big Bang Theory and BoJack Horseman all get better.

## The watchlist

Entries follow the same format as the [fantasy and sci-fi post](/posts/film/2026-11-05-us-tv-fantasy-scifi-superhero-en): recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: [Metacritic](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14478499933079-How-do-you-compute-METASCORES) is a weighted average of US critics (0–100: how good); [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/about) (RT) is the share of positive critics and viewers (whether you'll regret it); IMDb is worldwide viewers rating the whole show; Douban is Chinese-speaking viewers, shown only where readable. Metacritic, RT and Douban are for season 1.
- **Content**: converted from the [IMDb Parents Guide](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2861424/parentalguide/): ○○○ none, ●○○ mild, ●●○ moderate, ●●● severe.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" means only JustWatch Taiwan, older official evidence or a distributor-supplied article was found; if the link says the show is unavailable where you are, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Adult animation

Cartoon looks, grown-up content: infinite universes, depression, a pharma conspiracy. The only heavy show in this post is here.

#### Rick and Morty

Adult Swim's adult sci-fi cartoon: a cynical mad scientist drags his timid, good-hearted grandson between family life and adventures across infinite universes ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rick_and_Morty)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy for Outstanding Animated Program, won 2018 and 2020` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #76` `C IMDb 9.0` `E Guinness World Records 2026: most in-demand sitcom` `F PTT drama board GOAT thread and season threads`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 85 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/rick_and_morty/s01) 97% critics, 97% audience | IMDb 9.0
- **Reviews**: After two episodes, [Matt Zoller Seitz at Vulture](https://www.vulture.com/2013/12/tv-review-dan-harmons-rick-and-morty.html) said it could fill the hole Futurama left, and called it "one of the more accurate portrayals of how charismatic alcoholics bamboozle meek family members". [香功堂主 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/5efab2ddfd89780001205ba1) (in Chinese) reads the space adventures as wrapping paper around a family story.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2861424/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●○ | scares ●●○ | profanity ●●● | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 9 seasons and 91 episodes so far, renewed through season 12. Rick and Morty have new voice actors from season 7.
- **Watch first**: Nothing. Most episodes stand alone, but later seasons carry ongoing threads, so start at season 1.
- **Where to watch**: [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/ab553cdc-e15d-4597-b65f-bec9201fd2dd) (subscription)

#### BoJack Horseman

In a Hollywood shared by humans and talking animals, a washed-up 90s sitcom star who is a horse tries a comeback with a ghostwritten memoir while his mental state slides ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/BoJack_Horseman)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Animated Program nominations 2019, 2020` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #11` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #44` `C IMDb 8.8` `F PTT drama board GOAT thread (adult animation)`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 59 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/bojack_horseman/s01) 71% critics, 90% audience | IMDb 8.8
- **Reviews**: Reviewing season 1, [Willa Paskin at Slate](https://slate.com/culture/2014/08/bojack-horseman-on-netflix-will-arnett-comedy-cartoon-is-bleak-and-brilliant.html) found it "a little more clever than it is uproariously funny". Reviewing the finale, [傅紀鋼 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/131650) (in Chinese) reads every supporting character as a mirror of BoJack. Critics gave season 1 71% and the final season 96%: it gets better as it goes.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3398228/parentalguide/)): violence ●○○ | sex ●●○ | scares ●●● | profanity ●●○ | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 6 seasons, 76 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing. The high "frightening" rating is about depression, addiction and self-destruction, not horror; skip the binge on a low day.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/70300800) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### The Simpsons

Matt Groening's Fox animated sitcom, satirising American life through a family of five in the fictional town of Springfield ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Simpsons)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Animated Program wins 2019 and 2023, nominated in every other year 2016–2026` `C IMDb 8.6` `E Parrot Analytics 2025 Global Demand Awards comedy finalist` `F PTT drama board GOAT thread (adult animation)`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 80 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_simpsons/s01) 100% critics, 88% audience | IMDb 8.6 | Douban 9.1
- **Reviews**: In 1990, [Howard Rosenberg at the Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/archives/la-xpm-1990-01-12-ca-68-story.html) called it the season's best and smartest new show: "Suburbia has rarely been stranger or sweeter." [林齊晧 at 轉角國際](https://global.udn.com/global_vision/story/8664/1487168) (in Chinese) starts from why everyone is yellow and argues the show satirises the American dream while recording decades of pop culture.
- **Content** ([TV-PG](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0096697/parentalguide/)): violence ●●○ | sex ●●○ | scares ●○○ | profanity ●○○ | drugs ●●○
- **Length**: 38 seasons and 814 episodes so far, renewed through season 40.
- **Watch first**: Nothing. Mostly self-contained episodes, so start anywhere; IMDb contributors call it the tamest of the big adult cartoons. The Taiwanese Fox channel once aired a Mandarin dub with local jokes, which is not the Disney+ version.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-cac75c8f-a9e2-4d95-ac73-1cf1cc7b9568) (subscription)

#### Arcane

Set in the world of Riot Games' League of Legends, sisters Vi and Powder/Jinx are caught in the conflict between the undercity of Zaun and the city of Piltover above, and end up on opposite sides ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arcane_(TV_series))).

- **Reviews**: In a newsletter, [Jordan Calhoun at The Atlantic](https://www.theatlantic.com/newsletters/archive/2021/12/perfection-exists-and-its-name-is-arcane/676475/) admits "adult animation" plus "video-game adaptation" puts people off, then writes: "Arcane is—and I do not say this lightly—perfect." [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/204969) (in Chinese) says nine episodes give every character full motives, serving players and newcomers alike. Metacritic has too few season 1 reviews for a score, so the whole-series score is shown.
- **Content** ([TV-14](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt11126994/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●○○ | scares ●●○ | profanity ●○○ | drugs ●○○
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 18 episodes, finished. Season 2's audience score fell to 81%, with mixed views on its pacing.
- **Watch first**: No League of Legends needed. It is a co-production made by France's Fortiche for US-based Riot Games; it is filed under US shows because Riot led it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81435684) (subscription)

### Workplace, industry and cringe comedy

Offices, studios, stand-up and hip-hop. The laughs come from awkwardness and offence; the swearing runs high.

## Limits of this list

- **Season 1 scores don't describe long-running shows.** The Simpsons, The Office and The Big Bang Theory ran ten seasons or more; Metacritic and RT here cover season 1, while IMDb rates the whole run.
- **Many reviews are based on the pilot or first few episodes**, including The Office, The Big Bang Theory and Curb Your Enthusiasm.
- **Chinese-language reception only appears where it could be read.** Douban blocks automated reading, so only five shows with readable cached pages or review text show a Douban score; no full Taiwanese review could be found for Common Side Effects, Hacks, Curb Your Enthusiasm, The Office, The Chair Company, Atlanta, Only Murders in the Building or What We Do in the Shadows.
- **Arcane is a US–French co-production**, filed here because US-based Riot Games produced it.
- **Content levels come from IMDb user votes, not official ratings.** Common Side Effects and The Chair Company have very few votes.
- **Streaming licences change.** Cobra Kai is licensed from Sony, so it is more likely to leave Netflix than a Netflix original. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-05.
