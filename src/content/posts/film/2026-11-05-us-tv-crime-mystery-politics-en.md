This post covers American crime, mystery and political drama: a chemistry teacher turned drug lord, Baltimore's drug war, a Louisiana cold case, White House scheming and the CIA's war on terror. All 21 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan.

Every show here is TV-MA in the US. Compared with the [fantasy and sci-fi post](/posts/film/2026-11-05-us-tv-fantasy-scifi-superhero-en), there is a little less gore and more swearing and drugs. The heavy shows are all long; for something shorter, pick a limited series or a single anthology season.

## The watchlist

Entries follow the same format as the [fantasy and sci-fi post](/posts/film/2026-11-05-us-tv-fantasy-scifi-superhero-en): recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: [Metacritic](https://metacritichelp.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/14478499933079-How-do-you-compute-METASCORES) is a weighted average of US critics (0–100: how good); [Rotten Tomatoes](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/about) (RT) is the share of positive critics and viewers (whether you'll regret it); IMDb is worldwide viewers; Douban is Chinese-speaking viewers, shown only where readable. All for season 1.
- **Content**: converted from the [IMDb Parents Guide](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0903747/parentalguide/): ○○○ none, ●○○ mild, ●●○ moderate, ●●● severe.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" means only JustWatch Taiwan or older official evidence was found; if the link says the show is unavailable where you are, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Drugs and crime empires

Ordinary people turning criminal, or sinking with criminals. The heaviest group here.

#### Breaking Bad

A New Mexico chemistry teacher diagnosed with terminal lung cancer teams up with a former student to cook meth and leave money for his family ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Breaking_Bad)).

- **Recommended by**: `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #3` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #1` `C IMDb 9.5 (#1 on its Top 250 TV)` `F PTT drama board picks` `G GQ Taiwan list`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 73 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/breaking_bad/s01) 86% critics, 95% audience | IMDb 9.5 | Douban 9.2
- **Reviews**: Reviewing season 1, [Troy Patterson at Slate](https://slate.com/culture/2008/01/breaking-bad-reviewed.html) was guarded: "Its achievement rarely matches its ambitions, but the effect is still pretty dope." A column by [守夜人電影報 on The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/40311) (in Chinese) admits many find season 2 slow but says the first two seasons build a desperate, sympathetic middle-aged man. Its season 1 scores are unremarkable here; it got better every season.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0903747/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●○ | scares ●●● | profanity ●●○ | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 5 seasons, 62 episodes, finished. Season 1 has just 7.
- **Watch first**: Nothing. The prequel Better Call Saul and the film El Camino can come later.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/70143836) (subscription)

#### Better Call Saul

A Breaking Bad prequel: one-time con man Jimmy McGill tries to go straight as a lawyer and slowly becomes the criminal fixer Saul Goodman ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Better_Call_Saul)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Drama Series nominations ×6 (2016–2023, never won)` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #23` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #27` `C IMDb 9.0` `F PTT drama board picks`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 78 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/better_call_saul/s01) 97% critics, 95% audience | IMDb 9.0
- **Reviews**: After two episodes, [Tim Goodman at The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-reviews/better-call-saul-tv-review-765600) warned it is neither a comedy nor Breaking Bad: it "starts with a slow burn but is full of promise". Reviewing season 5, [重點就在括號裡 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/109771/) (in Chinese) said it is no mere prequel but a show that has truly lived its own life.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt3032476/parentalguide/)): violence ●●○ | sex ●○○ | scares ●●○ | profanity ●●○ | drugs ●●○
- **Length**: 6 seasons, 63 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Breaking Bad first is recommended but not required.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80021955) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### The Wire

David Simon's Baltimore drama sees the drug war from both police and dealers, widening each season to the docks, City Hall, schools and the press ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Wire)).

- **Recommended by**: `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #1` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #2` `C IMDb 9.3` `F PTT drama board picks`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 79 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/the_wire/s01) 86% critics, 96% audience | IMDb 9.3 | Douban 9.4
- **Reviews**: Back in 2002, [Steven Oxman at Variety](https://variety.com/2002/tv/reviews/the-wire-4-1200549294/) saw that "this police show rather boldly seeks drama in dullness", mature and important. It topped BBC's 2021 poll of 206 critics. It really is a slow starter, as RT's consensus admits.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt0306414/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●● | scares ●●● | profanity ●●● | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 5 seasons, 60 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing.
- **Where to watch**: [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/1bc3aff5-0d6a-4c0b-8ed0-5716ca30ab3b) (subscription)

#### Narcos

Shot in Colombia, its first two seasons follow Medellín cartel boss Pablo Escobar's rise and the DEA's hunt for him ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Narcos)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Globe Best Drama Series nomination 2016` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #84` `C IMDb 8.7`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 77 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/narcos/s01) 78% critics, 95% audience | IMDb 8.7
- **Reviews**: [Brian Lowry at Variety](https://variety.com/2015/tv/reviews/narcos-review-pablo-escobar-drama-netflix-1201570440/) called it a history lesson that is also "the kind of binge-worthy TV addiction that Netflix was born to import"; the characters are thin, but it is about the game. Critics found it flat; audiences gave it 95%, one of the widest gaps here.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2707408/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●● | scares ●●● | profanity ●●● | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 3 seasons, 30 episodes, finished. Narcos: Mexico is a separate show.
- **Watch first**: Nothing.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80025172) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Ozark

A Chicago financial adviser botches a cartel money-laundering job and moves his family to the Missouri Ozarks to build a bigger operation and stay alive ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ozark_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Drama Series nominations 2019, 2020, 2022` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #68` `F PTT drama board picks`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 66 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/ozark/s01) 70% critics, 92% audience | IMDb 8.4 | Douban 8.4
- **Reviews**: [Jen Chaney at Vulture](https://www.vulture.com/2017/07/ozark-tv-review.html) found it borrowed, somewhere between Bloodline and Breaking Bad and "sometimes leaning more toward the contrived than the believable", yet carried by Jason Bateman and Laura Linney. Its season 1 Metacritic 66 is among the lowest here; audiences gave it 92%.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5071412/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●● | scares ●●● | profanity ●●● | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 4 seasons, 44 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80117552) (subscription)

### Detectives, cold cases and criminal minds

The investigators have as much story as the suspects. Lots of anthologies and limited series: one season, one complete case.

#### True Detective

A crime anthology with a new cast and case each season. Season 1 has Matthew McConaughey and Woody Harrelson as Louisiana detectives chasing a ritual killing across 17 years ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/True_Detective)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Limited Series nomination 2024 (season 4)` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #31` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #35` `C IMDb 8.8` `F PTT drama board picks (season 1)`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 87 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/true_detective/s01) 91% critics, 93% audience | IMDb 8.8
- **Reviews**: Season 1 was near-universally praised; the best-known dissent is [Emily Nussbaum in The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/magazine/2014/03/03/cool-story-bro), who granted its style but called its women paper-thin and its philosophy dorm-room talk: "it reeks of macho nonsense." Seasons vary wildly: season 2 fell to 47% on RT, which is why PTT fans mostly recommend only season 1.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2356777/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●● | scares ●●● | profanity ●●● | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 4 seasons, 30 episodes so far, each standalone; season 5 is due 2027. Season 1 (8 episodes) works on its own.
- **Watch first**: Nothing.
- **Where to watch**: [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/9a4a3645-74e0-4e4d-9f35-31464b402357) (subscription); season 1 also on [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/18655/eps/1)

#### Fargo

Noah Hawley's darkly comic crime anthology, inspired by the Coen brothers' 1996 film, tells a different Midwest crime story each season ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fargo_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Limited Series nominations 2016, 2017, 2024` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #35` `C IMDb 8.8`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 85 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/fargo/s01) 97% critics, 97% audience | IMDb 8.8
- **Reviews**: [Mary McNamara in the Los Angeles Times](https://www.latimes.com/entertainment/tv/showtracker/la-et-st-fargo-review-20140415-story.html) said it is neither prequel, sequel nor remake but a tonal accompaniment, "a whole new breed of television". Critics and viewers both give it 97%, the most consistent show here, yet it gets little discussion in Taiwan.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt2802850/parentalguide/)): violence ●●○ | sex ●●● | scares ●●○ | profanity ●○○ | drugs ●●●
- **Length**: 5 seasons, 51 episodes, each standalone; season 6 not officially renewed.
- **Watch first**: No need to see the film.
- **Where to watch**: [Prime Video](https://www.primevideo.com/detail?gti=amzn1.dv.gti.3aa9f7ba-7328-4bc4-422f-a20ba2ad9584) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Mindhunter

In the late 1970s two FBI agents interview jailed serial killers and invent criminal profiling; David Fincher leads the show ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mindhunter_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #79` `C IMDb 8.6` `F PTT drama board picks (×7)`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 79 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/mindhunter/s01) 96% critics, 95% audience | IMDb 8.6
- **Reviews**: [Ben Travers at IndieWire](https://www.indiewire.com/criticism/shows/netflix-mindhunter-review-season-1-david-fincher-1201886179/) found the pilot overlong but "its mission is pure, aesthetic outstanding, and hook undeniable". [Filmaholic critic 黃以曦](https://filmaholic.tw/series/5fb358d6fd89780001f9729a) (in Chinese) argued its real subject is not the killers' minds but how the interviews shake and change the agents.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt5290382/parentalguide/)): violence ●●● | sex ●●● | scares ●●○ | profanity ●●○ | drugs ●○○
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 19 episodes. **Season 3 is shelved indefinitely; the story has no ending.**
- **Watch first**: Nothing.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80114855) (subscription)

#### Mare of Easttown

Kate Winslet plays a detective in a small town outside Philadelphia, investigating a girl's murder while her own family falls apart ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mare_of_Easttown)).

[林運鴻 at Opinion UDN](https://opinion.udn.com/opinion/story/120617/5518457) (in Chinese) read it as a portrait of a Rust Belt town's structural failure, where violence, addiction and suicide pass down the generations.

#### Task

### Politics, power and spies

The White House, the State Department, the CIA. Lighter on screen; heavy on scheming and swearing.

#### House of Cards

Passed over for secretary of state, Congressman Frank Underwood (Kevin Spacey) and his equally ambitious wife Claire take revenge and climb to power ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/House_of_Cards_(American_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmy Drama Series nominations 2016, 2017` `B BBC 21st-century top 100 #60` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #48` `C IMDb 8.6` `F PTT drama board picks` `G GQ Taiwan list`
- **Scores**: Metacritic 76 | [RT](https://www.rottentomatoes.com/tv/house_of_cards/s01) 87% critics, 97% audience | IMDb 8.6 | Douban 9.3
- **Reviews**: [Tim Goodman at The Hollywood Reporter](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/tv/tv-reviews/house-cards-netflix-tv-review-415589/) said Netflix's first heavyweight original looked as polished as HBO, with "Kevin Spacey shines" in every line. The big split is the end: Spacey was fired before the final season, whose audience score fell to 19%.
- **Content** ([TV-MA](https://www.imdb.com/title/tt1856010/parentalguide/)): violence ●●○ | sex ●●○ | scares ●●○ | profanity ●●○ | drugs ●●○

## Limits of this list

- **It leans classic.** The Wire, Breaking Bad and House of Cards began before 2016 and are here because of critics polls; shows from 2025 on are still gathering recommendations.
- **Many reviews are based on the first few episodes**, including Mindhunter and Better Call Saul.
- **Chinese-language reception only appears where it could be read.** Douban blocks automated reading, and no full Taiwanese review could be found for The Wire, True Detective, Fargo, Narcos, Ozark, Task, Paradise, The Beast in Me or Untamed.
- **Content levels come from IMDb user votes, not official ratings.**
- **Streaming licences change.** Streaming links checked: 2026-11-05.
