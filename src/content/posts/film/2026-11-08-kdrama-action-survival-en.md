This post covers Korean action, survival and superpower drama: death games, Joseon zombies, teenagers with superpowers and a model student who fights back against bullies. All 11 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. How K-dramas differ from American shows is covered at the start of the [romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-romance-fantasy-en).

This group differs from other K-dramas:

- **Much heavier content.** Ten of the 11 are rated 19+ in Korea, and sources conflict on the eleventh, The Uncanny Counter. Gore, bullying and suicide run heavier than in the romance or life dramas.
- **More sequels.** Most K-dramas end after one season, yet 7 shows here have two or more, mostly streaming originals. Check the season count first; most need watching in order.
- **Mostly webtoon adaptations.** Nine of the 11 come from webtoons, none of which you need to read first.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: most K-dramas have no Metacritic score, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_korean_dramas), IMDb and Douban; for multi-season shows, MyDramaList and Douban are season 1, IMDb the whole show. Douban scores appear only where a news report or review quoted them.
- **Content**: Korean ratings come from Korean Wikipedia, namu.wiki or IMDb's certification list, with Taiwan platform ratings where shown; content notes come from IMDb Parents Guide contributors.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" marks licensed shows whose latest Taiwan evidence is from 2023; if the link doesn't play, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Survival and desperate odds

Death games, zombies, reincarnation and the army: heroes dropped where giving less than everything means dying. The goriest corner of the series.

#### Squid Game

456 people in deep debt are invited to play a series of children's games where losing means death and the winner takes 45.6 billion won ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Squid_Game)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Emmys 2022 for lead actor and directing (6 in all)` `A Baeksang TV Grand Prize 2022` `B New York Times 21st-century top 100 #72` `B Guardian best of 2021 #5` `C IMDb 7.9 (783K votes)` `D Netflix's top three non-English series ever (all three seasons)` `E Parrot Analytics Global Demand Awards 2021` `G #1 on Netflix Taiwan (season 3)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (season 1) (169,082 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (783K votes) | Douban 7.7 (season 1)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing season 1, [Quinci LeGardye at Polygon](https://www.polygon.com/22692220/squid-game-review-netflix/) says the death-game genre isn't new, but its care for each character's choice between survival and humanity is: "Squid Game is exciting, and startling, and tense, but that care is what really makes it worth watching." [傅紀鋼 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/156927) (in Chinese) credits its melodrama for carrying the scenes between games. Of the final season, [Aramide Tinubu at Variety](https://variety.com/2025/tv/reviews/squid-game-season-3-review-netflix-1236440570/) wrote it is "much more of a continuation of the second season than a distinct entity".
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence and intensity: stabbings, shootings, beatings; also self-harm and suicidal thoughts.
- **Length**: 3 seasons, 22 episodes, finished (9/7/6). Later seasons slipped: Rotten Tomatoes audience scores fell from 84% to 51%, and season 3 sits at 6.4 on Douban.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. The three seasons are one story, so watch in order; the UK reality show is a separate thing.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81040344) (subscription)

#### D.P.

In 2014, a private is assigned to the military police unit that hunts deserters, and with his senior partner uncovers the hazing behind each case ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/D.P._(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2022` `A Blue Dragon Series Awards best drama 2022` `B Cine21 series of 2021 #3` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (season 1) (49,802 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (13K votes) | Douban 9.1 (season 1)
- **Reviews**: [Hidzir Junaini at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/dp-k-drama-review-jung-haein-kim-sung-kyun-3030699) says the partners' rapport gives the heavy subject room to breathe, adding "these fictional depictions of hazing are sadly far from exaggerated". [吳文哲 at 轉角國際](https://global.udn.com/global_vision/story/8664/5818610) (in Chinese) uses it to look at real hazing cases in the Korean military.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence and swearing: repeated beatings, hazing and sexual humiliation.
- **Length**: 2 seasons of 6 episodes; season 2 continues directly. Season 2 scored a little lower but held up.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it. It is not a fun action show: at heart it is social realism about military hazing.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81280917) (subscription)

#### Kingdom

Three years after the Imjin War, Joseon's crown prince uncovers a zombie plague sweeping the countryside amid a palace conspiracy ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kingdom_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2020` `B New York Times best international shows of the 2010s #20` `C IMDb 8.3` `C MyDramaList 8.9 (season 2)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (season 1) (53,963 ratings) | IMDb 8.3 (66K votes) | Douban 8.6 (season 1)
- **Reviews**: [Matt Zoller Seitz at Vulture](https://www.vulture.com/2019/03/kingdom-tv-review.html) says most zombie stories stay with a small group, while this one shows a whole society collapsing: "a parable about a society with a death wish that allows rot to spread a bit further every day". [林運鴻 at 鳴人堂](https://opinion.udn.com/opinion/story/120617/4505734) (in Chinese) reads the zombies as a peasant uprising driven by hunger. Critics and viewers barely disagree.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence and intensity: zombie attacks, beheadings, cannibalism; almost no sex or swearing.
- **Length**: 2 seasons of 6 episodes plus the special Ashin of the North. Season 3 was never made.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it. Watch season 1, season 2, then the special.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80180171) (subscription)

#### Death's Game

After taking his own life, a man who has failed for years to find work is punished by Death with twelve lives that are about to end; survive one, and he keeps it ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Death%27s_Game)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2024 #9` `C MyDramaList 8.8` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays (most upvoted)` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2024 #10`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (58,061 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (26K votes) | Douban 8.4
- **Reviews**: [Bhavna Agarwal at India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/television/story/deaths-game-review-seo-in-guk-park-so-dam-star-in-a-fantastic-fantasy-kdrama-2484919-2024-01-05) praises the parade of actors across twelve lives but finds that "the show appears somewhat harsh towards those who opt to end their lives". [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/7736057) (in Chinese) calls the eight episodes eight films in different genres.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. It opens with a suicide and includes suicide, gory accidents and murder; skip it on a low day.
- **Length**: 8 episodes, finished. Originally a TVING original in Korea.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it. It is more a fantasy of reincarnation, filed here because every life is a fight to survive.
- **Where to watch**: [Prime Video](https://www.primevideo.com/-/zh_TW/detail/0ST2GP2VV9J9AKW7YJ6PZ67UYN) (subscription)

### Superpowers and fantasy

Families with powers and ghost hunters who work at a noodle shop. Plenty of fights, but at heart it is about family and comrades.

#### Moving

Three high-school students hiding superpowers, and their parents with powers and secrets of their own, protect their families from intelligence agents and North Korean operatives ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moving_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Blue Dragon Series Awards Grand Prize 2024` `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2024` `B Cine21 series of 2023 #1` `C MyDramaList 9.0` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 drama grand prize` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #2`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (60,100 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (18K votes) | Douban 8.9
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/moving-k-drama-review-a-stunningly-original-complex-superhero-saga-with-a-terrific-ensemble/article67368804.ece) calls it the year's best K-drama, about families used as pawns by the state: "this expansive superhero saga might as well be the most stunningly original show to come out this year", though 20 episodes is long. [劉芷妤 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/650e6a43fd89780001b2b928/) (in Chinese) says its heroes are forced into heroism by their humanity.
- **Content**: Korea 19+ (Disney+ Taiwan 15+). It looks like a family superhero show, but the violence is severe: smashed faces, broken necks, children killing to survive.
- **Length**: 20 episodes. Season 2 began filming in May 2026, with no date yet.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Kang Full's webtoon; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-a775613d-a93c-4be4-bef5-654f6b752699) (subscription)

#### The Uncanny Counter

A high-school boy with a disabled leg joins the Counters, who work at a noodle shop by day and hunt evil spirits escaped from the afterlife by night ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Uncanny_Counter)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList 8.8` `C IMDb 8.0` `D OCN's highest-rated drama (11.0%)` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (season 1) (81,956 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (16K votes)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2020/12/09/the-uncanny-counter-netflix-review/) likens it to X-Men in red tracksuits at a noodle shop: "a fine, light sci-fi series that will help you take your mind off heavier dramas". [芮塔 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/5217967) (in Chinese) notes the original writer left after episode 12 and many found the last stretch absurd.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ or 19+ (sources conflict). Lots of fighting and blood, frequent deaths and some bullying.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 16 plus 12 episodes (OCN, then tvN).
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it. Season 2 continues season 1.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81323551) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

### Fists do the talking

Schools, streets and the world of hired killers. The heroes are mostly people who were once pushed around.

#### Weak Hero Class 1 / Class 2

A slight but brilliant student fights back against bullies with quick thinking, physics and whatever is at hand; in Class 2, after changing schools, he faces a cross-school gang ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Weak_Hero)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Blue Dragon Series Awards best drama nomination 2023` `B Cine21 series of 2022 #10` `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #4 (9.1)` `D Netflix H1 2025: 42M views for both seasons` `E FUNdex Awards 2025` `G #2 on Netflix Taiwan`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.1 (Class 1) (132,335 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (31K votes)
- **Reviews**: After the first episode of Class 2, [Johnny Loftus at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/05/01/weak-hero-class-2-netflix-review/) recommends it: "the bullies are monstrous. But they can't stuff the power of self-confidence in a locker entirely." [推坑人妻 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/356313/) (in Chinese) finds Class 2 more action-packed. Most rate Class 2 below Class 1 (MyDramaList 9.1 down to 8.8).
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Constant bullying and fighting, severe violence; one scene of sexual assault between boys; teenagers smoking, drinking and using drugs.
- **Length**: 2 parts of 8 episodes; watch Class 1 first.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Naver webtoon; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81742615) (subscription). Class 1 was a Wavve original and is licensed on Netflix

#### Teach You a Lesson

With school violence out of control, a government bureau authorises an inspector to use force on delinquent students, monster parents and corrupt teachers ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Teach_You_a_Lesson)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList 9.0` `D #10 among Netflix's non-English series ever` `D Netflix's top K-drama of H1 2026` `F Dcard must-watch picks` `G #3 on Netflix Taiwan`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (53,508 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (22K votes)
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/teach-you-a-lesson-k-drama-review-a-terrific-kim-mu-yeol-powers-his-way-through-revenge-and-reform/article71152170.ece) admits the hero's methods are "largely horrifying" and drew criticism in Korea, yet calls it "the no-frills entertainment that has been missing from K-Dramas for a while now". [許景雅 at The Reporter](https://www.twreporter.org/a/opinion-teach-you-a-lesson) (in Chinese) asks who guarantees the justice of a strongman above the law.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Blood in nearly every episode; student drug dealing, online bullying and a suicide attempt.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, a Netflix original released at once. A second season is undecided.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Naver webtoon; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81947300) (subscription)

#### Study Group

A boy whose only talent is fighting wants to get into university, so he starts a study group at a notorious vocational school and protects his classmates with his fists ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Study_Group_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 #7` `C MyDramaList 8.8` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G friDay 2025 top dramas #6`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (59,501 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (8.2K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed review from a major outlet could be found; on her own site, [Jae-Ha Kim](https://www.jaehakim.com/2025/02/study-group-kdrama-review/) praises a hero who wants to study and lifts up classmates the school gave up on: "It's this kindness and desire to help his friends (and even foes) that makes Ga-min a true winner." [芮塔的看劇筆記本 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/326852/) (in Chinese) calls it a Korean GTO, with a lot of violence.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Lots of fighting with broken bones; severe swearing, nearly every line.
- **Length**: 10 episodes. Season 2 is renewed but hasn't aired. Originally a TVING original in Korea.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it. The tone leans comic.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/3956) (free tier)

#### Bloodhounds

A young boxer teams up with a fellow ex-Marine and an old-school moneylender to take on the loan-shark gang targeting his mother ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloodhounds_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time K-drama picks 2023 (Netflix)` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `C IMDb 8.2` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (season 1) (65,588 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (32K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Hidzir Junaini at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/bloodhounds-review-kdrama-3452955) loves the realistic fight choreography but finds little story: "this K-drama might have been better served as a more compact two-hour film". [黃小惠 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/289078/) (in Chinese) notes that after actress Kim Sae-ron's drunk-driving case the ending was rewritten, and the last two episodes don't fit.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence and intensity: blood in nearly every episode, and a long torture and waterboarding scene in episode 3.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 15 episodes; season 2 arrived in April 2026 with a new enemy.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Naver webtoon; no need to read it. Season 2 follows the same two leads.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81444051) (subscription)

#### A Shop for Killers

Raised by her uncle, a student comes home after his death to find herself under attack by killers, and learns his online shop sold weapons to assassins ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/A_Shop_for_Killers)).

- **Recommended by**: `B New York Times best international shows of 2024` `B Time best K-dramas of 2024 #4` `B Cine21 series of 2024 #8` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `E FUNdex Awards 2024 finalist`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (season 1) (43,970 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (13K votes)
- **Reviews**: After two episodes, [Puah Ziwei at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/a-shop-for-killers-review-plot-holes-gripping-thriller-3572796) sees plot holes but calls it "a fun and appealing popcorn watch, one that fires all the right synapses in your brain". [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/286604/) (in Chinese) says season 1 leaves too many mysteries open.
- **Content**: Korea 19+ (Disney+ Taiwan 15+). Severe violence and swearing: sniper headshots, throat-slitting, a severed hand.

## Limits of this list

- **Most English reviews cover the first few episodes.** Decider saw one episode of Weak Hero and The Uncanny Counter, NME two of A Shop for Killers; Study Group has no major-outlet review (a journalist's own site is used) and Death's Game relies on India Today.
- **D.P. has no conventional Taiwanese review**; a 轉角國際 essay on military hazing is used instead.
- **Douban only via quotes.** Douban pages couldn't be read; Death's Game's 8.4 comes from an opinion column and D.P.'s 9.1 from a cinema chain's official account.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** Taiwan evidence for The Uncanny Counter stops in 2023; Weak Hero Class 1 is a Wavve original licensed to Netflix.
