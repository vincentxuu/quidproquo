This post covers Korean crime, mystery and revenge: a bullied woman turned teacher, a vigilante taxi company, a walkie-talkie across time and corruption among prosecutors. All 18 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. How K-dramas differ from American shows is covered at the start of the [romance post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-romance-fantasy-en); action, zombies and survival games are in the [action and survival post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-action-survival-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Lengths vary widely.** From 6-episode Netflix miniseries to Taxi Driver's 48 episodes over three seasons; the bar chart below helps you pick by length.
- **Two levels of content.** Broadcast shows are mostly 15+ in Korea and streaming originals mostly 19+; 8 of the 18 are 19+, and bullying, sexual violence, domestic abuse and suicide all appear, noted for each show.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: most K-dramas have no Metacritic score, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_korean_dramas), IMDb and Douban; for multi-season shows, MyDramaList is season 1 and IMDb the whole show. Douban scores appear only where a news report or review quoted them, all early in the run.
- **Content**: Korean ratings come from Korean Wikipedia or IMDb's certification list, with Taiwan platform ratings where shown; content notes come from IMDb Parents Guide contributors, usually only a handful of votes.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" marks licensed shows whose latest Taiwan evidence is from 2020–2022; if the link doesn't play, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Revenge

People who were bullied, framed or even killed come back to settle the score. The most satisfying group, and the one most often faulted for its endings.

#### The Glory

Years after being brutally bullied at school, a woman becomes homeroom teacher to her chief tormentor's child and carries out a long-planned revenge ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Glory_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2023` `A Asian Academy Creative Awards best drama series 2023` `B Cine21 series of 2023 #2` `C MyDramaList 8.9` `D #3 on all of Netflix, H1 2023` `E FUNdex Awards 2023, five wins` `F Dcard must-watch picks` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #1`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (120,801 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (48K votes) | Douban 8.8 (first days)
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/the-glory-part-2-review-razor-sharp-writing-elevates-this-disturbing-tale-of-bullying-and-revenge/article66610416.ece) feared Part 2 would falter, but found its twists never predictable: it "goes above and beyond, with its razor-sharp writing, compelling narrative, and impressive performances". [許婷婷 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/63db388dfd897800010a2ebe/) (in Chinese) praises its alliance of victims. The romance subplot is the main point of contention.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence, swearing and intensity: burns with a curling iron, beatings, choking; bullying is the spine of the story, with suicide and domestic abuse too.
- **Length**: 16 episodes released in two parts of 8, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Parts draw on a real 2006 bullying case in Korea.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81519223) (subscription)

#### Taxi Driver

A former special-forces soldier drives for a taxi company that is secretly a vigilante service, taking revenge for victims the law failed ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Taxi_Driver_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList 8.9 (season 2)` `D #2 in 2023 ratings (21%)` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites (season 3)` `G friDay 2025 top dramas #4`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (season 1) (57,318 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (10K votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing season 2, [雀雀 at 500輯](https://500times.udn.com/wtimes/story/120840/7098650) (in Chinese) likes that each case wraps up in two episodes with no cliffhanger bait. At the start of season 3, a [multi-critic review in Maeil Business](https://www.mk.co.kr/en/culture/11487206) praised Lee Je-hoon's disguises but noted that "the first episode … was criticized for its contrived plot". Ratings rose each season while reviews cooled.
- **Content**: Korea 15+, with some broadcasts at 19+. Cases often involve sexual violence and abduction (mostly implied), and the revenge is brutal.
- **Length**: 3 seasons, 48 episodes (16 each); season 3 ended January 2026.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it. Watch the seasons in order.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/1798) (free with ads, or the drama plan); [friDay (season 3)](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/4408) (free with ads, or the drama plan)

#### Marry My Husband

Dying of cancer, a woman catches her husband with her best friend and is killed by him; she wakes up in 2013 and plots to pass her fate, and her husband, to her friend ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marry_My_Husband)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2024 #8` `D #34 all-time Korean cable ratings (12.0%)` `E FUNdex 2024 finished-drama buzz #4` `F Dcard must-watch picks`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (78,489 ratings) | IMDb 7.8 (13K votes)
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/marry-my-husband-k-drama-review-park-min-young-and-song-ha-yoon-are-the-stars-of-this-chaotic-revenge-thriller/article67874522.ece) praises Song Ha-yoon's villain but notes "the K-drama does falter in episode 12 with the unnecessary introduction of yet another villain". [梅衍儂 at 噓！星聞](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10091/7759523/) (in Chinese) likes that rebirth doesn't make the heroine a superhero.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Repeated verbal and some physical abuse by the husband; otherwise light.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a web novel; no need to read it. A brisk time-slip revenge story with plenty of romance and office life.
- **Where to watch**: [Prime Video](https://www.primevideo.com/-/zh_TW/detail/0U9LVA2T8NCPW1W1B51AGU7KCR) (subscription)

#### Mercy for None

Eleven years after leaving the gang, a former enforcer returns when his younger brother is murdered, to find out why and take brutal revenge ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mercy_for_None)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 honourable mention` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G 6 weeks on Netflix Taiwan's chart`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (20,983 ratings) | IMDb 7.5 (12K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Jeff Ewing at Collider](https://collider.com/mercy-for-none-review-netflix/) calls it "a gritty, action-packed, twisty thriller that satisfies the itch to see hundreds of gangsters yeeted across a room by a very sad man". [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/358915/) (in Chinese) reads it as a story about desire and duty. It topped Netflix's global non-English chart in its second week, while Korean viewers were divided (The Korea Times).
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence, swearing and intensity: knife, axe and bat fights all season.
- **Length**: 7 episodes, a Netflix original, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a webtoon; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81702777) (subscription)

#### The Manipulated

An ordinary man is framed for a heinous crime and sent to prison; when he learns who set him up, he goes after revenge ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Manipulated)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 honourable mention` `F PTT KoreaDrama "the only show I waited for weekly"` `G Global Views 2025 must-watch K-dramas #4`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (19,149 ratings) | IMDb 7.7 (5.9K votes)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/11/05/the-manipulated-hulu-review/) calls it "a solid, competent thriller that takes the ‘falsely accused’ trope a step further". [Irene Chen at Tatler Asia](https://www.tatlerasia.com/lifestyle/entertainment/the-manipulated-review-zh-hant) (in Chinese) notes the villain is known from the start, so the question is how far he will go.
- **Content**: Korea 19+ (Disney+ Taiwan 15+). Severe violence, swearing, drugs and intensity; the plot involves a rape case.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Based on a 2017 film; no need to watch it. Heavy on action.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-5c49a8a7-8236-436c-918b-15b0aa9ae0ab) (subscription)

#### Reborn Rich

Killed by the chaebol family he served, an employee is reborn in 1987 as the family's youngest grandson and uses his knowledge of the future to take over the group ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reborn_Rich)).

- **Recommended by**: `A International Emmy nomination, TV movie/miniseries` `B Cine21 series of 2023 #9` `D #2 all-time Korean cable ratings (26.9%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (28,268 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (6.2K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Walter Marsh in The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2023/feb/15/reborn-rich-a-k-drama-like-succession-with-time-travel) calls it Succession with time travel: "It is not, in case you were wondering, a subtle show", though its critique of inherited wealth is real. [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/290428/) (in Chinese) lists the finale's plot holes. It has the most divisive ending here.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; murder and family warfare.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished. It aired three episodes a week in Korea.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a web novel; no need to read it. The drama's ending differs from the novel.
- **Where to watch**: [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/2657) (free with ads, or the drama plan); [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/260346.do?cs=2) (free)

#### Karma

A debt-ridden son hires a killer to murder his father for the insurance, a couple then hit the body with their car, and strangers bound by mistakes destroy one another ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Karma_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Blue Dragon Series Awards best drama nomination 2025` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G 5 weeks on Netflix Taiwan's chart` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 K-drama list`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.1 (15,252 ratings) | IMDb 7.6 (8.5K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Jasneet Singh at Collider](https://collider.com/karma-netflix-review/) loves how the main plot keeps reframing events, but "what's more frustrating is how Karma treats its female characters' storylines". [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/338068/) (in Chinese) warns that the shifting viewpoints punish a wandering eye.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence and intensity; a character recalls an attempted sexual assault.
- **Length**: 6 episodes, a Netflix original, finished; the shortest here.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Kakao webtoon; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81703410) (subscription)

### Detectives and cold cases

Prosecutors, profilers and undercover cops. Cases peeled back layer by layer, for those who like procedure.

#### Signal

A criminal profiler in 2015 finds a walkie-talkie that connects him to a detective in the past, and together they reopen cold cases ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Signal_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2016` `C MyDramaList 8.8` `D #29 all-time Korean cable ratings (12.5%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama viral thread "better than a masterpiece"`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (44,555 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (13K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed review from a major US or UK outlet could be found; [Vasia Orion at HanCinema](https://www.hancinema.net/hancinema-s-drama-review-signal-92589.html) admits plot holes but calls it "a well-crafted and captivating experience". [Initium Media](https://theinitium.com/article/20160419-culture-review-korean-drama-signal) (in Chinese) calls it a model of social realism made commercial, drawing on real unsolved Korean cases.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. A teenage girl's rape is part of the plot (off screen); moderate violence and tension.
- **Length**: 16 episodes. A sequel is due on tvN in Korea on 2026-12-07; no Taiwan platform announced.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original drawing on real cases such as the Hwaseong serial murders. Watch it before the sequel.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80987077) (subscription)

#### Stranger

A prosecutor who lost the ability to feel after surgery teams up with a dogged detective to expose corruption linking prosecutors and a chaebol ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Stranger_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2018` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `C IMDb 8.4` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (season 1) (29,956 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (13K votes)
- **Reviews**: At the end of season 1, [Park Jin-hai at The Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/entertainment/shows-dramas/20170809/stranger-ends-leaving-viewers-wanting-more) noted modest ratings, yet "critics and drama fans alike say that ‘Stranger’ is in a league of its own". [Lizzy at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/5faa066dfd89780001285232/) (in Chinese) says season 2 never simplifies for the audience.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. The mildest here: stabbings, choking and child prostitution as a topic, none of it graphic.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 32 episodes; season 2 continues season 1.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. No romance and dense plotting, so give it your full attention.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80187302) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Flower of Evil

A man hides his true identity and past from his wife, a detective, until her investigation into a string of murders starts to close in ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Flower_of_Evil_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2021` `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #8 (9.0)` `C IMDb 8.5` `F PTT KoreaDrama "my best drama this year" (85 upvotes)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (101,010 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (20K votes) | Douban 9.1 (after 4 episodes)
- **Reviews**: After four episodes, [潘光中 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/125608/) (in Chinese) praises its pace, three murders unearthing a serial case from 18 years earlier. No full review from a major US or UK outlet could be found. Its Korean ratings barely passed 5% early on, yet its MyDramaList 9.0 and IMDb 8.5 are the highest here.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (HBO Max Taiwan 16+). One brief sex scene in which the woman is visibly uncomfortable; a character nearly drowned at the bottom of a pool.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Romance weighs as much as mystery.
- **Where to watch**: [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/092af411-59c4-4748-933d-8660542eb028) (subscription)

#### Nine Puzzles

As a teenager she found her uncle murdered beside a puzzle piece; ten years later, now a profiler, she must work with the detective who always suspected her when new puzzle killings begin ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Nine_Puzzles)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 honourable mention` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 threads` `G ELLE Taiwan 2025 K-drama list`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (16,544 ratings) | IMDb 7.6 (5K votes)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/05/21/nine-puzzles-hulu-review/) says it "starts up slow, but sets up an intriguing thriller". ### Thrillers, the occult and conspiracies

Possession, plastic surgery and live-streaming, domestic abuse, exam leaks and a nuclear plot. The most mixed group; the common thread is tension.

#### Mask Girl

An office worker insecure about her looks becomes a masked streamer by night, until a chain of misfortunes swallows her life; three actresses play her at different ages ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mask_Girl)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Blue Dragon Series Awards best drama nomination 2024` `B Cine21 series of 2023 #6` `D #2 Korean series on Netflix, H2 2023` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.6 (25,328 ratings) | IMDb 7.3 (11K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Carmen Chin at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/mask-girl-review-netflix-k-drama-3486520) calls its shifting viewpoints experimental and the show "a masterclass in knife-sharp satire and social commentary". [雀雀 at 500輯](https://500times.udn.com/wtimes/story/120840/7458203) (in Chinese) reads appearance anxiety as survival anxiety. Several English reviews felt the second half came apart, and its MyDramaList 7.6 ties Friendly Rivalry for lowest here.
- **Content**: Korea 19+. Severe violence: a rape scene, a body in a fridge, and stripping and nudity on stream.
- **Length**: 7 episodes, a Netflix original, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Naver webtoon; no need to read it. The tone is darkly comic.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81516491) (subscription)

#### Revenant

A young woman possessed by an unknown entity and a folklore professor who can see ghosts investigate a run of mysterious suicides ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Revenant_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2024` `B Cine21 series of 2023 #8` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays (35 upvotes)` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #11`

Three poor sisters chase money, independence and love; the eldest unexpectedly inherits 70 billion won from a supposedly dead colleague and runs into Korea's richest family ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Little_Women_(2022_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2023` `B Cine21 series of 2022 #2` `D #40 all-time Korean cable ratings (11.1%)`

## Limits of this list

- **Many English reviews cover only the first few episodes, and 8 of 18 come from outlets outside the usual list** (Collider, Rolling Stone, RogerEbert.com, HanCinema, Soompi, Maeil Business). Signal, Flower of Evil and Stranger, from 2016–2020, have no signed review from a major US or UK outlet. Soompi belongs to the same group as Viki.
- **Taiwanese reviews cluster on 電影神搜 and the United Daily News group**; The Manipulated and Nine Puzzles rely on Tatler Asia and 太報.
- **Almost no Douban scores.** Only The Glory and Flower of Evil have quoted figures, both from early in the run.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** Taiwan evidence for Stranger and Little Women stops in 2020 and 2022; Marry My Husband's Prime Video link is only a language page, its Taiwan listing resting on a February 2026 news report. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-08.
