This post covers Korean slice-of-life, family and period drama: a Jeju woman's whole life, a Seoul alley in 1988, office workers with two-hour commutes, and the Joseon court. All 14 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan.

## How K-dramas differ from American shows

If you are coming from the [US posts](/posts/film/2026-11-05-us-tv-workplace-history-life-en), adjust your expectations:

- **One season is one complete story.** All 14 shows here have a single, finished season of 12 to 24 episodes. No waiting for renewals, no cancellations mid-story.
- **TV dramas air two episodes a week.** Korean channels (tvN, JTBC, MBC and others) usually air one episode on each of two nights; Netflix originals drop all at once or in batches. When Life Gives You Tangerines came out four episodes a week over four weeks.
- **Episodes are long.** 60 to 90 minutes is common; Reply 1988 episodes run over 90.
- **Content is usually lighter than American TV.** There is almost no sex here, and most are rated 15+ in Korea; the things to watch for are domestic violence, suicide and drinking, noted for each show.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch. Differences from the US posts:

- **Scores**: most K-dramas have no Metacritic score and only a handful of Rotten Tomatoes reviews, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_korean_dramas) (international Asian-drama fans), IMDb (worldwide viewers) and Douban (Chinese-speaking viewers). Douban blocks automated reading, so only scores from readable Douban page caches or news reports appear; "mid-run" means the score was taken before the finale.
- **Content**: the Korean rating comes from IMDb's certification list (user-submitted). Most K-dramas have only a handful of IMDb Parents Guide votes, so this post skips the dot scale and notes what is worth knowing.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" marks licensed shows whose latest Taiwan evidence is from 2022–2023, or that only JustWatch lists; if the link doesn't play, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Period and historical dramas

From the Joseon court to a Seoul alley in 1988. Pick from here if you want to see how an era lived.

#### When Life Gives You Tangerines

Ae-sun, a poor girl born on Jeju in 1951 who wants to be a poet, and Gwan-sik, who loves her from the start, live through a lifetime of ups and downs, narrated by their daughter ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/When_Life_Gives_You_Tangerines)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2025` `A Blue Dragon Series Awards Grand Prize 2025` `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 #1` `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #1` `D Netflix H1 2025: 35M views` `E FUNdex Awards 2025 grand prize` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 #1`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.3 (85,683 ratings) | IMDb 9.0 (44K votes) | Douban 9.4
- **Reviews**: [Sherin Nicole at RogerEbert.com](https://www.rogerebert.com/streaming/when-life-gives-you-tangerines-korean-netflix-tv-review-2025) says it fits no single genre and warns Squid Game viewers to expect a slow pace: "I cried as much as I giggled." [Yin at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/6824a2abfd89780001af6c8a/) (in Chinese) reads it as three generations of women, each with a little more choice than the last. Decider, the other review on Metacritic, gave it 60 for having too little conflict; with viewers, its MyDramaList 9.3 is the highest here.
- **Content**: Korea 12+. Very light: a few kisses and a stray swear word.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished. A Netflix original, first released four episodes a week over four weeks.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. It is often compared with Reply 1988 (both star Park Bo-gum), but they are unrelated.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81681535) (subscription)

#### Reply 1988

In 1988, five childhood friends and their families on one alley in Seoul's Ssangmun-dong lean on each other through adolescence ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reply_1988)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2016` `C IMDb 9.0` `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #9` `D #6 all-time Korean cable ratings (18.8%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (74,064 ratings) | IMDb 9.0 (20K votes) | Douban 9.7
- **Reviews**: [Matthew Trammell in The New Yorker](https://www.newyorker.com/culture/culture-desk/a-korean-television-show-about-the-power-of-pop-culture) sets it against America's gloomy prestige TV: "Good gags prevail over plot twists: there are no dramatic deaths, scenes of infidelity, or fixed villains." No full Taiwanese review could be found. The Korea Times reported many viewers were unhappy with which love interest the ending chose.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Almost no sex or violence; plenty of teenage jokes.
- **Length**: 20 episodes, finished. Episodes run 90 minutes or more, among the longest here.
- **Watch first**: Nothing. It is the third Reply series, but each has its own characters and story. Fans on PTT advise getting past episode 1.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80188351) (subscription)

#### Mr. Sunshine

Born a slave in Joseon, Eugene Choi flees to America, becomes a US Marine and returns to Hanseong in the early 1900s, where he falls for a nobleman's granddaughter secretly fighting with the resistance ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Sunshine_(South_Korean_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2019` `C IMDb 8.7` `D #9 all-time Korean cable ratings (18.1%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama "favourite ever" posts`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (37,656 ratings) | IMDb 8.7 (15K votes) | Douban 9.0
- **Reviews**: [Kim So-hyun at The Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/1758418) concedes the US never stationed Marines in Joseon, yet calls it "a fantasy created to inspire viewers living today's deep-rooted political and socioeconomic divisions". [丹青 at 想想論壇](https://www.thinkingtaiwan.net/content/7352) (in Chinese) praises its humour and willingness to criticise its own culture. During the run a petition accused it of distorting history; the producers apologised and renamed the Black Dragon Society (Korea JoongAng Daily).
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Plenty of violence, but not graphic.
- **Length**: 24 episodes, finished; the longest run here.
- **Watch first**: Nothing, though knowing the late Joseon period and Japan's annexation of Korea helps.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/80991107) (subscription)

#### Jeongnyeon: The Star Is Born

In the 1950s, just after the Korean War, Jeong-nyeon, a Mokpo girl with a gift for singing, joins an all-female gukgeuk troupe and grows up amid fierce rivalry ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jeongnyeon:_The_Star_Is_Born)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2024 #2` `D #17 all-time Korean cable ratings (16.5%)` `E FUNdex 2024 finished-drama buzz #3` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2024 year-end essays`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (5,059 ratings) | IMDb 8.3 (2.2K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Joanne Soh at The Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/life/entertainment/binge-worthy-kim-tae-ri-is-the-shining-light-in-jeongnyeon-the-star-is-born) gives it four stars for Kim Tae-ri, the sisterhood and rivalry, and for preserving a women's theatre tradition TV left behind: "Kim never fails to impress." [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8497601) (in Chinese) says Kim lets you watch an actor work out a role. The drama cut the webtoon character with whom Jeong-nyeon has a same-sex romance; the Hankyoreh argued that removed the world she stood for.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Almost no sex, violence or swearing; only a couple of IMDb votes.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a Naver webtoon; no need to read it first.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-ad06e18a-8afa-4271-9643-ab9f00dc36c1) (subscription)

#### The Tale of Lady Ok

In the Joseon era, a slave named Goo-deok takes on the name, status and even the husband of "Ok Tae-young", and becomes a legal advocate for the powerless ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Tale_of_Lady_Ok)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2025` `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 #8` `D #25 all-time Korean cable ratings (13.6%)` `F Dcard year-end picks`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (7,852 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (1.2K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Bhavna Agarwal at India Today](https://www.indiatoday.in/entertainment/ott/story/the-tale-of-lady-ok-review-lim-ji-yeon-stars-in-a-standout-historical-k-drama-2674122-2025-02-03) notes the king never appears and the focus is on commoners and class: it "breathes new life into the historical drama genre", though Choo Young-woo's double role confuses and the ending feels forced. No full Taiwanese review could be found. The finale drew 13.6% nationwide.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Hami Video](https://hamivideo.hinet.net/product/279408.do?cs=2) (subscription); [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81943794) (subscription). Hami splits it into 32 parts

#### The Red Sleeve

Loosely based on King Jeongjo and Consort Ui, told from the court lady's side: a king who puts his country before love, and a woman who wants to keep the life she chose ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Red_Sleeve)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2022` `B Cine21 series of 2022 #4` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `D finale ratings 17.4% nationwide`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (27,671 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (4.5K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Yim Seung-hye at Korea JoongAng Daily](https://www.koreajoongangdaily.com/lifestyle/fiction-vs-history-mbcs-the-red-sleeve-combines-fact-and-fiction-leaving-some-confused/11621166) checks it against the king's own epitaph for her: her two refusals are real, the courtship mostly invented, and the record says she gave in only after her servants were punished. [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/293809/) (in Chinese) says history is the biggest spoiler, and the dreamlike ending reads as happy either way.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Lots of kissing; IMDb contributors mention harassment scenes. Single-digit votes.
- **Length**: 17 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Kang Mi-kang's novel; no need to read it. It is also on friDay but marked as leaving soon.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81588190) (subscription)

### Contemporary lives

No villains or big twists: work, commutes, debt, old friends. The slowest group, and the one fans call "life dramas".

#### Our Unwritten Seoul

Opposite twins, one caring for their grandmother in the countryside and one bullied at a Seoul job, swap lives for a few months ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Unwritten_Seoul)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2026` `B Cine21 series of 2025 #1` `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 #5` `E FUNdex 2025 finished-drama buzz top 10` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites (most-named)` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 K-drama #2`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (36,530 ratings) | IMDb 7.9 (6K votes) | Douban 8.6 (mid-run)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/05/27/our-unwritten-seoul-netflix-review/) recommends it, saying Park Bo-young plays both twins so well "we weren't 100% sure that both sisters were being played by the same actor". [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://udn.com/news/story/7341/8868686) (in Chinese) calls it a variation on My Liberation Notes: the relief of swapping lives is itself the cure.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Almost no sex, violence or swearing; it deals with workplace bullying.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82024805) (subscription)

#### You and Everything Else

Eun-jung and Sang-yeon's lifelong friendship of admiration, envy and misunderstanding, until 42-year-old Sang-yeon, terminally ill, asks Eun-jung to stay by her side ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/You_and_Everything_Else)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2026` `B Cine21 series of 2025 #2` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 K-drama #3`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (7,586 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (2.6K votes)
- **Reviews**: In the analysis opening an interview with its stars, [Lee Yoon-seo at The Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10586793) says it is "less about plot twists than it is about the subtle accumulation of memory, regret and longing". [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://udn.com/news/story/7341/9111778) (in Chinese) places it in a line of stories about female friendship. The South China Morning Post gave it three stars, calling it melodramatic.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (IMDb also lists 19+). Suicide and assisted dying are central to the plot.
- **Length**: 15 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81687254) (subscription)

#### My Mister

A temp worker drowning in debt and caring for her sick grandmother meets a middle-aged engineer crushed by betrayal, office politics and family duty, and each finds comfort in the other ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Mister)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama 2019` `C IMDb 9.0` `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #17` `F PTT KoreaDrama "the masterpiece I nearly missed" (35 upvotes)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (39,014 ratings) | IMDb 9.0 (17K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed review from a major English-language outlet could be found. Before it aired it was criticised as an age-gap romance; a critic at the Korean film magazine Cine21 countered that there is no romance at all, and that it is about debt and precarious work. [怒怒心理師 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/62c06138fd89780001bc8ff4/) (in Chinese) says it depicts poverty without exploiting it, though the office-politics subplot drags.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No sex; drinking in nearly every episode. Heavy in tone.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished. Episodes run about 90 minutes.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Writer Park Hae-young also wrote My Liberation Notes.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-1a039f11-52aa-496b-b4f7-87a0159af889) (subscription); [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/8c299dc3-588c-459a-9ad3-b5ef5c791e8f) (subscription)

#### My Liberation Notes

Three siblings in a town outside Seoul endure long commutes every day; with a mysterious drifter working for their father, all four want out of their dead-end lives ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/My_Liberation_Notes)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2023` `B Cine21 series of 2022 #3` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (31,765 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (7.8K votes) | Douban 9.1 (mid-run)
- **Reviews**: After eight episodes, [Prabalika M. Borah at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/my-liberation-notes-review-a-mature-empathetic-k-drama-that-keeps-you-wanting-more/article65384579.ece) praises its feel for introverts' frustration: "A thought-provoking drama that shows the life of a simple hard-working family." [彭紹宇 at 鳴人堂](https://opinion.udn.com/opinion/story/120973/6392665) (in Chinese) reads "liberation" in three layers: others' expectations, self-doubt and feeling.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No sex; lots of drinking.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original, from the writer of My Mister.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81568411) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Our Blues

On Jeju Island, linked stories follow people at the end or the start of their lives, bitter and sweet ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Our_Blues)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2023` `D #20 all-time Korean cable ratings (14.6%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`

[Kristin at 釀電影](https://vocus.cc/article/62b7d12ffd89780001b1c4a4) (in Chinese) praises how it folds depression, Down syndrome and teenage pregnancy into an accessible drama.

### Family, satire and starting over

Mothers and sons, exam mania, re-entering the world. More dramatic packaging, with crime, mystery or romcom elements.

#### The Good Bad Mother

An accident leaves an ambitious prosecutor with the mind of a child and unable to walk, and he and his mother have to rebuild their relationship ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Good_Bad_Mother)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2024` `B Time K-drama picks 2023 (Netflix)` `C MyDramaList 8.9` `D #32 all-time Korean cable ratings (12.0%)` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #8`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (52,250 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (8K votes)
- **Reviews**: After four episodes, [Carmen Chin at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/tv-reviews/the-good-bad-mother-kdrama-netflix-review-lee-do-hyun-ra-mi-ran-3439849) found the leads frighteningly real: "unapologetically raw and heartfelt". [黃小惠 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/289133/) (in Chinese) calls it a family drama seasoned with crime and revenge. Digital Spy in the UK criticised it for using disability as a tool for the mother's redemption.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No sex; quite a lot of swearing and a crime-and-revenge plot.
- **Length**: 14 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81669775) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### SKY Castle

In an elite gated community, doctors' and professors' wives will do anything to get their children into top universities; a black-comic satire of Korea's upper class and exam mania ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sky_Castle)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2019` `D #4 all-time Korean cable ratings (23.8%)` `F Dcard must-watch picks`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (38,376 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (5.3K votes)
- **Reviews**: Writing in The Korea Times opinion pages, [Emanuel Pastreich](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/opinion/20190122/sky-castle-and-the-deep-fissures-in-korean-society) sees a class drama that lingers on mansions: "Ultimately the series serves as much as a celebration of the lives of the wealthy as it does as a criticism." [紀遠林 at 換日線](https://crossing.cw.com.tw/article/11368) (in Chinese) heard Koreans say it exaggerates, "but it's all true". The finale's 23.8% set a cable record at the time, yet Korean papers such as Seoul Economic Daily recorded complaints that everyone suddenly turned good.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No sex; a mystery plot and plenty of name-calling.
- **Length**: 20 episodes, finished.

## Limits of this list

- **Most English reviews cover the first few episodes.** Long K-drama reviews are rare in major US and UK outlets, so many here come from NME, Decider and English-language outlets in Korea, Singapore and India; no signed major-outlet review could be found for My Mister.
- **Taiwanese reviews are uneven.** No full Taiwanese review could be found for Reply 1988 or The Tale of Lady Ok; Mr. Sunshine and SKY Castle rely on opinion columns (想想論壇, 換日線).
- **Most Cine21 ranks are second-hand.** Only Cine21's 2025 series-of-the-year list was checked against the original; earlier years come from namu.wiki.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** Taiwan evidence for The Good Bad Mother, Our Blues, My Liberation Notes and Castaway Diva stops in 2022–2023; SKY Castle's licences on LINE TV and myVideo have expired. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-08.
