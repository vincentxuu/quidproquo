This post covers Korean romance: a chef who lands in the Joseon era, an heiress who crash-lands in North Korea, a hotel for ghosts and a teenage fencer in 1998. All 18 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. K-dramas are split into five posts and this is the first; life, family and period dramas are in the [life and family post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-life-family-period-en).

## How K-dramas differ from American shows

If you are coming from the [US posts](/posts/film/2026-11-05-us-tv-fantasy-scifi-superhero-en), adjust your expectations:

- **One season is one complete story.** All 18 shows here have a single, finished season of 8 to 20 episodes, some with extra specials. No waiting for renewals, no cancellations mid-story.
- **TV dramas air two episodes a week.** tvN, JTBC and SBS usually air one episode on each of two nights; Netflix originals drop all at once or in batches.
- **Episodes are long.** 60 to 80 minutes is common.
- **Content is usually lighter than American TV.** Most go no further than kissing and implication, and most are rated 15+ in Korea; the things to watch for are child abuse, suicide and harassment, noted for each show.

High Korean TV ratings don't guarantee high scores from international fans, and the reverse holds too. Twinkling Watermelon's finale drew only 4.46%, yet its MyDramaList 9.2 is the highest here; Bon Appétit, Your Majesty and Crash Course in Romance drew big audiences but score at the lower end.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: most K-dramas have no Metacritic score and only a handful of Rotten Tomatoes reviews, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/shows/top_korean_dramas) (international Asian-drama fans), IMDb (worldwide viewers) and Douban (Chinese-speaking viewers). Douban scores appear only where a news report quoted them; "mid-run" means before the finale.
- **Content**: the Korean rating comes from IMDb's certification list (user-submitted); Taiwan platform ratings are added where shown. Most K-dramas have only a handful of IMDb Parents Guide votes, so this post skips the dot scale and notes what is worth knowing.
- "Listed on JustWatch Taiwan" marks licensed shows whose latest Taiwan evidence is from 2021 to early 2024; if the link doesn't play, check [JustWatch Taiwan](https://www.justwatch.com/tw).

### Time travel and fantasy

Time slips, body swaps, a hotel for ghosts and an immortal goblin. Romance leads, but each has its own world.

#### Bon Appétit, Your Majesty

A prize-winning French-cuisine chef time-slips to the Joseon era on her way home and is made royal cook to a tyrant king with an exacting palate ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bon_App%C3%A9tit,_Your_Majesty)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2026` `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 honourable mention` `C IMDb 8.0` `D #12 all-time Korean cable ratings (17.1%)` `D Netflix H2 2025: 38M views` `E FUNdex Awards 2025 best TV drama` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G #1 on Netflix Taiwan's weekly chart`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (52,054 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (16K votes)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/08/25/bon-appetit-your-majesty-netflix-review/) says it "doubles down on interesting K-drama wrinkles, couching the romcom structure around both time-travel and cooking". [胡芩 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/258385) (in Chinese) reads the time travel as wrapping for a story about professional skill under a tyranny.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Some kissing, plus mild torture, killings and bloody fights.
- **Length**: 12 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a web novel; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82024853) (subscription)

#### Lovely Runner

After her idol dies, a devoted fan finds herself back in 2008, when they were both 19, and sets out to change both their fates ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lovely_Runner)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2025` `B Time best K-dramas of 2024 #1` `C MyDramaList 8.9` `E FUNdex 2024 finished-drama buzz #1` `F PTT KoreaDrama "terrible title, hidden gem"` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2024 #6`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (113,035 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (18K votes) | Douban 9.0 (mid-run)
- **Reviews**: Having seen it all, [Kayti Burt at Time](https://time.com/6983798/lovely-runner-ending-explained/) says it mixes nostalgia, romance, a killer subplot and repeated time loops into more than the sum of its tropes, "sticking its landing by prioritizing heart over plot mechanics". After two episodes, [芮塔的看劇筆記本](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/201685), republished by The News Lens (in Chinese), expected a mindless fan fantasy and was won over by the mutual-crush twist.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Bloody fights and a serial-killer subplot; it also touches on an idol's suicide.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from a web novel; no need to read it. It reached Netflix Taiwan only in May 2026.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81914565) (subscription)

#### Twinkling Watermelon

A music-loving boy raised by deaf parents falls back in time to 1995 after a fight with his father, and forms a band with his father's younger self ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twinkling_Watermelon)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #2 (9.2)` `C IMDb 8.8` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 finalist` `F PTT KoreaDrama "held back by its title"` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.2 (120,308 ratings) | IMDb 8.8 (17K votes)
- **Reviews**: After four episodes, [Gladys Yeo at NME](https://www.nme.com/reviews/twinkling-watermelon-review-a-touching-tale-of-time-travel-3509726) found it "spends far too much time getting its characters where they need to be", though the relationships hold it up. [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/287399/) (in Chinese) says its weight comes from a boy interpreting the world for his deaf family. The finale drew just 4.46% in Korea, yet its MyDramaList 9.2 is the highest here.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Repeated physical and verbal abuse of a child, and mentions of suicide.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. The core is father and son, a deaf family and a band; romance is one thread among several.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/82006291) (subscription). Only Taiwanese user posts say it arrived in late 2025; no official confirmation

#### Hotel del Luna

A hotel that serves only ghosts is run by Jang Man-wol, cursed to stay there for a millennium; a high-flying hotelier is forced to become its new manager ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hotel_del_Luna)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards Korea best drama 2019` `C IMDb 8.1` `D #33 all-time Korean cable ratings (12.0%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (117,752 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (19K votes)
- **Reviews**: Mid-run, [Im Eun-byel at The Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/2059566) noted it flips Goblin's protective-hero dynamic, with IU's ageless owner guarding a young manager: "IU exhibits a sense of eternal loneliness hidden beneath a cranky and grumpy temper." [夜間看劇筆記 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/190494/) (in Chinese) reads it as a meditation on letting go, though the leads have little chemistry.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (Disney+ Taiwan 15+). Some ghosts are gruesome and bloody; drinking in nearly every episode.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. JustWatch also lists Netflix, with no other Taiwan evidence.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-55025af9-5fb7-46a1-a6c3-3e27626652ca) (subscription)

#### Mr. Queen

A womanising Blue House chef falls into a pool and wakes up in the body of a Joseon queen, surviving palace intrigue alongside a king who wants to rule well ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Mr._Queen)).

- **Recommended by**: `C MyDramaList top Korean drama #18 (8.9)` `C IMDb 8.5` `D #11 all-time Korean cable ratings (17.4%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.9 (79,656 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (17K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Kelcie Mattson at Collider](https://collider.com/netflix-mr-queen/) says it crams in court intrigue, awkward romance and body-swapping and "couldn't offer more delights if it tried (and oh, does it try)". [Vivier Citygirl at 女人迷](https://womany.net/read/article/26581) (in Chinese) argues the cad becomes a voice for the court women, but asks whether that still leaves the women being spoken for.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (HBO Max Taiwan 13+, US TV-MA). Passionate kissing, implied sex and sexual jokes.
- **Length**: 20 episodes, finished; the longest here.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from the Chinese web series Go Princess Go; no need to watch it. A spin-off short streamed only on Korea's TVING.
- **Where to watch**: [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/80826f60-468a-4ac2-aaee-466e7889fd72) (subscription)

#### Guardian: The Lonely and Great God (Goblin)

Betrayed and executed by a young king, a Goryeo general is cursed to live forever as a goblin until his bride pulls the sword from his chest; she turns out to be a high-school girl who can see ghosts ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Guardian:_The_Lonely_and_Great_God)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2017` `C MyDramaList 8.8 (most ratings here)` `C IMDb 8.5` `D #7 all-time Korean cable ratings (18.7%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (175,311 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (39K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed review from a major US or UK outlet in 2016–17 could be found; in a recommendation column, [Hoai-Tran Bui at /Film](https://www.slashfilm.com/623832/the-daily-stream-its-time-to-ring-in-gong-yoo-season-with-the-fantasy-k-drama-goblin/) calls it "the epitome of the K-drama". [陳慶德 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/61097) (in Chinese) explains the folklore behind its goblin. During the run The Korea Herald reported Korean critics objecting to the schoolgirl-and-900-year-old age gap.
- **Content**: Korea 15+ (Disney+ Taiwan 12+). Sword fighting in flashbacks; the age gap between the leads drew criticism.
- **Length**: 16 episodes plus 3 specials, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-e62ac894-f6b0-4580-8fcc-da30de683560) (subscription); [HBO Max](https://www.hbomax.com/tw/zh/show/4b094f1c-f494-43e5-92d4-d35e2dcc04a0) (subscription)

### Romantic comedies

Heirs, star tutors, a seaside village and the boss at work. The classic K-drama romance: brisk and light.

#### Queen of Tears

Three years into a marriage on the brink, a chaebol heiress is diagnosed with a brain tumour, and she and her country-boy husband have to face what went wrong ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Queen_of_Tears)).

- **Recommended by**: `C IMDb 8.2` `D tvN's highest-rated drama ever (24.9%)` `D Netflix H1 2024 top K-drama` `E FUNdex Awards 2024 grand prize` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2024 year-end essays` `G 15 weeks on Netflix Taiwan's chart`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.7 (97,956 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (29K votes) | Douban 7.1 (mid-run)
- **Reviews**: [Geoffrey Bunting at Time](https://time.com/6972568/queen-of-tears-finale-netflix/) calls it two shows: a fresh marriage romance that spirals into coincidence and conspiracy, so that "in fixing Hae-in's brain, Queen of Tears loses its heart". [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/286040/) (in Chinese) agrees the ending is melodramatic but says the leads' chemistry carries it.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Light, though terminal illness and family feuds make it heavier than a typical romcom.
- **Length**: 16 episodes plus 2 specials, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original, from the writer of Crash Landing on You but unrelated.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81707951) (subscription)

#### Crash Landing on You

A South Korean heiress is blown across the border by a tornado while paragliding, lands in North Korea and is hidden by a North Korean officer; they fall in love across the divide ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_Landing_on_You)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards best drama series 2020` `C MyDramaList 9.0` `C IMDb 8.7` `D #5 all-time Korean cable ratings (21.7%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 9.0 (170,265 ratings) | IMDb 8.7 (62K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Jo Walker in The Guardian](https://www.theguardian.com/culture/2020/may/11/crash-landing-on-you-parachute-into-this-addictively-romantic-south-korean-soap-opera) finds it richer and stranger than its romance-novel premise, with most of the fun in the soldiers and village women: "it is brave and optimistic in its portrayal of the North". [雀雀 at 釀電影](https://filmaholic.tw/series/5e4d0217fd897800012376e1/) (in Chinese) calls it entirely predictable but carried by its leads.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Gunfights, stabbings and a little blood; plenty of drinking.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81159258) (subscription)

#### Crash Course in Romance

A side-dish shop owner whose daughter is in Korea's exam race and the country's star cram-school teacher fall into a bittersweet relationship ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Crash_Course_in_Romance)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time K-drama picks 2023 (Netflix)` `D #13 all-time Korean cable ratings (17.0%)` `E FUNdex Awards 2023 best TV drama` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers' poll #9`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.3 (48,345 ratings) | IMDb 7.7 (7.1K votes)
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/crash-course-in-romance-series-review-love-in-the-time-of-academic-pressure-competition-and-parental-trauma/article66587125.ece) likes the fortysomething leads but says the subplots pile up: "there are constant tonal jumps in the show, especially during its latter half". [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/289867/) (in Chinese) says only the romance holds up. High ratings, criticised second half.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a serial-killer subplot and a character's suicide.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81649877) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Welcome to Samdal-ri

When a Seoul photographer's career collapses overnight, she goes home to Jeju and reconnects with her childhood friend, now a weather forecaster ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Welcome_to_Samdal-ri)).

- **Recommended by**: `C IMDb 8.0` `D #30 all-time Korean cable ratings (12.4%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2023 year-end essays` `G #3 on Netflix Taiwan's weekly chart`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (62,386 ratings) | IMDb 8.0 (11K votes)
- **Reviews**: [Tanu I. [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/286769/) (in Chinese) likes that the heroine, accused of workplace bullying, reflects before defending herself.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Lots of drinking; the last episode implies sex, with no nudity.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81697983) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### Love Scout

A workaholic headhunting-firm CEO and her capable secretary, a single father, look out for each other at work and slowly fall in love ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_Scout)).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2025 honourable mention` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2025 favourites thread` `G Hami Video 2025 most-watched drama` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 K-drama list`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (30,987 ratings) | IMDb 7.7 (3.4K votes)
- **Reviews**: [S. Poorvaja at The Hindu](https://www.thehindu.com/entertainment/movies/love-scout-k-drama-review-han-ji-min-lee-jun-hyuk/article69222536.ece) praises how it flips the boss-and-secretary formula and lets the leads talk things through: "At 12 episodes, Love Scout is the rare K-Drama that leaves you wishing there were a few more episodes." [臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/322843/) (in Chinese) reviewed the first two episodes.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. No IMDb Parents Guide votes.
- **Length**: 12 episodes plus 2 specials, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. In Taiwan it is exclusive to Hami Video, not on Netflix.

#### Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha

A Seoul dentist opens a clinic in a seaside village and falls for its jack-of-all-trades, while the villagers' own stories unfold ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hometown_Cha-Cha-Cha)).

- **Recommended by**: `C IMDb 8.3` `C MyDramaList 8.7` `D #28 all-time Korean cable ratings (12.7%)` `F PTT KoreaDrama starter threads`

- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2021/08/30/hometown-cha-cha-cha-netflix-review/) recommends it for its village ensemble but warns "it moves so slowly at times, the momentum comes close to grinding to a halt." [雪影劇場 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/157942) (in Chinese) found every stage of life tucked into the side stories.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. One scene of sexual harassment, plus trauma and suicide in the story.
- **Length**: 16 episodes, finished.
- **Watch first**: A remake of the 2004 film Mr. Handy, Mr. Hong; no need to watch it.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81473182) (subscription, listed on JustWatch Taiwan)

#### King the Land

The heir to a hotel group, caught up in a succession fight, meets an employee who is always smiling ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/King_the_Land)).

#### Love Next Door

- **Content**: Korea 15+. Implied sex, no nudity; burnout and depression in the story.

#### Twenty-Five Twenty-One

During the 1998 Asian financial crisis, a teenage fencer and a young man whose family has lost everything chase their dreams, in a story spanning 1998 to 2021 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Twenty-Five_Twenty-One)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards best drama series 2022` `A Baeksang Best Drama nomination 2022` `B Cine21 series of 2022 #6` `C MyDramaList 8.8` `D peak Korean ratings 11.5%` `F PTT KoreaDrama "my No. 1 K-drama" (59 upvotes)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (101,281 ratings) | IMDb 8.5 (24K votes) | Douban 8.2 (down from 9.1 after the finale)
- **Reviews**: After episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2022/02/15/twenty-five-twenty-one-netflix-review/) says it "defies most of the clichés we see in romantic K-dramas"; the real romance is the heroine and fencing. [阿潑 at 鳴人堂](https://opinion.udn.com/opinion/story/5754/6304138) (in Chinese) accepts the cruel ending as true to life. When the leads did not end up together, its Douban score fell from 9.1 to 8.2 overnight; Time's Geoffrey Bunting called it a rare show that stuck an unconventional landing and was review-bombed for it.
- **Content**: Korea 15+. Light; occasional swearing and drinking.
- **Length**: 16 episodes plus 1 special, finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original. Knowing the ending is divisive may save some disappointment.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81517168) (subscription)

#### Love in the Big City

A gay man shares a flat with his straight best friend, then spends a decade navigating a mother who rejects him, lovers lost to social pressure, and himself ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Love_in_the_Big_City_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `B Time best K-dramas of 2024 #3` `B Cine21 series of 2024 #4` `F PTT KoreaDrama 2024 year-end essays` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2024 #4 (top K-drama)`

[臺灣歐妮 at 電影神搜](https://news.agentm.tw/307625/) (in Chinese) stresses it is no prettified BL drama. Before it aired, conservative groups in Korea opposed its public funding (Wikipedia).
- **Content**: Korea 19+, the only one here. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; themes include HIV and forced psychiatric treatment by family.
- **Length**: 8 episodes, finished. Originally a TVING original in Korea.
- **Watch first**: Adapted by Park Sang-young from his own novel; no need to read it. It plays more like a coming-of-age drama with romance as one thread.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81988253) (subscription); [friDay影音](https://video.friday.tw/drama/detail/3787) (buy to own)

## Limits of this list

- **Most English reviews cover the first few episodes.** Major US and UK outlets rarely review romance K-dramas, so many here come from Decider, NME and The Hindu; Goblin has only a recommendation column, Love in the Big City a review from a gay culture magazine, and Our Movie a review based on two episodes.
- **Taiwanese reviews cluster on one site.** 11 of the 18 come from pen-name writers at 電影神搜; Mr. Queen relies on a gender column at 女人迷, and Love Scout, Our Movie and Lovely Runner have only premiere reviews.
- **Only three Douban scores**, all taken mid-run or during a controversy, not final scores.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** Taiwan evidence for Crash Course in Romance, Welcome to Samdal-ri, Hometown Cha-Cha-Cha and King the Land stops between 2021 and early 2024; Twinkling Watermelon is backed only by Taiwanese users' posts. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-08.
