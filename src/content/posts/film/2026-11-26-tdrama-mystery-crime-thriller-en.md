The Taiwanese part of the series opens with mystery, crime and thrillers: serial killings modelled on a Japanese novel, a murder in a Taipei hostess bar, a death-penalty defence for an Indigenous migrant worker and an influencer detective solving cases on livestream. All 13 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. Social issues, workplace and history dramas are in the [society and history post](/posts/film/2026-11-26-tdrama-society-work-history-en); the Korean equivalent is the [Korean crime and mystery post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-crime-mystery-revenge-en).

Two rules differ from the Chinese posts:

- **The Golden Bell Awards count as award juries.** The culture ministry organises them, but an independent jury decides, so they count under A, unlike China’s state-run Feitian and Golden Eagle awards.
- **PTS+, TaiwanPlus and broadcasters’ official full-episode YouTube uploads count as legal channels.** Shows available only on iQIYI’s international site still go on the watch-later list.

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Short, and mostly on Netflix.** All 13 run 24 episodes or fewer, most 8 to 10; 10 are on Netflix.
- **Heavy content.** Dismemberment, sexual violence and war all appear; only 4 have a rating on a Taiwan platform page, noted for each show.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: Taiwanese dramas have no Metascores (Had I Not Seen the Sun has a page but too few reviews), so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/search?adv=titles&ty=68&co=5&so=top) and IMDb, flagging scores from fewer than 100 raters. Douban appears only as year-end list ranks under recommended by; Douban figures from beauty sites appear only in the review entries.
- **Content**: ratings shown come from Taiwan platform pages; most Netflix titles have no Taiwan rating data. Content notes come from the IMDb Parents Guide; most shows have no votes, so the notes follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages; where Netflix availability rests on media reports or official trailers, the entry says so.

### Netflix crime and mystery

Serial killings, a hostess-bar murder, the dead returning for revenge, a documentary maker tracking a killer and an influencer detective. All on Netflix, and the group that has reached the most viewers abroad.

#### Copycat Killer

In the fictional 1990s city of Songyan, prosecutor Kuo Hsiao-chi hunts a serial killer who manipulates the media, the police and victims’ families ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Copycat_Killer_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Awards 2023 drama series nomination (16 nominations in all)` `B The News Lens, 徐佑德’s five best Taiwanese series of 2023 (named in the headline)` `C IMDb 7.0 (3K votes)` `C MyDramaList top Taiwanese drama #21 (8.1)` `D #2 on Netflix’s global non-English weekly chart (2023, the first Taiwanese series to chart)` `E Google Taiwan 2023 trending Taiwanese drama #1` `E DailyView 2023 Taiwanese drama buzz #2` `F PTT TaiwanDrama 2023 mystery picks thread (original poster’s list)` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers’ favourite Taiwanese drama #1 (12.77%)` `G #3 on Netflix Taiwan`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.1 (3,176 ratings) | IMDb 7.0 (3K votes)
- **Reviews**: After all 10 episodes, [Jonathon Wilson at Ready Steady Cut](https://readysteadycut.com/2023/03/31/copycat-killer-season-1-review/) gave it 4/5 while finding ten hours too long: "Copycat Killer is a bit of a copycat itself where other crime fiction TV titans are concerned, but it’s nonetheless a high quality and engaging whodunit." [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/7070402/) (in Chinese) called it an excellent Taiwanese series in the end. The same week, [潘光中 at Movie Hunter](https://news.agentm.tw/252333/) (in Chinese) gave it a bare pass of 65/100, faulting the plot logic. It set Taiwanese records on Netflix’s global chart and in Golden Bell nominations, yet Douban’s 7.4, as compiled by a Taiwanese beauty site, ranks only fifth among Netflix Taiwanese originals.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. Three to eight IMDb Parents Guide votes per category: frightening scenes severe, violence moderate; several murders are shown, with dismembered victims.
- **Length**: 10 episodes (2023), finished; a single season. No word of a season 2.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Miyuki Miyabe’s mystery novel Mohōhan; no need to read it. The prosecutor lead is new to the adaptation.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81461539) (subscription). Availability is based on a February 2026 Netflix thriller list by Beauty321

#### The Resurrected

Two mothers, one whose daughter died and one whose daughter was left in a coma after a fraud-kidnapping, use a goddess ritual to revive the executed ringleader for seven days and punish him themselves ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Resurrected_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Awards 2026 miniseries nomination` `B Taiwanese critics’ 2025 year-end list, Taiwanese dramas #7` `C IMDb 7.0 (976 votes)` `D Netflix 2025 Chinese-language series #5 by views (2.4M)` `E Google Taiwan 2025 trending Taiwanese drama #8` `E DailyView 2025 Taiwanese drama buzz #5` `F PTT TaiwanDrama 2025 top-three Taiwanese dramas thread (2 replies)` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 Taiwanese dramas #2` `G #1 on Netflix Taiwan`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.7 (550 ratings) | IMDb 7.0 (983 votes)
- **Reviews**: Writing on launch day, [Karina Adelgaard at Heaven of Horror](https://www.heavenofhorror.com/reviews/the-resurrected-2025-netflix-series/), a horror site, recommended it: "The production quality of this Taiwanese series is very impressive. … The struggle can, however, come from the actual plot and pace. Fortunately, not this one!" [白鐵仔 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/260153) (in Chinese) called it a high point for Taiwanese drama, with one caveat: the first season’s story is a little convoluted because it is set up for more seasons. English and Taiwanese reviews lean positive and it reached #1 on Netflix Taiwan, while IMDb 7.0 and MDL 7.7 are more reserved.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. Only one to five IMDb Parents Guide votes: violence and drugs rated severe; a user warning flags several graphic rape scenes, and there is torture.
- **Length**: 9 episodes (2025), finished. No word of a season 2.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original script.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81715542) (subscription). Availability is based on Netflix’s press release and a February 2026 list by Beauty321

#### The Victims’ Game

Fang Yi-jen, a forensic officer with Asperger’s, finds his daughter tied to a murder and teams up with a crime reporter to chase a string of “last wish” deaths ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Victims%27_Game)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards, Taiwan best drama series 2020 and 2024 (season 2)` `A Global OTT Awards 2020 best actor (Joseph Chang)` `C IMDb 7.3 (2.4K votes)` `C MyDramaList top Taiwanese drama #27 and #37 (season 1 8.1, season 2 8.0)` `D Netflix H1 2023: 1.9M hours (season 1)` `E DailyView 2024 Taiwanese drama buzz #6` `F PTT TaiwanDrama 2023 mystery picks thread (7 replies)` `F PTT 2020 classic Taiwanese drama poll #23 (34 votes)` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2024 Taiwanese dramas #6 (season 2)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.1 (season 1) (2,739 ratings) | IMDb 7.3 (2.4K votes)
- **Reviews**: [James Marsh at the South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/lifestyle/entertainment/article/3267003/netflix-drama-review-victims-game-season-2-unconvincing-taiwanese-murder-thriller) gave season 2 two stars out of five, opening with a series "that sacrifices storytelling in favour of slick production". After all eight episodes of season 1, [地下電影 at Movie Hunter](https://news.agentm.tw/107151/) (in Chinese) said it took Taiwanese TV to a new peak, if a little preachy. [徐佑德 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/204351) (in Chinese), a co-founder of the production company 無垠, found season 2 still world-class in craft but full of unresolved plot bugs. Douban, as compiled by a Taiwanese beauty site, fell from 8.0 for season 1 to 7.1 for season 2.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a string of murders, including suicide-like “last wish” deaths.
- **Length**: 2 seasons, 16 episodes: season 1 has 8 (2020), season 2 has 8 (2024); both finished. No confirmed season 3.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from 天地無限’s mystery novel 第四名被害者; no need to read it. Season 2 continues the father–daughter story, so watch season 1 first.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81318134) (subscription, seasons 1–2)

#### Light the Night

In 1988 an unidentified woman’s body is found on a Taipei hillside, and the police inquiry exposes the friendship and feud of two hostesses running the Japanese-style bar Light ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Light_the_Night)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Asian Academy Creative Awards 2022, Taiwan best drama series` `A Asian Academy Creative Awards 2022 grand best supporting actor (Wu Kang-ren)` `C IMDb 7.4 (1.8K votes)` `C MyDramaList 7.9–8.0 across the three parts` `D Netflix H1 2023: 1.5M hours (part one)` `E Google Taiwan 2022 trending Taiwanese drama #1` `F PTT TaiwanDrama 2023 mystery picks thread (poster binged it till dawn)` `G LINE TODAY 2021 most-recommended Taiwanese drama #1 (16.84%)` `G #3 on Netflix Taiwan`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.0 (part one) (931 ratings) | IMDb 7.4 (1.8K votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing only part one, [Jonathon Wilson at Ready Steady Cut](https://readysteadycut.com/2021/11/26/light-the-night-review-a-soapy-east-asian-character-drama/) gave it 3/5: "Light the Night is a soapy and lightly melodramatic character drama that’ll probably divide audiences, but there’s enough mystery to keep one engaged just about to the end." Also on part one only, [潘光中 at Movie Hunter](https://news.agentm.tw/203102/) (in Chinese) called it a new benchmark for Taiwanese TV with hardly a wasted moment. Critics split from part one on, and parts two and three drew no signed reviews. Douban, as compiled by a Taiwanese beauty site, gives 8.1; it won none of its 11 Golden Bell nominations.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; it opens with a murder and is set among hostess bars and gangsters.
- **Length**: Three parts, 24 episodes, 8 each (2021–2022), finished.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original script. The three parts tell one story, so start with part one; the spin-off variety shows are optional.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81477952) (subscription, all three parts). Availability is based on a February 2026 Netflix thriller list by Beauty321

#### Had I Not Seen the Sun

Li Jen-yao, 25, confesses to killing several high-school classmates, and documentary researcher Chou Pin-yu is drawn into their school past while interviewing him ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Had_I_Not_Seen_the_Sun)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Seoul International Drama Awards 2026 best series` `A Global OTT Awards 2026 best lead actor (Tseng Jing-hua)` `B Taiwanese critics’ 2025 year-end list, Taiwanese dramas #3` `C MyDramaList top Taiwanese drama #4 and #5 (both parts 8.5)` `C IMDb 7.7 (768 votes)` `D Netflix 2025 Chinese-language series #4 by views (2.9M)` `F PTT TaiwanDrama 2025 top-three Taiwanese dramas thread (2 replies)` `G LINE TODAY Spotlight Awards 2025 Taiwanese dramas #9 (part one)` `G #4 on Netflix Taiwan`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.5 (part one) (3,209 ratings) | IMDb 7.7 (772 votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing only episode 1, [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2025/11/13/had-i-not-seen-the-sun-netflix-review) said STREAM IT: "The first episode of Had I Not Seen The Sun sets up an interesting dual story about a serial killer’s high school years and how a filmmaker connects with one of his victims years later." After both parts, [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/9241128) (in Chinese) said part two’s reveals turn the strange storytelling into a healing journey, its most original idea. Metacritic lists only Decider and Screen Rant, both 70, too few for a Metascore, while James Marsh at the South China Morning Post gave it 2.5/5 and called it a "ridiculous serial killer romance".
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; serial murder, school bullying and violent revenge, with a strong romance thread.
- **Length**: Two parts of 10 episodes each, 20 in all (2025), finished; one season in two parts. No word of a sequel.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original script. The two parts are one story, so start with part one.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81766275) (subscription, both parts)

#### Shards of Her

Headhunter Lin Chen-hsi wakes after a car crash back in her hometown of Yilan, where life is not as she remembers, apparently tied to the murder of her old middle-school teacher ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shards_of_Her)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Awards 2023 miniseries nomination` `C Douban top-rated Chinese-language series of 2022 #2` `C MyDramaList top Taiwanese drama #24 (8.1)` `D Netflix H1 2023: 1.5M hours` `E Google Taiwan 2022 trending Taiwanese drama #3` `F PTT TaiwanDrama 2025 mystery picks thread (original poster’s list)` `G LINE TODAY 2022 Taiwanese drama poll, interim #2 (10.98%)`

[凱特C at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/6730267) (in Chinese) recommended it, saying the dissociation premise explains its seemingly exaggerated plot and keeps it from preaching. Douban, as compiled by a Taiwanese beauty site, gives 8.6, the highest of Netflix’s Taiwanese originals, and ranked it second among 2022’s Chinese-language series; IMDb has just 6.9.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; the heroine was sexually assaulted by a teacher in middle school, and the story involves dissociation, hidden-camera footage and a suicide.
- **Length**: 9 episodes (2022), finished; a single season.
- **Watch first**: Nothing; it is an original script.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81622630) (subscription). Availability is based on a February 2026 Netflix thriller list by Beauty321

### True crime, courts and politics

An anthology of real cases, a death-penalty defence for an Indigenous migrant worker, a tabloid reporter’s death and an imagined cross-strait war.

An Indonesian fisherman is charged with killing an Amis captain’s family of three, and public defender Tung Pao-chu, from the victims’ own village, is forced to take his case ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E5%85%AB%E5%B0%BA%E9%96%80%E7%9A%84%E8%BE%AF%E8%AD%B7%E4%BA%BA)).

- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.7 (few ratings) (47 ratings) | IMDb 8.1 (552 votes)
- **Reviews**: Reviewing only the first two episodes on his personal site, [Brian Hioe](https://nomanisanis.land/port-of-lies-review/), editor of New Bloom magazine, recommended it with reservations: "If Port of Lies is somewhat problematic, it is in that its depiction of Indonesian migrants is always as passive victims." Lawyer [楊晴翔](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/189712) (in Chinese), in a personal essay republished by The News Lens, read it through language and the death penalty and praised its writing, direction and acting. Golden Bell, IMDb 8.1 and Douban’s year-end list agree; Hioe’s point about the migrants is the only dissent.

- **Length**: 8 episodes (2023), finished; a single season.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Tang Fu-jui’s novel of the same name; no need to read it. The book won the Mirror Fiction screen-novel prize, and the author directed and wrote the series.
- **Where to watch**: [公視+](https://www.ptsplus.tv/zh/programs/9d9bd8cd-83cb-4119-9a70-35ec815fa131) (available); [LINE TV](https://www.linetv.tw/drama/16600/eps/1) (available). TaiwanPlus streams it free with English subtitles (Mirror Media, 2024)

## Limits of this list

- **English reviews come mostly from outlets outside the usual list.** 12 of the 13 have a signed English review, mostly from the South China Morning Post, Ready Steady Cut and Heaven of Horror; the SCMP sits behind a paywall, so only openings and star ratings were read. Tabloid has no signed English review.
- **Taiwanese reviews come from a few writers.** 雀雀, 潘光中 and 徐佑德 each reviewed more than one show, and 徐佑德 co-founded a production company; Mirror Media shares a group with Mirror Fiction, which produced Port of Lies and Tabloid. Million-Follower Detective has no signed Taiwanese review.
- **Rating counts vary widely.** Port of Lies, Zero Day Attack, Taiwan Crime Stories and Tabloid have under 100 MyDramaList ratings each; Zero Day Attack scores 4.9 on IMDb, far below its buzz.
