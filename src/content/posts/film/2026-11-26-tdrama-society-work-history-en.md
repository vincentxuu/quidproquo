This post covers Taiwanese social-issue, workplace and historical dramas: two families after a random killing, party staffers in an election campaign, a Vietnamese doctor treating undocumented migrant workers, and an American consul negotiating with Indigenous chiefs in Hengchun in 1867. All 15 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. The selection rules (Golden Bell counted as an award jury; PTS+ and TaiwanPlus counted as legal channels) are explained at the start of the [mystery and crime post](/posts/film/2026-11-26-tdrama-mystery-crime-thriller-en); the Japanese equivalent is the [Japanese workplace post](/posts/film/2026-11-11-jdrama-work-family-period-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Loved by juries, little rated abroad.** 14 of the 15 have award recognition, but only 4 have audience ratings (C) behind them and only 1 has viewing data (D); several have under 50 MyDramaList ratings.
- **Mostly on PTS+.** 11 of the 15 are on PTS+, and Three Tears in Borneo is free worldwide; only Wave Makers is on Netflix.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: none of these has a Metascore, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/search?adv=titles&ty=68&co=5&so=top) and IMDb; several have very few ratings, which is flagged. The score line gives Douban only from news reports or year-end lists; Douban figures from beauty sites appear in the review entry.
- **Content**: none of the 15 Taiwan platform pages shows a rating and the IMDb Parents Guide has no votes, so content notes follow the plot.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages; where availability rests on a platform article or media report, the entry says so.

### Social issues

Two families after a random killing, party staffers in an election, construction workers dreaming of a windfall, the Chunghwa Market in 1985 and a crew that cleans up after lonely deaths.

#### The World Between Us

After a random killing, the killer’s family, the victims’ families, a defence lawyer and people living with mental illness each struggle to cope; Season 2 turns to a supermarket arson ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_World_Between_Us_(Taiwanese_TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Drama Series 2019, 2026 (Season 2)` `A Asian Academy Creative Awards 2019 Taiwan best drama series` `B Taiwan critics’ poll 2025, Taiwanese dramas #1 (Season 2)` `B 500times 2022, Ryan Cheng’s “three key Taiwanese dramas”` `C IMDb 8.8 (3.3K votes, #1 Taiwanese drama)` `C Douban 2025 highest-rated Chinese-language series #3 (Season 2)` `E Google Taiwan 2019 top trending searches #1` `E DailyView 2025 Taiwanese dramas #7 (Season 2)` `F PTT 2019 Taiwanese drama poll #1 (514 votes)` `F PTT 2020 all-time Taiwanese drama poll #1 (174 votes)` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Taiwanese dramas #3 (Season 2)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (535 ratings) | IMDb 8.8 (3.3K votes) | Douban 8.4 (Season 2; #3 among Douban’s highest-rated Chinese-language series of 2025)
- **Reviews**: [Anthony Kao at Cinema Escapist](https://cinemaescapist.com/?p=11712) recommends Season 1, calling its handling of a large cast “a testament to its tight screenwriting”. [王子榮 at The Reporter](https://www.twreporter.org/a/saturday-features-legal-profession-the-world-between-us-after-the-flames) (in Chinese) reads Season 2 through Taiwan’s new citizen-judge system and says it can become a shared memory for this generation of Taiwanese. This is Taiwan’s strongest consensus pick; the dissent centres on Season 1’s overly tidy ending. A beauty site’s Douban compilation puts Season 1 at 9.4.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, a random killing and schizophrenia, and in Season 2 an arson that kills five and injures twelve.
- **Length**: Season 1, 10 episodes (2019); Season 2, 10 episodes (2025); both finished. Season 2 has an entirely new cast; MyDramaList gives it 7.7 (35 ratings), and IMDb lists both seasons under one entry.
- **Watch first**: An original script; nothing to watch first. Season 2 starts over with new characters and issues, so it works without Season 1.
- **Where to watch**: [公視+](https://www.ptsplus.tv/zh/programs/55585715-e0e9-4e9f-8956-112319ecd7ac) (both seasons); [CATCHPLAY+](https://edsays.catchplay.com/tw/article-4865-mjaa6gxm) (subscription, both seasons); [TaiwanPlus](https://www.mirrormedia.mg/story/20241022insight004) (Season 1, free, English subtitles). CATCHPLAY+ availability is based on its own October 2026 article; TaiwanPlus on a 2024 Mirror Media report

#### Wave Makers

With a heated election approaching, a team of party campaign staffers face one tough choice after another ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wave_Makers)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries 2023` `A Asian Academy Creative Awards 2023 Taiwan best drama series` `B The Economist best TV of 2023` `B The News Lens, 徐佑德’s best Taiwanese dramas of 2023 #1` `C IMDb 8.2 (1.7K votes)` `D Netflix H1 2023: 8.2M hours viewed (#2 Taiwanese drama)` `E DailyView 2023 Taiwanese dramas #1` `E Google Taiwan 2023 trending dramas #6` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers’ favourite Taiwanese drama #3` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #1 (7 May 2023)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.4 (203 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (1.7K votes)
- **Reviews**: [James Marsh at SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/lifestyle/entertainment/article/3218237/) gives it 4/5, saying in his standfirst that it is “successful in many ways”, especially in showing the power of ordinary voters; the rest sits behind a paywall, so only the standfirst is quoted. [呂文慧 at Independent Opinion](https://opinion.cw.com.tw/blog/profile/423/article/13587) (in Chinese) binged it in a day and recommends it as natural as breathing. Juries and critics rate it highly while PTT stays cool; reviewers in both languages share one reservation, the fictional parties and the absence of China.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, workplace sexual harassment.
- **Length**: 8 episodes; on Netflix from April 2023 and on PTS from October 2023; finished. Its sexual-harassment storyline set off Taiwan’s 2023 #MeToo movement.
- **Watch first**: An original script; watch it straight away.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.beauty321.com/post/67277) (subscription). Availability is based on a September 2025 Netflix list by the Taiwanese site BEAUTY美人圈

- **Reviews**: [Rito P. Asilo at the Philippine Daily Inquirer](https://entertainment.inquirer.net/374465/workers-transcends-mindless-comedy) watched only the first two episodes and recommends it, saying the “uneven humor” does not dilute its issues. - **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, a comedy of life on a building site.

### Medicine and work

A Vietnamese doctor treating undocumented migrant workers, a rogue surgeon in the countryside, a prison guard, a fire station and a taxi company after the 921 earthquake.

### History and eras

The 1867 Rover shipwreck, a Tainan farming village in 1943, Taiwanese POW guards in Borneo, a tea factory in 1949 and political-prisoner doctors on Green Island.

#### Gold Leaf

In 1949 the only daughter of Taiwan’s largest tea factory, newly divorced, takes over the debt-ridden family business and leads it into the global tea wars ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gold_Leaf_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Drama Series nomination 2022` `B 500times 2022, Ryan Cheng’s “three key Taiwanese dramas”` `E Google Taiwan 2022 trending dramas #9` `F PTT 2022 successful Taiwanese dramas thread (5 mentions)` `G LINE TODAY 2021 most recommended Taiwanese drama #4` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #2 (26 December 2021)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.7 (35 ratings) | IMDb 8.3 (307 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Brian Hioe](https://nomanisanis.land/gold-leaf-review/) reviewed only episodes 1–2 and recommends it as “a much more capable work” than Seqalu or Island Nation, though its heroine slips into gender stereotypes. [傅紀鋼 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/160894) (in Chinese) praises the production but argues that shifting responsibility for the new Taiwan dollar onto the Americans blurs the Nationalist government’s role in draining Taiwan in 1945–1949. Well regarded, it was overshadowed by a dispute over that currency history; a beauty site’s Douban compilation gives 8.4, and MyDramaList has only 35 ratings.

- **Watch first**: Inspired by the story of tea merchant Chiang A-hsin; nothing to watch first.

#### Three Tears in Borneo

Late in the Pacific War, young Taiwanese are drafted to guard Allied prisoners in Borneo; after the war they are accused of massacring prisoners and tried by an Australian military court ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Three_Tears_in_Borneo)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries 2025` `A Series Mania 2024 International Panorama selection` `E DailyView 2024 Taiwanese dramas #7` `F PTT 2025 mystery thread (4 mentions, “the suspense is well built”)` `G LINE TODAY 2024 Taiwanese dramas #2` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #10 (24 November 2024)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (7 ratings) | IMDb 8.3 (176 votes)
- **Reviews**: There is no English review, only a [Taiwan Gazette feature](https://www.taiwangazette.org/news/2026/2/15/who-doesnt-want-to-be-good-in-war-three-tears-in-borneo-revisits-taiwans-wwii-war-crimes-trials) on the history and interviews with the director. [陳姝蓉 at Independent Opinion](https://opinion.cw.com.tw/blog/profile/513/article/16737) (in Chinese) watched all five episodes and recommends it, while finding the weight of the era almost too much to bear. After broadcast it was accused of distorting history and smearing the real people behind the characters; PTS held a forum, where the director said all characters are fictional. A beauty site’s Douban compilation gives 8.4 from about 1,700 ratings; MyDramaList has only 7.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, abuse of prisoners of war, a massacre and war-crimes trials.
- **Length**: 5 episodes, 2024, finished.
- **Watch first**: Drawn from the history of Taiwanese POW-camp guards and the post-war trials; the director says the characters are fictional. Nothing to watch first.
- **Where to watch**: [公視+](https://www.ptsplus.tv/zh/programs/63b046d9-794e-41df-a76b-cd96a3e330fd) (free, viewable worldwide)
