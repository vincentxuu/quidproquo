The last Taiwanese post covers romance, family and youth: a girl who travels through time to find her boyfriend, an ordinary woman raised in Tainan, a daughter caring for her father with dementia, and the friendship of two actresses. All 16 clear the bar from [How to Pick a Show by Country](/posts/film/2026-11-05-world-tv-drama-guide-overview-en): at least three of seven recommendation source types back each show, and each can be watched legally in Taiwan. The selection rules are explained at the start of the [mystery and crime post](/posts/film/2026-11-26-tdrama-mystery-crime-thriller-en), social and historical dramas are in the [society and history post](/posts/film/2026-11-26-tdrama-society-work-history-en), and the Korean equivalent is the [Korean life and family post](/posts/film/2026-11-08-kdrama-life-family-period-en).

Two things stand out in this group:

- **Women’s stories dominate.** 7 shows sit under “women and family”, from middle-aged friendships and a mother’s second act to dementia care.
- **International reach varies widely.** Someday or One Day has 11,000 MyDramaList ratings; The Making of an Ordinary Woman and Living won awards in Taiwan but have only one or two hundred ratings abroad. The chart below compares them on a log scale.

## The watchlist

Entries list recommended by, scores, reviews, content, length, watch first and where to watch.

- **Scores**: none of these has a Metascore, so this post uses [MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/search?adv=titles&ty=68&co=5&so=top) and IMDb, flagging small rating counts. The score line gives Douban only from year-end lists or Taiwanese news reports; figures compiled by beauty sites, fashion magazines or mainland media appear in the review entry with their source.
- **Content**: the 5 ratings shown come from Hami Video pages; other Taiwan platform pages show none. Content notes mostly follow the plot, as only Let’s Talk About CHU has IMDb Parents Guide votes.
- **Where to watch**: links go to Taiwan platform title pages; where Netflix availability rests on an official press release or media report, the entry says so.

### Romance

Lovers across time, a remake of a Korean tearjerker, a sex educator’s love life, pandemic-era couples, a doctor–patient line crossed, and a marriage on the rocks.

#### Someday or One Day

Grieving her late boyfriend, Huang Yu-hsuan of 2019 is sent back to 1998 into the body of her lookalike Chen Yun-ru, and meets Li Tzu-wei, who looks exactly like the boyfriend she lost ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Someday_or_One_Day)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Drama Series 2020` `B 500times 2022, Ryan Cheng’s “three key Taiwanese dramas”` `C MyDramaList 8.8 (11,133 ratings, #1 Taiwanese drama)` `C Douban 2020 highest-rated Chinese-language series #1` `D Netflix H1 2023: 2.2M hours viewed worldwide` `E Google Taiwan 2020 trending dramas #4` `F PTT 2020 all-time Taiwanese drama poll #2 (146 votes)` `F PTT 2022 successful Taiwanese dramas thread (about 12 mentions, “a phenomenon”)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.8 (11,150 ratings) | IMDb 8.4 (2.6K votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [唐肇陽 at Filmaholic](https://filmaholic.tw/series/5f59d6dafd89780001dc178b/) (in Chinese) watched all 13 episodes and strongly recommends it, finding it almost flawless by its own internal logic. Taiwan’s juries, viewers and Douban agree, while English-language media are absent. A WeChat-account piece carried by the People’s Daily app says its Douban score rose from 8.5 to 9.2 during the run.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, the death of the heroine’s boyfriend and a mystery around another character’s death, with thriller elements.
- **Length**: 13 episodes, 2019–2020; one season, finished. A sequel film with the original cast, also called Someday or One Day, opened in December 2022. The Korean series A Time Called You is a remake, not a sequel.
- **Watch first**: An original script; nothing to watch first. Finish the series before the sequel film.
- **Where to watch**: [Disney+](https://www.disneyplus.com/zh-tw/browse/entity-0ecf60e6-2247-4d59-bd18-7976772866a3) (subscription)

#### I Am Married...But!

Ad editor Lin Yi-ling has lived with her in-laws for three years and thinks about divorce once a week, until she meets a soulmate on a dating app ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/I_Am_Married...But!)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Drama Series nomination 2025` `B Taiwan critics’ poll 2025, Taiwanese dramas #9` `D Netflix 2025 Chinese-language series #7 (2.1M views)` `E Google Taiwan 2025 trending dramas #1` `E DailyView 2025 Taiwanese dramas #3` `F PTT 2025 top-three thread (5 mentions)` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Taiwanese dramas #6`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.8 (2,159 ratings) | IMDb 7.1 (663 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Sarah Musnicky at But Why Tho](https://butwhytho.net/2025/02/i-am-married-but-review-netflix-jasper-liu/) watched all 12 episodes and recommends it, though she says it “runs a little longer than necessary”. [雪奈 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/8553588) (in Chinese) recommends it, calling the quarrelling couple who turn out to be each other’s online soulmate the most romantic idea in the show. Big audiences, cool juries: it went home empty-handed from the Golden Bells, and a beauty site’s Douban compilation gives only 7.1. 徐佑德 at The News Lens found it short on comic tension.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, a marriage in crisis and emotional infidelity, played as comedy.
- **Length**: 12 episodes of about 30 minutes; on Netflix from February 2025; finished.
- **Watch first**: An original script; watch it straight away.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://about.netflix.com/zh_tw/news/happily-never-after-i-am-married-but-unveils-the-realities-of-marriage-in) (subscription). Availability is based on Netflix’s own January 2025 announcement

#### Let’s Talk About CHU

Waxing specialist Chu Ai runs a sex-education channel on the side and lives by “only sex, no love”, while each generation of her family has its own sexual troubles ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Let's_Talk_About_Chu)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries nomination 2024` `C IMDb 7.1 (938 votes)` `D Netflix H1 2024: 2.3M views (the top Taiwanese drama that half)` `E Google Taiwan 2024 trending dramas #6` `E DailyView 2024 Taiwanese dramas #10` `G LINE TODAY 2024 Taiwanese dramas #7` `G Netflix Taiwan weekly #2 (4 February 2024)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.7 (3,997 ratings) | IMDb 7.1 (938 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Joel Keller at Decider](https://decider.com/2024/02/02/lets-talk-about-chu-netflix-review/) reviewed only the first episode and says Stream It, calling it “light and funny” and saying its family dynamic makes it more than a show about sex. [劉沐恩 at The Crossing](https://crossing.cw.com.tw/article/18634) (in Chinese) recommends it for showing the generation gap over sex and marriage without casting either side as right or wrong. Its Netflix numbers were the best of any Taiwanese drama that half-year, but opinion is middling; 雀雀 at United Daily News says it remains a Taiwanese idol romance at heart. A beauty site’s Douban compilation gives 7.7.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. IMDb Parents Guide votes put Sex & Nudity at Moderate, and users note at least one sex scene an episode with partial nudity; there is also a gay storyline.
- **Length**: 8 episodes; on Netflix from February 2024; finished.
- **Watch first**: An original script; watch it straight away. The Chinese title puns on a line from a classical Chinese motto.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81718141) (subscription)

#### More Than Blue: The Series

A record producer chasing the rights to a song finds, in the late composer K’s diary, the story of K and Cream, and of the leukaemia K kept hidden ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/More_Than_Blue:_The_Series)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries nomination 2022` `C MyDramaList 8.4 (#9 Taiwanese drama)` `C IMDb 8.2 (720 votes)` `D Netflix H1 2023: 1.1M hours viewed` `G LINE TODAY 2021 most recommended Taiwanese drama #9 (3.91%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 8.4 (4,004 ratings) | IMDb 8.2 (720 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Greg Wheeler at The Review Geek](https://www.thereviewgeek.com/morethanblue-s1review/) watched all 10 episodes and gives it 7.5/10: a little overlong, but “a real tear-jerker from start to finish”. No signed Taiwanese review could be found. Four thousand MyDramaList users give it 8.4 while Taiwan’s critics stay almost silent; English reviews recommend it but find it too long or too sentimental. A beauty site’s Douban compilation gives 7.4.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, leukaemia, parents killed in a car crash and the death of a child.
- **Length**: 10 episodes; on Netflix from October 2021; finished.
- **Watch first**: A remake of a Korean film and of its 2018 Taiwanese film adaptation; no need to watch either first.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81442597) (subscription). The Taiwan evidence dates from 2021, so it may no longer be available

#### Adrift in Love

Chou Hsiao-chi, who measures love in money, falls for the psychiatrist she meets in therapy, while office worker Pan Hsin-tung faces a romance across a wide social gap ([MyDramaList](https://mydramalist.com/713703-love-afloat)).

- **Recommended by**: `D Netflix H1 2026 Taiwanese dramas #9 (0.1M views)` `E Google Taiwan 2025 trending dramas #5` `F PTT 2025 top-three thread (in the original poster’s top three, 1 mention)` `G MyVideo 2025 drama chart top 10 (a MyVideo co-production)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.3 (166 ratings) | IMDb 7.1 (55 votes)
- **Reviews**: No signed English review could be found. [雀雀 at United Daily News](https://stars.udn.com/star/story/10093/9253363) (in Chinese) recommends it, arguing that its unspoken villain is money, since love cannot survive without bread on the table. Platform numbers are good but reviews are scarce; the mainland’s Sanlian Lifeweek calls its Douban 7.4 middling and criticises the melodramatic main plot and stale subplots. IMDb has only 55 votes.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, infidelity and a doctor–patient relationship that crosses the line.
- **Length**: 14 episodes, October–December 2025 on PTS; finished.
- **Watch first**: Adapted from Hou Wen-yong’s novel of the same name; no need to read it.
- **Where to watch**: [公視+](https://www.ptsplus.tv/zh/programs/5d550a85-687e-44ce-8be2-f905040ed268) (available)

#### At the Moment

Ten love stories set in a city during the pandemic, linked by a dating show, with each episode’s leads turning up in the others ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E6%AD%A4%E6%99%82%E6%AD%A4%E5%88%BB_%28%E9%9B%BB%E8%A6%96%E5%8A%87%29)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Drama Series nomination 2024` `A Asian Academy Creative Awards 2024 Taiwan best supporting actress (Dee Hsu)` `C IMDb 6.9 (520 votes)` `E Google Taiwan 2023 trending dramas #5` `E DailyView 2023 Taiwanese dramas #6` `G LINE TODAY 2023 readers’ favourite Taiwanese drama #6 (6.27%)`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.5 (1,010 ratings) | IMDb 6.9 (520 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Jonathon Wilson at Ready Steady Cut](https://readysteadycut.com/2023/11/11/at-the-moment-season-1-review/) watched all 10 episodes and recommends it with reservations, saying the makers forgot to “end every episode half an hour earlier”. [米果 at Independent Opinion](https://opinion.cw.com.tw/blog/profile/57/article/14264) (in Chinese) recommends it as reading like ten popular short stories. Taiwanese opinion is split: 潘光中 at Movie Search tears into it as waste dressed up as romance. A beauty site’s Douban compilation gives 6.7, the lowest of these 16.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; a pandemic-era romance anthology, and episode 5 is a gay love story.
- **Length**: 10 episodes, one love story each; on Netflix from November 2023; finished.
- **Watch first**: An original anthology; the stories stand alone, so pick and choose.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81701115) (subscription)

### Women and family

Middle-aged women comparing lives, a Tainan girl growing up, two actresses, a mother’s second act, a father with dementia, a funeral-business family, and a family behind a sexual-assault case.

#### Forget You Not

Forty-year-old Cheng Le-le, an aspiring stand-up comic whose marriage is falling apart, cares for her father after he is diagnosed with Alzheimer’s ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forget_You_Not_(TV_series))).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries nomination 2026` `B Taiwan critics’ poll 2025, Taiwanese dramas #4` `C Douban 2025 highest-rated Chinese-language series #4 (8.3)` `E Google Taiwan 2025 trending dramas #4` `E DailyView 2025 Taiwanese dramas #2` `F PTT 2025 top-three thread (in the original poster’s top three, 3 mentions)` `G LINE TODAY 2025 Taiwanese dramas #4`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.8 (263 ratings) | IMDb 7.6 (362 votes) | Douban 8.3 (#4 among Douban’s highest-rated Chinese-language series of 2025)
- **Reviews**: [Sarah Musnicky at But Why Tho](https://butwhytho.net/2025/05/forget-you-not-review-netflix/) watched all 8 episodes and strongly recommends it as “not an easy watch, but a necessity”. [徐佑德 at The News Lens](https://www.thenewslens.com/article/253417) (in Chinese) recommends it with reservations: not flawless, but sincere and tender at heart. Douban and the juries rate it highly while PTT is split. Off screen, it faced a plagiarism accusation (denied by Netflix), and a family-caregivers’ group criticised its depiction of long-term care as unrealistic.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, dementia care, signing a resuscitation directive and a father’s death, with sexual harassment in the opening.
- **Length**: 8 episodes; on Netflix from May 2025; finished. Rene Liu’s first series as director.
- **Watch first**: An original script; watch it straight away.
- **Where to watch**: [Netflix](https://www.netflix.com/tw/title/81730394) (subscription)

#### Imperfect Us

Two women born on the same day who loved the same man spend 12 years comparing themselves on social media, then meet again around 40 ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Imperfect_Us)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Global OTT Awards 2024 Best Lead Actress (Ariel Lin) and Best OST` `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries nomination 2024` `C Douban 2024 highest-rated Chinese-language series #8` `C MyDramaList 7.9 (#50 Taiwanese drama)` `E Google Taiwan 2024 trending dramas #3` `E DailyView 2024 Taiwanese dramas #3` `G LINE TODAY 2024 Taiwanese dramas #3`
- **Scores**: MyDramaList 7.9 (320 ratings) | IMDb 7.7 (445 votes)
- **Reviews**: [Jan Lee at The Straits Times](https://www.straitstimes.com/life/entertainment/binge-worthy-imperfect-us-elevates-love-triangle-trope-to-centre-on-complex-female-leads) gives it 4 stars after about six episodes, before the finale, praising how unafraid it is to centre on “complicated female characters”. [米果 at Independent Opinion](https://opinion.cw.com.tw/blog/profile/57/article/14828) (in Chinese) recommends it after the finale, saying 徐譽庭’s writing is gentle yet cuts to the bone. A beauty site’s Douban compilation fell from 9.0 during the run to 8.3 after it, with the ending as the sticking point; 芬多經 at The News Lens, reviewing only the first two episodes, found the aphorisms preachy.
- **Content**: No Taiwan rating found on platform pages. No IMDb Parents Guide votes; going by the plot, infidelity and romantic rivalry.
- **Length**: 8 episodes, 2024, finished. The first two episodes premiered at the 2023 Golden Horse Film Festival. IMDb lists it as Our Fragile Strength.
- **Watch first**: An original script; watch it straight away.
- **Where to watch**: [公視+](https://www.ptsplus.tv/zh/programs/6c134e09-7811-4585-b270-89f2658197a4) (available)

#### The Making of an Ordinary Woman

At 39, Chen Chia-ling has no flat, no car, no husband and now no job; back in her Tainan hometown she learns to accept herself as an ordinary woman ([Wikipedia](https://zh.wikipedia.org/wiki/%E4%BF%97%E5%A5%B3%E9%A4%8A%E6%88%90%E8%A8%98)).

- **Recommended by**: `A Golden Bell Best Miniseries 2020 (Season 1)` `A Golden Bell Best Drama Series nomination 2022 (Season 2)` `C IMDb 8.5 (982 votes, both seasons)` `F PTT 2019 Taiwanese drama poll #2 (463 votes)` `F PTT 2020 all-time Taiwanese drama poll #3 (104 votes)` `G LINE TODAY 2021 most recommended Taiwanese drama #2 (Season 2, 14.6%)`

## Limits of this list

- **Few English reviews.** 7 of the 16 have no signed English review, including Someday or One Day and The Making of an Ordinary Woman; Decider’s reviews cover only the first episode.
- **Taiwanese reviews still cluster.** 雀雀 is the main reviewer for 3 shows, since only she reviewed Adrift in Love, Lovely Villain and The Six of Us; More Than Blue has no signed Taiwanese review.
- **Douban mostly comes from compilations.** The score line shows Douban for only 4 shows; the rest appear in the review entries with their source.
- **Licensed shows can disappear.** Taiwan evidence for More Than Blue stops in 2021. Streaming links checked: 2026-11-26.
