> 🌏 [English version](/en/posts/learning/2026-10-07-job-hunt-chat-english-en)

和也在找工作的朋友用英文傳訊息，聊面試進度、大公司和新創的差別、朋友在外商的工作狀況。整段聊得下去，對方也都懂，但回頭看自己打的句子，同樣的地方一直重複出錯。

這篇把那段對話裡我自己的句子整理成六種修正。重點不放在逐句改錯，而是找出「同一個原因」造成的一整批句子，這樣下次開口時只要記六件事。對話內容已經改寫成一般情境，不含對方的訊息。

## 我當時想說什麼

對象是朋友，語氣輕鬆，用訊息聊。想做的事有四種：

- 問對方到目前為止的進度：有沒有找到、有沒有投、有沒有試過
- 講自己最近的經驗：面試了幾家、上週去了哪裡
- 轉述第三個人說的話：朋友說、HR 說
- 解釋原因和猜測：因為資安、可能只是想壓低條件

## 在這個情境可以怎麼說

### 一、問「到目前為止有沒有」：Have you + 過去分詞

我原本一路用 Do you 開頭：

| 我原本打的 | 這個情境可用的說法 |
| --- | --- |
| Do you find any company…? | Have you found any companies where people work in English? |
| Do you apply big company…? | Have you applied to any big companies? |
| Do you try …? | Have you tried …? |

Cambridge 的說明是：present perfect 用來談到目前為止的經驗，發生的時間點不重要，例句有 *Have you ever tried to write your name and address with your left hand?*（[Present perfect simple](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/present-perfect-simple-i-have-worked)）。問朋友求職進度正是這種「到現在為止」的問法。

有明確的過去時間就換成過去式。Cambridge 寫 past simple 用在指明時間的過去，例如 *yesterday*、*two weeks ago*、*last year*（[Past simple or present perfect?](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/past-simple-or-present-perfect)）。所以「上週去參觀」是 *I visited the office last week*，不用現在式 *I visit*。

上面三句是依這個句型改寫的，不是 Cambridge 原句。

### 二、apply 和 interview 後面接什麼

**apply**：Cambridge 字典列出 *apply for* 接想要的東西（*I've applied for a new job with the local newspaper*），*apply to sb/sth* 接對象（*We've applied to a charitable organization for a grant*）（[apply](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/apply)）。所以：

- 投某家公司：*apply to a company*
- 應徵某個職缺：*apply for a job*

字典另一個例句 *She's applied for a job with an insurance company* 把兩者合在一起：職缺用 for，公司用 with。

我原本寫的 *apply big company*、*applied startup* 都少了介系詞。

**interview**：動詞有兩個方向。一個是「問別人問題」：*we only plan to interview about 20 of them*。另一個是「去面試」：*I interviewed for several jobs but I didn't get any of them*（[interview](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/interview)）。

我原本寫 *after interviewing many company*。interview 後面直接接受詞，讀起來是「我去訪問了很多公司」。要表達自己是求職者，可以說：

- *I've interviewed for several jobs.*
- *I've interviewed for jobs with several companies.*（字典商用英語例句是 *He interviewed for a number of jobs with banks and telephone companies*）
- *I've had interviews with several companies.*（依字典名詞例句 *an interview for a job with a publisher* 改寫）

### 三、other、others、複數與冠詞

| 我原本打的 | 這個情境可用的說法 |
| --- | --- |
| Others company is… | Other companies are… |
| two company | two companies |
| some colleague based abroad | some colleagues are based abroad |
| This is new era | This is a new era |
| a ex colleague | an ex-colleague |

Cambridge 的規則很直接：other 當限定詞時沒有複數形，後面接複數名詞（*The other girls went home*，不是 *The others girls*）；others 是代名詞，單獨使用（*others disagree*）（[Other, others, the other or another?](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/other-others-the-other-or-another)）。

所以「其他公司」是 *other companies*，如果前面已經提過公司，也可以只說 *others*。

冠詞的部分，Cambridge 說明 a/an 用在聽的人還不知道的事物，只搭配單數可數名詞，母音開頭的發音前用 *an*（[A/an and the](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/a-an-and-the)）。*ex* 是母音開頭，所以是 *an ex-colleague*。

中文沒有複數和冠詞，這一類最容易在打字快的時候漏掉。

### 四、because 接句子，because of 接名詞

我原本寫 *because of no one knows* 和 *because of security issue*。

Cambridge：because 是連接詞，後面接完整子句；because of 是介系詞，意思是「由於」，後面接名詞（*Because of the rain, the tennis match was stopped*）（[Because, because of](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/because-because-of-and-cos-cos-of)）。

- *…because no one knows who owns it.*（後面是句子）
- *…because of security concerns.*（後面是名詞）

同一個原因兩種寫法都可以，差別只在後面放什麼。

### 五、形容詞前要有 be，句子要有主詞

| 我原本打的 | 這個情境可用的說法 |
| --- | --- |
| Do you familiar with LeetCode? | Are you familiar with LeetCode? |
| maybe just want lowball me | They might just be lowballing the offer. |
| maybe not good | It might not be a good one. |

familiar 是形容詞，Cambridge 列的片語是 *be familiar with something/someone*，例句 *I'm not familiar with your poetry*（[familiar](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/familiar)）。所以問句用 *Are you*，不是 *Do you*。

中文可以直接說「可能只是想壓低」，英文需要主詞 *they*。表達不確定的猜測，Cambridge 說 might 最常用來指較弱的可能性（[Might](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/might)）。

lowball 在 Cambridge 標為美式、非正式，意思是把價格或水準算得不合理地低，例句有 *prompted would-be buyers to lowball their offers*（[lowball](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/lowball)）。Cambridge 例句的受詞是 offer、estimate 這類東西。Merriam-Webster 另外列出接人的用法，定義是 *to give (a customer) a deceptively low price or cost estimate*，例句 *lowballed him in contract negotiations*（[lowball](https://www.merriam-webster.com/dictionary/lowball)）。所以 *lowballing the offer* 和我原本想說的 *lowballing me* 都有字典依據，我原句真正缺的是主詞和動詞形式。

### 六、轉述別人的話：動詞跟著主詞變

| 我原本打的 | 這個情境可用的說法 |
| --- | --- |
| he say it depends on project you get | He says it depends on the project you get. |
| HR just want to check… | HR just wanted to check… |
| IT only use… | IT only uses… |
| he always eating on google office | He always eats at the office. |

Cambridge：present simple 用原形動詞，第三人稱單數加 -s；have、go、do、be 是不規則的（[Present simple](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/present-simple-i-work)）。*he say* 要改成 *he says*，習慣性的事（總是在公司吃）也用 present simple：*he always eats*。

地點的部分，Cambridge 寫 at 用在把公司、工作場所當成活動地點的時候，例句 *How many people are working at Microsoft?*（[At, on and in (place)](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/grammar/british-grammar/at-on-and-in-place)）。所以是 *at the office*、*a former colleague at that company*。

「同事在國外」可以用 based。Cambridge 的例句是 *The company is based in Toronto*、*Where are you based?*（[based](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/based)）；*based abroad* 是依這個用法改寫。

## 也可以這樣說嗎

| 英文 | 對誰／做什麼時用 | 是否仍是同一個意思 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Have you applied to any big companies? | 問朋友有沒有投大公司 | 主要說法 |
| Have you applied for any jobs at big companies? | 同樣情境，重點放在職缺 | 是，只是把受詞換成職缺 |
| Did you apply to any big companies last month? | 問某段已經結束的時間 | 不同：限定在上個月 |
| I've interviewed for several jobs. | 講自己去面試 | 主要說法 |
| I've interviewed several candidates. | 你是面試官 | 不同：角色反過來 |
| Other companies are still doing things the old way. | 講別家公司 | 主要說法 |
| Others are still doing things the old way. | 前面已經提過公司 | 是，但要有前文 |
| …because of security concerns. | 原因是一個名詞 | 主要說法 |
| …because they're worried about security. | 原因是一個句子 | 是 |
| They might just be lowballing the offer. | 重點在 offer 的金額 | 主要說法 |
| They might just be lowballing me. | 重點在被壓價的人是我 | 是，受詞從金額換成人 |

## 一個用字提醒：sugar daddy

我開玩笑說新創最好有個 *sugar daddy*。Cambridge 的定義是：有錢、通常年紀較大的男性，送禮或給錢給較年輕的人，通常是為了對方的陪伴和性關係（[sugar daddy](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/sugar-daddy)）。朋友之間聽得懂是玩笑，但這個詞的本義帶有性關係，在面試或和不熟的同事聊天時，直接說 *a startup with a lot of funding* 比較安全。這句是我自己用一般字詞寫的，沒有另外查來源。

## 換內容再說一次

先看中文，自己說一次，再看參考說法。

1. 你有找到可以遠端工作的公司嗎？
   - *Have you found any companies that allow remote work?*
2. 你有投新創嗎？
   - *Have you applied to any startups?*
3. 我面試了三個後端職缺。
   - *I've interviewed for three backend jobs.*
4. 其他團隊還沒開始用。
   - *Other teams haven't started using it yet.*
5. 會議取消了，因為颱風。
   - *The meeting was canceled because of the typhoon.*
6. 你熟這個框架嗎？
   - *Are you familiar with this framework?*

## 一段短對話

以下是自己寫的練習示例，不是真實對話。

> A: Have you applied to any big companies?
>
> B: A few. I've interviewed for three jobs so far. How about you?
>
> A: I got one offer, but they might just be lowballing it.
>
> B: Are other companies still deciding?
>
> A: Yeah, two are still in the approval process.
>
> B: A friend of mine works at one of them. He says it depends on the team you get.

## 不看英文，你會怎麼說

自評的標準是能不能把意思說出來，不是字有沒有完全一樣。

| 中文情境 | 參考說法 | 提醒 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 問朋友：你有找到用英文工作的公司嗎？ | Have you found any companies where people work in English? | 問到目前為止，用 Have you |
| 問朋友：你有投大公司嗎？ | Have you applied to any big companies? | apply to 公司 |
| 告訴朋友：我上週去參觀了朋友在東京的辦公室。 | I visited my friend's office in Tokyo last week. | 有 last week，用過去式 |
| 告訴朋友：我面試了好幾個職缺。 | I've interviewed for several jobs. | interview for，不直接接公司 |
| 評論：其他公司還在用舊方法做事。 | Other companies are still doing things the old way. | other + 複數名詞 |
| 解釋：他們只用一種工具，因為有資安顧慮。 | They only use one tool because of security concerns. | because of + 名詞 |
| 問朋友：你熟 LeetCode 嗎？ | Are you familiar with LeetCode? | be familiar with |
| 猜測：他們可能只是想壓低 offer。 | They might just be lowballing the offer. | 要有主詞 they |
| 告訴朋友：我有個前同事在那家公司。 | I have a former colleague at that company. | at + 公司 |
| 轉述：他說要看你分到哪個專案。 | He says it depends on the project you get. | says、depends 都加 -s |
| 轉述：她說有些同事在國外。 | She said some colleagues are based abroad. | colleagues 複數，加 are |
| 問朋友：你有用英文面試過嗎？ | Have you had a job interview in English? | 語言前面用 in |
| 說明：HR 只是想確認我能不能用英文對話。 | HR just wanted to check whether I could hold a conversation in English. | hold a conversation |
| 告訴朋友：面試總共有五輪。 | There were five rounds of interviews. | rounds 複數 |
| 轉述：他說沒上的話，隔年再投一次。 | He says if he doesn't get in, he reapplies the next year. | reapply＝再應徵一次 |
| 提議：我可以幫你問他一些建議。 | I can ask him for some advice. | ask 人 for 東西 |

## 補查到的五個說法

對話裡還有幾句當時只能猜，後來補查到依據：

| 想說的意思 | 這個情境可用的說法 | 依據 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 用英文面試 | I had a job interview in English. | British Council 的教材標題與提問都用 *a job interview in English*（[How to prepare for a job interview in English](https://learnenglish.britishcouncil.org/level/improve-your-english-level/how-prepare-job-interview-english)）；Cambridge 的 in 也有 *They spoke in Russian the whole time*（[in](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/in)） |
| 確認我能不能用英文對話 | HR wanted to check whether I could hold a conversation in English. | Cambridge 列出 *hold/carry on a conversation*（[conversation](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/conversation)） |
| 面試有五輪 | There were five rounds of interviews. | Cambridge 的 round 有 *another round of talks*、*a round of meetings*（[round](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/round)）；套用到面試是改寫 |
| 沒上就明年再投一次 | If he doesn't get in, he reapplies the next year. | Cambridge 的 reapply 例句 *he will not be reapplying for the job*（[reapply](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/reapply)） |
| 我可以幫你問他 | I can ask him for some advice. | Cambridge 的句型 *ask someone for something*，例句 *ask your accountant for some financial advice*（[ask](https://dictionary.cambridge.org/us/dictionary/english/ask)） |

British Council 那一頁同時給了情境層的依據：它直接問學習者 *Have you had a job interview in English?*，正是第一節的 Have you 句型用在求職話題上。

## 仍待核對

這些還沒找到合適來源，先不放進練習：

- 「薪資制度不同」：Cambridge 例句裡出現過 *pay system*、*base salary*、*starting salary*，但還沒有比較 pay structure 與 salary structure 的依據
- 「他在韌體團隊」用 on the team 還是 in the team
- 哪一種說法比較常用：這需要語料庫查詢，這次沒有做

## 整體來說

六項裡面，前兩項（Have you、apply／interview 的介系詞）影響意思最多，值得優先練。後四項對方多半猜得到，但修好之後句子會順很多。這次的修正大多只查到「字義與句型」這一層，來源是學習字典與文法書；只有「用英文面試」一句有 British Council 教材的情境示範。沒有母語者測試，也沒有頻率比較，所以文中只寫「這個情境可用」，不寫「最自然」。
