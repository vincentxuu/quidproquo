Certifications are investments, not collectibles. This post lays out the real numbers for three classic security certifications: what they cost, how long to prepare, what maintenance looks like, and how much salary uplift to expect.

Parts 1 and 2 of this series already mapped the [full certification landscape](/en/posts/tech/2026-09-10-security-cert-landscape-en) and the [AI security cert showdown](/en/posts/tech/2026-09-10-ai-security-cert-showdown-en). This post focuses on the three you're most likely to take first: Security+, AWS Security Specialty, and CISSP.

## At a glance

| | Security+ | AWS Security Specialty | CISSP |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Issuer** | CompTIA | AWS | ISC2 |
| **Focus** | General security fundamentals | AWS cloud security | Security management across 8 domains |
| **Exam code** | SY0-701 | SCS-C03 | — |
| **Questions / time** | 90 / 90 min | 65 / 170 min | 100–150 (CAT) / 3 hours |
| **Passing score** | 750/900 | 750/1000 | 700/1000 |
| **Experience req.** | None (2 years IT recommended) | None (5 years IT + 2 years AWS recommended) | **5 years** across 2 domains (degree waives 1 year) |

## Cost: it's not just the exam fee

### Exam fees

| Certification | Exam fee (USD) | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Security+ | $404–$439 (price increase from June 2026) | Student and partner discounts often available |
| AWS Security | $300 | **50% off ($150)** if you hold any active AWS certification |
| CISSP | $749 | Global flat rate, Pearson VUE |

**Sources**: [CompTIA official](https://www.comptia.org/certifications/security), [AWS certification](https://aws.amazon.com/certification/certified-security-specialty/), [ISC2 official](https://www.isc2.org/certifications/cissp)

|---|---|---|---|

| AWS Security | AWS Skill Builder free courses + whitepapers | Udemy Stephane Maarek / Neal Davis ($28–$120) + Tutorials Dojo question bank | $0–$150 |

### Five-year total cost of ownership

This is what people overlook — certifications don't end at the exam.

| Certification | Validity | Renewal method | Annual fee | 5-year maintenance |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Security+ | 3 years | 50 CEU + annual fee | $50/year | **$150** (per 3-year cycle) |
| AWS Security | 3 years | Re-exam or earn a higher cert | **None** | **$0** (or $150–$300 to re-exam) |
| CISSP | 3 years | 120 CPE + annual fee | $125/year | **$625** |

**Five-year acquisition + maintenance total**:

| Certification | Exam fee | Materials (est.) | 5-year maintenance | **5-year total** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Security+ | $404 | ~$30 | $150 | **~$584** |
| AWS Security | $150–$300 | ~$50 | $0 | **~$200–$350** |
| CISSP | $749 | ~$100 | $625 | **~$1,474** |

AWS Security Specialty has the lowest five-year holding cost, especially if you already hold another AWS certification for the 50% discount. CISSP is the most expensive, but it buys a fundamentally different level of market positioning.

## Study time

| Certification | Typical prep time | Recommended total hours | Background assumption |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security+ | 2–3 months | 80–120 hours | IT foundation but no security background |
| AWS Security | 2–3 months | 80–120 hours | Existing AWS hands-on experience |
| CISSP | 3–6 months | 150–300+ hours | 5 years cross-domain experience, needs systematic review |

Per [CertStud 2026 analysis](https://certstud.com/blog/do-it-certifications-increase-salary-data-studies), Security+ offers "the most efficient cert investment in cybersecurity" on a study-hours-per-dollar-return basis. CISSP delivers "the highest absolute dollar return of any cybersecurity cert."

**Stacking strategy**: Take Security+ first (2–3 months) → if you hold any AWS cert, take AWS Security next (another 2–3 months at the $150 discount) → accumulate experience, then take CISSP. Security+ counts for 1 year of CISSP experience credit. The first two certs take about 8–12 months total.

## Pass rates

All three certification bodies **do not publish official pass rates**. The following are estimates from community data and training providers.

### Security+

Per [SecuSpark 2026 statistical analysis](https://www.secuspark.com/blog/security-plus-pass-rate-statistics):

| Scenario | Estimated first-attempt pass rate |
|---|---|
| Self-study (100+ hours) | 50–65% |
| Structured training | 85–93% |
| Practice exam scores consistently 85%+ | 90%+ |
| Minimal preparation | < 50% |

**Key predictor**: Practice exam scores are the strongest indicator. If you consistently score 85%+ on practice tests, your real exam pass rate exceeds 90%.

### AWS Security Specialty

Per [Pruvos community data](https://www.pruvos.com/certifications/cloud-computing/aws-scs-c03):

| Scenario | Estimated first-attempt pass rate |
|---|---|
| General candidates | 45–55% |
| Hands-on AWS experience | 65–70% |
| Practice exam scores consistently 80%+ | High pass rate |
| Practice exam scores < 72% | Likely need more hands-on time |

This is one of the harder AWS exams. The 750/1000 passing threshold is 30 points higher than Associate-level exams.

### CISSP

Per [Training Camp 2026 analysis](https://trainingcamp.com/articles/cissp-pass-rate-what-the-numbers-actually-show-in-2026):

> "ISC2 keeps its pass rate data internal, and that remains true as of June 2026. Independent estimates from training providers and candidate communities generally place first attempt success somewhere between 50% and 60%. The widely repeated 20% figure has no verifiable source."

| Scenario | Estimated first-attempt pass rate |
|---|---|
| Structured study + cross-domain experience | 50–60% |
| Self-reported on Reddit | Varies wildly |
| "20% pass rate" claim | **No traceable source** — should not factor into planning |

CISSP's difficulty isn't about individual questions — it's the CAT (Computerized Adaptive Testing) format. Get a question right and the next one is harder; get it wrong and it gets easier. You never know how you're doing. Combined with 8 domains spanning cryptography to physical security to software development security, the breadth is the challenge.

## Salary premiums

Data primarily from the US market. Taiwan premiums are smaller in absolute terms but follow the same trend.

### US market

Per [CyberSecJobs 2026 analysis](https://cybersecjobs.com/best-cybersecurity-certifications) and [CertStud survey](https://certstud.com/blog/do-it-certifications-increase-salary-data-studies):

| Certification | Avg. holder salary (USD) | Premium vs. uncertified | ROI multiple |
|---|---|---|---|
| Security+ | $71,697 (entry-level) | +$10K–$20K | **25–37x** (based on $404 exam fee) |
| AWS Security | Varies by role | +$12K–$20K (on top of existing cloud skills) | **40–67x** (based on $300) |
| CISSP | $164,000 (median) | +$25K–$35K | **33–47x** (based on $749) |

Per [Kore1 Salary Guide](https://www.kore1.com/cybersecurity-salary-guide): "CISSP is the number that makes people recalculate their hiring budget mid-meeting. $25K to $35K over non-certified. That's not noise in the data."

### Taiwan market

Per [CloudInsight 2026 salary survey](https://cloudinsight.cc/zh/blog/security-engineer-guide) (in Mandarin):

| Experience | Monthly salary range (NTD) | Annual estimate |
|---|---|---|
| 0–2 years | 40,000–55,000 | 520K–720K |
| 3–5 years | 55,000–80,000 | 720K–1.04M |
| 5–8 years | 80,000–120,000 | 1.04M–1.56M |
| 8+ years | 120,000–200,000+ | 1.56M–2.6M+ |

Holding advanced certifications like CISSP or OSCP adds **10–20%** to salary. Financial sector pays highest; foreign companies pay 20–50% above local firms.

Taiwan's absolute premium is less dramatic than the US ($25K–$35K is roughly NT$800K–1.12M — close to a full junior annual salary in Taiwan), but certifications remain a hard signal for promotions and job switches. CISSP in particular — with only ~500 holders in Taiwan, its scarcity creates a strong trust signal for enterprise clients.

## ROI rankings

Combining exam cost, study time, maintenance cost, salary premium, and market recognition:

| Rank | Certification | Rationale |
|---|---|---|
| 🥇 | **AWS Security Specialty** | Lowest holding cost ($150–$300), highest ROI multiple (40–67x), directly aligned with AI platform developer work |
| 🥈 | **Security+** | Lowest entry barrier, fastest to earn (2–3 months), counts toward CISSP experience, recognized by Taiwan financial sector |
| 🥉 | **CISSP** | Highest absolute salary premium (+$25K–$35K), highest Taiwan recognition, but highest barrier and maintenance cost |

**Optimal stacking strategy**: Security+ (build foundation, bank 1 year CISSP credit) → AWS Security (align with work, lowest cost) → CISSP (long-term credential). All three over five years costs approximately **$2,258–$2,408**, getting you full coverage from entry to senior level.

## The bottom line

If you're taking one: go with **AWS Security Specialty**. Lowest holding cost, best work alignment, highest ROI multiple. If you already hold another AWS cert, the exam costs just $150.

If you're taking two: start with **Security+** for the foundation ($404, 2–3 months), then take **AWS Security**. Beyond its own value, Security+ banks a year of CISSP experience credit for later.

If you're taking three: add **CISSP** when the time is right. No rush — CISSP requires 5 years of experience, and you can pass the exam first as an Associate of ISC2, then upgrade when your hours are in. The $125/year maintenance fee is an ongoing commitment, so confirm your career path actually calls for it before investing.
